How to use this

Tap a word
Every entry opens a card with its root, what it means here, the grammar, and why this exact word was chosen.
Star what you want back
Saved words gather in the Saved tab and stay in this browser.
Then test it
Each surah has flashcards, a quiz that walks through every word ten at a time and keeps a basket of your mistakes, and Play & Learn — a game that drills the same words.

Transliteration follows a light academic scheme. Meanings are study glosses, not a translation of the Qur’an.
