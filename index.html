How to use this

Tap a word
The Words tab walks the surah verse by verse, with the taught words lit in gold. Every entry opens a card with its root, what it means here, the grammar, and why this exact word was chosen — and every verb comes with its full ṣarf ṣaghīr, from past tense to the noun of instrument. On a card, the arrows step to the next word; pull the card down to close it.
Star what you want back
Saved words gather in the Saved tab and stay in this browser.
Then test it
Each surah has flashcards you grade yourself, a quiz that walks through every word ten at a time and keeps a basket of your mistakes, and Word Rush — a race against the clock to pair each word with its meaning. Every word you get right counts toward the surah’s “learned” bar.
Share anything
Every surah, mode and word card has its own link, and your phone’s Back button steps back through them.

Transliteration follows a light academic scheme. Meanings are study glosses, not a translation of the Qur’an.
