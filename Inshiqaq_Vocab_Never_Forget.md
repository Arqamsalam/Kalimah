# Surah Al-Inshiqāq (84) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–25)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It contains the single best one-line description of human life in the Qur'an.**

> ***"Yā ayyuha'l-insānu innaka kādiḥun ilā rabbika kadḥan fa-mulāqīh"***
> **"O man! You are labouring toward your Lord with great labour — and you WILL meet Him."** (v.6)

⭐ **Every human being is already walking toward Him.** Not *should* walk. **Is walking.** The believer, the denier, the person who's never thought about it — ⭐ **all of them are on the same road, moving at the same speed, toward the same meeting.**

⭐ **The only variable is what you're carrying when you arrive.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐ **This surah doesn't ask you to start a journey. It informs you that you're already on one.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The Sky Obeys** (v.1–5) — ⭐ the sky splits, the earth empties — and both **"listen to their Lord, as is their duty."** Even the cosmos submits gracefully.
2. **The One Sentence** (v.6) — *you are labouring toward Him, and you will meet Him.*
3. **Two Handovers** (v.7–15) — the record in the **right hand** vs. **behind the back**. ⭐ Two homecomings.
4. **Three Oaths** (v.16–19) — the **twilight glow**, the night that **gathers**, the moon that **assembles** — and: *you will surely climb stage by stage.*
5. **The Strange Silence** (v.20–25) — *what's wrong with them that they don't believe?* ⭐ And a "**good news**" that isn't.

## §1 — The Sky Obeys (v.1–5)

**1. انشَقَّتْ — *inshaqqat*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**splitting / cleaving**" family — *shaqqa* = to split open; ***shiqq*** = a half; ⭐ ***mashaqqah*** = **HARDSHIP** (something that **splits you**); *shaqīq* = a full brother (**split from the same source**).
- **Here it means:** "When the sky **is split open**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐ **you own this root already from ʿAbasa 80:26** — *thumma **shaqaqna**'l-arḍa **shaqqā*** ("then We **split** the earth, a **splitting**") — where the earth was cleaved so **plants could come up alive.**
  
  ⭐ **Same verb, two events:** ⭐ **ʿAbasa splits the EARTH so life emerges. Inshiqāq splits the SKY so the Day arrives.** ⭐ **Both are things being broken open so something can come through.**
  
  ⭐ **And note the family across three consecutive surahs:**
  - **Takwīr 81:11** — sky ***kushiṭat*** (**flayed off**)
  - **Infiṭār 82:1** — sky ***infaṭarat*** (**cleft open**)
  - **Inshiqāq 84:1** — sky ***inshaqqat*** (**split apart**)
  
  ⭐ **Three surahs, three verbs, one sky.** ⚠️ **Learn them together or you'll swap them.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form VII (اِنْفَعَلَ)** — the **ان-** prefix, meaning it happens **to** the subject with **no doer named**. ⭐ **Same shape as *infaṭarat* (82:1), *inkadarat* (81:2), *inqalabū* (83:31).** **Fourth instance you own.**
- **Why this word:** *inshaqqat* over "broke" — because the root also gives ***mashaqqah* (hardship)**, which is exactly what v.6 is about to describe.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐ ***mashaqqah* = HARDSHIP** (from splitting); ***shaqīq*** = a full brother. ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:26 split the EARTH for life; here the SKY splits for the Day.** ⭐ **Sky verbs: *kushiṭat* (81) → *infaṭarat* (82) → *inshaqqat* (84).**

**2. وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا — *wa adhinat li-rabbihā*** · v.2, 5
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ **a beautiful double root** — *adhina* = ⭐ **to LISTEN attentively** *and* **to permit/obey**. Same letters give ***udhun*** = **an EAR**, ***adhān*** = ⭐ **the CALL TO PRAYER** (what you listen for!), and ***idhn*** = permission.
- **Here it means:** "and it **listens to its Lord** — and it is fitting for it [to do so]."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **This is the loveliest touch in the surah, and it turns a destruction scene into an act of worship.**
  
  ⭐ **The sky isn't destroyed. The sky OBEYS.** ⭐ **The root means "to give ear" — so the sky *hears the instruction and complies*, the way you'd respond to the *adhān*.**
  
  ⭐ **Think about the ordering: the sky splitting is described as LISTENING.** Not shattering, not failing — ⭐ **attending.** The cataclysm is reframed as ⭐ **the cosmos being a good servant.**
  
  ⭐ **And the phrase repeats VERBATIM in v.2 and v.5** — once for the **sky**, once for the **earth**. ⭐ **Both listen. Both comply. Both are "fitted" for it.** ⭐ **Then v.6 turns to YOU** — the one creature that gets a choice.
  
  ⭐ **The implicit argument: the sky listens, the earth listens — and man argues.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-rabbihā*** with **لِـ** — "listened **to** its Lord," where the *lām* marks ⭐ **obedient attention**, not just hearing. And ***ḥuqqat*** = "**and it was made its duty / it is only right**" — a passive from *ḥaqq* (**truth, right, obligation**).
- **Why this word:** *adhinat* over "obeyed" — because ⭐ **listening is gentler and more personal than obeying**, and it makes the sky sound **willing**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***udhun* = EAR; *adhān* = the CALL TO PRAYER.*** ⭐ **The sky doesn't break — it LISTENS.** ⭐ **Repeated for sky (v.2) AND earth (v.5). Then v.6 turns to you.**

**3. مُدَّتْ — *muddat*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**stretching / extending**" family — *madda* = to stretch out; ***madd*** = **the LENGTHENING of a vowel in tajwīd** (you use this word every time you recite!); *imtidād* = extension; *māddah* = material/substance.
- **Here it means:** "And when the earth **is stretched out** [flat]."
- **Word magic:** ⭐ **you already say this word in every recitation session.** ⭐ ***Madd* in tajwīd = holding a vowel long*** — **stretching a sound.** ⭐ **Here the EARTH gets stretched** — mountains flattened, valleys filled, curves pulled straight into an endless plain.
  
  ⭐ **And the reason it's stretched is chilling in its practicality:** ⭐ **to fit everyone.** Every human who ever lived, standing at once. ⭐ **The earth is being resized for the gathering.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare Nāziʿāt 79:14** — *fa-idhā hum bi's-**sāhirah*** ("and suddenly they are on **the wakeful plain**"). ⭐ **Nāziʿāt names the plain; Inshiqāq shows you it being MADE.** **Same location, two stages.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **a DOUBLED verb** (مضاعف — last two root letters identical: م-د-د), so they **merge with a shaddah**: *muddat*, not *mudidat*. ⭐ **Same doubled-verb rule as *yafirru* (ʿAbasa 80:34).**
- **Why this word:** *muddat* over "flattened" — because **stretching implies making ROOM**, which is the purpose.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***madd* = the vowel-STRETCH in tajwīd*** — you use it daily. ⭐ **Now picture the EARTH being stretched** — flattened to fit everyone. ⭐ **This is Nāziʿāt 79:14's *sāhirah* being built.**

**4. أَلْقَتْ ... وَتَخَلَّتْ — *alqat… wa takhallat*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *alqat* — the "**casting / throwing**" family (*alqā* = to throw out) — ⭐ **you own it from Qiyāmah 75:15** (*wa law **alqā** maʿādhīrah* — "even if he **flings out** his excuses"). *takhallat* — ⭐ the "**emptying / becoming vacant**" family — *khalā* = to be empty; ***khālī*** = empty; *takhallā ʿan* = **to relinquish, to let go of**.
- **Here it means:** "and it **casts out what is within it** and **empties itself**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **Two verbs, and the second one is the remarkable one.**
  
  ⭐ ***Alqat*** = throws out its contents (the dead, the buried, everything held). ⭐ **Compare Infiṭār 82:4** — *wa idha'l-qubūru **buʿthirat*** ("the graves are **turned inside out**"). ⭐ **Same event, different verb: Infiṭār TIPS the graves; Inshiqāq has the earth THROW.**
  
  ⭐ **But *takhallat* is the beautiful one.** ⭐ **Form V = REFLEXIVE: "it empties ITSELF."*** ⭐ **The earth isn't emptied — the earth LETS GO.**
  
  ⭐ **And that fits v.5 perfectly:** the very next verse says the earth ⭐ **"listens to its Lord."** ⭐ **So the emptying is described as VOLUNTARY** — the earth is **handing everything over willingly**, like a servant emptying its pockets when asked.
  
  ⭐ **Held next to Qiyāmah 75:15 (*alqā maʿādhīrah* — flinging out EXCUSES), there's a haunting pair:** ⭐ **the earth casts out its dead; man casts out his excuses.** **Same verb, and one of those actions is useful.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form V (تَفَعَّلَ) = reflexive** — *takhallat* = "**it emptied itself.**" ⭐ **You own Form V from ʿAbasa 80:6, 10** (*taṣaddā, talahhā*) and **Nāziʿāt 79:18** (*tazakkā*).
- **Why this word:** *takhallat* over "was emptied" — because ⭐ **the reflexive makes the earth a willing participant**, matching v.5.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***khālī* = EMPTY; *takhallā ʿan* = to LET GO of.*** ⭐ **The earth doesn't get emptied — it EMPTIES ITSELF.** ⭐ **Voluntary. Which is why v.5 says it "listens."**

**5. كَادِحٌ ... كَدْحًا ... فَمُلَاقِيهِ — *kādiḥun… kadḥan… fa-mulāqīh*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *kadaḥa* — ⭐ **an extraordinary root**: to **labour hard, to toil, to strive to exhaustion** — and the original physical sense is ⭐ **to SCRATCH or SCORE something**, to wear at it. A *kadḥ* on the skin is **a scrape**. *mulāqī* — the "**meeting**" family (*laqiya* = to meet) — ⭐ **you own it from Insān 76:11** (*fa-**waqāhum**… wa **laqqāhum***).
- **Here it means:** "O man! **You are labouring toward your Lord with great labour — and you will MEET Him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The most important verse in the surah, and every word is chosen.**
  
  ⭐ **First: *kādiḥ* is not "walking" or "traveling."*** The root means ⭐ **toiling to the point of being SCRAPED by it.** Life isn't described as a stroll — it's ⭐ **effortful, wearing, marking you.** ⭐ **You arrive scratched.**
  
  ⭐ **Second: it's a PARTICIPLE, not a verb — *kādiḥun*, "you ARE a labourer."*** ⭐ **This isn't something you do; it's what you ARE, right now, continuously.** ⭐ **Nobody is exempt. The atheist labours toward Him. The lazy person labours toward Him. Standing still is still moving.**
  
  ⭐ **Third: *kadḥan* is a cognate accusative*** (*maf'ūl muṭlaq*) — ⭐ **the verb's own noun repeated for emphasis**: "labouring **a labouring**." ⭐ **You own this from Nāziʿāt 79:1–4 and ʿAbasa 80:25–26.** ⭐ **Arabic's underline key — "and I mean REALLY labouring."**
  
  ⭐ **Fourth, and best: *fa-mulāqīh* — "and you WILL MEET HIM."*** ⭐ **The فَـ makes it an immediate, inevitable result.** ⭐ **Not "you may meet Him," not "you will be judged" — you will MEET Him.** ⭐ **The whole exhausting journey is reframed as a rendezvous.**
  
  ⭐ **Put it together:** ⭐ **You are already scraping your way toward a meeting you did not schedule and cannot miss.** ⭐ **That's human life in eleven Arabic words.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***innaka* + participle*** = a **strong present-continuous statement of fact.** ⭐ ***Mulāqīhi*** is a **Form III participle** (*lāqā* = to meet face-to-face — Form III often means **mutual/directed-at-another** action). ⭐ **Note it DROPS ITS NŪN** in *iḍāfah* with the pronoun: *mulāqin* → *mulāqīhi*.
- **Why this word:** *kadaḥa* over any gentler verb — because ⭐ **it admits life is HARD**, which makes the promise of the meeting land as relief, not threat.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***kadḥ* = a SCRAPE; *kādiḥ* = one who toils till worn.*** ⭐ **It's a PARTICIPLE: you don't *do* this, you ARE this.** ⭐ **Everyone is already walking toward Him — believer and denier alike.** ⭐ **And the ending isn't judgment. It's a MEETING.**

## §2 — Two Handovers, Two Homecomings (v.7–15)

**6. أُوتِيَ كِتَابَهُ بِيَمِينِهِ — *ūtiya kitābahu bi-yamīnih*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *ūtiya* — the "**giving**" family (*ātā* = to give); **passive**: "**he is GIVEN**." *yamīn* — ⭐ **the RIGHT hand** — and the same word means ⭐ **an OATH** (*yamīn* = a solemn vow, because you raise your right hand) and relates to ***yumn*** = **blessing, good fortune** (hence *al-Yaman*, the "blessed" land).
- **Here it means:** "As for the one **given his record in his right hand**…"
- **Word magic:** ⭐ **the word *yamīn* carries THREE meanings at once, and all three fit:**
  - ⭐ **the right hand** (the literal image)
  - ⭐ **an oath** (a solemn, binding commitment)
  - ⭐ **blessing / good fortune** (*yumn*)
  
  ⭐ **So receiving your record "in your *yamīn*" is receiving it in the hand of blessing, honour, and commitment.**
  
  ⭐ **And the PASSIVE (*ūtiya*, "he is GIVEN") is the point:** ⭐ **you don't take your record — it's handed to you.** ⭐ **Same passive in v.10.** **Both men receive; neither chooses the manner.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-ammā… fa-…*** — the classic **"as for X… then Y"** structure. ⭐ **You own it from Nāziʿāt 79:37–41** (*fa-ammā man ṭaghā… wa ammā man khāfa*). ⭐ **Watch for the paired *ammā* — it always signals a two-group contrast.**
- **Why this word:** *yamīn* over "right side" — because the **oath/blessing** senses turn a physical detail into an **honour**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐ ***yamīn* = right hand + an OATH + BLESSING (*yumn*).*** ⭐ **Three meanings, all fitting.** ⭐ **And it's PASSIVE — the record is HANDED to you.**

**7. حِسَابًا يَسِيرًا — *ḥisāban yasīrā*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *ḥisāb* — ⭐ the "**counting / reckoning**" family — **you own it from Nabaʾ 78:27, 36** (*lā yarjūna **ḥisābā*** / *ʿaṭāʾan **ḥisābā***) and **Qiyāmah 75:3** (*a-**yaḥsabu***). *yasīr* — ⭐ the "**ease**" family — ***yusr*** = **ease** (*"inna maʿa'l-ʿusri **yusrā**"*); ⭐ **you own it from ʿAbasa 80:20** (*thumma's-sabīla **yassarah*** — "the way — He **made it easy**").
- **Here it means:** "he will be **reckoned with an EASY reckoning**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The reward here is not "no reckoning" — it's an EASY one. That distinction is everything.**
  
  ⭐ **Even the best believer is still accounted for.** ⭐ **The file is still opened. The question is HOW.**
  
  ⭐ **And there's a well-known hadith the Prophet ﷺ related, that whoever is scrutinized in the reckoning is destroyed — with ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) asking about this very verse, and being told that this "easy reckoning" is the mere PRESENTATION of deeds, not the cross-examination.** ⭐ **The hadith names this exact verse.**
  
  ⭐ **So *ḥisāban yasīrā* means: shown, not interrogated.** ⭐ **A glance at the file, not a trial.**
  
  ⭐ **And the root-link to ʿAbasa 80:20 is lovely:** ⭐ **He *made the WAY easy* (*yassarah*) in life; He makes the *RECKONING easy* (*yasīrā*) after it.** ⭐ **Same root, both ends of the journey.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ḥisāban* is a cognate accusative*** again ("reckoned **a reckoning**") — **modified** by *yasīrā*. ⭐ **This is the cognate accusative used to describe HOW**, not just to emphasize. **Third construction of this type in the surah** (after *kadḥan*).
- **Why this word:** *yasīr* over "no reckoning" — because ⭐ **being seen is part of the honour**; the mercy is in the manner.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***yusr* = EASE.*** ⭐ **Not "no reckoning" — an EASY one: shown, not interrogated.** ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:20 made the WAY easy; here the RECKONING is easy.**

**8. يَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰ أَهْلِهِ مَسْرُورًا — *yanqalibu ilā ahlihi masrūrā*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *inqalaba* — ⭐ the "**turning over / returning**" family — ***qalb*** = **the HEART** (the thing that **turns**); *inqilāb* = **a coup**. *masrūr* — the "**joy**" family — ***surūr*** = **delight** — ⭐ **you own it from Insān 76:11** (*wa laqqāhum naḍratan wa **surūrā***).
- **Here it means:** "and he will **return to his family, rejoicing**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **This verse has an EXACT counterpart in the previous surah, and the pair is one of the finest things in this juzʾ.**
  
  ⭐ **Muṭaffifīn 83:31:** *wa idha'**nqalabū** ilā **ahlihimu**'nqalabū **fakihīn***
  ⭐ **"And when they RETURNED to their FAMILIES, they returned AMUSED"** — the mockers, laughing at believers over dinner.
  
  ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:9:** *wa **yanqalibu** ilā **ahlihi masrūrā***
  ⭐ **"And he will RETURN to his FAMILY, REJOICING"** — the man who passed.
  
  ⭐⭐ **SAME VERB. SAME PHRASE. Consecutive surahs. Opposite joys.** ⭐ **One returns home amused at someone else's expense; one returns home having been let through.**
  
  ⭐ **And then Inshiqāq 84:13 completes the trilogy:** *innahu **kāna fī ahlihi masrūrā*** — ⭐ **"he USED TO be, among his family, rejoicing"** — said of the **DOOMED** man, as **evidence against him.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Three verses, two surahs, one homecoming — and three completely different verdicts.** ⚠️ **This is the single most important cross-link in these five surahs.**
  
  ⭐ **And notice the detail: even in Paradise, the reward includes GOING HOME TO FAMILY.** ⭐ **Not solitude, not a throne — *ahlih*, his people.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form VII** (*inqalaba*) — the **ان-** shape, **fifth instance** you own. *Masrūrā* is a **ḥāl** (state): "returns **while** rejoicing."
- **Why this word:** *yanqalibu* (turning/returning) over "goes" — because ⭐ **the root means a REVERSAL**, and that's what the Day is.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***qalb* = HEART, the TURNER; *inqilāb* = a coup.*** ⭐⭐ **Muṭaffifīn 83:31 = same phrase, mockers returning AMUSED.** ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:9 = returning REJOICING (saved).** ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:13 = "he USED TO rejoice" (condemned).** ⭐ **One homecoming, three verdicts.**

**9. وَرَاءَ ظَهْرِهِ — *warāʾa ẓahrih*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *warāʾ* — **behind**; *ẓahr* — ⭐ **the BACK** — and the same root gives ***ẓāhir*** = **apparent, visible, outward** (what shows on the surface!) and ⭐ ***ẓuhr*** = **NOON prayer** (when the sun is most visible/prominent).
- **Here it means:** "But as for the one given his record **behind his back**…"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The image is deliberately awkward — try to picture it physically.**
  
  ⭐ **Receiving something behind your back means:**
  - ⭐ **You can't see it** — no chance to prepare
  - ⭐ **You must contort to take it** — an undignified, twisted posture
  - ⭐ **You're turned AWAY** — the body language of rejection
  
  ⭐ **Compare v.7: the right hand — open, forward, honourable.** ⭐ **Here: behind, hidden, twisted.** ⭐ **The two postures ARE the two verdicts.**
  
  ⭐ **And the root-irony:** ***ẓahr* (back) shares its root with *ẓāhir* (apparent/visible).*** ⭐ **The record is placed at the one part of the body you can never see** — from the root that means "**visible**." ⭐ **Everything is now apparent, except to him.**
  
  ⭐ **Also connect to Nāziʿāt 79:22:** *thumma **adbara** yasʿā* — Pharaoh "**turned his BACK** and hurried away." ⭐ **He turned his back in life; the record arrives at his back in the end.**
- **Grammar bite:** *warāʾa* is a **ẓarf makān** (adverb of place), accusative. ⭐ **Note the parallel structure with v.7:** *bi-yamīnih* (with his right hand) ↔ *warāʾa ẓahrih* (behind his back). **Matched phrases, opposite positions.**
- **Why this word:** naming the **body position** rather than the verdict makes the judgment **visible before it's stated**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***ẓahr* (back) shares a root with *ẓāhir* (VISIBLE) and *ẓuhr* (noon).*** ⭐ **The record goes to the one place he can't see.** ⭐ **v.7 right hand (open) ↔ v.10 behind the back (twisted).** ⭐ **Posture = verdict.**

**10. يَدْعُو ثُبُورًا — *yadʿū thubūrā*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *yadʿū* — the "**calling**" family (*duʿāʾ* = a supplication, a call). *thubūr* — ⭐ the "**utter ruin / destruction / perishing**" family — *thabara* = to be destroyed, to perish completely.
- **Here it means:** "he will **call out for DESTRUCTION**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The verb choice is the horror here.**
  
  ⭐ ***Yadʿū* is the DUʿĀʾ verb*** — the word for **supplication, calling on Allah, asking for what you want.** ⭐ **The verb of prayer.**
  
  ⭐ **And what does he pray FOR? *Thubūr* — his own annihilation.** ⭐ **The prayer-verb, used to beg for non-existence.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare Nabaʾ 78:40** — *yā laytanī kuntu **turābā*** ("**I wish I were dust!**"). ⭐ **Same desperate wish for non-being, two surahs apart.** ⭐ **Nabaʾ wishes it; Inshiqāq PRAYS for it.**
  
  ⭐ **And note the tragic logic: the only duʿāʾ he makes is one that cannot be answered.** ⭐ **He spent life not calling on Him; now he calls, and asks to stop existing.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***sawfa* (v.11) = "will" for the DISTANT future*** — and note it's used in **both** v.8 (*fa-sawfa yuḥāsabu*) and v.11 (*fa-sawfa yadʿū*). ⭐ **Matched openings for the two fates.** *Thubūrā* is accusative (the thing called for).
- **Why this word:** *yadʿū* over "he will scream" — because ⭐ **using the prayer-verb makes it a perverse supplication**, which is far darker.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***duʿāʾ* = supplication.*** ⭐ **He PRAYS — for his own destruction.** ⭐ **Compare Nabaʾ 78:40: "I wish I were dust."** ⭐ **The only duʿāʾ he makes is the one that can't be granted.**

**11. يَصْلَىٰ سَعِيرًا — *yaṣlā saʿīrā*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *yaṣlā* — ⭐ the "**roasting**" family — **THIRD surah in a row**: **Infiṭār 82:15** (*yaṣlawnahā*), **Muṭaffifīn 83:16** (*ṣālū'l-jaḥīm*), here. *saʿīr* — ⭐ the "**flaring blaze**" family — **you own it from Insān 76:4** (*aʿtadnā li'l-kāfirīna… **saʿīrā***) and **Takwīr 81:12** (*al-jaḥīmu **suʿʿirat***).
- **Here it means:** "and he will **burn in a Blaze**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐ **both words are ones you've already collected** — this verse is **free vocabulary** if you learned Insān and Infiṭār. ⭐ **Note *saʿīran* is INDEFINITE** ("**a** blaze") — leaving it unspecified and therefore more ominous.
- **Grammar bite:** weak-final verb (ص-ل-ي); note the singular *yaṣlā* here vs. the plural *yaṣlawna* in **Infiṭār 82:15** — ⭐ **watch how the weak final letter shifts between singular and plural.**
- **Why this word:** keeping the same roasting-verb across three surahs makes it a **fixed technical term** — learn once, own three.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐ **Third surah with *ṣaliya*: 82:15, 83:16, 84:12.** ⭐ **And *saʿīr* from Insān 76:4 + Takwīr 81:12.** **This whole verse is recycled vocabulary.**

**12. ظَنَّ أَن لَّن يَحُورَ — *ẓanna an lan yaḥūr*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *ẓanna* — ⭐ **you own its two senses**: usually "to suppose," but in **Qiyāmah 75:28** (*wa **ẓanna** annahu'l-firāq*) it meant **CERTAINTY**, and in **Muṭaffifīn 83:4** (*alā **yaẓunnu***) it meant **the faintest suspicion**. *yaḥūr* — ⭐ the "**returning**" family — *ḥāra* = to return, to come back to a starting point.
- **Here it means:** "Indeed, he **thought he would never RETURN**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **This is the diagnosis of the entire surah, and it links straight back to Muṭaffifīn.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare Muṭaffifīn 83:4:** *alā **yaẓunnu** ulāʾika annahum **mabʿūthūn*** — ⭐ **"Do those not THINK they will be RAISED?"**
  ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:14:** *innahu **ẓanna** an lan **yaḥūr*** — ⭐ **"Indeed, he THOUGHT he would never RETURN."**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Muṭaffifīn asked the question; Inshiqāq gives the answer.** ⭐ **Same verb (*ẓanna*), same subject (resurrection), consecutive surahs.** ⭐ **"Don't they think?" → "He thought he wouldn't."**
  
  ⭐ **And *yaḥūr* is beautifully specific: it means to RETURN to a point of origin.** ⭐ **Not "be resurrected" (a technical term) but "come back" (a homely one).** ⭐ **He assumed it was a one-way trip.**
  
  ⭐ **The whole surah's argument answers this:** v.6 said ⭐ **you are labouring TOWARD your Lord and will MEET Him.** ⭐ **He thought the road went nowhere. It goes to a meeting.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lan* + present = emphatic future negation*** ("will **never**") — ⭐ **you own it from Nabaʾ 78:30** (*fa-**lan** nazīdakum illā ʿadhābā*). *Yaḥūra* is **accusative** after *lan*. **Hollow verb** (weak middle و).
- **Why this word:** *yaḥūr* (a plain "come back") over a theological term — because ⭐ **his error was ordinary, not sophisticated.** He just assumed.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ **Muṭaffifīn 83:4 ASKED: "don't they think they'll be raised?"** ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:14 ANSWERS: "he thought he'd never come back."** ⭐ **Same verb, consecutive surahs — question and answer.**

**13. بَلَىٰ ... بَصِيرًا — *balā… baṣīrā*** · v.15
- **Root feelings:** *balā* — ⭐ **the emphatic "YES / on the contrary"** used specifically to **overturn a negative statement** — **you own it from Qiyāmah 75:4** (*balā qādirīna…*). *baṣīr* — the "**seeing**" family — ⭐ **you own it from Qiyāmah 75:14** (*bali'l-insānu ʿalā nafsihi **baṣīrah***).
- **Here it means:** "**Yes indeed!** His Lord was ever **Seeing of him**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐ ***Balā* is a specialist word:*** it exists **only** to **contradict a negative**. ⭐ **He said "I will NEVER return" — *balā* says "OH YES YOU WILL."** ⭐ **It's a one-word demolition of the previous verse.**
  
  ⭐ **And *kāna bihi baṣīrā* ("was ever Seeing of him") is the perfect answer to v.10.** ⭐ **He received his record BEHIND HIS BACK — the place he couldn't see.** ⭐ **But his Lord was seeing him the whole time.** ⭐ **The blindness was only ever on one side.**
  
  ⭐ **Cross-link to Qiyāmah 75:14:** there, ***man himself*** is *baṣīrah* — **a clear witness against himself.** ⭐ **Here, ***his Lord*** is *baṣīr* — **seeing him throughout.** ⭐ **Two witnesses, same root, and neither can be cross-examined.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kāna* + participle = a settled, ongoing attribute*** ("was ever / has always been") — **not** a past state that ended. ⭐ **Important nuance whenever you see *kāna* with a name/attribute of Allah.**
- **Why this word:** *baṣīr* over "knew" — because ⭐ **SEEING answers a verse about what he COULDN'T see** (v.10).
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐ ***balā* = "OH YES YOU WILL" — it only exists to flip a negative.*** ⭐ **He said "never"; *balā* says otherwise.** ⭐ **And he couldn't see his record (v.10) — but his Lord was *baṣīr* all along.** ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:14: HE is the witness. Here: HIS LORD is.**

## §3 — Three Oaths and the Climb (v.16–19)

**14. الشَّفَقِ — *ash-shafaq*** · v.16
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ **a gorgeous double root** — *shafaq* = ⭐ **the red-orange glow at twilight** (after sunset), *and* ***shafaqah*** = ⭐ **TENDER COMPASSION, tender concern** (*mushfiq* = **compassionate, anxious for someone's welfare**).
- **Here it means:** "**So I swear by the twilight glow.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The double meaning is one of the loveliest in the Qur'an.**
  
  ⭐ ***Shafaq* is BOTH the tender red light after sunset AND tender-hearted compassion.*** ⭐ **Why the same word?** Because ⭐ **twilight is the sky's SOFTEST light** — not the harsh sun, not full dark, but a **gentle, delicate, fading glow.** ⭐ **Compassion is the same quality in a person: soft, warm, and easily lost.**
  
  ⭐ **And you know *shafaq* practically:** ⭐ **it's the term used for the end-time of Maghrib and the start of ʿIshāʾ** — **the disappearance of the *shafaq*.** ⭐ **You've been tracking this word your whole prayer life.**
  
  ⭐ **Note the oath-set (v.16–18): TWILIGHT → NIGHT → FULL MOON.** ⭐ **All three are STAGES of the same evening** — and that's the setup for v.19's *"you will surely climb STAGE by STAGE."* ⭐ **The oaths demonstrate the point before it's made.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-lā uqsimu*** — **you own this construction from Qiyāmah 75:1 and Takwīr 81:15.** ⭐ **Third instance.** Same two readings (rebuttal-*lā* or emphatic-*lā*).
- **Why this word:** *shafaq* over "sunset" — because the **compassion** sense makes the oath feel **tender**, matching a surah that ends by offering mercy.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***shafaq* = TWILIGHT GLOW *and* TENDER COMPASSION.*** ⭐ **The sky's softest light = the heart's softest quality.** ⭐ **And it's the word marking the end of Maghrib time.**

**15. وَمَا وَسَقَ ... اتَّسَقَ — *wa mā wasaq… ittasaq*** · v.17–18
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**gathering / collecting into a whole**" family — *wasaqa* = **to gather together, to collect and pack**; ***wasq*** = **a load/measure** (a camel-load of collected goods). **Form VIII *ittasaqa* = to become gathered, complete, ordered.**
- **Here it means:** v.17 — "and by the night and **what it GATHERS**"; v.18 — "and by the moon when it **becomes FULL / gathered together**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The same root, two consecutive verses, two directions — this is a masterclass in Arabic root-play.**
  
  ⭐ **v.17: *wasaq* — the NIGHT gathers.*** ⭐ **Picture it: as darkness falls, everything comes IN.** Birds to nests, people to homes, animals to shelter, travelers off the road. ⭐ **Night is described as a COLLECTOR — it sweeps the world's scattered things into one place.**
  
  ⭐ **v.18: *ittasaq* — the MOON gathers ITSELF.*** ⭐ **Form VIII, reflexive:** the moon **assembles itself** night by night — crescent → half → gibbous → ⭐ **full: complete, gathered, whole.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **So: the night gathers THE WORLD; the moon gathers ITSELF.** ⭐ **Same root, one outward, one inward.**
  
  ⭐ **And THIS is the setup for v.19.** ⭐ **The moon doesn't jump to full — it climbs there in STAGES.** ⭐ **Which is exactly what the next verse says about you.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form VIII assimilation** — the original is *iwtasaqa*, and the **و assimilates into the ت** → ***ittasaqa***. ⭐ **Same assimilation family as *ittaqā → muttaqīn* (Nabaʾ 78:31) and *ittakhadha* (Insān 76:29).** ⭐ **Roots starting with و regularly do this in Form VIII.**
- **Why this word:** using **one root twice** makes v.17–18 feel like **one continuous thought**, and it **enacts** the gathering it describes.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***wasaqa* = TO GATHER.*** ⭐ **v.17: the NIGHT gathers the world in.** ⭐ **v.18: the MOON gathers itself full.** ⭐ **Outward, then inward — and both happen in STAGES, which is v.19.**

**16. لَتَرْكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقٍ — *la-tarkabunna ṭabaqan ʿan ṭabaq*** · v.19
- **Root feelings:** *tarkabunna* — ⭐ the "**mounting / riding / boarding**" family — *rakiba* = to ride, to mount; ⭐ **you own the related *rakkaba* (to ASSEMBLE) from Infiṭār 82:8.** *ṭabaq* — ⭐ the "**layer / stage / matching level**" family — ***ṭabaqah*** = **a layer, a floor of a building, a social class**; ***ṭibāq*** = layered upon one another (used of the seven heavens: *sabʿa samāwātin **ṭibāqā***).
- **Here it means:** "**You will surely mount stage upon stage / layer after layer.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The answer to the three oaths, and the most quietly profound verse in the surah.**
  
  ⭐ **The oaths were: twilight (a stage) → night (a stage) → full moon (the completion of stages).** ⭐ **Then: "you will surely climb stage by stage."** ⭐ **The evening sky was the illustration; you are the subject.**
  
  ⭐ **What are the *ṭabaqāt*?** Classical commentators offer several, and they layer beautifully:
  - ⭐ **Life stages** — drop → clot → infant → child → youth → old age → death (⭐ **exactly the sequence in ʿAbasa 80:18–22**)
  - ⭐ **States of ease and hardship** — one condition after another
  - ⭐ **The stages of the Hereafter** — death → grave → resurrection → gathering → reckoning
  
  ⭐ **All of them share the point: NOTHING IS PERMANENT.** ⭐ **You are always moving to the next layer.** ⭐ **Whatever stage you're in right now — it's a *ṭabaq*, and you're leaving it.**
  
  ⭐ **And *tarkabunna* is chosen with care:** ⭐ **"you will MOUNT/RIDE"** — the verb for **getting onto a moving animal or vehicle.** ⭐ **You don't walk through these stages; you're CARRIED through them.**
  
  ⭐ **This is v.6 restated:** ⭐ ***kādiḥun ilā rabbik*** ("labouring toward your Lord") — ⭐ **and here's HOW: one layer at a time.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***la-tarkabunna* = the EMPHATIC NŪN (nūn at-tawkīd).*** ⭐ **لَ + verb + نَّ = "you WILL MOST CERTAINLY."** ⭐ **This is one of Arabic's strongest oaths-of-affirmation — reserved for absolute certainties.** **Learn this shape; it always signals maximum emphasis.**
- **Why this word:** *ṭabaq* over "stage" — because ⭐ ***ṭabaqah* means a FLOOR of a building** — you can **feel** yourself ascending.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṭabaqah* = a FLOOR of a building / a layer.*** ⭐ **The moon climbed to full in stages (v.18); so do you.** ⭐ **Nothing you're in right now is permanent.** ⭐ **And *la-tarkabunna* uses the EMPHATIC NŪN — maximum certainty.**

## §4 — The Strange Silence (v.20–25)

**17. فَمَا لَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ — *fa-mā lahum lā yuʾminūn*** · v.20
- **Root feeling:** *āmana* — ⭐ the "**safety / trust / faith**" family — ***amn*** = **security**; ***amānah*** = a trust; ⭐ ***al-Amīn*** = **the Prophet's ﷺ title** — you own it from **Takwīr 81:21** (*muṭāʿin thamma **amīn***).
- **Here it means:** "**So what is [the matter] with them that they do not believe?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The tone here is genuine bewilderment, and that's what makes it land.**
  
  ⭐ **After: the sky obeys, the earth obeys, you're already walking toward Him, the moon climbs in stages, everything is ordered —** ⭐ **the surah just stops and asks: *"What's WRONG with them?"***
  
  ⭐ **It's not a threat. It's puzzlement.** ⭐ **Compare Takwīr 81:26** — *fa-**ayna** tadhhabūn* ("**so where are you going?**"). ⭐ **Same register: a bewildered question rather than a verdict.** ⭐ **Two surahs, both stopping mid-argument to ask, almost gently, what the problem is.**
  
  ⭐ **And note the root: *āmana* comes from SECURITY (*amn*).*** ⭐ **To believe is to find safety.** ⭐ **So the question is really: "what's stopping them from being safe?"**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mā lahum*** = an idiom: "**what is with them / what's the matter with them?**" — followed by a verb describing the puzzling behavior. **Very common Qur'anic construction.**
- **Why this word:** a **question** rather than a condemnation — ⭐ **it invites the listener to answer for himself.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***amn* = SECURITY; *al-Amīn* = the Prophet's ﷺ title.*** ⭐ **To BELIEVE = to be SAFE.** ⭐ **And compare Takwīr 81:26: "So where are you going?"** **Both surahs pause to ask, bewildered.**

**18. لَا يَسْجُدُونَ — *lā yasjudūn*** · v.21 · ⭐ **SAJDAH VERSE**
- **Root feeling:** the "**prostration**" family — *sajada* = to prostrate; ***sujūd*** = prostration; ***masjid*** = ⭐ **a MOSQUE** (literally "**the place of prostration**").
- **Here it means:** "And when the Qur'ān is recited to them, **they do not prostrate**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **This is one of the verses of prostration (*sajdat at-tilāwah*) — meaning that when you recite it, YOU prostrate.**
  
  ⭐ **Think about what that does.** ⭐ **The verse says "they do NOT prostrate" — and the reciter's response is TO PROSTRATE.** ⭐ **You answer the verse with your body.** ⭐ **The criticism becomes an invitation, and you accept it physically, in the moment of reading.**
  
  ⭐ **No other kind of verse works quite like this.** ⭐ **You're not told to prostrate; you're told about people who don't — and the natural response is to be the opposite of them.**
  
  ⭐ **And *masjid* means "place of prostration"** — ⭐ **so the whole institution of the mosque is named after this single action.**
- **Grammar bite:** *qurīʾa* is **passive** ("**is recited**"). Note *lā yasjudūn* — **present tense negation** = ⭐ **habitual: "they [just] don't prostrate."** The plainness is the point.
- **Why this word:** stating it as **a simple fact** ("they don't") rather than a rebuke makes the omission sound ⭐ **strange rather than sinful** — which fits v.20's bewilderment.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***masjid* = "the PLACE OF PROSTRATION."*** ⭐ **This is a SAJDAH verse: it says "they don't prostrate" — and you prostrate when you read it.** ⭐ **You answer the verse with your body.**

**19. بِمَا يُوعُونَ — *bimā yūʿūn*** · v.23
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**containing / storing in a vessel**" family — ***wiʿāʾ*** = **a container, a vessel, a bag**; *awʿā* = **to store up, to keep inside a container**; *waʿā* = to retain/comprehend (⭐ modern Arabic ***waʿy* = CONSCIOUSNESS/awareness** — what your mind "holds").
- **Here it means:** "And Allah is most knowing of **what they are storing up inside**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **A stunning image, and easy to walk past.**
  
  ⭐ ***Wiʿāʾ* is a CONTAINER.*** ⭐ **So the verse says: Allah knows what they're KEEPING IN THEIR CONTAINERS** — their chests, their hearts, the private stock of intentions and denials nobody sees.
  
  ⭐ **The picture: they're carrying sealed bags, and He knows the contents.**
  
  ⭐ **Now connect it to the surah's other container-language:**
  - ⭐ **v.4:** the earth ***casts out what is within it* and *empties itself***
  - ⭐ **v.23:** they are ***storing up*** what's inside them
  
  ⭐⭐ **The EARTH empties itself willingly. THEY keep filling themselves up.** ⭐ **Same surah, opposite behaviors — and the earth is the one that "listens to its Lord."**
  
  ⭐ **And there's a third link:** ⭐ **Muṭaffifīn 83:14** — *rāna ʿalā qulūbihim* ("**RUST** has covered their hearts"). ⭐ **Muṭaffifīn shows what's ON the heart; Inshiqāq shows what's IN it.**
- **Grammar bite:** **Form IV** (*awʿā*), present tense. *Bimā* = *bi* + *mā* ("of **what**"). ⭐ **Note *aʿlamu* here = "**most knowing**," an *ism tafḍīl* (superlative) form.
- **Why this word:** *yūʿūn* (storing in a vessel) over "hiding" — because ⭐ **a container is deliberate**; you don't accidentally store something.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***wiʿāʾ* = a CONTAINER; *waʿy* = CONSCIOUSNESS (what your mind holds).*** ⭐ **He knows what's in your sealed bag.** ⭐ **v.4: the earth EMPTIES itself. v.23: they keep FILLING themselves.**

**20. فَبَشِّرْهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ — *fa-bashshirhum bi-ʿadhābin alīm*** · v.24
- **Root feelings:** *bashshara* — ⭐ the "**good news**" family — ***bushrā*** = glad tidings; ***bashīr*** = a bearer of good news; ⭐ **and *bashar* = A HUMAN BEING**, from *bashrah* = **the SKIN** — because ⭐ **good news SHOWS on your face.** **You own this from ʿAbasa 80:39** (*ḍāḥikatun **mustabshirah***). *alīm* — the "**pain**" family — ⭐ **you own it from Insān 76:31** (*ʿadhāban **alīmā***).
- **Here it means:** "**So give them glad tidings of a painful punishment.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **One of the sharpest ironies in the Qur'an, and the vocabulary makes it bite.**
  
  ⭐ ***Bashshir* is the GOOD NEWS verb.*** It's what you say to someone about a birth, a victory, Paradise. ⭐ **And it's paired here with "a PAINFUL punishment."**
  
  ⭐ **The effect is *tahakkum* — mocking irony.** ⭐ **Same technique you own from:**
  - ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:30** — *fa-**lan nazīdakum** illā ʿadhābā* ("We will only **INCREASE** you in punishment" — gift-language for the opposite)
  - ⭐ **Muṭaffifīn 83:36** — *hal **thuwwiba**'l-kuffār* ("have the disbelievers been **REWARDED**?" — the *thawāb* word for punishment)
  
  ⭐⭐ **Three surahs, three times the Qur'an wraps punishment in the language of reward.** ⭐ **Collect them as a set.**
  
  ⭐ **And the root-detail makes it sharper:** ⭐ ***bashshara* comes from *bashrah*, the SKIN* — good news that **shows on the face.** ⭐ **In ʿAbasa 80:39, faces were *mustabshirah* — LIT UP with good news.** ⭐ **Here, the "good news" is the kind that would drain a face white.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form II imperative** (*bashshir*) with an attached pronoun. ⭐ **The *bāʾ* after *bashshara* is standard** — you always give news **bi-** something.
- **Why this word:** ⭐ **the irony does what a plain threat can't** — it makes the punishment sound **deserved and announced**, like a delivery they ordered.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***bashrah* = SKIN → *bushrā* = GOOD NEWS (it shows on your face) → *bashar* = a human.*** ⭐ **"Give them GOOD NEWS of a PAINFUL punishment."** ⭐ **Third instance of this irony: Nabaʾ 78:30, Muṭaffifīn 83:36, here.**

**21. أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ — *ajrun ghayru mamnūn*** · v.25
- **Root feelings:** *ajr* — **a wage, a reward for work**. *mamnūn* — ⭐ **a rich double root**: *manna* = ⭐ **to cut off / diminish** *and* ⭐ **to remind someone of a favour you did them** (***mann*** = a favour, but also **the obnoxious act of bringing it up**). Related: ***mannān*** = one who reproaches with gifts.
- **Here it means:** "…they will have **a reward UNCUT / UNBROKEN / never held over them**."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The double meaning makes this one of the most generous endings in the Qur'an.**
  
  ⭐ ***Ghayru mamnūn* means BOTH:***
  - ⭐ **(a) "NEVER CUT OFF"** — from *manna* = to diminish/sever. ⭐ **A reward that never runs out, never shrinks, never expires.**
  - ⭐ **(b) "NEVER HELD OVER YOU"** — from *mann* = reproaching someone with a favour. ⭐ **Nobody will ever say "remember what I gave you?"**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Think about how perfect (b) is.** ⭐ **The worst part of receiving a great gift from a person is often the reminders afterward.** ⭐ **The verse promises: infinite generosity, and **zero** reproach.** ⭐ **Given freely, and never mentioned again.**
  
  ⭐ **And it's the final word of the surah.** ⭐ **A surah that opened with the sky splitting closes on "a reward with no strings."**
  
  ⭐ **Compare the endings you've collected:**
  - **Takwīr 81:29** — closes on ***Rabb al-ʿālamīn*** (the Nurturer)
  - **Infiṭār 82:19** — closes on ***lillāh*** (it all belongs to Him)
  - ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:25** — closes on ***ghayru mamnūn*** (never cut, never reproached)
  
  ⭐ **Notice: after all the terror, each of these surahs ends on mercy or ownership, never on the fire.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ghayr* + genitive*** = the standard way to negate an adjective ("**non**-X, **un**-X"). *Mamnūn* is a **passive participle**. ⭐ **And note the *illā* (v.25) carving the believers OUT of the punishment announced in v.24** — a classic exception-clause rescue.
- **Why this word:** *mamnūn* over "endless" — because ⭐ **the second meaning (no reproach) adds a DIGNITY that "endless" alone can't.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***mann* = a favour — AND the act of rubbing it in.*** ⭐ **"A reward NEVER CUT OFF and NEVER HELD OVER YOU."** ⭐ **Infinite, and nobody ever mentions it again.** ⭐ **The last word of the surah.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **يَنقَلِبُ (84:9) / انقَلَبُوا (83:31) / كَانَ ... مَسْرُورًا (84:13)** — ⚠️ **Three verses, two surahs, ONE homecoming phrase, THREE verdicts.** **This is the most important cross-link in these five surahs.**
2. ⭐⭐ **The three sky-verbs**: *kushiṭat* (**flayed**, 81:11) / *infaṭarat* (**cleft**, 82:1) / *inshaqqat* (**split**, 84:1). ⚠️ **Learn as a set or you'll swap them.**
3. ⭐⭐ **وَسَقَ (v.17) and اتَّسَقَ (v.18)** — **same root, consecutive verses.** ⚠️ **Night gathers THE WORLD (outward); the moon gathers ITSELF (inward, Form VIII).**
4. ⭐⭐ **لَتَرْكَبُنَّ (v.19)** — the **EMPHATIC NŪN** (*nūn at-tawkīd*): **لَ + verb + نَّ** = maximum certainty. ⚠️ **Recognize this shape.**
5. ⭐⭐ **غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ (v.25)** — **BOTH** "never cut off" **AND** "never held over you." ⚠️ **Translating only the first loses half the mercy.**
6. ⭐ **الشَّفَقِ (v.16)** — the **twilight glow** *and* **tender compassion**. Also the **end of Maghrib time**.
7. ⭐ **v.21 is a SAJDAH verse.** ⚠️ **Prostrate when you recite it.** The verse says "they don't" — you do.
8. ⭐ **أَذِنَتْ (v.2, 5)** — from ***udhun* (EAR) / *adhān*.** ⚠️ **The sky LISTENS; it doesn't merely break.**
9. ⭐ **تَخَلَّتْ (v.4)** — **Form V reflexive**: the earth empties **ITSELF**, willingly. ⚠️ **Not "was emptied."**
10. ⭐ **حِسَابًا يَسِيرًا (v.8)** — ⚠️ **NOT "no reckoning" — an EASY one.** The file still opens.
