# Surah Al-Maʿārij (70) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–44) · **Complete coverage — every important word**

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It uses a rare word to diagnose human nature — and then DEFINES that word in the very next two verses.**

⭐ **v.19:** *inna'l-insāna khuliqa **halūʿā*** — "Man was created **HALŪʿ**."

⭐ What's *halūʿ*? Most readers wouldn't know. **So the Qurʾān tells you:**
- ⭐ **v.20:** *idhā massahu'sh-sharru **jazūʿā*** — "**when harm touches him — FRETFUL**,"
- ⭐ **v.21:** *wa idhā massahu'l-khayru **manūʿā*** — "**and when good touches him — WITHHOLDING**."

⭐⭐⭐ ***Halūʿ* = panicky when things go wrong, stingy when things go right.** ⭐ **The Qurʾān is its own dictionary** (*tafsīr al-Qurʾān bi'l-Qurʾān*). ⭐ **All three words are the same intensive shape — *faʿūl* — a disposition, not a mood.**

⭐⭐ **Then v.22: *illa'l-muṣallīn* — "EXCEPT those who PRAY"** — ⭐ **and eight traits follow, BRACKETED by prayer at both ends (v.23 and v.34).** ⭐ **The cure for anxious, grasping human nature is a life built inside prayer.**

⭐⭐⭐ **And recall Māʿūn 107:4–5:** ⭐ ***fa-waylun li'l-muṣallīn · alladhīna hum ʿan ṣalātihim sāhūn*** — **WOE to the praying who are heedless of their prayer.** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:22–23:** ⭐ ***illa'l-muṣallīn · alladhīna hum ʿalā ṣalātihim dāʾimūn*** — **EXCEPT the praying who are CONSTANT in their prayer.** ⭐⭐ **Same word *muṣallīn*: one group cursed, one group exempted — and the difference is *sāhūn* (heedless) vs *dāʾimūn* (constant).**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HALŪʿ* = *JAZŪʿ* (panics in loss) + *MANŪʿ* (hoards in gain).** ⭐ **The cure: *dāʾimūn* (constant) in prayer — the opposite of Māʿūn's *sāhūn*.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The mocking request** (v.1–4) — someone asked for the punishment; it's coming from the Lord of the Ascents.
2. **Beautiful patience** (v.5–7) — they see it far; We see it near.
3. **That Day** (v.8–14) — sky like molten metal, mountains like wool; no friend asks of a friend; a man would ransom his whole family.
4. **Laẓā** (v.15–18) — the flame that strips the scalp and calls those who turned away and hoarded.
5. **Human nature** (v.19–21) — *halūʿ*: fretful and withholding.
6. **The exceptions** (v.22–35) — eight traits of those who pray.
7. **The scattered deniers** (v.36–41) — why do they rush around you in clusters? Each wants Paradise…
8. **Leave them** (v.42–44) — let them wade and play until their Day.

## §1 — The Mocking Request (v.1–4)

**1. سَأَلَ سَائِلٌ بِعَذَابٍ وَاقِعٍ — *saʾala sāʾilun bi-ʿadhābin wāqiʿ*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *saʾala* — ⭐ the "**ASKING**" family — *suʾāl* = a question; ⭐ ***sāʾil* = one who asks / a BEGGAR** (⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:10** — *wa ammā **as-sāʾila** fa-lā tanhar*!); ⭐ ***masʾūl* = RESPONSIBLE** (one who will be **asked**!). *wāqiʿ* — ⭐ the "**FALLING / happening**" family — ⭐ ***al-Wāqiʿah*** (**Ḥāqqah 69:15**!); ⭐ ***wāqiʿ* = REALITY**.
- **Here it means:** "**A questioner asked for a punishment bound to fall…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A MOCKING request — answered with the very word for certainty.** ⭐ Classical: **an-Naḍr ibn al-Ḥārith (among others) said: *"O Allah, if this is the truth from You, rain stones on us from the sky!"*** (8:32) — ⭐ **demanding the punishment as a dare.** ⭐⭐ ***Bi-ʿadhābin WĀQIʿ*** — ⭐ **"a punishment that WILL FALL"** — ⭐ **he asked mockingly; the Qurʾān answers: it is *wāqiʿ* — REAL, certain, already falling.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the previous surah, Ḥāqqah 69:15** — *fa-yawmaʾidhin **waqaʿati'l-wāqiʿah*** — ⭐ **the same root in back-to-back surahs: the Event FELL (69:15); the punishment WILL FALL (70:1).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Saʾala sāʾil*** — a cognate pair: **"an asker asked"** — ⭐ **unnamed, contemptuous.** ⭐ **And the *sāʾil* returns in v.25** — *li's-**sāʾili** wa'l-maḥrūm* — ⭐ **the BEGGAR who asks for charity.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same word: v.1 a man ASKS for punishment; v.25 a poor man ASKS for food.** ⭐ **One request mocks, the other is a right.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***saʾala bi-*** = "**asked FOR / called for**" (the *bi-* conveys "calling for"). ⭐ ***sāʾilun*** = indefinite (unnamed, contempt). ⭐ ***wāqiʿin*** = adjective.
- **Why this exact word:** *wāqiʿ* — ⭐ **answers mockery with certainty in one adjective.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A MOCKER asked for punishment ("rain stones on us!" 8:32) → it is *WĀQIʿ* — certain to FALL.** ⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:15 *waqaʿati'l-wāqiʿah* → Maʿārij 70:1 *ʿadhābin wāqiʿ* — back-to-back.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SĀʾIL*: v.1 asks for PUNISHMENT; v.25 asks for FOOD.** ⭐ ***Masʾūl* = RESPONSIBLE; *al-wāqiʿ* = REALITY.**

**2. لِّلْكَافِرِينَ لَيْسَ لَهُ دَافِعٌ — *li'l-kāfirīna laysa lahu dāfiʿ*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**PUSHING AWAY / repelling**" family — *dafaʿa* = **to push, to repel, to PAY**; ⭐⭐ ***difāʿ* = DEFENCE** (⭐ *wizārat ad-difāʿ* = the **Ministry of Defence**!); ⭐ ***dafʿ* = PAYMENT** (⭐ *dafʿ ilikitrūnī* = electronic **payment**!); ⭐ ***madfaʿ* = a CANNON**; ⭐ ***dāfiʿ* = a repeller / motive** (⭐ *dāfiʿ* = **a MOTIVE / incentive**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…upon the disbelievers — none can repel it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Laysa lahu DĀFIʿ* — "it has NO REPELLER"** — ⭐ **no defence, no army, no shield, no payment can push it back.** ⭐⭐ ***Difāʿ* = DEFENCE** — ⭐ **every "Ministry of Defence" on earth combined couldn't repel it.** ⭐⭐ **Compare Ṭāriq 86:10** — *fa-mā lahu min quwwatin wa lā nāṣir* (no strength, no helper) ⭐ **and Ḥāqqah 69:47** — *fa-mā minkum min aḥadin ʿanhu ḥājizīn* (no one can BLOCK it). ⭐ **No *nāṣir* (helper), no *ḥājiz* (barrier), no *dāfiʿ* (repeller).**
  ⭐ ⭐ **And *dafʿ* = PAYMENT** — ⭐ **you can't PAY your way out of it** (⭐ **v.11–14: he'd ransom his whole family — rejected**).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***laysa lahu dāfiʿun*** — fronted *lahu* ("**for it** — no repeller"). ⭐ ***li'l-kāfirīna*** = "**for / upon** the disbelievers."
- **Why this exact word:** *dāfiʿ* — ⭐ **names the futility of every defence, army or payment.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Laysa lahu DĀFIʿ* = NO REPELLER — no DEFENCE (*difāʿ*), no PAYMENT (*dafʿ*).** ⭐⭐ **No *nāṣir* (86:10), no *ḥājiz* (69:47), no *dāfiʿ* (70:2).** ⭐ ***Madfaʿ* = a CANNON; *dāfiʿ* = a MOTIVE.**

**3. مِّنَ اللَّهِ ذِي الْمَعَارِجِ — *mina'llāhi dhi'l-maʿārij*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐⭐ the "**ASCENDING / climbing / stairways**" family — *ʿaraja* = **to ascend, to climb**; ⭐ ***maʿārij* = ASCENTS, STAIRWAYS, ladders** (plural of *miʿraj*); ⭐⭐⭐ ***al-MIʿRĀJ* = the Prophet's ﷺ ASCENSION** through the heavens!; ⭐ *ʿurūj* = ascent; ⭐ and — surprisingly — ***aʿraj* = LAME / limping** (one who goes **up and down** in his step!).
- **Here it means:** "**…from Allah, Lord of the Ascending Stairways.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SURAH'S NAME — and the root of *AL-MIʿRĀJ*.** ⭐ ***Dhu'l-maʿārij*** = **the Owner of the STAIRWAYS** — ⭐ classical: **the heavens rising in levels, which the angels ascend** (v.4). ⭐⭐⭐ ***Al-MIʿRĀJ*** — **the Night of Ascension**, when the Prophet ﷺ was taken up through the heavens — ⭐ **the same root: he ascended the *maʿārij* of the One who owns them.** ⭐⭐ **The mocker in v.1 demands punishment from below; the punishment comes from the One whose stairways reach the highest heaven.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Dhu*** — ⭐ **one of the FIVE SPECIAL NOUNS** (*dhū / dhā / dhī*) — ⭐ ***dhi'l-maʿārij*** (genitive) — ⭐ **like *dhi'l-ʿarsh* (Takwīr 81:20), *dhū'l-ʿarsh* (Burūj 85:15).**
  ⭐ Odd hook: ⭐ ***aʿraj* = LAME** — **the limp goes "up and down."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***dhī*** = genitive five-noun, describing *Allāh*. ⚠️ ⭐ ***maʿārij*** = **DIPTOTE** (*mafāʿil* pattern) — here genitive with kasrah because it has *al-*.
- **Why this exact word:** *maʿārij* — ⭐ **lifts the reader's eyes upward, toward the source of the punishment and of the angels' ascent.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAʿĀRIJ* = STAIRWAYS — the root of *AL-MIʿRĀJ* (the Night Ascension).** ⭐ ***Dhu'l-maʿārij* = Lord of the Stairways.** ⭐⭐ ***Dhī* = five-noun (81:20, 85:15).** ⭐ ***Aʿraj* = LAME (up and down).** ⚠️ **Diptote.**

**4. تَعْرُجُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ إِلَيْهِ — *taʿruju'l-malāʾikatu wa'r-rūḥu ilayh*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *taʿruju* — **ASCEND** (*ʿaraja*). *al-malāʾikah* — **the ANGELS**. *ar-Rūḥ* — ⭐ **the SPIRIT** (Jibrīl, per most) — ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:38, Qadr 97:4**.
- **Here it means:** "**The angels and the Spirit ascend to Him…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MIRROR OF SŪRAT AL-QADR — descent vs ascent.** ⭐ **Qadr 97:4** — ***tanazzalu'l-malāʾikatu wa'r-rūḥu*** fīhā — ⭐ "**the angels and the Spirit DESCEND**" (on the Night of Decree). ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:4** — ***taʿruju'l-malāʾikatu wa'r-rūḥu*** ilayh — ⭐ "**the angels and the Spirit ASCEND**" (to Him). ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME SUBJECTS, SAME ORDER, opposite directions: *tanazzalu* (DOWN) vs *taʿruju* (UP).** ⭐ **The stairways (*maʿārij*) go both ways.**
  ⭐⭐ **And *ar-Rūḥ* named separately again** (⭐ ***ʿaṭf al-khāṣṣ ʿala'l-ʿāmm*** — joining the special to the general for honour) — ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:38** (standing silent in ranks), **Qadr 97:4** (descending), **Maʿārij 70:4** (ascending). ⭐ **Three appearances of the Rūḥ: silent, descending, ascending.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***taʿruju*** = **feminine present** (for the plural *malāʾikah*). ⭐ ***ilayhi*** = "**to Him**."
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿaraja* — ⭐ **completes the picture of the stairways: traffic upward to the Throne.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Qadr 97:4: *TANAZZALU'l-malāʾikatu wa'r-rūḥ* (DESCEND). Maʿārij 70:4: *TAʿRUJU'l-malāʾikatu wa'r-rūḥ* (ASCEND).** ⭐ **Same subjects, opposite directions.** ⭐⭐ **The Rūḥ: silent (78:38), descending (97:4), ascending (70:4).**

**5. فِي يَوْمٍ كَانَ مِقْدَارُهُ خَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ — *fī yawmin kāna miqdāruhu khamsīna alfa sanah*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *miqdār* — ⭐ the "**MEASURE / amount**" family — ***qadr*** (⭐ **Qadr 97:1**, **Muzzammil 73:20** *yuqaddiru*); ⭐ ***miqdār* = an AMOUNT, a measure, a quantity** (everyday!). *khamsīn* — **FIFTY** (⭐ *khamsah* = five; ⭐ *al-khamāsīn* = the **hot desert wind** of ~50 days!). *alf* — **THOUSAND** (⭐ **Qadr 97:3** *alf shahr*). *sanah* — **a YEAR**.
- **Here it means:** "**…in a Day whose measure is fifty thousand years.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FIFTY THOUSAND YEARS — and the *qadr* root is the measuring word.** ⭐ Classical readings: ⭐ **(a) the length of Judgment Day itself** — ⭐ **long for the disbeliever** — ⭐ (a ḥadīth: **for the believer it will feel shorter than the time of an obligatory prayer**); ⭐ **(b) the distance of the angels' ascent** — what would take humans 50,000 years, they cover in a day. ⭐⭐ ***Miqdār* (measure) — the *qadr* root:** ⭐ **Qadr 97:3 — one night *khayrun min ALFI shahr* (better than a THOUSAND months).** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:4 — one day = FIFTY THOUSAND years.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Two "measurings" with *alf*: a night worth a lifetime; a Day lasting fifty millennia.** ⭐ **Time is His to stretch and compress.**
  ⭐ **And Muzzammil 73:20** — *wa'llāhu **yuqaddiru**'l-layla wa'n-nahār* — ⭐ **He measures night and day.** ⭐ **Here He measures a Day of 50,000 years.**
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***miqdār* = an AMOUNT**; ⭐ ***al-khamāsīn*** = the 50-day desert wind.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***khamsīna*** = accusative (*khabar* of *kāna*); ***alfa*** = *iḍāfah*; ⚠️ ***sanatin*** = **singular genitive after *alf*** (numbers 100/1000 take a **singular** counted noun!).
- **Why this exact word:** *miqdār* — ⭐ **ties the vastness of the Day to the root of divine measuring.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A Day of FIFTY THOUSAND years** — ⭐ **(ḥadīth: shorter than one prayer for the believer).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Qadr 97:3: a NIGHT > a THOUSAND months. Maʿārij 70:4: a DAY = FIFTY THOUSAND years.** ⭐ **Time is His to compress and stretch.** ⭐ ***Miqdār* = an AMOUNT** (*qadr* root). ⚠️ ***Alf* + SINGULAR noun.**

## §2 — Beautiful Patience (v.5–7)

**6. فَاصْبِرْ صَبْرًا جَمِيلًا — *fa'ṣbir ṣabran jamīlā*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *ṣabr* — ⭐ **RESTRAINT / patience** (⭐ *ṣabir* = ALOES — bitter, healing). *jamīl* — ⭐ **BEAUTIFUL** (*jamāl*).
- **Here it means:** "**So be patient with a beautiful patience.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *JAMĪL* TRIAD — and this is the second of the three.** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:10** — *wa'hjurhum **hajran jamīlā*** — **BEAUTIFUL LEAVING.** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:5** — *fa'ṣbir **ṣabran jamīlā*** — **BEAUTIFUL PATIENCE.** ⭐ **(Ḥijr 15:85** — *fa'ṣfaḥi'**ṣ-ṣafḥa'l-jamīl*** — **BEAUTIFUL FORGIVENESS.)** ⭐⭐⭐ **Three beautiful responses to being wronged: leave, endure, forgive — all *jamīl*.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ṣabr jamīl* — the definition (classical): ⭐ patience WITHOUT COMPLAINT to anyone but Allah.*** ⭐ **Yaʿqūb (peace be upon him), losing Yūsuf: *fa-ṣabrun jamīl*** (12:18, 12:83) — ⭐ **and he said *innamā ashkū baththī wa ḥuznī ila'llāh*** — "**I complain of my grief ONLY to Allah**" (12:86). ⭐ **Crying to Allah is allowed; complaining about Him to people is not.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the *fa* = "SO"** — ⭐ **because the punishment is certain (v.1) and none can repel it (v.2) — SO be patient** with the mockers. ⭐ **Justice is coming; you don't need to force it.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ imperative + **cognate accusative with adjective** (*ṣabran jamīlan*) — **the adjective specifies the MANNER.**
- **Why this exact word:** *jamīl* — ⭐ **because the quality of patience matters, not just its duration.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *JAMĪL* TRIAD: *hajr jamīl* (beautiful LEAVING, 73:10), *ṣabr jamīl* (beautiful PATIENCE, 70:5), *ṣafḥ jamīl* (beautiful FORGIVENESS, 15:85).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ṣabr jamīl* = NO COMPLAINT except to Allah — Yaʿqūb: "I complain ONLY to Allah" (12:86).** ⭐ **SO be patient — justice is coming.**

**7. إِنَّهُمْ يَرَوْنَهُ بَعِيدًا · وَنَرَاهُ قَرِيبًا — *innahum yarawnahu baʿīdā · wa narāhu qarībā*** · v.6–7
- **Root feelings:** *yarawn / narā* — **SEE** (*raʾā*). *baʿīd* — ⭐ **FAR** (*buʿd* = distance; ⭐ *baʿīdan ʿan* = far from). *qarīb* — ⭐ **NEAR** (*qurb*; ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:19** *iqtarib*).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, they see it as far off, but We see it as near.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Two "seeings," two distances — a perfect *ṭibāq*.** ⭐ **THEY see it FAR; WE see it NEAR.** ⭐ **Same verb (*raʾā*), opposite adjectives.** ⭐⭐ **Classical: "far" in their eyes = **impossible, unlikely**; "near" in His = **certain, imminent**.** ⭐ **Because what is certain IS near** — ⭐ **everything coming is close** (*kullu ātin qarīb*).
  ⭐⭐ **And the Fifty-Thousand-Year Day (v.4)** — ⭐ **seems impossibly far — yet "We see it near."** ⭐ **Their "far" is a measure of their doubt, not of time.**
  ⭐⭐ **Compare Jinn 72:25** — *qul in adrī **a-qarībun** mā tūʿadūna am yajʿalu lahu rabbī amadā* — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ: "I don't know if it's NEAR or far."** ⭐ **Maʿārij: Allah says it IS near.** ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ doesn't know the date; Allah knows it's near.**
  ⭐ ⭐ **The "seeing" thread across your surahs** (*yarā* — Balad 90:7, ʿAlaq 96:14, Zalzalah 99:7, Takāthur 102:6) — ⭐ **here: two ways of seeing the SAME thing.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yarawnahu*** / ***narāhu*** — *raʾā* in the sense of **judging / considering** (a verb of the heart, taking two objects). ⭐ ***baʿīdan / qarīban*** = second objects.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the same verb with opposite adjectives — so the gap is in the viewer, not the event.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THEY see it FAR; WE see it NEAR — same verb, opposite distances.** ⭐ **Their "far" = their DOUBT, not the calendar.** ⭐⭐ **Jinn 72:25: the Prophet ﷺ — "I don't know if it's NEAR." Here: Allah — "it IS near."** ⭐ **Everything coming is close.**

## §3 — That Day (v.8–14)

**8. يَوْمَ تَكُونُ السَّمَاءُ كَالْمُهْلِ — *yawma takūnu's-samāʾu ka'l-muhl*** · v.8
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ ***muhl*** — classically **two meanings**: **(a) the DREGS of oil** (the dark, turbid sludge at the bottom of an oil jar); **(b) MOLTEN METAL** (copper, brass, silver — melted and bubbling). ⭐ The letters *m-h-l* also give ***mahl* = slowness** (⭐ **Muzzammil 73:11** *mahhilhum* — "give them RESPITE"; ⭐ ***mahlan!*** = "slow down!").
- **Here it means:** "**The Day the sky will be like molten metal [or the dregs of oil].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The SKY — the bright, clear canopy — becomes ⭐ MOLTEN BRASS, or the black sludge at the bottom of an oil jar.** ⭐ **Both images: liquid, dark, burning, unstable.** ⭐⭐ **Compare Ḥāqqah 69:16** — *fa-hiya yawmaʾidhin **wāhiyah*** (the sky **FRAIL**) ⭐ **and Raḥmān 55:37** — *fa-kānat wardatan **ka'd-dihān*** (red like **oil-dregs**). ⭐⭐ **The sky-fates thread:** ⭐ **split (*infaṭarat*, 82:1), cracked (*inshaqqat*, 84:1), stripped (*kushiṭat*, 81:11), frail (*wāhiyah*, 69:16), ⭐ MOLTEN (*ka'l-muhl*, 70:8).**
  ⭐⭐ **And the letters *m-h-l*:** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:11 — *wa MAHHILHUM qalīlā*** — "**give them RESPITE a little.**" ⭐⭐⭐ **The respite (*mahl*) ends; the sky becomes *muhl*.** ⭐ *(Same letters; classical lexicons list both under m-h-l.)*
  ⭐ **And Kahf 18:29** — *bi-māʾin **ka'l-muhli** yashwi'l-wujūh* — ⭐ **Hell's water "like *muhl*," that GRILLS faces** — ⭐ (and *yashwī* = grill → v.16's *shawā*!).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yawma*** = adverb of time (linked to v.6–7 or v.1). ⭐ ***ka-*** = simile.
- **Why this exact word:** *muhl* — ⭐ **liquefies the sky — the most stable-looking thing made molten.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUHL* = MOLTEN METAL or OIL-DREGS — the SKY melts.** ⭐⭐ **Sky-fates: split (82:1), cracked (84:1), stripped (81:11), frail (69:16), MOLTEN (70:8).** ⭐⭐ **Muzzammil 73:11: *mahhilhum* (RESPITE) — same letters: the respite ends, the sky melts.** ⭐ **Kahf 18:29: water like *muhl* that GRILLS faces.**

**9. وَتَكُونُ الْجِبَالُ كَالْعِهْنِ — *wa takūnu'l-jibālu ka'l-ʿihn*** · v.9
- **Root feeling:** ***ʿihn*** — ⭐ **DYED, coloured WOOL**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and the mountains will be like [tufts of dyed] wool.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NEARLY IDENTICAL to Qāriʿah 101:5.** ⭐ **Qāriʿah 101:5** — *wa takūnu'l-jibālu **ka'l-ʿihni'l-manfūsh*** (like **CARDED** wool). ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:9** — *wa takūnu'l-jibālu **ka'l-ʿihn*** (like wool). ⭐⭐ **Same opening, same simile — Qāriʿah adds *manfūsh* (carded, fluffed).** ⭐ **And *ʿihn* = DYED wool** — ⭐ classical: **mountains of different colours (white, red, black strata) → like tufts of multicoloured wool blowing loose.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the PAIR in v.8–9: SKY like molten metal, MOUNTAINS like wool** — ⭐ **the HEAVIEST things made liquid and light.** ⭐ **Mountain-fates — now EIGHT images:** mirage (78:20), moving (81:3), pounded (89:21), carded wool (101:5), sliding sand (73:14), crushed once (69:14), ⭐ **dyed wool (70:9)**, vs. pegs (78:7) and set up (88:19).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ parallel to v.8 (*wa takūnu… ka-…*).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **echoes Qāriʿah so the reader hears the same Day in both surahs.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= Qāriʿah 101:5 (minus *manfūsh*).** ⭐ ***ʿIhn* = DYED wool — multicoloured strata blowing loose.** ⭐⭐ **v.8 SKY → molten; v.9 MOUNTAINS → wool.** ⭐ **Mountain-fates: EIGHT images.**

**10. وَلَا يَسْأَلُ حَمِيمٌ حَمِيمًا — *wa lā yasʾalu ḥamīmun ḥamīmā*** · v.10
- **Root feeling:** ***ḥamīm*** — ⭐⭐⭐ **a CLOSE, WARM FRIEND** (one whose love is "hot") — ⭐ **and SCALDING WATER** — root ***ḥ-m-m* = HEAT** (⭐ *ḥammām* = bathhouse; ⭐ *ḥummā* = fever).
- **Here it means:** "**And no close friend will ask about a close friend.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *ḤAMĪM* THREAD — now in THREE consecutive appearances.**
  ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:25** — *illā **ḥamīman** wa ghassāqā* — ⭐ **SCALDING WATER.**
  ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:35** — *fa-laysa lahu'l-yawma hāhunā **ḥamīm*** — ⭐ **NO FRIEND** (for the man who didn't feed the poor).
  ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:10** — *wa lā yasʾalu **ḥamīmun ḥamīmā*** — ⭐ **no FRIEND asks about a FRIEND** (for EVERYONE).
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The word is DOUBLED here: *ḥamīmun ḥamīmā*** — ⭐ **friend-friend: two best friends, side by side** — ⭐ **and NEITHER asks "how are you?"** ⭐ **Each is too consumed by his own fate.** ⭐⭐ **Compare ʿAbasa 80:37** — *li-kulli'mriʾin minhum yawmaʾidhin **shaʾnun yughnīh*** — ⭐ **"every person will have an affair occupying him."**
  ⭐⭐ **And the next verse (v.11): *yubaṣṣarūnahum*** — ⭐ **they will SEE each other** — ⭐ **they're not separated; they're right there, face to face — and still don't ask.** ⭐ **That's what makes it unbearable.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ḥamīmun*** (subject) + ***ḥamīman*** (object) — ⭐ **the same word in two cases: one friend, then the other** (*jinās tāmm*).
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥamīm* — ⭐ **the warmest word for friendship, doubled, then frozen into silence.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤAMĪM* = WARM FRIEND / SCALDING WATER.** ⭐⭐⭐ **78:25 SCALDING water → 69:35 NO friend → 70:10 FRIEND doesn't ask FRIEND.** ⭐⭐ ***Ḥamīmun ḥamīmā* — two best friends, side by side, SILENT.** ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:37: each has his own concern.** ⭐ **v.11: they SEE each other — and still don't ask.**

**11. يُبَصَّرُونَهُمْ — *yubaṣṣarūnahum*** · v.11
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**SEEING clearly / insight**" family — *baṣura*; ⭐ **Form II passive *buṣṣira* = to BE MADE TO SEE, to be shown** (⭐ *tabṣirah* = enlightenment / a clear lesson; ⭐ *baṣīrah* = insight).
- **Here it means:** "**They will be shown them [made to see each other].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ONE WORD — and it makes v.10 devastating.** ⭐ ***Yubaṣṣarūnahum*** = "**they will be MADE TO SEE them**" — ⭐ **friends, family — right there, in plain view.** ⭐ **Not hidden, not lost in a crowd — VISIBLE.** ⭐⭐ **So the silence of v.10 isn't because they can't find each other — ⭐ it's because each is drowning in his own terror.**
  ⭐⭐ **The *baṣar* root:** ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:14** (*baṣīrah* — witness against oneself), **Ḥāqqah 69:38** (*bimā tubṣirūn* — by what you SEE). ⭐ **Here: forced to see — and unable to help.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ **Form II PASSIVE** + *-hum* (them) — ⭐ **"they are shown them."** ⭐ **A rare, compact form — a whole clause in one word.**
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **visibility makes the silence intentional — a choice forced by fear.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUBAṢṢARŪNAHUM* = they are MADE TO SEE each other — face to face.** ⭐ **The silence (v.10) isn't distance — it's TERROR.** ⭐ **A whole clause in one word.** ⭐ ***Tabṣirah* = a clear LESSON.**

**12. يَوَدُّ الْمُجْرِمُ لَوْ يَفْتَدِي مِنْ عَذَابِ يَوْمِئِذٍ — *yawaddu'l-mujrimu law yaftadī min ʿadhābi yawmiʾidh*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *yawaddu* — ⭐⭐ the "**LOVE / longing / wishing**" family — *wadda* = **to love, to wish dearly**; ⭐ ***mawaddah* = AFFECTION** (⭐ *mawaddah wa raḥmah* — between spouses, 30:21!); ⭐ ***al-Wadūd* = the Loving (a Name of Allah — Burūj 85:14!)**; ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:23 — *Wadd*, an idol named "Love"!** *mujrim* — ⭐ **a CRIMINAL** (*jarīmah* = a crime; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:41**). *yaftadī* — ⭐ the "**RANSOM**" family — ***fidāʾ / fidyah* = a RANSOM, compensation** (⭐ ***fidyah*** for missed fasts!; ⭐ *fidāʾī* = one who **sacrifices himself**; ⭐ ***fadaytuka!*** = "may I be your ransom!").
- **Here it means:** "**The criminal will wish he could ransom himself from the punishment of that Day…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Yawaddu* — he will LONG, dearly** — ⭐ **the root of LOVE (*mawaddah*).** ⭐ **The only thing he'll love that Day: escape.** ⭐⭐ **And *al-Wadūd* (the Loving — Burūj 85:14)** — ⭐ **the One whose love he ignored; now his *wadd* is for a ransom.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Yaftadī* — RANSOM** — ⭐ **the Arab practice of paying to free a captive** — ⭐ **and in fiqh, *fidyah*** (feeding the poor for fasts you couldn't make up). ⭐⭐ **And recall Muddaththir 74:38** — *kullu nafsin bimā kasabat **rahīnah*** — ⭐ **every soul is HELD as COLLATERAL.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The collateral (74:38) tries to pay a RANSOM (70:11) — and the ransom he offers is his own FAMILY (v.11–14).**
  ⭐ ***Law*** = "**IF ONLY**" (a wish, contrary to fact).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yawaddu*** + ***law*** = "**wishes that / if only**." ⭐ ***yaftadī*** = **Form VIII** (ransom **oneself**). ⭐ ***yawmiʾidhin*** — ⚠️ **two recitations: *yawmiʾidhin* (genitive) and *yawmaʾidhin* (built on fatḥah)** — both authentic.
- **Why this exact word:** *fidāʾ* — ⭐ **a transaction word: he wants to BUY his way out — with people.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAWADDU* ← *MAWADDAH* (LOVE) — his only love that Day: escape.** ⭐ ***Al-Wadūd* (85:14).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FIDYAH* = a RANSOM (and the fasting compensation!).** ⭐ ***Fadaytuka!* = "may I be your ransom!"** ⭐⭐ **Muddaththir 74:38: HELD as COLLATERAL → tries to pay a RANSOM.**

**13. بِبَنِيهِ · وَصَاحِبَتِهِ وَأَخِيهِ · وَفَصِيلَتِهِ الَّتِي تُؤْوِيهِ · وَمَن فِي الْأَرْضِ جَمِيعًا ثُمَّ يُنجِيهِ — *bi-banīh · wa ṣāḥibatihi wa akhīh · wa faṣīlatihi'llatī tuʾwīh · wa man fi'l-arḍi jamīʿan thumma yunjīh*** · v.11–14
- **Root feelings:** *banīn* — **SONS**. *ṣāḥibah* — **WIFE / companion**. *akh* — **BROTHER**. *faṣīlah* — ⭐⭐ the "**SEPARATING / dividing into sections**" family — *faṣala* = to separate; ⭐ ***faṣīlah* = the CLAN, the close kin-group** (a "section" of the tribe); ⭐ ***yawm al-faṣl* = the Day of SORTING** (**Nabaʾ 78:17**!); ⭐ ***faṣl* = a CHAPTER / a SEASON / a CLASSROOM**!; ⭐ *faṣīlah dammawiyyah* = **a BLOOD GROUP / blood type** (clinical!). *tuʾwīh* — ⭐ the "**SHELTERING / giving refuge**" family — *awā* = to take shelter; ⭐ ***maʾwā* = a REFUGE** (⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:39, 41**!); ⭐ *īwāʾ* = **SHELTER / housing** (*markaz īwāʾ* = a **shelter**!). *yunjīh* — ⭐ the "**RESCUE**" family — *najā* = to be saved; ⭐ ***najāt* = SALVATION**; ⭐ *munjī* = a rescuer.
- **Here it means:** "**…[ransom himself] with his sons, his wife, his brother, his clan that sheltered him, and everyone on earth — if only it would then save him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE RANSOM LIST — and it's the EXACT REVERSE of ʿAbasa.**
  ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:34–36** — *yawma yafirru'l-marʾu **min akhīh · wa ummihi wa abīh · wa ṣāḥibatihi wa banīh*** — ⭐ **he FLEES: brother → mother → father → wife → SONS** (ending with the **dearest**).
  ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:11–14** — *yaftadī **bi-banīh · wa ṣāḥibatihi wa akhīh · wa faṣīlatih… wa man fi'l-arḍi jamīʿā*** — ⭐ **he RANSOMS: SONS → wife → brother → clan → everyone** (starting with the **dearest**).
  ⭐⭐⭐ **ʿAbasa builds UP to the dearest he flees from; Maʿārij STARTS with the dearest he'd sacrifice.** ⭐ **In dunya, he'd have died for his sons; that Day, he'd hand them over FIRST.** ⭐ **The order reveals how total the terror is.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Faṣīlatihi'llatī TUʾWĪH* — "his clan that SHELTERED him"** — ⭐ **the people who protected him all his life** — ⭐ **he'd throw them in too.** ⭐⭐ ***Tuʾwīh* — the *maʾwā* root:** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:39** — *fa-inna'l-jaḥīma hiya'l-**maʾwā*** (Hell is the REFUGE). ⭐ **The clan was his *maʾwā* in dunya; now Hell is.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Wa man fi'l-arḍi JAMĪʿĀ*** — ⭐ **and EVERYONE ON EARTH** — ⭐ **the whole of humanity offered as ransom for one man.** ⭐ ***Thumma yunjīh*** — ⭐ **"THEN it would save him"** — ⭐ the *thumma* = **even after all that, if only…** ⭐ **The answer (v.15): *KALLĀ!* — NO!**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***faṣl* = a CHAPTER / SEASON**; ⭐ ***faṣīlah dammawiyyah* = BLOOD TYPE**; ⭐ ***īwāʾ* = SHELTER**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ all joined to ***bi-*** (instrument of ransom) → **genitive**. ⭐ ***jamīʿan*** = *ḥāl* ("**all together**"). ⭐ ***yunjīhi*** — ⚠️ the verb's subject is "the ransom" (implied).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the list's order — dearest first — is the argument.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ʿABASA 80:34–36: he FLEES — brother → mother → father → wife → SONS (dearest LAST). MAʿĀRIJ 70:11–14: he RANSOMS — SONS → wife → brother → clan → EVERYONE (dearest FIRST).** ⭐ **In dunya he'd die for his sons; that Day he'd hand them over FIRST.** ⭐⭐ ***Tuʾwīh* ← *MAʾWĀ* (refuge — Nāziʿāt 79:39).** ⭐⭐ ***Faṣīlah* ← *faṣl* (CHAPTER/SEASON); *faṣīlah dammawiyyah* = BLOOD TYPE.** ⭐ ***Najāt* = SALVATION.**

## §4 — Laẓā (v.15–18)

**14. كَلَّا ۖ إِنَّهَا لَظَىٰ — *kallā innahā laẓā*** · v.15
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**LEAPING FLAME / blazing**" family — *laẓiya* = **for fire to blaze, to flare with leaping tongues**; ⭐ ***laẓā* = a NAME of Hell — "the Flame"**; ⭐ ***talaẓẓā* = to flare up** (**Layl 92:14**!); ⭐ ***talaẓẓin* = (modern) a flaring / burning thirst / ardent longing**.
- **Here it means:** "**No! Indeed, it is Laẓā [the flaming Fire]…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KALLĀ!* — the answer to v.11–14's ransom fantasy: NO.** ⭐ **No sons, no wife, no clan, no humanity — will be accepted.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the NAME: *Laẓā*** — ⭐ **Layl 92:14** — *fa-andhartukum nāran **talaẓẓā*** — ⭐ "**I warn you of a Fire that FLARES**." ⭐ **Layl used the VERB; Maʿārij uses the NAME.** ⭐⭐ **Hell-names you own:** *Jahannam, Jaḥīm* (blaze), *Saʿīr* (flare), *Hāwiyah* (abyss), *Ḥuṭamah* (crusher), *Saqar* (scorcher), ⭐ ***Laẓā* (leaping flame)**.
  ⭐ ***Innahā*** — "**indeed IT**" — ⭐ **the feminine pronoun pointing at the Fire before it's named** — ⭐ **suspense: "it is… LAẒĀ."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kallā*** = stern rejection. ⭐ ***laẓā*** = **DIPTOTE** (feminine proper name ending in *alif maqṣūrah*). ⭐ **Note: in Layl 92:14 *talaẓẓā* drops a *tāʾ* (*tatalaẓẓā*).**
- **Why this exact word:** *Laẓā* — ⭐ **the name of leaping flame, refusing the ransom with heat.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KALLĀ!* — NO to the ransom.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Layl 92:14: *nāran TALAẒẒĀ* (the VERB). Maʿārij 70:15: *LAẒĀ* (the NAME).** ⭐⭐ **Hell-names: Jaḥīm, Saʿīr, Hāwiyah, Ḥuṭamah, Saqar, LAẒĀ.** ⚠️ **Diptote.**

**15. نَزَّاعَةً لِّلشَّوَىٰ — *nazzāʿatan li'sh-shawā*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *nazzāʿah* — ⭐⭐ the "**PLUCKING OUT / tearing away**" family — *nazaʿa* = **to pull out, to extract forcefully**; ⭐⭐ ***an-nāziʿāt* = those who pull out (souls) — Nāziʿāt 79:1!**; ⭐ ***nizāʿ* = a DISPUTE / conflict** (pulling against each other!); ⭐ *naziʿa* = to remove (*nazaʿa thiyābahu* = took off his clothes). *shawā* — ⭐⭐ the "**GRILLING / roasting**" family — ***shawā* = the SCALP / the extremities** (hands, feet, head-skin — what's exposed to fire); ⭐⭐ ***shiwāʾ* = GRILLING, BARBECUE** (⭐ ***mashwī* = GRILLED meat** — everyday Arabic!; ⭐ ***shawarma*** — from Turkish *çevirme*, not this root — but *mashwī* is!).
- **Here it means:** "**…stripping away the scalp [and limbs].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO ROOTS — and one is the opening of Sūrat an-Nāziʿāt.** ⭐ ***Nazzāʿah*** — ⭐ **the *faʿʿālah* INTENSIVE: "one that PULLS OUT again and again"** — ⭐ **it strips the skin, then it grows back, then it strips again** (⭐ compare **Muddaththir 74:28** — *lā tubqī wa lā tadhar*; ⭐ and **Nisāʾ 4:56** — *kullamā naḍijat julūduhum baddalnāhum julūdan ghayrahā* — "every time their skins are roasted through, We replace them"). ⭐⭐⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:1** — *wa'n-**nāziʿāti** gharqā* — ⭐ **the angels who PULL OUT the souls of the wicked "drowningly" (violently).** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:16** — ***nazzāʿatan*** li'sh-shawā — ⭐ **the Fire that PULLS OFF the skin.** ⭐⭐ **The pulling starts at death (Nāziʿāt) and continues in the Fire (Maʿārij).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Shawā* = scalp / extremities** — ⭐ **the root of *SHIWĀʾ* = BARBECUE / GRILLING** — ⭐ ***mashwī* = grilled meat.** ⭐ **The word for the body part is the word for grilling.** ⭐ **Kahf 18:29** — *bi-māʾin ka'l-muhli **yashwi**'l-wujūh* (water like molten metal that **GRILLS faces**) — ⭐ **same root.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Faʿʿāl(ah)* intensive thread:** *lawwāmah* (75:2), *lawwāḥah* (74:29), *tawwāb* (110:3), *ḥammālah* (111:4), *khannās* (114:4), ⭐ ***nazzāʿah* (70:16)**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***nazzāʿatan*** = **accusative *ḥāl*** (describing *Laẓā*) — ⚠️ **there's also a recitation *nazzāʿatun* (nominative, predicate)** — ⭐ both authentic. ⭐ ***faʿʿālah*** intensive.
- **Why this exact word:** *nazzāʿah* + *shawā* — ⭐ **repeated stripping of the body's exposed surface, in the language of the butcher's grill.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***NAZZĀʿAH* — PULLS OFF again and again (*faʿʿālah*).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:1: angels PULL OUT souls (*nāziʿāt*) → Maʿārij 70:16: the Fire PULLS OFF skin (*nazzāʿah*).** ⭐⭐ ***SHAWĀ* (scalp) ← *SHIWĀʾ* = BARBECUE; *MASHWĪ* = GRILLED meat.** ⭐ ***Nizāʿ* = a DISPUTE.** ⭐ **Nisāʾ 4:56: skins replaced.**

**16. تَدْعُو مَنْ أَدْبَرَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ — *tadʿū man adbara wa tawallā*** · v.17
- **Root feelings:** *tadʿū* — ⭐ **it CALLS / summons** (*daʿā*; *duʿāʾ*; *daʿwah* = an invitation!). *adbara* — ⭐ **turned his BACK** (*dubur*). *tawallā* — ⭐ **turned AWAY** (*walla*; ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:1** *ʿabasa wa **tawallā***; ⭐ **Layl 92:16** *kadhdhaba wa **tawallā***).
- **Here it means:** "**It calls whoever turned his back and turned away…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE FIRE CALLS PEOPLE BY NAME.** ⭐ ***Tadʿū*** — ⭐ **the Fire itself issues a *daʿwah* (an invitation / summons)** — ⭐ classical: **literally calling them — "come to me, O so-and-so!"** — ⭐ **or figuratively, drawing them like a magnet.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The bitter irony: they REJECTED the *daʿwah* of the Messenger** (*yadʿū* — **Jinn 72:19** — *qāma ʿabdu'llāhi **yadʿūhu*** — the servant of Allah stood **calling**) — ⭐ **so the Fire issues its own *daʿwah*.** ⭐ **Refuse the call to Paradise, receive the call to Laẓā.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Adbara wa tawallā* — the two "turning" verbs:** ⭐ ***adbara*** — **Muddaththir 74:23** — *thumma **adbara** wa'stakbar* (al-Walīd turned his back); ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:22** — *thumma **adbara** yasʿā* (Pharaoh). ⭐ ***tawallā*** — **Layl 92:16** — *alladhī kadhdhaba wa **tawallā*** (the one who denied and turned away — **who burns in *nāran talaẓẓā*** (92:14–15)!). ⭐⭐⭐ **Layl 92:14–16: *nāran TALAẒẒĀ… alladhī kadhdhaba wa TAWALLĀ*. Maʿārij 70:15–17: *LAẒĀ… tadʿū man adbara wa TAWALLĀ*.** ⭐ **The SAME Fire, the SAME turning-away, in two surahs.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***tadʿū*** = feminine present (subject: *Laẓā*). ⭐ ***man*** = "**whoever**." ⭐ ***adbara / tawallā*** — two near-synonyms (back turned + face turned away).
- **Why this exact word:** *tadʿū* — ⭐ **the Fire's "invitation" answers their rejection of the Prophet's ﷺ invitation.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The FIRE issues a *DAʿWAH* — calling them by name.** ⭐ **They rejected the Prophet's ﷺ *daʿwah* (Jinn 72:19) → the Fire's *daʿwah* comes.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Layl 92:14–16: *talaẓẓā… kadhdhaba wa TAWALLĀ*. Maʿārij 70:15–17: *Laẓā… adbara wa TAWALLĀ*.** ⭐ **Same Fire, same turning.** ⭐⭐ ***Adbara*: Pharaoh (79:22), al-Walīd (74:23).**

**17. وَجَمَعَ فَأَوْعَىٰ — *wa jamaʿa fa-awʿā*** · v.18
- **Root feelings:** *jamaʿa* — ⭐ **GATHERED** (*jamʿ*; ⭐ **Humazah 104:2** *jamaʿa mālan*). *awʿā* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**CONTAINING / retaining in a VESSEL**" family — *waʿā* = to hold, retain; ⭐ ***wiʿāʾ* = a CONTAINER, a VESSEL**; ⭐ ***awʿā* (Form IV) = to put away in a container, to HOARD**; ⭐⭐ ***waʿy* = CONSCIOUSNESS** (**Ḥāqqah 69:12**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and gathered [wealth] and hoarded it [in containers].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BEST CROSS-SURAH ROOT IN THIS SURAH — back to back with Ḥāqqah.**
  ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:12** — *wa taʿiyahā **udhunun wāʿiyah*** — ⭐ "**that a RETAINING ear might RETAIN it**" — ⭐ **an ear like a VESSEL, holding the REMINDER.**
  ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:18** — *wa jamaʿa **fa-awʿā*** — ⭐ "**he gathered and put it in a VESSEL**" — ⭐ **a man hoarding MONEY.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME ROOT (*w-ʿ-y*), CONSECUTIVE SURAHS: one man's VESSEL holds the REMINDER; another's holds GOLD.** ⭐ **What does your *wiʿāʾ* hold?** ⭐⭐ **And *waʿy* = CONSCIOUSNESS** — ⭐ **the hoarder's "consciousness" is full of money.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Jamaʿa* — Humazah 104:2** — *alladhī **jamaʿa** mālan wa ʿaddadah* — ⭐ **gathered and COUNTED.** ⭐ **Maʿārij: gathered and LOCKED AWAY.** ⭐ **Fajr 89:20** — *tuḥibbūna'l-māla ḥubban jammā* (**loving** it). ⭐ **Gather, count, lock up, love — the hoarder's whole life.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the two charges in v.17–18 are the TWO HALVES of every sinner in the juzʾ:** ⭐ **v.17: turned away from ALLAH (*adbara wa tawallā*). ⭐ v.18: hoarded from PEOPLE (*jamaʿa fa-awʿā*).** ⭐ **Like Ḥāqqah 69:33–34 (didn't believe + didn't urge feeding).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa*** = immediately ("gathered, **then** stored away"). ⭐ ***awʿā*** = **Form IV**, weak-final (doubly weak root *w-ʿ-y*). ⭐ **Object deleted** — gathered **what**? (obviously money).
- **Why this exact word:** *awʿā* — ⭐ **the vessel-word, making him the dark mirror of Ḥāqqah's "retaining ear."**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:12: *udhunun WĀʿIYAH* — an ear-VESSEL holding the REMINDER. Maʿārij 70:18: *fa-AWʿĀ* — a VESSEL holding MONEY.** ⭐ **Same root, consecutive surahs: what does YOUR container hold?** ⭐⭐ **Humazah 104:2: gathered and COUNTED; here: gathered and LOCKED AWAY.** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.17 turned from ALLAH + v.18 hoarded from PEOPLE = the two halves.** ⭐ ***Wiʿāʾ* = a CONTAINER; *waʿy* = CONSCIOUSNESS.**

## §5 — Human Nature (v.19–21)

**18. إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ خُلِقَ هَلُوعًا — *inna'l-insāna khuliqa halūʿā*** · v.19
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**ANXIOUS PANIC / restless greed**" family — *haliʿa* = **to be seized by anxious alarm, to be restlessly impatient and greedy**; ⭐⭐ ***halaʿ* = PANIC, terror, extreme anxiety** (⭐ modern: ***halaʿ* = PANIC** — *nawbat halaʿ* = a **PANIC ATTACK**!); ⭐ ***halūʿ* = intensely anxious and greedy by nature** (*faʿūl* intensive).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, man was created anxious and impatient.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE DIAGNOSIS — and the Qurʾān DEFINES its own word.** ⭐ ***Halūʿ*** was a rare word even for the Arabs — ⭐ classical report: **Ibn ʿAbbās was asked what *halūʿ* means, and he answered: "Allah has explained it: *idhā massahu'sh-sharru jazūʿā wa idhā massahu'l-khayru manūʿā*."** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān is its own dictionary** (*tafsīr al-Qurʾān bi'l-Qurʾān*): ⭐ ***halūʿ* = *jazūʿ* (v.20) + *manūʿ* (v.21).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Halaʿ* = PANIC** in modern Arabic — ⭐ ***nawbat halaʿ* = a PANIC ATTACK.** ⭐ **The Qurʾān names a human tendency that clinical language still uses.** ⭐ **And "*khuliqa*" (was CREATED so)** — ⭐ **it's a starting condition, not a fixed fate** — ⭐ **v.22 shows the way out.**
  ⭐⭐ **The *faʿūl* intensive** — ⭐ ***halūʿ, jazūʿ, manūʿ*** — ⭐ **three on the same pattern** = **a disposition, a temperament** — ⭐ (like *kafūr*, Insān 76:3; *kanūd*, ʿĀdiyāt 100:6). ⭐⭐ **And compare: ʿĀdiyāt 100:6** — *inna'l-insāna li-rabbihi **la-kanūd*** (man is ungrateful) ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:6** — *inna'l-insāna **la-yaṭghā*** (man transgresses) ⭐ **ʿAṣr 103:2** — *inna'l-insāna **la-fī khusr*** (man is in loss) ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:19** — *inna'l-insāna khuliqa **halūʿā*** (man is anxious). ⭐⭐⭐ **FOUR "*inna'l-insān*" diagnoses: ungrateful, transgressing, losing, anxious** — ⭐ **and every one is followed by an EXCEPTION or a cure.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***khuliqa*** = passive (created — by Allah). ⭐ ***halūʿan*** = *ḥāl* (in a state of…). ⭐ ***al-insān*** = the genus (all humans), hence the exception in v.22.
- **Why this exact word:** *halūʿ* — ⭐ **a rare, compressed word, unpacked by the next two verses.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HALŪʿ* = DEFINED BY THE QURʾĀN ITSELF: *jazūʿ* (v.20) + *manūʿ* (v.21)** — Ibn ʿAbbās. ⭐⭐⭐ ***NAWBAT HALAʿ* = a PANIC ATTACK** (modern Arabic). ⭐⭐⭐ ***Inna'l-insāna*… FOUR diagnoses: *kanūd* (100:6), *yaṭghā* (96:6), *fī khusr* (103:2), *halūʿ* (70:19) — each with a cure.** ⭐ ***Khuliqa* = a STARTING condition, not a fate.**

**19. إِذَا مَسَّهُ الشَّرُّ جَزُوعًا — *idhā massahu'sh-sharru jazūʿā*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *massa* — ⭐ **to TOUCH** (*mass* = a touch; ⭐ *mass kahrabāʾī* = an **electric shock**!; ⭐ *mamsūs* = **possessed / touched by jinn**). *sharr* — **HARM / evil**. *jazūʿ* — ⭐ the "**IMPATIENT DISTRESS / fretting**" family — *jaziʿa* = **to lose patience, to be overcome with distress, to wail**; ⭐ ***jazaʿ* = the OPPOSITE of *ṣabr*** — **anxious, complaining distress**.
- **Here it means:** "**When harm touches him — fretful [in despair].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Half of *halūʿ*: PANIC in hardship.** ⭐ ***Jazaʿ* is the exact OPPOSITE of *ṣabr*** — ⭐ **v.5 commanded *ṣabran jamīlā* (beautiful patience); v.20 describes *jazaʿ* (fretful despair).** ⭐⭐⭐ **The surah sets them face to face: *ṢABR* (v.5) vs *JAZAʿ* (v.20).** ⭐ **Same hardship — one meets it with beautiful patience, the other with wailing.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Massahu* — "TOUCHES him"** — ⭐ **just a TOUCH of harm** — ⭐ **not a flood, a touch** — ⭐ **and he collapses.** ⭐ **The smallness of the trigger shows the fragility.**
  ⭐⭐ **Compare Fajr 89:16** — *wa ammā idhā mā'btalāhu fa-qadara ʿalayhi rizqahu fa-yaqūlu rabbī **ahānan*** — ⭐ "**when He tests him by restricting his provision, he says: 'My Lord has HUMILIATED me!'**" ⭐ **That's *jazaʿ* in speech.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***idhā*** = "**whenever**" (habitual condition). ⭐ ***jazūʿan*** = second *ḥāl* (continuing *halūʿā*). ⭐ ***faʿūl*** intensive.
- **Why this exact word:** *jazūʿ* — ⭐ **names the exact failure of patience, set against v.5's command.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***JAZAʿ* = the OPPOSITE of *ṢABR*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.5 *ṣabran jamīlā* (beautiful PATIENCE) vs v.20 *jazūʿā* (fretful DESPAIR).** ⭐ **Just a TOUCH (*massa*) of harm — and he collapses.** ⭐⭐ **Fajr 89:16: "my Lord HUMILIATED me!" = *jazaʿ* in words.** ⭐ ***Mass kahrabāʾī* = an ELECTRIC SHOCK.**

**20. وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ الْخَيْرُ مَنُوعًا — *wa idhā massahu'l-khayru manūʿā*** · v.21
- **Root feelings:** *khayr* — ⭐ **GOOD / wealth** (⚠️ as in **ʿĀdiyāt 100:8**, *li-ḥubbi'l-khayr* = love of **WEALTH**). *manūʿ* — ⭐ the "**WITHHOLDING / preventing**" family — *manaʿa* = to withhold; ⭐ ***mamnūʿ* = FORBIDDEN**; ⭐⭐ ***mamnūʿ min aṣ-ṣarf* = a DIPTOTE** (grammar!); ⭐ ***manūʿ* = habitually WITHHOLDING, stingy** (*faʿūl* intensive).
- **Here it means:** "**…and when good touches him — withholding.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The other half of *halūʿ*: STINGINESS in ease.** ⭐ **Hardship → he panics (v.20); prosperity → he grips it tight (v.21).** ⭐⭐ **Both from the same root: FEAR** — ⭐ **fear of losing makes him wail when he loses, and clutch when he has.** ⭐ **That's *halaʿ* — PANIC — in both directions.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the *manaʿa* root — MĀʿŪN 107:7** — *wa **yamnaʿūna**'l-māʿūn* — ⭐ **they WITHHOLD even the small things (a pot, a bucket).** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:21** — ***manūʿā*** — **WITHHOLDING by nature.** ⭐⭐ **And the cure in v.22–25: ⭐ *illa'l-MUṢALLĪN* … *fī amwālihim ḥaqqun maʿlūm*** — ⭐ **those who PRAY and GIVE a known right.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Māʿūn's sinners: heedless in prayer + withhold. Maʿārij's exception: constant in prayer + give.** ⭐ **The two surahs are mirror images.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Massahu'l-KHAYR*** — ⭐ **"when GOOD touches him"** — ⭐ **again just a TOUCH** — ⭐ **and he grips it.**
  ⭐ Grammar hook: ⭐ ***mamnūʿ min aṣ-ṣarf* = DIPTOTE** — "WITHHELD from full declension" — **same root as *manūʿ*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ parallel to v.20 (*idhā massahu… X*). ⭐ ***manūʿan*** = third *ḥāl*, *faʿūl*.
- **Why this exact word:** *manūʿ* — ⭐ **names hoarding as a temperament, setting up the surah's cure.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **HARDSHIP → *JAZŪʿ* (panic). EASE → *MANŪʿ* (grip). Both = FEAR = *HALAʿ*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Māʿūn 107:7 *yamnaʿūna'l-māʿūn* — withhold even a POT.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Māʿūn: heedless prayer + withholding. Maʿārij: CONSTANT prayer + GIVING.** ⭐ ***Mamnūʿ min aṣ-ṣarf* = DIPTOTE — same root.**

## §6 — The Exceptions (v.22–35)

*⭐ Eight qualities, **bracketed by prayer** — v.23 (*dāʾimūn* — constant in it) and v.34 (*yuḥāfiẓūn* — guarding it). Prayer is the frame; everything else is inside it.*

**21. إِلَّا الْمُصَلِّينَ — *illa'l-muṣallīn*** · v.22
- **Root feeling:** *muṣallīn* — **those who PRAY** (Form II participle of *ṣallā*).
- **Here it means:** "**Except those who pray…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE EXCEPTION — and the mirror of Māʿūn.** ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:4** — ***fa-waylun li'l-MUṢALLĪN*** — ⭐ **"WOE to those who pray —"** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:22** — ***illa'l-MUṢALLĪN*** — ⭐ **"EXCEPT those who pray."** ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME WORD — one cursed, one exempted.** ⭐ **The next verse explains the difference.**
  ⭐⭐ **And why PRAYER is the cure for *halaʿ* (panic):** ⭐ **five times a day, you stand before the One who controls harm and good** — ⭐ **so harm can't crush you (He's in control) and good can't own you (it's His).** ⭐ **Prayer resets the anxious heart.**
  ⭐ **The *illā* exception structure:** ⭐ **ʿAṣr 103:3** (*illa'lladhīna āmanū* — except believers, from loss), **Tīn 95:6** (except believers, from the lowest), **Muddaththir 74:39** (*illā aṣḥāba'l-yamīn* — except the right-handed, from pledge), ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:22** (*illa'l-muṣallīn* — except those who pray, from *halaʿ*).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***illā*** = exception from *al-insān* (v.19). ⭐ ***al-muṣallīna*** = accusative (excepted).
- **Why this exact word:** *muṣallīn* — ⭐ **names prayer as the antidote to the anxious nature just described.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Māʿūn 107:4: *fa-WAYLUN li'l-muṣallīn* (WOE to those who pray). Maʿārij 70:22: *ILLA'l-muṣallīn* (EXCEPT those who pray).** ⭐ **Same word — cursed vs exempted.** ⭐⭐ **Prayer = the antidote to *halaʿ*: harm can't crush you, good can't own you.** ⭐ ***Illā* exceptions: 103:3, 95:6, 74:39, 70:22.**

**22. الَّذِينَ هُمْ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمْ دَائِمُونَ — *alladhīna hum ʿalā ṣalātihim dāʾimūn*** · v.23
- **Root feeling:** *dāʾimūn* — ⭐ the "**LASTING / continuing / constancy**" family — *dāma* = **to last, to continue**; ⭐ ***dāʾiman* = ALWAYS** (everyday!); ⭐ ***dawām* = WORKING HOURS / a shift** (*dawām kāmil* = **full-time**!); ⭐ ***mā dāma* = as long as**; ⭐ ***ad-Dāʾim* = the Everlasting**.
- **Here it means:** "**…those who are constant in their prayer.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE CURSED AND THE SAVED — in one word.** ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:5** — *alladhīna hum **ʿan** ṣalātihim **sāhūn*** — ⭐ **HEEDLESS of their prayer.** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:23** — *alladhīna hum **ʿalā** ṣalātihim **dāʾimūn*** — ⭐ **CONSTANT in their prayer.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Look at the prepositions: *ʿAN* (AWAY from) vs *ʿALĀ* (UPON, holding firm to).** ⭐ **One drifts away from prayer; one is planted on it.** ⭐ **And *sāhūn* (heedless) vs *dāʾimūn* (constant).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Dāʾimūn* = CONSTANT, unbroken** — ⭐ **the ḥadīth: "*The most beloved deeds to Allah are the most CONSTANT (adwamuhā), even if small.*"** ⭐ **Same root.** ⭐ **Not dramatic bursts — steady presence.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Dawām* = WORKING HOURS** — ⭐ **prayer as a shift you never skip.** ⭐ Classical readings: **(a) never missing it; (b) remaining focused within it** (not turning the face/heart away).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***hum*** (fronted subject) + ***ʿalā ṣalātihim*** (fronted) + ***dāʾimūna*** — ⭐ **fronting = emphasis on the prayer.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿalā*** = being firmly **upon** something.
- **Why this exact word:** *dāʾimūn* — ⭐ **constancy, the precise antidote to the drifting heedlessness of Māʿūn.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Māʿūn 107:5: *ʿAN ṣalātihim SĀHŪN* (drifting AWAY, heedless). Maʿārij 70:23: *ʿALĀ ṣalātihim DĀʾIMŪN* (planted UPON it, constant).** ⭐ **Two prepositions, two destinies.** ⭐⭐ **Ḥadīth: "most beloved deeds = the most CONSTANT, even if small."** ⭐ ***Dāʾiman* = ALWAYS; *dawām* = WORKING HOURS.**

**23. وَالَّذِينَ فِي أَمْوَالِهِمْ حَقٌّ مَّعْلُومٌ — *wa'lladhīna fī amwālihim ḥaqqun maʿlūm*** · v.24
- **Root feelings:** *amwāl* — **WEALTH** (*māl*). *ḥaqq* — ⭐ **a RIGHT, what is DUE** (⭐ *ḥuqūq* = rights; ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:1**!). *maʿlūm* — ⭐ **KNOWN, specified** (*ʿilm*; ⭐ *maʿlūmāt* = **INFORMATION / data**!).
- **Here it means:** "**And those in whose wealth there is a known right…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NOT "they give charity" — "in their wealth is a RIGHT."** ⭐ **The poor person's share is ALREADY THERE, inside your money** — ⭐ **it belongs to them before you hand it over.** ⭐⭐ **That's *ḥaqq* — what is OWED.** ⭐ **Giving isn't generosity; it's paying a debt.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And recall: *ṭaʿāmi'l-miskīn* — "the food OF the poor" (Māʿūn 107:3, Ḥāqqah 69:34, Fajr 89:18)** — ⭐ **the poor's food, held in your hands.** ⭐ **Same idea.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Maʿlūm* — KNOWN, specified** — ⭐ classical: **zakāh — a fixed, KNOWN percentage** (2.5% etc.) — ⭐ **or a portion they've personally set and stick to.** ⭐ **Not random, not when the mood strikes — KNOWN.** ⭐ **The opposite of v.21's *manūʿ*: instead of gripping, a fixed portion already marked for others.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And *ḥaqq* — the root of AL-ḤĀQQAH (69:1).** ⭐ **Ḥāqqah: the Day that is TRUE and DUE. Maʿārij: the poor's share that is DUE.** ⭐ **Pay the *ḥaqq* now, or face *al-Ḥāqqah* later.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fī amwālihim*** (fronted predicate) + ***ḥaqqun maʿlūmun*** (subject, indefinite) — ⭐ **fronting the wealth = "IN THEIR WEALTH [specifically] is a right."**
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥaqq* (**a right owed**) — ⭐ **reframes charity as obligation, not favour.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NOT "they give charity" — "in their wealth is a *ḤAQQ* (a RIGHT)."** ⭐ **The poor's share is ALREADY inside your money.** ⭐⭐ ***MAʿLŪM* = KNOWN, fixed — zakāh's set portion; opposite of *manūʿ*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥaqq* ← *AL-ḤĀQQAH* (69:1): pay the *ḥaqq* now or face *al-Ḥāqqah*.** ⭐ ***Maʿlūmāt* = INFORMATION.**

**24. لِّلسَّائِلِ وَالْمَحْرُومِ — *li's-sāʾili wa'l-maḥrūm*** · v.25
- **Root feelings:** *sāʾil* — ⭐ **one who ASKS / a beggar** (⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:10** *as-sāʾil*; ⭐ **v.1** *sāʾil*!). *maḥrūm* — ⭐⭐ the "**FORBIDDEN / SACRED / DEPRIVED**" family — *ḥarama* = **to deprive, to forbid**; ⭐ ***ḥarām* = FORBIDDEN**; ⭐ ***al-Ḥaram* = the SANCTUARY** (Makkah!); ⭐ ***ḥirmān* = DEPRIVATION**; ⭐ ***maḥrūm* = the DEPRIVED one**; ⭐ ***muḥarram* = the sacred month**; ⭐ ***ḥarīm* = women's quarters** (protected!).
- **Here it means:** "**…for the one who asks, and the one who is deprived [but doesn't ask].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO kinds of poor — and the second is the hidden one.** ⭐ ***As-SĀʾIL*** — ⭐ **the one who ASKS openly.** ⭐ ***Al-MAḤRŪM*** — ⭐ classical: **the poor person who does NOT ask** — ⭐ **too dignified, too ashamed, or unknown** — ⭐ **deprived, but silent.** ⭐⭐ **You must SEEK OUT the *maḥrūm*; the *sāʾil* comes to you.** ⭐ **Compare Baqarah 2:273** — *yaḥsabuhumu'l-jāhilu aghniyāʾa mina't-taʿaffuf* — "**the ignorant think them rich because of their restraint.**"
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Sāʾil* in THIS surah: v.1 — a man ASKS for PUNISHMENT (mockingly). v.25 — a poor man ASKS for FOOD (rightfully).** ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:10** — *wa ammā **as-sāʾila** fa-lā tanhar* — ⭐ **"as for the one who ASKS — do not repel him."** ⭐ **Ḍuḥā: don't repel the asker. Maʿārij: he has a RIGHT in your wealth.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Maḥrūm* — the root of *ḤARĀM* and *AL-ḤARAM*** — ⭐ **the same three letters for "forbidden," "sacred," and "deprived."** ⭐ **The core idea: something set APART, off-limits.** ⭐ **The *maḥrūm* is someone set apart from provision.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-*** = "**for**" (the right is owed **to** them). ⭐ two participles: active (*sāʾil*) and passive (*maḥrūm*) — ⭐ **one acts (asks), one is acted upon (deprived).**
- **Why this exact word:** *maḥrūm* — ⭐ **directs attention to the poor who stay invisible.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SĀʾIL* = the poor who ASKS; *MAḤRŪM* = the poor who DOESN'T ask — you must SEEK HIM OUT.** ⭐ **Baqarah 2:273: "the ignorant think them rich."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Sāʾil* v.1: asks for PUNISHMENT; v.25: asks for FOOD.** ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:10: don't repel the asker.** ⭐⭐ ***Maḥrūm* ← *ḤARĀM*, *AL-ḤARAM*, *ḥirmān* (deprivation).**

**25. وَالَّذِينَ يُصَدِّقُونَ بِيَوْمِ الدِّينِ — *wa'lladhīna yuṣaddiqūna bi-yawmi'd-dīn*** · v.26
- **Root feelings:** *yuṣaddiqūn* — ⭐ the "**TRUTH / affirming as true**" family — *ṣadaqa* = to be truthful; ⭐ ***ṣaddaqa* (Form II) = to CONFIRM as true, to believe**; ⭐ ***ṣadaqah* = CHARITY** (proof of TRUE faith!); ⭐ ***ṣadīq* = a FRIEND** (one TRUE to you!); ⭐ ***aṣ-Ṣiddīq* = Abū Bakr's title** (the constant affirmer!); ⭐ **Layl 92:6** — *wa **ṣaddaqa** bi'l-ḥusnā*. *yawm ad-dīn* — ⭐ **the Day of RECKONING** (*dayn* = debt).
- **Here it means:** "**And those who affirm the Day of Reckoning…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE EXACT OPPOSITE of the root sin you've tracked through FOUR surahs.** ⭐ **Infiṭār 82:9** — *bal **tukadhdhibūna** bi'd-dīn*. ⭐ **Tīn 95:7** — *fa-mā **yukadhdhibuka** baʿdu bi'd-dīn*. ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:1** — *alladhī **yukadhdhibu** bi'd-dīn*. ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:46** — *wa kunnā **nukadhdhibu** bi-yawmi'd-dīn*. ⭐⭐⭐ **And now: Maʿārij 70:26 — *yuṢADDIQŪNA bi-yawmi'd-dīn*.** ⭐ ***Kadhdhaba* (call it a LIE) vs *ṣaddaqa* (affirm it TRUE)** — ⭐ **two Form II verbs, the whole difference between the damned and the saved.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the ORDER makes sense:** ⭐ **they give a *ḥaqq maʿlūm* (v.24) BECAUSE they affirm the Day of Reckoning (v.26).** ⭐ **Māʿūn reasoned the same way in reverse: denies the Day → shoves the orphan.** ⭐ **Belief in the Reckoning produces generosity.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ṣaddaqa* — Layl 92:6** — *wa **ṣaddaqa bi'l-ḥusnā*** (affirmed the best — and was "eased toward ease"). ⭐ ***Ṣadaqah* (charity) = proof of *taṣdīq* (affirmation)** — ⭐ **the ḥadīth: "*aṣ-ṣadaqatu burhān*" — charity is a PROOF.** ⭐ **Your charity proves your belief.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ṣaddaqa bi-*** (fixed preposition). ⭐ Present (*yuṣaddiqūna*) = **ongoing, habitual**.
- **Why this exact word:** *taṣdīq* — ⭐ **the precise antonym of *takdhīb*, closing the denial thread with its cure.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUKADHDHIBU bi'd-dīn* (DENY — 82:9, 95:7, 107:1, 74:46) vs *YUṢADDIQŪNA bi-yawmi'd-dīn* (AFFIRM — 70:26).** ⭐ **Two Form II verbs = damned vs saved.** ⭐⭐ ***Ṣadaqah* = CHARITY — "charity is a PROOF" (of *taṣdīq*).** ⭐ ***Aṣ-Ṣiddīq* = Abū Bakr; *ṣadīq* = a FRIEND.** ⭐ **Layl 92:6 *ṣaddaqa bi'l-ḥusnā*.**

**26. وَالَّذِينَ هُم مِّنْ عَذَابِ رَبِّهِم مُّشْفِقُونَ — *wa'lladhīna hum min ʿadhābi rabbihim mushfiqūn*** · v.27
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐⭐ the "**AFTERGLOW / tender anxious care**" family — ***shafaq* = the TWILIGHT AFTERGLOW** (the red glow after sunset — **Inshiqāq 84:16**!); ⭐ ***shafaqah* = PITY, compassion**; ⭐ ***ashfaqa* = to be anxiously concerned, to fear for someone/something WITH TENDERNESS**; ⭐ ***mushfiq* = one who fears with care** (⭐ *mushfiq* also = **kind, compassionate**).
- **Here it means:** "**And those who are fearful [with tender concern] of their Lord's punishment…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST BEAUTIFUL ROOT IN THE LIST — from the evening sky.** ⭐ ***Shafaq* = the RED AFTERGLOW after sunset** — ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:16** — *fa-lā uqsimu bi'**sh-shafaq*** — ⭐ **"I swear by the TWILIGHT GLOW."** ⭐⭐ **Why would "fear" share a root with "twilight"?** ⭐ Classical: ***shafaq* is a soft, thin, fading light** — ⭐ ***ishfāq* is a soft, tender, careful FEAR** — ⭐ **not panic (*halaʿ*), not terror — ⭐ the anxious tenderness of a mother watching a sick child** (⭐ *shafaqah* = **compassion**). ⭐⭐⭐ **It's fear mixed with LOVE.** ⭐ **The *mushfiq* fears losing something precious — his Lord's pleasure.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And notice: the CURE for *halaʿ* (panic) includes a kind of FEAR!** ⭐ **v.19: *halūʿ* — panicked about the world (losses, gains). ⭐ v.27: *mushfiq* — tenderly anxious about his Lord.** ⭐⭐ **The fear doesn't disappear — it's redirected: from the world to Allah, from panic to tender care.**
  ⭐ **The fear-words you own:** ⭐ ***khawf*** (plain fear — Muddaththir 74:53), ***khashyah*** (awe from knowing — Nāziʿāt 79:19, Aʿlā 87:10, Bayyinah 98:8), ⭐ ***ishfāq*** (tender, anxious care — 70:27), and its opposite ***halaʿ*** (panic — 70:19).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***hum*** + ***min ʿadhābi rabbihim*** (fronted) + ***mushfiqūna*** — ⭐ **Form IV participle**; ***ashfaqa min*** = "fear **of**."
- **Why this exact word:** *ishfāq* — ⭐ **a fear that is gentle, loving, and alert — the opposite of panic.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHAFAQ* = the TWILIGHT AFTERGLOW (Inshiqāq 84:16) → *ISHFĀQ* = soft, tender FEAR.** ⭐ **A mother watching a sick child.** ⭐⭐ ***Shafaqah* = COMPASSION.** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.19 *HALAʿ* (PANIC about the world) → v.27 *ISHFĀQ* (TENDER FEAR of his Lord).** ⭐ **Fear redirected, not removed.** ⭐ **Fear-words: *khawf*, *khashyah*, *ishfāq*.**

**27. إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّهِمْ غَيْرُ مَأْمُونٍ — *inna ʿadhāba rabbihim ghayru maʾmūn*** · v.28
- **Root feeling:** *maʾmūn* — ⭐ the "**SAFETY / trust**" family — *amina* = to be safe; ⭐ ***maʾmūn* = SAFE FROM / trusted** (⭐ *maʾmūn al-jānib* = **reliable**!); ⭐ ***amn* = security** (**Quraysh 106:4** *āmanahum*); ⭐ ***īmān***; ⭐ ***al-Maʾmūn*** — the Abbasid caliph's name ("the trusted one")!
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, the punishment of their Lord is not to be felt safe from.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY they're *mushfiqūn* (v.27): no one is SAFE from it — not even the righteous.** ⭐ **The pious don't assume they're immune** — ⭐ **that's what keeps them pious.** ⭐ ⭐ Classical: **the believer lives between *khawf* and *rajāʾ* — fear and hope** — ⭐ **like a bird with two wings.** ⭐ **Feeling SAFE from Allah's plan is a sign of danger** (*a-fa-aminū makra'llāh*, 7:99).
  ⭐⭐ ***Maʾmūn* — the *amn* root:** ⭐ **Quraysh 106:4** — *wa **āmanahum** min khawf* — ⭐ **He made them SAFE from fear (in dunya).** ⭐ **Tīn 95:3** — *al-baladi'l-**amīn*** (the SECURE city). ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:28** — ⭐ **but His punishment is NOT something to feel safe from.** ⭐⭐ **Allah gives *amn* in this world — but no one should feel *amn* from His justice.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Ghayru maʾmūn*** — ⭐ **the *ghayr* + adjective negation** (⭐ **Tīn 95:6** *ghayru mamnūn*; **Muddaththir 74:10** *ghayru yasīr*). ⭐ **And v.30** — *ghayru malūmīn*. ⭐ **Three *ghayr*s in this surah's list.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***inna*** + subject + ***ghayru maʾmūnin*** (predicate). ⭐ ***ghayr*** + genitive = negating an adjective.
- **Why this exact word:** *maʾmūn* — ⭐ **explains why fear stays alive in the righteous.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAYRU MAʾMŪN* = NOT to be felt SAFE from — not even by the righteous.** ⭐ **Fear + hope = two wings (*khawf* + *rajāʾ*).** ⭐⭐ **Quraysh 106:4: He gives *amn* in dunya — but no *amn* from His justice.** ⭐ ***Ghayr* + adjective: 95:6, 74:10, 70:28, 70:30.** ⭐ ***Al-Maʾmūn* = the caliph "the trusted."**

**28. وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ لِفُرُوجِهِمْ حَافِظُونَ — *wa'lladhīna hum li-furūjihim ḥāfiẓūn*** · v.29
- **Root feelings:** *furūj* — ⭐ the "**OPENING / gap / relief**" family — ***farj* = an OPENING, a gap** → **the private parts** (a modest Qurʾānic term); ⭐⭐ ***faraj* = RELIEF, a way out of hardship** (⭐ *al-faraj baʿda'sh-shiddah* = **relief after hardship**!); ⭐ ***furjah* = a SPECTACLE / sightseeing** (*tafarraja* = to watch!); ⭐ *infirāj* = **easing (of a crisis)**. *ḥāfiẓūn* — ⭐ the "**GUARDING / preserving / memorizing**" family — ***ḥafiẓa* = to guard, to keep safe, to MEMORIZE**; ⭐ ***ḥāfiẓ* = a GUARDIAN / one who has MEMORIZED the Qurʾān**; ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:4** — *lammā ʿalayhā **ḥāfiẓ*** (over every soul is a GUARDIAN); ⭐ **Burūj 85:22** — *fī lawḥin **maḥfūẓ*** (the PRESERVED Tablet); ⭐ ***ḥifẓ*** = memorization.
- **Here it means:** "**And those who guard their chastity…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The word for CHASTITY is the word for MEMORIZING the Qurʾān.** ⭐ ***Ḥāfiẓ*** = **one who GUARDS** — ⭐ **his private life** (here) — ⭐ **or the Qurʾān in his heart** (a *ḥāfiẓ*). ⭐⭐ **Both are acts of PROTECTING something precious from loss.** ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:4** — *in kullu nafsin lammā ʿalayhā **ḥāfiẓ*** — ⭐ **every soul has a GUARDIAN over it (from Allah); here, the believer GUARDS himself.** ⭐ **Guarded by Him, guarding for Him.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Farj* — "an OPENING"** — ⭐ **the Qurʾān's modest, indirect term** — ⭐ **and the same root gives *FARAJ* = RELIEF** (a "gap" opening in hardship). ⭐ **(Kept modest here.)**
  ⭐ ⭐ **And note the Form I *ḥāfiẓūn* (v.29) vs Form III *yuḥāfiẓūn* (v.34 — guarding their PRAYERS)** — ⭐ **same root, guarding body and guarding prayer.** ⭐ **The list's guarding begins and ends with *ḥ-f-ẓ*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-furūjihim*** — the ***li-*** strengthens the object of a participle (***lām at-taqwiyah***). ⭐ ***ḥāfiẓūna*** = Form I participle.
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥifẓ* — ⭐ **frames chastity as GUARDING a trust, the same act as preserving revelation.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤĀFIẒ* = one who GUARDS his chastity — and one who has MEMORIZED the Qurʾān.** ⭐ **Both = PROTECTING something precious.** ⭐⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:4: a *ḥāfiẓ* over every soul; Burūj 85:22: *lawḥ maḥfūẓ*.** ⭐⭐ ***Ḥ-f-ẓ*: v.29 guarding the BODY; v.34 guarding the PRAYER.** ⭐ ***Faraj* = RELIEF** (same root as *farj*).

**29. إِلَّا عَلَىٰ أَزْوَاجِهِمْ أَوْ مَا مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَانُهُمْ فَإِنَّهُمْ غَيْرُ مَلُومِينَ — *illā ʿalā azwājihim aw mā malakat aymānuhum fa-innahum ghayru malūmīn*** · v.30
- **Root feelings:** *azwāj* — ⭐ the "**PAIRING**" family — ***zawj* = a SPOUSE / one of a pair** (⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:8** — *wa khalaqnākum **azwājā*** — created in PAIRS!; ⭐ **Takwīr 81:7** — *wa idha'n-nufūsu **zuwwijat***!; ⭐ ***zawāj* = MARRIAGE**). *mā malakat aymānuhum* — ⭐ **"what their right hands possess"** — a historical legal category in the Qurʾān (bondwomen), referring to the institution of that era. *malūmīn* — ⭐ the "**BLAME**" family — *lāma* = to blame; ⭐ ***lawm* = BLAME**; ⭐⭐ ***an-nafs al-lawwāmah* = the SELF-BLAMING soul** (**Qiyāmah 75:2**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…except from their spouses or those their right hands possess — for they are not to be blamed.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The exception sets lawful limits — and the key word is BLAME.** ⭐ ***Ghayru malūmīn*** — ⭐ **"NOT blameworthy"** — ⭐ **lawful intimacy carries no blame.** ⭐ **Islam doesn't treat lawful desire as shameful; it defines its proper place.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Malūm* ← *lawm* — the root of AN-NAFS AL-LAWWĀMAH (Qiyāmah 75:2)** — ⭐ **the soul that blames itself.** ⭐ **Here: no need for self-blame — within the lawful.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Azwāj* — PAIRS** — ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:8** — ***wa khalaqnākum azwājā*** — "**We created you in PAIRS**." ⭐ **The pairing is part of creation; marriage honours it.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Mā malakat aymānuhum*** — ⭐ **a historical category** that Islamic law regulated heavily and steadily worked to reduce (through manumission as expiation, charity, *mukātabah*); ⭐ **understood within its time.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***illā ʿalā*** = exception. ⭐ ***ghayru malūmīna*** — *ghayr* + participle (third *ghayr* in the list).
- **Why this exact word:** *malūmīn* — ⭐ **lifts blame from what is lawful, linking to the self-blaming soul.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAYRU MALŪMĪN* = NOT BLAMEWORTHY — lawful intimacy carries no blame.** ⭐⭐ ***Malūm* ← *lawm* — *an-nafs al-LAWWĀMAH* (Qiyāmah 75:2).** ⭐⭐ ***Azwāj* = PAIRS — Nabaʾ 78:8 "created in pairs"; *zawāj* = MARRIAGE.** ⭐ ***Mā malakat aymānuhum* = a historical category, regulated toward reduction.**

**30. فَمَنِ ابْتَغَىٰ وَرَاءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْعَادُونَ — *fa-mani'btaghā warāʾa dhālika fa-ulāʾika humu'l-ʿādūn*** · v.31
- **Root feelings:** *ibtaghā* — ⭐ **to SEEK** (Form VIII of *baghā*; ⭐ **Layl 92:20** *ibtighāʾa wajhi rabbih*; ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** *yabtaghūna min faḍli'llāh*). *warāʾa* — ⭐ **BEYOND / behind**. *ʿādūn* — ⭐⭐ the "**RUNNING / exceeding / aggression**" family — *ʿadā* = **to run, to exceed**; ⭐ ***ʿudwān* = AGGRESSION, transgression**; ⭐ ***ʿaduww* = an ENEMY**; ⭐ ***al-ʿādiyāt* = the CHARGERS** (**ʿĀdiyāt 100:1**!); ⭐ ***ʿādūn* = those who RUN PAST the limit**.
- **Here it means:** "**But whoever seeks beyond that — they are the transgressors.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Al-ʿĀDŪN* — those who RUN PAST the line.** ⭐ **The root of *al-ʿĀDIYĀT* — the war horses CHARGING at full gallop (ʿĀdiyāt 100:1)!** ⭐⭐ **A charging horse runs; a transgressor runs too — past the boundary.** ⭐ **Same root: running, and running over the limit.** ⭐ ***ʿUdwān* = AGGRESSION** (running AT someone); ⭐ ***ʿaduww* = an ENEMY** (one who runs against you).
  ⭐⭐ ***Ibtaghā* — the SEEKING verb** — ⭐ **Layl 92:20: seeking His FACE; Muzzammil 73:20: seeking His BOUNTY; Maʿārij 70:31: seeking BEYOND the limit.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same verb — three objects: His face, His bounty, or what lies past His boundary.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Ulāʾika HUMU'l-ʿādūn*** — ⭐ **the separating pronoun *hum*** = restriction: "**THEY — they specifically — are the transgressors.**" (⭐ Bayyinah 98:6–7, Kawthar 108:3.)
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** + past + ***fa*** = conditional. ⭐ ***hum*** = *ḍamīr al-faṣl* (separating pronoun).
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿādūn* (**those who run past**) — ⭐ **pictures transgression as overrunning a marked line.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-ʿĀDŪN* = those who RUN PAST the line — same root as *AL-ʿĀDIYĀT* (the CHARGING horses, 100:1).** ⭐ ***ʿUdwān* = AGGRESSION; *ʿaduww* = an ENEMY.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ibtaghā*: His FACE (92:20), His BOUNTY (73:20), BEYOND the limit (70:31).** ⭐ ***Hum* = separating pronoun.**

**31. وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ لِأَمَانَاتِهِمْ وَعَهْدِهِمْ رَاعُونَ — *wa'lladhīna hum li-amānātihim wa ʿahdihim rāʿūn*** · v.32
- **Root feelings:** *amānāt* — ⭐ **TRUSTS** — plural of ***amānah*** (⭐ *amn* root; ⭐ ***al-Amīn*** — the Prophet's ﷺ title; ⭐ **Tīn 95:3** *al-baladi'l-amīn*; ⭐ ***amānah* = (modern) a SECRETARIAT** / trusteeship!). *ʿahd* — ⭐ **a COVENANT / promise** (⭐ ***muʿāhadah* = a TREATY**; ⭐ ***maʿhad* = an INSTITUTE**!; ⭐ ***wilāyat al-ʿahd* = CROWN PRINCEHOOD**). *rāʿūn* — ⭐⭐ the "**SHEPHERDING / caring for**" family — *raʿā* = **to shepherd, to tend, to look after**; ⭐ ***rāʿī* = a SHEPHERD**; ⭐⭐ ***riʿāyah* = CARE** (⭐ ***riʿāyah ṣiḥḥiyyah* = HEALTHCARE**!); ⭐ ***raʿiyyah* = the SUBJECTS / citizens** (the "flock"!).
- **Here it means:** "**And those who are shepherds of their trusts and their covenants…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RĀʿŪN* — SHEPHERDS of their trusts.** ⭐ **Not just "keepers" — SHEPHERDS** — ⭐ **who watch over their flock day and night, guard it from wolves, lead it to water.** ⭐⭐ **That's how the believer treats an *amānah* (trust) or an *ʿahd* (promise): with a shepherd's constant watchful care.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the famous ḥadīth: "*Kullukum rāʿin wa kullukum masʾūlun ʿan raʿiyyatih*" — ⭐ "EACH of you is a SHEPHERD, and each of you is RESPONSIBLE for his flock."** ⭐ **Same root — and *masʾūl* (responsible) is the *saʾala* root of v.1 and v.25!**
  ⭐⭐ ***Amānah* — the Prophet ﷺ was *al-AMĪN*** (the Trustworthy) **before prophethood.** ⭐ **The ḥadīth: "*No faith for one who has no amānah, no religion for one who has no ʿahd.*"** ⭐ **The two are paired here exactly.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***riʿāyah ṣiḥḥiyyah* = HEALTHCARE** (shepherding health!); ⭐ ***maʿhad* = an INSTITUTE**; ⭐ ***muʿāhadah* = a TREATY**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-amānātihim*** — ***lām at-taqwiyah*** (strengthening the participle's object). ⭐ ***rāʿūna*** = active participle, weak-final.
- **Why this exact word:** *rāʿī* (**shepherd**) — ⭐ **makes trustworthiness an active, constant care, not a passive non-betrayal.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RĀʿŪN* = SHEPHERDS of their trusts — watchful, constant care.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥadīth: "EACH of you is a SHEPHERD (*rāʿin*), each RESPONSIBLE (*masʾūl*) for his flock."** ⭐⭐ ***RIʿĀYAH ṢIḤḤIYYAH* = HEALTHCARE.** ⭐ ***Al-AMĪN* = the Prophet's ﷺ title; *maʿhad* = an INSTITUTE; *muʿāhadah* = a TREATY.** ⭐ **"No faith without *amānah*, no religion without *ʿahd*."**

**32. وَالَّذِينَ هُم بِشَهَادَاتِهِمْ قَائِمُونَ — *wa'lladhīna hum bi-shahādātihim qāʾimūn*** · v.33
- **Root feelings:** *shahādāt* — ⭐ **TESTIMONIES** (*shahādah*; ⭐ ***ash-Shahādah*** — the declaration of faith!; ⭐ ***shahādah* = a CERTIFICATE / diploma**!). *qāʾimūn* — ⭐ **STANDING / upholding** (the *q-w-m* root — ⭐ *qiyām*, *mustaqīm*, *taqwīm*, *qayyimah*).
- **Here it means:** "**And those who stand firm in their testimonies…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QĀʾIMŪN* — STANDING by their testimony** — ⭐ **the *q-w-m* root again** (stand → upright). ⭐ **They don't sit on the truth, hide it, or bend it — they STAND it up.** ⭐ **Even against themselves or relatives** (⭐ *kūnū qawwāmīna bi'l-qisṭi shuhadāʾa li'llāhi wa law ʿalā anfusikum*, 4:135 — "**stand firm for justice, witnesses for Allah, even against yourselves**"). ⭐⭐ **The *q-w-m* thread:** *qum* (Muzzammil 73:2, Muddaththir 74:2), *aqwam* (73:6), *istaqāmū* (Jinn 72:16), *taqwīm* (Tīn 95:4), *qayyimah* (Bayyinah 98:5), ⭐ ***qāʾimūn* (Maʿārij 70:33).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Shahādah* — the *sh-h-d* root** — ⭐ **ʿĀdiyāt 100:7** — *wa innahu ʿalā dhālika **la-shahīd*** (man is a WITNESS against himself) ⭐ **Burūj 85:3** (*shāhid wa mashhūd*). ⭐ ***Shahādah* = also a CERTIFICATE** — ⭐ **and THE *Shahādah*** — **the first testimony you uphold.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bi-shahādātihim*** + ***qāʾimūna*** — "**upholding** their testimonies." ⚠️ ⭐ **Recitation variant: *bi-shahādatihim* (singular).**
- **Why this exact word:** *qāʾim* — ⭐ **truth-telling as an upright posture, maintained under pressure.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QĀʾIMŪN* = STANDING UP their testimony — not sitting on it, hiding it, bending it.** ⭐ **4:135: stand firm for justice, even against yourselves.** ⭐⭐ ***Q-w-m* thread: *qum*, *aqwam*, *istaqāmū*, *taqwīm*, *qayyimah*, *qāʾimūn*.** ⭐ ***Shahādah* = a CERTIFICATE — and THE Shahādah.**

**33. وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمْ يُحَافِظُونَ — *wa'lladhīna hum ʿalā ṣalātihim yuḥāfiẓūn*** · v.34
- **Root feeling:** *yuḥāfiẓūn* — ⭐⭐ **Form III of *ḥafiẓa*** = **to GUARD diligently, to preserve carefully, to watch over persistently** — ⭐ ***muḥāfaẓah* = a GOVERNORATE / province** (a region **guarded**!); ⭐ ***muḥāfiẓ* = a GOVERNOR** (and ⭐ ***muḥāfiẓ* = CONSERVATIVE** — one who **preserves**!); ⭐ ***miḥfaẓah* = a WALLET** (where you **keep** things safe!).
- **Here it means:** "**And those who carefully guard their prayers.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE CLOSING BRACKET — the list ends where it began: PRAYER.** ⭐ **v.23** — *alladhīna hum ʿalā ṣalātihim **dāʾimūn*** — **CONSTANT** in prayer. ⭐ **v.34** — *alladhīna hum ʿalā ṣalātihim **yuḥāfiẓūn*** — **GUARDING** prayer. ⭐⭐⭐ **Classical distinction:** ⭐ ***dāʾimūn* = they never MISS it** (constancy — **frequency**); ⭐ ***yuḥāfiẓūn* = they PROTECT its quality** — its times, its conditions, its ablution, its focus (**care**). ⭐ **One is about doing it always; the other is about doing it RIGHT.** ⭐⭐ **And everything between v.23 and v.34 — charity, belief, fear, chastity, trusts, testimony — sits INSIDE the prayer-frame.**
  ⭐⭐ **Form III (*fāʿala*) = sustained, effortful, back-and-forth engagement** — ⭐ **guarding it like a governor guards a province: constantly, actively.** ⭐ ***Muḥāfiẓ* = GOVERNOR**; ⭐ ***miḥfaẓah* = WALLET** — ⭐ **guard your prayers like your wallet.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the list mirrors al-Muʾminūn 23:1–9** — ⭐ **which also opens with prayer (*khāshiʿūn* — humble in it) and closes with prayer (*yuḥāfiẓūn* — guarding it).** ⭐ **Two surahs, the same frame.**
  ⭐ ⭐ **And *ḥ-f-ẓ*: v.29 Form I (guarding chastity) → v.34 Form III (guarding prayer — more intensive).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form III present** (*yuḥāfiẓūna*) — ongoing, effortful. ⭐ ***ʿalā*** = "**over / upon**" (watching over).
- **Why this exact word:** *yuḥāfiẓūn* — ⭐ **closes the list on the quality of prayer, as v.23 opened on its constancy.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **PRAYER BRACKETS THE LIST: v.23 *DĀʾIMŪN* (never MISS it) → v.34 *YUḤĀFIẒŪN* (do it RIGHT).** ⭐ **Frequency + quality.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same frame as al-Muʾminūn 23:1–9.** ⭐⭐ ***MUḤĀFIẒ* = a GOVERNOR / CONSERVATIVE; *MIḤFAẒAH* = a WALLET** — guard prayer like your wallet. ⭐ ***Ḥ-f-ẓ*: chastity (v.29, Form I) → prayer (v.34, Form III).**

**34. أُولَٰئِكَ فِي جَنَّاتٍ مُّكْرَمُونَ — *ulāʾika fī jannātin mukramūn*** · v.35
- **Root feeling:** *mukramūn* — ⭐ the "**HONOUR / generosity**" family — *karuma* = to be noble; ⭐ ***akrama* (Form IV) = to HONOUR someone**; ⭐ ***ikrām* = honouring / hospitality**; ⭐ ***karāmah* = DIGNITY** (*al-karāmah al-insāniyyah* = **human dignity**!); ⭐ **Fajr 89:15** — *fa-**akramahu** wa naʿʿamah*; ⭐ **Fajr 89:17** — *kallā bal lā **tukrimūna**'l-yatīm*!
- **Here it means:** "**Those will be in gardens, honoured.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUKRAMŪN* — HONOURED** — ⭐ **the *ikrām* thread from Fajr completes:** ⭐ **Fajr 89:15** — *fa-**akramahu*** — **Allah HONOURED him (with wealth)** — ⭐ **and he said "my Lord honoured me!" as if he deserved it.** ⭐ **Fajr 89:17** — *kallā bal lā **tukrimūna**'l-yatīm* — ⭐ **"No! You do not HONOUR the orphan!"** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:35** — ***mukramūn*** — ⭐ **those who DID honour others (gave the *ḥaqq*, shepherded trusts) — are HONOURED forever.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Fajr: honoured with wealth, didn't honour the orphan. Maʿārij: honoured others, honoured in Paradise.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the PASSIVE: *mukramūn* — "HONOURED (by Another)"** — ⭐ **they don't honour themselves; they're honoured BY Allah.** ⭐ **The honour of being a guest.** ⭐ ***Ikrām aḍ-ḍayf*** = honouring a guest — ⭐ **they are His guests.**
  ⭐ ***Karāmah* = DIGNITY** — your free hook.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ulāʾika*** = "**those**" (far demonstrative — elevated status). ⭐ ***mukramūna*** = **Form IV passive participle**.
- **Why this exact word:** *mukram* — ⭐ **the reward is honour, matching a life of honouring others.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Fajr 89:15: honoured with wealth (*akramahu*) → 89:17: didn't HONOUR the orphan (*lā tukrimūn*) → Maʿārij 70:35: those who honoured others are *MUKRAMŪN* forever.** ⭐⭐ **PASSIVE = honoured BY Him — His GUESTS.** ⭐ ***Karāmah* = DIGNITY; *ikrām aḍ-ḍayf* = hospitality.**

## §7 — The Scattered Deniers (v.36–41)

**35. فَمَالِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا قِبَلَكَ مُهْطِعِينَ — *fa-māli'lladhīna kafarū qibalaka muhṭiʿīn*** · v.36
- **Root feelings:** *fa-mā li-* — ⭐ **"So what is WRONG with…?"** (⭐ **Muddaththir 74:49** — *fa-mā lahum*; ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:3** — *mā lahā*). *qibal* — ⭐ the "**FACING / in front of**" family — ***qibal* = toward, in the direction of** (⭐ ***qiblah*** = the direction you FACE in prayer!; ⭐ ***qabila* = to ACCEPT**; ⭐ ***istiqbāl* = RECEPTION**). *muhṭiʿīn* — ⭐ **rushing forward with necks STRETCHED OUT and eyes FIXED, staring** — the posture of a frightened or eager crowd running toward something.
- **Here it means:** "**So what is wrong with those who disbelieve, rushing toward you with necks outstretched…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A STRANGE SCENE — the deniers RUSH toward the Prophet ﷺ.** ⭐ **Why?** ⭐ Classical: **they crowded around him to MOCK, to stare, to hear (and ridicule) what he said** — ⭐ **necks craning, eyes fixed (*ihṭāʿ*)** — ⭐ **close to the truth physically, far from it spiritually.** ⭐⭐ ***Fa-mā li-*** — ⭐ **"WHAT'S WRONG with them?"** — **Muddaththir 74:49** asked the same about those **fleeing** like donkeys. ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir: they FLEE from the reminder. Maʿārij: they RUSH toward the Prophet ﷺ — but only to mock.** ⭐ **Running away or running toward — both without faith.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Muhṭiʿīn*** — **Qamar 54:8** — ***muhṭiʿīna ila'd-dāʿ*** — ⭐ **"rushing, necks stretched, toward the CALLER"** — ⭐ **on Judgment Day.** ⭐⭐⭐ **They rush with craned necks toward the Prophet ﷺ to mock (70:36) — and they'll rush with craned necks toward the Caller on the Day (54:8).** ⭐ **The same posture, voluntary now, forced then.**
  ⭐ ***Qibalaka*** — "**toward YOU**" — ⭐ **the *qiblah* root** — ⭐ **they faced him, but not in the way of a *qiblah*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-māli*** — the *lām* is written separately in the muṣḥaf (*fa-māli'lladhīna*). ⭐ ***muhṭiʿīna*** = ***ḥāl*** (Form IV participle).
- **Why this exact word:** *ihṭāʿ* — ⭐ **a vivid posture: craning, staring — close but not open.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUHṬIʿĪN* = necks CRANED, eyes FIXED — rushing toward him to MOCK.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir 74:49–50: they FLEE like donkeys. Maʿārij 70:36: they RUSH toward him — to mock.** ⭐⭐ **Qamar 54:8: same posture toward the CALLER on the Day.** ⭐ ***Qibal* ← *QIBLAH*; *istiqbāl* = RECEPTION.**

**36. عَنِ الْيَمِينِ وَعَنِ الشِّمَالِ عِزِينَ — *ʿani'l-yamīni wa ʿani'sh-shimāli ʿizīn*** · v.37
- **Root feelings:** *yamīn* / *shimāl* — ⭐ **RIGHT / LEFT** (⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:19, 25**!; ⭐ *al-Yaman / ash-Shām* — **Quraysh 106:2**). *ʿizīn* — ⭐ **plural of *ʿizah* = a small GROUP, a cluster, a knot of people** — ⭐ **scattered in separate groups**.
- **Here it means:** "**…from the right and from the left, in scattered groups?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **RIGHT and LEFT — the two hands of the previous surah.** ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:19** — book **in the RIGHT** (saved); **69:25** — book **in the LEFT** (damned). ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:37** — they cluster ***ʿani'l-yamīni wa ʿani'sh-shimāl*** — ⭐ **on the right and the left of the Prophet ﷺ** — ⭐ **standing physically on both sides, but their books will all go to one side.** ⭐⭐ **And Balad 90:18–19** (*maymanah / mashʾamah*), **Quraysh 106:2** (Yemen / Shām). ⭐ **The right-left thread across five surahs.**
  ⭐⭐ ***ʿIzīn* — scattered CLUSTERS** — ⭐ **not one united crowd — little knots of people, each muttering, each mocking** — ⭐ **divided even in their opposition.** ⭐ **Compare Jinn 72:11** — *kunnā ṭarāʾiqa **qidadā*** (we were split into factions) ⭐ **and Zalzalah 99:6** — *yaṣduru'n-nāsu **ashtātā*** (people in scattered groups). ⭐ **Disbelief scatters; faith gathers.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the contrast with v.22–35:** ⭐ **the believers are described ONE BY ONE, trait by trait — a united character.** ⭐ **The deniers are *ʿizīn* — fragments.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿizīna*** = **second *ḥāl***, plural of *ʿizah* (a sound-masculine-style plural of a feminine noun — unusual). ⭐ ***ʿan*** = "**from / on**" (the side).
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿizīn* — ⭐ **pictures disbelief as fragmented clusters around a single centre.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **RIGHT and LEFT of the Prophet ﷺ — like Ḥāqqah 69:19/25's right-hand and left-hand books.** ⭐ **Standing on both sides; their books will all go one way.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿIZĪN* = scattered CLUSTERS — divided even in opposition.** ⭐ **Jinn 72:11 *qidad*, Zalzalah 99:6 *ashtāt*.** ⭐ **Disbelief scatters; faith gathers.**

**37. أَيَطْمَعُ كُلُّ امْرِئٍ مِّنْهُمْ أَن يُدْخَلَ جَنَّةَ نَعِيمٍ — *a-yaṭmaʿu kullu'mriʾin minhum an yudkhala jannata naʿīm*** · v.38
- **Root feelings:** *yaṭmaʿu* — ⭐ **to COVET, crave greedily** (⭐ ***ṭammāʿ*** = greedy; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:15** — *thumma yaṭmaʿu an azīd*!). *kullu'mriʾin* — ⭐ **EVERY single man** (⭐ **Muddaththir 74:52** — *bal yurīdu **kullu'mriʾin** minhum*!). *yudkhala* — **to BE ADMITTED** (passive). *naʿīm* — ⭐ **BLISS** (⭐ **Insān 76:20, Infiṭār 82:13, Muṭaffifīn 83:22, Takāthur 102:8**).
- **Here it means:** "**Does every one of them covet being admitted into a garden of bliss?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NEARLY IDENTICAL to TWO verses in Muddaththir.** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:15** — *thumma **yaṭmaʿu** an azīd* — ⭐ **al-Walīd COVETED MORE (wealth).** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:52** — *bal yurīdu **kullu'mriʾin minhum** an yuʾtā ṣuḥufan munashsharah* — ⭐ **EVERY ONE of them wanted personal scrolls.** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:38** — ***a-yaṭmaʿu kullu'mriʾin minhum*** an yudkhala jannata naʿīm — ⭐ **EVERY ONE of them COVETS Paradise.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Yaṭmaʿu* + *kullu'mriʾin minhum* — BOTH phrases from Muddaththir, fused into one verse.** ⭐ **They covet MORE WEALTH (74:15), PERSONAL SCROLLS (74:52), and now PARADISE (70:38) — all without earning any of it.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ṭamaʿ* = GREED** — ⭐ **not hope (*rajāʾ*), which is paired with effort; GREED — wanting the reward while mocking the path.** ⭐ ⭐ **They think: we're rich and honoured here, so we'll be honoured there** (⭐ **Fajr 89:15** — *rabbī akraman*; ⭐ **Kahf 18:36** — *wa la-in rudidtu ilā rabbī la-ajidanna khayran minhā*). ⭐⭐ **Contrast v.35: the believers are *mukramūn* — honoured — after a life of prayer, giving, trust.** ⭐ **The deniers COVET what the believers EARNED.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Yudkhala* — PASSIVE: "to BE ADMITTED"** — ⭐ **entry is granted, not taken.** ⭐ **Fajr 89:29–30** — *fa'**dkhulī** fī ʿibādī · wa'**dkhulī** jannatī* — ⭐ **the invitation to the content soul — not to the greedy.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***a-*** = rebuking question. ⭐ ***yudkhala*** = **passive subjunctive** (Form IV). ⭐ ***jannata naʿīmin*** — *iḍāfah*.
- **Why this exact word:** *ṭamaʿ* — ⭐ **exposes the delusion: reward expected without the path.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAṬMAʿU* (74:15) + *KULLU'MRIʾIN MINHUM* (74:52) — both Muddaththir phrases fused here.** ⭐⭐⭐ **They COVET: more wealth (74:15), personal scrolls (74:52), PARADISE (70:38) — without earning any.** ⭐⭐ **v.35 believers EARNED *mukramūn*; v.38 deniers COVET it.** ⭐ ***Yudkhala* = PASSIVE — entry is GRANTED.** ⭐ ***Ṭamaʿ* (greed) ≠ *rajāʾ* (hope with effort).**

**38. كَلَّا ۖ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَاهُم مِّمَّا يَعْلَمُونَ — *kallā innā khalaqnāhum mimmā yaʿlamūn*** · v.39
- **Root feelings:** *kallā* — **NO!** *khalaqnāhum* — **We CREATED them**. *mimmā yaʿlamūn* — ⭐ **"from what they KNOW"** (a deliberate euphemism).
- **Here it means:** "**No! Indeed, We created them from what they know.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A CRUSHING ANSWER — through MODESTY.** ⭐ ***Mimmā yaʿlamūn*** — ⭐ **"from what they KNOW"** — ⭐ **the Qurʾān won't even NAME it** — ⭐ **the despised drop of fluid** (⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:6** — *māʾin dāfiq* (gushing fluid); ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:19** — *min nuṭfah*; ⭐ **Mursalāt 77:20** — *māʾin mahīn* (a despised fluid); ⭐ **Insān 76:2** — *nuṭfatin amshāj*). ⭐⭐ **The *euphemism* is itself the insult:** ⭐ **"you arrogant men who think you've EARNED Paradise by birth and rank — you came from something you'd be embarrassed to name."** ⭐ **Your origin doesn't qualify you; only your deeds do.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And note: *kallā* answers v.38's GREED:** ⭐ **"Do they covet Paradise? — NO!"** ⭐ **The basis of their claim (pedigree, wealth, status) is demolished by reminding them of their humble origin.** ⭐ **Classical: noble lineage means nothing — all came from the same fluid.**
  ⭐ ⭐ **Compare Ṭāriq 86:5** — ***fal-yanẓuri'l-insānu mimma khuliq*** — ⭐ **"let man LOOK at what he was created FROM."** ⭐ **Maʿārij doesn't tell them to look — they already KNOW.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mimmā*** = *min* + *mā* (relative — "**from what**"). ⭐ ***yaʿlamūna*** — ⭐ **the object left unnamed = euphemism (*kināyah*).**
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ ***kināyah* (euphemism) as rebuke** — **unnamed, and therefore more humiliating.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIMMĀ YAʿLAMŪN* = "from what they KNOW" — the Qurʾān won't even NAME it.** ⭐ **The euphemism IS the insult.** ⭐⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:6 *māʾ dāfiq*, ʿAbasa 80:19 *nuṭfah*, Insān 76:2 *nuṭfatin amshāj*.** ⭐⭐ ***KALLĀ* answers GREED: your ORIGIN doesn't qualify you.** ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:5: "LOOK at what you were created from."**

**39. فَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِرَبِّ الْمَشَارِقِ وَالْمَغَارِبِ — *fa-lā uqsimu bi-rabbi'l-mashāriqi wa'l-maghārib*** · v.40
- **Root feelings:** *mashāriq* — ⭐ **plural of *mashriq* = EASTS / places of sunrise**. *maghārib* — ⭐ **plural of *maghrib* = WESTS / places of sunset** (⭐ ***al-Maghrib*** = Morocco; ⭐ *Ṣalāt al-Maghrib*).
- **Here it means:** "**So I swear by the Lord of the Easts and the Wests…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SINGULAR-DUAL-PLURAL PUZZLE — solved by astronomy.** ⭐ The Qurʾān uses **ALL THREE** numbers for East and West:
  - ⭐ **SINGULAR — Muzzammil 73:9** — *rabbu'l-**mashriqi** wa'l-**maghrib*** — ⭐ **the EAST and the WEST** (the two **directions**).
  - ⭐ **DUAL — Raḥmān 55:17** — *rabbu'l-**mashriqayni** wa rabbu'l-**maghribayn*** — ⭐ **the TWO easts and TWO wests** (the two **extreme** points of sunrise — at the **summer and winter solstices**).
  - ⭐ **PLURAL — Maʿārij 70:40** — *bi-rabbi'l-**mashāriqi** wa'l-**maghārib*** — ⭐ **ALL the easts and wests** (the sun rises at a **slightly different point EVERY DAY** — some 365 sunrise-points across the year!).
  ⭐⭐⭐ **No contradiction — three zoom levels of the same sky:** ⭐ **the general direction (1), the two solstice extremes (2), every single day's point (many).** ⭐ **The Arabs, watching the horizon in the desert, knew the sun "walks" along it through the year.**
  ⭐⭐ **And why HERE?** ⭐ **v.41: "We are ABLE to REPLACE them with better."** ⭐ **The One who moves the sunrise to a new point every single day — can easily replace a people.** ⭐ **Constant renewal in the sky = power to renew humanity.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Fa-lā uqsimu*** — ⭐ **the oath-opener** (Ḥāqqah 69:38, Qiyāmah 75:1, Takwīr 81:15, Inshiqāq 84:16, Balad 90:1).
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ ***mashāriq* / *maghārib*** = **DIPTOTES** (*mafāʿil* pattern) — here genitive with kasrah because of *al-*. ⭐ ***lā uqsimu*** — emphatic *lā*.
- **Why this exact word:** **the plural** — ⭐ **pictures daily, ceaseless change, which grounds v.41's claim of power to replace.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **SINGULAR (73:9) = the DIRECTIONS. DUAL (55:17) = the two SOLSTICE extremes. PLURAL (70:40) = EVERY DAY'S sunrise point (~365).** ⭐ **Three zoom levels — no contradiction.** ⭐⭐ **He moves the sunrise DAILY → He can replace a people (v.41).** ⚠️ **Diptotes.** ⭐ ***Al-Maghrib* = MOROCCO.**

**40. إِنَّا لَقَادِرُونَ · عَلَىٰ أَن نُّبَدِّلَ خَيْرًا مِّنْهُمْ وَمَا نَحْنُ بِمَسْبُوقِينَ — *innā la-qādirūn · ʿalā an nubaddila khayran minhum wa mā naḥnu bi-masbūqīn*** · v.40–41
- **Root feelings:** *qādirūn* — ⭐ **ABLE / powerful** (the *qadr* root — POWER sense; ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:4** — *bālā **qādirīna** ʿalā an nusawwiya banānah*!). *nubaddila* — ⭐ the "**REPLACING / exchanging**" family — *badala* = to replace; ⭐ ***badal* = a SUBSTITUTE** (⭐ ***badal* in GRAMMAR = APPOSITION**!; ⭐ ***badlah* = a SUIT** (a "change" of clothes!); ⭐ *tabdīl* = changing). *masbūqīn* — ⭐ the "**OUTRUNNING / preceding**" family — *sabaqa* = to outrun, to go ahead; ⭐ ***musābaqah* = a RACE / COMPETITION**; ⭐ ***as-sābiqāt* — Nāziʿāt 79:4** (those who **race ahead**); ⭐ ***masbūq* = OUTRUN / beaten** (⭐ and in fiqh, ***masbūq* = a LATECOMER to congregational prayer**!).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, We are able to replace them with better than them, and We are not to be outrun.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *QĀDIRŪN* echo — from Qiyāmah.** ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:4** — *bālā **qādirīna** ʿalā an nusawwiya banānah* — ⭐ **"Yes, We are ABLE to restore his very FINGERTIPS."** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:40–41** — *innā la-**qādirūna** ʿalā an nubaddila khayran minhum* — ⭐ **"We are ABLE to REPLACE them with better."** ⭐⭐ ***Qādirūna ʿalā an…*** — **identical construction.** ⭐ **Rebuild the fingertip; replace a whole people — both within His power.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Nubaddila khayran minhum* — REPLACE them with BETTER** — ⭐ **and history did exactly that: the mocking chiefs of Quraysh were replaced by the generation of the Companions** — ⭐ **who carried the message to the ends of the earth.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Wa mā naḥnu bi-MASBŪQĪN* — "We are not OUTRUN"** — ⭐ **no one can race ahead of Him, escape Him, beat Him to the finish.** ⭐ **Compare Jinn 72:12** — *lan nuʿjiza'llāha fi'l-arḍi wa lan nuʿjizahu **harabā*** (can't escape Him by FLIGHT). ⭐ ⭐ ***Masbūq*** in fiqh = **a LATECOMER to the jamāʿah** — ⭐ **the one who arrives after the imam started** — ⭐ **Allah is never a *masbūq*.**
  ⭐ Grammar hook: ⭐ ***badal* = APPOSITION** — ⭐ **the grammar term you've used (e.g., Quraysh 106:2) — same root as "replace."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***inna* + *lām*** (*la-qādirūna*) = emphasis. ⭐ ***khayran*** = object ("**better ones**"). ⚠️ ⭐ ***bi-masbūqīna*** — **extra *bi-* after negation** (emphasis — like *bi-majnūn*, 81:22).
- **Why this exact word:** *masbūq* — ⭐ **a race image: no one gets ahead of Him.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QĀDIRŪNA ʿALĀ AN…* — Qiyāmah 75:4 (restore FINGERTIPS) & Maʿārij 70:40–41 (REPLACE a people).** ⭐⭐ **History: mocking chiefs REPLACED by the Companions.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Mā naḥnu bi-MASBŪQĪN* = We are NOT OUTRUN.** ⭐ ***Musābaqah* = a RACE; *masbūq* = a LATECOMER to prayer.** ⭐⭐ ***BADAL* = SUBSTITUTE / grammatical APPOSITION; *badlah* = a SUIT.** ⚠️ **Extra *bi-* after negation.**

## §8 — Leave Them (v.42–44)

**41. فَذَرْهُمْ يَخُوضُوا وَيَلْعَبُوا — *fa-dharhum yakhūḍū wa yalʿabū*** · v.42
- **Root feelings:** *dhar* — ⭐ **LEAVE (them)** — the **DEFECTIVE verb *wadhara*** (no past tense in use; ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:11** *dharnī*; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:11** *dharnī*!). *yakhūḍū* — ⭐ the "**WADING into water / plunging into talk**" family — ***khawḍ*** (⭐ **Muddaththir 74:45** — *wa kunnā **nakhūḍu** maʿa'l-khāʾiḍīn*!). *yalʿabū* — ⭐ the "**PLAY**" family — *laʿiba* = to play; ⭐ ***laʿib* = PLAY, games**; ⭐ ***malʿab* = a STADIUM / playground**; ⭐ ***luʿbah* = a TOY / a game**; ⭐ ***laʿb* (*luʿāb*) = SALIVA** (drooling — a child's play!).
- **Here it means:** "**So leave them to wade [in falsehood] and play…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO CROSS-SURAH PAYOFFS IN THREE WORDS.**
  ⭐ **ONE — *DHAR* (leave them):** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:11** — *wa **dharnī** wa'l-mukadhdhibīn* — ⭐ "**leave Me with the deniers**." ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:11** — ***dharnī** wa man khalaqtu waḥīdā* — ⭐ "**leave Me with the one I created alone**." ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:42** — ***fa-dharhum*** — ⭐ "**so LEAVE THEM**." ⭐⭐⭐ **Muzzammil & Muddaththir: "leave [them] to ME." Maʿārij: "leave THEM [be]."** ⭐ **The same defective verb — from "hand them over" to "let them go."**
  ⭐ **TWO — *KHAWḌ* (wading):** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:45** — *wa kunnā **nakhūḍu** maʿa'l-khāʾiḍīn* — ⭐ **the CONFESSION from Saqar: "we used to WADE with the waders."** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:42** — ***yakhūḍū*** — ⭐ **"let them WADE"** — ⭐⭐⭐ **Maʿārij shows them wading NOW; Muddaththir hears them confess it LATER.** ⭐ **Same verb — present permission, future confession.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Yalʿabū* — PLAY** — ⭐ **like children** — ⭐ **mocking the truth as a game.** ⭐ ***Malʿab* = a STADIUM** — ⭐ **life treated as a playground** (⭐ *innamā'l-ḥayātu'd-dunyā **laʿibun** wa lahw*, 47:36).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***dhar*** = imperative of a **defective verb**. ⭐⭐ ***yakhūḍū / yalʿabū*** = **JUSSIVE** (the *nūn* dropped) — ⭐ **because they're the "answer" (*jawāb*) of the command *dharhum*** ("leave them [and they will] wade and play").
- **Why this exact word:** *khawḍ* + *laʿib* — ⭐ **wading deeper into falsehood, treating it as a game — until v.42's end.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHAR*: Muzzammil 73:11 & Muddaththir 74:11 "leave them TO ME" → Maʿārij 70:42 "LEAVE THEM [be]."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KHAWḌ*: Maʿārij 70:42 — WADING now → Muddaththir 74:45 — CONFESSING it from Saqar.** ⭐⭐ ***Laʿib* = PLAY; *malʿab* = a STADIUM** — life as a playground. ⚠️ **Jussive (answer of the command).**

**42. حَتَّىٰ يُلَاقُوا يَوْمَهُمُ الَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ — *ḥattā yulāqū yawmahumu'lladhī yūʿadūn*** · v.42
- **Root feelings:** *yulāqū* — ⭐ the "**MEETING face to face**" family — *lāqā* (Form III) = to meet, encounter; ⭐ ***liqāʾ* = a MEETING / an INTERVIEW** (⭐ *liqāʾ ṣaḥafī* = a **press interview**!); ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:6** — *fa-**mulāqīh***; ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:20** — *annī **mulāqin** ḥisābiyah*. *yūʿadūn* — ⭐ **they are PROMISED / threatened** (*waʿd*; ⭐ ***mawʿid* = an APPOINTMENT**).
- **Here it means:** "**…until they meet their Day which they are promised.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥattā* — "UNTIL"** — ⭐ **the play has an END-POINT.** ⭐ **The wading and playing continue — UNTIL the meeting.** ⭐⭐ ***Yulāqū* — Form III = a FACE-TO-FACE MEETING** — ⭐ **like an appointment you can't skip.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *liqāʾ* thread:** ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:6** — *fa-**mulāqīh*** — **you WILL meet Him.** ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:20** — *innī ẓanantu annī **mulāqin** ḥisābiyah* — ⭐ **the righteous man: "I KNEW I'd meet my account."** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:42** — ***yulāqū** yawmahum* — ⭐ **the deniers will meet it — without having prepared.** ⭐⭐ **One expected the meeting (69:20); they'll stumble into it (70:42).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Yawmahum* — "THEIR Day"** — ⭐ **personal: the Day assigned to THEM.** ⭐ ***Alladhī yūʿadūn*** — **"which they are PROMISED"** — ⭐ **repeated as the surah's last words (v.44)** — ⭐ **a bookend.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ḥattā*** + subjunctive (*yulāqū*, nūn dropped) = "**until**." ⭐ ***yūʿadūna*** = passive present.
- **Why this exact word:** *liqāʾ* — ⭐ **makes the Day an unavoidable personal appointment.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤATTĀ* = UNTIL — the play has an END-POINT.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Liqāʾ*: Inshiqāq 84:6 (you WILL meet Him), Ḥāqqah 69:20 (he KNEW he'd meet it), Maʿārij 70:42 (they'll meet it UNPREPARED).** ⭐ ***Liqāʾ ṣaḥafī* = a PRESS INTERVIEW; *mawʿid* = an APPOINTMENT.** ⭐ ***Yawmahum* = THEIR day.**

**43. يَوْمَ يَخْرُجُونَ مِنَ الْأَجْدَاثِ سِرَاعًا — *yawma yakhrujūna mina'l-ajdāthi sirāʿā*** · v.43
- **Root feelings:** *yakhrujūn* — **they COME OUT** (*kharaja*; ⭐ *khurūj* = exit; ⭐ ***makhraj* = an EXIT** — and ⭐ in tajwīd, ***makhraj* = the POINT OF ARTICULATION** of a letter!). *ajdāth* — ⭐ **GRAVES** (plural of *jadath*; ⭐ **Yāsīn 36:51** — *fa-idhā hum mina'l-**ajdāthi** ilā rabbihim yansilūn*). *sirāʿ* — ⭐ the "**SPEED / haste**" family — ***sarīʿ* = FAST**; ⭐ ***surʿah* = SPEED** (⭐ *surʿat aḍ-ḍawʾ* = the **speed of light**!); ⭐ ***sirāʿan* = hastening, racing**.
- **Here it means:** "**The Day they will emerge from the graves, rushing…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They WADED and PLAYED slowly (v.42) — now they RUSH (v.43).** ⭐ ***Sirāʿan* = at SPEED** — ⭐ **no more leisure, no more games — racing out of the graves.** ⭐⭐ ***Ajdāth* — a word for GRAVES** — ⭐ **the *qabr* thread you own (ʿAbasa 80:21, Infiṭār 82:4, ʿĀdiyāt 100:9, Takāthur 102:2) — here a different word, *jadath*.** ⭐ **Yāsīn 36:51** — ⭐ **same word, same scene: "from the GRAVES to their Lord, they RACE."**
  ⭐⭐ **And Zalzalah 99:6** — *yawmaʾidhin **yaṣduru'n-nāsu** ashtātā* — ⭐ **people ISSUING FORTH in scattered groups.** ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:18** — *fa-taʾtūna afwājā* (you come in CROWDS). ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:43** — ⭐ **RUSHING out.** ⭐ **Three pictures of the emergence: scattered, in crowds, at speed.**
  ⭐ Tajwīd hook: ⭐ ***makhraj* = the POINT OF ARTICULATION** — **same root as "come out."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yawma*** = *badal* of *yawmahum* (v.42) — **specifying the Day.** ⭐ ***sirāʿan*** = *ḥāl* (plural of *sarīʿ*).
- **Why this exact word:** *sirāʿ* — ⭐ **the leisurely players forced into a sprint.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They WADED and PLAYED slowly (v.42) → they RUSH out of the graves (*sirāʿā*, v.43).** ⭐⭐ ***AJDĀTH* = GRAVES — Yāsīn 36:51 (same scene).** ⭐⭐ **Emergence: SCATTERED (99:6), in CROWDS (78:18), at SPEED (70:43).** ⭐ ***Surʿat aḍ-ḍawʾ* = SPEED OF LIGHT; *makhraj* = tajwīd ARTICULATION POINT.**

**44. كَأَنَّهُمْ إِلَىٰ نُصُبٍ يُوفِضُونَ — *ka-annahum ilā nuṣubin yūfiḍūn*** · v.43
- **Root feelings:** *nuṣub* — ⭐⭐ the "**SETTING UP / erecting**" family — *naṣaba* = **to set up, to erect, to plant upright**; ⭐ ***nuṣub* = an ERECTED STONE** — classically: **(a) an IDOL-STONE / altar they rushed to in pre-Islamic rituals; (b) a TARGET-POST / a raised banner** that racers dash toward; ⭐ ***nuṣb tidhkārī* = a MEMORIAL MONUMENT** (modern!); ⭐ ***manṣib* = a POSITION / post** (a job title!); ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:19** — *wa ila'l-jibāli kayfa **nuṣibat*** (mountains SET UP!); ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:3** — *ʿāmilatun **nāṣibah*** (toiling, WEARIED — standing exhausted!); ⭐ **Sharḥ 94:7** — *fa-idhā faraghta **fa'nṣab*** (stand up and STRIVE!). *yūfiḍūn* — ⭐ **to RACE / hurry toward a goal** (from *w-f-ḍ*) — ⚠️ **NOT *afāḍa* (f-y-ḍ, "pour forth / depart from ʿArafāt")**.
- **Here it means:** "**…as if they were racing toward an erected idol / a target-post.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE IRONY OF THE SIMILE — they rush like they used to rush to their IDOLS.** ⭐ Classical: **in the pre-Islamic era, people RACED toward the *nuṣub* (idol-stones) at dawn, competing to be first to touch them** — ⭐ **so the very eagerness they once showed toward false gods — they'll show on the Day, racing toward the Caller.** ⭐⭐ **Or the image of a RACE: competitors sprinting toward the finishing POST (*nuṣub*)** — ⭐ **but this race has no prize.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The *n-ṣ-b* root — FOUR places in your study:** ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:19 — mountains *nuṣibat* (SET UP firm).** ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:3 — faces *nāṣibah* (WEARIED, toiling).** ⭐ **Sharḥ 94:7 — *fa'nṣab* (stand up and STRIVE).** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:43 — racing to a *nuṣub* (an erected STONE).** ⭐⭐ **Set up, wearied, striving, an idol-stone — all from "standing something upright."** ⭐ **Sharḥ: STRIVE (*inṣab*) toward your Lord in dunya — or race to a *nuṣub* on the Day.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***manṣib* = a POSITION / job**; ⭐ ***nuṣb tidhkārī* = a MONUMENT**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ka-annahum*** = "**as if they**." ⭐ ***yūfiḍūna*** = Form IV present. ⚠️ ⭐ **Recitation variant: *naṣbin* (singular-looking).**
- **Why this exact word:** *nuṣub* — ⭐ **turns their old idolatrous eagerness into the image of their terrified rush.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They RACE like they used to race to their IDOL-STONES (*nuṣub*) at dawn.** ⭐ **Old eagerness for false gods → forced haste on the Day.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***N-ṣ-b*: mountains SET UP (88:19), faces WEARIED (88:3), STRIVE! (94:7), an idol-STONE (70:43).** ⭐ ***Manṣib* = a POSITION; *nuṣb tidhkārī* = a MONUMENT.** ⚠️ ***Yūfiḍūn* (w-f-ḍ) ≠ *afāḍa* (f-y-ḍ, ʿArafāt).**

**45. خَاشِعَةً أَبْصَارُهُمْ تَرْهَقُهُمْ ذِلَّةٌ — *khāshiʿatan abṣāruhum tarhaquhum dhillah*** · v.44
- **Root feelings:** *khāshiʿah* — ⭐ the "**HUMBLED LOWERING**" family — *khashaʿa* = **to be lowered, subdued, humbled** (of the gaze, the voice, the heart); ⭐⭐ ***khushūʿ* = HUMILITY / focus IN PRAYER** (everyday Islamic vocabulary!). *abṣār* — ⭐ **EYES / gazes** (*baṣar*). *tarhaquhum* — ⭐ **OVERTAKES / covers them** (the *rahaq* root — ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:41**, **Jinn 72:6, 13**, **Muddaththir 74:17**). *dhillah* — ⭐ the "**HUMILIATION / lowliness**" family — *dhalla* = to be abased; ⭐ ***dhull* = HUMILIATION**; ⭐ ***dhalīl* = humiliated**; ⭐ **but also *dhalūl* = DOCILE, tame** (of a riding animal!; ⭐ **Insān 76:14** — *wa **dhullilat** quṭūfuhā* — fruit made **EASY / tame**!).
- **Here it means:** "**Their eyes humbled, humiliation covering them.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NEARLY IDENTICAL to Nāziʿāt 79:9.** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:9** — ***abṣāruhā khāshiʿah*** — ⭐ **"its EYES HUMBLED"** (the hearts on that Day). ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:44** — ***khāshiʿatan abṣāruhum*** — ⭐ **"their EYES HUMBLED."** ⭐⭐ **Same words, reversed order.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And *KHUSHŪʿ* — the word you use for FOCUS and humility IN PRAYER** — ⭐ **al-Muʾminūn 23:2** — *alladhīna hum fī ṣalātihim **khāshiʿūn*** (the believers are HUMBLE in their prayer). ⭐⭐⭐ **The believers chose *khushūʿ* in PRAYER; the deniers are FORCED into *khushūʿ* on the Day.** ⭐ **Lower your gaze before Him now — or have it lowered for you then.** ⭐ **The same humility — voluntary and rewarded, or forced and useless.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Tarhaquhum dhillah* — "HUMILIATION OVERTAKES them"** — ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:41** — ***tarhaquhā qatarah*** — ⭐ "**DARKNESS OVERTAKES them [their faces]**." ⭐⭐ **Same verb (*tarhaqu*) — ʿAbasa: darkness covers the faces; Maʿārij: humiliation covers them.** ⭐ **The *rahaq* thread: burden added (Jinn 72:6), no burden for believers (72:13), a steep climb imposed (74:17), darkness (80:41), humiliation (70:44).**
  ⭐⭐ **And the arc of the surah: they ran AROUND the Prophet ﷺ with necks CRANED and eyes STARING (*muhṭiʿīn*, v.36) — ⭐ now their eyes are LOWERED (*khāshiʿah*).** ⭐ **Proud staring → forced lowering.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***khāshiʿatan*** = ***ḥāl***, feminine (agreeing with the broken plural *abṣār*), **FRONTED before its subject** (*abṣāruhum*). ⭐ ***tarhaquhum dhillatun*** = verbal clause, *ḥāl*.
- **Why this exact word:** *khushūʿ* — ⭐ **the prayer-word, forced on those who refused prayer.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= Nāziʿāt 79:9 (*abṣāruhā khāshiʿah*), reversed.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KHUSHŪʿ* = HUMILITY IN PRAYER (23:2) — the believers CHOSE it; the deniers are FORCED into it.** ⭐ **Lower your gaze now, or have it lowered then.** ⭐⭐ ***TARHAQUHUM*: ʿAbasa 80:41 (DARKNESS covers) → Maʿārij 70:44 (HUMILIATION covers).** ⭐⭐ **v.36 eyes STARING → v.44 eyes LOWERED.** ⭐ ***Dhalūl* = DOCILE; Insān 76:14 *dhullilat* = made easy.**

**46. ذَٰلِكَ الْيَوْمُ الَّذِي كَانُوا يُوعَدُونَ — *dhālika'l-yawmu'lladhī kānū yūʿadūn*** · v.44
- **Root feelings:** *dhālika* — ⭐ **THAT** (far demonstrative). *yūʿadūn* — **they were PROMISED** (passive).
- **Here it means:** "**That is the Day which they were being promised.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BOOKEND — the surah ends where it began.** ⭐ **v.1** — *saʾala sāʾilun bi-ʿadhābin **wāqiʿ*** — ⭐ **a man MOCKINGLY demanded the punishment.** ⭐ **v.44** — ***dhālika'l-yawmu'lladhī kānū yūʿadūn*** — ⭐ **"THAT is the Day they were promised"** — ⭐ **here it is.** ⭐⭐ **And v.42 said: *yawmahumu'lladhī yūʿadūn*** (their Day which they are promised) — ⭐ **now (v.44): *kānū yūʿadūn*** — ⭐ **"they WERE promised" — the promise is past; the Day is present.** ⭐ **The tense shift is the arrival.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Dhālika* — "THAT"** — ⭐ **the far demonstrative** — ⭐ **pointing at the Day as something massive, standing apart.** ⭐ **Compare Nabaʾ 78:39** — ***dhālika'l-yawmu'l-ḥaqq*** — "**THAT is the TRUE Day**." ⭐ **Nabaʾ: "that is the TRUE Day." Maʿārij: "that is the PROMISED Day."** ⭐ **And Ḥāqqah: *al-Ḥāqqah* — the TRUE one.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Kānū yūʿadūn*** — ⭐ **past continuous: "they used to be promised — again and again"** — ⭐ **every warning they mocked.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***dhālika*** (subject) + ***al-yawmu*** (predicate / badal). ⭐ ***kānū yūʿadūna*** = **habitual past passive**.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **closing on the promise fulfilled — the answer to v.1's mocking request.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **BOOKEND: v.1 a mocker DEMANDS the punishment → v.44 "THAT is the Day they were promised."** ⭐⭐ **v.42 *yūʿadūn* (ARE promised) → v.44 *KĀNŪ yūʿadūn* (WERE promised) — the tense shift IS the arrival.** ⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:39: "that is the TRUE Day." Maʿārij 70:44: "that is the PROMISED Day."**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **هَلُوعًا (v.19)** — ⚠️ **DEFINED by the next two verses** (*jazūʿ* + *manūʿ*). ⭐ **Don't translate it independently; let v.20–21 define it.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمْ (v.23) vs عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ (Māʿūn 107:5)** — ⚠️ ***ʿalā* (planted ON it) vs *ʿan* (drifting AWAY).** ⭐ **One preposition, two destinies.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْمَشَارِقِ وَالْمَغَارِبِ (v.40)** — ⚠️ **PLURAL** — ⭐ **singular in 73:9, dual in 55:17. No contradiction: three zoom levels.**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْمَحْرُومِ (v.25)** — ⚠️ **the poor who does NOT ask**, ⭐ **distinct from *as-sāʾil* (who asks).**
5. ⭐⭐⭐ **مُّشْفِقُونَ (v.27)** — ⚠️ **TENDER, caring fear** (from *shafaq*, twilight) — ⭐ **NOT panic (*halaʿ*).**
6. ⭐⭐ **يُوفِضُونَ (v.43)** — ⚠️ **from *w-f-ḍ* (to race)**, ⭐ **NOT *afāḍa* (f-y-ḍ — departing ʿArafāt).**
7. ⭐⭐ **حَمِيمٌ حَمِيمًا (v.10)** — ⚠️ **here = FRIENDS**, ⭐ **not scalding water (Nabaʾ 78:25).**
8. ⭐⭐ **يَخُوضُوا وَيَلْعَبُوا (v.42)** — ⚠️ **JUSSIVE** (nūn dropped) — **answer to the command *dharhum*.**
9. ⭐⭐ **مَسْبُوقِينَ (v.41)** — ⚠️ **extra *bi-* after negation** (*bi-masbūqīn*) for emphasis. ⭐ **And *masbūq* in fiqh = a latecomer to prayer.**
10. ⭐⭐ **نَزَّاعَةً (v.16)** — ⚠️ **two recitations: *nazzāʿatan* (accusative ḥāl) / *nazzāʿatun* (nominative).**
11. ⭐⭐ **يَوْمِئِذٍ (v.11)** — ⚠️ **recitation variant *yawmaʾidhin*.**
12. ⭐⭐ **خَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ (v.4)** — ⚠️ ***alf* + SINGULAR genitive** (*sanah*, not *sanawāt*).
13. ⭐⭐ **الْمَعَارِجِ · الْمَشَارِقِ · الْمَغَارِبِ** — ⚠️ **DIPTOTE patterns** (*mafāʿil*) — kasrah only because of *al-*.
14. ⭐ **ذِي (v.3)** — ⚠️ **the five-noun *dhū/dhā/dhī*** (genitive).
15. ⭐ **فَمَالِ (v.36)** — ⚠️ **the *lām* is written SEPARATELY** in the muṣḥaf (*fa-māli'lladhīna*).
16. ⭐ **بِشَهَادَاتِهِمْ (v.33)** — ⚠️ **variant *bi-shahādatihim* (singular).**
17. ⭐ **مَا مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَانُهُمْ (v.30)** — ⚠️ **a historical legal category**, understood in its time and regulated toward reduction.
