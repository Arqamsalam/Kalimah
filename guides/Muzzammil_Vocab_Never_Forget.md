# Surah Al-Muzzammil (73) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–20) · **Complete coverage — every important word**

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**Nineteen short verses give a heavy command — and then one enormous verse lifts it.**

⭐ **v.1–19:** short, rhythmic verses, nearly all ending in **-īlā** (*qalīlā, tartīlā, thaqīlā, qīlā, ṭawīlā, tabtīlā, wakīlā, jamīlā…*). ⭐ The command: **stand most of the night in prayer, reciting the Qurʾān slowly.**

⭐ **v.20:** one of the longest verses in this part of the Qurʾān — ⭐ and it **eases** the command: ⭐ *"He knows you cannot keep exact count of it, so He has **turned to you in mercy** — so recite **what is easy** of the Qurʾān."*

⭐⭐ **It is reported (from ʿĀʾishah, in Muslim) that Allah held back the end of this surah for about a year**, during which the Prophet ﷺ and companions prayed through the nights until their feet swelled — ⭐ **then v.20 came down with the relief.** ⭐ Many scholars consider v.20 Madinan, since it mentions fighting in Allah's way.

⭐⭐⭐ **The surah teaches by STRUCTURE: first the training, then the mercy.** ⭐ And the key word of the relief is ***tayassara*** — "**what is EASY**" — ⭐ **the *yusr* root you traced through ʿAbasa, Aʿlā, Layl, and Sharḥ** (*inna maʿa'l-ʿusri yusrā*). ⭐ **Hardship (v.1–19), then ease (v.20) — Sharḥ 94:5–6, enacted.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **19 verses of HARD night prayer → 1 verse of EASE.** ⭐ **One hardship, then ease — the Sharḥ promise, lived out.**

## The Big Picture

*⭐ Context: after the first revelation at Ḥirāʾ, the Prophet ﷺ returned trembling and said to Khadījah, "**Zammilūnī, zammilūnī**" — "**Wrap me up! Wrap me up!**" ⭐ This surah's first word answers that moment — **addressing him by the very state he was in.***

1. **The address** (v.1) — *O you wrapped in garments!*
2. **The night prayer** (v.2–4) — half the night, a little less or more; recite slowly.
3. **Why the night** (v.5–7) — a heavy Word is coming; night is firmer and more upright; the day is for swimming through tasks.
4. **Devotion** (v.8–9) — remember His name, devote yourself wholly; take Him as your Trustee.
5. **Patience** (v.10–11) — bear what they say; leave them beautifully; leave the deniers to Me.
6. **What awaits** (v.12–14) — shackles, blaze, choking food — the day the mountains become sliding sand.
7. **The Pharaoh precedent** (v.15–16) — a witness sent to you, as to Pharaoh.
8. **The Day that greys children** (v.17–19) — and *this is a reminder*.
9. **The relief** (v.20) — recite what is easy; pray, give, lend to Allah, seek forgiveness.

## §1 — The Address (v.1)

**1. يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُزَّمِّلُ — *yā ayyuha'l-muzzammil*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** the "**WRAPPING oneself up in clothing**" family — *zammala* = to wrap; ***tazammala* (Form V) = to wrap ONESELF in garments**; *zimmal* = a bundle wrapped up.
- **Here it means:** "**O you who wraps himself [in garments]!**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **He isn't called by NAME — he's called by his STATE.** ⭐ When the Prophet ﷺ came home from Ḥirāʾ shaking and said "***zammilūnī***" ("wrap me up!"), ⭐ **Allah addresses him with that exact word: *al-muzzammil*, "the wrapped one."** ⭐⭐ Arabs addressed someone by their current state **as affection** — like calling a sleeping child "**O little sleeper**." ⭐ It's **tender**, not a rebuke: ⭐ *I saw you wrapped up, frightened — now rise.* ⭐⭐⭐ And its twin is the very next surah: **Muddaththir 74:1** — *yā ayyuha'l-**muddaththir*** — "**O you covered in a cloak**." ⭐ **Two surahs, two garment-words, two first commands: rise to PRAY (73:2), rise to WARN (74:2).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ **Form V with ASSIMILATION:** *mutazammil* → ***muzzammil*** — ⭐ **the *tāʾ* of Form V melts into the *zāy* and doubles it.** ⭐ **The SAME rule you learned in ʿAbasa 80:3–4** (*yatazakkā → yazzakkā*, *yatadhakkaru → yadhdhakkaru*). ⭐ And Muddaththir 74:1 (*mutadaththir → muddaththir*) does it again.
- **Why this exact word:** *muzzammil* — ⭐ **meets him in the exact moment he was in, before lifting him out of it.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"*ZAMMILŪNĪ!*" — "wrap me up!" after Ḥirāʾ → Allah calls him "*al-MUZZAMMIL*", the wrapped one.** ⭐ **Called by his STATE, with tenderness.** ⭐⭐ ***Mutazammil → muzzammil* — Form V assimilation (like ʿAbasa's *yazzakkā*).** ⭐ **Twin: Muddaththir 74:1.**

## §2 — The Night Prayer (v.2–4)

**2. قُمِ اللَّيْلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا — *qumi'l-layla illā qalīlā*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *qum* — the "**STANDING**" family — *qāma* = to stand; ***qiyām* = standing (in prayer)**; ⭐ ***qiyām al-layl* = the NIGHT PRAYER** (literally "standing the night"!); ⭐ *istaqāma*, *mustaqīm* (the straight path). *qalīl* — **little, few** (⭐ *aqalliyyah* = a minority; **Jinn 72:24** *aqallu ʿadadā*).
- **Here it means:** "**Stand [in prayer through] the night — except a little.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The first command is to STAND.** ⭐ ***Qum*** — "rise up." ⭐ **The one who was lying wrapped (v.1) is told to get UP.** ⭐⭐ And ***qumi'l-layl*** — "**stand the night**" — is literally the origin of ***qiyām al-layl***, the term you use for night prayer. ⭐ **You stand the night, not just pray in it.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir 74:2 begins the same way: *qum fa-andhir* — "rise and WARN."** ⭐ **Two *qum*s: rise in private to worship (73:2), rise in public to warn (74:2).** ⭐ **The private standing comes first in the Qurʾān's order — the night builds the man who warns by day.**
  ⭐ And ***illā qalīlā*** — "**except a little**" — ⭐ the **flexibility is built in from verse 2**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***qum*** = imperative of a hollow verb (the long *ū* drops). ⚠️ ⭐ ***qumi*** — a **kasrah** added to avoid two sukūns before *al-layl*. ⭐ *al-layla* = **accusative of time** ("through the night").
- **Why this exact word:** *qum* (**stand**) rather than *ṣalli* (pray) — ⭐ **names the physical act of rising from sleep — the hardest part.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QIYĀM AL-LAYL* = "STANDING the night"** — the term comes from THIS verse. ⭐⭐⭐ **Two *qum*s: 73:2 rise to PRAY (private); 74:2 rise to WARN (public).** ⭐ **The night builds the man who warns by day.**

**3. نِّصْفَهُ أَوِ انقُصْ مِنْهُ قَلِيلًا · أَوْ زِدْ عَلَيْهِ — *niṣfahu awi'nquṣ minhu qalīlā · aw zid ʿalayh*** · v.3–4
- **Root feelings:** *niṣf* — **HALF** (⭐ *muntaṣaf* = the **middle**; *munṣif* = **fair** — one who gives the half that's due!). *unquṣ* — the "**DECREASING**" family — *naqaṣa* = to decrease; ⭐ ***naqṣ* = a deficiency** (⭐ ***naqīṣah* = a DEFECT, a shortcoming**; ⭐ *munāqaṣah* = a **TENDER / auction** — bidding the price down!). *zid* — the "**INCREASING**" family — *zāda*; *ziyādah*; ⭐ ***mazād* = an AUCTION** (bidding UP!).
- **Here it means:** "**Half of it — or reduce it a little — or add to it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A command with THREE built-in options.** ⭐ **Half the night** — or **a little less than half** — or **a little more than half**. ⭐ **Allah gives a range, not a stopwatch.** ⭐⭐ And the auction hook makes it unforgettable: ⭐ ***munāqaṣah* (bidding DOWN) and *mazād* (bidding UP)** — ⭐ **the verse lets you bid down (*unquṣ*) or bid up (*zid*)** from the half.
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And this flexibility foreshadows v.20** — ⭐ *"He knows you can't keep exact count"* — ⭐ **the room to adjust was there from verse 3.**
  ⭐ The *zāda* root you own from **Nabaʾ 78:30** (*fa-lan **nazīdakum*** — "We'll only INCREASE you in punishment") and **Jinn 72:6** (*fa-**zādūhum** rahaqā*). ⭐ **Increasing punishment, increasing burden — and here, increasing worship.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***niṣfahu*** — accusative, a *badal* (apposition) of *al-layl* or of *qalīlā*. ⭐ ***unquṣ*** / ***zid*** — imperatives; *zid* is from a hollow verb (long *ī* drops). ⭐ ***aw*** = "or" (options).
- **Why this exact word:** offering ***naqṣ* and *ziyādah*** — ⭐ **makes worship a range you choose within, not a rigid quota.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **HALF the night — bid DOWN (*unquṣ*) or bid UP (*zid*).** ⭐ ***Munāqaṣah* = a TENDER (bidding down); *mazād* = an AUCTION (bidding up).** ⭐⭐ ***Naqīṣah* = a DEFECT; *munṣif* = FAIR.** ⭐ **The flexibility foreshadows v.20.**

**4. وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا — *wa rattili'l-qurʾāna tartīlā*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**EVENLY SPACED / well-ordered**" family — *ratila* = to be evenly arranged; ⭐ ***thaghr rattal* = TEETH that are evenly spaced, well-set** (the classical image!); ***rattala* (Form II) = to recite in measured, distinct, well-ordered sequence**; ⭐ ***tartīl* = slow, clear, measured recitation**; ⭐ ***tartīlah* = a HYMN / a chant** (modern).
- **Here it means:** "**…and recite the Qurʾān with measured, distinct recitation.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The root image is TEETH — evenly spaced, each one distinct.** ⭐ ***Tartīl* = recitation where every letter and word stands clearly SEPARATE, evenly paced** — ⭐ **no rushing, no swallowing letters, no running words together.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿAlī* (رضي الله عنه) is reported to have defined *tartīl* as: "**perfecting the letters (*tajwīd al-ḥurūf*) and knowing the stops (*maʿrifat al-wuqūf*).**" ⭐ **That's tajwīd and *waqf* — the two pillars you learn.**
  ⭐⭐ **The cognate accusative *tartīlā*** — ⭐ "**recite it with a reciting**" — ⭐ **Arabic's underline: REALLY slowly, REALLY clearly.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And compare Qiyāmah 75:16** — *lā tuḥarrik bihi lisānaka li-taʿjala bih* — "**don't move your tongue to HURRY with it**." ⭐ **Qiyāmah: don't RUSH. Muzzammil: recite with *TARTĪL*.** ⭐ **Two surahs, one instruction for reciting.** ⭐ And **Aʿlā 87:6** — *sa-nuqriʾuka fa-lā tansā* ("We'll make you recite; you won't forget").
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form II imperative** (*rattil*) + **cognate accusative** (*tartīlā*) = maximum emphasis. ⚠️ ⭐ *rattili* — kasrah added before *al-qurʾān* to avoid two sukūns.
- **Why this exact word:** *tartīl* (**evenly spaced**) — ⭐ **because the night is for UNDERSTANDING, and speed kills understanding.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Thaghr rattal* = EVENLY-SPACED TEETH** — ⭐ ***tartīl* = every word distinct, evenly paced.** ⭐⭐ **ʿAlī: "perfecting the LETTERS and knowing the STOPS."** ⭐⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:16: don't RUSH. Muzzammil 73:4: recite with TARTĪL.** ⭐ ***Tartīlah* = a HYMN.**

## §3 — Why the Night (v.5–7)

**5. إِنَّا سَنُلْقِي عَلَيْكَ قَوْلًا ثَقِيلًا — *innā sa-nulqī ʿalayka qawlan thaqīlā*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *nulqī* — the "**CASTING / throwing down**" family — *alqā* = to throw, to cast; ⭐ ***talaqqā* = to RECEIVE** (catching what's thrown!); ⭐ ***ilqāʾ* = a LECTURE / delivery** (modern: *ilqāʾ kalimah* = giving a speech); ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:4** *wa **alqat** mā fīhā* (the earth **casts out** its contents). *thaqīl* — the "**HEAVINESS**" family — ⭐ **Insān 76:27** (*yawman **thaqīlā***), **Zalzalah 99:2** (*athqālahā*), **99:7** (*mithqāl*), **Qāriʿah 101:6** (*thaqulat*).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, We will cast upon you a heavy Word.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY must he stand the night? Because the Qurʾān is HEAVY — and you train for heavy loads.**
  ⭐ **Heavy in three ways (classical):** ⭐ **(a) PHYSICALLY** — it's reported that when revelation came, the Prophet's ﷺ **forehead streamed with sweat even on a cold day**, and if he was riding, **his camel would kneel under the weight**; ⭐ **(b) in RESPONSIBILITY** — its commands are weighty to carry out; ⭐ **(c) in VALUE** — heavy as gold is heavy, **weighty with meaning**.
  ⭐⭐ ***Nulqī* = We will CAST it upon you** — like a load **dropped onto your shoulders**. ⭐ **The night prayer (v.2) is the gym; the heavy Word (v.5) is the weight.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The *thaqīl* thread:** ⭐ **Insān 76:27 — the HEAVY Day (they leave behind). Muzzammil 73:5 — the HEAVY Word (you carry).** ⭐ **Carry the heavy Word now, or face the heavy Day later.** ⭐ **And Qāriʿah 101:6 — *man thaqulat mawāzīnuh* — "whose scales are HEAVY."** ⭐⭐ **The heavy Word, carried well, makes the scales heavy.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***sa-*** + present = near future ("We **will** cast"). ⭐ **Form IV** *alqā*, weak-final. ⭐ *qawlan thaqīlan* = object + adjective.
- **Why this exact word:** *thaqīl* — ⭐ **explains the command to stand the night: you build strength for a weight.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A HEAVY Word — he SWEATED on cold days; his CAMEL KNELT under it.** ⭐ **The night prayer = the gym; the Qurʾān = the weight.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Insān 76:27: the HEAVY Day. Muzzammil 73:5: the HEAVY Word. Qāriʿah 101:6: HEAVY scales.** ⭐ ***Ilqāʾ* = a LECTURE; *talaqqā* = to RECEIVE.**

**6. نَاشِئَةَ اللَّيْلِ — *nāshiʾata'l-layl*** · v.6
- **Root feeling:** the "**ARISING / coming into being / growing up**" family — *nashaʾa* = to arise, to originate, to grow; ⭐ ***nashʾah* = ORIGIN, upbringing** (⭐ *an-nashʾah al-ūlā / al-ukhrā* = the first / the second creation — dunya and resurrection!); ⭐ ***munshaʾah* = a FACILITY / installation** (something "brought into being"); ⭐ ***inshāʾ* = construction / composition**; ⭐ *inshāʾallāh* is a different root (*shāʾa*)!
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, the rising by night [for prayer]…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Nāshiʾat al-layl* = "what ARISES in the night"** — ⭐ classical readings: ⭐ **(a) the act of RISING from sleep to pray**; ⭐ **(b) the hours of the night themselves, as they come into being one after another**; ⭐ **(c) worship that is "born" in the night.** ⭐⭐ The root is **origin and growth** — ⭐ ***nashʾah* = upbringing** — ⭐ **the night prayer is where a person is RAISED UP, spiritually.** ⭐ And note: ***rising after sleep*** — Ibn ʿAbbās and others specified that *nāshiʾah* is **prayer after having slept** — ⭐ **the harder, and therefore the more powerful.**
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Don't confuse with *inshāʾallāh*** — that's from ***shāʾa*** (to will), a different root.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ active participle, feminine (*fāʿilah*), used as a noun; ⭐ *iḍāfah* with *al-layl*. ⭐ Subject of *inna*.
- **Why this exact word:** *nāshiʾah* (**arising/originating**) — ⭐ **makes the night prayer the birthplace of something new in you.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***NASHʾAH* = UPBRINGING / origin.** ⭐ ***Nāshiʾat al-layl* = what ARISES at night — prayer after sleeping.** ⭐ ***Munshaʾah* = a FACILITY.** ⚠️ **NOT the root of *inshāʾallāh* (*shāʾa*).**

**7. هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطْئًا — *hiya ashaddu waṭʾā*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *ashadd* — **STRONGER / firmer / more intense** (the *shadīd* thread). *waṭʾ* — ⭐ the "**TREADING / pressing down underfoot**" family — *waṭiʾa* = to tread on, to step on firmly; ⭐ ***mawṭiʾ* = a FOOTHOLD / a footstep**; ⭐ ***waṭʾah* = PRESSURE, impact** (modern: *taḥta waṭʾat* = "**under the pressure of**" — e.g. under the pressure of debt!).
- **Here it means:** "**…is firmer in impact [heavier on the body, deeper in effect].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Waṭʾ* = a FIRM FOOTSTEP pressing down.** ⭐ Night prayer is ***ashaddu waṭʾā*** — two classical meanings, both true: ⭐ **(a) HARDER on the body** — it presses down on you (waking, standing tired); ⭐ **(b) leaves a DEEPER IMPRINT** — a firm footstep leaves a clear mark; ⭐ **the night's worship presses deeper into the heart.** ⭐⭐ **The difficulty and the depth are the same thing** — ⭐ **what costs more, marks more.**
  ⭐ The *shadīd* thread again: ⭐ **firm sky (78:12), firm frame (76:28), His severe grip (85:12), man's intense love of wealth (100:8), the strong guards (72:8) — ⭐ and now: the night prayer's FIRM tread.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ashaddu*** = comparative (*afʿal*). ⭐ *waṭʾan* = **tamyīz** ("firmer **in terms of** impact"). ⭐ ***hiya*** = separating/emphatic pronoun.
- **Why this exact word:** *waṭʾ* (**a firm tread**) — ⭐ **captures both the weight on the body and the mark it leaves.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Taḥta WAṬʾAT* = "under the PRESSURE of."** ⭐ ***Waṭʾ* = a FIRM FOOTSTEP.** ⭐⭐ **Night prayer: HARDER on the body AND a DEEPER imprint — what costs more, marks more.** ⭐ ***Mawṭiʾ* = a FOOTHOLD.**

**8. وَأَقْوَمُ قِيلًا — *wa aqwamu qīlā*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *aqwam* — ⭐ **comparative of *qawīm* = MORE UPRIGHT / more straight / more correct** — ⭐ **the *q-w-m* root** (*qāma*, *qiyām*, *mustaqīm*, *taqwīm*, *qayyimah*). *qīl* — ⭐ **a noun from *qāla* (to say) = SPEECH, utterance** (⭐ *qīl wa qāl* = **gossip, "he said, she said"**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and more upright in speech [more fitting for recitation].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **STANDING the night → SPEECH that STANDS STRAIGHT.**
  ⭐ ***Qum*** (v.2) = **stand**. ⭐ ***Aqwam*** (v.6) = **more upright** — **SAME ROOT (*q-w-m*)**. ⭐⭐ **The physical standing produces upright words.** ⭐ At night, with no noise and no audience, ⭐ **the tongue and the heart are aligned** — what you recite, you mean; ⭐ the words "stand straight."
  ⭐⭐ ***Qīl*** — ⭐ the everyday phrase ***qīl wa qāl*** = **idle chatter, gossip**. ⭐ **The night turns *qīl* from gossip into upright speech.**
  ⭐⭐ **The *q-w-m* thread now includes the body and the tongue:** ⭐ ***qum*** (stand, 73:2), ***aqwam*** (upright speech, 73:6), ***istaqāmū*** (keep straight, Jinn 72:16), ***taqwīm*** (upright form, Tīn 95:4), ***qayyimah*** (upright religion, Bayyinah 98:5), ***mustaqīm*** (the straight path, 17× a day).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***aqwamu*** = comparative; *qīlan* = **tamyīz**. ⭐ **Parallel structure with *ashaddu waṭʾā***: two comparatives + two specifications.
- **Why this exact word:** *aqwam* — ⭐ **ties upright speech to upright standing through one root.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QUM* (stand, v.2) → *AQWAM* (more upright speech, v.6) — SAME ROOT.** ⭐ **Standing straight at night makes your words stand straight.** ⭐⭐ ***Qīl wa qāl* = GOSSIP** — the night turns *qīl* into upright speech.

**9. إِنَّ لَكَ فِي النَّهَارِ سَبْحًا طَوِيلًا — *inna laka fi'n-nahāri sabḥan ṭawīlā*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *sabḥ* — ⭐⭐ the "**SWIMMING / gliding / moving freely about**" family — *sabaḥa* = to swim; ⭐ ***sibāḥah* = SWIMMING**; ⭐ ***masbaḥ* = a SWIMMING POOL**; ⭐ ***tasbīḥ*** = glorifying (swimming **away** from every flaw). *ṭawīl* — **LONG** (*ṭūl* = length).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, for you in the day is prolonged occupation [moving about in your affairs].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The DAY is pictured as SWIMMING.** ⭐ ***Sabḥ* = swimming** — **moving freely, back and forth, through many tasks** — ⭐ **like a swimmer moving through water**: busy, in motion, covering distance. ⭐⭐ **That's why the night is for prayer: ⭐ the day is for swimming through your affairs (da'wah, work, people); the night is for standing still before Allah.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The *s-b-ḥ* root across your surahs:**
  - **Nāziʿāt 79:3** — *wa's-**sābiḥāti** sabḥā* — the angels who **GLIDE/swim** through the heavens
  - **Aʿlā 87:1** — ***sabbiḥ*** isma rabbika'l-aʿlā — **GLORIFY** (swim away from every flaw)
  - **Naṣr 110:3** — ***fa-sabbiḥ*** bi-ḥamdi rabbik — **GLORIFY** at the moment of victory
  - ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:7** — ***sabḥan*** ṭawīlā — the day's **SWIMMING** through tasks
  ⭐⭐ **Same root: angels gliding, glorifying Allah, and the believer swimming through the day's work.** ⭐ ⚠️ **And here's the subtle point: *sabḥ* (swimming/busy-ness) vs *tasbīḥ* (glorification) — the same root.** ⭐ **Even your day's "swimming" can become *tasbīḥ* if aimed right.**
  ⭐ The cognate-style ***sabḥan ṭawīlā*** mirrors **Nāziʿāt 79:3**'s ***sabḥā***.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***inna laka*** (fronted predicate) + subject *sabḥan* (delayed). ⭐ *fi'n-nahāri* = "**in** the day." ⭐ Note the **contrast**: *al-layl* (v.2, 6) vs *an-nahār* (v.7).
- **Why this exact word:** *sabḥ* (**swimming**) — ⭐ **pictures daytime work as movement through a medium — tiring, scattered, horizontal; the night is vertical.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MASBAḤ* = a SWIMMING POOL.** ⭐ **The DAY = SWIMMING through tasks; the NIGHT = STANDING still before Him.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***S-b-ḥ*: angels GLIDE (79:3), GLORIFY (87:1, 110:3), the day's SWIMMING (73:7).** ⭐ **Your day's swimming can become *tasbīḥ*.**

## §4 — Devotion (v.8–9)

**10. وَاذْكُرِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ — *wa'dhkuri'sma rabbik*** · v.8
- **Root feeling:** the "**REMEMBERING / mentioning**" family — *dhikr* — ⭐ **the juzʾ's master thread** (76:29, 80:11, 81:27, 87:9, 88:21, 89:23, 114:4).
- **Here it means:** "**And remember the name of your Lord…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **You own this exact phrase from Aʿlā 87:15** — *wa **dhakara'sma rabbihi** fa-ṣallā* — "**he remembered the name of his Lord and prayed**" — ⭐ **the definition of *falāḥ* (success)**. ⭐ **Aʿlā described the successful man; Muzzammil COMMANDS the Prophet ﷺ to be him.** ⭐ And **Aʿlā 87:1** — *sabbiḥi'**sma** rabbik* — "**glorify the NAME of your Lord**." ⭐⭐ **The NAME is remembered, glorified, mentioned** — ⭐ **the gateway to the One named.** ⭐⭐ And recall **Nās 114:4** — *al-khannās* — ⭐ **the whisperer RETREATS at *dhikr*.** ⭐ **Remembering His name is the first line of defence.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ imperative (*udhkur*); ⚠️ kasrah on *udhkuri* before *ism* (two sukūns). ⭐ ***isma*** = object.
- **Why this exact word:** *dhikr* of the **name** — ⭐ **the simplest, most constant form of worship, there in every moment.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Aʿlā 87:15 DESCRIBED the successful man: *dhakara'sma rabbih*. Muzzammil 73:8 COMMANDS it: *udhkuri'sma rabbik*.** ⭐ **Aʿlā 87:1: glorify the NAME.** ⭐ **Nās 114:4: the whisperer retreats at *dhikr*.**

**11. وَتَبَتَّلْ إِلَيْهِ تَبْتِيلًا — *wa tabattal ilayhi tabtīlā*** · v.8
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐⭐ the "**CUTTING OFF / severing completely**" family — *batala* = **to cut off, to sever**; ***tabattala* = to devote oneself COMPLETELY, cutting oneself off from all else**; ⭐⭐ ***al-Batūl* = "the Devoted one, cut off from all but Allah"** — ⭐ **the title of MARYAM (عليها السلام), and of FĀṬIMAH (رضي الله عنها)!** ⭐ *tabattul* = devotion / (also) celibacy in some usage.
- **Here it means:** "**…and devote yourself to Him with complete devotion.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The root is CUTTING — devotion as SEVERANCE.** ⭐ ***Tabattul* = cutting every other attachment so you are wholly His.** ⭐⭐ **Maryam was called *al-Batūl*** — ⭐ the woman wholly devoted, cut off from worldly concerns in her *miḥrāb*. ⭐ **Fāṭimah too was called *al-Batūl*.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE GRAMMAR IS UNUSUAL — and it's a lesson.** ⭐ The verb is ***tabattal*** (**Form V** — devote **yourself**, with effort). ⭐ But the cognate accusative is ***tabtīlā*** (**Form II** — the verbal noun of *battala*, to **devote / sever** actively). ⭐ **The "expected" form would be *tabattulā* (Form V's own verbal noun).** ⭐⭐ **Classical explanation: the two forms are COMBINED so the verse means BOTH — "devote yourself (Form V) and sever yourself thoroughly (Form II)."** ⭐ **Effort on your part, intensity in the act.** ⭐ **One verse, two forms, double meaning.**
  ⭐ **Note *ilayhi*** — devote **TO HIM** — ⭐ **it's not cutting off for its own sake (monasticism); it's cutting off in order to turn TO Him.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***tabattal* (Form V imperative) + *tabtīlā* (Form II maṣdar)** — ⭐ **mixed forms, deliberately combining meanings.** ⭐ *ilayhi* = "**to** Him."
- **Why this exact word:** *tabattul* — ⭐ **devotion defined by what you let go of, not just what you add.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-BATŪL* = MARYAM's title (and FĀṬIMAH's) — "wholly devoted, cut off from all but Him."** ⭐ **Devotion = SEVERANCE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Tabattal* (Form V) + *tabtīlā* (Form II) — MIXED forms on purpose: effort + intensity.** ⭐ ***Ilayhi* = cut off in order to turn TO Him.**

**12. رَّبُّ الْمَشْرِقِ وَالْمَغْرِبِ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ — *rabbu'l-mashriqi wa'l-maghribi lā ilāha illā huwa*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *mashriq* — the "**RISING / shining**" family — *sharaqa* = to rise (sun); ⭐ ***mashriq* = the EAST** (place of sunrise); ⭐ *ishrāq* = radiance; ⭐ ***ash-Sharq al-Awsaṭ* = the MIDDLE EAST**. *maghrib* — the "**SETTING / going away**" family — *gharaba* = to set, to go far; ⭐ ***maghrib* = the WEST / sunset** (⭐ **Ṣalāt al-Maghrib**; ⭐ ***al-Maghrib* = MOROCCO** — the land of the sunset!); ⭐ *gharīb* = a **STRANGER** (one far from home). *ilāh* — **the one worshipped** (⭐ **Nās 114:3**).
- **Here it means:** "**Lord of the East and the West — there is no god but Him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A *merism*: East and West = EVERYWHERE.** ⭐ The two ends of the sun's journey = **all of space, all of time** (every sunrise and sunset). ⭐⭐ **And why mention it right after *tabattul*?** ⭐ Because you're being told to **cut off from everything** — ⭐ **so you're reminded Who owns everything you're cutting off from.** ⭐ **You're not losing the world; you're attaching to its Lord.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Lā ilāha illā huwa*** — ⭐ **the *shahādah* in third person.** ⭐ And ***ilāh*** connects to **Nās 114:3** (*ilāhi'n-nās*). ⭐⭐ **Rabb (Lord) + Ilāh (God) in one verse — the two dimensions of tawḥīd** (Nās's triad).
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***al-Maghrib* = MOROCCO** (land of sunset); ⭐ ***gharīb* = a STRANGER**; ⭐ ***ash-Sharq al-Awsaṭ* = the Middle East**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***rabbu*** — nominative (a new sentence: "[He is] the Lord…") — ⚠️ **there's also a recognized recitation *rabbi* (genitive), linking to *rabbika* in v.8.** ⭐ ***lā ilāha illā huwa*** — ***lā* of total negation of the genus** + exception.
- **Why this exact word:** **East and West** — ⭐ **the widest possible frame, given to a man told to narrow his heart to one focus.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MAGHRIB* = MOROCCO (land of SUNSET); *gharīb* = a STRANGER; *ash-Sharq al-Awsaṭ* = the MIDDLE EAST.** ⭐⭐ **East + West = EVERYWHERE** — ⭐ **you cut off from the world (v.8) to attach to its Lord (v.9).** ⭐⭐ ***Rabb* + *Ilāh* = Nās's triad.**

**13. فَاتَّخِذْهُ وَكِيلًا — *fa'ttakhidhhu wakīlā*** · v.9
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**ENTRUSTING / relying on**" family — *wakala* = to entrust; ***wakīl* = one to whom you ENTRUST your affairs, a trustee, an agent**; ⭐ ***tawakkul* = RELIANCE on Allah**; ⭐⭐ ***ḥasbuna'llāhu wa niʿma'l-WAKĪL*** = "Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best **Trustee**" (3:173); ⭐ modern: ***wakīl* = an AGENT, an attorney, a DEPUTY** (*wakīl wizārah* = deputy minister!); ⭐ ***wakālah* = an AGENCY** (*wakālat al-anbāʾ* = a **news agency**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…so take Him as your Trustee [to whom you entrust all affairs].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *fa* is the logic:** ⭐ **He is Lord of East and West, the only god — *SO* take Him as your *wakīl*.** ⭐⭐ ***Wakīl* = the one you hand your affairs to — your agent, your attorney.** ⭐ **You do the standing (v.2), the reciting (v.4), the remembering (v.8) — and you hand the OUTCOMES to Him.** ⭐ That's *tawakkul*: **effort is yours; results are your Wakīl's.**
  ⭐⭐ **And this is said right before v.10 — "bear what they say."** ⭐ **You can't control the deniers; you entrust them to your Wakīl.** ⭐ Compare **Ghāshiyah 88:22** (*lasta ʿalayhim bi-muṣayṭir* — "you're not a controller") and **Jinn 72:21** (*lā amliku lakum ḍarran wa lā rashadā*). ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ controls nothing — so he entrusts everything.**
  ⭐ And ***ittakhidh*** — **Form VIII of *akhadha*** = **take FOR YOURSELF** — ⭐ the same verb as **Insān 76:29 / 73:19** (*ittakhadha ilā rabbihi sabīlā*) and **Jinn 72:3** (*mā'ttakhadha ṣāḥibah*). ⭐⭐ **He took no consort (Jinn 72:3); you take HIM as Trustee (Muzzammil 73:9).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ittakhidh*** + *-hu* + *wakīlan* — **two objects** ("take Him [as] a trustee"). ⭐ The *fa* = **causal**.
- **Why this exact word:** *wakīl* — ⭐ **the precise word for handing over the outcomes while keeping the effort.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤASBUNA'LLĀHU WA NIʿMA'L-WAKĪL*** — ⭐ **the same word.** ⭐⭐ ***Wakīl* = an AGENT/ATTORNEY; *wakālat al-anbāʾ* = a NEWS AGENCY.** ⭐⭐⭐ **EFFORT is yours (v.2–8); OUTCOMES are your Wakīl's (v.9).** ⭐ **Jinn 72:3: He took no consort. Here: YOU take HIM as Trustee.**

## §5 — Patience with the Deniers (v.10–11)

**14. وَاصْبِرْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ — *wa'ṣbir ʿalā mā yaqūlūn*** · v.10
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**RESTRAINING / tying down / holding firm**" family — *ṣabara* = originally **to confine, to hold something in check**; ***ṣabr* = patience/steadfastness**; ⭐ ***ṣabir* = ALOES** (bitter, healing!); ⭐ **ʿAṣr 103:3** (*tawāṣaw bi'ṣ-ṣabr*), **Balad 90:17**.
- **Here it means:** "**And be patient over what they say…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **Patience with WORDS specifically** — ⭐ *ʿalā mā yaqūlūn* — "**over what they SAY**." ⭐ Not physical persecution here — ⭐ **the mockery, the accusations ("poet, madman, sorcerer")**. ⭐ ***Ṣabr* = holding your tongue in check** while theirs runs free. ⭐⭐ **And it immediately follows *wakīl* (v.9)** — ⭐ **patience is easier when you've handed the outcome to your Trustee.** ⭐⭐ **And Muddaththir 74:7** — *wa li-rabbika **fa'ṣbir*** — "**and for your Lord, be patient**." ⭐ **Twin surahs, twin commands of *ṣabr*.**
  ⭐ ***Ṣabir* = ALOES — bitter, but it heals.** ⭐ The thread of **virtue as restraint**: *ḥijr* (a wall, Fajr 89:5), *ḥikmah* (a bridle, Tīn 95:8), *ṣabr* (a tether, ʿAṣr 103:3).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ imperative; ⚠️ ***ṣabara ʿalā*** = "be patient **over / with** [a hardship]" — ⭐ the preposition fixed. ⭐ *mā yaqūlūna* = "**what** they say."
- **Why this exact word:** *ṣabr* — ⭐ **the restraint needed when the attack is verbal and the temptation is to answer back.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ṣabir* = ALOES — BITTER but HEALING.** ⭐ **Patience over what they SAY — hold your tongue while theirs runs.** ⭐⭐ **Twins: Muzzammil 73:10 *iṣbir* / Muddaththir 74:7 *fa'ṣbir*.** ⭐ **Easier once you've handed outcomes to your *Wakīl* (v.9).**

**15. وَاهْجُرْهُمْ هَجْرًا جَمِيلًا — *wa'hjurhum hajran jamīlā*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *hajr* — ⭐⭐ the "**LEAVING / abandoning / departing from**" family — *hajara* = to leave, to forsake; ⭐⭐ ***HIJRAH* = EMIGRATION** — the Prophet's ﷺ **leaving** Makkah for Madīnah!; ⭐ *muhājir* = an emigrant; ⭐ *hajr* = avoidance, shunning; ⭐ *hājirah* = the **midday heat** (when people "leave off" travelling!). *jamīl* — ⭐ **BEAUTIFUL** (*jamāl* = beauty; ⭐ ***ṣabrun jamīl*** — Yaʿqūb's "beautiful patience," 12:18).
- **Here it means:** "**…and leave them with a beautiful leaving.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"Leave them — BEAUTIFULLY."** ⭐ Classical definition of ***hajr jamīl***: ⭐ **avoidance with NO harm, NO reproach, NO bitterness** — ⭐ you step away from their company and their argument, **without insult, without revenge**. ⭐⭐ **You can leave someone ugly (with cursing and slammed doors) or beautiful (quietly, with dignity).** ⭐ **The Qurʾān commands the second.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The *jamīl* triad in the Qurʾān (often noted together):** ⭐ ***ṣabr jamīl*** — **beautiful patience** (complaint to no one but Allah, 12:18; 70:5); ⭐ ***ṣafḥ jamīl*** — **beautiful forgiveness** (forgiving without reproach, 15:85); ⭐ ***hajr jamīl*** — **beautiful leaving** (73:10). ⭐ **Three beautiful responses to hurt.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the *hajr* root will become *HIJRAH*** — ⭐ **the beautiful leaving of a whole city, years later.** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:5** — *wa'r-rujza **fa'hjur*** — "**and idolatry — LEAVE it**." ⭐⭐ **73:10: leave the PEOPLE, beautifully. 74:5: leave the IDOLATRY, absolutely.** ⭐ **People get gentleness; falsehood gets none.**
  ⭐ The cognate accusative ***hajran jamīlā*** — **leave them with a leaving that is beautiful.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ imperative + *-hum* + **cognate accusative with adjective** (*hajran jamīlan*) — ⭐ **the adjective on the cognate accusative specifies the MANNER.**
- **Why this exact word:** *hajr jamīl* — ⭐ **because how you leave matters as much as that you leave.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HIJRAH* = EMIGRATION** — same root. ⭐ ***Hajr jamīl* = leaving with NO harm, NO reproach.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *jamīl* triad: *ṣabr jamīl* (patience), *ṣafḥ jamīl* (forgiveness), *hajr jamīl* (leaving).** ⭐⭐ **73:10: leave PEOPLE beautifully. 74:5: leave IDOLATRY absolutely.**

**16. وَذَرْنِي وَالْمُكَذِّبِينَ — *wa dharnī wa'l-mukadhdhibīn*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *dhar* — ⭐⭐ the "**LEAVING / letting be**" family — ***wadhara*** — ⭐ **a DEFECTIVE verb: its past tense is not used** (only the present *yadharu* and the imperative *dhar* survive; *taraka* fills in the past). *mukadhdhibīn* — **the deniers** (Form II participle of *kadhaba* — those who **call the truth a lie**).
- **Here it means:** "**And leave Me with the deniers…**" ⭐ (i.e. "leave them to Me — I'll deal with them")
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **One of the most powerful threats in the Qurʾān — and it's said GENTLY to the Prophet ﷺ.** ⭐ ***Dharnī* = "LEAVE ME [with them]."** ⭐ It's what a strong protector says to someone being bullied: ⭐ **"Step aside. Leave him to me."** ⭐⭐ **To the Prophet ﷺ: comfort. To the deniers: terror.** ⭐ And it pairs with v.10 — ⭐ **"leave them beautifully (*hajr jamīl*) — and leave them TO ME."** ⭐ **You don't take revenge; you hand them over.** ⭐ (That's the *wakīl* of v.9 in action.)
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE CROSS-SURAH SYMMETRY: Muddaththir 74:11 — *dharnī wa man khalaqtu waḥīdā* — "Leave Me with the one I created alone."** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:11 and Muddaththir 74:11 — BOTH VERSE 11, BOTH "*dharnī*" — "Leave Me with…"** ⭐ **Twin surahs, twin threats, same verse number.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the defective verb you own:** ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:21** — *wa **tadharūna**'l-ākhirah* ("you **forsake** the Hereafter") and **Insān 76:27** (*wa **yadharūna** warāʾahum*). ⭐ **The same quirky verb with no past tense.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***dhar*** = imperative of the **defective verb *wadhara*** (no past in use). ⭐ ***wa'l-mukadhdhibīn*** — the *wāw* here is ***wāw al-maʿiyyah*** ("**with**"), so the noun is **accusative** (*maf'ūl maʿah*): "**leave Me together WITH the deniers**."
- **Why this exact word:** *dharnī* — ⭐ **a threat that requires no description of punishment — the speaker is enough.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHARNĪ* = "LEAVE THEM TO ME."** ⭐ **Comfort for the Prophet ﷺ, terror for them.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muzzammil 73:11 & Muddaththir 74:11 — BOTH VERSE 11, BOTH "*dharnī*".** ⭐⭐ **Defective verb *wadhara* (no past) — Qiyāmah 75:21, Insān 76:27.** ⭐ ***Wāw al-maʿiyyah* = "WITH."**

**17. أُولِي النَّعْمَةِ — *uli'n-naʿmah*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *ulī* — ⭐ **"possessors of / people of"** (the plural of *dhū*; *ulū* nom. / *ulī* acc.-gen.). *naʿmah* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**SOFTNESS / ease / comfort**" family — ⚠️ ***naʿmah* (with FATḤAH) = LUXURY, soft easy living**; ⭐ ***niʿmah* (with KASRAH) = a BLESSING / favour**; ⭐ ***naʿīm*** = bliss (Insān 76:20); ⭐ ***nāʿim*** = soft (Ghāshiyah 88:8); ⭐ ***naʿam*** = yes!
- **Here it means:** "**…the possessors of luxury [the comfortable elite].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ONE VOWEL CHANGES THE MEANING — the best trap in this surah.**
  ⭐ ***NIʿMAH*** (kasrah) = **a BLESSING** — something given, to be thanked for. ⭐ (*wa ammā bi-**niʿmati** rabbika fa-ḥaddith* — **Ḍuḥā 93:11**.)
  ⭐ ***NAʿMAH*** (fatḥah) = **LUXURY, soft comfortable living** — enjoyed, often **without** thanks. ⭐ (*wa **naʿmatin** kānū fīhā fākihīn* — "and **luxury** they used to delight in," 44:27, of Pharaoh's people.)
  ⭐⭐ **The deniers are *uli'n-NAʿMAH* — not "people of blessing" but "people of LUXURY."** ⭐ **They had the comfort without recognizing the Giver.** ⭐ **That's the difference between *niʿmah* and *naʿmah*: gratitude.**
  ⭐⭐ **And who were they?** ⭐ The **wealthy chiefs of Quraysh** — ⭐ the same type as **ʿAbasa 80:5** (*istaghnā*), **Muddaththir 74:11–14** (al-Walīd, given wealth and sons), and **Fajr 89:15** (*fa-akramahu wa **naʿʿamahu*** — "honoured him and **pampered** him" — same root!). ⭐ **Luxury breeds *istighnāʾ*, and *istighnāʾ* breeds denial.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ulī*** — ⚠️ written with an extra *wāw* (أُولِي) that is **not pronounced**. ⭐ It's in **apposition** to *al-mukadhdhibīn* (accusative/genitive form *ulī*).
- **Why this exact word:** *naʿmah* (**luxury**) rather than *ghinā* (wealth) — ⭐ **names the softness of their lives, which made hardship-free denial easy.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ⚠️ ***NIʿMAH* (kasrah) = a BLESSING. *NAʿMAH* (fatḥah) = LUXURY.** ⭐ **One vowel: gratitude vs. self-indulgence.** ⭐⭐ **Fajr 89:15: *naʿʿamahu* (pampered him). ʿAbasa 80:5: *istaghnā*.** ⭐ **Luxury → self-sufficiency → denial.** ⚠️ ***Ulī* — the wāw is SILENT.**

**18. وَمَهِّلْهُمْ قَلِيلًا — *wa mahhilhum qalīlā*** · v.11
- **Root feeling:** the "**GENTLENESS / slowness / giving time**" family — *mahala* = to act slowly, gently; ***mahhala* (Form II) / *amhala* (Form IV) = to give RESPITE, to grant a delay**; ⭐ ***mahlan!* = "SLOW DOWN! / easy!"** (everyday Arabic!); ⭐ ***muhlah* = a DEADLINE / grace period** (modern: *muhlah zamaniyyah* = a time limit!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and give them respite for a little while.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **You own this from the END of Ṭāriq.** ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:17** — ***fa-mahhili**'l-kāfirīna **amhilhum** ruwaydā* — "**so give respite to the disbelievers; give them respite a little.**" ⭐⭐ **Almost identical command, two surahs.** ⭐ ⭐ **And look at the gentle threat inside the mercy:** ⭐ *qalīlā* — "**a LITTLE while**." ⭐ The respite is real, ⭐ **but SHORT.** ⭐ Historically: ⭐ **Badr came within about 15 years** — and many of these "people of luxury" died there. ⭐⭐ ***Muhlah* = a DEADLINE** — ⭐ **a grace period is a deadline with a smile.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form II imperative** (*mahhil*). ⭐ ***qalīlan*** = adverbial ("**a little** [time]"). ⭐ **Compare Ṭāriq 86:17's double — *mahhil… amhil* — Form II then Form IV.**
- **Why this exact word:** *mahhala* — ⭐ **makes the delay a deliberate gift of time, not neglect.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAHLAN!* = "SLOW DOWN!"; *MUHLAH* = a DEADLINE.** ⭐ **A grace period is a deadline with a smile.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:17: *fa-mahhili'l-kāfirīna amhilhum ruwaydā* — nearly IDENTICAL.** ⭐ ***Qalīlā* = SHORT respite.**

## §6 — What Awaits (v.12–14)

**19. إِنَّ لَدَيْنَا أَنكَالًا — *inna ladaynā ankālā*** · v.12
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**SHACKLING / restraining**" family — ***nikl* = a heavy SHACKLE / fetter** (plural *ankāl*); ⭐⭐ ***nakāl* = an EXEMPLARY punishment** (one that **restrains** others — ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:25!**); ⭐ *nakala ʿan* = to **shrink back / renege** (to be held back!).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, with Us are heavy shackles…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ROOT YOU OWN FROM PHARAOH'S PUNISHMENT.** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:25** — *fa-akhadhahu'llāhu **nakāla**'l-ākhirati wa'l-ūlā* — "**Allah seized him with the EXEMPLARY punishment** of the Hereafter and the first." ⭐ There I explained ***nakāl*** comes from ***nikl*, a SHACKLE** — a punishment that **restrains others** by example. ⭐⭐⭐ **Here is the shackle itself: *ankāl*.** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt gave you the word; Muzzammil gives you the object.** ⭐⭐ And it comes **right after "people of luxury" (v.11)** — ⭐ **soft living → heavy shackles.** ⭐ Compare **Insān 76:4** (*salāsila wa aghlālā* — chains and collars) and **Fajr 89:26** (*wa lā yūthiqu wathāqahu aḥad*). ⭐ ⭐ And ***ladaynā*** — "**WITH US**" — ⭐ **ready, present, kept close.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***inna ladaynā*** — **fronted predicate** ("**with Us** [are]…"). ⭐ *ankālan* = subject of *inna* (delayed), broken plural.
- **Why this exact word:** *ankāl* (**heavy fetters**) — ⭐ **the exact opposite of *naʿmah*'s softness.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:25: *NAKĀL* (exemplary punishment). Muzzammil 73:12: *ANKĀL* (the SHACKLES themselves).** ⭐ **Same root — the word, then the object.** ⭐⭐ **Soft LUXURY (v.11) → heavy SHACKLES (v.12).** ⭐ ***Ladaynā* = "kept WITH Us," ready.**

**20. وَجَحِيمًا — *wa jaḥīmā*** · v.12
- **Root feeling:** the "**FIERCELY BLAZING fire**" family — *jaḥama* = to burn fiercely; ***jaḥīm* = the Blaze** (a name of Hell).
- **Here it means:** "**…and a Blazing Fire.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The *jaḥīm* thread across SIX surahs:** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:36, 39** (displayed / the refuge), **Takwīr 81:12** (kindled), **Infiṭār 82:14** (the wicked in it), **Muṭaffifīn 83:16** (they will burn in it), **Takāthur 102:6** (you WILL see it), ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:12** (it is **with Us**, ready). ⭐ **Note the list in v.12–13: SHACKLES, BLAZE, CHOKING FOOD, PAINFUL PUNISHMENT** — ⭐ **four items, each worse — restraint, heat, food that won't go down, and pain.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ joined to *ankālan* by *wa*; accusative (subject of *inna*), indefinite — ⭐ **indefinite = vast, beyond description**.
- **Why this exact word:** *jaḥīm* — ⭐ **the fierce-blaze name, set against the comfortable cool of their luxury.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Jaḥīm* — SIX surahs: 79:36, 81:12, 82:14, 83:16, 102:6, 73:12.** ⭐ **The four-item list: SHACKLES → BLAZE → CHOKING food → PAIN.**

**21. وَطَعَامًا ذَا غُصَّةٍ — *wa ṭaʿāman dhā ghuṣṣah*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *ṭaʿām* — **food** (⭐ *maṭʿam* = a restaurant; **Quraysh 106:4** *aṭʿamahum*). *dhā* — **possessor of** (one of the **five special nouns**). *ghuṣṣah* — ⭐⭐ the "**CHOKING / a lump stuck in the throat**" family — *ghaṣṣa* = to choke on food; ⭐⭐ ***ghuṣṣah* = a LUMP IN THE THROAT** — ⭐ **and in everyday Arabic, the lump in your throat from GRIEF** (*fī ḥalqī ghuṣṣah* = "I have a lump in my throat")!
- **Here it means:** "**…and food that chokes…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Food that STICKS in the throat — it won't go down, won't come up.** ⭐ The horror isn't that it tastes bad; ⭐ **it's that you can't swallow it.** ⭐⭐ Classical reports name it: the ***zaqqūm*** tree, the ***ḍarīʿ*** thorn (**Ghāshiyah 88:6** — the thorn **a starving camel refuses**), the ***ghislīn***. ⭐ **Ghāshiyah: food that doesn't NOURISH. Muzzammil: food that won't even GO DOWN.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the contrast with "people of LUXURY" (v.11):** ⭐ **they ate the softest, finest food — now food that chokes.** ⭐ **Fine dining → *ghuṣṣah*.** ⭐ And **Quraysh 106:4** — *aṭʿamahum min jūʿ* (**He FED them**) — ⭐ **the same people, fed in dunya, choking in Hell.**
  ⭐ The everyday hook is perfect: ⭐ ***ghuṣṣah* = the lump in your throat when you're about to cry.** ⭐ **Food that feels like grief.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***dhā*** — **accusative** of the five-noun *dhū/dhā/dhī* (⭐ **Balad 90:15**, *yatīman **dhā** maqrabah*). ⭐ *ghuṣṣatin* = genitive (*iḍāfah*).
- **Why this exact word:** *ghuṣṣah* — ⭐ **a physical sensation everyone has felt, magnified into permanence.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHUṢṢAH* = a LUMP IN THE THROAT** — ⭐ **the one you get from GRIEF.** ⭐ **Food that won't go down.** ⭐⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:6: *ḍarīʿ* — food that doesn't NOURISH. Here: food that won't even SWALLOW.** ⭐ **LUXURY (v.11) → CHOKING (v.13).** ⭐ ***Dhā* = five-noun accusative.**

**22. يَوْمَ تَرْجُفُ الْأَرْضُ وَالْجِبَالُ — *yawma tarjufu'l-arḍu wa'l-jibāl*** · v.14
- **Root feeling:** the "**VIOLENT TREMBLING / quaking**" family — *rajafa* = to tremble violently; ⭐ ***ar-rājifah* = the Quaker** (Nāziʿāt 79:6!); ⭐ *irjāf* = spreading **alarming rumours** (that make hearts tremble).
- **Here it means:** "**On the Day the earth and the mountains will quake…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **You own this root from Nāziʿāt 79:6** — *yawma **tarjufu'r-rājifah*** — "**the Day the Quaker quakes**." ⭐ **Same verb, same opening "*yawma tarjufu*".** ⭐⭐ **And the mountains are named too** — ⭐ **even the earth's "pegs" (Nabaʾ 78:7) and "anchors" (Nāziʿāt 79:32) tremble.** ⭐ And compare **Zalzalah 99:1** (*zulzilati'l-arḍu zilzālahā*). ⭐ **Three quake-words: *rajf* (trembling), *zalzalah* (convulsive shaking), *dakk* (pounding flat, Fajr 89:21).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yawma*** = adverb of time (accusative), linked to v.12 ("with Us are shackles… **on the Day**…"). ⭐ Present *tarjufu* = vivid.
- **Why this exact word:** *rajf* — ⭐ **the violent tremor that comes first, before the mountains dissolve.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Yawma TARJUFU* — IDENTICAL opening to Nāziʿāt 79:6.** ⭐ **Quake-words: *rajf* (73:14, 79:6), *zalzalah* (99:1), *dakk* (89:21).** ⭐ ***Irjāf* = alarming RUMOURS.**

**23. كَثِيبًا مَّهِيلًا — *kathīban mahīlā*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *kathīb* — ⭐ **a SAND DUNE, a heap of sand** (⭐ *kuthbān* = dunes). *mahīl* — ⭐ the "**POURING / sliding loose**" family — *hāla* = **to pour out (sand, earth) so that it slides and flows**; ***mahīl* = loose, flowing, sliding sand** — the kind that pours through your fingers and won't hold a shape.
- **Here it means:** "**…and the mountains become a heap of sliding sand.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Mountains → a sand dune that won't hold its shape.** ⭐ ***Kathīb*** = a **dune** — still a heap. ⭐ ***Mahīl*** = **pouring, sliding** — the dune itself **won't stay put**; ⭐ **step on it, it flows away under you.** ⭐⭐ **The firmest thing → the least firm thing.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOUNTAIN-FATES THREAD — now SIX images:**
  - **Nabaʾ 78:20** — ***sarābā*** — a **MIRAGE**
  - **Takwīr 81:3** — ***suyyirat*** — set **MOVING**
  - **Fajr 89:21** — ***dakkan dakkā*** — **POUNDED** flat
  - **Qāriʿah 101:5** — ***ka'l-ʿihni'l-manfūsh*** — **CARDED WOOL**
  - ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:14** — ***kathīban mahīlā*** — **SLIDING SAND**
  - *(and the opposite: Ghāshiyah 88:19 — **SET UP firmly**, Nabaʾ 78:7 — **PEGS**)*
  ⭐⭐ **From pegs to sliding sand.** ⭐ Every desert Arab knew how **a dune collapses under a foot** — ⭐ **that's what the mountains become.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kānat* (past for certain future) + *kathīban* (khabar) + *mahīlan* (adjective).** ⭐ *mahīl* = *mafʿūl*-sense on the *faʿīl* pattern ("**poured**").
- **Why this exact word:** *mahīl* — ⭐ **chosen for its instability: sand that can't be stood on.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KATHĪB MAHĪL* = a DUNE that POURS away under your foot.** ⭐ **The firmest thing → the least firm.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Mountain-fates: MIRAGE (78:20), MOVING (81:3), POUNDED (89:21), CARDED WOOL (101:5), SLIDING SAND (73:14).** ⭐ **vs. PEGS (78:7) and SET UP (88:19).**

## §7 — The Pharaoh Precedent (v.15–16)

**24. إِنَّا أَرْسَلْنَا إِلَيْكُمْ رَسُولًا شَاهِدًا عَلَيْكُمْ — *innā arsalnā ilaykum rasūlan shāhidan ʿalaykum*** · v.15
- **Root feelings:** *arsalnā* — the "**SENDING**" family (*rasūl* = messenger; *risālah* = a message/letter; **Fīl 105:3** *arsala ʿalayhim ṭayran*). *shāhid* — the "**WITNESSING / being present**" family — *shahida* = to witness; ⭐ *shahādah* = testimony; ⭐ **Burūj 85:3** (*shāhidin wa mashhūd*), **ʿĀdiyāt 100:7** (*la-shahīd*).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, We have sent to you a Messenger as a witness over you…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Messenger is a WITNESS — and a witness testifies.** ⭐ He doesn't just **deliver** (*balāgh*, Jinn 72:23); ⭐ he **watches how you respond** — and will **testify** about it. ⭐⭐ **And the address shifts: *ilaykum* — "to YOU [people of Makkah]."** ⭐ The surah, which spoke **to** the Prophet ﷺ (v.1–14), now turns and speaks **to the deniers** directly.
  ⭐⭐ **The Prophet's ﷺ titles across your surahs:** ⭐ ***mundhir*** (warner, Nāziʿāt 79:45), ***mudhakkir*** (reminder, Ghāshiyah 88:21), ***balāgh*** (deliverer, Jinn 72:23), ⭐ ***shāhid*** (witness, Muzzammil 73:15). ⭐ **Warn, remind, deliver, testify.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***innā*** = "indeed **We**." ⭐ ***shāhidan*** = adjective of *rasūlan*. ⭐ ***ʿalaykum*** = "**over / against** you."
- **Why this exact word:** *shāhid* — ⭐ **makes the Messenger's presence a testimony to be reckoned with later.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Messenger = a WITNESS (*shāhid*) — he'll TESTIFY.** ⭐⭐ **Titles: *mundhir* (79:45), *mudhakkir* (88:21), *balāgh* (72:23), *shāhid* (73:15).** ⭐ **The surah turns from addressing HIM to addressing THEM.**

**25. كَمَا أَرْسَلْنَا إِلَىٰ فِرْعَوْنَ رَسُولًا · فَعَصَىٰ فِرْعَوْنُ الرَّسُولَ — *kamā arsalnā ilā firʿawna rasūlā · fa-ʿaṣā firʿawnu'r-rasūl*** · v.15–16
- **Root feelings:** *firʿawn* — **Pharaoh** (a title; ⚠️ **a diptote** — *firʿawna* in the genitive). *ʿaṣā* — the "**DISOBEYING**" family (⭐ and *ʿaṣā* = a **STAFF** — Mūsā's!).
- **Here it means:** "**…just as We sent to Pharaoh a messenger. But Pharaoh disobeyed THE messenger…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A GRAMMAR GEM — indefinite, then definite.**
  ⭐ **v.15:** *arsalnā ilā firʿawna **rasūlan*** — "**A** messenger" (**indefinite** — first mention).
  ⭐ **v.16:** *fa-ʿaṣā firʿawnu **ar-rasūl*** — "**THE** messenger" (**definite** — referring back).
  ⭐⭐ **This is *al-ʿahd adh-dhikrī*** — ⭐ **the definite article pointing back to something just mentioned** ("**that** messenger I just told you about"). ⭐ English does it too: "A man came in. **The** man sat down." ⭐ **Recognize this and Arabic narrative reads much more smoothly.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the Pharaoh thread — you own it fully:** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:17–25** (*innahu ṭaghā… fa-kadhdhaba wa **ʿaṣā***), **Fajr 89:10** (*dhi'l-awtād*), **Burūj 85:18**. ⭐⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:21** — *fa-kadhdhaba wa **ʿaṣā*** — ⭐ **SAME VERB for Pharaoh.** ⭐ **And Mūsā carried an *ʿaṣā* (a staff) — Pharaoh did *ʿaṣā* (disobeyed).** ⭐ Same letters.
  ⭐ **The logic of the comparison (*kamā*):** ⭐ "**You are being sent a messenger JUST AS Pharaoh was** — ⭐ **and you know what happened to Pharaoh.**"
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***rasūlan* (indefinite, v.15) → *ar-rasūl* (definite, v.16) = *al-ʿahd adh-dhikrī*** (referring back). ⚠️ ⭐ ***firʿawn* = DIPTOTE.**
- **Why this exact word:** the **same word, first indefinite then definite** — ⭐ **makes the second mention land as "the very one I sent."**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RASŪLAN* (A messenger) → *AR-RASŪL* (THE messenger) = referring BACK** (*al-ʿahd adh-dhikrī*). ⭐ **Like English: "A man came in. THE man sat down."** ⭐⭐ ***ʿAṣā*: Pharaoh DISOBEYED (79:21, 73:16); Mūsā carried an *ʿAṢĀ* (staff).** ⭐ **"Just AS Pharaoh" — and you know how that ended.**

**26. فَأَخَذْنَاهُ أَخْذًا وَبِيلًا — *fa-akhadhnāhu akhdhan wabīlā*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *akhadha* — **to SEIZE, to take** (⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:25** *fa-**akhadhahu**'llāh*). *wabīl* — ⭐ the "**HEAVY / crushing / unwholesome**" family — ***w-b-l***: ⭐ ***wābil* = a HEAVY DOWNPOUR** (torrential rain — 2:264–265!); ***wabīl* = heavy, severe, oppressive, unwholesome** (like food that sits badly, or air that's foul).
- **Here it means:** "**…so We seized him with a crushing seizure.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Wabīl* = HEAVY like a TORRENTIAL DOWNPOUR** — ⭐ **the image is rain so heavy it flattens everything.** ⭐ **Pharaoh was seized by something that came down on him like a deluge** — ⭐ **and fittingly, he was DROWNED.** ⭐⭐ **A word rooted in heavy rain for a man destroyed by water.**
  ⭐⭐ The cognate accusative ***akhdhan wabīlā*** — "**seized him with a seizing that was crushing**."
  ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:25** — *fa-**akhadhahu**'llāhu nakāla'l-ākhirati wa'l-ūlā* — ⭐ **same verb, same victim.** ⭐ **And Muzzammil 73:12's *ankāl* (shackles) is the root of Nāziʿāt's *nakāl*.** ⭐⭐ **Two surahs telling Pharaoh's end with overlapping words.**
  ⭐ ⚠️ **TRAP: *wabīl* (w-b-**l**) is NOT *wabāʾ* (w-b-**ʾ**, an epidemic/plague).** ⭐ **They look and sound related — different roots.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **cognate accusative + adjective** (*akhdhan wabīlan*) — the adjective gives the **manner**. ⭐ Past tense *akhadhnā* + *-hu*.
- **Why this exact word:** *wabīl* (**downpour-heavy**) — ⭐ **matches the weight of the punishment to the weight of the arrogance — and to the drowning.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WĀBIL* = a HEAVY DOWNPOUR.** ⭐ ***Wabīl* = heavy as a deluge — for a man who DROWNED.** ⭐⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:25: *fa-akhadhahu'llāh* — same verb, same Pharaoh.** ⚠️⭐ ***Wabīl* (w-b-l) ≠ *wabāʾ* (w-b-ʾ, EPIDEMIC).**

## §8 — The Day That Greys Children (v.17–19)

**27. فَكَيْفَ تَتَّقُونَ إِن كَفَرْتُمْ يَوْمًا — *fa-kayfa tattaqūna in kafartum yawmā*** · v.17
- **Root feelings:** *tattaqūn* — the "**SHIELDING / guarding**" family — *ittaqā* = to protect oneself (⭐ *taqwā* — **NOT "fear," but ARMOUR**; ⭐ *wiqāyah* = protection). *kafartum* — **you disbelieve** (*kafara* = to bury a seed / cover over).
- **Here it means:** "**So how will you shield yourselves, if you disbelieve, from a Day…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Tattaqūn* = "how will you SHIELD yourselves?"** — ⭐ **the root of *taqwā* used in its most literal sense: putting a SHIELD between you and harm.** ⭐ **The question: if you reject faith — what's your armour against THAT Day?** ⭐ ⭐ It proves the point you've learned: ⭐ ***taqwā* = armour, not trembling** — ⭐ here the verb literally means "**protect yourselves FROM a Day**" (*yawman* is its object!). ⭐⭐ **The Day is the threat; *taqwā* is the shield; disbelief is throwing the shield away.**
  ⭐ You own the root from **Nabaʾ 78:31** (*al-muttaqīn*), **Shams 91:8** (*taqwāhā*), **Layl 92:5, 17** (*ittaqā, al-atqā*), **ʿAlaq 96:12** (*bi't-taqwā*).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kayfa*** = rhetorical ("**HOW** could you…?" = you can't). ⭐ ***yawman*** = **object of *tattaqūna*** — ⚠️ "shield yourselves **FROM a Day**" (not "on a day"). ⭐ ***in kafartum*** = conditional.
- **Why this exact word:** *ittaqā* — ⭐ **the clearest demonstration that *taqwā* means self-protection.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"HOW will you SHIELD yourselves FROM a Day?" — *taqwā* in its LITERAL sense.** ⭐ ***Yawman* is the OBJECT — the Day is what you shield FROM.** ⭐⭐ **Disbelief = throwing the shield away.** ⭐ ***Wiqāyah* = PROTECTION.**

**28. يَجْعَلُ الْوِلْدَانَ شِيبًا — *yajʿalu'l-wildāna shībā*** · v.17
- **Root feelings:** *wildān* — ⭐ **plural of *walīd* = young boys, children** (⭐ **Insān 76:19**, *wildānun mukhalladūn*!). *shīb* — ⭐ the "**GREY / white HAIR of old age**" family — *shāba* = to go grey; ⭐ ***shayb* = grey hair**; ⭐ ***ashyab* (pl. *shīb*) = grey-haired**; ⭐ ***shaybah* = old age**.
- **Here it means:** "**…a Day that will turn children grey-haired.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST VIVID IMAGE OF TERROR IN THE SURAH — and it inverts a verse you own.**
  ⭐ **A Day so terrifying that CHILDREN'S hair turns WHITE.** ⭐ Not adults — **children**, who don't grey. ⭐ **Fear so intense it skips a lifetime of ageing in a moment.** ⭐⭐ (The Prophet ﷺ is reported to have said: ***"Hūd and its sisters have made me grey"*** — surahs about the Day aged him.)
  ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW COMPARE INSĀN 76:19** — *wa yaṭūfu ʿalayhim **wildānun mukhalladūn*** — "**youths made ETERNAL**" (forever young) **serving in Paradise**. ⭐ **Same word, *wildān*:** ⭐ **Insān: children who NEVER age. Muzzammil: children who age INSTANTLY.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The two destinations, shown through the same children: eternal youth in the Garden, instant greyness on the Day.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yajʿalu*** takes **two objects** ("makes the children [into] grey-haired ones"). ⭐ *shīban* = plural of *ashyab*. ⭐ The whole clause describes *yawman* (a Day **that** makes…).
- **Why this exact word:** *shīb* — ⭐ **makes abstract terror visible on the most innocent heads.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A Day that turns CHILDREN'S hair WHITE.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Insān 76:19: *wildān* who NEVER age (Paradise). Muzzammil 73:17: *wildān* who age INSTANTLY.** ⭐ **Same children, two fates.** ⭐ **"Hūd and its sisters made me grey."** ⭐ ***Shayb* = GREY HAIR.**

**29. السَّمَاءُ مُنفَطِرٌ بِهِ — *as-samāʾu munfaṭirun bih*** · v.18
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**SPLITTING / cleaving open**" family — *faṭara* = to split; ⭐ ***infaṭara* = to be split apart**; ⭐ ***fiṭrah*** = original nature; ⭐ ***Fāṭir*** = the Originator; ⭐ ***ʿĪd al-Fiṭr*** (breaking the fast).
- **Here it means:** "**The sky will be split apart by it [that Day].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **You own this word from INFIṬĀR 82:1** — *idha's-samāʾu'**nfaṭarat*** — "**when the sky is split**." ⭐ **Same root, same subject (the sky), same Form VII.** ⭐⭐ And recall the **three "splitting" roots**: ⭐ ***f-j-r*** (*fajr*, dawn bursting — Fajr 89:1), ***f-ṭ-r*** (*infaṭarat* — Infiṭār 82:1, Muzzammil 73:18), ***f-l-q*** (*falaq*, daybreak — Falaq 113:1). ⭐ **Creation began with a splitting (*Fāṭir*); it ends with one.**
  ⭐ And ***bihi*** = "**by it**" — ⭐ **by that Day's terror** (or: on that Day). ⭐ **The Day that greys children (v.17) also splits the sky (v.18).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***munfaṭirun* is MASCULINE — but *as-samāʾ* is normally FEMININE!** ⭐ **Compare Infiṭār 82:1: *infaṭarat* (feminine verb).** ⭐ **Classical explanations:** ⭐ **(a) *munfaṭir* = "a thing of splitting" (*dhāt infiṭār*), describing a quality, not agreeing in gender; ⭐ (b) *samāʾ* can be treated as masculine in the sense of "**the canopy / the ceiling**" (*saqf*).** ⭐ **A recognized feature — not an error.**
- **Why this exact word:** *infiṭār* — ⭐ **links this surah's terror to the splitting-sky imagery of Infiṭār.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Infiṭār 82:1: *infaṭarat* (the sky splits). Muzzammil 73:18: *munfaṭir* (the sky split).** ⭐ **Same root.** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***Munfaṭir* is MASCULINE for a feminine *samāʾ*** — ⭐ **"a thing of splitting" / sky as "ceiling."** ⭐ **Three splitting roots: *f-j-r*, *f-ṭ-r*, *f-l-q*.**

**30. كَانَ وَعْدُهُ مَفْعُولًا — *kāna waʿduhu mafʿūlā*** · v.18
- **Root feelings:** *waʿd* — the "**PROMISE**" family — *waʿada* = to promise; ⭐ ***waʿd* = a promise** (good); ⭐ ***waʿīd* = a THREAT** (a promise of harm); ⭐ *mawʿid* = **an APPOINTMENT** (⭐ modern: *mawʿid* = a doctor's appointment!). *mafʿūl* — ⭐ **DONE, accomplished, carried out** (passive participle of *faʿala*; ⭐ ***mafʿūl* = the grammatical OBJECT** — "the thing acted on"!).
- **Here it means:** "**His promise is ever fulfilled [done].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The promise is described as ALREADY DONE (*mafʿūl*).** ⭐ Not "will be fulfilled" — ***mafʿūl*** — **"a done thing."** ⭐ **His promise is as good as accomplished the moment it's made.** ⭐⭐ And ***kāna*** — "**has always been**" — ⭐ **permanence.** ⭐⭐ **Grammar hook:** ⭐ ***mafʿūl bihi* = the DIRECT OBJECT** in grammar — **"that which is acted upon."** ⭐ **You've used *mafʿūl* in every grammar note; here it means "carried out."**
  ⭐ And ***mawʿid* = an APPOINTMENT** — ⭐ **compare Nabaʾ 78:17** — *inna yawma'l-faṣli kāna **mīqātā*** (an **appointed time**). ⭐ **The Day has an appointment, and the promise is already done.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kāna*** with Allah's promises = **permanence** ("ever has been"). ⭐ ***mafʿūlan*** = *khabar* of *kāna*.
- **Why this exact word:** *mafʿūl* — ⭐ **describes a future event in the language of a completed one: total certainty.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAFʿŪL* = DONE** — ⭐ **His promise is "a done thing."** ⭐⭐ ***Mafʿūl bihi* = the DIRECT OBJECT** (grammar!). ⭐⭐ ***Mawʿid* = an APPOINTMENT; *waʿīd* = a THREAT.** ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:17: the Day is a *mīqāt* (appointment).**

**31. إِنَّ هَٰذِهِ تَذْكِرَةٌ ۖ فَمَن شَاءَ اتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِ سَبِيلًا — *inna hādhihi tadhkiratun fa-man shāʾa'ttakhadha ilā rabbihi sabīlā*** · v.19
- **Root feelings:** *tadhkirah* — **a REMINDER** (the *dhikr* thread). *shāʾa* — **to will** (*mashīʾah*; ⭐ *inshāʾallāh*!). *sabīl* — **a WAY, a road** (⭐ *ibn as-sabīl* = **a traveller**, "son of the road"; ⭐ *fī sabīli'llāh*).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, this is a reminder; so whoever wills may take a way to his Lord.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THIS VERSE IS WORD-FOR-WORD IDENTICAL TO INSĀN 76:29.**
  ⭐ **Insān 76:29** — *inna hādhihi tadhkiratun · fa-man shāʾa'ttakhadha ilā rabbihi sabīlā*
  ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:19** — *inna hādhihi tadhkiratun · fa-man shāʾa'ttakhadha ilā rabbihi sabīlā*
  ⭐⭐⭐ **EXACT.** ⭐ **Free vocabulary — and a sign the Qurʾān repeats its key invitation in identical words.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the "*fa-man shāʾa*" open door — now in SIX surahs:**
  - **Insān 76:29** — *fa-man shāʾa'ttakhadha ilā rabbihi **sabīlā***
  - **Nabaʾ 78:39** — *fa-man shāʾa'ttakhadha ilā rabbihi **maʾābā***
  - **ʿAbasa 80:12** — *fa-man shāʾa **dhakarah***
  - **Takwīr 81:28** — *li-man shāʾa minkum an **yastaqīm***
  - ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:19** — *fa-man shāʾa'ttakhadha ilā rabbihi **sabīlā***
  - ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:37, 55** — *li-man shāʾa minkum an **yataqaddama**… / fa-man shāʾa **dhakarah***
  ⭐⭐⭐ **After all the terror of v.12–18 — shackles, blaze, choking, greyed children, a split sky — the surah ends its first part with an OPEN DOOR.** ⭐ ***Fa-man shāʾa*** — **whoever wants to, can.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***hādhihi*** (feminine "this") — referring to the surah / these verses (*āyāt*, feminine). ⭐ ***ittakhadha*** = Form VIII ("take **for oneself**").
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **repeating Insān's exact words — so the invitation sounds identical wherever it appears.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD = INSĀN 76:29.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Fa-man shāʾa* — SIX surahs: 76:29, 78:39, 80:12, 81:28, 73:19, 74:37/55.** ⭐ **After all the terror — an OPEN DOOR.** ⭐ ***Ibn as-sabīl* = a TRAVELLER.**

## §9 — The Relief (v.20)

*⭐ One verse — but it carries more vocabulary than the previous nineteen combined. It is the surah's answer to its own hardship: **Allah saw the effort, knew its limits, and eased it.** Every phrase below is from v.20.*

**32. أَدْنَىٰ مِن ثُلُثَيِ اللَّيْلِ وَنِصْفَهُ وَثُلُثَهُ — *adnā min thuluthayi'l-layli wa niṣfahu wa thuluthah*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *adnā* — ⭐ the "**NEARNESS / lowness**" family — *danā* = to come near; ⭐ ***adnā* = NEARER / LESS / LOWER**; ⭐⭐ ***dunyā* = "the NEARER [life]"** (Aʿlā 87:16!). *thuluth* — **a THIRD** (⭐ *thuluthayn* = **two thirds**; ⭐ *thalāthah* = three). *niṣf* — **HALF** (v.3).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, your Lord knows that you stand [in prayer] nearly two-thirds of the night, and half of it, and a third of it…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Allah RECORDS their effort precisely — before easing it.** ⭐ **Two-thirds, half, a third** — ⭐ **He names exactly how long they stood**, night by night. ⭐⭐ **The relief begins with RECOGNITION:** ⭐ "**I know what you've been doing.**" ⭐ **Mercy starts by being seen.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Adnā* = "LESS than / NEARLY"** — ⭐ **the same root as *dunyā*** (the "nearer/lower" life — Aʿlā 87:16). ⭐ **One root: nearness and lowness.**
  ⭐⭐ **And look back at v.2–3:** ⭐ ***niṣfahu** awi'nquṣ… aw zid* — "**half**, or less, or more." ⭐ **v.20 confirms they did exactly that: ⭐ two-thirds (more), half, a third (less).** ⭐ **The command was obeyed in full.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***thuluthayi*** — **DUAL** ("two thirds"), ⚠️ **the dual *nūn* is dropped in *iḍāfah*** (*thuluthayn* → *thuluthay*). ⭐ ⚠️ ***niṣfahu wa thuluthahu*** — **accusative** (joined to *adnā*: "you stand nearly two-thirds, **and** half, **and** a third") — ⭐ **there's also an authentic recitation in the genitive (*niṣfihi wa thuluthihi*)**, joined to *thuluthay* ("less than two-thirds, and [less than] half, and [less than] a third"). ⭐ **Both authentic.**
- **Why this exact word:** **exact fractions** — ⭐ **so the believers know their effort was counted, down to the hour.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO-THIRDS, HALF, A THIRD — He RECORDED their effort precisely.** ⭐ **Mercy begins by being SEEN.** ⭐⭐ ***Adnā* (less/nearer) — same root as *DUNYĀ* (the nearer life, 87:16).** ⚠️ ***Thuluthay* = DUAL, nūn dropped.** ⭐ **v.3's options were all obeyed.**

**33. وَطَائِفَةٌ مِّنَ الَّذِينَ مَعَكَ — *wa ṭāʾifatun mina'lladhīna maʿak*** · v.20
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**GOING AROUND / circling**" family — *ṭāfa* = to go around, to circle; ⭐⭐ ***ṭawāf* = CIRCUMAMBULATION of the Kaʿbah**; ⭐ ***ṭāʾifah* = a GROUP, a party** (people who "go around" together — a circle of people); ⭐ ***aṭ-Ṭāʾif* = the CITY near Makkah** (named — reportedly — for the **wall that went around it**!); ⭐ *ṭāʾifiyyah* = sectarianism (modern); ⭐ **Ghāshiyah? no — Insān 76:15, 19: *yuṭāfu / yaṭūfu*** (served by those who **circle** around them!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and so does a group of those with you.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The companions stood the night too** — ⭐ **not alone; a *ṭāʾifah*, a circle of people around the Prophet ﷺ.** ⭐ ***Ṭāʾifah* comes from *ṭawāf*** — ⭐ **a group is "those who move around together."** ⭐⭐ **And you own the root from Insān 76:15, 19** — ***yuṭāfu** ʿalayhim bi-āniyatin* / *wa **yaṭūfu** ʿalayhim wildānun* — ⭐ **servants CIRCLING around the people of Paradise.** ⭐ **Those who circled the Prophet ﷺ in night prayer → circled by servants in Paradise.**
  ⭐ The everyday hooks: ⭐ ***ṭawāf*** (you know it from Hajj/ʿUmrah) and ⭐ ***aṭ-Ṭāʾif*** (the city).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ṭāʾifatun*** — **nominative**, joined to the subject of *taqūmu* ("you stand… **and** a group"). ⭐ *maʿaka* = "**with** you."
- **Why this exact word:** *ṭāʾifah* — ⭐ **a circle of companions, echoing the circling of ṭawāf.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṬAWĀF* (circling the Kaʿbah) → *ṬĀʾIFAH* (a group that "goes around" together) → *AṬ-ṬĀʾIF* (the walled city).** ⭐⭐ **Insān 76:15, 19: servants *yaṭūfu* (CIRCLE) around the people of Paradise.** ⭐ **They circled him at night; they'll be circled in Paradise.**

**34. وَاللَّهُ يُقَدِّرُ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارَ — *wa'llāhu yuqaddiru'l-layla wa'n-nahār*** · v.20
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**MEASURE / POWER / WORTH**" family — *qaddara* = **to measure out precisely**; ⭐ ***qadr* (Laylat al-Qadr)**; ⭐ your **most-tracked root** after *sawwā* (75:4, 76:16, 80:19, 87:3, 89:16, 90:5, 97:1).
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah measures out the night and the day.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The relief's LOGIC in four words:** ⭐ **You can't measure the night precisely — ⭐ but ALLAH does (*yuqaddiru*).** ⭐ **Only the One who measures the hours can know them exactly.** ⭐⭐ **And that's the setup for the very next phrase: "**He knows you cannot keep count**."** ⭐ **Humans estimate; Allah measures.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Yuqaddiru*** — **Form II, intensive: He measures with full precision**, ⭐ **lengthening nights in winter, shortening them in summer, minute by minute.** ⭐ And recall **Qadr 97:1** — ***laylat al-QADR*** — ⭐ **the night of measuring.**
  ⭐ And note the word order: ***wa'llāhu yuqaddiru*** — ⭐ **"Allah" FRONTED** — ⭐ **"It is ALLAH [and not you] who measures."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***Allāhu*** fronted (subject before verb) = **emphasis/restriction**. ⭐ **Form II present** (*yuqaddiru*) = ongoing, precise.
- **Why this exact word:** *qaddara* — ⭐ **contrasts divine precision with human estimation, justifying the relief.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **YOU can't measure the night exactly — ALLAH does (*yuqaddiru*).** ⭐ **Humans ESTIMATE; Allah MEASURES.** ⭐⭐ ***Qadr* root — SEVENTH+ surah (75:4 … 97:1, 73:20).** ⭐ ***Allāhu* FRONTED = "it is ALLAH who measures."**

**35. عَلِمَ أَن لَّن تُحْصُوهُ — *ʿalima an lan tuḥṣūh*** · v.20
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**COUNTING with PEBBLES**" family — ***ḥaṣā* = pebbles**; ***aḥṣā* = to count precisely, item by item**; ⭐ ***iḥṣāʾ* = STATISTICS**; ⭐ *ḥaṣāh* = a kidney stone.
- **Here it means:** "**He knows that you will not be able to keep exact count of it…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE PEBBLE ROOT — and this time, it's about what HUMANS can't do.**
  ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:29** — *wa kulla shayʾin **aḥṣaynāhu** kitābā* — ⭐ **ALLAH counts every thing.**
  ⭐ **Jinn 72:28** — *wa **aḥṣā** kulla shayʾin ʿadadā* — ⭐ **ALLAH counts every thing by number.**
  ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** — *ʿalima an lan **tuḥṣūhu*** — ⭐ **YOU cannot count it.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Allah does *iḥṣāʾ* of everything (78:29, 72:28); humans can't even do *iḥṣāʾ* of one night (73:20).** ⭐ **The pebble-count He keeps of your deeds — you couldn't keep of your own prayer hours.** ⭐ **And because He KNOWS that limit — He eases.**
  ⭐ And ***lan*** = "**will NEVER be able to**" — ⭐ **a permanent human limit, acknowledged by its Creator.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lan* + subjunctive** — ⚠️ ***tuḥṣūhu*** — the plural *nūn* dropped (subjunctive). ⭐ **Form IV**, weak-final.
- **Why this exact word:** *aḥṣā* — ⭐ **the same verb used for divine counting, now denied to humans, making the contrast explicit.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Iḥṣāʾ* = STATISTICS (pebble-counting).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:29 & Jinn 72:28: ALLAH counts EVERYTHING. Muzzammil 73:20: YOU can't even count ONE NIGHT.** ⭐ **He knows the limit — so He eases.**

**36. فَتَابَ عَلَيْكُمْ — *fa-tāba ʿalaykum*** · v.20
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**TURNING BACK / returning**" family — *tāba* = to turn back; ⭐ ***tawbah* = repentance** (the servant turning back to Allah); ⭐⭐ ***tāba ʿalā* (said of Allah) = He TURNED BACK toward someone in MERCY / relented / lightened**; ⭐ ***at-Tawwāb* = the Ever-Relenting** (⭐ **Naṣr 110:3!**).
- **Here it means:** "**…so He has turned to you [in mercy, lightening the obligation].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A crucial nuance: here *tāba ʿalaykum* is NOT about SIN.** ⭐ The companions **did nothing wrong** — they stood the night until their feet swelled. ⭐⭐ ***Tāba ʿalā* here = He TURNED TOWARD you with EASE — He LIGHTENED the burden.** ⭐ **Classical scholars: *tawbah* from Allah can mean forgiveness of sin OR the lifting of a hardship.** ⭐ Here it's the second.
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And you own *tawwāb* from Naṣr 110:3** — *innahu kāna **tawwābā*** — ⭐ **the *faʿʿāl* intensive: He TURNS BACK again and again, without limit.** ⭐ **Naṣr: He turns back to forgive. Muzzammil: He turns back to EASE.** ⭐ **Same root, two mercies.**
  ⭐ ⭐ And the ***fa*** ("**SO** He turned") — ⭐ **because He knew you couldn't count it** (*ʿalima an lan tuḥṣūhu*). ⭐ **Knowledge → mercy.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***tāba ʿalā*** = (of Allah) "relented toward." ⚠️ **Compare *tāba ilā* = (of the servant) "repented to."** ⭐ **The preposition tells you who's turning.**
- **Why this exact word:** *tāba ʿalaykum* — ⭐ **frames the easing as Allah turning toward them — personal, merciful.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Tāba ʿALĀ* (Allah) = turned toward you in MERCY. *Tāba ILĀ* (servant) = repented TO Him.** ⭐ **The preposition shows who's turning.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Here it's NOT about sin — it's LIFTING A HARDSHIP.** ⭐⭐ **Naṣr 110:3: *tawwāb* forgives. Muzzammil 73:20: He turns to EASE.**

**37. فَاقْرَءُوا مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ — *fa'qraʾū mā tayassara mina'l-qurʾān*** · v.20 (said twice)
- **Root feelings:** *iqraʾū* — **RECITE / read** (⭐ ***iqraʾ*** — ʿAlaq 96:1, the first word revealed!; *Qurʾān* = the Recitation). *tayassara* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**EASE / smoothness**" family — **Form V of *yasura*** = **to become easy, to be made easy**; ⭐ ***yusr* = ease**; ⭐ *taysīr* = facilitation (⭐ modern: *taysīr* = **making things easier**, simplifying!).
- **Here it means:** "**So recite what is easy of the Qurʾān.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE HEART OF THE RELIEF — and it completes the *yusr* thread you've traced for SIX surahs.**
  ⭐ **The *y-s-r* thread:**
  - **ʿAbasa 80:20** — *thumma's-sabīla **yassarah*** (the way — He **made it easy**)
  - **Inshiqāq 84:8** — *ḥisāban **yasīrā*** (an **easy** reckoning)
  - **Aʿlā 87:8** — *wa **nuyassiruka li'l-yusrā*** (We ease **you** toward ease)
  - **Layl 92:7, 10** — *fa-sa-**nuyassiruhu** li'l-yusrā / li'l-ʿusrā* (eased toward ease — or hardship)
  - **Sharḥ 94:5–6** — *inna maʿa'l-ʿusri **yusrā*** (with hardship, ease — **twice**)
  - ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** — *mā **tayassara*** (what is **easy**) — **TWICE**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And look at the structure of the WHOLE SURAH: ⭐ v.1–19 = the *ʿusr* (hardship of night prayer); v.20 = the *yusr* (recite what is easy).** ⭐ **Sharḥ 94:5–6 — *inna maʿa'l-ʿusri yusrā* — ENACTED.**
  ⭐⭐ **And *mā tayassara* appears TWICE in v.20** — ⭐ **just as *yusrā* appears twice in Sharḥ.** ⭐ **Two eases, again.**
  ⭐ And the command is ***iqraʾū*** — ⭐ **the very first word ever revealed (ʿAlaq 96:1).** ⭐ **The first command (read!) returns — now with the word EASE attached.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***tayassara*** — **Form V** (became easy — reflexive). ⭐ ***mā*** = "**whatever**" (relative). ⭐ ***iqraʾū*** — plural imperative, hamzah root.
- **Why this exact word:** *tayassara* — ⭐ **closes the hardship of v.1–19 with the exact root of Sharḥ's promise.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Mā TAYASSARA* = "what is EASY" — said TWICE (like *yusrā* twice in Sharḥ).** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHOLE SURAH: v.1–19 = HARDSHIP; v.20 = EASE → Sharḥ 94:5–6 ENACTED.** ⭐⭐ ***Y-s-r* thread: 80:20, 84:8, 87:8, 92:7, 94:5, 73:20.** ⭐ ***Iqraʾū* — the FIRST word revealed (96:1) returns, with EASE.** ⭐ ***Taysīr* = SIMPLIFYING.**

**38. عَلِمَ أَن سَيَكُونُ مِنكُم مَّرْضَىٰ — *ʿalima an sa-yakūnu minkum marḍā*** · v.20
- **Root feeling:** the "**ILLNESS**" family — *mariḍa* = to fall ill; ⭐ ***maraḍ* = DISEASE**; ⭐ ***marīḍ* (pl. *marḍā*) = a SICK person, a PATIENT**; ⭐ *mumarriḍ / mumarriḍah* = a **NURSE**; ⭐ *tamrīḍ* = **NURSING**; ⭐ *maraḍ fī qulūbihim* = **"disease in their hearts"** (Muddaththir 74:31 — spiritual illness!).
- **Here it means:** "**He knows that among you there will be the sick…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The FIRST of three reasons for the ease — and it's ILLNESS.** ⭐ Allah names the **sick** explicitly as people whose limits He accounts for. ⭐⭐ **The law is shaped around the patient.** ⭐ This verse is a foundation for the principle that **worship adjusts to capacity** — ⭐ ***lā yukallifu'llāhu nafsan illā wusʿahā*** ("Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity," 2:286).
  ⭐⭐ **Three reasons, three kinds of people (v.20):** ⭐ **the SICK (*marḍā*), the TRAVELLER-TRADERS (*yaḍribūna fi'l-arḍ*), the FIGHTERS (*yuqātilūna*).** ⭐ **Body, livelihood, defence** — ⭐ **every major demand on human time is acknowledged.**
  ⭐ And note ***sa-yakūnu*** — "**there WILL be**" — ⭐ **future tense: Allah planned for illness before it arrived.**
  ⭐ The root's spiritual sense returns in **Muddaththir 74:31** — *alladhīna fī qulūbihim **maraḍ*** — ⭐ **"those in whose hearts is DISEASE."** ⭐ **Physical illness excused (73:20); spiritual illness exposed (74:31).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***sa-*** + *yakūnu* = future. ⭐ ***marḍā*** — broken plural of *marīḍ* (*faʿlā* pattern), weak-final.
- **Why this exact word:** *marḍā* — ⭐ **the first named reason for mercy is human frailty itself.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MARĪḌ* = a PATIENT; *mumarriḍah* = a NURSE; *tamrīḍ* = NURSING.** ⭐ **The law is SHAPED AROUND THE PATIENT.** ⭐⭐ **Three reasons: the SICK, the TRADERS, the FIGHTERS — body, livelihood, defence.** ⭐⭐ **73:20: physical illness EXCUSED. 74:31: heart-disease EXPOSED.** ⭐ ***Sa-yakūnu* — He planned for illness in advance.**

**39. يَضْرِبُونَ فِي الْأَرْضِ يَبْتَغُونَ مِن فَضْلِ اللَّهِ — *yaḍribūna fi'l-arḍi yabtaghūna min faḍli'llāh*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *yaḍribūn* — ⭐ the "**STRIKING**" family — *ḍaraba* = to strike; ⭐⭐ **but the idiom *ḍaraba fi'l-arḍ* = to TRAVEL (for trade)** — literally "**to strike out into the land**" (⭐ like a traveller's feet striking the ground); ⭐ ***ḍarb* = a type / a strike**; ⭐ *ḍarībah* = a **TAX**; ⭐ *iḍrāb* = a **STRIKE** (workers'!). *yabtaghūn* — ⭐ **seeking out** (*ibtighāʾ* — ⭐ **Layl 92:20**, *ibtighāʾa wajhi rabbih*). *faḍl* — ⭐ the "**SURPLUS / bounty / grace**" family — *faḍl* = bounty, extra; ⭐ ***tafaḍḍal!* = "PLEASE, go ahead!"** (literally "do me the favour"!); ⭐ *afḍal* = better/best.
- **Here it means:** "**…and others travelling through the land seeking of the bounty of Allah…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TRADE is called "seeking Allah's BOUNTY."** ⭐ Earning a living isn't treated as worldly distraction — ⭐ **it's *ibtighāʾ min faḍli'llāh* — seeking HIS grace.** ⭐⭐ **And the trade is recognized as a valid reason to ease the night prayer** — ⭐ **you can't travel all day and stand all night.** ⭐ **The Qurʾān dignifies earning.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And you know who traded: Quraysh 106:2** — *riḥlata'sh-shitāʾi wa'ṣ-ṣayf* — ⭐ **the winter and summer CARAVANS.** ⭐ **Quraysh's travels were praised as Allah's *īlāf* (security); here, believers' travels are called seeking His *faḍl*.**
  ⭐⭐ **And *ibtighāʾ* — Layl 92:20** — *illa'**btighāʾa wajhi** rabbihi'l-aʿlā* — ⭐ **seeking His FACE (in charity).** ⭐ **Here: seeking His BOUNTY (in trade).** ⭐ **Same verb — the face and the bounty both sought from Him.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***tafaḍḍal!* = "Please!"**; ⭐ ***ḍarībah* = a TAX**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***ḍaraba fi'l-arḍ* = an IDIOM for "travel."** ⚠️ **Don't translate "strike."** ⭐ ***yabtaghūna*** = *ḥāl* ("**while** seeking"). ⭐ ***wa ākharūna*** ("**and others**") introduces each group.
- **Why this exact word:** *faḍl* — ⭐ **frames earning as receiving grace, not grabbing wealth.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḌARABA FI'L-ARḌ* = an IDIOM: to TRAVEL** ("strike out into the land"). ⚠️ **Not "strike."** ⭐⭐⭐ **TRADE = "seeking Allah's BOUNTY (*faḍl*)."** ⭐ **The Qurʾān dignifies earning.** ⭐⭐ **Layl 92:20: seeking His FACE. Muzzammil 73:20: seeking His BOUNTY.** ⭐ ***Tafaḍḍal!* = "PLEASE!"; *ḍarībah* = a TAX.** ⭐ **Quraysh 106:2: the caravans.**

**40. وَآخَرُونَ يُقَاتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ — *wa ākharūna yuqātilūna fī sabīli'llāh*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *ākharūn* — ⭐ **OTHERS** (*ākhar* = another; ⚠️ **NOT *ākhir* = last** — one vowel!). *yuqātilūn* — ⭐ **Form III of *qatala*** = **to FIGHT (mutually — fighting with someone who fights back)**; ⭐ *qitāl* = combat; ⭐ *muqātil* = a fighter. *sabīl* — **a WAY / road** (v.19 *sabīlā*).
- **Here it means:** "**…and others fighting in the way of Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The third reason for ease: those defending the community.** ⭐ ⭐ **Form III (*fāʿala*) = MUTUAL action** — ⭐ ***qātala* = to fight against someone who also fights** — ⭐ **combat, not one-sided killing.** ⭐ (Compare *qatala*, Form I = to kill; ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:17** *qutila'l-insān*.)
  ⭐⭐ **This mention of fighting is why many scholars hold v.20 was revealed in MADĪNAH** — ⭐ **armed defence wasn't permitted in the Makkan period.** ⭐ **So one verse may span a decade.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Fī sabīli'llāh*** — ⭐ **"in the WAY of Allah"** — ⭐ and v.19 just said *ittakhadha ilā rabbihi **sabīlā*** (take **a way** to his Lord). ⭐ **The *sabīl* to Him (v.19) and fighting in His *sabīl* (v.20).**
  ⭐ ⚠️ **TRAP: *ākharūn* (OTHERS, short *a* after kh) ≠ *ākhirah* (the Hereafter / the LAST, *i* after kh).** ⭐ **One vowel.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form III = reciprocity/mutual action** (⭐ like *qātala*, *ḥāraba*, *jāhada*). ⭐ ***ākharūna*** = plural of *ākhar* (another), a diptote-shaped pattern in some forms.
- **Why this exact word:** *yuqātilūna* (Form III) — ⭐ **names defensive, reciprocal combat as a recognized demand on time.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FORM III = MUTUAL** — *qātala* = fight someone who fights BACK (≠ *qatala*, to kill). ⭐⭐ **Mentions fighting → many scholars: v.20 is MADINAN.** ⭐ **One verse may span a decade.** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***ĀKHARŪN* (OTHERS) ≠ *ĀKHIRAH* (Hereafter/LAST).**

**41. وَأَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَآتُوا الزَّكَاةَ — *wa aqīmu'ṣ-ṣalāta wa ātu'z-zakāh*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *aqīmū* — ⭐ **Form IV of *qāma*** = **to MAKE STAND, to establish** (⭐ the *q-w-m* root). *ātū* — ⭐ **Form IV of *atā*** = **to GIVE** (⭐ *yuʾtī*, Layl 92:18). *zakāh* — ⭐ **purify + grow**.
- **Here it means:** "**And establish the prayer and give the zakāh…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The ease does NOT remove the core.** ⭐ **Night prayer is lightened — but the FIVE PRAYERS and ZAKĀH stand firm.** ⭐ **The relief is in the extra; the foundation stays.**
  ⭐⭐ **And you own this pair from Bayyinah 98:5** — *wa **yuqīmu'ṣ-ṣalāta wa yuʾtu'z-zakāh*** — ⭐ **the definition of "the upright religion."** ⭐ **Same two pillars, two surahs.** ⭐ And **Kawthar 108:2** — *fa-ṣalli li-rabbika wa'nḥar* — ⭐ **pray + give.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Aqīmū* (MAKE IT STAND) — the *q-w-m* root, again.** ⭐ **The surah opened with *qum* (STAND, v.2) — and ends with *aqīmū* (MAKE THE PRAYER STAND, v.20).** ⭐ **From standing yourself up at night to standing the prayer up all day.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***aqīmū*** — Form IV imperative of a hollow verb. ⭐ ***ātū*** — Form IV imperative of a weak verb (*ātā*, to give).
- **Why this exact word:** *aqīmū* (**make it stand**) — ⭐ **because what's commanded is a structure kept upright, not a performance.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The ease lightens the EXTRA; the five prayers and zakāh STAND FIRM.** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.2 *QUM* (stand YOURSELF) → v.20 *AQĪMŪ* (make the PRAYER stand).** ⭐ **Same root, bookends.** ⭐⭐ **Bayyinah 98:5: *yuqīmu'ṣ-ṣalāta wa yuʾtu'z-zakāh* — same two pillars.**

**42. وَأَقْرِضُوا اللَّهَ قَرْضًا حَسَنًا — *wa aqriḍu'llāha qarḍan ḥasanā*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *aqriḍū* — ⭐⭐ the "**CUTTING OFF a piece**" family — *qaraḍa* = **to cut, to snip**; ⭐⭐ ***miqrāḍ* = SCISSORS!**; ⭐ ***qarḍ* = a LOAN** (a piece **cut off** from your wealth and handed over); ⭐ ***qurūḍ* = loans** (modern banking!); ⭐ *inqaraḍa* = to become **EXTINCT** (cut off entirely!); ⭐ *qāriḍ* = a **RODENT** (one that gnaws/cuts!). *ḥasan* — **good, beautiful** (⭐ *al-ḥusnā*, Layl 92:6).
- **Here it means:** "**…and lend Allah a goodly loan.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST ASTONISHING PHRASE IN THE SURAH.**
  ⭐ ***LEND ALLAH a loan.*** ⭐ **The One who is *aṣ-Ṣamad*** (Ikhlāṣ 112:2) — **who needs nothing** — ⭐ **asks for a LOAN.** ⭐⭐ **Why "loan" and not "gift"?** ⭐ **Because a loan is REPAID.** ⭐ **The word itself is a guarantee:** ⭐ **what you give will come back — and (2:245) multiplied many times.** ⭐ **Allah frames charity as borrowing from you — so you know He'll pay it back.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the root image: *qaraḍa* = to CUT — *miqrāḍ* = SCISSORS.** ⭐ **A *qarḍ* is a piece you SNIP from your wealth and hand over.** ⭐ **Snip a piece off — it grows back larger.**
  ⭐⭐ **Contrast the juzʾ's misers:** ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:7** — *yamnaʿūna'l-māʿūn* (they won't even lend a **POT**). ⭐ **Humazah 104:2** — *jamaʿa mālan wa ʿaddadah* (gathered and **COUNTED** it). ⭐ **Layl 92:8** — *bakhila wa'staghnā* (**withheld**). ⭐ **Here: lend to ALLAH Himself.**
  ⭐ And ***ḥasanan*** — "**goodly**" — ⭐ classical: **from lawful wealth, with sincerity, without *mann* (reminding of the favour)** — ⭐ compare **Muddaththir 74:6** (*wa lā tamnun tastakthir*) and **Tīn 95:6** (*ajrun ghayru **mamnūn***).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV imperative** + *Allāha* (object) + **cognate accusative with adjective** (*qarḍan ḥasanan*).
- **Why this exact word:** *qarḍ* (**a loan**) — ⭐ **the most reassuring possible word for giving: it implies certain repayment.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIQRĀḌ* = SCISSORS — a *qarḍ* (LOAN) is a piece SNIPPED from your wealth.** ⭐⭐⭐ **LEND ALLAH a loan — the One who needs NOTHING (*aṣ-Ṣamad*, 112:2) asks to BORROW.** ⭐ **"Loan," not "gift" — because a loan is REPAID.** ⭐⭐ **vs. Māʿūn 107:7 (won't lend a POT), Humazah 104:2 (COUNTED it).** ⭐ ***Inqaraḍa* = EXTINCT; *qāriḍ* = a RODENT.**

**43. وَمَا تُقَدِّمُوا لِأَنفُسِكُم مِّنْ خَيْرٍ تَجِدُوهُ عِندَ اللَّهِ هُوَ خَيْرًا وَأَعْظَمَ أَجْرًا — *wa mā tuqaddimū li-anfusikum min khayrin tajidūhu ʿinda'llāhi huwa khayran wa aʿẓama ajrā*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *tuqaddimū* — ⭐ the "**SENDING FORWARD / ahead**" family — ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:13**, **Nabaʾ 78:40**, **Infiṭār 82:5**, **Fajr 89:24**. *anfus* — **SELVES** (*nafs*). *tajidūhu* — **you will FIND it** (*wajada*). *aʿẓam* — **GREATER** (*ʿaẓīm*). *ajr* — ⭐ **a WAGE / reward** (⭐ *ujrah* = a fee; ⚠️ not *yujīr* from Jinn 72:22!).
- **Here it means:** "**And whatever good you send ahead for yourselves, you will find it with Allah — better and greater in reward.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The "sending ahead" thread — and THIS time it's an encouragement, not a regret.**
  - **Qiyāmah 75:13** — man will be **informed** of *mā qaddama wa akhkhar* (what he sent ahead / left behind)
  - **Nabaʾ 78:40** — *mā qaddamat yadāh* (what his hands **sent ahead**)
  - **Infiṭār 82:5** — *ʿalimat nafsun mā qaddamat* (a soul will **know** what it sent ahead)
  - **Fajr 89:24** — *yā laytanī **qaddamtu** li-ḥayātī* ⭐ ("**I wish I had sent ahead** for my life!" — REGRET)
  - ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** — *mā **tuqaddimū li-anfusikum*** ⭐ ("whatever you **send ahead for yourselves**" — INVITATION)
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Fajr 89:24 is the wish — too late. Muzzammil 73:20 is the chance — now.** ⭐ **Same verb, same "for my/your-SELF"!** ⭐ **Fajr: *li-ḥayātī* (for MY life). Muzzammil: *li-anfusikum* (for YOURSELVES).** ⭐ **Do now what the regretful man wishes he'd done.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Tajidūhu* = you will FIND it** — ⭐ **like a deposit waiting in an account.** ⭐ **Nothing lost** (Jinn 72:13 — *lā yakhāfu bakhsā*, no short-changing).
  ⭐ **And note: *li-anfusikum* — "for YOURSELVES."** ⭐ **The loan to Allah (previous phrase) turns out to be a deposit for YOU.** ⭐ **You lend Him; you're the beneficiary.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mā* + jussive (*tuqaddimū*) + jussive (*tajidūhu*)** = conditional ("whatever… you will find") — ⚠️ **both drop the plural nūn**. ⭐⭐ ***huwa khayran*** — ⚠️ ***huwa* (separating pronoun) + *khayran* (ACCUSATIVE)** — ⭐ **because *tajidūhu* takes TWO objects ("find it [to be] better"), so *khayran* is the second object, and *huwa* just adds emphasis.** ⭐ **A famous grammar point.**
- **Why this exact word:** *qaddama* — ⭐ **turns the Hereafter's regret-verb into today's opportunity.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Fajr 89:24: "I WISH I had sent ahead (*qaddamtu*) for my life" — REGRET. ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20: "whatever you send ahead (*tuqaddimū*) for yourselves" — INVITATION.** ⭐ **Do NOW what he wished he'd done.** ⭐⭐ ***Tajidūhu* = you'll FIND it — a deposit waiting.** ⭐ **The loan to Allah is a deposit for YOU.** ⚠️⭐ ***Huwa khayran* — *khayran* ACCUSATIVE (2nd object of *tajidū*).**

**44. وَاسْتَغْفِرُوا اللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ — *wa'staghfiru'llāha inna'llāha ghafūrun raḥīm*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *istaghfirū* — ⭐ **Form X of *ghafara*** = **SEEK covering / forgiveness**; ⭐ ***mighfar* = a HELMET** (covers and protects!); ⭐ **Naṣr 110:3** *wa'staghfirh*. *ghafūr* — ⭐ ***faʿūl* intensive = ever-forgiving, covering again and again**. *raḥīm* — the "**MERCY / womb**" family — ⭐ ***raḥim* = the WOMB**; *raḥmah* = mercy (⭐ **Balad 90:17** *marḥamah*).
- **Here it means:** "**And seek forgiveness of Allah. Indeed, Allah is Ever-Forgiving, Most Merciful.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The surah ends — after all that worship — with *ISTIGHFĀR*.**
  ⭐ **They stood the night until their feet swelled; they recited with *tartīl*; they're told to pray, give, lend to Allah — ⭐ AND THEN: "seek forgiveness."** ⭐⭐ **Why?** ⭐ **Because no worship is ever perfect.** ⭐ ***Istighfār* covers the gaps in your good deeds**, not just your sins. ⭐ **It's how you close every act of worship** — ⭐ ***astaghfiru'llāh* ×3 after every prayer.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And you own the identical move from Naṣr 110:3** — ⭐ ***fa-sabbiḥ bi-ḥamdi rabbika **wa'staghfirh*** — **victory ends in *istighfār*.** ⭐⭐ **Naṣr: VICTORY ends in *istighfār*. Muzzammil: WORSHIP ends in *istighfār*.** ⭐ **Every completed task closes with asking forgiveness.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And Form X again:** ⭐ ***istaghnā*** ("I need **nothing**" — ʿAbasa 80:5, Layl 92:8, ʿAlaq 96:7) **vs** ***istaghfir*** ("I need **forgiveness**"). ⭐ **Same form, opposite souls.**
  ⭐ And the pair ***ghafūr raḥīm*** — ⭐ **Ghafūr = covers your sins (the *mighfar*, helmet); Raḥīm = shows active mercy (from the *raḥim*, womb).** ⭐ **Covering + nurturing.** ⭐⭐ **The surah that began with a man WRAPPED UP in garments (*muzzammil*) ends with Allah's *maghfirah* — His covering.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Muzzammil* (wrapped) → *ghafūr* (the One who covers).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form X imperative** (*istaghfirū*). ⭐ ***ghafūrun raḥīmun*** — two intensive adjectives (*faʿūl*, *faʿīl*), *khabar* of *inna*.
- **Why this exact word:** ending on ***istighfār* + *Ghafūr Raḥīm*** — ⭐ **so the believer leaves even their best effort in His covering.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **After ALL that worship → "SEEK FORGIVENESS."** ⭐ ***Istighfār* covers the GAPS in your good deeds.** ⭐ **(*Astaghfiru'llāh* ×3 after every prayer.)** ⭐⭐⭐ **Naṣr 110:3: VICTORY ends in *istighfār*. Muzzammil 73:20: WORSHIP ends in it.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUZZAMMIL* (wrapped in garments, v.1) → *GHAFŪR* (the One who COVERS, v.20).** ⭐ **The surah opens wrapped and closes covered.** ⭐ ***Mighfar* = HELMET; *raḥim* = WOMB.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **النَّعْمَةِ (v.11)** — ⚠️ ***naʿmah* (FATḤAH) = LUXURY**; ⭐ ***niʿmah* (KASRAH) = a BLESSING.** **One vowel — gratitude vs. self-indulgence.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **v.19 = Insān 76:29 word-for-word** — ⚠️ **don't learn them as two verses.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **فَتَابَ عَلَيْكُمْ (v.20)** — ⚠️ **NOT about sin here** — ⭐ **Allah turning to them with EASE.** ⭐ ***Tāba ʿalā* (Allah relents) ≠ *tāba ilā* (servant repents).**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْمُزَّمِّلُ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **Form V with ASSIMILATION** (*mutazammil* → *muzzammil*). ⭐ **Same rule as ʿAbasa's *yazzakkā* and Muddaththir's *muddaththir*.**
5. ⭐⭐ **مُنفَطِرٌ (v.18)** — ⚠️ **MASCULINE for a feminine *samāʾ*** — ⭐ **"a thing of splitting" / sky as "ceiling."** **Not an error.**
6. ⭐⭐ **تَبَتَّلْ ... تَبْتِيلًا (v.8)** — ⚠️ **MIXED forms (Form V verb + Form II maṣdar)** — ⭐ **deliberate, combining both meanings.**
7. ⭐⭐ **وَذَرْنِي (v.11)** — ⚠️ ***dhar* is from a DEFECTIVE verb (no past tense)**; ⭐ ***wāw al-maʿiyyah*** ("with") makes *al-mukadhdhibīn* accusative.
8. ⭐⭐ **يَضْرِبُونَ فِي الْأَرْضِ (v.20)** — ⚠️ **an IDIOM: to TRAVEL**, not "strike."
9. ⭐⭐ **وَبِيلًا (v.16)** — ⚠️ **from *w-b-l* (heavy downpour)**, ⭐ **NOT *wabāʾ* (epidemic, *w-b-ʾ*).**
10. ⭐⭐ **آخَرُونَ (v.20)** — ⚠️ ***ākharūn* = OTHERS**; ⭐ **≠ *ākhirah* (the Hereafter/last).**
11. ⭐⭐ **هُوَ خَيْرًا (v.20)** — ⚠️ ***khayran* is ACCUSATIVE** — the second object of *tajidūhu*; *huwa* is just emphasis.
12. ⭐⭐ **رَسُولًا → الرَّسُولَ (v.15–16)** — ⚠️ **indefinite then definite = "THE one just mentioned"** (*al-ʿahd adh-dhikrī*).
13. ⭐ **ثُلُثَيِ (v.20)** — ⚠️ **DUAL with nūn dropped**; ⭐ ***niṣfahu/thuluthahu* has two authentic recitations** (accusative / genitive).
14. ⭐ **نَاشِئَةَ (v.6)** — ⚠️ **NOT related to *inshāʾallāh*** (that's *shāʾa*).
15. ⭐ **أُولِي (v.11)** — ⚠️ **the *wāw* is written but SILENT.**
16. ⭐ **يَوْمًا (v.17)** — ⚠️ **OBJECT of *tattaqūn*** — "shield yourselves **FROM** a Day," not "on a day."
