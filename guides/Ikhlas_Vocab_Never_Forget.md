# Surah Al-Ikhlāṣ (112) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–4)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It opens and closes on the same word — *aḥad* — and that one word means two opposite things.**

⭐ **v.1:** *qul huwa'llāhu **AḤAD*** — "Say: He is Allah, **the ONE**."
⭐ **v.4:** *wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan **AḤAD*** — "And there is not to Him any equal — **ANYONE**."

⭐⭐⭐ ***Aḥad* has two lives in Arabic:**
- ⭐ **In an AFFIRMATIVE sentence** — ⭐ ***aḥad* = THE absolutely unique One** — ⭐ **and it is used this way ONLY for Allah.**
- ⭐ **In a NEGATIVE sentence** — ⭐ ***aḥad* = "anyone at all"** — ⭐ **a general word for any person.**

⭐⭐ **So the surah begins "**He is AḤAD**" and ends "**there is no AḤAD like Him**."** ⭐ **The first *aḥad* is Him; the last *aḥad* is everyone else — and none of them compares.** ⭐ **One word as the two bookends of tawḥīd.**

⭐ **The name of the surah is the word you met in Bayyinah 98:5** — ***mukhliṣīna** lahu'd-dīn* — ⭐ ***ikhlāṣ* = PURITY, unmixed**, like pure gold. ⭐ **This is the surah of **unmixed** belief about Allah.** ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ said it is equal to **a third of the Qurʾān**.

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ ***AḤAD* … *AḤAD*.** ⭐ **First: "He is the One." Last: "there is no one like Him."** ⭐ **Same word, both ends.**

---

## The Big Picture

*⭐ Context: it is reported the idolaters (and others) asked the Prophet ﷺ, "Describe your Lord to us — what is His lineage?" ⭐ **This surah is the answer: four verses that describe Him mostly by what He is NOT.***

1. **Who He is** (v.1) — ⭐ **He is Allah, the One.**
2. **What He is** (v.2) — ⭐ **Allah, the Self-Sufficient Master to whom all turn.**
3. **What He is not** (v.3) — ⭐ **He neither begets nor is begotten.**
4. **The seal** (v.4) — ⭐ **nothing is comparable to Him.**

## §1 — Who He Is (v.1)

**1. قُلْ هُوَ — *qul huwa*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *qul* — ⭐ **SAY** (the second of the four *qul* surahs). *huwa* — ⭐ **HE**.
- **Here it means:** "**Say: He is…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the answer begins with a PRONOUN — before the Name.**
  
  ⭐ **They asked: "**Who is your Lord?**"** ⭐ **The answer doesn't start with "Allah." ⭐ **It starts with ***HUWA*** — "**HE**."
  
  ⭐⭐ **Two classical readings, both beautiful:**
  - ⭐ **(a) *ḍamīr ash-shaʾn*** — ⭐ **the "pronoun of the matter": "**The matter is this:** Allah is One."** ⭐ **A pronoun that announces something weighty is coming.**
  - ⭐ **(b) "He" — the One you're asking about** — ⭐ **as if He needs no introduction; ⭐ **the questioner already, deep down, knows Who is meant.**
  
  ⭐ **And *qul* again proves verbatim transmission** — ⭐ **you own this from Kāfirūn 109:1.** ⭐ **The four Quls:** ⭐ **Kāfirūn 109 (disavowal), Ikhlāṣ 112 (who He is), Falaq 113 & Nās 114 (seeking refuge in Him).**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the pairing with Kāfirūn is famous:** ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ recited them together in the two sunnah rakʿahs before Fajr.** ⭐ **Kāfirūn says **what I do NOT worship**; ⭐ **Ikhlāṣ says **Whom I DO**.** ⭐ **Negation, then affirmation — exactly like *lā ilāha illa'llāh*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***huwa*** as *ḍamīr ash-shaʾn* (pronoun of the matter) or as a direct referent. ⭐ ***Qul*** — hollow-verb imperative.
- **Why this word:** starting with ***huwa*** ⭐ **treats Him as already known** — the opposite of how you'd introduce a stranger.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The answer starts with *HUWA* — "HE" — before the Name.** ⭐ **"The matter is this…" (*ḍamīr ash-shaʾn*).** ⭐⭐ **Kāfirūn = what I DON'T worship; Ikhlāṣ = Whom I DO** — ⭐ **like *lā ilāha* + *illa'llāh*.** ⭐ **Recited together before Fajr.**

**2. أَحَدٌ — *aḥad*** · v.1, 4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**ONENESS**" family — *w-ḥ-d* (the *hamzah* in *aḥad* replaced an original *wāw*) — ⭐ ***wāḥid* = one*** (a number); ⭐ ***tawḥīd* = making/declaring ONE**; ⭐ ***waḥdah* = unity / solitude.**
- **Here it means:** "**the One [and Only].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **why *aḥad* and not *wāḥid*? — the most important word distinction in the surah.**
  
  ⭐ ***WĀḤID* = "one" as a NUMBER*** — ⭐ **it can START a count: one, two, three…** ⭐ **One apple; there could be another.**
  ⭐ ***AḤAD* = ABSOLUTELY UNIQUE*** — ⭐ **one that has no second of any kind, cannot be counted with anything, cannot be divided.** ⭐ **Not "the first of a series" — ⭐ **outside every series.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And in an AFFIRMATIVE statement, *aḥad* is used for NO ONE but Allah.** ⭐ **You can say "*wāḥid* man came" — ⭐ **you cannot say "*aḥad* man came" as a positive statement.** ⭐ **The word is reserved.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And then v.4 uses the SAME word in a NEGATIVE sentence** — ⭐ ***wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan **aḥad*** — "**not ANYONE** is equal to Him."** ⭐ **In negation, *aḥad* = "anyone."**
  
  ⭐⭐ **So the surah is framed: ⭐ **v.1 *aḥad* = HIM; v.4 *aḥad* = ALL OTHERS.** ⭐ **He is *aḥad*; no *aḥad* is like Him.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **The historical hook: ⭐ **Bilāl ibn Rabāḥ (رضي الله عنه)**, pinned under a boulder in the Makkan sun, ⭐ **repeating only: "*AḤAD… AḤAD…*"** ⭐ **One word was his entire creed.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note *aḥadun* is INDEFINITE here** — ⭐ **while v.2's *aṣ-ṣamad* is DEFINITE.** ⭐ **Classical note: the indefinite *aḥad* expresses **magnificence beyond full comprehension**; the definite *aṣ-ṣamad* expresses **a known, established reality** (everyone turns to Him).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***Allāhu aḥad*** — *Allāh* is the *khabar* of *huwa* (or subject), and *aḥad* a second *khabar*. ⚠️ ⭐ ***Aḥad* in affirmation = only Allah; in negation = "anyone."**
- **Why this word:** *aḥad* over *wāḥid* — ⭐ **because *wāḥid* would allow a second.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WĀḤID* = one (can begin a COUNT). *AḤAD* = UNIQUE (outside every count).** ⭐⭐⭐ **In AFFIRMATION, *aḥad* is used ONLY for Allah. In NEGATION, it means "anyone."** ⭐⭐ **v.1: He is AḤAD. v.4: no AḤAD like Him.** ⭐ **Bilāl under the rock: "AḤAD, AḤAD."**

## §2 — What He Is (v.2)

**3. اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ — *Allāhu'ṣ-ṣamad*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**SOLID / turned-to / relied upon**" family — ***ṣamad*** has two classical core meanings, both ancient: ⭐ **(a) the MASTER to whom people GO in their needs** (*ṣamada ilayhi* = to direct oneself toward someone for help); ⭐ **(b) SOLID, with NO HOLLOW inside** — ⭐ **something that doesn't eat, doesn't drink, has no inner need to be filled.**
- **Here it means:** "**Allah, the Self-Sufficient, the One to whom all turn in need.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE GREATEST PAYOFF IN THE ENTIRE JUZʾ — it answers *istaghnā*.**
  
  ⭐ ***Aṣ-Ṣamad* holds two facts at once:**
  - ⭐ **HE needs NOTHING** — ⭐ **solid, no hollow, no inner lack.**
  - ⭐ **EVERYTHING needs HIM** — ⭐ **every creature turns to Him for its needs.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW RECALL THE JUZʾ'S DEEPEST DISEASE — *istaghnāʾ*:**
  - **ʿAbasa 80:5** — *ammā mani'**staghnā*** (⭐ the chief who **considered himself needing nothing**)
  - **Layl 92:8** — *bakhila wa'**staghnā*** (⭐ the withholder)
  - **ʿAlaq 96:7** — *an raʾāhu'**staghnā*** (⭐ **the ROOT CAUSE** of all transgression)
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Self-sufficiency — needing nothing — is ***aṣ-Ṣamad***'s attribute.** ⭐ **The *mustaghnī* was claiming **a quality that belongs only to Allah.** ⭐⭐ **That's why it's the root of transgression: ⭐ **it's a quiet claim to be *ṣamad* yourself.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the cure was always the same: ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:8** — *wa wajadaka ʿāʾilan **fa-aghnā*** (⭐ **"He found you in need and HE enriched you"**), ⭐ **Naṣr 110:3** — ***wa'staghfirh*** (⭐ **"seek His forgiveness" — admit your need**).** ⭐ **The servant needs; ⭐ **only *aṣ-Ṣamad* doesn't.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the "no hollow" meaning has a vivid echo:** ⭐ **Quraysh 106:4** — *aṭʿamahum min jūʿ* — ⭐ **He FED them from hunger.** ⭐ **Creatures are hollow — they get hungry, they must be filled. ⭐ **He is *ṣamad* — never hungry, and the One who fills every hunger.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note *Allāh* is REPEATED** (v.1 *huwa'llāhu aḥad*, v.2 *Allāhu'ṣ-ṣamad*) — ⭐ **rather than "He is the Ṣamad."** ⭐ **The Name stated twice for emphasis and majesty.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **And *aṣ-ṣamad* is DEFINITE** — ⭐ **"THE Ṣamad" = the ONLY one** (restriction by the article). ⭐ **Not "a" master — *the* one, exclusively.**
  
  ⭐ **This is the ONLY occurrence of *aṣ-Ṣamad* in the entire Qurʾān.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***Allāhu'ṣ-ṣamad*** — a nominal sentence; ⭐ **the definite predicate = restriction ("Allah — and only Allah — is the Ṣamad").**
- **Why this word:** *ṣamad* — ⭐ **because it names in one word both His total independence and everyone's total dependence on Him.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AṢ-ṢAMAD* = (a) needs NOTHING (solid, no hollow) + (b) EVERYTHING needs Him.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Istaghnā* (80:5, 92:8, 96:7) = claiming to need nothing = ⭐ **claiming *aṣ-Ṣamad*'s attribute.** ⭐ **That's why it's the root of transgression.** ⭐⭐ **The cure: *aghnā* (93:8, HE enriches) and *istaghfir* (110:3, admit your need).** ⭐ **The ONLY occurrence of *aṣ-Ṣamad* in the Qurʾān.**

## §3 — What He Is Not (v.3)

**4. لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ — *lam yalid wa lam yūlad*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**BEGETTING / giving birth**" family — *walada* = to beget, give birth; ⭐ ***wālid* = a parent***; ⭐ ***walad* = a child***; ⭐ ***mawlid* = a birth/birthday***; ⭐ ***wildān* = boys** (⭐ **Insān 76:19!**).
- **Here it means:** "**He neither begets, nor is He begotten.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **two verbs — active then passive — and the root closes a thread from Balad.**
  
  ⭐ ***Lam YALID* (active) = "He does NOT BEGET"*** — ⭐ **no children, no offspring.**
  ⭐ ***Lam YŪLAD* (passive) = "He was NOT BEGOTTEN"*** — ⭐ **no parent, no origin.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Downstream and upstream, both closed.** ⭐ **Nothing came from Him (as a child), nothing came before Him (as a parent).** ⭐ **The *ṣamad* of v.2 has no lineage in either direction.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the ORDER is deliberate:** ⭐ ***lam yalid* comes FIRST** — ⭐ **because the more widespread false claim was that Allah HAS offspring** (angels as "daughters," or a divine son). ⭐ **Refute the common error first, then the rarer one.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW THE BALAD CONNECTION:** ⭐ **Balad 90:3** — *wa **wālidin wa mā walad*** — ⭐ "**by a BEGETTER and what he BEGOT**." ⭐ **Balad SWORE BY the pattern of begetting — every parent and child — ⭐ **as the defining pattern of creation, the root of human *kabad* (toil, 90:4).** ⭐⭐ **Ikhlāṣ declares Allah ⭐ **OUTSIDE that pattern entirely.** ⭐ **Every created thing is either a *wālid* or a *mawlūd* (usually both); ⭐ **He is neither.**
  
  ⭐ **And it answers the original question** ("**what is His lineage?**"): ⭐ **He has none.** ⭐ **Lineage is a creaturely thing.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note *lam* + jussive = PAST negation** ("did not"), ⭐ **but with Allah's attributes it means **never, at any time**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lam* + jussive** ×2. ⚠️ ⭐ ***yalid*** — from *walada*, the initial *wāw* **drops** in the present (*yalidu*), ⭐ **jussive *yalid*.** ⭐ ***yūlad*** — **passive**, the *wāw* returns as the long *ū*.
- **Why this word:** two verbs, **active then passive** — ⭐ **the most complete possible denial of lineage.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Lam YALID* = no CHILD (downstream). *Lam YŪLAD* = no PARENT (upstream).** ⭐ **Both directions closed.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Balad 90:3 swore by "a BEGETTER and what he BEGOT" — creation's pattern. ⭐ **Ikhlāṣ: He is OUTSIDE it.** ⭐ ***Mawlid* = a birthday** — same root. ⭐ **Common error refuted FIRST.**

## §4 — The Seal (v.4)

**5. وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ — *wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan aḥad*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**EQUIVALENCE / being a match**" family — *kafuʾa* = to be equal, equivalent; ⭐ ***kufʾ* = an equal, a peer, a match***; ⭐ ***kafāʾah* = COMPATIBILITY in marriage*** (⭐ **and in modern Arabic, *kafāʾah* = COMPETENCE / qualification!**); *mukāfaʾah* = a reward (an equivalent return).
- **Here it means:** "**And there is none comparable to Him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the final verse seals every remaining door — and the word order does the work.**
  
  ⭐ **v.1 said He is ONE. v.2 said He needs nothing. v.3 said He has no lineage.** ⭐ **One door remained: ⭐ **could there be something that, without being His child or parent, is simply EQUAL to Him?** ⭐⭐ **v.4 closes it: ⭐ **no *kufʾ* — no equal, no match, no peer, no rival, no comparison.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the WORD ORDER is unusual:** ⭐ **normal Arabic would be *lam yakun aḥadun kufuwan lahu*.** ⭐ **Instead: *lam yakun **LAHU** **KUFUWAN** aḥad*** — ⭐ **"*TO HIM*" and "*COMPARABLE*" are both FRONTED, and *aḥad* is pushed to the very end.** ⭐⭐ **The fronting puts ⭐ **"to Him — an equal"** up front (the very idea being denied), ⭐ **and ends the surah on *aḥad* — so the last word rhymes with, and echoes, the first.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the everyday hooks:** ⭐ ***kafāʾah* in marriage = being a suitable MATCH*** — ⭐ **so *lā kufuwa lahu* = He has no match.** ⭐ ***Kafāʾah* in modern Arabic = COMPETENCE.***
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the full four-verse structure:**
  - ⭐ **v.1** — ⭐ **He is ONE** (no second)
  - ⭐ **v.2** — ⭐ **He NEEDS nothing; all need Him** (no dependence)
  - ⭐ **v.3** — ⭐ **No child, no parent** (no lineage)
  - ⭐ **v.4** — ⭐ **No equal** (no comparison)
  
  ⭐ **Every way the human mind imagines a god — ⭐ **with partners, needs, family, or rivals — ⭐ **removed.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ ***Kufuwan*** has recognized recitation variants (*kufuʾan*, *kufʾan*) — ⭐ **same meaning.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lam yakun*** (past negation of *kāna*) — ⚠️ **jussive *yakun* keeps its nūn here** (unlike *yaku* in Qiyāmah 75:37's *a-lam yaku*). ⭐ ***Kufuwan*** = *khabar* of *yakun*, **fronted**; ⭐ ***aḥadun*** = its subject, **delayed to the end**.
- **Why this word:** *kufʾ* (**a match/peer**) — ⭐ **the broadest word for comparability, closing the final possibility.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Kafāʾah* = a MARRIAGE MATCH / COMPETENCE.** ⭐ **No *kufʾ* = He has no match, no peer.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Word order: "TO HIM — COMPARABLE — anyone"** — ⭐ ***aḥad* pushed to the END so the surah's last word echoes its first.** ⭐⭐ **Four verses: no SECOND, no NEED, no LINEAGE, no EQUAL.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَحَدٌ vs وَاحِد** — ⚠️ ***wāḥid* = one (a countable number); *aḥad* = UNIQUE (outside every count).** ⭐ **In affirmation, *aḥad* is used ONLY for Allah.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَحَدٌ appears TWICE with DIFFERENT senses** — ⚠️ **v.1 (affirmative) = THE One; v.4 (negative) = ANYONE.** **Same word, two meanings.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **الصَّمَدُ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **BOTH "needs nothing" AND "all turn to Him."** ⭐ **Don't translate it with only half.** ⭐ **And it occurs ONLY here in the Qurʾān.**
4. ⭐⭐ **هُوَ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **possibly *ḍamīr ash-shaʾn*** ("the matter is…"), ⭐ **not only "He."**
5. ⭐⭐ **لَمْ يَلِدْ before لَمْ يُولَدْ** — ⚠️ **the order refutes the MORE COMMON error first** (claims of divine offspring).
6. ⭐⭐ **v.4 word order** — ⚠️ ***lahu* and *kufuwan* FRONTED, *aḥad* DELAYED to the end.** ⭐ **Deliberate, so the surah closes on the word it opened with.**
7. ⭐ **يَلِدْ** — ⚠️ **the initial *wāw* of *walada* DROPS in the present/jussive** (*yalid*); ⭐ **it reappears in the passive *yūlad*.**
8. ⭐ **الصَّمَدُ is DEFINITE, أَحَدٌ INDEFINITE** — ⚠️ **definite = restriction ("THE only Ṣamad"); indefinite = magnificence beyond grasp.**
9. ⭐ **كُفُوًا** — ⚠️ **recognized recitation variants** (*kufuʾan*, *kufʾan*) — same meaning.
10. ⭐ **"A third of the Qurʾān"** — ⚠️ **in reward/meaning (the Qurʾān's themes: law, stories, and tawḥīd), not a replacement** for reciting the rest.
