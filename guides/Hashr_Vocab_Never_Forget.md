# Surah Al-Ḥashr (59) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–24) · **Complete coverage — every important word**

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It begins with a FORTRESS that couldn't protect its people — and ends with the most famous list of Allah's NAMES in the Qurʾān.**

⭐ **v.2:** the tribe of Banū an-Naḍīr thought ***māniʿatuhum ḥuṣūnuhum*** — "**their FORTRESSES would protect them from Allah**" — ⭐ **and Allah came at them *min ḥaythu lam yaḥtasibū*, from where they never expected.**
⭐ **v.22–24:** ***al-Malik, al-Quddūs, as-Salām, al-Muʾmin, al-Muhaymin, al-ʿAzīz, al-Jabbār, al-Mutakabbir… al-Khāliq, al-Bāriʾ, al-Muṣawwir*** — ⭐ **SIXTEEN Names in three verses** — ⭐ **the real fortress.**

⭐⭐⭐ **Stone walls failed (v.2); the Names of Allah are the shelter (v.22–24).** ⭐ **And in between: the best generation (v.8–10), the hypocrites' empty promises (v.11–17), and a mountain that would CRACK if the Qurʾān came down on it (v.21).**

⭐⭐ **It's a *Musabbiḥah* that opens with *SABBAḤA* (past — v.1) and closes with *YUSABBIḤU* (present — v.24)** — ⭐ **glorification that HAS happened and IS STILL happening — bookended by *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* (v.1, v.24).**

⭐ **Background:** revealed about the expulsion of the Jewish tribe of **Banū an-Naḍīr** from Madīnah (4 AH) after they broke their treaty (classical accounts mention a plan against the Prophet's ﷺ life). **Ibn ʿAbbās called it "Sūrat Banī an-Naḍīr"** (Bukhārī).

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FORTRESSES that failed (v.2) → the NAMES that never fail (v.22–24).** ⭐ **Opens *sabbaḥa* (past) → closes *yusabbiḥu* (present).**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The first exile** (v.1–5) — their forts didn't protect them; terror thrown into their hearts; "take a lesson, people of insight."
2. **The returned wealth (*fayʾ*)** (v.6–7) — who it goes to, so it doesn't circulate only among the rich; take what the Messenger gives.
3. **The three generations** (v.8–10) — the Emigrants, the Helpers, and those who come after.
4. **The hypocrites' promise** (v.11–17) — "we'll stand with you" — they won't; like Shayṭān who says "disbelieve" and then disowns you.
5. **Look at what you've sent for tomorrow** (v.18–20) — don't forget Allah, or He'll make you forget yourselves.
6. **The mountain and the Names** (v.21–24) — a mountain would crumble; He is Allah — and here are His Names.

## §1 — The First Exile (v.1–5)

**1. سَبَّحَ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۖ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ — *sabbaḥa li'llāhi mā fi's-samāwāti wa mā fi'l-arḍ · wa huwa'l-ʿazīzu'l-ḥakīm*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *sabbaḥa* — ⭐ **GLORIFIED** (*sabaḥa* = to swim far; ⭐ ***masbaḥ* = POOL**; ⭐ ***misbaḥah* = PRAYER BEADS**). *al-ʿAzīz* — ⭐ **the MIGHTY / DEAR**. *al-Ḥakīm* — ⭐ **the WISE** (← BRIDLE).
- **Here it means:** "**Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth has glorified Allah, and He is the Almighty, the Wise.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SABBAḤA* — PAST TENSE** — ⭐ **unlike Jumuʿah 62:1 and Taghābun 64:1 (*YUSABBIḤU* — present).** ⭐ **The *Musabbiḥāt*: *sabbaḥa* (Ḥadīd 57, Ḥashr 59, Ṣaff 61) / *yusabbiḥu* (Jumuʿah 62, Taghābun 64).** ⭐⭐⭐ **AND THIS SURAH DOES BOTH: opens *SABBAḤA* (v.1 — has glorified) → closes *YUSABBIḤU* (v.24 — is glorifying).** ⭐ **Past and present in one surah — the glorification never stopped.** ⭐⭐ ***Al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*** — ⭐ **opens AND closes (v.1, v.24)** — ⭐ **ALMIGHTY (no fortress stands against Him, v.2) + WISE (His distribution of wealth, v.7, is perfect).** ⭐ **And Ṣaff 61:1 (the 3rd surah of this set) — word-for-word identical.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***sabbaḥa*** = Form II **past** (complete, established). ⭐ ***huwa'l-ʿazīzu'l-ḥakīmu*** — definite predicates = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **past tense — the whole creation's praise as an established fact, before the story of a fallen fortress.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SABBAḤA* (PAST — v.1) → *YUSABBIḤU* (PRESENT — v.24) — BOTH tenses in ONE surah.** ⭐⭐ ***Sabbaḥa*: Ḥadīd 57, Ḥashr 59, Ṣaff 61 / *yusabbiḥu*: Jumuʿah 62, Taghābun 64.** ⭐⭐ ***Al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* opens & closes (v.1, v.24).** ⭐ **= Ṣaff 61:1 word-for-word.**

**2. هُوَ الَّذِي أَخْرَجَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ مِن دِيَارِهِمْ — *huwa'lladhī akhraja'lladhīna kafarū min ahli'l-kitābi min diyārihim*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *akhraja* — ⭐ **EXPELLED / brought out** (*kharaja*; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:1** — *lā tukhrijūhunna*; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:8** — *la-yukhrijanna'l-aʿazz*!). *ahl al-kitāb* — ⭐ **the PEOPLE OF THE BOOK** (Jews and Christians). *diyār* — ⭐ **HOMES / dwellings** (plural of *dār*; ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:26** — *dayyārā*!; ⭐ ***dāʾirah* = a CIRCLE / department**).
- **Here it means:** "**It is He who expelled those who disbelieved among the People of the Book from their homes…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HUWA'LLADHĪ AKHRAJA* — "It is HE who expelled"** — ⭐ **not the Muslims' army — ALLAH.** ⭐ **The Muslims didn't even fight a battle (v.6) — the outcome was His doing.** ⭐⭐ ***Akhraja* — the *kharaja* thread:** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:8** — Ibn Ubayy boasted: *la-yukhrijanna'l-aʿazzu minha'l-adhall* — ⭐ **"the mightier will EXPEL the lowlier"** — ⭐ **and in Ḥashr, the ones who thought themselves mighty (in fortresses!) were EXPELLED.** ⭐ **The boast inverted, in history.** ⭐⭐ ***Min DIYĀRIHIM*** — ⭐ **from their HOMES** — ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:26** — *lā tadhar ʿala'l-arḍi mina'l-kāfirīna **dayyārā*** (not a single DWELLER).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***huwa'lladhī*** = "**HE is the one who**" (restriction — Allah, not you). ⭐ ***min*** ×2 — partitive (*min ahli'l-kitāb*) and "from" (*min diyārihim*).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **attributing the expulsion to Allah — the theme of the surah: His power, not human strength.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HUWA'LLADHĪ AKHRAJA* = HE (not the army) expelled them — no battle even fought (v.6).** ⭐⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:8 boast "the MIGHTY will EXPEL" → here the "mighty" in fortresses were EXPELLED.** ⭐ ***Diyār* — Nūḥ 71:26 *dayyārā*; *dāʾirah* = CIRCLE / DEPARTMENT.**

**3. لِأَوَّلِ الْحَشْرِ — *li-awwali'l-ḥashr*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *awwal* — ⭐ **FIRST**. *ḥashr* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**GATHERING / herding / mustering**" family — *ḥashara* = **to GATHER, round up, muster, herd**; ⭐ ***al-Ḥashr* = THE GATHERING** (the surah's NAME!); ⭐ ***yawm al-ḥashr* = the Day of GATHERING (Resurrection)**; ⭐ ***ḥasharah* = an INSECT** (things that swarm together!); ⭐ ***al-Ḥāshir*** = **a NAME OF THE PROPHET ﷺ** (⭐ "I am al-Ḥāshir, at whose feet people will be GATHERED" — Bukhārī!); ⭐ **Takwīr 81:5** — *wa idha'l-wuḥūshu **ḥushirat***; ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:23** — *fa-**ḥashara** fa-nādā*; ⭐ **Mulk 67:24** — *wa ilayhi **tuḥsharūn***!.
- **Here it means:** "**…at the first gathering [and exile].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LI-AWWALI'L-ḤASHR* — "at the FIRST GATHERING"** — ⭐ **the SURAH'S NAME.** ⭐ Classical readings: ⭐ **(a) the FIRST exile — they were gathered and driven out of Madīnah (to Khaybar and Shām) — a later "gathering" (exile from Khaybar under ʿUmar) followed;** ⭐ **(b) the "first gathering" of this world — a PREVIEW of the FINAL *Ḥashr* (Resurrection), when all are gathered.** ⭐⭐⭐ **A people herded out of their homes = a small picture of humanity herded to Judgment.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *ḤASHR* / *JAMʿ* THREAD — gatherings in your study:** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:23** — *fa-**ḥashara** fa-nādā* (Pharaoh GATHERED his people to proclaim himself god). ⭐ **Takwīr 81:5** — *wa idha'l-wuḥūshu **ḥushirat*** (even WILD BEASTS gathered). ⭐ **Mulk 67:24** — *wa ilayhi **tuḥsharūn*** (to Him you'll be GATHERED). ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:9** — *yawm al-**jumuʿah*** (the weekly GATHERING). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:9** — *yawm al-**jamʿ*** (the final GATHERING). ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:2** — *awwal al-**ḥashr*** (the FIRST gathering/exile).
  ⭐⭐ ***AL-ḤĀSHIR* — a NAME of the Prophet ﷺ** — ⭐ **"*I am Muḥammad, Aḥmad, al-Māḥī… al-ḤĀSHIR at whose feet people are gathered, and al-ʿĀqib*" (Bukhārī)** — ⭐ **(and AṢ-ṢAFF 61:6 names *AḤMAD* — two names from the same ḥadīth in this set of surahs!).** ⭐ ***Ḥasharah* = an INSECT.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-*** = "**at / for**" (*lām al-waqt* — timing). ⭐ ***awwali*** — *iḍāfah* ("**the first of** the gathering").
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥashr* — ⭐ **a historical exile named with the word for the final Gathering.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AWWAL AL-ḤASHR* = the FIRST GATHERING — the surah's NAME — a historical exile + a PREVIEW of the Final Gathering.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥashr*/*jamʿ*: Pharaoh gathered (79:23), wild beasts (81:5), to Him (67:24), weekly (62:9), final (64:9), FIRST (59:2).** ⭐⭐ ***AL-ḤĀSHIR* = a NAME of the Prophet ﷺ (Bukhārī) — Ṣaff 61:6 names *Aḥmad* from the same ḥadīth.** ⭐ ***Ḥasharah* = INSECT.**

**4. مَا ظَنَنتُمْ أَن يَخْرُجُوا ۖ وَظَنُّوا أَنَّهُم مَّانِعَتُهُمْ حُصُونُهُم مِّنَ اللَّهِ — *mā ẓanantum an yakhrujū · wa ẓannū annahum māniʿatuhum ḥuṣūnuhum mina'llāh*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *ẓanantum / ẓannū* — ⭐⭐ the "**SUPPOSING / thinking (from doubt to certainty)**" family — ***ẓann* = SUPPOSITION** (⭐ ranges from a guess to firm belief — context decides!; ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:20** — *innī **ẓanantu*** (CERTAIN!); ⭐ **Jinn 72:5** (wrong assumption)). *māniʿah* — ⭐ **PREVENTING / protecting** (*manaʿa*; ⭐ ***manāʿah* = IMMUNITY** — clinical!; ⭐ ***mamnūʿ* = FORBIDDEN**; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:21** *manūʿā*; ⭐ **Qalam 68:12** *mannāʿ*!). *ḥuṣūn* — ⭐⭐⭐ **FORTRESSES** (plural of *ḥiṣn*; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:12** — *aḥṣanat* (Maryam FORTIFIED her chastity)!; ⭐ ***Ḥiṣn al-Muslim*** — the duʿāʾ book!; ⭐ ***taḥṣīn* = VACCINATION**; ⭐ ***ḥaṣānah* = IMMUNITY**).
- **Here it means:** "**You did not think they would leave — and they thought their fortresses would protect them from Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO WRONG "THINKINGS" — side by side.** ⭐ ***Mā ẒANANTUM an yakhrujū*** — ⭐ **YOU (believers) didn't think they'd ever leave** (their forts were too strong). ⭐ ***Wa ẒANNŪ annahum māniʿatuhum ḥuṣūnuhum*** — ⭐ **THEY thought their forts would protect them.** ⭐⭐⭐ **BOTH sides misjudged — the believers underestimated Allah's help; the besieged overestimated their walls.** ⭐ **Allah's decree surprised everyone.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***MĀNIʿATUHUM ḤUṢŪNUHUM MINA'LLĀH* — "their FORTRESSES would PREVENT [Allah]"** — ⭐ **the most absurd hope: stone walls against ALLAH.** ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD ORDER: *māniʿatuhum* (protecting them) is FRONTED before *ḥuṣūnuhum* (their forts)** — ⭐ classical (az-Zamakhsharī): **the fronting shows their CERTAINTY: "PROTECTED, for sure — their forts"** — ⭐ **total confidence in walls.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤIṢN* (FORTRESS) — the TAḤRĪM link:** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:12** — Maryam ***aḥṣanat*** farjahā — ⭐ **she made her CHASTITY a fortress — and it held, because it was built for Allah.** ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:2** — ***ḥuṣūnuhum*** — ⭐ **stone fortresses built AGAINST Allah — and they fell.** ⭐⭐ **The fortress of faith stands; the fortress against faith crumbles.** ⭐ ***Ḥiṣn al-Muslim*** — **the true fortress = remembrance of Allah.** ⭐ ***Taḥṣīn* = VACCINATION; *manāʿah* = IMMUNITY** — ⭐ **no "immunity" against Allah's decree.**
  ⭐ Clinical hooks: ⭐ ***manāʿah* = IMMUNITY** (*jihāz al-manāʿah* = the **immune system**!); ⭐ ***taḥṣīn* = VACCINATION.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***māniʿatuhum*** (predicate) **fronted** before ***ḥuṣūnuhum*** (subject) — ⭐ **emphasis on their certainty**. ⭐ ***ẓanantum / ẓannū*** — two "*ẓann*"s (*ṭibāq* of perspectives).
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥuṣūn* — ⭐ **walls trusted against Allah — the image that the surah's ending (His Names) answers.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO WRONG THINKINGS: believers didn't think they'd LEAVE / they thought their FORTS would PROTECT them.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MĀNIʿATUHUM* FRONTED = total CERTAINTY in walls — against ALLAH.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤIṢN*: Maryam's fortress of CHASTITY (Taḥrīm 66:12 — held) vs stone FORTS against Allah (Ḥashr 59:2 — fell).** ⭐⭐ ***Ḥiṣn al-Muslim* — the true fortress.** ⭐⭐ ***Manāʿah* = IMMUNITY (*jihāz al-manāʿah* = immune system); *taḥṣīn* = VACCINATION.** ⭐ ***Manaʿa*: Maʿārij 70:21, Qalam 68:12.**

**5. فَأَتَاهُمُ اللَّهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَمْ يَحْتَسِبُوا — *fa-atāhumu'llāhu min ḥaythu lam yaḥtasibū*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *atāhum* — ⭐ **CAME TO them**. *yaḥtasibū* — ⭐⭐ **RECKON / calculate / expect** (*ḥasiba*; ⭐ ***ḥisāb* = ACCOUNT**; ⭐ ***ḥāsūb* = a COMPUTER**!; ⭐ ***iḥtisāb* = expecting reward** — *īmānan wa iḥtisāban*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…but Allah came at them from where they had not reckoned…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIN ḤAYTHU LAM YAḤTASIBŪ* — WORD-FOR-WORD (nearly) with ṬALĀQ 65:3!** ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:3** — *wa yarzuqhu **min ḥaythu lā yaḥtasib*** — ⭐ **PROVISION from where you never CALCULATED (for the God-conscious).** ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:2** — *fa-atāhumu'llāhu **min ḥaythu lam yaḥtasibū*** — ⭐ **PUNISHMENT from where they never CALCULATED (for those who defied Him).** ⭐⭐⭐ **The SAME "unexpected direction" — for one, rescue; for the other, ruin.** ⭐ **Allah is outside everyone's spreadsheet** (⭐ *ḥāsūb* = a COMPUTER).
  ⭐⭐ **They guarded every wall, every gate — and the attack came from INSIDE (next phrase: terror in their HEARTS).** ⭐ **The one place a fortress can't guard.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Atāhumu'llāh*** — ⭐ classical: **His command / His punishment came** (⭐ the attribute affirmed as fits His majesty).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lam yaḥtasibū*** (past negation — "**had not**") vs Ṭalāq's ***lā yaḥtasib*** (general present).
- **Why this exact word:** *iḥtisāb* — ⭐ **the same unexpected direction that brings provision to the pious brings ruin to the defiant.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIN ḤAYTHU LAM YAḤTASIBŪ* ≈ ṬALĀQ 65:3: PROVISION from the unexpected (pious) / PUNISHMENT from the unexpected (defiant).** ⭐ **Allah is outside EVERYONE's spreadsheet (*ḥāsūb* = COMPUTER).** ⭐⭐ **They guarded every WALL — the attack came from INSIDE (their hearts).**

**6. وَقَذَفَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ الرُّعْبَ — *wa qadhafa fī qulūbihimu'r-ruʿb*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *qadhafa* — ⭐⭐ the "**HURLING / throwing hard**" family — *qadhafa* = **to HURL, to throw forcefully**; ⭐ ***qadhīfah* = a PROJECTILE / MISSILE / shell** (⭐ modern: *qadhāʾif* = **artillery SHELLS**!); ⭐ ***qadhf* = SLANDER (of chastity)** — a fiqh term (hurling an accusation!); ⭐ ***miqdhāf* = a CATAPULT / oar**. *qulūb* — ⭐ **HEARTS** (*qalb* ← *qalaba*, to turn). *ruʿb* — ⭐⭐ the "**TERROR / panic-fear**" family — ***ruʿb* = TERROR, dread** (⭐ ***rāʿib* = TERRIFYING**; ⭐ ***murʿib* = HORRIFYING** — *film murʿib* = a **HORROR film**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and He hurled terror into their hearts…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QADHAFA* — HURLED — like a MISSILE.** ⭐ **The Muslims didn't fire a single shot at the forts** — ⭐ **ALLAH fired the "missile": TERROR, straight into their HEARTS.** ⭐ ***Qadhīfah* = a MISSILE / SHELL** — ⭐ **the weapon no wall can block.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the ḥadīth: "*I have been given five things no one before me was given… I was HELPED by TERROR (ar-RUʿB) at a distance of a month's journey*" (Bukhārī)** — ⭐ **the same word *ruʿb*.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Fī QULŪBIHIM*** — ⭐ **into their HEARTS** — ⭐ **the fortress guarded the BODY; the terror went for the HEART.** ⭐ **The hearts thread: Munāfiqūn 63:3 (SEALED), Taghābun 64:11 (GUIDED), Ḥashr 59:2 (TERRIFIED), 59:14 (*shattā* — SCATTERED).**
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***film murʿib* = HORROR FILM**; ⭐ ***qadhāʾif* = artillery SHELLS**; ⭐ ***qadhf* = SLANDER (fiqh).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***qadhafa*** = past (subject: Allah). ⭐ ***ar-ruʿba*** = object — ⚠️ **delayed to the end** (suspense: hurled into their hearts… TERROR).
- **Why this exact word:** *qadhafa* — ⭐ **a weapon-word: terror launched past the walls into the heart.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QADHAFA… AR-RUʿB* = HURLED TERROR — like a MISSILE (*qadhīfah*) — past the walls, into the HEART.** ⭐⭐⭐ **"I was helped by TERROR (*ar-ruʿb*) a month's distance away" (Bukhārī).** ⭐⭐ **HEARTS: SEALED (63:3), GUIDED (64:11), TERRIFIED (59:2), SCATTERED (59:14).** ⭐ ***Film murʿib* = HORROR film; *qadhf* = SLANDER (fiqh).**

**7. يُخْرِبُونَ بُيُوتَهُم بِأَيْدِيهِمْ وَأَيْدِي الْمُؤْمِنِينَ — *yukhribūna buyūtahum bi-aydīhim wa aydi'l-muʾminīn*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *yukhribūn* — ⭐⭐ the "**RUIN / DESTRUCTION / desolation**" family — *khariba* = to be ruined, desolate; ⭐ ***kharāb* = RUIN, DESTRUCTION** (⭐ everyday: ***kharbān* = BROKEN / out of order** — *as-sayyārah kharbānah* = the car is BROKEN!); ⭐ ***takhrīb* = SABOTAGE / vandalism**; ⭐ ***kharābah* = RUINS / a ruin**. *buyūt* — ⭐ **HOMES** (*bayt*; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:1** — *min **buyūtihinna***!). *aydī* — ⭐ **HANDS**.
- **Here it means:** "**…they were destroying their houses with their own hands and the hands of the believers.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST VIVID SCENE: they DESTROYED THEIR OWN HOUSES.** ⭐ Classical: **they were allowed to take what their camels could carry — so they TORE DOWN their homes to take the doors, beams, and window frames** — ⭐ **and so the Muslims couldn't use them.** ⭐⭐ ***Bi-AYDĪHIM*** — ⭐ **"with THEIR OWN hands"** — ⭐ **the fortress-builders became the fortress-wreckers.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Wa aydi'l-MUʾMINĪN*** — ⭐ **"and the hands of the BELIEVERS"** — ⭐ **who demolished the outer defences during the siege.** ⭐ **Destruction from inside and outside.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Kharāb* = RUIN; *kharbān* = BROKEN** (everyday!) ⭐ **What they built to protect them, they ruined themselves.** ⭐ **A spiritual lesson the scholars drew: *the sinner destroys his own house with his own hands*.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Buyūt* — Ṭalāq 65:1** — *lā tukhrijūhunna min buyūtihinna* — ⭐ **a house protected by law (Ṭalāq) vs houses torn down by their owners (Ḥashr).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yukhribūna*** = Form IV present (*ḥāl* — "**[while] destroying**"). ⚠️ ⭐ **Recitation variant: *yukharribūna*** (Form II — intensive: demolishing thoroughly).
- **Why this exact word:** *kharāb* — ⭐ **self-ruin — the builders of the fortress tearing it down.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUKHRIBŪNA BUYŪTAHUM BI-AYDĪHIM* = destroying their OWN houses with their OWN hands — taking doors and beams, leaving ruins.** ⭐⭐ **+ *aydi'l-muʾminīn* — destruction from INSIDE and OUTSIDE.** ⭐ **The fortress-builders became the fortress-wreckers.** ⭐⭐ ***KHARĀB* = RUIN; *KHARBĀN* = BROKEN (everyday!); *takhrīb* = SABOTAGE.** ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:1 *buyūtihinna*.** ⚠️ **Variant *yukharribūna*.**

**8. فَاعْتَبِرُوا يَا أُولِي الْأَبْصَارِ — *fa'ʿtabirū yā uli'l-abṣār*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *iʿtabirū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**CROSSING OVER**" family — *ʿabara* = **to CROSS (a river, a road)**; ⭐ ***ʿubūr* = CROSSING** (⭐ *ʿubūr al-mushāh* = a **pedestrian CROSSING**!); ⭐ ***maʿbar* = a CROSSING POINT / ferry**; ⭐⭐ ***ʿibrah* = a LESSON** (you "CROSS OVER" from someone else's story to your own life!); ⭐ ***iʿtibār* = TAKING A LESSON / CONSIDERATION** (⭐ ***bi-ʿtibār* = "considering" / "as"**!); ⭐ ***ʿibārah* = an EXPRESSION / phrase** (words that CARRY meaning across!); ⭐ ***ʿabrah* = a TEAR** (that CROSSES the cheek!); ⭐ ***taʿbīr* = EXPRESSION / dream-INTERPRETATION** (*taʿbīr ar-ruʾyā*!). *uli'l-abṣār* — ⭐ **people of INSIGHT / EYES** (⭐ ***ulū*** — **Ṭalāq 65:10** *uli'l-albāb*!; ⭐ *baṣar* = sight).
- **Here it means:** "**So take a lesson, O people of insight!**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST FAMOUS COMMAND OF THE SURAH — and one of the most important words in Islamic thought.** ⭐ ***IʿTABIRŪ* = CROSS OVER** — ⭐ **from THEIR story to YOUR life** — ⭐ **see what happened to them, and carry the lesson across to yourself.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿIbrah* (lesson) ← *ʿubūr* (crossing)** — ⭐ **a lesson is a BRIDGE you cross from history into your own heart.** ⭐ ***ʿAbrah* = a TEAR** — ⭐ **a tear "crosses" your cheek — and a true *ʿibrah* often comes with one.** ⭐ ***ʿIbārah* = an EXPRESSION** — **words carry meaning across.** ⭐ ***Taʿbīr ar-ruʾyā*** — **dream INTERPRETATION** (crossing from the image to its meaning!).
  ⭐⭐⭐ **USŪL AL-FIQH: many classical scholars cited *fa'ʿtabirū* as Qurʾānic support for *QIYĀS* (ANALOGICAL REASONING)** — ⭐ **"CROSS OVER" a ruling from a known case to a similar new one.** ⭐ **A legal method built on this word.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ya ULI'L-ABṢĀR*** — ⭐ **"O people of EYES / INSIGHT"** — ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:10** — *yā **uli'l-albāb*** (people of KERNELS/minds). ⭐ **Ṭalāq: those who reach the CORE; Ḥashr: those who SEE clearly.** ⭐ **Mulk 67:3–4** — *fa'rjiʿi'l-**baṣar*** (look again!) ⭐ **— the surah of looking trained the *abṣār*; Ḥashr says: now LEARN from what you see.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***ʿubūr al-mushāh* = PEDESTRIAN CROSSING**; ⭐ ***ʿabrah* = a TEAR**; ⭐ ***taʿbīr* = EXPRESSION / dream interpretation.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa'ʿtabirū*** = **Form VIII** imperative (*iftaʿala* — "**take** a crossing for yourselves"). ⭐ ***yā uli*** = vocative of ***ulū***.
- **Why this exact word:** *iʿtibār* — ⭐ **history as a bridge — cross from their ruin to your reform.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA'ʿTABIRŪ* = CROSS OVER — from THEIR story to YOUR life.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿIBRAH* (LESSON) ← *ʿUBŪR* (CROSSING) — a lesson is a BRIDGE.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿABRAH* = a TEAR (crosses the cheek); *ʿIBĀRAH* = EXPRESSION; *taʿbīr ar-ruʾyā* = DREAM INTERPRETATION.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Classical support for *QIYĀS* (analogy) in uṣūl al-fiqh.** ⭐⭐ ***Uli'l-ABṢĀR* (people of SIGHT) / *uli'l-ALBĀB* (people of CORE — Ṭalāq 65:10).** ⭐ ***ʿUbūr al-mushāh* = PEDESTRIAN CROSSING.**

**9. وَلَوْلَا أَن كَتَبَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِمُ الْجَلَاءَ لَعَذَّبَهُمْ فِي الدُّنْيَا — *wa lawlā an kataba'llāhu ʿalayhimu'l-jalāʾa la-ʿadhdhabahum fi'd-dunyā*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *lawlā* — ⭐ **"WERE IT NOT for…"** *kataba* — ⭐ **DECREED / wrote** (*kitāb*). *jalāʾ* — ⭐⭐ the "**CLEARING / leaving / becoming CLEAR**" family — *jalā* = **to CLEAR OUT, to leave (a place), to become CLEAR / visible**; ⭐ ***jalāʾ* = EVACUATION / EXILE** (⭐ *jalāʾ al-quwwāt* = **troop WITHDRAWAL**!); ⭐⭐ ***jāliyah* = an EXPATRIATE COMMUNITY / diaspora** (⭐ *al-jāliyah al-pākistāniyyah* = the **Pakistani DIASPORA**!); ⭐ ***jaliyy* = CLEAR, obvious** (⭐ *bi-wuḍūḥ wa jalāʾ* = with clarity!); ⭐ ***tajallī* = MANIFESTATION / revelation** (⭐ — of Allah to the mountain — 7:143!); ⭐ ***jallā* = to POLISH / reveal** — ⭐ **Shams 91:3** — *wa'n-nahāri idhā **jallāhā*** (the day when it REVEALS it)!.
- **Here it means:** "**And had Allah not decreed exile for them, He would have punished them in this world…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-JALĀʾ* = EXILE — and its root means CLEARING / REVEALING.** ⭐ **Exile = "CLEARING OUT" of a land** — ⭐ **and the same root gives *tajallī* (divine manifestation) and *jaliyy* (CLEAR).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***JĀLIYAH* = an EXPATRIATE COMMUNITY / DIASPORA** — ⭐ **literally "those who've LEFT / cleared out" from home** — ⭐ **every diaspora community in the world is a *jāliyah*.** ⭐⭐ ***Lawlā… la-ʿadhdhabahum*** — ⭐ **"were it not for exile — He'd have PUNISHED them [more severely, by death/captivity]"** — ⭐ **exile was the LIGHTER sentence — a mercy compared to what could have been** (⭐ v.3b: but the Hereafter's punishment awaits).
  ⭐⭐ ***JALLĀ* — SHAMS 91:3** — *wa'n-nahāri idhā **jallāhā*** — ⭐ **the day that REVEALS / CLEARS the sun** — ⭐ **same root.**
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***jalāʾ al-quwwāt* = troop WITHDRAWAL.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lawlā*** + *an* + verb = "**were it not that**" — ⭐ answered by ***la-ʿadhdhabahum*** (with *lām*).
- **Why this exact word:** *jalāʾ* — ⭐ **exile as clearing-out — the lighter sentence, from the root of clarity.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-JALĀʾ* = EXILE — "CLEARING OUT" — the LIGHTER sentence (else worse punishment in dunya).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***JĀLIYAH* = DIASPORA / expatriate community (*al-jāliyah al-pākistāniyyah*!).** ⭐⭐ ***Jaliyy* = CLEAR; *tajallī* = MANIFESTATION (7:143); Shams 91:3 *jallāhā*.** ⭐ ***Jalāʾ al-quwwāt* = troop WITHDRAWAL.**

**10. وَلَهُمْ فِي الْآخِرَةِ عَذَابُ النَّارِ — *wa lahum fi'l-ākhirati ʿadhābu'n-nār*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *ākhirah* — ⭐ **the HEREAFTER** (*ākhir* = LAST; ⚠️ ≠ *ākhar*, other). *ʿadhāb* — ⭐ **PUNISHMENT**. *nār* — ⭐ **FIRE**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and for them in the Hereafter is the punishment of the Fire.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **TWO STAGES again — the pattern you've seen in Ṭalāq 65:9–10 and Taghābun 64:5:** ⭐ **worldly consequence (exile) → Hereafter's punishment (the Fire).** ⭐ **Exile spared them a worse WORLDLY fate — but not the next.** ⭐ ***Wa lahum*** — "**and [still] for them**" — ⭐ **the account isn't closed by the worldly penalty.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lahum*** fronted (predicate) + ***ʿadhābu'n-nāri*** (subject).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the worldly sentence doesn't settle the final account.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ **TWO STAGES: EXILE (dunya, lighter) → FIRE (Hereafter) — like Ṭalāq 65:9–10, Taghābun 64:5.** ⭐ **The worldly penalty doesn't close the account.**

**11. ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمْ شَاقُّوا اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ — *dhālika bi-annahum shāqqu'llāha wa rasūlah*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *shāqqū* — ⭐⭐⭐ **Form III of *shaqqa* (to SPLIT)** = **to OPPOSE, to take the OTHER SIDE, to break ranks** — ⭐ ***shiqāq* = SCHISM, discord, hostile opposition** (⭐ *shiqāq bayna'z-zawjayn* = **marital DISCORD** — 4:35!); ⭐ ***shaqq* = a SPLIT / crack**; ⭐ ***inshiqāq* = SPLITTING / DEFECTION** (⭐ **Sūrat al-INSHIQĀQ 84!**; ⭐ *inshiqāq siyāsī* = a political **DEFECTION / split**!); ⭐ ***shiqqah* = an APARTMENT** (a split-off section!); ⭐ ***mashaqqah* = HARDSHIP**; ⭐ ***shaqīq* = a FULL BROTHER** (split from the same origin!); ⭐ ***ash-shaqīqah* = MIGRAINE** — the headache that SPLITS the head in half!).
- **Here it means:** "**That is because they opposed Allah and His Messenger…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHĀQQŪ* — they TOOK THE OTHER SIDE / SPLIT AWAY** — ⭐ **Form III = a mutual, deliberate opposition** — ⭐ **as if Allah is on one SIDE (*shiqq*) and they chose the other.** ⭐ **They had a treaty with the Prophet ﷺ — and SPLIT from it.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *SHAQQA* ROOT — SPLITTING, everywhere in your study:** ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:1** — *idha's-samāʾu'**nshaqqat*** (the sky SPLITS). ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:16** — *wa'**nshaqqati**'s-samāʾ*. ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:26** — *thumma **shaqaqna**'l-arḍa shaqqā* (We SPLIT the earth — for plants!). ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:4** — ***shāqqū**'llāh* — ⭐ **they SPLIT from Allah.** ⭐⭐ **The earth splits to give life; the sky splits at the End; a people split from Allah — and are expelled.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ASH-SHAQĪQAH* = MIGRAINE** — ⭐ **the HALF-head headache — "split" in two!** (clinical!) ⭐ ***Shaqīq* = a full BROTHER** (from the same split origin). ⭐ ***Shiqāq* = marital DISCORD** (4:35).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***shāqqū*** = **Form III** (*fāʿala* — opposition AGAINST), doubled root. ⭐ ***bi-annahum*** = "**because they**."
- **Why this exact word:** *shāqqa* — ⭐ **opposition as deliberately standing on the other side of a split.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHĀQQŪ* = took the OTHER SIDE of the SPLIT — opposing Allah (Form III — deliberate).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHAQQA*: earth SPLIT for plants (ʿAbasa 80:26), sky SPLITS (Inshiqāq 84:1, Ḥāqqah 69:16), a people SPLIT from Allah (Ḥashr 59:4).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ASH-SHAQĪQAH* = MIGRAINE (half-head!); *shaqīq* = full BROTHER; *shiqāq* = marital DISCORD (4:35); *shiqqah* = APARTMENT.** ⭐ ***Inshiqāq* = political DEFECTION.**

**12. وَمَن يُشَاقِّ اللَّهَ فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ شَدِيدُ الْعِقَابِ — *wa man yushāqqi'llāha fa-inna'llāha shadīdu'l-ʿiqāb*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *yushāqqi* — ⭐ **OPPOSES** (jussive). *shadīd* — ⭐ **SEVERE**. *ʿiqāb* — ⭐⭐ the "**HEEL / what FOLLOWS**" family — ***ʿaqib* = the HEEL**; ⭐ ***ʿiqāb* = PUNISHMENT** (what follows ON THE HEELS of a deed!); ⭐ ***ʿāqibah* = OUTCOME** (⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:9**!); ⭐ ***ʿuqbā*** (⭐ **Shams 91:15**); ⭐ ***ʿaqabah* = an OBSTACLE / steep road** (⭐ **Balad 90:11**!); ⭐ ***al-ʿĀqib*** = **a NAME OF THE PROPHET ﷺ** — "the one who comes LAST / follows all prophets" (Bukhārī — the same ḥadīth as *al-Ḥāshir*!); ⭐ ***taʿqīb* = FOLLOW-UP / commentary**; ⭐ ***ʿuqūbah* = a PENALTY / SANCTION** (⭐ *ʿuqūbāt iqtiṣādiyyah* = **economic SANCTIONS**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and whoever opposes Allah — then indeed Allah is severe in penalty.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿIQĀB* — PUNISHMENT that comes ON THE HEELS (*ʿaqib*)** — ⭐ **it FOLLOWS the deed as surely as your heel follows your foot.** ⭐⭐ **THE *ʿAQIB* (HEEL) THREAD:** ⭐ **Balad 90:11** — ***al-ʿaqabah*** (the steep ROAD — climbed step by step). ⭐ **Shams 91:15** — *wa lā yakhāfu **ʿuqbāhā*** (He fears no OUTCOME). ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:9** — *wa kāna **ʿāqibatu** amrihā khusrā* (the OUTCOME was loss). ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:4, 7** — ***shadīd al-ʿiqāb*** (severe PENALTY — ×2 in this surah!). ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:17** — *fa-kāna **ʿāqibatahumā*** (the outcome of Shayṭān and his victim). ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-ʿĀQIB* and *AL-ḤĀSHIR* — two NAMES of the Prophet ﷺ in ONE ḥadīth (Bukhārī) — and BOTH roots are in this surah (*ḥashr*, v.2; *ʿiqāb*, v.4).** ⭐ ***ʿUqūbāt iqtiṣādiyyah* = economic SANCTIONS.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** + jussive (*yushāqqi* — ⚠️ kasrah, doubled root unmerged in jussive with following hamzah-word) + ***fa-inna*** (answer). ⭐ ***shadīdu'l-ʿiqābi*** = *iḍāfah* (severe **of** penalty).
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿiqāb* — ⭐ **penalty as the heel following the foot — inescapable consequence.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿIQĀB* ← *ʿAQIB* (HEEL) = PUNISHMENT that FOLLOWS on the heels.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿAqib* thread: steep ROAD (Balad 90:11), OUTCOME (Shams 91:15, Ṭalāq 65:9), PENALTY (Ḥashr 59:4, 7), OUTCOME (Ḥashr 59:17).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-ʿĀQIB* & *AL-ḤĀSHIR* — two Names of the Prophet ﷺ (Bukhārī) — both roots in Ḥashr!** ⭐ ***ʿUqūbāt iqtiṣādiyyah* = economic SANCTIONS; *taʿqīb* = FOLLOW-UP.**

**13. مَا قَطَعْتُم مِّن لِّينَةٍ أَوْ تَرَكْتُمُوهَا قَائِمَةً عَلَىٰ أُصُولِهَا فَبِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ — *mā qaṭaʿtum min līnatin aw taraktumūhā qāʾimatan ʿalā uṣūlihā fa-bi-idhni'llāh*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *qaṭaʿtum* — ⭐ **you CUT DOWN** (*qaṭʿ*; ⭐ ***qiṭʿah* = a PIECE**; ⭐ ***inqiṭāʿ* = a POWER CUT / interruption**!). *līnah* — ⭐⭐ the "**SOFTNESS**" family — ***līn* = SOFTNESS** (⭐ ***layyin* = SOFT / gentle** — *kalām layyin* = gentle speech!); ⭐ ***līnah* = a (fine) PALM TREE** — classically, any palm except the ʿajwah, or a palm of soft, choice dates. *taraktumūhā* — ⭐ **you LEFT them**. *qāʾimah* — ⭐ **STANDING**. *uṣūl* — ⭐⭐⭐ **ROOTS** (plural of ***aṣl*** = ROOT, origin, foundation; ⭐⭐ ***uṣūl al-fiqh* = the PRINCIPLES of jurisprudence**!; ⭐ ***uṣūl ad-dīn* = the FOUNDATIONS of religion (theology)**; ⭐ ***aṣlī* = ORIGINAL / authentic**; ⭐ ***aṣīl* = of noble origin / genuine**; ⭐ ***uṣūliyyah* = FUNDAMENTALISM**!). *bi-idhni'llāh* — ⭐ **by Allah's PERMISSION** (⭐ **Taghābun 64:11**!).
- **Here it means:** "**Whatever palm trees you cut down, or left standing on their roots — it was by Allah's permission…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A CONTROVERSY SETTLED.** ⭐ **During the siege, some palms were cut down (to pressure the besieged); others were left.** ⭐ **The tribe objected: "you claim to forbid corruption — yet you cut down trees!"** ⭐ **The verse answers: both choices were *BI-IDHNI'LLĀH* — by His PERMISSION** — ⭐ **a specific wartime decision, not wanton destruction.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BI-IDHNI'LLĀH* — TAGHĀBUN 64:11** — *mā aṣāba min muṣībatin illā **bi-idhni'llāh*** — ⭐ **"no calamity strikes except by His PERMISSION."** ⭐ **The same phrase: every outcome under His permission.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***QĀʾIMATAN ʿALĀ UṢŪLIHĀ* — "STANDING on its ROOTS"** — ⭐ **a tree upright on its foundations** — ⭐ ***UṢŪL* = ROOTS → *UṢŪL AL-FIQH*** — ⭐ **the "roots" of jurisprudence: the Qurʾān, Sunnah, consensus, analogy (⭐ and v.2's *fa'ʿtabirū* = analogy!).** ⭐ **The law, like a tree, stands on its roots.** ⭐ ***Aṣlī* = ORIGINAL; *uṣūliyyah* = FUNDAMENTALISM.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Līnah* — from *līn* (SOFTNESS)** — ⭐ **a palm of soft, choice dates** — ⭐ **Āl ʿImrān 3:159** — *fa-bimā raḥmatin mina'llāhi **linta** lahum* — ⭐ **"by Allah's mercy you were SOFT/gentle with them."** ⭐ **Same root.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mā*** = conditional ("**whatever**"). ⭐ ***min līnatin*** — *min* = explanatory ("**of** palm trees"). ⭐ ***fa-bi-idhni'llāhi*** = answer (with *fa*).
- **Why this exact word:** *uṣūl* — ⭐ **a tree on its roots — the image behind the very name of legal theory.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Palms CUT or LEFT STANDING — both *BI-IDHNI'LLĀH* (by His PERMISSION) — a specific wartime decision.** ⭐⭐ **Taghābun 64:11: *illā bi-idhni'llāh* — every outcome under His permission.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QĀʾIMATAN ʿALĀ UṢŪLIHĀ* = standing on its ROOTS → *UṢŪL AL-FIQH* (roots of jurisprudence), *uṣūl ad-dīn*.** ⭐ ***Aṣlī* = ORIGINAL; *uṣūliyyah* = FUNDAMENTALISM.** ⭐⭐ ***LĪNAH* ← *līn* (SOFTNESS) — 3:159 *linta lahum*; *layyin* = SOFT.** ⭐ ***Inqiṭāʿ* = POWER CUT.**

**14. وَلِيُخْزِيَ الْفَاسِقِينَ — *wa li-yukhziya'l-fāsiqīn*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *yukhziya* — ⭐ **DISGRACE** (*khizy*; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — *yawma **lā yukhzi'llāhu'n-nabiyy***!). *fāsiqīn* — ⭐⭐ **DEFIANT SINNERS** (⭐ a ripe DATE BURSTING from its skin; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:6** — *lā yahdi'l-qawma'l-**fāsiqīn***!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and so that He might disgrace the defiantly disobedient.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LI-YUKHZIYA* — to DISGRACE** — ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — *yawma **lā yukhzi**'llāhu'n-nabiyya wa'lladhīna āmanū maʿah* — ⭐ **"the Day Allah will NOT disgrace the Prophet and the believers."** ⭐ **Taḥrīm: NO disgrace for the believers; Ḥashr: DISGRACE for the defiant.** ⭐ **The same verb — two outcomes.** ⭐⭐ ***Al-FĀSIQĪN* — the DATE bursting its skin** — ⭐ **a perfect echo: they "burst out" of their treaty — and the PALM trees (whose dates are the image of *fisq*!) became part of their disgrace.** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:6, Ḥashr 59:5, 19 — the *fāsiqūn* thread.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa li-*** + subjunctive = "**and in order to**" (a second purpose, joined to an implied first).
- **Why this exact word:** *khizy* — ⭐ **public disgrace — the reverse of the believers' honour on the Day.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LI-YUKHZIYA'L-FĀSIQĪN* = to DISGRACE the defiant — vs Taḥrīm 66:8: NO disgrace for the believers.** ⭐⭐ ***FĀSIQ* = a DATE bursting its skin — they BURST out of their treaty; their PALMS became part of the disgrace.** ⭐ ***Fāsiqūn*: Munāfiqūn 63:6, Ḥashr 59:5, 19.**

## §2 — The Returned Wealth (*Fayʾ*) (v.6–7)

**15. وَمَا أَفَاءَ اللَّهُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِ مِنْهُمْ — *wa mā afāʾa'llāhu ʿalā rasūlihi minhum*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *afāʾa* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**RETURNING / SHADE that comes back**" family — *fāʾa* = **to RETURN, to come back**; ⭐⭐ ***fayʾ* = SHADE that RETURNS after noon** (the afternoon shadow "comes back" as the sun moves west!); ⭐⭐ ***fayʾ* = WEALTH that "RETURNS" to the Muslims WITHOUT fighting** (a fiqh term!); ⭐ ***afāʾa* = to cause to RETURN, to restore**; ⭐ ***fāʾa ilā* = to RETURN to (repentance / reconciliation)** (⭐ *ḥattā tafīʾa ilā amri'llāh*, 49:9 — "until it RETURNS to Allah's command"!).
- **Here it means:** "**And what Allah restored to His Messenger from them…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FAYʾ* = the RETURNING SHADE.** ⭐ **Classical: the afternoon shadow is called *fayʾ* because it RETURNS — after shrinking to nothing at noon, it grows back eastward.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And wealth taken WITHOUT battle is *FAYʾ* — because, in the Islamic view, all wealth originally belongs to Allah and was created for the service of His obedient servants; when it comes to them, it "RETURNS" to its rightful use.** ⭐ **Not "spoils grabbed" — wealth RESTORED.** ⭐⭐ **Distinguished from *ghanīmah* (spoils won in battle — distributed differently, 8:41).** ⭐ **The *fayʾ* has its own rules — v.7.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Fāʾa ilā* = to RETURN to (the right path)** — ⭐ **49:9** — *ḥattā **tafīʾa** ilā amri'llāh*.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mā*** = relative ("**what**") — the subject of the whole sentence. ⭐ ***afāʾa*** = Form IV past (caused to return).
- **Why this exact word:** *fayʾ* — ⭐ **wealth as returning shade — coming back to its proper place by Allah's arrangement.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FAYʾ* = the afternoon SHADE that RETURNS → wealth that RETURNS to the believers WITHOUT battle.** ⭐ **Not "spoils grabbed" — wealth RESTORED to its rightful use.** ⭐⭐ ***Fayʾ* (no battle — v.6–7) vs *ghanīmah* (battle spoils — 8:41).** ⭐ ***Fāʾa ilā* = to RETURN to (49:9).**

**16. فَمَا أَوْجَفْتُمْ عَلَيْهِ مِنْ خَيْلٍ وَلَا رِكَابٍ — *fa-mā awjaftum ʿalayhi min khaylin wa lā rikāb*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *awjaftum* — ⭐⭐ the "**GALLOPING / urging on at speed / trembling**" family — *wajafa* = **to move fast, to gallop — and to TREMBLE / palpitate**; ⭐ ***awjafa* = to SPUR on (horses, camels) at a gallop**; ⭐ ***wajīf* = rapid running**; ⭐⭐ ***wājif* = TREMBLING / pounding** (⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:8** — *qulūbun yawmaʾidhin **wājifah*** — "hearts that day POUNDING / trembling"!). *khayl* — ⭐ **HORSES / cavalry** (⭐ ***khayl* ← *khuyalāʾ* = PRIDE** — the proud-stepping animal!; ⭐ ***khayāl* = IMAGINATION** — same letters!; ⭐ ***khayyāl* = a RIDER**; ⭐ **ʿĀdiyāt 100:1** — the war-horses!). *rikāb* — ⭐ **RIDING CAMELS** (*rakiba* = to ride; ⭐ ***markab* = a SHIP / vehicle**; ⭐ ***rākib* = a PASSENGER**; ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:19** — *la-**tarkabunna** ṭabaqan ʿan ṭabaq*!; ⭐ ***murakkab* = COMPOUND** — chemistry!; ⭐ ***tarkīb* = INSTALLATION / structure**).
- **Here it means:** "**…you did not spur on any horses or camels for it…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NO BATTLE — "you didn't even GALLOP a horse for it."** ⭐ ***Mā AWJAFTUM*** — ⭐ **"you didn't SPUR on"** — ⭐ **Banū an-Naḍīr were in Madīnah's outskirts — the Muslims walked there, no long cavalry march, no battle.** ⭐ **So the wealth is *fayʾ* (returned without fighting) — and goes to the Prophet ﷺ to distribute (v.7), NOT divided among the soldiers (who did no fighting).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAJAFA* — NĀZIʿĀT 79:8** — *qulūbun yawmaʾidhin **wājifah*** — ⭐ **hearts POUNDING like galloping hooves on the Day.** ⭐ **Same root: the GALLOP of horses and the GALLOP of a terrified heart.** ⭐ **(Clinical image: *wajīf* = palpitations!)**
  ⭐⭐ ***Khayl* — ʿĀdiyāt 100** (the charging war-horses) — ⭐ ***khayāl* = IMAGINATION** (same letters!) — ⭐ **Ḥashr: not a single horse needed.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Rikāb* — *markab* = a SHIP / vehicle; *murakkab* = a chemical COMPOUND** — ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:19** — *la-tarkabunna ṭabaqan ʿan ṭabaq* (you'll RIDE stage after stage).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-mā*** = negation. ⭐⭐ ***min khaylin*** — ***min*** + indefinite = "**any** horse at all" (total negation).
- **Why this exact word:** *awjafa* — ⭐ **the absence of even a gallop — the defining feature of *fayʾ*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MĀ AWJAFTUM* = you didn't even GALLOP a horse for it — NO battle → it's *FAYʾ*, distributed by the Prophet ﷺ, not divided among soldiers.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAJAFA*: horses GALLOP / hearts POUND (Nāziʿāt 79:8 *qulūbun wājifah*) — palpitations!** ⭐⭐ ***KHAYL* (horses — ʿĀdiyāt 100) / *KHAYĀL* (IMAGINATION) — same letters.** ⭐⭐ ***RIKĀB* — *markab* = SHIP; *murakkab* = COMPOUND; Inshiqāq 84:19.**

**17. وَلَٰكِنَّ اللَّهَ يُسَلِّطُ رُسُلَهُ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَاءُ — *wa lākinna'llāha yusalliṭu rusulahu ʿalā man yashāʾ*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *yusalliṭu* — ⭐⭐ the "**AUTHORITY / POWER / control**" family — *saluṭa* = to be powerful, to dominate; ⭐ ***sallaṭa* (Form II) = to give POWER / AUTHORITY over**; ⭐ ***sulṭān* = AUTHORITY, POWER — and a PROOF** (⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:29** — *halaka ʿannī **sulṭāniyah***!; ⭐ ***Sulṭān* = SULTAN**); ⭐ ***sulṭah* = AUTHORITY** (⭐ *as-sulṭah at-tashrīʿiyyah* = the **LEGISLATIVE authority**!; ⭐ *as-sulṭah al-filasṭīniyyah* = the **Palestinian AUTHORITY**); ⭐ ***taslīṭ aḍ-ḍawʾ* = SHINING a spotlight / highlighting**!.
- **Here it means:** "**…but Allah gives His messengers power over whom He wills.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUSALLIṬU RUSULAHU* — He gives His messengers AUTHORITY** — ⭐ **without armies, without battle — by His decree.** ⭐ **The victory wasn't won by force; it was GRANTED.** ⭐⭐ ***Sulṭān* — ḤĀQQAH 69:29** — the left-hand man: *halaka ʿannī **sulṭāniyah*** — ⭐ **"my POWER has perished!"** ⭐ **Human *sulṭān* perishes; the *sulṭān* Allah gives His messengers prevails.** ⭐ ***Taslīṭ aḍ-ḍawʾ* = SPOTLIGHTING** — ⭐ **He "turns the spotlight" of power onto whom He wills.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lākinna*** = "**but**" (correction — not your horses, but HIS empowerment). ⭐ ***yusalliṭu*** = Form II present (habitual — this is how He works).
- **Why this exact word:** *sallaṭa* — ⭐ **authority granted, not seized — the explanation of a battle-free victory.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUSALLIṬU RUSULAHU* = He GRANTS His messengers AUTHORITY — victory GRANTED, not won by force.** ⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:29: "my *sulṭān* has PERISHED" — human power perishes; His grant prevails.** ⭐⭐ ***Sulṭah* = AUTHORITY; *Sulṭān* = SULTAN; *taslīṭ aḍ-ḍawʾ* = SPOTLIGHTING.**

**18. مَّا أَفَاءَ اللَّهُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْقُرَىٰ فَلِلَّهِ وَلِلرَّسُولِ وَلِذِي الْقُرْبَىٰ وَالْيَتَامَىٰ وَالْمَسَاكِينِ وَابْنِ السَّبِيلِ — *mā afāʾa'llāhu ʿalā rasūlihi min ahli'l-qurā fa-li'llāhi wa li'r-rasūli wa li-dhi'l-qurbā wa'l-yatāmā wa'l-masākīni wa'bni's-sabīl*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *qurā* — ⭐ **TOWNS** (plural of *qaryah*; ⭐ ***Umm al-Qurā* = MAKKAH**). *dhi'l-qurbā* — ⭐ **the near RELATIVES** (of the Prophet ﷺ — Banū Hāshim and Banū al-Muṭṭalib, classically) (⭐ *qurb* = nearness; ⭐ **Balad 90:15** — *yatīman **dhā maqrabah***!). *yatāmā* — ⭐ **ORPHANS** (*yatīm* — ⭐ from *yatama* = to be ALONE, unique — ⭐ ***durrah yatīmah* = a UNIQUE pearl**!; ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:6, 9**; ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:2**; ⭐ **Fajr 89:17**). *masākīn* — ⭐ **the POOR** (*miskīn* — made still by poverty). *ibn as-sabīl* — ⭐ **the "SON OF THE ROAD" = a stranded TRAVELLER**.
- **Here it means:** "**Whatever Allah restored to His Messenger from the people of the towns — it is for Allah and for the Messenger, and for near relatives, orphans, the needy, and the stranded traveller…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE LIST OF THE VULNERABLE — the same people you've met all through the juzʾ.** ⭐ ***Al-YATĀMĀ*** — ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:9** (*fa-ammā'l-yatīma fa-lā taqhar*), **Māʿūn 107:2** (*yaduʿʿu'l-yatīm*), **Fajr 89:17** (*lā tukrimūna'l-yatīm*), **Balad 90:15** (*yatīman dhā maqrabah*). ⭐ ***Al-MASĀKĪN*** — **SEVEN surahs** of the *miskīn* thread (and Qalam 68:24 — the garden owners who BLOCKED the poor!). ⭐⭐⭐ **In the surahs you've studied, these people were NEGLECTED, PUSHED AWAY, BLOCKED — here they're WRITTEN INTO the law of public wealth.** ⭐ **From moral exhortation to economic legislation.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***IBN AS-SABĪL* — "SON OF THE ROAD"** — ⭐ **a traveller cut off from his resources** — ⭐ **so attached to the road he's called its "son."** ⭐ **One of the 8 zakāh categories (9:60).** ⭐⭐ ***Yatīm* ← *yatama* (to be ALONE / UNIQUE)** — ⭐ ***durrah yatīmah* = a UNIQUE, priceless pearl** — ⭐ **the orphan is a "unique pearl" — precious, alone.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Dhi'l-QURBĀ* — Balad 90:15** — *yatīman dhā **MAQRABAH*** (an orphan who's a RELATIVE) — ⭐ **same root (*q-r-b*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-li'llāhi wa li'r-rasūli…*** — the ***li-*** = "**for / belongs to**" — ⭐ **six recipients joined.** ⭐ ***wa'bni's-sabīli*** — *iḍāfah* idiom.
- **Why this exact word:** **the list** — ⭐ **the juzʾ's neglected people, now named as rightful recipients of public wealth.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FAYʾ* goes to: Allah & the Messenger, RELATIVES, ORPHANS, the POOR, the STRANDED TRAVELLER.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The juzʾ's NEGLECTED (Ḍuḥā 93:9, Māʿūn 107:2, Fajr 89:17, Qalam 68:24) — here WRITTEN INTO economic LAW.** ⭐⭐ ***IBN AS-SABĪL* = "SON OF THE ROAD" — stranded traveller (zakāh category, 9:60).** ⭐⭐ ***Yatīm* ← *yatama* (ALONE) — *durrah yatīmah* = a UNIQUE pearl.** ⭐ ***Dhi'l-qurbā* — Balad 90:15 *dhā maqrabah*.** ⭐ ***Umm al-Qurā* = MAKKAH.**

**19. كَيْ لَا يَكُونَ دُولَةً بَيْنَ الْأَغْنِيَاءِ مِنكُمْ — *kay lā yakūna dūlatan bayna'l-aghniyāʾi minkum*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *kay* — ⭐ **SO THAT** (purpose). *dūlah* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**ROTATING / passing from hand to hand / turns**" family — *dāla* = **to ROTATE, to change hands, to take turns**; ⭐ ***dūlah* = something CIRCULATED / passed around (wealth)**; ⭐⭐⭐ ***dawlah* = a STATE / a DYNASTY** (power that "ROTATES" between peoples!; ⭐ *ad-dawlah al-ʿUthmāniyyah* = the **OTTOMAN STATE**!); ⭐⭐ ***tadāwul* = TRADING / CIRCULATION** (⭐ ***sūq at-tadāwul* = STOCK EXCHANGE**!; ⭐ *tadāwul al-ashum* = **share TRADING**!); ⭐ ***duwalī* = INTERNATIONAL** (between *duwal* — states!); ⭐ ***mudāwalah* = DELIBERATION / rotation**; ⭐ ***wa tilka'l-ayyāmu nudāwiluhā bayna'n-nās*** (3:140 — "these days We ROTATE among people"!). *aghniyāʾ* — ⭐⭐ **the RICH** (plural of *ghaniyy*; ⭐ **the *ghinā* thread** — **Taghābun 64:6** *wa'llāhu ghaniyyun ḥamīd*!; ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:5** *mani'staghnā*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…so that it does not become a [wealth] circulating only among the rich among you.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE GREATEST ECONOMIC PRINCIPLE IN THE QURʾĀN — in six words.** ⭐ ***Kay lā yakūna DŪLATAN bayna'l-AGHNIYĀʾ*** — ⭐ **"so wealth doesn't just CIRCULATE among the RICH."** ⭐ **Money that only passes from rich hand to rich hand = a closed loop; the poor never touch it.** ⭐ **The *fayʾ* rules (v.7a) BREAK the loop — sending wealth to orphans, the poor, travellers.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Classical scholars cite this verse as a foundational principle of Islamic economics: wealth must FLOW through society, not pool at the top** — ⭐ **the logic behind zakāh, inheritance shares, and the prohibition of hoarding.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***DŪLAH* → *DAWLAH* (STATE) → *TADĀWUL* (TRADING / STOCK EXCHANGE)** — ⭐ **one root for circulating wealth, rotating power, and market trading.** ⭐ ***Sūq at-tadāwul* = STOCK EXCHANGE** — ⭐ **the Qurʾān's word for "circulation" became the modern word for "exchange."** ⭐ ***Dawlah* = STATE** — ⭐ **because power "rotates" between peoples (3:140).** ⭐ ***Duwalī* = INTERNATIONAL.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AGHNIYĀʾ* — the RICH — and the *GHINĀ* thread:** ⭐ ***istaghnā*** (thinking oneself free of need — ʿAbasa 80:5, Layl 92:8, ʿAlaq 96:7) ⭐ ***mā aghnā*** (wealth that didn't avail — Masad 111:2, Ḥāqqah 69:28) ⭐ ***wa'llāhu ghaniyyun ḥamīd*** (Taghābun 64:6 — Allah truly rich) ⭐ ***dūlatan bayna'l-aghniyāʾ*** (Ḥashr 59:7 — don't let the rich HOARD the circulation). ⭐ **And Humazah 104:2 — *jamaʿa mālan wa ʿaddadah*; Maʿārij 70:18 — *jamaʿa fa-awʿā* (HOARDING).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kay lā*** + subjunctive = "**so that… not**." ⭐ ***dūlatan*** = *khabar* of *yakūna* (subject = implied *al-fayʾ*). ⚠️ ⭐ **Recitation variant: *dūlatun*** (with *yakūna* complete — "so that there be no circulation…").
- **Why this exact word:** *dūlah* — ⭐ **wealth as something that circulates — and the command to widen its circle.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KAY LĀ YAKŪNA DŪLATAN BAYNA'L-AGHNIYĀʾ* = so wealth doesn't CIRCULATE only among the RICH — the foundational ECONOMIC principle.** ⭐ **Break the closed loop; let wealth FLOW.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DŪLAH* → *DAWLAH* (STATE — power rotates, 3:140) → *TADĀWUL* (TRADING) → *SŪQ AT-TADĀWUL* (STOCK EXCHANGE) → *DUWALĪ* (INTERNATIONAL).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHINĀ* thread: *istaghnā* (80:5, 92:8, 96:7), *mā aghnā* (111:2, 69:28), *ghaniyy* (64:6), *aghniyāʾ* (59:7); hoarding (104:2, 70:18).**

**20. وَمَا آتَاكُمُ الرَّسُولُ فَخُذُوهُ وَمَا نَهَاكُمْ عَنْهُ فَانتَهُوا — *wa mā ātākumu'r-rasūlu fa-khudhūhu wa mā nahākum ʿanhu fa'ntahū*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *ātākum* — ⭐ **GAVE you** (Form IV of *atā*). *khudhūhu* — ⭐ **TAKE it** (*akhadha*). *nahākum* — ⭐⭐ the "**FORBIDDING / END / limit**" family — *nahā* = **to FORBID**; ⭐ ***nahy* = a PROHIBITION** (⭐ *al-amr bi'l-maʿrūf wa'n-**nahy** ʿani'l-munkar*!); ⭐ ***intahā* = to STOP / END** (⭐ ***intahā!* = "it's OVER / FINISHED!"**; ⭐ ***nihāyah* = the END**; ⭐ ***muntahā* = the ultimate end** — **Nāziʿāt 79:44** — *ilā rabbika **muntahāhā***!; ⭐ ***sidrat al-muntahā*** — the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary!); ⭐ ***nuhā* = INTELLECT** (what STOPS you from folly! — ⭐ the RESTRAINT family again — *ʿaql*, *ḥijr*, *ḥikmah*!).
- **Here it means:** "**And whatever the Messenger gives you — take it; and whatever he forbids you — refrain from it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE AUTHORITY OF THE SUNNAH — in one sentence.** ⭐ **The context: distributing *fayʾ* — "accept the Prophet's ﷺ allocation."** ⭐ **But the wording is GENERAL: *mā ātākum… wa mā nahākum*** — ⭐ **"WHATEVER he gives… WHATEVER he forbids."** ⭐⭐⭐ **The famous story (Bukhārī, Muslim): ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd said "Allah has cursed the woman who tattoos…" — a woman objected: "I've read the whole Qurʾān and didn't find that!" He replied: "If you had READ it, you would have found it — didn't you read *wa mā ātākumu'r-rasūlu fa-khudhūhu wa mā nahākum ʿanhu fa'ntahū*?"** ⭐ **The verse as a GATEWAY: the Qurʾān itself commands following the Sunnah.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***NAHY* (FORBIDDING) → *INTAHĀ* (STOP / END) → *NUHĀ* (INTELLECT)** — ⭐ **a prohibition marks an END-POINT; you STOP there.** ⭐ ***Nuhā* = INTELLECT** — ⭐ **the mind that knows where to STOP** — ⭐ **the RESTRAINT family: *ʿaql* (hobble — Mulk 67:10), *ḥijr* (wall — Fajr 89:5), *ḥikmah* (bridle — Tīn 95:8), *nuhā* (stopping-point).** ⭐ ***Sidrat al-muntahā*** — **the Lote Tree at the UTMOST END** (53:14). ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:44** — *ilā rabbika **muntahāhā*** (its END is with your Lord). ⭐ ***Intahā!* = "It's OVER!"**
  ⭐⭐ **And Taghābun 64:12** — *wa aṭīʿu'llāha wa **aṭīʿu'r-rasūl*** — ⭐ **"obey Allah and OBEY the Messenger" (verb repeated).** ⭐ **Ḥashr makes it concrete: TAKE what he gives, STOP at what he forbids.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mā*** ×2 = conditional relatives ("**whatever**") + ***fa*** answers. ⭐ ***fa'ntahū*** = **Form VIII** imperative ("**bring yourselves to a stop**").
- **Why this exact word:** **general *mā*** — ⭐ **a specific allocation phrased so that it establishes a universal principle.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MĀ ĀTĀKUMU'R-RASŪLU FA-KHUDHŪHU WA MĀ NAHĀKUM ʿANHU FA'NTAHŪ* = TAKE what he gives, STOP at what he forbids — the SUNNAH's authority.** ⭐⭐⭐ **IBN MASʿŪD (Bukhārī, Muslim): "If you'd READ the Qurʾān, you'd have found it" — this verse as the GATEWAY.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***NAHY* → *INTAHĀ* (STOP / "it's OVER!") → *NUHĀ* (INTELLECT — knowing where to stop) → *SIDRAT AL-MUNTAHĀ*.** ⭐⭐ **RESTRAINT family: *ʿaql*, *ḥijr*, *ḥikmah*, *nuhā*.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:12 *aṭīʿu'r-rasūl*.** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:44 *muntahāhā*.**

**21. وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ شَدِيدُ الْعِقَابِ — *wa'ttaqu'llāh · inna'llāha shadīdu'l-ʿiqāb*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *ittaqū* — ⭐ **SHIELD yourselves** (*taqwā*). *shadīd al-ʿiqāb* — ⭐ **SEVERE in PENALTY** (⭐ v.4!).
- **Here it means:** "**And fear Allah; indeed Allah is severe in penalty.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***SHADĪD AL-ʿIQĀB* — the SECOND time (v.4, v.7)** — ⭐ **v.4: for those who OPPOSE Allah (the exiled tribe).** ⭐ **v.7: for believers who'd DISOBEY the Messenger's allocation.** ⭐⭐ **The same warning for outsiders and insiders** — ⭐ **no one is exempt.** ⭐ ***ʿIqāb* ← *ʿaqib* (HEEL) — the penalty FOLLOWS on the heels.** ⭐ **And the *taqwā* — after the distribution rule — because money disputes test *taqwā* most** (⭐ like Ṭalāq's *taqwā* around divorce finances!).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa'ttaqū*** = imperative. ⭐ ***inna*** = reason.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the same warning, now applied to the believers' own conduct.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***SHADĪD AL-ʿIQĀB* ×2: v.4 (OUTSIDERS who oppose) / v.7 (INSIDERS who'd disobey) — no one exempt.** ⭐ ***Taqwā* where MONEY is at stake (like Ṭalāq).** ⭐ ***ʿIqāb* ← HEEL.**

## §3 — The Three Generations (v.8–10)

*⭐ The *fayʾ* goes to three groups — and they're the three generations of the ummah: the **Emigrants** (v.8), the **Helpers** (v.9), and **those who came after** (v.10). Imām Mālik and others drew from this: whoever reviles the Companions has excluded himself from the third group.*

**22. لِلْفُقَرَاءِ الْمُهَاجِرِينَ الَّذِينَ أُخْرِجُوا مِن دِيَارِهِمْ وَأَمْوَالِهِمْ — *li'l-fuqarāʾi'l-muhājirīna'lladhīna ukhrijū min diyārihim wa amwālihim*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *fuqarāʾ* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**BROKEN BACKBONE**" family — *faqara* = **to break the VERTEBRAE**; ⭐⭐ ***faqārah* (pl. *fiqār*) = a VERTEBRA** (⭐ clinical: ***al-ʿamūd al-faqarī* = the SPINAL COLUMN / vertebral column**!; ⭐ ***faqāriyyāt* = VERTEBRATES**!); ⭐⭐ ***faqīr* (pl. *fuqarāʾ*) = POOR** — classically: **"one whose BACKBONE is broken by need"**; ⭐ ***faqr* = POVERTY**; ⭐ ***fāqirah* = a back-breaking calamity** (⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:25** — *taẓunnu an yufʿala bihā **fāqirah***!); ⭐ ***fiqrah* = a PARAGRAPH** (a "vertebra" of text!). *muhājirīn* — ⭐⭐ the "**ABANDONING / leaving**" family — *hajara* = to abandon, to leave; ⭐ ***hijrah* = EMIGRATION** (⭐ the Hijrah of 622 — start of the Islamic calendar!); ⭐ ***muhājir* = an EMIGRANT / immigrant** (⭐ modern: ***muhājir* = an IMMIGRANT / migrant**!); ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:10** — *wa'**hjurhum hajran** jamīlā*!; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:5** — *wa'r-rujza **fa'hjur***!. *ukhrijū* — ⭐ **were EXPELLED** (passive).
- **Here it means:** "**[It is also] for the poor emigrants who were expelled from their homes and their properties…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **GENERATION 1: the MUHĀJIRŪN — the EMIGRANTS.** ⭐ ***Al-FUQARĀʾ*** — ⭐ **the POOR — literally "those whose BACKBONES were broken"** — ⭐ **they'd left EVERYTHING in Makkah: homes, wealth, businesses — and arrived in Madīnah with nothing.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FAQĪR* ← *FAQĀRAH* (a VERTEBRA)** — ⭐ **poverty as a broken spine — the weight of need cracking you.** ⭐ ***Al-ʿamūd al-faqarī* = the SPINAL COLUMN; *faqāriyyāt* = VERTEBRATES** (clinical/biology!). ⭐ ***Fāqirah* — Qiyāmah 75:25** — *a "BACK-BREAKING" calamity.* ⭐ ***Fiqrah* = a PARAGRAPH.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***UKHRIJŪ* — EXPELLED (passive)** — ⭐ **v.2: Allah EXPELLED (*akhraja*) the treaty-breakers — from their homes. v.8: the Muhājirūn were EXPELLED (*ukhrijū*) by the Quraysh — from their homes.** ⭐ **Same verb, two expulsions: one deserved, one suffered for faith.** ⭐ **And the "*fayʾ*" of the first goes to the victims of the second — justice in the distribution.**
  ⭐⭐ ***HIJRAH* — Muzzammil 73:10 (*hajran jamīlā* — beautiful LEAVING), Muddaththir 74:5 (*fa'hjur* — LEAVE the filth)** — ⭐ **the Muhājirūn lived it: they LEFT everything for Allah.** ⭐ ***Muhājir* = an IMMIGRANT** (modern).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li'l-fuqarāʾi*** — the ***li-*** = "**for**" — classically a ***badal*** (specification) of the recipients in v.7. ⭐ ***ukhrijū*** = **passive** (expelled — by others).
- **Why this exact word:** *faqīr* — ⭐ **poverty as a broken backbone — the cost of emigration.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **GENERATION 1: the MUHĀJIRŪN — left EVERYTHING.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FAQĪR* ← *FAQĀRAH* (VERTEBRA) = poverty as a BROKEN BACKBONE — *al-ʿamūd al-faqarī* = SPINAL COLUMN; *faqāriyyāt* = VERTEBRATES; *fiqrah* = PARAGRAPH; Qiyāmah 75:25 *fāqirah*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AKHRAJA* (v.2 — Allah expelled the treaty-breakers) / *UKHRIJŪ* (v.8 — believers expelled for faith) — the *fayʾ* of one goes to the victims of the other.** ⭐⭐ ***Hijrah*: Muzzammil 73:10, Muddaththir 74:5; *muhājir* = IMMIGRANT.**

**23. يَبْتَغُونَ فَضْلًا مِّنَ اللَّهِ وَرِضْوَانًا وَيَنصُرُونَ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ ۚ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الصَّادِقُونَ — *yabtaghūna faḍlan mina'llāhi wa riḍwānan wa yanṣurūna'llāha wa rasūlah · ulāʾika humu'ṣ-ṣādiqūn*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *yabtaghūna* — ⭐ **SEEKING** (⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:10** *wa'btaghū min faḍli'llāh*!; ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20**). *faḍl* — ⭐ **BOUNTY** (⭐ ***min faḍlik* = PLEASE**!). *riḍwān* — ⭐⭐ **GOOD PLEASURE** (*riḍā*; ⭐ ***Riḍwān*** = the name of the keeper of Paradise!; ⭐ ***Bayʿat ar-Riḍwān*** — 48:18!). *yanṣurūna* — ⭐ **SUPPORT / help** (*naṣr*; ⭐ ***al-Anṣār***!). *ṣādiqūn* — ⭐⭐⭐ **the TRUTHFUL** (*ṣidq*; ⭐ ***ṣadīq* = FRIEND**; ⭐ ***aṣ-Ṣiddīq***!).
- **Here it means:** "**…seeking bounty from Allah and His pleasure, and supporting Allah and His Messenger — those are the truthful.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HUMU'Ṣ-ṢĀDIQŪN* — "THEY are THE TRUTHFUL" — and the contrast with MUNĀFIQŪN is perfect.** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:1** — *wa'llāhu yashhadu inna'l-munāfiqīna **la-KĀDHIBŪN*** — ⭐ **the hypocrites — LIARS (they CLAIMED faith, didn't live it).** ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:8** — ***ulāʾika humu'Ṣ-ṢĀDIQŪN*** — ⭐ **the Emigrants — the TRUTHFUL (they PROVED faith by leaving everything).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Their *ṣidq* (truthfulness) was demonstrated by SACRIFICE** — ⭐ **anyone can SAY "we testify" (63:1); the Muhājirūn PAID for it.** ⭐ **And v.11 (this surah!) — the hypocrites again: *wa'llāhu yashhadu innahum la-KĀDHIBŪN*.** ⭐ **Ḥashr: TRUTHFUL (v.8) vs LIARS (v.11).**
  ⭐⭐ ***FAḌLAN wa RIḌWĀNAN*** — ⭐ **BOUNTY (provision — material) + PLEASURE (approval — spiritual)** — ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:10** — *wa'btaghū min **faḍli'llāh*** (seek His bounty in trade). ⭐ **The Muhājirūn sought BOTH — and gave up all else for them.** ⭐ ***Riḍwān*** — **Bayyinah 98:8** — *raḍiya'llāhu ʿanhum wa raḍū ʿanh*.
  ⭐⭐ ***Yanṣurūna'LLĀHA*** — ⭐ **"they SUPPORT ALLAH"** — ⭐ **by supporting His religion and Messenger** — ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:14 (this set!)** — *kūnū **anṣāra'llāh*** — ⭐ **"be HELPERS of Allah."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yabtaghūna, yanṣurūna*** = *ḥāl* (present — ongoing). ⭐⭐ ***ulāʾika humu'ṣ-ṣādiqūna*** = separating pronoun + definite = **"THEY are THE truthful"** (restriction).
- **Why this exact word:** *ṣādiqūn* — ⭐ **truthfulness proven by sacrifice — the opposite of the hypocrites' *kādhibūn*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HUMU'Ṣ-ṢĀDIQŪN* (Ḥashr 59:8 — the Emigrants, TRUTHFUL by SACRIFICE) vs *LA-KĀDHIBŪN* (Munāfiqūn 63:1, Ḥashr 59:11 — hypocrites, LIARS by claim).** ⭐ **Anyone can SAY; the Muhājirūn PAID.** ⭐⭐ ***FAḌL* (bounty — material) + *RIḌWĀN* (pleasure — spiritual) — Jumuʿah 62:10.** ⭐⭐ ***Yanṣurūna'llāh* → Ṣaff 61:14 *anṣāra'llāh*.** ⭐ ***Bayʿat ar-Riḍwān*; *Riḍwān* = Paradise's keeper.**

**24. وَالَّذِينَ تَبَوَّءُوا الدَّارَ وَالْإِيمَانَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ — *wa'lladhīna tabawwaʾu'd-dāra wa'l-īmāna min qablihim*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *tabawwaʾū* — ⭐⭐ the "**SETTLING / taking up residence / returning to one's place**" family — *bāʾa* = to return; ⭐ ***tabawwaʾa* (Form V) = to SETTLE IN, to take as a home**; ⭐ ***mubawwaʾ* = a dwelling-place / position**; ⭐ ***bīʾah* = an ENVIRONMENT / habitat** (⭐ modern: ***bīʾah* = the ENVIRONMENT**!; ⭐ *ṣadīq li'l-bīʾah* = **eco-FRIENDLY**!; ⭐ *bīʾī* = **ENVIRONMENTAL**); ⭐ ***bāʾa bi-* = to RETURN WITH (carry) [guilt/anger]** (⭐ *bāʾū bi-ghaḍab* — they returned with anger!). *ad-dār* — ⭐ **the ABODE** (= Madīnah — *Dār al-Hijrah*!). *al-īmān* — ⭐ **FAITH**.
- **Here it means:** "**And [also for] those who settled in the Abode and [in] faith before them…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **GENERATION 2: the ANṢĀR — and a BEAUTIFUL GRAMMATICAL "STRETCH."** ⭐ ***Tabawwaʾu'D-DĀRA wa'l-ĪMĀNA*** — ⭐ **"they SETTLED IN the abode AND [settled in] FAITH"** — ⭐ **one verb with TWO objects of different kinds: a PLACE (Madīnah) and a STATE (faith).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Classical (a famous *balāghah* point — *taḍmīn* / zeugma): you "settle" in a HOUSE — but the Anṣār also "settled" in FAITH, as if faith were a HOME they lived in.** ⭐ **Faith for them wasn't a visit — it was RESIDENCE.** ⭐ ***Mubawwaʾ* = a dwelling-place.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***BĪʾAH* = the ENVIRONMENT — same root!** ⭐ **the "place you settle in"** — ⭐ **the Anṣār created an ENVIRONMENT of faith (*bīʾah īmāniyyah*) into which the Emigrants could come.** ⭐ ***Ṣadīq li'l-bīʾah* = eco-FRIENDLY; *bīʾī* = ENVIRONMENTAL.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ad-DĀR* = Madīnah — *Dār al-Hijrah*, *Dār al-Īmān*** — ⭐ **the Anṣār's city (formerly Yathrib) — Munāfiqūn 63:8 (*al-madīnah*).** ⭐ ***Min QABLIHIM*** — ⭐ **"before THEM [the Emigrants arrived]"** — ⭐ **the Anṣār had embraced Islam at ʿAqabah and prepared the city.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***tabawwaʾū*** with **two objects: *ad-dāra* (literal) and *al-īmāna* (figurative)** — ⭐ classically explained with an **implied verb** ("settled the abode and *embraced* faith") or as ***taḍmīn*** (one verb carrying both senses).
- **Why this exact word:** *tabawwaʾa* — ⭐ **faith as a home one settles in — the Anṣār's defining trait.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **GENERATION 2: the ANṢĀR — *TABAWWAʾU'D-DĀRA WA'L-ĪMĀNA* = SETTLED in the ABODE and in FAITH — ONE verb, a PLACE + a STATE (zeugma!).** ⭐ **Faith wasn't a visit — it was RESIDENCE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BĪʾAH* = ENVIRONMENT (same root!) — they created an ENVIRONMENT of faith; *ṣadīq li'l-bīʾah* = eco-FRIENDLY.** ⭐⭐ ***Ad-dār* = MADĪNAH (*Dār al-Hijrah*); *min qablihim* = before the Emigrants.** ⭐ ***Mubawwaʾ* = dwelling.**

**25. يُحِبُّونَ مَنْ هَاجَرَ إِلَيْهِمْ وَلَا يَجِدُونَ فِي صُدُورِهِمْ حَاجَةً مِّمَّا أُوتُوا — *yuḥibbūna man hājara ilayhim wa lā yajidūna fī ṣudūrihim ḥājatan mimmā ūtū*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *yuḥibbūna* — ⭐ **they LOVE** (*ḥubb*; ⭐ ***ḥabīb* = BELOVED**; ⭐ ***ḥabb* = a GRAIN / seed** — ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:27** *ḥabbā*; ⭐ ***ḥabbah* = a PILL / tablet** — clinical!). *hājara* — ⭐ **EMIGRATED** (*hijrah*). *ṣudūr* — ⭐ **CHESTS** (⭐ **Taghābun 64:4** *dhāt aṣ-ṣudūr*!). *ḥājah* — ⭐⭐ the "**NEED / want**" family — ***ḥājah* = a NEED / want / desire** (⭐ everyday: ***ḥājah* = a THING / need** — *ʿindī ḥājah* = "I have something/a need"!; ⭐ ***ḥawāʾij* = NEEDS / groceries / belongings**!; ⭐ ***iḥtiyāj* = NEED** (*dhawū al-iḥtiyājāt al-khāṣṣah* = **people with SPECIAL NEEDS**!)). *ūtū* — ⭐ **[what] they were GIVEN** (the Emigrants).
- **Here it means:** "**…they love those who emigrated to them, and find no desire [envy] in their chests for what [the Emigrants] were given…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ANṢĀR'S HEARTS — TWO qualities:** ⭐ **(1) *YUḤIBBŪNA man hājara ilayhim*** — ⭐ **they LOVE the newcomers** — ⭐ **not tolerate — LOVE** — ⭐ **refugees arriving with nothing, welcomed with love.** ⭐ **(2) *Wa lā yajidūna fī ṣudūrihim ḤĀJATAN mimmā ūtū*** — ⭐ **they feel NO envy / no "need" in their CHESTS for what the Emigrants received** (the *fayʾ* of Banū an-Naḍīr was given mostly to the Emigrants — and the Anṣār didn't resent it!). ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤĀJAH* = a NEED / want** — ⭐ **"they found no WANT in their chests"** — ⭐ **no wish for what the others got, no inner grudge.** ⭐ **The hardest test: watching someone else receive what you didn't — and feeling nothing but happiness for them.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***FĪ ṢUDŪRIHIM* — the CHEST thread:** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:4** — *ʿalīmun bi-**dhāti'ṣ-ṣudūr*** (He knows what's in the chests). ⭐ **Nās 114:5** — *yuwaswisu fī **ṣudūri**'n-nās* (whispering INTO chests). ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:9** — ⭐ **the Anṣār's chests: EMPTY of envy.** ⭐ **v.13 (this surah): the hypocrites' chests — full of FEAR of people.** ⭐ **v.10: "put no RANCOUR in our hearts."** ⭐⭐ **What's in the chest decides who you are.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ḥawāʾij* = NEEDS / GROCERIES; *dhawū al-iḥtiyājāt al-khāṣṣah* = SPECIAL NEEDS** (everyday). ⭐ ***Ḥabbah* = a PILL** (from *ḥubb*'s cousin *ḥabb*, seed!).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yuḥibbūna*** = *ḥāl* / predicate (present — ongoing love). ⭐ ***ḥājatan*** = object — **indefinite in negation = ANY need/envy at all**. ⭐ ***mimmā ūtū*** — ***ūtū*** = **passive** ("what THEY [the Emigrants] were given").
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥājah* — ⭐ **the absence of even a "want" for another's share — the purity of the Anṣār's chests.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ANṢĀR'S HEARTS: (1) they LOVE the newcomers (*yuḥibbūna man hājara*); (2) NO ENVY (*lā yajidūna… ḥājatan*) for what the Emigrants received (the *fayʾ*!).** ⭐ **Watching someone else get what you didn't — and feeling only joy.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ṣudūr* thread: He KNOWS (64:4), Shayṭān WHISPERS (114:5), the Anṣār's EMPTY of envy (59:9), hypocrites' full of FEAR (59:13).** ⭐⭐ ***ḤĀJAH* = NEED; *ḥawāʾij* = GROCERIES; *iḥtiyājāt khāṣṣah* = SPECIAL NEEDS.** ⭐ ***Ḥabbah* = a PILL.**

**26. وَيُؤْثِرُونَ عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ وَلَوْ كَانَ بِهِمْ خَصَاصَةٌ — *wa yuʾthirūna ʿalā anfusihim wa law kāna bihim khaṣāṣah*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *yuʾthirūna* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**TRACE / FOOTPRINT → PREFERENCE**" family — ***athar* = a TRACE, a FOOTPRINT, a mark left behind** (⭐ ***āthār* = ANTIQUITIES / ruins / effects** — *ʿilm al-āthār* = **ARCHAEOLOGY**!; ⭐ ***athar* = a ḤADĪTH report** — *ahl al-athar* = the people of narration!); ⭐ ***taʾthīr* = INFLUENCE / EFFECT** (⭐ *muʾaththir* = **INFLUENTIAL** — *muʾaththir* = an **INFLUENCER**!; ⭐ *taʾthīrāt jānibiyyah* = **SIDE EFFECTS** — clinical!); ⭐⭐ ***āthara* (Form IV) = to PREFER someone over oneself, to give priority**; ⭐ ***īthār* = SELFLESS PREFERENCE / altruism**; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:24** — *in hādhā illā siḥrun **yuʾthar*** (magic "handed down" — a TRACE from the past!). *khaṣāṣah* — ⭐⭐ the "**GAPS / poverty**" family — *khaṣṣa* = to single out; ⭐ ***khaṣāṣah* = dire NEED, POVERTY** (⭐ classically from ***khuṣṣ* = a HUT with GAPS** in its walls — a poor man's shelter!); ⭐ ***khāṣṣ* = PRIVATE / special**; ⭐ ***takhaṣṣuṣ* = SPECIALIZATION** (⭐ — *ṭabīb mutakhaṣṣiṣ* = a **SPECIALIST** doctor!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and they prefer [others] over themselves, even though they themselves are in dire need.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ĪTHĀR* — THE HIGHEST LEVEL OF GENEROSITY in the Qurʾān.** ⭐ **Three levels (classical): (1) *sakhāʾ* — giving from SURPLUS; (2) *jūd* — giving most and keeping a little; (3) *ĪTHĀR* — giving what YOU NEED to someone else, while you go without.** ⭐ ***Wa law kāna bihim KHAṢĀṢAH*** — ⭐ **"EVEN IF they're in dire need themselves."** ⭐⭐⭐ **The famous story (Bukhārī, Muslim): a guest came to the Prophet ﷺ; an Anṣārī (Abū Ṭalḥah, in many reports) took him home — there was only enough food for the children. His wife put the children to sleep hungry, then they PUT OUT THE LAMP and pretended to eat in the dark so the guest would eat his fill.** ⭐ **The next morning the Prophet ﷺ said: "*Allah marvelled at what you two did last night*" — and this verse was revealed.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ĪTHĀR* ← *ATHAR* (a FOOTPRINT / TRACE)** — ⭐ **classically: to "put someone ahead of you," as if letting them walk FIRST while you follow in their FOOTPRINTS.** ⭐ ***Āthār* = ANTIQUITIES (*ʿilm al-āthār* = ARCHAEOLOGY); *athar* = a ḤADĪTH report; *taʾthīr* = INFLUENCE; *muʾaththir* = an INFLUENCER; *taʾthīrāt jānibiyyah* = SIDE EFFECTS (clinical!).** ⭐ **The Anṣār left an *athar* (trace) that still *yuʾaththir* (influences) us — through their *īthār*.**
  ⭐⭐ ***KHAṢĀṢAH* ← *KHUṢṢ* (a HUT with GAPS)** — ⭐ **poverty pictured as a shelter full of holes** — ⭐ **and they STILL gave.** ⭐ ***Takhaṣṣuṣ* = SPECIALIZATION; *ṭabīb mutakhaṣṣiṣ* = SPECIALIST.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yuʾthirūna*** = Form IV present (habitual). ⭐ ***ʿalā anfusihim*** = "**over themselves**." ⭐⭐ ***wa law*** = "**EVEN IF**" (concessive).
- **Why this exact word:** *īthār* — ⭐ **the highest generosity — giving away what you yourself need.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ĪTHĀR* = the HIGHEST generosity — giving what YOU NEED to another (*sakhāʾ* < *jūd* < *īthār*).** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE STORY (Bukhārī, Muslim): children put to sleep hungry, LAMP put out, pretending to eat in the dark — "Allah MARVELLED at you two."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ĪTHĀR* ← *ATHAR* (FOOTPRINT): letting them go first — *āthār* = ANTIQUITIES / ARCHAEOLOGY; *athar* = ḤADĪTH; *taʾthīr* = INFLUENCE; *muʾaththir* = INFLUENCER; *taʾthīrāt jānibiyyah* = SIDE EFFECTS.** ⭐⭐ ***KHAṢĀṢAH* ← *khuṣṣ* = a HUT with GAPS — poverty as a holey shelter.** ⭐ ***Takhaṣṣuṣ* = SPECIALIZATION.** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:24 *yuʾthar*.**

**27. وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهِ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ — *wa man yūqa shuḥḥa nafsihi fa-ulāʾika humu'l-mufliḥūn*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *yūqa* — ⭐ **is PROTECTED** (passive of *waqā* — the *taqwā* root!). *shuḥḥ* — ⭐⭐ **greedy AVARICE** (⭐ ***shuḥḥ* = SHORTAGE** — *shuḥḥ al-miyāh* = WATER SHORTAGE!). *mufliḥūn* — ⭐ **the SUCCESSFUL** (*falāḥ* ← the FARMER).
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever is protected from the greed of his own soul — those are the successful ones.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD IDENTICAL to TAGHĀBUN 64:16** — ⭐ ***wa man yūqa shuḥḥa nafsihi fa-ulāʾika humu'l-mufliḥūn*** — ⭐ **you met it as an ABSTRACT principle in Taghābun; here it's illustrated by REAL PEOPLE — the ANṢĀR.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Taghābun: "whoever is saved from inner greed succeeds" (the RULE). Ḥashr: here are the people who were (the PROOF).** ⭐ **The lamp put out in the dark = freedom from *shuḥḥ* in action.** ⭐⭐ ***Shuḥḥ* = SHORTAGE (modern)** — ⭐ **the greedy soul always feels SCARCITY; the Anṣār, in real scarcity (*khaṣāṣah*), felt abundance enough to give.** ⭐ ***Yūqa* — PASSIVE: PROTECTED by Allah** — ⭐ **even the Anṣār's generosity was a gift from Him.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yūqa*** = passive jussive. ⭐ ***humu'l-mufliḥūna*** = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the Taghābun principle, now with faces and names.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= TAGHĀBUN 64:16 WORD-FOR-WORD — Taghābun gives the RULE; Ḥashr gives the PROOF (the Anṣār).** ⭐⭐ **In REAL scarcity (*khaṣāṣah*) — free from FELT scarcity (*shuḥḥ*).** ⭐ ***Yūqa* = PROTECTED (by Him) — even generosity is His gift.** ⭐ ***Shuḥḥ al-miyāh* = WATER SHORTAGE.**

**28. وَالَّذِينَ جَاءُوا مِن بَعْدِهِمْ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لَنَا وَلِإِخْوَانِنَا الَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِالْإِيمَانِ — *wa'lladhīna jāʾū min baʿdihim yaqūlūna rabbana'ghfir lanā wa li-ikhwāninā'lladhīna sabaqūnā bi'l-īmān*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *jāʾū min baʿdihim* — ⭐ **CAME AFTER them**. *ikhwān* — ⭐ **BROTHERS** (*akh*; ⭐ ***ukhuwwah* = BROTHERHOOD**). *sabaqūnā* — ⭐⭐ the "**RACING AHEAD / preceding**" family — *sabaqa* = to precede, to outrun; ⭐ ***sābiq* = PREVIOUS / former** (⭐ *ar-raʾīs as-sābiq* = the **FORMER president**!); ⭐ ***musābaqah* = a COMPETITION / race**; ⭐ ***as-sābiqūn* = the FORERUNNERS** (⭐ *wa's-sābiqūna's-sābiqūn* — 56:10!); ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:4** — *fa's-**sābiqāti** sabqā*; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:41** — *mā naḥnu bi-**masbūqīn***.
- **Here it means:** "**And those who came after them, saying: 'Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith…'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **GENERATION 3: "THOSE WHO CAME AFTER" — that's US.** ⭐ **Every Muslim since the Companions — including everyone reading this — is in this group.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And our defining trait isn't hijrah (we didn't emigrate) or nuṣrah (we didn't host) — it's a DUʿĀʾ: *rabbana'ghfir lanā wa li-ikhwāninā'lladhīna sabaqūnā bi'l-īmān*** — ⭐ **"forgive us AND our BROTHERS who PRECEDED us in faith."** ⭐ **We earn a share by LOVING and PRAYING for those who came before.** ⭐⭐ **Imām Mālik (and others) drew from this: whoever reviles the Companions has left the third category — he has no share in the *fayʾ*.** ⭐ **Love for the Companions is part of the definition of this ummah.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***IKHWĀNINĀ* — "our BROTHERS"** — ⭐ **across centuries — we've never met them, but they're our brothers.** ⭐ ***Ukhuwwah* = BROTHERHOOD.** ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ: "*I wish we could see our brothers*" — "aren't we your brothers?" — "*You are my COMPANIONS; my BROTHERS are those who will come after and believe in me without seeing me*" (Muslim).** ⭐⭐ **They (the Companions) are our brothers; we (later believers) are his ﷺ brothers.**
  ⭐⭐ ***SABAQŪNĀ*** — ⭐ **"PRECEDED us / RACED AHEAD of us"** — ⭐ ***sābiq* = FORMER; *musābaqah* = a RACE** — ⭐ **faith as a race; they reached the finish first.** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:4** — *fa's-sābiqāti sabqā* (the angels that RACE ahead).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yaqūlūna*** = *ḥāl* — **their defining feature is what they SAY (their duʿāʾ).** ⭐ ***rabbana'ghfir*** — vocative + imperative (supplication).
- **Why this exact word:** **a duʿāʾ as an identity** — ⭐ **the later generations defined by prayer for the earlier.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **GENERATION 3: THOSE WHO CAME AFTER = US — defined by a DUʿĀʾ: "forgive us and our BROTHERS who PRECEDED us in faith."** ⭐⭐ **Mālik: whoever reviles the Companions has LEFT this category.** ⭐⭐⭐ **"My COMPANIONS — my BROTHERS are those who believe without seeing me" (Muslim).** ⭐⭐ ***SABAQŪNĀ* = RACED AHEAD — *sābiq* = FORMER; *musābaqah* = RACE; Nāziʿāt 79:4.** ⭐ ***Ukhuwwah* = BROTHERHOOD.**

**29. وَلَا تَجْعَلْ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلًّا لِّلَّذِينَ آمَنُوا رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ رَءُوفٌ رَّحِيمٌ — *wa lā tajʿal fī qulūbinā ghillan li'lladhīna āmanū rabbanā innaka raʾūfun raḥīm*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *ghill* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**COLLAR / SHACKLE → hidden RANCOUR**" family — ***ghull* = an iron COLLAR** binding the neck (⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:30** — *khudhūhu **fa-ghullūh***!; ⭐ **Insān 76:4** — *salāsila wa **aghlālā***); ⭐⭐ ***ghill* = RANCOUR, hidden resentment, malice** (a "collar" on the heart!); ⭐ ***ghalīl* = burning THIRST** (⭐ *shafāʾ al-ghalīl* = quenching a burning thirst / satisfying a grudge!); ⭐ ***ghullah* = a YIELD / harvest**. *raʾūf* — ⭐⭐ the "**TENDER COMPASSION**" family — ***raʾfah* = TENDERNESS, gentle compassion** (⭐ classically: *raʾfah* = **intense mercy that removes harm**; *raḥmah* = mercy that **brings good**); ⭐ ***ar-Raʾūf*** — a Name of Allah.
- **Here it means:** "**…and put not in our hearts any rancour toward those who believe. Our Lord, You are Kind and Merciful.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHILL* = RANCOUR — from *GHULL* = an iron COLLAR.** ⭐ **Resentment is a COLLAR around the heart** — ⭐ **heavy, choking, binding.** ⭐⭐⭐ **ḤĀQQAH 69:30** — *khudhūhu **fa-ghullūh*** — ⭐ **"seize him and COLLAR him!" — the man in Hell, iron collar on his NECK.** ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:10** — *wa lā tajʿal fī qulūbinā **ghillan*** — ⭐ **"don't put a COLLAR (rancour) on our HEARTS."** ⭐⭐ **The collar of rancour in this life → the iron collar in the next.** ⭐ **And in PARADISE: *wa nazaʿnā mā fī ṣudūrihim min GHILL*** (7:43, 15:47) — ⭐ **"We will REMOVE whatever RANCOUR is in their chests."** ⭐⭐⭐ **This duʿāʾ asks for in DUNYA what Allah will do for the people of Paradise.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Li'lladhīna ĀMANŪ*** — ⭐ **toward ANY believer — past, present, future** — ⭐ **the antidote to sectarian bitterness.** ⭐ **The ḥadīth of the man promised Paradise who simply slept each night with "no ill-feeling toward any Muslim" (Aḥmad).**
  ⭐⭐ ***RAʾŪFUN RAḤĪM*** — ⭐ ***raʾfah* = removing HARM; *raḥmah* = bringing GOOD** — ⭐ **"remove the rancour (harm) and fill us with love (good)."**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Ghalīl* = burning THIRST** — ⭐ **rancour burns like thirst.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā tajʿal*** = negative supplication (jussive). ⭐ ***ghillan*** = indefinite in negation = "**ANY rancour at all**." ⭐ ***raʾūfun raḥīmun*** — two intensives.
- **Why this exact word:** *ghill* — ⭐ **rancour as a collar on the heart — the opposite of the Anṣār's envy-free chests.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHILL* (RANCOUR) ← *GHULL* (iron COLLAR) — resentment = a COLLAR on the heart.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:30 *fa-ghullūh* (COLLAR on the neck in Hell) / Ḥashr 59:10 (no COLLAR on our hearts) / 7:43 (Paradise: rancour REMOVED).** ⭐ **The duʿāʾ asks NOW for what Paradise brings.** ⭐⭐ **Toward ANY believer — the antidote to bitterness; the man who slept with no ill-feeling (Aḥmad).** ⭐⭐ ***RAʾŪF* (removes HARM) + *RAḤĪM* (brings GOOD).** ⭐ ***Ghalīl* = burning THIRST.**

## §4 — The Hypocrites' Promise (v.11–17)

**30. أَلَمْ تَرَ إِلَى الَّذِينَ نَافَقُوا يَقُولُونَ لِإِخْوَانِهِمُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ — *a-lam tara ila'lladhīna nāfaqū yaqūlūna li-ikhwānihimu'lladhīna kafarū min ahli'l-kitāb*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *nāfaqū* — ⭐⭐⭐ **were HYPOCRITES** (Form III of *nafaqa*; ⭐ ***nafaq* = a TUNNEL** with a hidden exit; ⭐ **Sūrat al-MUNĀFIQŪN 63**!). *ikhwānihim* — ⭐ **THEIR BROTHERS** (⭐ v.10 *ikhwāninā*!).
- **Here it means:** "**Have you not seen those who were hypocrites, saying to their brothers who disbelieved among the People of the Book…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO KINDS OF "BROTHERHOOD" — back to back.** ⭐ **v.10** — the believers: *rabbana'ghfir lanā wa li-**IKHWĀNINĀ**'lladhīna sabaqūnā bi'l-īmān* — ⭐ **"our BROTHERS in FAITH" — praying for them across time.** ⭐ **v.11** — the hypocrites: *yaqūlūna li-**IKHWĀNIHIMU**'lladhīna kafarū* — ⭐ **"their BROTHERS in DISBELIEF" — making false promises to them.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Brotherhood in faith (prays, forgives, loves) vs brotherhood in hypocrisy (promises, lies, abandons).** ⭐ **The contrast is the point.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***NĀFAQŪ* — the MUNĀFIQŪN (Sūrah 63)** — ⭐ **the tunnel with the secret exit** — ⭐ **and the same ʿAbdullāh ibn Ubayy (classically) — this time promising to stand with Banū an-Naḍīr.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***a-lam tara ilā*** = "**haven't you seen / considered**." ⭐ ***nāfaqū*** = Form III past.
- **Why this exact word:** *ikhwān* — ⭐ **the same word for two opposite brotherhoods, placed side by side.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO BROTHERHOODS: v.10 *IKHWĀNINĀ* (brothers in FAITH — prayed for) / v.11 *IKHWĀNIHIM* (brothers in DISBELIEF — lied to).** ⭐⭐ ***NĀFAQŪ* — MUNĀFIQŪN (63) — the TUNNEL root.** ⭐ ***A-lam tara* = haven't you SEEN?**

**31. لَئِنْ أُخْرِجْتُمْ لَنَخْرُجَنَّ مَعَكُمْ وَلَا نُطِيعُ فِيكُمْ أَحَدًا أَبَدًا وَإِن قُوتِلْتُمْ لَنَنصُرَنَّكُمْ — *la-in ukhrijtum la-nakhrujanna maʿakum wa lā nuṭīʿu fīkum aḥadan abadan wa in qūtiltum la-nanṣurannakum*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *la-in* — ⭐ **"I SWEAR, IF…"** (oath-conditional). *la-nakhrujanna* — ⭐ **we WILL SURELY go out** (oath + heavy *nūn*). *lā nuṭīʿu* — ⭐ **we'll NOT OBEY** (*ṭāʿah*). *aḥadan abadan* — ⭐ **ANYONE, EVER**. *qūtiltum* — ⭐ **you are FOUGHT** (passive). *la-nanṣurannakum* — ⭐ **we WILL SURELY help you** (*naṣr*).
- **Here it means:** "**'If you are expelled, we will surely leave with you, and we will never obey anyone against you; and if you are fought, we will surely help you.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE SWORN PROMISES — each with *lām* + heavy *nūn*:** ⭐ **(1) *la-NAKHRUJANNA maʿakum*** — "**we'll SURELY leave WITH you**" ⭐ **(2) *lā nuṭīʿu fīkum AḤADAN ABADĀ*** — "**we'll NEVER obey ANYONE against you**" ⭐ **(3) *la-NANṢURANNAKUM*** — "**we'll SURELY HELP you**." ⭐⭐⭐ **The over-emphasis again — just like MUNĀFIQŪN 63:1 (*nashhadu innaka LA-rasūlu'llāh* — double emphasis) and 63:8 (*la-in rajaʿnā… la-yukhrijanna*).** ⭐ **The hypocrite's signature: the more he swears, the less he means it** (⭐ **Qalam 68:10** *ḥallāf*!).
  ⭐⭐ ***Lā nuṭīʿu fīkum AḤADAN ABADĀ*** — ⭐ **"NEVER obey ANYONE"** — ⭐ **with *aḥad* + *abadā* — maximum totality** — ⭐ **and they'd break it within days.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***la-in*** = *lām muwaṭṭiʾah* + condition → ⭐ answers ***la-nakhrujanna*** / ***la-nanṣurannakum*** = **oath-answers** (heavy *nūn*). ⭐ ***ukhrijtum / qūtiltum*** = passives.
- **Why this exact word:** **heavy-*nūn* oaths** — ⭐ **promises so emphatic they betray their emptiness.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE SWORN PROMISES (*lām* + HEAVY NŪN): we'll LEAVE with you, NEVER obey anyone against you, surely HELP you.** ⭐⭐ **The hypocrite's signature: over-emphasis = Munāfiqūn 63:1, 63:8; Qalam 68:10 *ḥallāf*.** ⭐ ***Aḥadan abadā* = ANYONE, EVER — broken in days.**

**32. وَاللَّهُ يَشْهَدُ إِنَّهُمْ لَكَاذِبُونَ — *wa'llāhu yashhadu innahum la-kādhibūn*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *yashhadu* — ⭐ **TESTIFIES**. *kādhibūn* — ⭐ **LIARS**.
- **Here it means:** "**But Allah testifies that they are liars.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD with MUNĀFIQŪN 63:1** — ⭐ ***wa'llāhu yashhadu inna'l-munāfiqīna la-kādhibūn*** (63:1) ⭐ ***wa'llāhu yashhadu innahum la-kādhibūn*** (59:11) ⭐⭐⭐ **The SAME divine testimony against the SAME group — in two surahs.** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn: they lied in claiming FAITH. Ḥashr: they lied in promising LOYALTY.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And v.8 (this surah!) — the Emigrants: *humu'Ṣ-ṢĀDIQŪN*** — ⭐ **TRUTHFUL (v.8) vs LIARS (v.11) — in one surah.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***inna*** + ***la-*** = double emphasis.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **Allah's testimony, repeated from Munāfiqūn — the hypocrites' consistent trait.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= MUNĀFIQŪN 63:1 WORD-FOR-WORD — liars in FAITH (63:1) / liars in LOYALTY (59:11).** ⭐⭐⭐ **In ONE surah: Emigrants *ṢĀDIQŪN* (v.8) vs hypocrites *KĀDHIBŪN* (v.11).**

**33. لَئِنْ أُخْرِجُوا لَا يَخْرُجُونَ مَعَهُمْ… وَلَئِن نَّصَرُوهُمْ لَيُوَلُّنَّ الْأَدْبَارَ ثُمَّ لَا يُنصَرُونَ — *la-in ukhrijū lā yakhrujūna maʿahum… wa la-in naṣarūhum la-yuwallunna'l-adbāra thumma lā yunṣarūn*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *yuwallunna* — ⭐ **they WILL SURELY TURN** (*walla* — Form II; ⭐ ***tawallā* = to turn away** — **ʿAbasa 80:1**, **Layl 92:16**). *adbār* — ⭐⭐ the "**BACK / REAR / what comes behind**" family — ***dubur* (pl. *adbār*) = the BACK / rear** (⭐ ***wallā'd-dubur* = to FLEE — "turn the back"**!); ⭐ ***adbara* = to turn one's back** (⭐ **Muddaththir 74:23** — *thumma **adbara** wa'stakbar*!; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:17** — *man **adbara** wa tawallā*; ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:22**); ⭐ ***tadbīr* = MANAGEMENT / planning** (thinking about the "end/back" of a matter!; ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:5** — *fa'l-**mudabbirāti** amrā*!); ⭐ ***dabbara* = to PLAN** (⭐ ***tadbīr manzilī* = HOME ECONOMICS**!). *yunṣarūn* — ⭐ **will be HELPED** (passive).
- **Here it means:** "**If they are expelled, [the hypocrites] will not leave with them; and if they are fought, they will not help them; and even if they did help them, they would surely turn their backs [and flee] — then they would not be helped.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ALLAH ANSWERS EACH PROMISE — one by one:** ⭐ **v.11 "we'll LEAVE with you" → v.12 *lā YAKHRUJŪNA maʿahum* (they WON'T leave with them).** ⭐ **v.11 "we'll HELP you" → v.12 *lā YANṢURŪNAHUM* (they WON'T help them).** ⭐ **And EVEN IF they did help → *la-YUWALLUNNA'L-ADBĀR* — they'd TURN THEIR BACKS and FLEE.** ⭐ **And then → *thumma LĀ YUNṢARŪN* — no one will help THEM.** ⭐⭐⭐ **A PROPHECY fulfilled: Banū an-Naḍīr waited for the hypocrites' help — it never came.** ⭐ **The heavy-*nūn* oath of the hypocrites (*la-nanṣurannakum*) answered by Allah's heavy-*nūn* oath (*la-yuwallunna*).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ADBĀR* — the BACK — and the *ADBARA* thread:** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:23** — al-Walīd: *thumma **adbara** wa'stakbar* (turned his BACK — to the truth). ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:22** — Pharaoh: *thumma **adbara** yasʿā*. ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:17** — the Fire calls *man **adbara** wa tawallā*. ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:12** — the hypocrites: *la-yuwallunna'l-**adbār*** (turn their BACKS — in battle). ⭐⭐ **Turning the back on TRUTH (74, 79) and turning the back in FLIGHT (59) — the same cowardice of the heart.**
  ⭐⭐ ***TADBĪR* = MANAGEMENT / PLANNING** — ⭐ **thinking about the "back end" (consequences) of a matter** — ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:5** — *fa'l-**mudabbirāti** amrā* (the angels who MANAGE affairs). ⭐ **The hypocrites had no *tadbīr* — only *adbār*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***la-in*** ×3 + answers. ⭐⭐ ***la-yuwallunna*** = oath-answer + heavy *nūn*. ⭐ ***thumma lā yunṣarūna*** — ***thumma*** = then (a further consequence), passive.
- **Why this exact word:** *adbār* — ⭐ **the back turned in flight — answering every sworn promise.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ALLAH ANSWERS EACH PROMISE: "we'll LEAVE with you" → WON'T; "we'll HELP" → WON'T; even if → they'd TURN THEIR BACKS; then → no one helps THEM.** ⭐ **A PROPHECY — fulfilled.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ADBĀR* thread: al-Walīd (74:23), Pharaoh (79:22), the Fire calls (70:17) — backs turned on TRUTH; Ḥashr 59:12 — backs turned in FLIGHT.** ⭐⭐ ***TADBĪR* = MANAGEMENT (Nāziʿāt 79:5 *mudabbirāt*) — they had *adbār*, not *tadbīr*.** ⭐ **Their heavy-*nūn* oath vs His.**

**34. لَأَنتُمْ أَشَدُّ رَهْبَةً فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنَ اللَّهِ — *la-antum ashaddu rahbatan fī ṣudūrihim mina'llāh*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *ashadd* — ⭐ **MORE intense** (*shadīd*). *rahbah* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**AWED FEAR / dread with withdrawal**" family — *rahiba* = to fear, to dread; ⭐ ***rahbah* = FEAR / dread**; ⭐⭐ ***rāhib* (pl. *ruhbān*) = a MONK** (one who withdraws in fear of God!); ⭐ ***rahbāniyyah* = MONASTICISM** (57:27!); ⭐⭐ ***irhāb* = TERRORISM** (modern — "causing dread"!; ⭐ originally in 8:60 *turhibūna bihi ʿaduwwa'llāh* — DETERRENCE); ⭐ ***rahīb* = TERRIFYING / tremendous**; ⭐ ***rahbah* vs *raghbah*** — ⭐ **fear vs desire** (*yadʿūnanā **raghaban wa rahabā*** — 21:90 — "they called on Us in HOPE and FEAR"!).
- **Here it means:** "**You [believers] are more fearsome in their chests than Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE HYPOCRITE'S PSYCHOLOGY — they FEAR PEOPLE more than ALLAH.** ⭐ ***La-antum ASHADDU RAHBATAN fī ṣudūrihim MINA'LLĀH*** — ⭐ **"YOU are more DREADED in their hearts than ALLAH"** — ⭐ **a devastating diagnosis: their fear is pointed at the wrong target.** ⭐ **If they feared Allah, they'd keep their promises (v.11) — but they fear the Muslim army more, so they abandon their allies.** ⭐⭐ **MUNĀFIQŪN 63:4** — *yaḥsabūna kulla ṣayḥatin ʿalayhim* — ⭐ **they think every SHOUT is against them** — ⭐ **the same terror of PEOPLE.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***RAHBAH* — and *RĀHIB* (a MONK)** — ⭐ **a monk is "one who FEARS (God) and withdraws"** — ⭐ **the right fear makes a monk; the wrong fear makes a hypocrite.** ⭐ ***Irhāb* = TERRORISM (modern)** — ⭐ **originally "DETERRENCE" (8:60)** — ⭐ **the root's meaning shifted with usage.** ⭐⭐ ***RAHBAH* vs *RAGHBAH* (21:90 — *raghaban wa rahabā*)** — ⭐ **FEAR and HOPE — the two wings of the believer** (⭐ **Qalam 68:32** — *innā ilā rabbinā **rāghibūn***!).
  ⭐⭐ ***Fī ṢUDŪRIHIM*** — ⭐ **in their CHESTS** — ⭐ **v.9: the Anṣār's chests — no envy. v.13: the hypocrites' chests — fear of people.** ⭐ **Two kinds of chest in one surah.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***la-antum*** = *lām al-ibtidāʾ* (emphasis) + *antum*. ⭐ ***ashaddu*** = comparative; ***rahbatan*** = ***tamyīz*** ("more intense **in terms of** dread").
- **Why this exact word:** *rahbah* — ⭐ **misdirected fear — of people instead of Allah — the root of hypocrisy.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They FEAR YOU (people) MORE than ALLAH — fear pointed at the wrong target.** ⭐ **Fear Allah → keep promises; fear people → abandon allies.** ⭐⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4: every SHOUT terrifies them.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RAHBAH* → *RĀHIB* (MONK — fears GOD, withdraws) → *IRHĀB* (TERRORISM — originally DETERRENCE, 8:60).** ⭐⭐ ***RAHBAH* vs *RAGHBAH* (fear vs hope — 21:90; Qalam 68:32).** ⭐⭐ **CHESTS: Anṣār — no ENVY (v.9) / hypocrites — FEAR of people (v.13).**

**35. ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمْ قَوْمٌ لَّا يَفْقَهُونَ — *dhālika bi-annahum qawmun lā yafqahūn*** · v.13

**36. لَا يُقَاتِلُونَكُمْ جَمِيعًا إِلَّا فِي قُرًى مُّحَصَّنَةٍ أَوْ مِن وَرَاءِ جُدُرٍ — *lā yuqātilūnakum jamīʿan illā fī quran muḥaṣṣanatin aw min warāʾi judur*** · v.14

**37. بَأْسُهُم بَيْنَهُمْ شَدِيدٌ ۚ تَحْسَبُهُمْ جَمِيعًا وَقُلُوبُهُمْ شَتَّىٰ — *baʾsuhum baynahum shadīd · taḥsabuhum jamīʿan wa qulūbuhum shattā*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *baʾs* — ⭐⭐ the "**MIGHT / VIOLENCE / harm**" family — ***baʾs* = MIGHT, fighting strength, violence** (⭐ ***lā baʾs!* = "NO PROBLEM / it's fine!"** — everyday!; ⭐ ***biʾsa* = how WRETCHED**; ⭐ ***bāʾis* = MISERABLE**; ⭐ ***baʾsāʾ* = HARDSHIP / misery**). *taḥsabuhum* — ⭐ **you THINK them** (*ḥasiba*). *jamīʿan* — ⭐ **UNITED / all together**. *shattā* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**SCATTERED / dispersed / diverse**" family — *shatta* = to scatter, to be dispersed; ⭐ ***shattā* = SCATTERED, DIVERSE, disunited** (⭐ **Layl 92:4** — *inna saʿyakum **la-shattā***!); ⭐ ***ashtāt* = scattered groups** (⭐ **Zalzalah 99:6** — *yaṣduru'n-nāsu **ashtātā***!); ⭐⭐ ***shatāt* = DIASPORA / dispersion** (⭐ *ash-shatāt* = the **DIASPORA**!); ⭐ ***tashattut* = DISTRACTION / lack of focus** (⭐ *tashattut adh-dhihn* = **DISTRACTION / scattered attention**!); ⭐ ***mutashattit* = SCATTERED / fragmented**.

- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BRILLIANT ILLUSION: *TAḤSABUHUM JAMĪʿAN WA QULŪBUHUM SHATTĀ*** — ⭐ **"you THINK them UNITED — but their HEARTS are SCATTERED."** ⭐ **From the outside: one coalition, one front.** ⭐ **Inside: each with his own agenda, no real loyalty** — ⭐ **as v.11–12 proved: the hypocrites abandoned Banū an-Naḍīr the moment it mattered.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***JAMĪʿAN* (UNITED — appearance) vs *SHATTĀ* (SCATTERED — reality)** — ⭐ **a perfect *ṭibāq*.** ⭐ **The opposite of the believers: AṢ-ṢAFF 61:4 (this set!)** — *ṣaffan ka-annahum **bunyānun marṣūṣ*** — ⭐ **"in RANKS, like a building SOLDERED with LEAD."** ⭐⭐⭐ **Hearts SCATTERED (59:14) vs hearts SOLDERED (61:4).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHATTĀ* — the LAYL thread:** ⭐ **Layl 92:4** — *inna saʿyakum **la-shattā*** — ⭐ **"your EFFORTS are DIVERSE (scattered in different directions)."** ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:6** — *yaṣduru'n-nāsu **ashtātā*** — ⭐ **people emerging in SCATTERED groups.** ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:14** — *qulūbuhum **shattā*** — ⭐ **HEARTS scattered.** ⭐⭐ **Scattered efforts (92), scattered crowds (99), scattered hearts (59).** ⭐ ***Ash-shatāt* = the DIASPORA; *tashattut adh-dhihn* = DISTRACTION** (scattered attention!).
  ⭐⭐ ***BAʾSUHUM BAYNAHUM SHADĪD*** — ⭐ **"their VIOLENCE AMONG THEMSELVES is severe"** — ⭐ **fierce against EACH OTHER, cowardly against the believers (v.14a).** ⭐ ***Lā baʾs!* = "NO PROBLEM!"** (everyday Arabic!).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***taḥsabuhum jamīʿan*** — verb of the heart + two objects. ⭐⭐ ***wa qulūbuhum shattā*** = ***ḥāl* clause** ("**WHILE** their hearts are scattered"). ⭐ ***shattā*** = plural of *shatīt* (fem. form, *faʿlā*).
- **Why this exact word:** *shattā* — ⭐ **the inner fragmentation behind an outward alliance — the opposite of the soldered ranks in Aṣ-Ṣaff.**

**38. ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمْ قَوْمٌ لَّا يَعْقِلُونَ — *dhālika bi-annahum qawmun lā yaʿqilūn*** · v.14

**39. كَمَثَلِ الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ قَرِيبًا ۖ ذَاقُوا وَبَالَ أَمْرِهِمْ وَلَهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ — *ka-mathali'lladhīna min qablihim qarīban dhāqū wabāla amrihim wa lahum ʿadhābun alīm*** · v.15
- **Root feelings:** *mathal* — ⭐ **LIKENESS** (⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:10**; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:5**!). *qarīban* — ⭐ **RECENTLY / close in time**. *dhāqū* — ⭐ **TASTED**. *wabāl* — ⭐ **heavy CONSEQUENCE** (⭐ ***wabl* = a DOWNPOUR**).

- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ka-mathali*** = predicate of an implied subject ("[their likeness is] like…"). ⭐ ***qarīban*** = adverb (time).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **a recent example — making the lesson (*ʿibrah*) of v.2 urgent.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHĀQŪ WABĀLA AMRIHIM* = Ṭalāq 65:9, Taghābun 64:5, Ḥashr 59:15 — THREE surahs, the same DOWNPOUR of consequences, two stages.** ⭐⭐ ***QARĪBAN* = RECENTLY (Badr / Banū Qaynuqāʿ) — "you JUST SAW it, and didn't learn" (v.2 *fa'ʿtabirū*).** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:16 *wabīlā*.**

**40. كَمَثَلِ الشَّيْطَانِ إِذْ قَالَ لِلْإِنسَانِ اكْفُرْ فَلَمَّا كَفَرَ قَالَ إِنِّي بَرِيءٌ مِّنكَ — *ka-mathali'sh-shayṭāni idh qāla li'l-insāni'kfur fa-lammā kafara qāla innī barīʾun mink*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *ash-shayṭān* — ⭐ **SATAN** (*shaṭana* = to be FAR — far from mercy; ⭐ **Nās 114:4** *al-waswās al-khannās*!). *ukfur* — ⭐ **DISBELIEVE!** *barīʾ* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**FREE / CLEAR / innocent — CREATE free of flaw**" family — *bariʾa* = to be FREE (of), CLEAR (of), innocent; ⭐ ***barīʾ* = INNOCENT / free of / DISOWNING** (⭐ *barīʾ* = **INNOCENT** — legal!); ⭐ ***barāʾah* = INNOCENCE / acquittal / disavowal** (⭐ ***Sūrat al-Barāʾah*** = at-Tawbah!; ⭐ *barāʾat ikhtirāʿ* = a **PATENT**!); ⭐ ***tabriʾah* = ACQUITTAL**; ⭐⭐ ***al-BĀRIʾ* = the CREATOR who brings into being free of flaw** (⭐ **v.24 OF THIS SURAH!**); ⭐ ***bariyyah* = CREATION** (⭐ **Bayyinah 98:6–7** — *sharr / khayr al-**bariyyah***!); ⭐ ***burʾ* = RECOVERY from illness** (⭐ ***bariʾa min al-maraḍ* = RECOVERED** — "free of" disease — clinical!).

- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***ʿāqibatahumā*** = **ACCUSATIVE** — ⭐ **the *khabar* of *kāna* FRONTED; the subject is *annahumā fi'n-nāri*** ("the outcome… WAS that they're both in the Fire"). ⚠️ ⭐ ***khālidayni*** = **DUAL** *ḥāl*.
- **Why this exact word:** **the dual** — ⭐ **inciter and victim locked together — the disowning failed.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĀQIBATAHUMĀ… KHĀLIDAYN* = BOTH (DUAL) — Satan and his victim — TOGETHER in the Fire forever.** ⭐ **"I'm FREE of you" (v.16) — didn't work.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿĀqibah* ← HEEL — Ṭalāq 65:9; Ḥashr *ʿiqāb* (v.4, 7).** ⚠️ ***ʿĀqibatahumā* = ACCUSATIVE (*khabar* of *kāna* fronted).**

## §5 — Look at What You've Sent for Tomorrow (v.18–20)

**43. يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَلْتَنظُرْ نَفْسٌ مَّا قَدَّمَتْ لِغَدٍ — *yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanu'ttaqu'llāha wal-tanẓur nafsun mā qaddamat li-ghad*** · v.18
- **Root feelings:** *ittaqū* — ⭐ **SHIELD yourselves** (*taqwā*). *wal-tanẓur* — ⭐ **LET [each soul] LOOK** (*lām al-amr*; *naẓar*; ⭐ ***naẓẓārah* = GLASSES**!). *nafs* — ⭐ **a SOUL**. *qaddamat* — ⭐⭐⭐ **SENT AHEAD** (*qaddama*; ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:40** — *mā **qaddamat** yadāh*!; ⭐ **Fajr 89:24** — *yā laytanī **qaddamtu***!; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:7** — *bimā **qaddamat** aydīhim*!). *ghad* — ⭐⭐⭐ **TOMORROW** (⭐ ***ghadan* = TOMORROW** — everyday!; ⭐ ***ghadāʾ* = LUNCH**; ⭐ **Qalam 68:22** — *ani'**ghdū** ʿalā ḥarthikum* (go EARLY)!).
- **Here it means:** "**O you who believe, fear Allah — and let every soul look at what it has sent ahead for tomorrow…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST QUOTED VERSE FOR SELF-AUDIT — *MUḤĀSABAT AN-NAFS*.** ⭐ ***Wal-tanẓur nafsun mā qaddamat LI-GHAD*** — ⭐ **"let each soul LOOK at what it has SENT AHEAD for TOMORROW."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAD* = TOMORROW** — ⭐ **the Day of Judgment called "TOMORROW"** — ⭐ **as if it's as close as the next day.** ⭐ **(⭐ Maʿārij 70:6–7 — they see it FAR; We see it NEAR.)** ⭐⭐ **ʿUmar's saying: "*Audit yourselves before you are audited (ḥāsibū anfusakum qabla an tuḥāsabū).*"** ⭐ **This verse is that audit's command.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***QADDAMAT* — "SENT AHEAD" — the thread:** ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:40** — *yawma yanẓuru'l-marʾu **mā qaddamat yadāh*** — ⭐ **"the Day a man LOOKS at what his hands SENT AHEAD"** — ⭐ **THE SAME TWO WORDS (*naẓara* + *mā qaddamat*)!** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ: he'll LOOK at it THEN (too late). Ḥashr: LOOK at it NOW (while you can change it).** ⭐ **Fajr 89:24** — *yā laytanī qaddamtu li-ḥayātī* (I WISH I'd sent ahead). ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:7** — *bimā qaddamat aydīhim* (what they sent ahead makes them dread death). ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** — *wa mā tuqaddimū li-anfusikum min khayr*.
  ⭐⭐ ***NAFSUN* — INDEFINITE: "EACH / any soul"** — ⭐ **individual — your OWN audit, not someone else's.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Ghadan* = TOMORROW; *ghadāʾ* = LUNCH (originally the morning meal); Qalam 68:22 *ighdū*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***wal-tanẓur*** = ***lām al-amr*** + jussive ("**LET** [it] look"). ⭐ ***nafsun*** = indefinite (each one). ⭐ ***li-ghadin*** = "**for** tomorrow."
- **Why this exact word:** *ghad* — ⭐ **Judgment Day as "tomorrow" — near enough to plan for now.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAL-TANẒUR NAFSUN MĀ QADDAMAT LI-GHAD* = LET EACH SOUL LOOK at what it SENT AHEAD for TOMORROW — the SELF-AUDIT verse.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAD* = TOMORROW — Judgment Day as close as the next day.** ⭐⭐⭐ **NABAʾ 78:40: he'll LOOK at *mā qaddamat* THEN (too late) / ḤASHR 59:18: LOOK at it NOW.** ⭐⭐ **ʿUmar: "Audit yourselves before you're audited."** ⭐⭐ ***Qaddamat* thread: 78:40, 89:24, 62:7, 73:20.** ⭐ ***Ghadan* = TOMORROW; *ghadāʾ* = LUNCH.**

**44. وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ خَبِيرٌ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ — *wa'ttaqu'llāh · inna'llāha khabīrun bimā taʿmalūn*** · v.18
- **Root feelings:** *ittaqū* — ⭐ **SHIELD yourselves** (*taqwā* — 2nd time in one verse!). *khabīr* — ⭐ **INSIDE-aware** (⭐ ***mukhtabar* = LABORATORY**; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:11**!).
- **Here it means:** "**And fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is fully aware of what you do.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ITTAQŪ'LLĀH* TWICE in ONE verse** — ⭐ classical (ar-Rāzī and others): **the 1st *taqwā* — before the deed (do what's right, send good ahead); the 2nd *taqwā* — about the deed (sincerity, and repentance for what you've done wrong).** ⭐ **Or: the 1st for what you'll DO; the 2nd for what you've DONE.** ⭐ **The audit (*tanẓur*) sandwiched between two *taqwā*s.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KHABĪRUN BIMĀ TAʿMALŪN* — MUNĀFIQŪN 63:11's last words** — ⭐ **He knows the INSIDE of what you sent ahead — the intention behind every deed.** ⭐ ***Mukhtabar* = LABORATORY** — **He tests the inside of your samples.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa'ttaqū*** — repeated imperative (*takrār* — emphasis, or two distinct objects).
- **Why this exact word:** **the repeated *taqwā*** — ⭐ **before and after the deed, framing the self-audit.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ITTAQŪ'LLĀH* ×2 in ONE verse — before the deed (what you'll DO) / about the deed (what you've DONE) — the audit sandwiched between.** ⭐⭐ ***Khabīrun bimā taʿmalūn* = Munāfiqūn 63:11 — He knows the INSIDE of what you sent ahead.** ⭐ ***Mukhtabar* = LABORATORY.**

**45. وَلَا تَكُونُوا كَالَّذِينَ نَسُوا اللَّهَ فَأَنسَاهُمْ أَنفُسَهُمْ — *wa lā takūnū ka'lladhīna nasu'llāha fa-ansāhum anfusahum*** · v.19
- **Root feelings:** *nasū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**FORGETTING / neglecting**" family — *nasiya* = to forget, to neglect; ⭐ ***nisyān* = FORGETFULNESS** (⭐ clinical: ***nisyān* = AMNESIA / memory loss**!); ⭐ ***ansā* (Form IV) = to MAKE someone forget**; ⭐ **Aʿlā 87:6** — *sa-nuqriʾuka **fa-lā tansā***! (⭐ "We will make you recite — so you will NOT FORGET"); ⭐⭐ classically, ***insān* (HUMAN) is linked by many to *nisyān* (FORGETTING)** — ⭐ Ibn ʿAbbās: "*he was called *insān* because he was given a covenant and FORGOT (*nasiya*)*" (20:115 — *wa la-qad ʿahidnā ilā Ādama min qablu **fa-nasiya***!). *anfusahum* — ⭐ **THEMSELVES**.
- **Here it means:** "**And do not be like those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST PROFOUND DIAGNOSIS IN THE SURAH — forget ALLAH → forget YOURSELF.** ⭐ ***Nasu'llāha FA-ANSĀHUM ANFUSAHUM*** — ⭐ **"they FORGOT Allah — so He made them FORGET THEMSELVES."** ⭐⭐⭐ **Why does forgetting Allah make you forget yourself?** ⭐ **Because your TRUE self — your purpose, your origin, your destination — only makes sense in relation to Him.** ⭐ **Forget Him, and you forget what you're FOR — you become busy with everything except your own soul's needs.** ⭐ **You chase the body's wants and starve the soul — "self-forgetting."** ⭐⭐ **The measure-for-measure: they NEGLECTED Allah → they were made to NEGLECT their own good.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***INSĀN* (HUMAN) ← *NISYĀN* (FORGETTING)** — ⭐ **Ibn ʿAbbās: man is "*insān*" because he FORGETS** — ⭐ **Ādam: *fa-nasiya* (20:115) — the first forgetting.** ⭐ **We are, by name, the forgetting creature** — ⭐ **and the cure is *DHIKR* (REMEMBRANCE) — the juzʾ's spine!** ⭐⭐ **AʿLĀ 87:6** — *sa-nuqriʾuka **fa-lā tansā*** — ⭐ **"We'll make you RECITE — so you WON'T FORGET"** — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ protected from forgetting the revelation.** ⭐ ***Nisyān* = AMNESIA (clinical).**
  ⭐⭐ ***FA-ANSĀHUM*** — ⭐ **Form IV: He CAUSED them to forget** — ⭐ **a consequence, not a first cause (they forgot first).** ⭐ **Like Munāfiqūn 63:3 — *fa-ṭubiʿa* (sealed AFTER they chose disbelief).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***nasū*** = past, weak-final. ⭐⭐ ***fa-ansāhum anfusahum*** = **Form IV + TWO objects** ("**made THEM forget THEMSELVES**"). ⭐ ***fa*** = consequence.
- **Why this exact word:** *nisyān* — ⭐ **the forgetting creature, warned that forgetting God means losing himself.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***NASU'LLĀHA FA-ANSĀHUM ANFUSAHUM* = forgot ALLAH → made to FORGET THEMSELVES.** ⭐ **Your true self (purpose, origin, end) only makes sense in relation to Him.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INSĀN* ← *NISYĀN* (Ibn ʿAbbās): man is the FORGETTING creature — Ādam *fa-nasiya* (20:115) — cure: *DHIKR*.** ⭐⭐ **Aʿlā 87:6 *fa-lā tansā*.** ⭐ ***Nisyān* = AMNESIA (clinical).** ⭐ **Consequence (*fa*) — like Munāfiqūn 63:3 *fa-ṭubiʿa*.**

**46. أُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْفَاسِقُونَ — *ulāʾika humu'l-fāsiqūn*** · v.19
- **Root feeling:** *fāsiqūn* — ⭐⭐ **DEFIANT SINNERS** (a DATE BURSTING its skin; ⭐ v.5; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:6**).
- **Here it means:** "**Those are the defiantly disobedient.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***HUMU'L-FĀSIQŪN*** — ⭐ **those who forgot Allah = THE *fāsiqūn*** — ⭐ **they burst out of the "skin" of their purpose.** ⭐ **Ḥashr's *fāsiq* thread: v.5 (*li-yukhziya'l-fāsiqīn*), v.19 (*humu'l-fāsiqūn*).** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:6** — *lā yahdi'l-qawma'l-fāsiqīn*.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***humu'l-fāsiqūna*** = restriction ("THEY are THE defiant").
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **self-forgetting named as defiance — bursting out of one's own nature.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***HUMU'L-FĀSIQŪN* — forgetting Allah = BURSTING out of your purpose (date from its skin).** ⭐ **Ḥashr v.5, v.19; Munāfiqūn 63:6.**

**47. لَا يَسْتَوِي أَصْحَابُ النَّارِ وَأَصْحَابُ الْجَنَّةِ ۚ أَصْحَابُ الْجَنَّةِ هُمُ الْفَائِزُونَ — *lā yastawī aṣḥābu'n-nāri wa aṣḥābu'l-jannah · aṣḥābu'l-jannati humu'l-fāʾizūn*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *yastawī* — ⭐⭐⭐ **are EQUAL / level** (Form VIII of ***s-w-y*** — your LONGEST thread!; ⭐ ***istiwāʾ* = the EQUATOR**; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:6** — *sawāʾun ʿalayhim*; ⭐ **Mulk 67:22** — *sawiyyan*; ⭐ **Infiṭār 82:7** — *fa-sawwāka*). *fāʾizūn* — ⭐⭐ **the WINNERS** (*fawz*; ⭐ **Taghābun 64:9** — *al-fawz al-ʿaẓīm*!; ⭐ ***mafāzah* = DESERT** (place of winning)).
- **Here it means:** "**Not equal are the companions of the Fire and the companions of Paradise. The companions of Paradise — they are the winners.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ YASTAWĪ* — NOT EQUAL — the *S-W-Y* thread's verdict form.** ⭐ ***Lā yastawī*** — ⭐ **"they are NOT on the same LEVEL"** — ⭐ **the two outcomes of the self-audit (v.18) and self-forgetting (v.19).** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *S-W-Y* THREAD — balance, equality, levelness:** ⭐ **Infiṭār 82:7** — *fa-**sawwāka*** (He BALANCED you). ⭐ **Mulk 67:22** — *yamshī **sawiyyan*** (walking UPRIGHT/level). ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:6** — ***sawāʾun*** ʿalayhim (it's all the SAME to them). ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:20** — *lā **yastawī*** (NOT EQUAL). ⭐⭐ **He made you balanced; walk level; but the end-points are NOT level.** ⭐ ***Istiwāʾ* = EQUATOR.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***HUMU'L-FĀʾIZŪN* — THE WINNERS** — ⭐ **TAGHĀBUN 64:9** — *dhālika'l-**fawzu'l-ʿaẓīm*** (the GREAT WIN) ⭐ **→ Ḥashr 59:20 — ⭐ the WINNERS named.** ⭐ ***Fawz* = a WIN (sports!); *mafāzah* = DESERT.** ⭐⭐ **Only ONE side is called *fāʾizūn* — the other isn't named, only implied.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***lā yastawī*** = **Form VIII** (*iftaʿala*) negated — "**are not on a par**." ⭐ ***humu'l-fāʾizūna*** = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** *yastawī* — ⭐ **the levelling root, here denying any level between the two destinies.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ YASTAWĪ* = NOT EQUAL — Fire and Paradise are NOT on the same level.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***S-W-Y*: BALANCED (82:7), UPRIGHT (67:22), "all the SAME" (63:6), NOT EQUAL (59:20).** ⭐⭐ ***HUMU'L-FĀʾIZŪN* = THE WINNERS — Taghābun 64:9 *al-fawz al-ʿaẓīm*.** ⭐ ***Istiwāʾ* = EQUATOR; *fawz* = a WIN.**

## §6 — The Mountain and the Names (v.21–24)

**48. لَوْ أَنزَلْنَا هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنَ عَلَىٰ جَبَلٍ لَّرَأَيْتَهُ خَاشِعًا مُّتَصَدِّعًا مِّنْ خَشْيَةِ اللَّهِ — *law anzalnā hādha'l-qurʾāna ʿalā jabalin la-raʾaytahu khāshiʿan mutaṣaddiʿan min khashyati'llāh*** · v.21
- **Root feelings:** *law* — ⭐ **IF (hypothetical)**. *anzalnā* — ⭐ **We SENT DOWN**. *jabal* — ⭐ **a MOUNTAIN** (⭐ the mountain-fates thread!). *khāshiʿ* — ⭐⭐ **HUMBLED, bowed** (⭐ ***khushūʿ*** — in PRAYER!; ⭐ **Qalam 68:43, Maʿārij 70:44** — *khāshiʿatan abṣāruhum*!). *mutaṣaddiʿ* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**CRACKING / SPLITTING apart**" family — *ṣadaʿa* = **to SPLIT, crack open**; ⭐ ***taṣaddaʿa* (Form V) = to CRACK APART, split into fissures**; ⭐⭐ ***ṣudāʿ* = a HEADACHE** (⭐ clinical!: the head feels like it's CRACKING!; ⭐ *ṣudāʿ niṣfī* = **MIGRAINE**!); ⭐ ***ṣadʿ* = a CRACK / fissure** (⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:12** — *wa'l-arḍi **dhāti'ṣ-ṣadʿ*** — "the earth that SPLITS [for plants]"!); ⭐ ***taṣdīʿ* = a CRACK** (in a wall — *taṣaddaʿ al-jidār* = the wall CRACKED!). *khashyah* — ⭐⭐ **AWE born of KNOWING** (⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:19, Aʿlā 87:10, Bayyinah 98:8, Mulk 67:12**).
- **Here it means:** "**If We had sent down this Qurʾān upon a mountain, you would have seen it humbled and splitting apart from the awe of Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOUNTAIN THAT WOULD CRACK — and the most humbling verse for the human heart.** ⭐ **A MOUNTAIN — the most solid thing you know — if it RECEIVED the Qurʾān (with understanding), it would BOW (*khāshiʿ*) and CRACK APART (*mutaṣaddiʿ*) from awe.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And YOUR heart receives it — and stays unmoved?** ⭐ **The verse doesn't say it outright — it lets you feel it.** ⭐ ***Wa tilka'l-amthālu naḍribuhā li'n-nāsi laʿallahum yatafakkarūn*** (next) — ⭐ **"these PARABLES We strike — that they might THINK."**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUTAṢADDIʿ* — the *ṢADʿ* root — and its CLINICAL face:** ⭐ ***ṢUDĀʿ* = a HEADACHE** (the head "cracking"!); ⭐ ***ṣudāʿ niṣfī* = MIGRAINE.** ⭐ **ṬĀRIQ 86:12** — *wa'l-arḍi **dhāti'ṣ-ṣadʿ*** — ⭐ **"the earth that CRACKS OPEN"** (for plants to sprout). ⭐⭐ **The earth CRACKS for life (Ṭāriq); the mountain would CRACK from awe (Ḥashr); the head "cracks" in pain (*ṣudāʿ*).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***KHĀSHIʿ* — the *KHUSHŪʿ* of PRAYER** — ⭐ **Qalam 68:43, Maʿārij 70:44** — ⭐ **the deniers' eyes FORCED into *khushūʿ* on the Day.** ⭐ **Al-Muʾminūn 23:2** — believers ***khāshiʿūn*** in prayer. ⭐ **Here: a MOUNTAIN would be *khāshiʿ*.** ⭐⭐ **The mountain-fates thread — now with a HYPOTHETICAL:** ⭐ **mirage (78:20), pounded (89:21), carded wool (101:5), moving (81:3), crushed once (69:14), dyed wool (70:9), sliding sand (73:14) — and here: CRACKED BY AWE (59:21).** ⭐ **(⭐ and 7:143 — the mountain that CRUMBLED when Allah manifested to it — *jaʿalahu dakkā*!)**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***law*** + past → ***la-raʾaytahu*** (*lām* in the answer) = **hypothetical**. ⭐ ***khāshiʿan, mutaṣaddiʿan*** = two ***ḥāl***s. ⭐ ***mutaṣaddiʿ*** = **Form V participle** (cracking — gradually, progressively).
- **Why this exact word:** *taṣaddaʿa* — ⭐ **the image of solid rock cracking — to shame the unmoved heart.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A MOUNTAIN would BOW (*khāshiʿ*) and CRACK APART (*mutaṣaddiʿ*) from awe of the Qurʾān — and YOUR heart?** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṢADʿ*: ṢUDĀʿ = HEADACHE (*ṣudāʿ niṣfī* = MIGRAINE); Ṭāriq 86:12 earth CRACKS for plants; Ḥashr 59:21 mountain CRACKS from awe.** ⭐⭐ ***KHĀSHIʿ* — the *khushūʿ* of prayer; Qalam 68:43, Maʿārij 70:44.** ⭐⭐ **Mountain-fates + 7:143 (crumbled at the manifestation).** ⭐ ***Khashyah* thread.**

**49. وَتِلْكَ الْأَمْثَالُ نَضْرِبُهَا لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ — *wa tilka'l-amthālu naḍribuhā li'n-nāsi laʿallahum yatafakkarūn*** · v.21
- **Root feelings:** *amthāl* — ⭐ **PARABLES** (*mathal*; ⭐ v.15, 16!; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:10**). *naḍribuhā* — ⭐ **We STRIKE / present them** (*ḍaraba mathalan*; ⭐ ***ḍarībah* = TAX**!). *yatafakkarūn* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**THINKING / reflecting**" family — *fakara* = to think; ⭐ ***fikr* = THOUGHT**; ⭐ ***tafakkur* = REFLECTION / contemplation**; ⭐ ***fikrah* = an IDEA**; ⭐ ***mufakkir* = a THINKER**; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:18** — *innahu **fakkara** wa qaddar*!.
- **Here it means:** "**And these examples We present to people, that perhaps they will reflect.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YATAFAKKARŪN* — REFLECT — and the MUDDATHTHIR contrast.** ⭐ **MUDDATHTHIR 74:18** — *innahu **fakkara** wa qaddar* — ⭐ **al-Walīd THOUGHT (Form II — *fakkara*) — and used his thinking to manufacture a SLANDER ("it's magic!").** ⭐ **ḤASHR 59:21** — *laʿallahum **yatafakkarūn*** — ⭐ **"that they might REFLECT" (Form V — *tafakkara*) — thinking that leads to HUMILITY (like the mountain).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same root: thinking can produce a SLANDER (74:18) or a CRACKED, humbled heart (59:21).** ⭐ **Not all thinking is *tafakkur* — some is just scheming.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Tilka'l-AMTHĀL*** — ⭐ **"THESE parables"** — ⭐ **Ḥashr is a surah of parables: v.15 (the recent nations), v.16 (Satan), v.21 (the mountain).** ⭐ **Three *amthāl* in one surah.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***naḍribuhā*** = present (ongoing — He keeps presenting). ⭐ ***yatafakkarūna*** = **Form V** (*tafaʿʿala* — sustained, deliberate reflection).
- **Why this exact word:** *tafakkur* — ⭐ **reflection that humbles, set against the scheming *fakkara* of al-Walīd.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LAʿALLAHUM YATAFAKKARŪN* = that they might REFLECT (Form V).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir 74:18 *FAKKARA* (al-Walīd's thinking → a SLANDER) vs Ḥashr 59:21 *YATAFAKKARŪN* (reflection → HUMILITY, like the mountain).** ⭐ **Not all thinking is *tafakkur*.** ⭐⭐ **THREE parables in Ḥashr: recent nations (v.15), Satan (v.16), the mountain (v.21).** ⭐ ***Mufakkir* = THINKER; *fikrah* = IDEA.**

**50. هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ ۖ هُوَ الرَّحْمَٰنُ الرَّحِيمُ — *huwa'llāhu'lladhī lā ilāha illā huw · ʿālimu'l-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādah · huwa'r-raḥmānu'r-raḥīm*** · v.22
- **Root feelings:** *lā ilāha illā huwa* — ⭐⭐⭐ **NO GOD BUT HIM**. *ʿālim al-ghayb wa'sh-shahādah* — ⭐⭐ **KNOWER of the UNSEEN and the WITNESSED** (⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:8**, **Taghābun 64:18**!). *ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**WOMB / mercy**" family — ***raḥim* = the WOMB** (⭐ clinical!); ⭐ ***raḥmah* = MERCY** (the womb's protective love!); ⭐ ***ar-Raḥmān* = the ALL-MERCIFUL** (*faʿlān* — overflowing, encompassing mercy — for ALL); ⭐ ***ar-Raḥīm* = the ESPECIALLY merciful** (*faʿīl* — constant, especially for believers).
- **Here it means:** "**He is Allah — there is no god but Him — Knower of the unseen and the witnessed. He is the Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE NAMES BEGIN — three verses, each starting *HUWA'LLĀHU* ("HE is Allah").** ⭐ **v.22: *HUWA'LLĀHU*… (knowledge + mercy)** ⭐ **v.23: *HUWA'LLĀHU*… (sovereignty + power)** ⭐ **v.24: *HUWA'LLĀHU*… (creation).** ⭐⭐ **Classical: after the mountain cracks (v.21), the Qurʾān shows you WHY — WHO sent it down.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĀLIM AL-GHAYB WA'SH-SHAHĀDAH* — the TRIO'S closing Name, here OPENING the list!** ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:8** — *ilā ʿālimi'l-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādah* ⭐ **Taghābun 64:18** — *ʿālimu'l-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādah* ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:22** — *ʿālimu'l-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādah* ⭐ **THREE surahs — the Knower of the hidden and the visible.** ⭐ **In Ḥashr: He saw the HIDDEN plots of the hypocrites (v.11) and the VISIBLE fortresses (v.2).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AR-RAḤMĀN AR-RAḤĪM* ← *RAḤIM* (the WOMB)** — ⭐ **mercy named after the WOMB — the first place you were protected, fed, held, without asking.** ⭐ ***Ar-Raḥmān* (*faʿlān*) = OVERFLOWING mercy (like *ghaḍbān* = boiling with anger, *ʿaṭshān* = burning with thirst) — for ALL creation.** ⭐ ***Ar-Raḥīm* (*faʿīl*) = ENDURING mercy — especially for the believers.** ⭐ **The *basmalah*'s two Names — at the head of the list.**
  ⭐ Clinical hook: ⭐ ***raḥim* = UTERUS / WOMB.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***huwa'llāhu'lladhī lā ilāha illā huwa*** — ⭐ ***huwa*** = subject; ***Allāhu*** = predicate; relative clause = the *tawḥīd* formula. ⭐ **Names in apposition** (*ʿālimu, ar-raḥmānu, ar-raḥīmu*).
- **Why this exact word:** **knowledge + mercy first** — ⭐ **the Names begin with what you most need: He knows, and He is merciful.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE verses, each opening *HUWA'LLĀHU*: v.22 KNOWLEDGE + MERCY / v.23 SOVEREIGNTY + POWER / v.24 CREATION.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĀLIM AL-GHAYB WA'SH-SHAHĀDAH* ×3 surahs: Jumuʿah 62:8, Taghābun 64:18, Ḥashr 59:22.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AR-RAḤMĀN AR-RAḤĪM* ← *RAḤIM* (WOMB) — mercy named after the first place you were protected.** ⭐⭐ ***Raḥmān* (*faʿlān* — OVERFLOWING, for all) / *Raḥīm* (*faʿīl* — ENDURING, for believers).** ⭐ ***Raḥim* = UTERUS.**

**51. هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوسُ السَّلَامُ الْمُؤْمِنُ الْمُهَيْمِنُ — *huwa'llāhu'lladhī lā ilāha illā huwa'l-maliku'l-quddūsu's-salāmu'l-muʾminu'l-muhaymin*** · v.23
- **Root feelings:** ⭐ ***al-Malik*** — **the KING** (⭐ **Nās 114:2, Jumuʿah 62:1**). ⭐ ***al-Quddūs*** — **the HOLY** (⭐ ***al-Quds* = JERUSALEM**; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:1**!). ⭐⭐ ***as-Salām*** — the "**PEACE / SAFETY / soundness**" family — ***salām* = PEACE** (⭐ ***as-salāmu ʿalaykum***!; ⭐ ***salāmah* = SAFETY**; ⭐ ***islām* = SUBMISSION**; ⭐ ***sālim* = SOUND** — **Qalam 68:43** *wa hum sālimūn*; ⭐ **Qadr 97:5** — ***salāmun** hiya ḥattā maṭlaʿi'l-fajr*!) — ⭐ **the One FREE of every defect, and the SOURCE of peace.** ⭐⭐ ***al-Muʾmin*** — the "**SAFETY / SECURITY / faith**" family — ***amn* = SAFETY** (⭐ ***al-Muʾmin* = the GIVER of SAFETY / the One who CONFIRMS (His messengers' truth)**; ⭐ **Quraysh 106:4** — *wa **āmanahum** min khawf*!; ⭐ ***amān* = SECURITY**; ⭐ ***taʾmīn* = INSURANCE**!). ⭐⭐⭐ ***al-Muhaymin*** — the "**WATCHFUL GUARDIAN / overseer**" — ⭐ **the One who WATCHES OVER, preserves, and witnesses all** (⭐ **Māʾidah 5:48** — the Qurʾān is ***muhayminan ʿalayh*** — a GUARDIAN / standard over previous scriptures!).
- **Here it means:** "**He is Allah — there is no god but Him — the King, the Holy, the Perfect Peace, the Giver of Safety, the Watchful Guardian…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SOVEREIGNTY NAMES — and EACH answers something in the surah:** ⭐ ***AL-MALIK* (the KING)** — ⭐ **the fortresses (v.2) belonged to Him; the *fayʾ* (v.6) was His to distribute.** ⭐ ***AL-QUDDŪS* (the HOLY)** — ⭐ **utterly pure — unlike the hypocrites' tainted promises.** ⭐ ***AS-SALĀM* (PEACE / SOUNDNESS)** — ⭐ **free of every flaw — and the Source of the safety the believers felt while besieging forts.** ⭐ ***AL-MUʾMIN* (GIVER of SAFETY)** — ⭐ **the besieged tribe sought safety in walls; the believers found it in Him.** ⭐ ***AL-MUHAYMIN* (the WATCHFUL GUARDIAN)** — ⭐ **watching over the hidden plots (v.11) — the hypocrites thought no one saw.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MALIK AL-QUDDŪS* — JUMUʿAH 62:1!** ⭐ *al-Maliki'l-Quddūsi'l-ʿAzīzi'l-Ḥakīm* — ⭐ **the same opening pair.** ⭐ ***Al-Quds* = JERUSALEM.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AS-SALĀM* — QADR 97:5** — ***salāmun** hiya* (the Night of Decree is PEACE). ⭐ ***As-salāmu ʿalaykum*** — **you invoke His NAME every time you greet.** ⭐ ***Sālim* = SOUND (Qalam 68:43); *salāmah* = SAFETY.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MUʾMIN* — the same word as "believer"!** ⭐ **For Allah: the GIVER of *amn* (safety) / the One who CONFIRMS truth; for us: the one who TRUSTS / has found safety.** ⭐ **Quraysh 106:4** — *āmanahum min khawf*. ⭐ ***Taʾmīn* = INSURANCE** — ⭐ **the ultimate insurer.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MUHAYMIN* — a RARE Name** — ⭐ **Māʾidah 5:48: the Qurʾān is *muhaymin* (GUARDIAN/STANDARD) over earlier books** — ⭐ **Allah is *al-Muhaymin* over all creation.** ⭐ **Watching, preserving, witnessing.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **a long list of definite Names in apposition** — ⭐ **no *wāw* between them** (⭐ like Taḥrīm 66:5's six qualities — ALL belonging to ONE subject!).
- **Why this exact word:** **the sovereignty Names** — ⭐ **each one answering a failure in the surah's story.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **KING (forts & *fayʾ* are His), HOLY (vs tainted promises), PEACE (free of flaw), GIVER OF SAFETY (vs walls), WATCHFUL GUARDIAN (sees hidden plots).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MALIK AL-QUDDŪS* = JUMUʿAH 62:1; *al-Quds* = JERUSALEM.** ⭐⭐ ***AS-SALĀM* — Qadr 97:5; *as-salāmu ʿalaykum* invokes His Name.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MUʾMIN* — same word as "believer": He GIVES safety; we FIND it — *taʾmīn* = INSURANCE; Quraysh 106:4.** ⭐⭐ ***AL-MUHAYMIN* — Māʾidah 5:48 (the Qurʾān as GUARDIAN over earlier books).** ⚠️ **No *wāw* — all ONE subject (like Taḥrīm 66:5).**

**52. الْعَزِيزُ الْجَبَّارُ الْمُتَكَبِّرُ ۚ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ — *al-ʿazīzu'l-jabbāru'l-mutakabbir · subḥāna'llāhi ʿammā yushrikūn*** · v.23
- **Root feelings:** ⭐ ***al-ʿAzīz*** — **the ALMIGHTY / precious** (⭐ ***ʿizzah*** — **Munāfiqūn 63:8**!). ⭐⭐⭐ ***al-Jabbār*** — the "**SETTING A BROKEN BONE / COMPELLING**" family — *jabara* = **to SET a broken BONE, to MEND, to restore** — ⭐ and **to COMPEL**; ⭐⭐ ***jabīrah* = a SPLINT / CAST** (⭐ clinical!: *jabīrah* = **a SPLINT**; ⭐ fiqh: *al-masḥ ʿala'l-jabīrah* = **wiping over a CAST in wuḍūʾ**!); ⭐ ***mujabbir* = a BONE-SETTER** (⭐ traditional orthopaedist!); ⭐⭐⭐ ***al-jabr* = ALGEBRA** (⭐ al-Khwārizmī's *Kitāb al-Jabr wa'l-Muqābalah* — "RESTORING" broken parts of an equation!); ⭐ ***jabr al-khāṭir* = CONSOLING someone / "mending a broken heart"**!; ⭐ ***ijbārī* = COMPULSORY** (⭐ *at-taʿlīm al-ijbārī* = **COMPULSORY education**!); ⭐ ***jabbār* = a TYRANT** (for humans — blameworthy!). ⭐⭐⭐ ***al-Mutakabbir*** — **Form V of *kabura*** = **the SUPREMELY GREAT, the One rightly possessing all greatness** (⭐ ***Allāhu akbar***!; ⭐ for humans: ***takabbur* = ARROGANCE** — blameworthy!; ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:7** *istakbarū*; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:5** *mustakbirūn*; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:3** — *wa rabbaka **fa-kabbir***!). *subḥāna'llāh* — ⭐ **GLORY be to Allah** (*tasbīḥ*). *yushrikūn* — ⭐ **they ASSOCIATE (partners)** (*shirk*; ⭐ ***sharikah* = a COMPANY**).
- **Here it means:** "**…the Almighty, the Compeller / Restorer, the Supremely Great. Glory be to Allah above what they associate with Him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE POWER-NAMES — and two of them are SINS when used for humans!** ⭐ ***AL-JABBĀR*** — ⭐ **for a HUMAN: a TYRANT (*jabbār* = blameworthy — ⭐ *wa idhā baṭashtum baṭashtum jabbārīn*, 26:130).** ⭐ **For ALLAH: the One who RESTORES what's broken + COMPELS what He wills.** ⭐ ***AL-MUTAKABBIR*** — ⭐ **for a HUMAN: ARROGANT (*takabbur* — Iblīs, Nūḥ's people, the hypocrites!).** ⭐ **For ALLAH: the One to whom greatness TRULY belongs.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The ḥadīth qudsī: "*Greatness (al-kibriyāʾ) is My cloak and Majesty My garment — whoever competes with Me in either, I will cast him into the Fire*" (Muslim, Abū Dāwūd).** ⭐ **Some attributes are PRAISE for Allah and POISON for us** — ⭐ **because greatness is His by right; for us, it's theft.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-JABBĀR* — the BONE-SETTER root!** ⭐ ***Jabara* = to SET a broken bone** — ⭐ ***jabīrah* = a SPLINT** (clinical!) — ⭐ ***mujabbir* = a traditional BONE-SETTER** — ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-JABR* = ALGEBRA** (al-Khwārizmī — "RESTORATION" of equation terms!). ⭐ **So *al-Jabbār* = the ONE WHO MENDS the broken — the broken heart (*jabr al-khāṭir* = consoling!), the poor, the weak.** ⭐ **The duʿāʾ between the two prostrations: *Allāhumma'ghfir lī wa'rḥamnī wa'JBURNĪ…*** — ⭐ **"O Allah, forgive me, have mercy on me, and MEND me / SET my broken state"** (Abū Dāwūd, Tirmidhī)! ⭐ **You ask *al-Jabbār* to set your bones every prayer.** ⭐ ***Ijbārī* = COMPULSORY.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MUTAKABBIR* — the *K-B-R* thread's resolution:** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:3** — *wa rabbaka **fa-kabbir*** (MAGNIFY your Lord) — ⭐ **74:23** — *wa'**stakbar*** (al-Walīd made HIMSELF great). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:7** — *istakbarū'stikbārā*. ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:5** — *mustakbirūn*. ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:23** — ***AL-MUTAKABBIR*** — ⭐ **the ONLY one who rightly claims greatness.** ⭐⭐ ***ALLĀHU AKBAR*** — ⭐ **every *takbīr* you say affirms this Name.**
  ⭐⭐ ***SUBḤĀNA'LLĀHI ʿAMMĀ YUSHRIKŪN*** — ⭐ **after the power-Names — "GLORY be to Allah above their associating partners"** — ⭐ **no idol, no tyrant, no "*jabbār*" shares these.** ⭐ ***Sharikah* = a COMPANY** (partners!).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Names in apposition** (continuing v.23a). ⭐ ***al-jabbāru*** = *faʿʿāl* intensive. ⭐ ***al-mutakabbiru*** = **Form V participle**. ⭐ ***subḥāna*** = *maf'ūl muṭlaq* (implied verb). ⭐ ***ʿammā*** = *ʿan* + *mā*.
- **Why this exact word:** *al-Jabbār / al-Mutakabbir* — ⭐ **Names that are praise for Him and sins for us — teaching humility through contrast.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-JABBĀR* & *AL-MUTAKABBIR* — PRAISE for Allah, SINS for humans (TYRANT, ARROGANT).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥadīth qudsī: "Greatness is My CLOAK — whoever competes with Me in it, I cast into the Fire" (Muslim).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***JABARA* = to SET a broken BONE → *JABĪRAH* = SPLINT; *MUJABBIR* = BONE-SETTER; *AL-JABR* = ALGEBRA (al-Khwārizmī); *jabr al-khāṭir* = CONSOLING; *ijbārī* = COMPULSORY.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Allāhumma… wa'JBURNĪ* — between the prostrations: "MEND me."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***K-B-R*: *fa-kabbir* (74:3), *istakbara* (74:23, 71:7), *mustakbirūn* (63:5) → *AL-MUTAKABBIR* (59:23) — *ALLĀHU AKBAR*.** ⭐ ***Sharikah* = COMPANY.**

**53. هُوَ اللَّهُ الْخَالِقُ الْبَارِئُ الْمُصَوِّرُ — *huwa'llāhu'l-khāliqu'l-bāriʾu'l-muṣawwir*** · v.24
- **Root feelings:** ⭐⭐ ***al-Khāliq*** — the "**MEASURING / PLANNING → CREATING**" family — *khalaqa* = originally **to MEASURE / estimate (leather before cutting!)** → **to CREATE**; ⭐ ***khalq* = CREATION**; ⭐ ***khuluq* = CHARACTER** (⭐ **Qalam 68:4** *khuluqun ʿaẓīm*!; ⭐ ***akhlāq* = ETHICS**). ⭐⭐⭐ ***al-Bāriʾ*** — the "**BRINGING INTO BEING free of flaw / DISTINCT**" family — *baraʾa* = **to CREATE, bring into existence — separate, distinct, free of flaw**; ⭐ ***bariyyah* = CREATION / created beings** (⭐ **Bayyinah 98:6–7** — *sharru / khayru'l-**bariyyah***!); ⭐ ***barīʾ* = FREE / innocent** (⭐ **v.16 OF THIS SURAH** — Satan: *innī **barīʾun** minka*!); ⭐ ***burʾ* = RECOVERY** (clinical!). ⭐⭐⭐ ***al-Muṣawwir*** — the "**FORM / IMAGE / PHOTO**" family — ***ṣawwara* = to FASHION, shape**; ⭐ ***ṣūrah* = a FORM / PHOTO**; ⭐ ***taṣwīr* = PHOTOGRAPHY / IMAGING** (⭐ ***taṣwīr bi'l-ashiʿʿah* = X-RAY**; ⭐ *ar-raʾīn al-maghnāṭīsī* = **MRI**!); ⭐ **Taghābun 64:3** — *wa **ṣawwarakum** fa-aḥsana ṣuwarakum*!; ⭐ **Infiṭār 82:8** — *fī ayyi **ṣūratin***!.
- **Here it means:** "**He is Allah, the Creator, the Originator, the Fashioner.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE THREE STAGES OF CREATION — like an ARCHITECT, a BUILDER, and a SCULPTOR, in one.** ⭐ ***AL-KHĀLIQ* — the PLANNER / MEASURER** — ⭐ ***khalaqa* originally = to MEASURE leather before cutting** — ⭐ **He DESIGNS and DETERMINES the measure of everything** (⭐ the BLUEPRINT). ⭐ ***AL-BĀRIʾ* — the ORIGINATOR** — ⭐ **He BRINGS it INTO EXISTENCE from nothing, distinct and flawless** (⭐ the CONSTRUCTION). ⭐ ***AL-MUṢAWWIR* — the FASHIONER** — ⭐ **He gives each thing its unique FORM / SHAPE / APPEARANCE** (⭐ the FINISHING — the face, the fingerprint). ⭐⭐⭐ **Plan → build → shape. Blueprint → construction → sculpture.** ⭐ **(Classical: al-Ghazālī and others explained the three Names exactly this way.)**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-BĀRIʾ* & v.16's *BARĪʾ* — ONE ROOT, ONE SURAH:** ⭐ **v.16: Satan says *innī BARĪʾun minka* — "I'm FREE of you" — separating to ABANDON.** ⭐ **v.24: Allah is *AL-BĀRIʾ* — bringing creatures into being DISTINCT and FREE of flaw — separating to CREATE.** ⭐⭐ ***Bariyyah* — BAYYINAH 98:6–7** — ⭐ ***sharr al-bariyyah / khayr al-bariyyah*** — **the WORST / BEST of CREATION.** ⭐ ***Burʾ* = RECOVERY** — ⭐ **becoming "free" of illness (clinical!).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MUṢAWWIR* — TAGHĀBUN 64:3 (*ṣawwarakum fa-aḥsana ṣuwarakum*), INFIṬĀR 82:8 (*fī ayyi ṣūratin*)** — ⭐ **the Name behind your beautiful form.** ⭐ ***Taṣwīr* = IMAGING — X-RAY, MRI** (clinical!) — ⭐ **every scan images the work of *al-Muṣawwir*.**
  ⭐⭐ ***AL-KHĀLIQ* — *khuluq* (CHARACTER, Qalam 68:4)** — ⭐ **the same root for creation and character.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **three Names in apposition — in LOGICAL ORDER** (plan → originate → fashion). ⭐ ***al-bāriʾu*** = active participle (*bāriʾ*). ⭐ ***al-muṣawwiru*** = **Form II participle**.
- **Why this exact word:** **the sequence** — ⭐ **three Names that trace creation from design to final form.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE STAGES: *AL-KHĀLIQ* (PLANNER — measures, BLUEPRINT) → *AL-BĀRIʾ* (ORIGINATOR — brings into being flawless, CONSTRUCTION) → *AL-MUṢAWWIR* (FASHIONER — unique FORM, SCULPTURE).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-BĀRIʾ* (v.24 — separates to CREATE) & *BARĪʾ* (v.16 — Satan separates to ABANDON) — ONE root, ONE surah.** ⭐⭐ ***Bariyyah* — Bayyinah 98:6–7 (worst/best of CREATION); *burʾ* = RECOVERY.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MUṢAWWIR* — Taghābun 64:3, Infiṭār 82:8 — *taṣwīr* = X-RAY / MRI.** ⭐ ***Khalaqa* = to MEASURE leather; *khuluq* = CHARACTER (68:4).**

**54. لَهُ الْأَسْمَاءُ الْحُسْنَىٰ — *lahu'l-asmāʾu'l-ḥusnā*** · v.24
- **Root feelings:** *asmāʾ* — ⭐ **NAMES** (plural of *ism*; ⭐ ***ism* ← *simah* = a MARK / or *sumuww* = HEIGHT** — classical debate!; ⭐ ***tasmiyah* = saying *bismillāh***; ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:4** — *ajal **musammā***). *ḥusnā* — ⭐⭐ **the MOST BEAUTIFUL** (feminine superlative of *aḥsan*; ⭐ ***ḥusn* = BEAUTY**; ⭐ **Layl 92:6, 9** — *ṣaddaqa / kadhdhaba bi'l-**ḥusnā***!; ⭐ **Mulk 67:2** — *aḥsanu ʿamalā*).
- **Here it means:** "**To Him belong the most beautiful names.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-ASMĀʾ AL-ḤUSNĀ* — THE MOST BEAUTIFUL NAMES.** ⭐ **After listing SIXTEEN Names in three verses — "and to Him belong ALL the most beautiful names."** ⭐⭐⭐ **The ḥadīth: "*Allah has NINETY-NINE names — whoever ENUMERATES them (aḥṣāhā) will enter Paradise*" (Bukhārī, Muslim)** — ⭐ ***aḥṣāhā*** — the PEBBLE-COUNTING root (**Nabaʾ 78:29, Jinn 72:28, Ṭalāq 65:1**)! ⭐ **"Count" them = learn, understand, live by them.** ⭐⭐ ***Lahu* FRONTED** — ⭐ **ONLY His names are *ḥusnā* (perfectly beautiful) — every one of them, with no flaw.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-ḤUSNĀ* — the LAYL connection** — ⭐ **Layl 92:6** — *wa ṣaddaqa **bi'l-ḥusnā*** (affirmed the BEST — eased to ease). ⭐ **Layl 92:9** — *wa kadhdhaba **bi'l-ḥusnā*** (denied the BEST — eased to hardship). ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:24** — *al-asmāʾu'l-**ḥusnā*** — ⭐ **the Names that are the BEST.**
  ⭐⭐ ***ISM* — debated: from *simah* (a MARK — identifies) or *sumuww* (HEIGHT — elevates)** — ⭐ **both fit His Names: they IDENTIFY Him and EXALT Him.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***lahu*** fronted = restriction. ⭐ ***al-ḥusnā*** = feminine superlative (agreeing with the broken plural *asmāʾ*).
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥusnā* — ⭐ **the summary verdict on every Name just listed — and all the others.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LAHU'L-ASMĀʾU'L-ḤUSNĀ* = to HIM (alone) belong the MOST BEAUTIFUL Names.** ⭐⭐⭐ **"99 Names — whoever ENUMERATES (*aḥṣāhā*) them enters Paradise" (Bukhārī, Muslim) — the PEBBLE-COUNTING root (78:29, 72:28, 65:1).** ⭐⭐ ***Al-ḥusnā* — Layl 92:6, 9.** ⭐ ***Ism* ← *simah* (MARK) or *sumuww* (HEIGHT).**

**55. يُسَبِّحُ لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۖ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ — *yusabbiḥu lahu mā fi's-samāwāti wa'l-arḍ · wa huwa'l-ʿazīzu'l-ḥakīm*** · v.24
- **Root feelings:** *yusabbiḥu* — ⭐ **GLORIFIES** (present!). *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* — ⭐ **the ALMIGHTY, the WISE**.
- **Here it means:** "**Whatever is in the heavens and earth glorifies Him. And He is the Almighty, the Wise.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE PERFECT BOOKEND — and a TENSE SHIFT.** ⭐ **v.1** — ***SABBAḤA*** li'llāhi mā fi's-samāwāti wa mā fi'l-arḍ · wa huwa'l-ʿAZĪZU'L-ḤAKĪM — ⭐ **PAST: it HAS glorified.** ⭐ **v.24** — ***YUSABBIḤU*** lahu mā fi's-samāwāti wa'l-arḍ · wa huwa'l-ʿAZĪZU'L-ḤAKĪM — ⭐ **PRESENT: it IS STILL glorifying.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The surah opens with glorification as an ESTABLISHED FACT and closes with it as an ONGOING ACTION** — ⭐ **between them: fortresses fell, hypocrites lied, the Anṣār gave, Satan betrayed, a mountain would crack — and through ALL of it, creation never stopped glorifying.** ⭐⭐ **And *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* — first and last words.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The *Musabbiḥāt* connected:** ⭐ **Ḥashr (59): *sabbaḥa* → *yusabbiḥu*** ⭐ **Ṣaff (61): *sabbaḥa*** ⭐ **Jumuʿah (62): *yusabbiḥu*** ⭐ **Taghābun (64): *yusabbiḥu*** ⭐ **Ḥashr alone holds BOTH tenses — a bridge.**
  ⭐ ⭐ **The last 3 verses of Ḥashr are widely recited in morning and evening adhkār** (a report in Tirmidhī promises great reward — **its chain is considered weak by many scholars**; the practice of reciting them remains common).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yusabbiḥu lahu*** — ⭐ **present + *lahu* (TO Him)** — ⭐ (v.1 had *li'llāhi*). ⭐ ***huwa'l-ʿazīzu'l-ḥakīmu*** — definite = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** **the tense shift** — ⭐ **past glorification becomes present glorification — the story inside doesn't interrupt it.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **BOOKEND: v.1 *SABBAḤA* (PAST — has glorified) → v.24 *YUSABBIḤU* (PRESENT — still glorifying); *AL-ʿAZĪZ AL-ḤAKĪM* first and last.** ⭐ **Through every event of the surah, creation never stopped glorifying.** ⭐⭐ **Ḥashr alone holds BOTH tenses of the *Musabbiḥāt* (57, 59, 61 *sabbaḥa* / 62, 64 *yusabbiḥu*).** ⭐ **Last 3 verses — popular morning/evening adhkār (Tirmidhī report — weak chain).**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **سَبَّحَ (v.1) vs يُسَبِّحُ (v.24)** — ⚠️ **PAST then PRESENT** — the surah holds both tenses.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **بَرِيءٌ (v.16) / الْبَارِئُ (v.24)** — ⚠️ **same root *b-r-ʾ*** — Satan "free of" you vs Allah the flawless Originator.
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **عَاقِبَتَهُمَا (v.17)** — ⚠️ **ACCUSATIVE** (*khabar* of *kāna* fronted); ⭐ ***khālidayn* = DUAL.**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **تَبَوَّءُوا الدَّارَ وَالْإِيمَانَ (v.9)** — ⚠️ **ONE verb, TWO objects** (a place + a state) — zeugma.
5. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْجَبَّارُ · الْمُتَكَبِّرُ (v.23)** — ⚠️ **praise for Allah, blame for humans.**
6. ⭐⭐ **مَّانِعَتُهُمْ حُصُونُهُم (v.2)** — ⚠️ **predicate FRONTED** — their certainty.
7. ⭐⭐ **لَمَّا كَفَرَ (v.16)** — ⚠️ ***lammā* + PAST = "when"** — vs ***lammā* + JUSSIVE = "not yet"** (Jumuʿah 62:3).
8. ⭐⭐ **دُولَةً (v.7)** — ⚠️ **variant *dūlatun*.**
9. ⭐⭐ **يُخْرِبُونَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **variant *yukharribūna*.**
10. ⭐⭐ **اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ ×2 (v.18)** — ⚠️ **not redundant** — before and about the deed.
11. ⭐⭐ **فَأَنسَاهُمْ أَنفُسَهُمْ (v.19)** — ⚠️ **Form IV + TWO objects.**
12. ⭐⭐ **قَرِيبًا (v.15)** — ⚠️ **RECENTLY** (time), not "near" (place).
13. ⭐⭐ **لِيُخْزِيَ (v.5)** — ⚠️ ***wa li-*** = a second purpose (joined to an implied first).
14. ⭐⭐ **الْأَسْمَاءُ الْحُسْنَىٰ** — ⚠️ ***ḥusnā* = feminine SUPERLATIVE** (agreeing with broken plural).
15. ⭐ **الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوسُ ... (v.23)** — ⚠️ **no *wāw*** — all Names of ONE subject.
16. ⭐ **وَلْتَنظُرْ (v.18)** — ⚠️ ***lām al-amr*** ("LET it look").
17. ⭐ **لَأَنتُمْ (v.13)** — ⚠️ ***lām al-ibtidāʾ*** (emphasis).
18. ⭐ **لَوْ أَنزَلْنَا ... عَلَىٰ جَبَلٍ (v.21)** — ⚠️ **hypothetical *law*** — the mountain didn't receive it.
