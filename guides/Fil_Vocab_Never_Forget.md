# Surah Al-Fīl (105) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–5)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**The biggest animal on earth is defeated by the smallest creatures in the sky — and ends up as chewed straw.**

⭐ **Look at the scale of the reversal:**
- ⭐ **v.1** — ***al-fīl*** — **THE ELEPHANT**, the largest land animal, the ancient world's tank.
- ⭐ **v.3** — ***ṭayran*** — **BIRDS**, small, light, ordinary.
- ⭐ **v.4** — ***ḥijārah*** — **little STONES**, pebbles.
- ⭐ **v.5** — ***ʿaṣfin maʾkūl*** — **CHEWED STRAW**, the most worthless leftover of a harvest.

⭐⭐ **Heaviest → lightest → smallest → most worthless.** ⭐ **Five verses, and the army goes from an elephant to cattle-fodder.**

⭐⭐ **And notice: the army's commander (Abrahah) is NEVER NAMED.** ⭐ **They're called *aṣḥāb al-fīl* — "**the people of the ELEPHANT**."** ⭐ **Named after their animal, not their king.** ⭐ **Contempt by omission.**

⭐ **And this happened in the year the Prophet ﷺ was born** — ⭐ ***ʿĀm al-Fīl*, the Year of the Elephant.** ⭐ **The first audience's grandparents watched it.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **ELEPHANT → birds → pebbles → CHEWED STRAW.** ⭐ **And the general's name isn't worth mentioning.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The question** (v.1) — ⭐ *have you not seen what your Lord did to the People of the Elephant?*
2. **The plot undone** (v.2) — ⭐ *did He not make their scheme go astray?*
3. **The birds** (v.3–4) — ⭐ **flocks upon flocks, pelting with stones of baked clay.**
4. **The ending** (v.5) — ⭐ **like straw, chewed.**

## §1 — The Question (v.1)

**1. أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *tara* — ⭐ **"see"** (from *raʾā*) — here meaning ⭐ **"know with the certainty of sight."** *faʿala* — **to do/act** (*fiʿl* = an act; ⭐ **and in grammar, *fiʿl* = A VERB!**).
- **Here it means:** "**Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the People of the Elephant?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **you own this opening WORD-FOR-WORD from Fajr 89:6.**
  
  ⭐ **Fajr 89:6 — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka **bi-ʿĀd*** ("have you not seen how your Lord dealt with **ʿĀd**?")
  ⭐ **Fīl 105:1 — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka **bi-aṣḥābi'l-fīl***
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **IDENTICAL six words — only the target changes.** ⭐ **Fajr asked about an ancient civilization; ⭐ **Fīl asks about something sixty years old.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And "SEEN" is striking: ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ was born that very year — he didn't literally watch it.** ⭐ **So *tara* here = ⭐ **"know so certainly it's as if you saw it."** ⭐ **The event was so famous, so widely witnessed, that knowing it = seeing it.**
  
  ⭐ **Connect it to Takāthur 102:5–7** — ⭐ ***ʿilm al-yaqīn* (certainty by knowledge) vs *ʿayn al-yaqīn* (certainty by sight).** ⭐⭐ **Here the two merge: ⭐ **the knowledge is so certain the Qurʾān calls it *seeing*.**
  
  ⭐ **And *kayfa* ("HOW") — ⭐ **not "THAT" He dealt with them, but HOW.** ⭐ **The method is the miracle.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **أَلَمْ + jussive** = a rhetorical question expecting "yes." ⚠️ ⭐ ***tara*** has **lost its final alif** (jussive of *yarā*). ⭐ **You own this from Fajr 89:6, Ḍuḥā 93:6, Sharḥ 94:1.**
- **Why this word:** *tara* (**see**) instead of *taʿlam* (know) — ⭐ **it elevates common knowledge to eyewitness certainty.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **IDENTICAL to Fajr 89:6** — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka* — ⭐ **only the target changes: ʿĀd → the Elephant.** ⭐⭐ **"SEEN" — though he was born that year.** ⭐ **Knowledge so certain it counts as sight** (Takāthur 102:5–7). ⭐ **And *fiʿl* = A VERB in grammar.**

**2. بِأَصْحَابِ الْفِيلِ — *bi-aṣḥābi'l-fīl*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *aṣḥāb* — ⭐ **plural of *ṣāḥib* = COMPANION / owner / people of** — you own it from **Burūj 85:4** (*aṣḥābu'l-ukhdūd*), **Balad 90:18–19** (*aṣḥābu'l-maymanah / al-mashʾamah*), **Takwīr 81:22** (*ṣāḥibukum*). *fīl* — ⭐ **THE ELEPHANT.**
- **Here it means:** "**the People of the Elephant.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the naming is an insult, and it's precise.**
  
  ⭐ **The army belonged to Abrahah, the Abyssinian viceroy of Yemen, ⭐ **who marched to destroy the Kaʿbah.** ⭐ **The Qurʾān never names him.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Instead: *aṣḥāb al-FĪL* — ⭐ "**the ELEPHANT people**."** ⭐ **Defined by their ANIMAL.** ⭐ **The one thing they thought made them unstoppable becomes their whole identity — ⭐ **and the thing that's about to become a joke.**
  
  ⭐ **The *aṣḥāb al-X* pattern across your surahs:**
  - **Burūj 85:4** — *aṣḥābu'l-**ukhdūd*** (**the People of the DITCH** — persecutors)
  - **Balad 90:18** — *aṣḥābu'l-**maymanah*** (**the people of the RIGHT**)
  - **Balad 90:19** — *aṣḥābu'l-**mashʾamah*** (**the people of the LEFT**)
  - ⭐ **Fīl 105:1** — *aṣḥābu'l-**fīl*** (**the people of the ELEPHANT**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **Burūj and Fīl are a matched pair: ⭐ **both named after the instrument of their aggression** — a ditch of fire, an elephant.** ⭐ **Both against believers / the House.** ⭐ **Both undone.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***iḍāfah*** ("the companions **of** the elephant"); genitive after *bi-*. ⭐ ***Al-fīl*** is **singular** — ⭐ **though they had several, one is named — or the collective "elephant-kind."**
- **Why this word:** naming them by **the elephant** ⭐ **erases the general** and makes their strength the punchline.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ABRAHAH IS NEVER NAMED.** ⭐ **They're called "the ELEPHANT people" — defined by their animal.** ⭐⭐ **Burūj 85:4: *aṣḥāb al-ukhdūd* (the DITCH people). Fīl 105:1: *aṣḥāb al-fīl* (the ELEPHANT people).** ⭐ **Named after their weapons.** ⭐ **And *ʿĀm al-Fīl* = the Prophet's ﷺ birth year.**

## §2 — The Plot Undone (v.2)

**3. كَيْدَهُمْ فِي تَضْلِيلٍ — *kaydahum fī taḍlīl*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *kayd* — ⭐ the "**scheming / plotting / covert stratagem**" family — *kāda* = to plot; ⭐ ***makīdah* = a trap/snare***. *taḍlīl* — ⭐ **Form II verbal noun of *ḍalla* (to go astray / be lost)** = ⭐ **to CAUSE to go astray, to send something off course, to make it come to nothing** — ⭐ **the root of *ḍāllah* = A LOST CAMEL** (⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:7!**).
- **Here it means:** "**Did He not make their plot go astray?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **two roots you own, and the image is beautiful.**
  
  ⭐ **ONE — *kayd* (the PLOT).** ⭐ **You own it from Ṭāriq 86:15–16** — *innahum **yakīdūna kaydā · wa akīdu kaydā*** ("they **plot a plot**, and **I plot a plot**"). ⭐⭐ **Ṭāriq stated the principle: their *kayd* vs. His *kayd*.** ⭐ **Fīl shows the principle in action: ⭐ **their *kayd* (march an army to destroy the Kaʿbah) vs. His (birds).**
  
  ⭐ **TWO — *taḍlīl* (sent astray).** ⭐⭐⭐ **The root of *ḍāllah* — A LOST CAMEL.** ⭐ **You own it from Ḍuḥā 93:7** — *wa wajadaka **ḍāllan** fa-hadā* ("He found you **searching/lost** and guided you").
  
  ⭐⭐ **So their master plan became ⭐ **a lost camel** — ⭐ **wandering off, never arriving, going nowhere.** ⭐ **They had a destination (the Kaʿbah); ⭐ **their plan simply lost its way.**
  
  ⭐ **And note *fī taḍlīl* — "IN" a state of being lost** — ⭐ **immersed in failure, like *fī khusr* (ʿAṣr 103:2) and *fī kabad* (Balad 90:4).**
  
  ⭐ **Historical note: ⭐ **the lead elephant (named Maḥmūd in the reports) famously **refused to advance toward Makkah**, kneeling down, though it would walk any other direction.** ⭐ **The plan literally went astray at the gate.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **أَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ** = rhetorical question (expecting "yes"). ⭐ ***Taḍlīl*** = **Form II verbal noun** (*tafʿīl*) — intensive/causative.
- **Why this word:** *taḍlīl* (**sent astray**) rather than *ibṭāl* (nullified) — ⭐ **it pictures the plan **losing its way**, which is exactly what happened.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḍāllah* = A LOST CAMEL** (Ḍuḥā 93:7). ⭐ **Their master plan became a lost camel — wandering, never arriving.** ⭐⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:15–16 stated it: their *kayd* vs His *kayd*.** ⭐ **Fīl shows it.** ⭐ **And the lead elephant refused to walk toward Makkah.**

## §3 — The Birds (v.3–4)

**4. طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ — *ṭayran abābīl*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *ṭayr* — ⭐ **BIRDS** — from *ṭāra* = **to fly**; ⭐ ***ṭāʾirah* = an AIRPLANE*** (modern!); ⭐ ***maṭār* = an AIRPORT***; ⭐ **and you own the root from Insān 76:7** (*sharruhu **mustaṭīrā*** — an evil that **flies/spreads** everywhere). *abābīl* — ⭐ **FLOCKS, group after group, in successive waves** — ⭐ **a word with **no commonly used singular**.
- **Here it means:** "**And He sent against them birds in flocks.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **two hooks — one modern, one grammatical.**
  
  ⭐ **ONE — the *ṭ-y-r* root is everywhere in modern Arabic:** ⭐ ***ṭāʾirah* = an AIRPLANE; *maṭār* = an AIRPORT; *ṭayyār* = a PILOT.*** ⭐ **Free hooks.** ⭐ **And Insān 76:7's *mustaṭīr* — "an evil that **flies out** in every direction."**
  
  ⭐⭐ **TWO — *abābīl* is famous for having NO SINGULAR.*** ⭐ **You can't say "one ababīl."** ⭐ **The word only exists as **a multitude** — ⭐ **flocks arriving in waves, one after another.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And that's the point: ⭐ **the defense of the Kaʿbah wasn't one dramatic act — ⭐ **it was **wave after wave** of small things.** ⭐ **The word can't even be singular, because the event wasn't.**
  
  ⭐ **Note *ṭayran* is INDEFINITE** — ⭐ **"birds" — unspecified kind.** ⭐ **Not eagles, not hawks — just *birds*.** ⭐ **The ordinariness is the insult to the elephant.**
  
  ⭐ **And *arsala* ("He SENT")** — ⭐ **the same verb as *rasūl* (messenger)!** ⭐ **The birds were *sent*, like messengers.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ ***abābīl* is a DIPTOTE*** (*mamnūʿ min aṣ-ṣarf*) — ⭐ **no tanwīn**, fatḥah in the accusative. ⭐ **You own diptotes from *ḥadāʾiq* (78:32), *zarābiyy* (88:16), *ḥunafāʾ* (98:5), *maqābir* (102:2).**
- **Why this word:** *abābīl* (**endless successive flocks, no singular**) — ⭐ **conveys overwhelming numbers made of tiny units.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ṭāʾirah* = AN AIRPLANE; *maṭār* = AN AIRPORT.** ⭐ **Same root as *ṭayr*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ABĀBĪL* HAS NO SINGULAR** — ⭐ **it only exists as WAVES of flocks.** ⭐ **The rescue wasn't one act; it was wave after wave of small things.** ⭐ **And *arsala* (sent) = same root as *rasūl*.**

**5. تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ مِّن سِجِّيلٍ — *tarmīhim bi-ḥijāratin min sijjīl*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *tarmī* — ⭐ the "**THROWING / pelting / shooting**" family — *ramā* = to throw; ⭐⭐ ***ramy al-jamarāt* = THE STONING OF THE PILLARS IN HAJJ!***; *rāmī* = an archer. *ḥijārah* — ⭐ **STONES** — ⭐⭐ **from *ḥ-j-r*** — ⭐ **the root of *ḥijr* = a WALL / restraint / INTELLECT** (⭐ **Fajr 89:5!**) and **the Ḥijr of the Kaʿbah**. *sijjīl* — ⭐ **baked clay, stone-hard clay** — ⭐ **commonly explained as an Arabized Persian compound: *sang* (stone) + *gil* (clay)** = ⭐ **"stone-clay."**
- **Here it means:** "**striking them with stones of baked clay.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE hooks, and two of them are ones you physically perform.**
  
  ⭐ **ONE — *ramā* = the STONING IN HAJJ.*** ⭐⭐⭐ **In Hajj, pilgrims perform *ramy al-jamarāt* — **throwing small pebbles at the pillars**.** ⭐ **Same root, same act: small stones thrown at a target.** ⭐⭐ **The birds did *ramy* on the army that came to destroy the House; ⭐ **pilgrims do *ramy* every year, near that same House.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **TWO — *ḥijārah* (stones) shares a root with *ḥijr* (WALL / INTELLECT).** ⭐ **Fajr 89:5** — *hal fī dhālika qasamun li-**dhī ḥijr*** ("is there an oath here for **one with sense**?" — literally **one with a WALL**). ⭐ **And *Ḥijr Ismāʿīl* — the semicircular wall of the Kaʿbah.** ⭐⭐ **Stones build walls; walls protect.** ⭐ **The Kaʿbah's own Ḥijr, and the stones that defended it, share three letters.**
  
  ⭐ **THREE — *sijjīl* = "stone-clay"** — ⭐ **clay baked so hard it behaves like stone.** ⭐ **Small, but lethal.**
  
  ⭐ **And the verb *tarmī* is PRESENT tense** — ⭐ **"they KEEP pelting"** — ⭐ **ongoing, continuous, wave after wave (matching *abābīl*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***tarmī*** — weak-final present (ر-م-ي), **feminine singular** because its subject *ṭayr* is a non-human plural (treated as feminine singular). ⭐ ***Bi-ḥijāratin*** — instrumental *bi-* ("**with** stones"). ⚠️ ⭐ ***sijjīl*** carries the *-īl* rhyme.
- **Why this word:** *ramā* (**pelting**) rather than *qatala* (killed) — ⭐ **it describes the **method**, which is the humiliation.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RAMY AL-JAMARĀT* — the STONING in Hajj.** ⭐ **Same root, same act.** ⭐ **Pilgrims still do *ramy* near the House the birds defended.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥijārah* (stones) ← *ḥ-j-r*** — ⭐ **same as *ḥijr* (WALL/INTELLECT, Fajr 89:5) and *Ḥijr Ismāʿīl*.** ⭐⭐ ***Sijjīl* = *sang* + *gil* = STONE-CLAY** (Persian origin).

## §4 — The Ending (v.5)

**6. فَجَعَلَهُمْ كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ — *fa-jaʿalahum ka-ʿaṣfin maʾkūl*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *ʿaṣf* — ⭐ **the dry leaves, husks, and stalks of grain crops** — ⭐ **what's LEFT after the grain is harvested; straw, chaff** — ⭐ **from *ʿaṣafa* = the wind blowing violently**: ⭐ ***ʿāṣifah* = A STORM / gale*** (⭐ ***ʿāṣifah thaljiyyah* = a snowstorm!**) — ⭐ **because chaff is what the wind blows away.** *maʾkūl* — ⭐ **EATEN** — **passive participle of *akala* (to eat).**
- **Here it means:** "**And He made them like straw that has been eaten / chewed.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the surah's final image — and the most perfectly calculated humiliation in the juzʾ.**
  
  ⭐ **Look at what *ʿaṣfin maʾkūl* actually describes:** ⭐ **not just straw — ⭐ **straw that has already been EATEN.** ⭐ **Chewed by cattle and spat out, or eaten through by insects.** ⭐ **The leftover OF a leftover.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW TRACE THE WHOLE SURAH'S SCALE:**
  ⭐ **v.1 — the ELEPHANT** (largest, heaviest, mightiest)
  ⭐ **v.5 — CHEWED STRAW** (lightest, emptiest, most worthless)
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the elephant is a HERBIVORE.** ⭐ **It eats straw.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The army of the elephant ends up as ⭐ **the kind of thing an elephant chews.** ⭐ **The predator becomes the fodder.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the *ʿāṣifah* (storm) connection: ⭐ ***ʿaṣf* is named after the wind that blows it away.** ⭐ **So they became **something the wind scatters**.**
  
  ⭐ **The "worthless debris" thread across your surahs:**
  - **Aʿlā 87:5** — *fa-jaʿalahu **ghuthāʾan aḥwā*** (⭐ **pasture → dark flood-debris**)
  - **Qāriʿah 101:5** — *ka'l-**ʿihni'l-manfūsh*** (⭐ **mountains → carded wool**)
  - **Humazah 104:4** — ***al-ḥuṭamah*** (⭐ **the Crusher — *ḥuṭām* = wreckage**)
  - ⭐ **Fīl 105:5** — ***ʿaṣfin maʾkūl*** (⭐ **an army → chewed straw**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **And *fa-jaʿalahu* in Aʿlā 87:5 vs *fa-jaʿalahum* here** — ⭐ **SAME verb + same structure: "**and He MADE it/them into [debris]**."** ⭐ **Aʿlā did it to grass; Fīl does it to an army.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ka-*** = *kāf at-tashbīh* (simile). ⭐ ***Maʾkūl*** = **passive participle** — ⭐ **"having been eaten."** ⚠️ ⭐ **Note *fa-jaʿala* = "and He made [X into Y]" — *jaʿala* takes two objects.**
- **Why this word:** *ʿaṣfin maʾkūl* — ⭐ **the most worthless organic matter imaginable, deliberately chosen to match the most powerful animal imaginable.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĀṣifah* = A STORM** — ⭐ ***ʿaṣf* = chaff the wind blows away.** ⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE ELEPHANT EATS STRAW.** ⭐ **The army of the elephant became **what an elephant chews**.** ⭐ **The predator became the fodder.** ⭐⭐ **Debris thread: grass → debris (87:5), mountains → wool (101:5), wreckage (104:4), army → chewed straw (105:5).**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَبَابِيلَ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **has NO commonly used SINGULAR.** ⭐ **It only exists as waves of flocks.** ⭐ **And it's a DIPTOTE (no tanwīn).**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **عَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **not just straw — straw ALREADY EATEN/chewed.** ⭐ **The leftover of a leftover. And elephants eat straw.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **حِجَارَةٍ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **same root as *ḥijr* (WALL / intellect, Fajr 89:5).** ⭐ **Don't treat "stones" as an isolated word.**
4. ⭐⭐ **أَلَمْ تَرَ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **"SEE," though the Prophet ﷺ was born that year.** ⭐ **Means "know with eyewitness certainty."** ⭐ **And *tara* drops its alif (jussive).**
5. ⭐⭐ **تَضْلِيلٍ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **Form II of *ḍalla* — the plan was SENT ASTRAY** like a lost camel (*ḍāllah*, Ḍuḥā 93:7).
6. ⭐⭐ **Abrahah is NEVER NAMED** — ⚠️ ⭐ **the army is called after its ELEPHANT. Contempt by omission.**
7. ⭐ **تَرْمِيهِم (v.4)** — ⚠️ **FEMININE singular verb** for the birds — ⭐ **non-human plurals take feminine singular agreement.**
8. ⭐ **سِجِّيلٍ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **commonly explained as Arabized Persian *sang* (stone) + *gil* (clay).**
9. ⭐ **تَرْمِيهِم is PRESENT tense** — ⚠️ **continuous pelting**, wave after wave.
10. ⭐ **فَجَعَلَهُمْ (v.5)** — ⚠️ ***jaʿala* takes TWO objects** ("made THEM into straw"). ⭐ **Same structure as Aʿlā 87:5.**
