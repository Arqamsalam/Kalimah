# Surah Al-Mumtaḥanah (60) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–13) · **Complete coverage — every important word**

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**Its name means "The Woman Who Is EXAMINED" — and the whole surah is an EXAM of loyalty: where does your love go when faith and family pull in different directions?**

⭐ ***Al-Mumtaḥanah*** — from ***imtiḥān* = an EXAM** (everyday Arabic — school exams!). ⭐ **v.10: *fa'mtaḥinūhunna* — "EXAMINE them" (the women who emigrated to Madīnah).**

⭐ **It OPENS with a real failure of the exam — a Companion who wrote a secret letter.** ⭐ **Ḥāṭib ibn Abī Baltaʿah (a veteran of Badr!) secretly wrote to Quraysh warning them the Prophet ﷺ was marching on Makkah — to protect his family there.** ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ sent ʿAlī, az-Zubayr and al-Miqdād to intercept the woman carrying it; they found the letter in her hair.** ⭐ **ʿUmar wanted to punish him; the Prophet ﷺ said: "*He fought at Badr — perhaps Allah looked at the people of Badr and said: do as you wish, I have forgiven you*"** (Bukhārī, Muslim).

⭐⭐⭐ **The surah's balance: v.1 — DON'T take hostile enemies as allies. v.8 — Allah does NOT forbid you from being KIND and JUST to non-Muslims who don't fight you.** ⭐ **Firm loyalty, without hostility to the peaceful.** ⭐ **And at its centre: IBRĀHĪM — the model (*uswah*) of loyalty to Allah above family.**

⭐⭐ **It's BOOKENDED: v.1 *lā tattakhidhū ʿaduwwī… awliyāʾ* — v.13 *lā tatawallaw qawman ghaḍiba'llāhu ʿalayhim*** — ⭐ **both: "don't take them as allies."**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUMTAḤANAH* ← *IMTIḤĀN* = an EXAM — the surah of the LOYALTY test.** ⭐ **Ḥāṭib's secret letter (failed, forgiven); Ibrāhīm (the model); v.8 — be KIND and JUST to the peaceful.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The secret letter** (v.1–3) — don't take My enemies as allies, secretly showing them affection; your kin won't help you on the Day.
2. **The model of Ibrāhīm** (v.4–6) — "we are free of you and what you worship"; except his promise to his father; his duʿāʾ.
3. **Perhaps there will be love** (v.7) — Allah may put affection between you and your enemies.
4. **Kindness and justice** (v.8–9) — to those who don't fight you; not to those who do.
5. **The women who emigrated** (v.10–11) — examine them; don't send believers back; settle the dowries justly.
6. **The women's pledge** (v.12) — six conditions.
7. **The closing warning** (v.13) — don't ally with those under Allah's anger.

## §1 — The Secret Letter (v.1–3)

**1. يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تَتَّخِذُوا عَدُوِّي وَعَدُوَّكُمْ أَوْلِيَاءَ — *yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū lā tattakhidhū ʿaduwwī wa ʿaduwwakum awliyāʾ*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *tattakhidhū* — ⭐ **TAKE (as)** (Form VIII of *akhadha*; ⭐ ***ittikhādh* = ADOPTING** — *ittikhādh al-qarār* = **DECISION-MAKING**!). *ʿaduww* — ⭐⭐⭐ **ENEMY** (*ʿadā* — to run against; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4** *humu'l-ʿaduww*; ⭐ **Taghābun 64:14** *ʿaduwwan lakum*!; ⭐ ***ʿadwā* = CONTAGION**). *awliyāʾ* — ⭐⭐ **ALLIES / close friends / protectors** (plural of *walī*; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:2, 4** *mawlā*; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:6** *awliyāʾu li'llāh*; ⭐ ***walī al-ʿahd* = CROWN PRINCE**!).
- **Here it means:** "**O you who believe, do not take My enemy and your enemy as allies…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿADUWWĪ WA ʿADUWWAKUM* — "MY enemy AND YOUR enemy"** — ⭐ **Allah puts Himself FIRST — "My enemy" — then "yours."** ⭐ **The logic: those who are at war with Allah's religion and with you (they expelled you — v.1b!) — how can you secretly ally with them?** ⭐⭐ ***ʿAduww* — SINGULAR for a group (again!)** — ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4** (*humu'l-ʿaduww*), **Taghābun 64:14** (*ʿaduwwan*) — ⭐ **the enemy as one hostile front.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *ʿADUWW* THREAD — across FOUR consecutive surahs:** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4** — the hypocrites in the COMMUNITY = *al-ʿaduww*. ⭐ **Taghābun 64:14** — some in your own HOME = *ʿaduwwan lakum*. ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:1** — hostile outsiders at WAR = *ʿaduwwī wa ʿaduwwakum*. ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:14** — *fa-ayyadna'lladhīna āmanū ʿalā **ʿaduwwihim*** (We supported the believers against their ENEMY). ⭐⭐ **Enemies in the community, in the home, outside, and the final victory over them.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Awliyāʾ* — ALLIES / those you hand your loyalty to** — ⭐ **the core issue: *walāʾ* (loyalty)** — ⭐ ***walī al-ʿahd* = CROWN PRINCE**; ⭐ ***Mawlānā*** (Taḥrīm 66:2).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā tattakhidhū*** = prohibition + **two objects** (*ʿaduwwī*, *awliyāʾa*). ⚠️⭐ ***ʿaduwwī wa ʿaduwwakum*** — **singular form, plural sense**. ⚠️ ⭐ ***awliyāʾa*** = **diptote**.
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿaduwwī* — ⭐ **Allah's enmity named first — framing loyalty as a matter of His cause, not mere tribe.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿADUWWĪ WA ʿADUWWAKUM* = "MY enemy AND YOUR enemy" — Allah puts Himself FIRST.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿAduww* ×4 surahs: COMMUNITY (Munāfiqūn 63:4), HOME (Taghābun 64:14), at WAR outside (Mumtaḥanah 60:1), VICTORY over them (Ṣaff 61:14).** ⭐⭐ ***Awliyāʾ* = ALLIES — the issue of LOYALTY; *walī al-ʿahd* = CROWN PRINCE.** ⭐ ***Ittikhādh al-qarār* = DECISION-MAKING.** ⚠️ **Singular *ʿaduww* for a group.**

**2. تُلْقُونَ إِلَيْهِم بِالْمَوَدَّةِ — *tulqūna ilayhim bi'l-mawaddah*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *tulqūna* — ⭐ **you CAST / throw [to them]** (*alqā*; ⭐ ***ilqāʾ* = a LECTURE / delivery**; ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:5** — *innā sa-**nulqī** ʿalayka qawlan thaqīlā*!). *mawaddah* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**AFFECTION / LOVING closeness**" family — *wadda* = to love, to wish dearly; ⭐ ***mawaddah* = AFFECTION / love** (⭐ ***mawaddah wa raḥmah*** — between spouses, 30:21!); ⭐ ***al-Wadūd* = the LOVING** (a Name — **Burūj 85:14**!); ⭐ ***Wadd* = an IDOL named "Love"** (⭐ **Nūḥ 71:23**!); ⭐ ***wudd* = friendship**; ⭐ ***waddī* = FRIENDLY** (⭐ *mubārāh waddiyyah* = a **FRIENDLY match**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…extending affection to them…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TULQŪNA ILAYHIM BI'L-MAWADDAH* — "THROWING affection to them"** — ⭐ **like tossing something across a gap** — ⭐ **secret messages, warnings, favours — to people at war with you.** ⭐ ***Ilqāʾ* = also DELIVERING a message** — ⭐ **Ḥāṭib literally "DELIVERED" a letter of affection/warning.** ⭐⭐ **Muzzammil 73:5** — *sa-**nulqī** ʿalayka qawlan thaqīlā* — ⭐ **Allah "casts" the HEAVY WORD (revelation) to His Prophet; Ḥāṭib "cast" affection to the enemy.** ⭐ **Same verb — what you cast, and to whom.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAWADDAH* — the *WADD* thread:** ⭐ **Burūj 85:14** — *wa huwa'l-ghafūru'l-**wadūd*** (Allah, the LOVING). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:23** — ***Wadd*** (an IDOL named "Love"!). ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:11** — ***yawaddu*** l-mujrim (the criminal will WISH…). ⭐ **Qalam 68:9** — ***waddū*** law tudhinu (they WISH you'd compromise). ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:1** — ***bi'l-mawaddah*** (affection to the enemy) — ⭐ **and v.2 *wa waddū law takfurūn* — they WISH you'd disbelieve!** ⭐ **and v.7 — *mawaddah* PROMISED (between you and them, eventually).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Mumtaḥanah alone uses the root THREE times: misplaced affection (v.1), their wish against you (v.2), and hoped-for future affection (v.7).**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***mawaddah wa raḥmah* (30:21)**; ⭐ ***mubārāh waddiyyah* = FRIENDLY MATCH.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***tulqūna*** = *ḥāl* ("**[while] casting**"). ⭐ ***bi'l-mawaddati*** — the ***bi-*** is classically **extra** (casting affection) or **instrumental** (casting [news] WITH affection).
- **Why this exact word:** *mawaddah* — ⭐ **love — the deepest thing — sent across enemy lines.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TULQŪNA ILAYHIM BI'L-MAWADDAH* = THROWING affection to the enemy — Ḥāṭib "DELIVERED" (*ilqāʾ*) a letter.** ⭐⭐ **Muzzammil 73:5: Allah CASTS revelation (*nulqī*); Ḥāṭib CAST affection to the enemy.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WADD* thread: *al-Wadūd* (85:14), the idol *Wadd* (71:23), *yawaddu* (70:11), *waddū* (68:9) — Mumtaḥanah ×3: v.1 misplaced, v.2 their wish, v.7 future love.** ⭐ ***Mawaddah wa raḥmah* (30:21); *waddī* = FRIENDLY.**

**3. وَقَدْ كَفَرُوا بِمَا جَاءَكُم مِّنَ الْحَقِّ يُخْرِجُونَ الرَّسُولَ وَإِيَّاكُمْ — *wa qad kafarū bimā jāʾakum mina'l-ḥaqqi yukhrijūna'r-rasūla wa iyyākum*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *kafarū* — ⭐ **they DISBELIEVED** (covered the truth). *al-ḥaqq* — ⭐ **the TRUTH**. *yukhrijūna* — ⭐ **they EXPEL** (⭐ **Ḥashr 59:8** — *ukhrijū min diyārihim*!). *iyyākum* — ⭐ **YOU (also)** (detached object pronoun).
- **Here it means:** "**…while they have disbelieved in the truth that came to you, expelling the Messenger and you [from Makkah]…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TWO REASONS — why affection to them is wrong:** ⭐ **(1) *kafarū bimā jāʾakum mina'l-ḤAQQ*** — ⭐ **they REJECTED the TRUTH you received.** ⭐ **(2) *yukhrijūna'r-RASŪLA wa IYYĀKUM*** — ⭐ **they EXPELLED the Messenger AND YOU from your homes.** ⭐⭐ ***Iyyākum* — a SEPARATE pronoun, for emphasis** — ⭐ **"the Messenger — and YOU too!"** ⭐ **"They threw YOU out — and you send them love letters?"**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And ḤASHR 59:8 (the previous surah!)** — *li'l-fuqarāʾi'l-muhājirīna'lladhīna **ukhrijū min diyārihim*** — ⭐ **the EMIGRANTS — EXPELLED from their homes (by the Quraysh).** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:1: the same expellers — and one Emigrant (Ḥāṭib) secretly sending them affection.** ⭐ **Consecutive surahs, the same expulsion.**
  ⭐ ⭐ **Note *YUKHRIJŪNA* — PRESENT tense** — ⭐ **as if the expulsion is still happening — the wound still fresh.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa qad kafarū*** = *ḥāl* ("**while** they have disbelieved"). ⭐ ***yukhrijūna*** = *ḥāl* (present — vivid). ⭐⭐ ***iyyākum*** = detached pronoun (emphasis).
- **Why this exact word:** *iyyākum* — ⭐ **"and YOU" — reminding the reader of his own expulsion.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO REASONS: they REJECTED the TRUTH + they EXPELLED the Messenger AND YOU (*iyyākum* — emphatic).** ⭐ **"They threw YOU out — and you send love letters?"** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥashr 59:8 — the Emigrants *ukhrijū* (expelled) — the same expellers.** ⭐ ***Yukhrijūna* — PRESENT: the wound still fresh.**

**4. أَن تُؤْمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ رَبِّكُمْ — *an tuʾminū bi'llāhi rabbikum*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *an* — ⭐ **BECAUSE / for [the reason that]**. *tuʾminū* — ⭐ **you BELIEVE**.
- **Here it means:** "**…[only] because you believe in Allah, your Lord.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE "CRIME" — believing in Allah.** ⭐ **Why were you expelled? Not for theft, not for violence — *AN TUʾMINŪ BI'LLĀH*** — ⭐ **"because you BELIEVE in Allah, YOUR LORD."** ⭐⭐ **BURŪJ 85:8** — *wa mā naqamū minhum **illā an yuʾminū bi'llāhi'l-ʿazīzi'l-ḥamīd*** — ⭐ **"they resented them ONLY because they BELIEVED in Allah"** (the people of the trench). ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME STRUCTURE — *an yuʾminū / an tuʾminū bi'llāh*** — ⭐ **Burūj: the believers BURNED for faith; Mumtaḥanah: the believers EXPELLED for faith.** ⭐ **Faith itself as the only "charge."**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Rabbikum* — "YOUR Lord"** — ⭐ **a reminder of the relationship they're endangering.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***an tuʾminū*** = ***maṣdar muʾawwal*** (*an* + verb = verbal noun) — **object of reason** (*mafʿūl li-ajlih* — "because of your believing").
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **naming faith as the only charge — echoing the trench of Burūj.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY expelled? *AN TUʾMINŪ BI'LLĀH* — ONLY because you BELIEVE.** ⭐⭐⭐ **= BURŪJ 85:8 *illā an yuʾminū bi'llāh*: BURNED for faith (85) / EXPELLED for faith (60).** ⭐ ***Rabbikum* = YOUR Lord — the relationship at stake.**

**5. إِن كُنتُمْ خَرَجْتُمْ جِهَادًا فِي سَبِيلِي وَابْتِغَاءَ مَرْضَاتِي — *in kuntum kharajtum jihādan fī sabīlī wa'btighāʾa marḍātī*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *kharajtum* — ⭐ **you WENT OUT** (emigrated). *jihād* — ⭐ **STRIVING** (*juhd* = effort; ⭐ ***ijtihād***; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:9** — *jāhid*!). *sabīlī* — ⭐ **MY WAY**. *ibtighāʾ* — ⭐ **SEEKING** (⭐ **Layl 92:20** — *illā **ibtighāʾa** wajhi rabbihi'l-aʿlā*!). *marḍātī* — ⭐⭐ **MY PLEASURE** (⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:1** — *tabtaghī **marḍāta** azwājik*!).
- **Here it means:** "**If you have gone out to strive in My way and to seek My pleasure…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"IF you left for MY sake — act like it."** ⭐ **You EMIGRATED (*kharajtum*) seeking *MARḌĀTĪ* (MY pleasure)** — ⭐ **so how can you now secretly please MY enemies?** ⭐ **The emigration and the secret letter contradict each other.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***IBTIGHĀʾA MARḌĀTĪ* — TAḤRĪM 66:1!** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:1** — *tabtaghī **marḍāta azwājik*** — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ seeking his WIVES' pleasure (gently corrected).** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:1** — *wa'btighāʾa **marḍātī*** — ⭐ **seeking MY pleasure — the right object.** ⭐ ***Ibtighāʾ marḍāt*** — ⭐ **whose pleasure do you seek? — Wives'? Enemies'? — Mine.** ⭐⭐ **Layl 92:20** — *illā **ibtighāʾa wajhi rabbihi'l-aʿlā*** (seeking only the FACE of his Lord) — **the purest *ibtighāʾ*.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Sabīlī / marḍātī* — "MY way, MY pleasure"** — ⭐ **Allah speaking in the FIRST person** — ⭐ **personal, intimate.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***in kuntum*** = condition — **answer DELETED** (implied: "[then don't take them as allies]"). ⭐ ***jihādan, ibtighāʾa*** = ***mafʿūl li-ajlih*** (purpose — "**for** striving, **for** seeking").
- **Why this exact word:** *marḍātī* — ⭐ **His pleasure as the reason you emigrated — now contradicted by pleasing His enemies.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"IF you left for MY sake (*jihādan fī sabīlī*, *ibtighāʾa marḍātī*) — act like it."** ⭐ **Emigration and the secret letter CONTRADICT.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***IBTIGHĀʾ MARḌĀT*: WIVES' pleasure (Taḥrīm 66:1) / MY pleasure (Mumtaḥanah 60:1) / His FACE (Layl 92:20).** ⭐ **Allah in the FIRST person — *sabīlī*, *marḍātī*.** ⚠️ **Answer of *in* DELETED.**

**6. تُسِرُّونَ إِلَيْهِم بِالْمَوَدَّةِ وَأَنَا أَعْلَمُ بِمَا أَخْفَيْتُمْ وَمَا أَعْلَنتُمْ — *tusirrūna ilayhim bi'l-mawaddati wa ana aʿlamu bimā akhfaytum wa mā aʿlantum*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *tusirrūna* — ⭐⭐ **you SECRETLY convey** (*sirr*; ⭐ ***sirrī* = CONFIDENTIAL**). *akhfaytum* — ⭐ **you HID** (*khafiya*; ⭐ ***ikhfāʾ*** — the tajwīd CONCEALMENT rule!; ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:18** — *lā takhfā minkum **khāfiyah***). *aʿlantum* — ⭐ **you DECLARED** (*iʿlān* = an ADVERTISEMENT).
- **Here it means:** "**…you secretly convey affection to them — but I know best what you hid and what you declared.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SECRET LETTER — and the PERFECT echo of TAGHĀBUN 64:4.** ⭐ ***TUSIRRŪNA ilayhim bi'l-mawaddah*** — ⭐ **"you SECRETLY send them affection"** — ⭐ **Ḥāṭib's letter, hidden in a woman's hair.** ⭐ ***Wa ANA aʿlamu*** — ⭐ **"and *I* know best"** — ⭐ **Allah in the first person, again** — ⭐ **the letter was intercepted because ALLAH told His Prophet ﷺ** (⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:3** — *nabbaʾaniya'l-ʿAlīmu'l-Khabīr*!). ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *SIRR / ʿALAN* THREAD — now FIVE surahs:** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:9** — *aʿlantu… asrartu* (Nūḥ preached BOTH ways). ⭐ **Mulk 67:13** — *asirrū… awi'jharū* (secret or loud — He knows). ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:3** — *asarra'n-nabiyyu* (a secret confided — revealed). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:4** — *mā tusirrūna wa mā tuʿlinūn* (He knows both). ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:1** — *tusirrūna… akhfaytum… aʿlantum* (the secret LETTER — exposed). ⭐⭐ **From principle (Mulk, Taghābun) to real cases (Taḥrīm: a household secret; Mumtaḥanah: a military secret).**
  ⭐⭐ ***AKHFAYTUM* — the *khafiya* root** — ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:18** — *lā takhfā minkum khāfiyah* (no HIDDEN thing will stay hidden). ⭐ ***Ikhfāʾ* = the tajwīd CONCEALMENT rule.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***tusirrūna*** = *badal* / explanation of *tulqūna* (v.1a). ⭐⭐ ***wa ana aʿlamu*** = *ḥāl* — **first-person divine speech**. ⭐ ***akhfaytum / aʿlantum*** = *ṭibāq* pair.
- **Why this exact word:** *tusirrūna* — ⭐ **secrecy named — and immediately exposed by the One who knows.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TUSIRRŪNA ILAYHIM BI'L-MAWADDAH* = SECRETLY sending affection — Ḥāṭib's letter hidden in a woman's HAIR.** ⭐⭐ ***WA ANA AʿLAMU* — "*I* know" (Allah told the Prophet ﷺ — Taḥrīm 66:3).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SIRR / ʿALAN* ×5: Nūḥ 71:9, Mulk 67:13, Taḥrīm 66:3, Taghābun 64:4, Mumtaḥanah 60:1 — principle → REAL cases (household secret, MILITARY secret).** ⭐ ***Akhfaytum* — Ḥāqqah 69:18; *ikhfāʾ* (tajwīd).**

**7. وَمَن يَفْعَلْهُ مِنكُمْ فَقَدْ ضَلَّ سَوَاءَ السَّبِيلِ — *wa man yafʿalhu minkum fa-qad ḍalla sawāʾa's-sabīl*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *ḍalla* — ⭐ **STRAYED** (⭐ *ḍāllah* = a lost camel; ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:7** *ḍāllan*). *sawāʾ* — ⭐⭐⭐ **the MIDDLE / LEVEL / straight centre** (⭐ ***s-w-y*** — your LONGEST thread!; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:6** *sawāʾun*; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:20** *lā yastawī*; ⭐ **Mulk 67:22** *sawiyyan*). *sabīl* — ⭐ **the ROAD**.
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever of you does it has strayed from the straight [middle] of the road.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḌALLA SAWĀʾA'S-SABĪL* — "strayed from the MIDDLE of the road"** — ⭐ ***sawāʾ* = the LEVEL, CENTRE part of the road** — ⭐ **not off a cliff — drifted from the CENTRE line** — ⭐ **a gentle but clear warning for a believer who slipped.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *S-W-Y* THREAD — now SEVEN meanings:** ⭐ ***sawwā*** (balanced — 82:7, 87:2, 91:7) ⭐ ***sawiyy*** (upright walker — Mulk 67:22) ⭐ ***sawāʾun ʿalayhim*** (all the same to them — Munāfiqūn 63:6) ⭐ ***lā yastawī*** (not equal — Ḥashr 59:20) ⭐ ***sawāʾ as-sabīl*** (the CENTRE of the road — Mumtaḥanah 60:1). ⭐⭐ **Mulk 67:22: walk *sawiyyan* (UPRIGHT) on *ṣirāṭin mustaqīm* — here: don't drift from *sawāʾ as-sabīl* (the CENTRE of the road).**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Man yafʿalHU MINKUM*** — ⭐ **"whoever OF YOU does it"** — ⭐ **addressed to BELIEVERS — a believer can slip.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** + jussive (*yafʿal*) + ***fa-qad*** (answer). ⭐ ***sawāʾa*** = object (accusative) — ⭐ ***ḍalla*** transitive here ("**lost** the middle of the road").
- **Why this exact word:** *sawāʾ* — ⭐ **a precise picture of drifting from the centre line — serious but recoverable.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḌALLA SAWĀʾA'S-SABĪL* = drifted from the CENTRE of the road — not off a cliff; recoverable.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***S-W-Y*: BALANCED (82:7), UPRIGHT (67:22), "all the SAME" (63:6), NOT EQUAL (59:20), the CENTRE of the ROAD (60:1).** ⭐ ***Man yafʿalhu MINKUM* — a BELIEVER can slip.**

**8. إِن يَثْقَفُوكُمْ يَكُونُوا لَكُمْ أَعْدَاءً — *in yathqafūkum yakūnū lakum aʿdāʾ*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *yathqafūkum* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**GRASPING SKILFULLY / straightening / CULTURE**" family — *thaqifa* = **to FIND, CATCH, GRASP (with skill); to be quick-witted**; ⭐ ***thiqāf* = a tool for STRAIGHTENING spear-shafts** (classically!); ⭐⭐⭐ ***thaqāfah* = CULTURE / EDUCATION** (⭐ the mind "straightened" and sharpened — like a spear!; ⭐ *wizārat ath-thaqāfah* = the **Ministry of CULTURE**!); ⭐ ***muthaqqaf* = an INTELLECTUAL / educated person**; ⭐ ***tathqīf* = EDUCATING / enlightening** (⭐ *tathqīf ṣiḥḥī* = **HEALTH EDUCATION** — clinical!). *aʿdāʾ* — ⭐ **ENEMIES** (plural of *ʿaduww*).
- **Here it means:** "**If they gain the upper hand over you [catch you], they will be enemies to you…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YATHQAFŪKUM* — "if they CATCH / get HOLD of you"** — ⭐ **the root of *THAQĀFAH* (CULTURE)!** ⭐ **The original picture: *thiqāf* — a tool to STRAIGHTEN a bent spear-shaft; *thaqifa* = to grasp skilfully, to be sharp.** ⭐ **A "cultured" person (*muthaqqaf*) = one whose mind has been SHARPENED and STRAIGHTENED.** ⭐⭐ **Here: if they SEIZE the advantage over you** — ⭐ **your secret affection won't save you — they'll be ENEMIES (*aʿdāʾ*).** ⭐ **Ḥāṭib wrote to protect his family; the verse says: their goodwill is an illusion.**
  ⭐⭐ ***THAQĀFAH* = CULTURE; *muthaqqaf* = INTELLECTUAL; *tathqīf ṣiḥḥī* = HEALTH EDUCATION (clinical!)** ⭐ **— all from "straightening a spear."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***in*** + jussive (*yathqafūkum*) + jussive answer (*yakūnū*). ⚠️⭐ ***aʿdāʾa*** = **diptote** (*afʿilāʾ*).
- **Why this exact word:** *thaqifa* — ⭐ **seizing with skill — the advantage they'd press, whatever affection you'd sent.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YATHQAFŪKUM* = if they CATCH / SEIZE the advantage over you — they'll be ENEMIES regardless.** ⭐ **Secret affection won't protect you.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***THAQIFA* → *THAQĀFAH* (CULTURE) ← *thiqāf* (tool to STRAIGHTEN a spear) — *muthaqqaf* = INTELLECTUAL; *tathqīf ṣiḥḥī* = HEALTH EDUCATION.** ⚠️ ***Aʿdāʾ* = diptote.**

**9. وَيَبْسُطُوا إِلَيْكُمْ أَيْدِيَهُمْ وَأَلْسِنَتَهُم بِالسُّوءِ وَوَدُّوا لَوْ تَكْفُرُونَ — *wa yabsuṭū ilaykum aydiyahum wa alsinatahum bi's-sūʾi wa waddū law takfurūn*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *yabsuṭū* — ⭐⭐ **STRETCH OUT** (*basaṭa*; ⭐ ***bisāṭ* = a CARPET** — **Nūḥ 71:19**!; ⭐ ***basīṭ* = SIMPLE**; ⭐ ***al-Bāsiṭ*** — the Expander; ⭐ **Mulk 67:19** — birds *ṣāffāt wa yaqbiḍn* (spread & fold!)). *aydī* — ⭐ **HANDS**. *alsinah* — ⭐ **TONGUES** (plural of *lisān*; ⭐ ***lisān* = TONGUE / LANGUAGE**!; ⭐ ***lisāniyyāt* = LINGUISTICS**!). *sūʾ* — ⭐ **EVIL / harm**. *waddū* — ⭐ **they WISH** (⭐ v.1 *mawaddah*!; ⭐ **Qalam 68:9** — *waddū law tudhinu*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and stretch out their hands and their tongues against you with harm — and they wish you would disbelieve.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE WEAPONS:** ⭐ **(1) HANDS — physical harm** ⭐ **(2) TONGUES — verbal harm (insults, propaganda)** ⭐ **(3) WISHES — *waddū law takfurūn* — the deepest one: they WISH you'd leave your faith.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WADDŪ LAW TAKFURŪN* — QALAM 68:9!** ⭐ **Qalam 68:9** — *waddū law **tudhinu** fa-yudhinūn* — ⭐ **they WISH you'd COMPROMISE.** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:2** — *waddū law **takfurūn*** — ⭐ **they WISH you'd DISBELIEVE.** ⭐⭐ **Compromise is the first step; disbelief the goal.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the WADD irony: v.1 — YOU send them *MAWADDAH* (affection). v.2 — THEY *WADDŪ* (wish) your disbelief.** ⭐ **Same root: your love for them vs their "love" for your ruin.**
  ⭐⭐ ***YABSUṬŪ* — STRETCH OUT** — ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:19** — *al-arḍa **bisāṭā*** (the earth SPREAD as a carpet — mercy) ⭐ **vs here — hands STRETCHED to harm.** ⭐ **Same root: spreading for kindness or for cruelty.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***lisāniyyāt* = LINGUISTICS**; ⭐ ***bisāṭ* = CARPET**; ⭐ ***basīṭ* = SIMPLE.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yabsuṭū*** = jussive (joined to *yakūnū*). ⭐⭐ ***wa waddū*** = **PAST** (switched from jussive!) — ⭐ classically: **their wish ALREADY exists — it's not conditional on catching you.**
- **Why this exact word:** *waddū* — ⭐ **their real "affection" — for your disbelief — answering your misplaced *mawaddah*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE WEAPONS: HANDS (physical), TONGUES (verbal), WISHES — *waddū law takfurūn* (they WISH you'd DISBELIEVE).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Qalam 68:9 — WISH you'd COMPROMISE → Mumtaḥanah 60:2 — WISH you'd DISBELIEVE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WADD* irony: YOU send *MAWADDAH* (v.1) / THEY *WADDŪ* your ruin (v.2).** ⭐⭐ ***Basaṭa*: earth SPREAD as carpet (71:19) / hands STRETCHED to harm (60:2).** ⚠️ ***Waddū* = PAST — their wish already exists.** ⭐ ***Lisāniyyāt* = LINGUISTICS.**

**10. لَن تَنفَعَكُمْ أَرْحَامُكُمْ وَلَا أَوْلَادُكُمْ ۚ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ يَفْصِلُ بَيْنَكُمْ — *lan tanfaʿakum arḥāmukum wa lā awlādukum · yawma'l-qiyāmati yafṣilu baynakum*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *tanfaʿa* — ⭐ **BENEFIT** (*nafʿ*; ⭐ ***manfaʿah* = BENEFIT / utility**; ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:4** — *fa-tanfaʿahu'dh-dhikrā*!). *arḥām* — ⭐⭐⭐ **WOMBS → BLOOD TIES / kinship** (plural of ***raḥim* = WOMB** — ⭐ clinical: ***raḥim* = UTERUS**!; ⭐ ***ṣilat ar-raḥim* = maintaining KINSHIP ties**!; ⭐ ***raḥmah* = MERCY**; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:22** — *ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm*!). *awlād* — ⭐ **CHILDREN**. *yafṣilu* — ⭐⭐ **will SEPARATE / judge between** (*faṣl*; ⭐ ***yawm al-faṣl*** — **Nabaʾ 78:17**!; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:13** — *faṣīlatih* (his clan)!; ⭐ ***faṣl* = a CHAPTER / SEASON / CLASSROOM**; ⭐ ***infiṣāl* = SEPARATION / divorce**).
- **Here it means:** "**Your blood relations and your children will never benefit you; on the Day of Resurrection He will separate you…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE REASON HĀṬIB WROTE — answered directly.** ⭐ **He wrote to protect his FAMILY in Makkah** — ⭐ ***LAN TANFAʿAKUM ARḤĀMUKUM WA LĀ AWLĀDUKUM*** — ⭐ **"your BLOOD TIES and CHILDREN will NEVER benefit you"** (if you betray Allah for them). ⭐⭐⭐ ***ARḤĀM* ← *RAḤIM* (the WOMB)** — ⭐ **kinship named after the womb** — ⭐ **the same root as *RAḤMAH* (MERCY) and *AR-RAḤMĀN* (Ḥashr 59:22)!** ⭐ **The ḥadīth: "*The raḥim (kinship) is derived from ar-Raḥmān — whoever maintains it, Allah maintains him*" (Bukhārī).** ⭐⭐ **Kinship is sacred (*ṣilat ar-raḥim*) — but it can't outrank the One whose Name it carries.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAFṢILU BAYNAKUM* — "He will SEPARATE you"** — ⭐ **NABAʾ 78:17** — *inna **yawma'l-faṣli** kāna mīqātā* — ⭐ **the DAY OF SORTING / SEPARATION.** ⭐ **MAʿĀRIJ 70:11–13** — the criminal would ransom ***bi-banīh… wa faṣīlatihi'llatī tuʾwīh*** — ⭐ **his SONS and his CLAN (*faṣīlah* — same root!).** ⭐⭐ **ʿABASA 80:34–36** — *yawma yafirru'l-marʾu min akhīh…* — ⭐ **he FLEES from family.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The family-on-the-Day thread: flee them (ʿAbasa), ransom them (Maʿārij), separated from them (Mumtaḥanah).** ⭐ **And Taghābun 64:14–15 — spouses/children may be ENEMIES / a TEST.**
  ⭐ Clinical hooks: ⭐ ***raḥim* = UTERUS**; ⭐ ***infiṣāl* = SEPARATION.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lan*** + subjunctive = emphatic future negation. ⭐ ***yawma'l-qiyāmati*** — ⚠️ **can attach to EITHER side** (classical pause-debate: "won't benefit you ON THE DAY" / "ON THE DAY He'll separate") — ⭐ both meanings true. ⚠️ ⭐ **Recitation variants: *yufṣalu / yufaṣṣilu / yufaṣṣalu*.**
- **Why this exact word:** *arḥām* — ⭐ **the womb-ties Ḥāṭib was protecting, named as powerless on the Day.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LAN TANFAʿAKUM ARḤĀMUKUM WA LĀ AWLĀDUKUM* = blood ties and children will NEVER benefit you — the reason Ḥāṭib wrote, ANSWERED.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ARḤĀM* ← *RAḤIM* (WOMB) — same root as *RAḤMAH* & *AR-RAḤMĀN* — "the *raḥim* is derived from ar-Raḥmān" (Bukhārī); *ṣilat ar-raḥim*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAFṢILU* — *yawm al-FAṢL* (Nabaʾ 78:17); *faṣīlah* (Maʿārij 70:13).** ⭐⭐ **FAMILY ON THE DAY: FLEE (80:34), RANSOM (70:11), SEPARATED (60:3).** ⭐ ***Raḥim* = UTERUS; *infiṣāl* = SEPARATION.**

**11. وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ — *wa'llāhu bimā taʿmalūna baṣīr*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** *baṣīr* — ⭐ **ALL-SEEING** (⭐ ***al-Baṣīr***; ⭐ **Taghābun 64:2**!).
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah sees what you do.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***BAṢĪR* — ALL-SEEING** — ⭐ **He SAW the letter written, sealed, and hidden** — ⭐ **TAGHĀBUN 64:2** — *wa'llāhu bimā taʿmalūna **baṣīr*** — ⭐ **identical.** ⭐ **You can hide a letter in a woman's hair — not from *al-Baṣīr*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bimā taʿmalūna*** fronted = emphasis on deeds.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **sight — of the hidden letter, and of every deed.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Wa'llāhu bimā taʿmalūna baṣīr* = Taghābun 64:2 — He SAW the hidden letter.** ⭐ **Hide it in a woman's hair — not from *al-Baṣīr*.**

## §2 — The Model of Ibrāhīm (v.4–6)

**12. قَدْ كَانَتْ لَكُمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ فِي إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَالَّذِينَ مَعَهُ — *qad kānat lakum uswatun ḥasanatun fī Ibrāhīma wa'lladhīna maʿah*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *uswah* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**MODEL to follow / CONSOLATION**" family — ***uswah* = an EXAMPLE, a ROLE MODEL** (one you follow and take comfort in); ⭐ ***taʾassā bi-* = to FOLLOW the example of** — and ⭐ ***taʾassā* = to CONSOLE oneself** (by seeing others went through the same!); ⭐ ***āsā* = GRIEF / sorrow** (⭐ *maʾsāh* = a **TRAGEDY**!; ⭐ ***maʾsāwī* = TRAGIC**); ⭐ ***muwāsāh* = CONSOLATION / sympathy / mutual support**. *ḥasanah* — ⭐ **GOOD / beautiful** (*ḥusn*). *Ibrāhīm* — ⭐ **ABRAHAM** (the *Khalīl* — Friend of Allah).
- **Here it means:** "**There has already been for you an excellent example in Ibrāhīm and those with him…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***USWATUN ḤASANAH* — AN EXCELLENT MODEL** — ⭐ **the phrase used ONLY THREE times in the Qurʾān:** ⭐ **Aḥzāb 33:21** — *la-qad kāna lakum fī rasūli'llāhi **uswatun ḥasanah*** — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ.** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:4** — ***uswatun ḥasanatun fī Ibrāhīm*** — ⭐ **Ibrāhīm.** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:6** — *la-qad kāna lakum **fīhim uswatun ḥasanah*** — ⭐ **Ibrāhīm and those with him (again!).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Only two people are called "*uswatun ḥasanah*" — MUHAMMAD ﷺ and IBRĀHĪM.** ⭐ **And Mumtaḥanah says it TWICE about Ibrāhīm.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***USWAH* — MODEL and CONSOLATION, one root** — ⭐ ***taʾassā bi-* = to FOLLOW; *taʾassā* = to CONSOLE oneself** — ⭐ **an *uswah* is someone whose example you FOLLOW and whose struggle COMFORTS you** — ⭐ **"he went through it too."** ⭐ **Ibrāhīm had to stand against his own FATHER and PEOPLE — exactly Ḥāṭib's dilemma (family vs faith).** ⭐ **For anyone torn between family and faith: Ibrāhīm walked that road first.** ⭐⭐ ***Maʾsāh* = a TRAGEDY; *muwāsāh* = CONSOLATION** (same root — the grief that a model consoles).
  ⭐⭐ ***Wa'lladhīna MAʿAH*** — ⭐ **"and those WITH him"** — ⭐ **his followers (Lūṭ, Sārah, and the believers)** — ⭐ **a COMMUNITY model, not just one hero.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***qad kānat*** = "**there HAS been**" (established). ⭐ ***uswatun*** = subject (feminine — *kānat*); ***ḥasanatun*** = adjective. ⚠️ ⭐ **Recitation variant: *iswatun*.** ⚠️ ⭐ ***Ibrāhīma*** = **diptote** (foreign name).
- **Why this exact word:** *uswah* — ⭐ **a model whose story consoles — exactly what a torn believer needs.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***USWATUN ḤASANAH* — ONLY 3 times: the PROPHET ﷺ (33:21), IBRĀHĪM (60:4, 60:6).** ⭐ **Only TWO people are called the excellent model.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***USWAH* = MODEL + CONSOLATION (*taʾassā* = to FOLLOW / to CONSOLE oneself) — "he went through it too."** ⭐⭐ **Ibrāhīm stood against his FATHER and PEOPLE — Ḥāṭib's dilemma, walked first.** ⭐⭐ ***Maʾsāh* = TRAGEDY; *muwāsāh* = CONSOLATION.** ⭐ ***Wa'lladhīna maʿah* = a COMMUNITY model.** ⚠️ **Variant *iswah*.**

**13. إِذْ قَالُوا لِقَوْمِهِمْ إِنَّا بُرَآءُ مِنكُمْ وَمِمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ — *idh qālū li-qawmihim innā buraʾāʾu minkum wa mimmā taʿbudūna min dūni'llāh*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *buraʾāʾ* — ⭐⭐⭐ **FREE / DISSOCIATED / innocent** (plural of ***barīʾ***; ⭐ ***barāʾah* = DISAVOWAL / INNOCENCE**; ⭐ ***Sūrat al-Barāʾah*** = at-Tawbah!; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:16** — Satan: *innī **barīʾun** minka*!; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:24** — ***al-Bāriʾ***!). *taʿbudūn* — ⭐ **you WORSHIP**. *min dūni'llāh* — ⭐ **BESIDES Allah**.
- **Here it means:** "**…when they said to their people: 'Indeed, we are free of you and of whatever you worship besides Allah…'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INNĀ BURAʾĀʾU MINKUM* — "we are FREE of you"** — ⭐ **the *barāʾah* (disavowal) of Ibrāhīm and his people.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And Ḥashr (the PREVIOUS surah) used the SAME root for the OPPOSITE kind of disavowal:** ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:16** — SATAN: *innī **BARĪʾUN** minka* — ⭐ **"I'm free of you" — ABANDONING his victim after leading him to disbelief (betrayal).** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:4** — IBRĀHĪM: *innā **BURAʾĀʾU** minkum* — ⭐ **"we're free of you" — DISOWNING idolatry to stay loyal to Allah (integrity).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same root, consecutive surahs: Satan's *barāʾah* abandons a person; Ibrāhīm's *barāʾah* rejects a falsehood.** ⭐ **And Ḥashr 59:24 — *al-BĀRIʾ* — the Creator.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Minkum WA MIMMĀ taʿbudūna*** — ⭐ **"free of YOU AND of what you WORSHIP"** — ⭐ **the dissociation is from their DISBELIEF and its objects** — ⭐ **(and v.7–8 make clear: this doesn't forbid kindness to peaceful people; it's about not sharing their worship or helping their war on faith).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Buraʾāʾ* — a rare plural of *barīʾ*** (⭐ like *ʿulamāʾ*, *fuqarāʾ* — *fuʿalāʾ* pattern).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***idh*** = "**when**" (*badal* of *Ibrāhīm* — "the example is: WHEN they said…"). ⚠️⭐ ***buraʾāʾu*** = ***fuʿalāʾ*** plural of *barīʾ* (diptote).
- **Why this exact word:** *barāʾah* — ⭐ **integrity's disavowal — set against Satan's treacherous one in the previous surah.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INNĀ BURAʾĀʾU MINKUM* = "we are FREE of you" — Ibrāhīm's DISAVOWAL of idolatry.** ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME ROOT, CONSECUTIVE SURAHS: Satan *barīʾ* (Ḥashr 59:16 — ABANDONS a person, betrayal) vs Ibrāhīm *buraʾāʾ* (Mumtaḥanah 60:4 — REJECTS falsehood, integrity) — and *al-Bāriʾ* (59:24).** ⭐⭐ **Free of YOU and what you WORSHIP — v.7–8 keep kindness to the peaceful.** ⭐ ***Sūrat al-Barāʾah* = at-Tawbah.** ⚠️ ***Buraʾāʾ* = *fuʿalāʾ* plural.**

**14. كَفَرْنَا بِكُمْ وَبَدَا بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَكُمُ الْعَدَاوَةُ وَالْبَغْضَاءُ أَبَدًا حَتَّىٰ تُؤْمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ وَحْدَهُ — *kafarnā bikum wa badā baynanā wa baynakumu'l-ʿadāwatu wa'l-baghḍāʾu abadan ḥattā tuʾminū bi'llāhi waḥdah*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *kafarnā bikum* — ⭐ **we REJECT you** (*kufr* — here = **deny/disown**). *badā* — ⭐⭐ the "**APPEARING / becoming visible / open country**" family — *badā* = **to APPEAR, become manifest**; ⭐ ***bādiyah* = the DESERT / open country** (where everything is VISIBLE!; ⭐ ***badawī* = a BEDOUIN**!); ⭐ ***ibdāʾ* = EXPRESSING / disclosing** (⭐ *ibdāʾ ar-raʾy* = **expressing an OPINION**!); ⭐ ***bādī* = APPARENT**; ⭐ ⚠️ **different from *badaʾa* (hamzah) = to BEGIN** (⭐ *mabdaʾ* = principle!). *ʿadāwah* — ⭐ **ENMITY** (*ʿaduww*). *baghḍāʾ* — ⭐⭐ the "**HATRED / intense dislike**" family — ***bughḍ* = HATRED**; ⭐ ***baghḍāʾ* = HATRED / loathing** (*faʿlāʾ* — intense); ⭐ ***mabghūḍ* = HATED / disliked**; ⭐ ***abghaḍ* = MOST hateful** (⭐ *abghaḍu'l-ḥalāli ila'llāhi aṭ-ṭalāq* — the famous (debated) narration — **Ṭalāq**!). *waḥdah* — ⭐⭐ **ALONE** (*w-ḥ-d* — ⭐ ***tawḥīd***!; ⭐ ***wāḥid* = ONE**; ⭐ ***waḥdah* = UNITY / a UNIT** — *waḥdat al-ʿināyah al-murakkazah* = **ICU**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…we reject you, and there has appeared between us and you enmity and hatred forever — until you believe in Allah ALONE.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE FIRMEST LINE — with a DOOR LEFT OPEN.** ⭐ ***BADĀ baynanā wa baynakumu'l-ʿADĀWATU wa'l-BAGHḌĀʾ ABADĀ*** — ⭐ **"ENMITY and HATRED have APPEARED between us FOREVER"** — ⭐ **the strongest possible separation** — ⭐ ***ḤATTĀ TUʾMINŪ BI'LLĀHI WAḤDAH*** — ⭐ **"UNTIL you believe in Allah ALONE."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Abadā* (FOREVER) + *ḥattā* (UNTIL)** — ⭐ **"forever — unless you change"** — ⭐ **the enmity is toward the SHIRK, not the PERSON** — ⭐ **the moment they embrace *tawḥīd*, the "forever" ends.** ⭐ **(And v.7 — *ʿasā'llāhu an yajʿala baynakum… mawaddah* — love may yet come!)**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAḤDAH* — "ALONE" — the *TAWḤĪD* word** — ⭐ **not just "believe in Allah" (the Quraysh believed in Allah!) — believe in Allah ALONE.** ⭐ **The whole dispute is the word *waḥdah*.** ⭐ ***Waḥdat al-ʿināyah al-murakkazah* = ICU** (clinical!) — **a "unit."**
  ⭐⭐ ***BADĀ* — APPEARED openly** — ⭐ ***bādiyah* = the DESERT** (everything visible!); ⭐ ***badawī* = a BEDOUIN**; ⭐ ***ibdāʾ ar-raʾy* = expressing an OPINION.** ⭐ **Ibrāhīm's disavowal was OPEN — not a secret letter (like Ḥāṭib's)!** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāṭib: SECRET affection (*tusirrūna*, v.1). Ibrāhīm: OPEN disavowal (*badā*, v.4).** ⭐ **The model is the opposite of the mistake.** ⚠️ ***Badā* (appear) ≠ *badaʾa* (begin).**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Baghḍāʾ* — HATRED** — ⭐ ***mabghūḍ* = HATED.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***badā*** = **masculine verb for feminine *ʿadāwah*** (allowed — separated, non-real feminine). ⭐⭐ ***abadan ḥattā*** — "**forever — UNTIL**" (a limit on "forever"). ⚠️ ⭐ ***al-baghḍāʾu*** = **diptote** (*faʿlāʾ*).
- **Why this exact word:** *waḥdah* — ⭐ **the single word that defines the whole dispute: belief in Allah ALONE.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ENMITY and HATRED FOREVER (*abadā*) — UNTIL (*ḥattā*) you believe in Allah ALONE — the door stays OPEN.** ⭐ **Enmity toward the SHIRK, not the person.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAḤDAH* = ALONE — the TAWḤĪD word; the Quraysh believed in Allah — not ALONE.** ⭐ ***Waḥdat al-ʿināyah al-murakkazah* = ICU.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāṭib: SECRET affection (*tusirrūna*, v.1) vs Ibrāhīm: OPEN disavowal (*badā*, v.4).** ⭐⭐ ***Bādiyah* = DESERT; *badawī* = BEDOUIN; *ibdāʾ ar-raʾy* = expressing an OPINION.** ⚠️ ***Badā* (APPEAR) ≠ *badaʾa* (BEGIN).** ⭐ ***Mabghūḍ* = HATED.**

**15. إِلَّا قَوْلَ إِبْرَاهِيمَ لِأَبِيهِ لَأَسْتَغْفِرَنَّ لَكَ وَمَا أَمْلِكُ لَكَ مِنَ اللَّهِ مِن شَيْءٍ — *illā qawla Ibrāhīma li-abīhi la-astaghfiranna laka wa mā amliku laka mina'llāhi min shayʾ*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *illā* — ⭐ **EXCEPT**. *abīhi* — ⭐ **his FATHER** (Āzar, per 6:74). *la-astaghfiranna* — ⭐ **I WILL SURELY seek forgiveness** (Form X + *lām* of oath + heavy *nūn*; ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:10**, **Munāfiqūn 63:5–6**!). *amliku* — ⭐ **I OWN / have power** (*m-l-k*; ⭐ **Jinn 72:21** — *lā **amliku** lakum ḍarran wa lā rashadā*!; ⭐ **Infiṭār 82:19** — *lā **tamliku** nafsun li-nafsin shayʾā*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…except for Ibrāhīm's saying to his father: 'I will surely ask forgiveness for you — but I have no power [to do anything] for you against Allah.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ONE EXCEPTION — "follow Ibrāhīm in EVERYTHING… EXCEPT this."** ⭐ **Ibrāhīm promised his idol-worshipping father: *la-astaghfiranna lak*** — ⭐ **"I'll SURELY seek forgiveness for you"** (a son's love) — ⭐ **this ONE act is NOT part of the model to imitate** (for a disbeliever who died on disbelief). ⭐⭐ **Why? At-Tawbah 9:114** — *wa mā kāna'stighfāru Ibrāhīma li-abīhi illā ʿan mawʿidatin waʿadahā iyyāh, fa-lammā tabayyana lahu annahu ʿaduwwun li'llāhi tabarraʾa minh* — ⭐ **"Ibrāhīm's prayer for his father was only due to a PROMISE he'd made; when it became clear he was an enemy of Allah, he DISOWNED him (*tabarraʾa* — the *b-r-ʾ* root again!)."** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān carefully marks what NOT to copy from even the greatest model.** ⭐ **Precision in imitation.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA MĀ AMLIKU LAKA MINA'LLĀHI MIN SHAYʾ* — "I OWN NOTHING for you against Allah"** — ⭐ **JINN 72:21** — the Prophet ﷺ: *qul innī lā **amliku** lakum ḍarran wa lā rashadā* — ⭐ **"I own NO harm or guidance for you."** ⭐ **INFIṬĀR 82:19** — *yawma lā **tamliku** nafsun li-nafsin shayʾā* — ⭐ **"the Day no soul OWNS anything for another."** ⭐⭐ **Even Ibrāhīm couldn't save his father; even the Prophet ﷺ couldn't save Abū Ṭālib** (⭐ and Taḥrīm 66:10 — Nūḥ and Lūṭ couldn't save their wives — *fa-lam yughniyā ʿanhumā mina'llāhi shayʾā*!). ⭐ **The *mā aghnā* / *lā amliku* thread: love can PRAY but cannot RESCUE.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***illā qawla*** = **exception** from *uswah* ("an example in everything **except** his saying"). ⭐ ***la-astaghfiranna*** = oath + heavy *nūn*. ⭐⭐ ***min shayʾin*** — ***min*** + indefinite = "**anything at all**."
- **Why this exact word:** *illā* — ⭐ **an exception carved precisely out of the perfect model.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FOLLOW IBRĀHĪM IN EVERYTHING — *ILLĀ* (EXCEPT) his promise to pray for his idolatrous father.** ⭐⭐ **9:114: it was a PROMISE; when it became clear, he DISOWNED (*tabarraʾa*) him.** ⭐ **Precision in imitation.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MĀ AMLIKU LAKA… MIN SHAYʾ* = I OWN NOTHING for you — Jinn 72:21 (*lā amliku*), Infiṭār 82:19 (*lā tamliku*), Taḥrīm 66:10 (*fa-lam yughniyā*).** ⭐ **Love can PRAY, cannot RESCUE.**

**16. رَّبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ — *rabbanā ʿalayka tawakkalnā wa ilayka anabnā wa ilayka'l-maṣīr*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *tawakkalnā* — ⭐⭐ **we RELIED** (*tawakkul*; ⭐ ***wakīl* = AGENT / LAWYER**; ⭐ **Mulk 67:29** — *wa ʿalayhi tawakkalnā*!; ⭐ **Taghābun 64:13**!). *anabnā* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**TURNING BACK repeatedly / taking turns**" family — *nāba* = **to come back again and again, to take one's TURN, to substitute**; ⭐ ***anāba* (Form IV) = to TURN to (Allah) in repentance, repeatedly**; ⭐ ***inābah* = turning back to Allah (constantly)**; ⭐⭐ ***nawbah* = a TURN / SHIFT / an ATTACK (of illness)** (⭐ *nawbah qalbiyyah* = a **HEART ATTACK**!; ⭐ *nawbah* = a **work SHIFT**!; ⭐ *nawbat ṣarʿ* = an **EPILEPTIC SEIZURE** — clinical!); ⭐ ***nāʾib* = a DEPUTY / representative** (⭐ *nāʾib ar-raʾīs* = **VICE-PRESIDENT**!; ⭐ *nāʾib* = an **MP**!); ⭐ ***niyābah* = PROSECUTION / deputyship** (⭐ *an-niyābah al-ʿāmmah* = **PUBLIC PROSECUTION**!); ⭐ ***munīb* = one who turns (to Allah)**. *maṣīr* — ⭐ **DESTINATION** (*ṣāra* = to become; ⭐ **Taghābun 64:3, 10**!).
- **Here it means:** "**Our Lord, upon You we have relied, and to You we have turned, and to You is the destination.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **IBRĀHĪM'S DUʿĀʾ — THREE FRONTINGS, three exclusive devotions:** ⭐ ***ʿALAYKA tawakkalnā*** — "**upon YOU [alone] we RELY**" (TRUST) ⭐ ***wa ILAYKA anabnā*** — "**to YOU [alone] we TURN**" (REPENTANCE) ⭐ ***wa ILAYKA'l-maṣīr*** — "**to YOU [alone] is the DESTINATION**" (RETURN). ⭐⭐⭐ **Trust in the PRESENT, repentance from the PAST, return in the FUTURE — all to HIM ALONE.** ⭐ **After cutting ties with his people (v.4a), Ibrāhīm needs somewhere to stand — and it's this.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ANĀBA* — TURNING BACK AGAIN AND AGAIN** — ⭐ ***nawbah* = a TURN / SHIFT** — ⭐ **like returning for your turn, again and again** — ⭐ **the *munīb* doesn't repent once — he keeps coming back.** ⭐⭐ **Clinical hooks: *NAWBAH QALBIYYAH* = HEART ATTACK; *nawbat ṣarʿ* = SEIZURE** — ⭐ **an "episode" that RECURS — the same root!** ⭐ ***Nāʾib* = DEPUTY / MP; *nāʾib ar-raʾīs* = VICE-PRESIDENT; *an-niyābah al-ʿāmmah* = PUBLIC PROSECUTION.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***TAWAKKUL* — FIVE surahs now:** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:9** (*wakīlā* — command), **Mulk 67:29** (*ʿalayhi tawakkalnā* — fronted!), **Ṭalāq 65:3** (*fa-huwa ḥasbuh* — result), **Taghābun 64:13** (*fal-yatawakkal* — command to all), **Mumtaḥanah 60:4** (***ʿalayka tawakkalnā*** — **Ibrāhīm's own duʿāʾ, fronted**). ⭐ **Mulk 67:29 and Mumtaḥanah 60:4 — the same fronting (*ʿalayhi / ʿalayka*).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ilayka'l-MAṢĪR*** — ⭐ **Taghābun 64:3** — *wa ilayhi'l-maṣīr* — ⭐ ***maṣīr* ← *ṣāra* (BECOME).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE FRONTINGS** (*ʿalayka*, *ilayka*, *ilayka*) = **three restrictions**. ⭐ ***rabbanā*** = vocative.
- **Why this exact word:** **the triple fronting** — ⭐ **total exclusivity of trust, repentance, and return — the anchor after cutting ties.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **IBRĀHĪM'S DUʿĀʾ — THREE FRONTINGS: upon YOU we RELY (present), to YOU we TURN (past), to YOU is the DESTINATION (future) — all to HIM ALONE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ANĀBA* = turning back AGAIN AND AGAIN — *NAWBAH* = a TURN / SHIFT / *NAWBAH QALBIYYAH* = HEART ATTACK / *nawbat ṣarʿ* = SEIZURE (a recurring episode!).** ⭐⭐ ***Nāʾib* = DEPUTY / MP; *nāʾib ar-raʾīs* = VICE-PRESIDENT; *an-niyābah al-ʿāmmah* = PUBLIC PROSECUTION.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TAWAKKUL* ×5: 73:9, 67:29, 65:3, 64:13, 60:4 — Mulk & Mumtaḥanah both FRONT it.** ⭐ ***Maṣīr* — Taghābun 64:3.**

**17. رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا رَبَّنَا ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ — *rabbanā lā tajʿalnā fitnatan li'lladhīna kafarū wa'ghfir lanā rabbanā · innaka anta'l-ʿazīzu'l-ḥakīm*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *fitnah* — ⭐⭐⭐ **a TRIAL / TEST** (⭐ ***fatana* = to smelt GOLD in FIRE**; ⭐ **Taghābun 64:15** — *innamā amwālukum wa awlādukum **fitnah***!; ⭐ **Burūj 85:10** — *fatanū*!). *ighfir* — ⭐ **FORGIVE**. *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* — ⭐ **the ALMIGHTY, the WISE**.
- **Here it means:** "**Our Lord, do not make us a trial for those who disbelieve, and forgive us, our Lord. Indeed, You are the Almighty, the Wise.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ TAJʿALNĀ FITNATAN LI'LLADHĪNA KAFARŪ* — "don't make US a TRIAL for the disbelievers"** — ⭐ **a profound duʿāʾ with two classical meanings:** ⭐ **(1) don't let them DEFEAT us — so they think "we must be right, since we beat them" (our defeat becomes THEIR *fitnah* — a test they fail).** ⭐ **(2) don't let our SINS and weaknesses become THEIR reason to reject Islam** — ⭐ **"don't let US be the obstacle between them and the truth."** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāṭib's slip could have harmed the whole mission; Ibrāhīm prays never to be such a stumbling block.** ⭐ **The Muslim as ambassador: your conduct can be someone else's *fitnah*.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***FITNAH* — gold in the furnace — TAGHĀBUN 64:15** — *amwālukum wa awlādukum **fitnah*** (your wealth/children are a TEST for YOU) ⭐ **→ Mumtaḥanah 60:5** — *lā tajʿalnā **fitnatan** li'lladhīna kafarū* (don't make US a TEST for THEM). ⭐⭐ **Consecutive surahs: the things that test you / you as a test for others.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Wa'GHFIR LANĀ*** — ⭐ **the *istighfār* thread** — ⭐ **Ibrāhīm, the Friend of Allah, still asks forgiveness** (⭐ **Nūḥ 71:28** — *rabbi'ghfir lī*; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — *atmim lanā nūranā wa'ghfir lanā*).
  ⭐⭐ ***Innaka anta'l-ʿAZĪZU'L-ḤAKĪM*** — ⭐ **ALMIGHTY (protect us from defeat) + WISE (choose what's best)** — ⭐ **the Ḥashr/Jumuʿah/Taghābun Name-pair.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā tajʿalnā*** = negative supplication + **two objects** (*-nā*, *fitnatan*). ⭐ ***anta*** = separating pronoun (restriction).
- **Why this exact word:** *fitnah* — ⭐ **the believer's fear of becoming someone else's stumbling block.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ TAJʿALNĀ FITNATAN LI'LLADHĪNA KAFARŪ* = don't make US a TRIAL for them — (1) don't let them DEFEAT us ("we must be right"); (2) don't let OUR SINS block them from Islam.** ⭐ **Your conduct can be someone else's *fitnah*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Taghābun 64:15: things that test YOU / Mumtaḥanah 60:5: YOU as a test for OTHERS.** ⭐⭐ ***Wa'ghfir lanā* — Nūḥ 71:28, Taḥrīm 66:8.** ⭐ ***Al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*.**

**18. لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِيهِمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرْجُو اللَّهَ وَالْيَوْمَ الْآخِرَ — *la-qad kāna lakum fīhim uswatun ḥasanatun li-man kāna yarju'llāha wa'l-yawma'l-ākhir*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *uswah ḥasanah* — ⭐ **an EXCELLENT MODEL** (v.4!). *yarjū* — ⭐⭐ the "**HOPE / EXPECT**" family — *rajā* = to hope, to expect; ⭐ ***rajāʾ* = HOPE** (⭐ ***ar-rajāʾ!* = "PLEASE!"** — on signs!); ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:13** — *mā lakum lā **tarjūna** li'llāhi waqārā*!; ⭐ ***marjūw* = HOPED-FOR / requested**.
- **Here it means:** "**There has certainly been for you in them an excellent example — for whoever hopes in Allah and the Last Day…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MODEL REPEATED (v.4, v.6) — but now with a CONDITION:** ⭐ ***Li-man kāna YARJU'LLĀHA wa'l-YAWMA'L-ĀKHIR*** — ⭐ **"for whoever HOPES in Allah and the Last Day"** — ⭐ **the model only works for someone who's LOOKING FORWARD to meeting Allah.** ⭐⭐⭐ **AḤZĀB 33:21** — *la-qad kāna lakum fī rasūli'llāhi uswatun ḥasanatun **li-man kāna yarju'llāha wa'l-yawma'l-ākhir** wa dhakara'llāha kathīrā* — ⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD the same condition for the Prophet ﷺ as a model!** ⭐ **Both models — Ibrāhīm and Muhammad ﷺ — are for those with HOPE in the Hereafter.** ⭐⭐ **Why repeat *uswah* (v.4 and v.6)?** ⭐ Classical: **v.4 — the model in DISAVOWAL (cutting ties with falsehood); v.6 — the model in DUʿĀʾ and TAWAKKUL (v.4b–5).** ⭐ **Or: emphasis — "I REALLY mean it: follow him."**
  ⭐⭐ ***YARJŪ* — HOPE** — ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:13** — *mā lakum lā **tarjūna** li'llāhi waqārā* (why don't you HOPE for / reckon with Allah's dignity?). ⭐ ***Ar-rajāʾ!* = PLEASE** (on signs).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***la-qad*** = oath + certainty (stronger than v.4's *qad*!). ⭐ ***li-man*** = ***badal*** of *lakum* (specifying WHICH "you").
- **Why this exact word:** *yarjū* — ⭐ **the model works only for those whose hope is pinned on the Hereafter.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **MODEL REPEATED (v.4, v.6) + CONDITION: *li-man kāna YARJU'LLĀHA wa'l-YAWMA'L-ĀKHIR* — for those who HOPE in Allah and the Last Day.** ⭐⭐⭐ **= AḤZĀB 33:21 WORD-FOR-WORD — the same condition for the PROPHET ﷺ as model.** ⭐⭐ **v.4 model in DISAVOWAL / v.6 model in DUʿĀʾ & TAWAKKUL.** ⭐ ***La-qad* (v.6) stronger than *qad* (v.4).** ⭐ ***Rajāʾ* — Nūḥ 71:13; *ar-rajāʾ!* = PLEASE.**

**19. وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الْغَنِيُّ الْحَمِيدُ — *wa man yatawalla fa-inna'llāha huwa'l-ghaniyyu'l-ḥamīd*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *yatawalla* — ⭐ **TURNS AWAY** (*tawallā*; ⭐ **Taghābun 64:6, 12** — *tawallaw / tawallaytum*!; ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:1**). *al-Ghaniyy* — ⭐⭐⭐ **the FREE OF ALL NEED** (⭐ **Taghābun 64:6** — *wa'staghna'llāhu wa'llāhu ghaniyyun ḥamīd*!). *al-Ḥamīd* — ⭐ **the PRAISEWORTHY** (*ḥamd*).
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever turns away — then indeed Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD with TAGHĀBUN 64:6!** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:6** — *fa-kafarū wa **tawallaw** wa'staghna'llāh · **wa'llāhu ghaniyyun ḥamīd*** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:6** — *wa man **yatawalla** fa-inna'llāha **huwa'l-ghaniyyu'l-ḥamīd*** ⭐⭐⭐ **The same response to the same refusal — two surahs apart: TURN AWAY → He doesn't need you.** ⭐ **If you won't follow Ibrāhīm's model — Allah loses nothing; YOU lose.** ⭐⭐ ***Huwa* — SEPARATING pronoun** — ⭐ **"HE — HE ALONE — is the Free of need"** (stronger than Taghābun's version!).
  ⭐⭐ ***AL-GHANIYY AL-ḤAMĪD*** — ⭐ **RICH + PRAISEWORTHY** — ⭐ **many rich are not praiseworthy; He is both** (Taghābun lesson).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** + jussive (*yatawalla* — final *ā* drops) + ***fa-inna*** (answer). ⭐⭐ ***huwa*** = *ḍamīr al-faṣl* (restriction).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the Taghābun answer, repeated — refusal costs only the refuser.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA MAN YATAWALLA FA-INNA'LLĀHA HUWA'L-GHANIYYU'L-ḤAMĪD* = TAGHĀBUN 64:6 WORD-FOR-WORD (+ emphatic *huwa*).** ⭐ **Turn away — He loses NOTHING; YOU lose.** ⭐⭐ ***Al-Ghaniyy al-Ḥamīd* = RICH + PRAISEWORTHY.** ⭐ ***Tawallā* — Taghābun 64:6, 12; ʿAbasa 80:1.**

## §3 — Perhaps There Will Be Love (v.7)

**20. عَسَى اللَّهُ أَن يَجْعَلَ بَيْنَكُمْ وَبَيْنَ الَّذِينَ عَادَيْتُم مِّنْهُم مَّوَدَّةً — *ʿasa'llāhu an yajʿala baynakum wa bayna'lladhīna ʿādaytum minhum mawaddah*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *ʿasā* — ⭐ **PERHAPS** (from Allah = a promise!). *ʿādaytum* — ⭐ **you have been HOSTILE to** (Form III of *ʿadā* — mutual enmity; ⭐ v.1 *ʿaduww*, v.4 *ʿadāwah*). *mawaddah* — ⭐⭐⭐ **AFFECTION / love** (⭐ v.1 *bi'l-mawaddah*!; ⭐ ***al-Wadūd***).
- **Here it means:** "**Perhaps Allah will put affection between you and those of them with whom you are now at enmity.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST HOPEFUL VERSE IN THE SURAH — right after the hardest lines.** ⭐ **v.1–6: don't ally with them; enmity "forever" (v.4).** ⭐ **v.7: *ʿASA'LLĀHU an yajʿala baynakum… MAWADDAH*** — ⭐ **"PERHAPS Allah will put LOVE between you."** ⭐⭐⭐ **And it HAPPENED — two years later, at the CONQUEST OF MAKKAH (8 AH), the Quraysh embraced Islam en masse** — ⭐ **the enemies of v.1 became the brothers of the Muslims.** ⭐ **Abū Sufyān — the leader of the enemy — became Muslim; his daughter Umm Ḥabībah was already married to the Prophet ﷺ** (classically cited as part of this verse's fulfilment). ⭐ **Classical: *ʿasā* from Allah is WĀJIB (certain).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *MAWADDAH* ARC — in ONE surah:** ⭐ **v.1** — *tulqūna ilayhim **bi'l-mawaddah*** — ⭐ **affection given WRONGLY (secretly, to active enemies, at the cost of the mission).** ⭐ **v.2** — *wa **waddū** law takfurūn* — ⭐ **their "wish" against you.** ⭐ **v.7** — *an yajʿala baynakum… **mawaddah*** — ⭐ **affection given RIGHTLY — by ALLAH, when He brings them to faith.** ⭐⭐ **Ḥāṭib tried to MAKE affection happen by betrayal; Allah would MAKE it happen by guidance.** ⭐ **Wait for His way.**
  ⭐⭐ ***ʿĀDAYTUM* — Form III (MUTUAL enmity)** — ⭐ **"those you've been at enmity WITH"** — ⭐ **even mutual, deep enmity can be transformed.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿasā*** + *an* + subjunctive. ⭐ ***mawaddatan*** = object (delayed to the END — the surprise!).
- **Why this exact word:** *mawaddah* — ⭐ **the same word as Ḥāṭib's misplaced affection, now promised the right way.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿASA'LLĀHU AN YAJʿALA BAYNAKUM… MAWADDAH* = PERHAPS Allah will put LOVE between you and your enemies — FULFILLED at the CONQUEST OF MAKKAH (8 AH).** ⭐ ***ʿAsā* from Allah = CERTAIN.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAWADDAH* ARC: v.1 given WRONGLY (betrayal) → v.2 THEIR wish against you → v.7 given RIGHTLY (by Allah, through guidance).** ⭐ **Ḥāṭib tried to force it; Allah would GIVE it.** ⭐⭐ **Abū Sufyān; Umm Ḥabībah.**

**21. وَاللَّهُ قَدِيرٌ ۚ وَاللَّهُ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ — *wa'llāhu qadīr · wa'llāhu ghafūrun raḥīm*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *qadīr* — ⭐ **ALL-POWERFUL**. *ghafūr raḥīm* — ⭐ **FORGIVING, MERCIFUL**.
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah is All-Powerful; and Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO REASONS the hope (v.7a) is REAL:** ⭐ ***Wa'llāhu QADĪR*** — ⭐ **He has the POWER to turn enemies into friends** — ⭐ **hearts are in His hand** (⭐ *law anfaqta mā fi'l-arḍi jamīʿan mā allafta bayna qulūbihim wa lākinna'llāha allafa baynahum* — 8:63 — "had you spent all on earth you couldn't have UNITED their hearts — but ALLAH did!"). ⭐ ***Wa'llāhu GHAFŪRUN RAḤĪM*** — ⭐ **He FORGIVES their past enmity** (once they believe) — ⭐ **and FORGIVES Ḥāṭib's slip** (v.1!). ⭐⭐ ***Wa'llāhu* REPEATED** — ⭐ **two separate sentences — two separate grounds for hope.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **two nominal sentences** — each opening ***wa'llāhu*** (emphasis).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **power to change hearts + mercy to forgive the past — the basis of v.7's hope.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO REASONS the hope is REAL: *QADĪR* (POWER to turn enemies into friends — 8:63: "ALLAH united their hearts") + *GHAFŪRUN RAḤĪM* (FORGIVES their past — and Ḥāṭib's slip).** ⭐ ***Wa'llāhu* ×2 — two grounds.**

## §4 — Kindness and Justice (v.8–9)

**22. لَّا يَنْهَاكُمُ اللَّهُ عَنِ الَّذِينَ لَمْ يُقَاتِلُوكُمْ فِي الدِّينِ وَلَمْ يُخْرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَارِكُمْ — *lā yanhākumu'llāhu ʿani'lladhīna lam yuqātilūkum fi'd-dīni wa lam yukhrijūkum min diyārikum*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *lā yanhākum* — ⭐ **does NOT FORBID you** (*nahy*; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:7** — *wa mā nahākum ʿanhu fa'ntahū*!). *yuqātilūkum* — ⭐ **FIGHT you** (Form III). *fi'd-dīn* — ⭐ **because of [your] RELIGION**. *yukhrijūkum* — ⭐ **EXPEL you** (⭐ v.1!). *diyār* — ⭐ **HOMES**.
- **Here it means:** "**Allah does not forbid you from those who have not fought you because of religion and have not expelled you from your homes…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE GREAT CLARIFICATION — so v.1–6 isn't misread.** ⭐ **v.1 forbade allying with those who (a) FIGHT you over religion and (b) EXPEL you.** ⭐ **v.8: those who DIDN'T fight you and DIDN'T expel you — Allah does NOT forbid you from them.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The two criteria are EXACTLY the two charges of v.1 (*yukhrijūna'r-rasūla wa iyyākum*).** ⭐ **The hostility of v.1–6 was never about religion difference ALONE — it was about AGGRESSION.** ⭐⭐ **A principle of Islamic international relations — peace with the peaceful.**
  ⭐⭐ ***LĀ YANHĀKUM* — "does NOT FORBID"** — ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:7** — *wa mā **nahākum** ʿanhu fa'ntahū* (whatever the Messenger FORBIDS, stop) ⭐ **→ here Allah tells you what He does NOT forbid.** ⭐ **Knowing what's NOT forbidden matters as much as knowing what is.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā yanhākumu'llāhu ʿan*** = "**Allah doesn't forbid you regarding**." ⭐ ***lam yuqātilūkum… wa lam yukhrijūkum*** — two negated conditions (both must hold).
- **Why this exact word:** **the two criteria** — ⭐ **mirroring v.1's charges exactly, so the ruling is precise.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ YANHĀKUMU'LLĀH* — Allah does NOT forbid you regarding those who DIDN'T FIGHT you over religion and DIDN'T EXPEL you.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The two criteria = v.1's two charges EXACTLY — the issue was AGGRESSION, not religious difference alone.** ⭐ **Peace with the peaceful.** ⭐⭐ **Ḥashr 59:7: what's FORBIDDEN / Mumtaḥanah 60:8: what's NOT.**

**23. أَن تَبَرُّوهُمْ وَتُقْسِطُوا إِلَيْهِمْ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ الْمُقْسِطِينَ — *an tabarrūhum wa tuqsiṭū ilayhim · inna'llāha yuḥibbu'l-muqsiṭīn*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *tabarrūhum* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**GOODNESS / VAST KINDNESS / dry LAND**" family — *barra* = to be good, dutiful, kind; ⭐⭐ ***birr* = RIGHTEOUSNESS / generous KINDNESS** (⭐ ***birr al-wālidayn* = DUTIFULNESS to PARENTS**!; ⭐ *laysa'l-birra an tuwallū wujūhakum* — 2:177!); ⭐ ***barr* = the dry LAND** (as opposed to sea — vast, open! — ⭐ *barran wa baḥran* = **by land and sea**!); ⭐ ***al-Barr*** = a Name — the Most Kind (52:28); ⭐ ***abrār* = the RIGHTEOUS** (⭐ **Infiṭār 82:13** — *inna'l-**abrāra** la-fī naʿīm*!; ⭐ **Muṭaffifīn 83:18, 22**; ⭐ **Insān 76:5**!); ⭐ ***barīyah*** — wilderness; ⭐ ***mabarrah* = a CHARITY / good deed**. *tuqsiṭū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**JUST SHARE / portion**" family — ***qisṭ* = JUSTICE / a just SHARE** (⭐ ***qisṭ* = an INSTALMENT**!; ⭐ *bi'l-aqsāṭ* = **in INSTALMENTS**!); ⭐⭐ ***aqsaṭa* (Form IV) = to ACT JUSTLY** → ⭐ ***muqsiṭ* = JUST**; ⭐⭐⭐ BUT ***qasaṭa* (Form I) = to DEVIATE / be UNJUST** → ⭐ ***qāsiṭ* = an UNJUST DEVIATOR** (⭐ **JINN 72:14–15** — *wa minna'l-**qāsiṭūn** · wa ammā'l-**qāsiṭūna** fa-kānū li-jahannama ḥaṭabā*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…from being kind to them and acting justly toward them. Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NOT JUST "ALLOWED" — TWO VIRTUES COMMANDED IN SPIRIT: *BIRR* and *QISṬ*.** ⭐ ***TABARRŪHUM*** — ⭐ **show them *BIRR* — the same word used for KINDNESS TO PARENTS (*birr al-wālidayn*)!** ⭐ **Not grudging tolerance — generous, warm goodness.** ⭐ ***TUQSIṬŪ ilayhim*** — ⭐ **give them their just SHARE — *qisṭ*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The ḥadīth (Bukhārī, Muslim): ASMĀʾ bint Abī Bakr's mother — a non-Muslim — came to visit her in Madīnah; Asmāʾ asked the Prophet ﷺ: "Should I be kind to her?" He said: "*Yes — keep ties with your mother.*"** ⭐ **Classically cited with this verse.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***BIRR* — the *ABRĀR* thread:** ⭐ **Infiṭār 82:13** — *inna'l-**abrāra** la-fī naʿīm* (the RIGHTEOUS in bliss). ⭐ **Muṭaffifīn 83:18, 22** — *kitāba'l-abrār* / *inna'l-abrāra*. ⭐ **Insān 76:5** — *inna'l-**abrāra** yashrabūna min kaʾs*. ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:8** — ***tabarrūhum*** — ⭐ **the *abrār*'s quality — extended even to peaceful non-Muslims.** ⭐⭐ ***Barr* = dry LAND (vast, open) — *birr* = VAST goodness.** ⭐ ***Birr al-wālidayn*** — the highest human *birr* — ⭐ **and THAT word is used here.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AQSAṬA* (JUST) vs *QASAṬA* (UNJUST) — the GREAT PARADOX of Arabic morphology!** ⭐ **Form I *qasaṭa* = to DEVIATE / be UNJUST** — ⭐ **JINN 72:14–15** — *minna'l-**QĀSIṬŪN*** (among us are the DEVIATORS) — ***fa-kānū li-jahannama ḥaṭabā*** (FIREWOOD for Hell)! ⭐ **Form IV *aqsaṭa* = to be JUST** — ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:8** — *inna'llāha yuḥibbu'l-**MUQSIṬĪN*** (Allah LOVES the JUST)! ⭐⭐⭐ **The Form IV *hamzah* REMOVES the meaning (*hamzat as-salb* — the "stripping" hamzah): *qasaṭa* = to be unjust → *aqsaṭa* = to REMOVE injustice = to be just.** ⭐ **One letter flips Hell's firewood into Allah's beloved.** ⭐ ***Qisṭ* = INSTALMENT (*bi'l-aqsāṭ*) — paying a fair share at a time.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***an tabarrūhum*** = *badal* (what's not forbidden: "being kind to them"). ⭐ ***tabarrū*** = doubled verb. ⚠️⭐⭐⭐ ***muqsiṭīn* (Form IV — JUST) ≠ *qāsiṭūn* (Form I — UNJUST)** — ⭐ ***hamzat as-salb*** (privative hamzah).
- **Why this exact word:** *birr* — ⭐ **the word for kindness to parents, extended to peaceful neighbours of another faith.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TABARRŪHUM WA TUQSIṬŪ ILAYHIM* = be KIND (*birr*) and JUST (*qisṭ*) to peaceful non-Muslims.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BIRR* = the word for *BIRR AL-WĀLIDAYN* (dutifulness to PARENTS) — not grudging tolerance, generous goodness.** ⭐⭐ **ASMĀʾ's non-Muslim mother: "Yes — keep ties with your mother" (Bukhārī, Muslim).** ⭐⭐ ***Abrār*: Infiṭār 82:13, Muṭaffifīn 83:18, Insān 76:5; *barr* = dry LAND.** ⭐⭐⭐ **PARADOX: *QĀSIṬŪN* (Form I — UNJUST — Jinn 72:14–15, Hell's FIREWOOD) vs *MUQSIṬĪN* (Form IV — JUST — Allah LOVES them) — the "stripping" hamzah (*hamzat as-salb*).** ⭐ ***Qisṭ* = INSTALMENT.**

**24. إِنَّمَا يَنْهَاكُمُ اللَّهُ عَنِ الَّذِينَ قَاتَلُوكُمْ فِي الدِّينِ وَأَخْرَجُوكُم مِّن دِيَارِكُمْ وَظَاهَرُوا عَلَىٰ إِخْرَاجِكُمْ أَن تَوَلَّوْهُمْ — *innamā yanhākumu'llāhu ʿani'lladhīna qātalūkum fi'd-dīni wa akhrajūkum min diyārikum wa ẓāharū ʿalā ikhrājikum an tawallawhum*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *innamā* — ⭐ **ONLY**. *qātalūkum* — ⭐ **FOUGHT you**. *akhrajūkum* — ⭐ **EXPELLED you**. *ẓāharū* — ⭐⭐⭐ **BACKED [others] / SUPPORTED** (Form III of *ẓahara* ← ***ẓahr* = the BACK**; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:4** — *wa in **taẓāharā** ʿalayh* — two wives "backing each other"!; ⭐ ***muẓāharah* = a DEMONSTRATION / PROTEST**!; ⭐ ***ẓahīr* = a SUPPORTER**). *ikhrāj* — ⭐ **EXPULSION** (⭐ ***ikhrāj* = DIRECTING (a film)** — *mukhrij* = director!). *tawallawhum* — ⭐ **take them as ALLIES** (*tawallā* + object = to befriend, ally with).
- **Here it means:** "**Allah only forbids you from those who fought you because of religion, expelled you from your homes, and supported [others] in your expulsion — [forbids you] from making allies of them.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INNAMĀ* — "ONLY"** — ⭐ **the prohibition is RESTRICTED to THREE kinds of aggressors:** ⭐ **(1) *qātalūkum fi'd-dīn* — those who FOUGHT you over religion** ⭐ **(2) *akhrajūkum min diyārikum* — those who EXPELLED you** ⭐ **(3) *ẓāharū ʿalā ikhrājikum* — those who BACKED / supported your expulsion** (even if they didn't do it themselves!). ⭐⭐⭐ **v.8 (permitted) and v.9 (forbidden) are PERFECT MIRRORS** — ⭐ **the criterion is AGGRESSION, not belief.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ẒĀHARŪ* — the *ẒAHR* (BACK) root** — ⭐ **TAḤRĪM 66:4** — *wa in **taẓāharā** ʿalayh* — ⭐ **two wives "backing each other" against the Prophet ﷺ (a household tension).** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:9** — ***ẓāharū** ʿalā ikhrājikum* — ⭐ **enemies "backing" the expulsion of the believers (a war).** ⭐ ***Muẓāharah* = a PROTEST** — ⭐ **people standing BACK TO BACK.** ⭐⭐ **And Ṣaff 61:14 (next surah!) — *fa-aṣbaḥū ẒĀHIRĪN*** — ⭐ **the believers became DOMINANT (on top — with their backs protected).**
  ⭐⭐ ***An TAWALLAWHUM*** — ⭐ **"to take them as ALLIES"** — ⭐ ***tawallā* + object = to befriend / ally** (⭐ vs *tawallā* alone = to turn away — v.6!). ⚠️ **Context decides!**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***innamā*** = restriction. ⭐ ***an tawallawhum*** = *badal* ("**from** befriending them"). ⚠️⭐ ***tawallā*** **WITH object = BEFRIEND; WITHOUT = TURN AWAY** (v.6!).
- **Why this exact word:** *ẓāharū* — ⭐ **even backing an aggression counts — complicity, not just direct action.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INNAMĀ* = ONLY three aggressors: those who FOUGHT you over religion, EXPELLED you, or BACKED your expulsion (*ẓāharū*).** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.8 (permitted) & v.9 (forbidden) = PERFECT MIRRORS — the criterion is AGGRESSION.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ẒAHR* (BACK): wives back each other (Taḥrīm 66:4), enemies back an expulsion (60:9), believers DOMINANT (Ṣaff 61:14); *muẓāharah* = PROTEST.** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***TAWALLĀ* + OBJECT = BEFRIEND (v.9) / alone = TURN AWAY (v.6).** ⭐ ***Ikhrāj* = film DIRECTING.**

**25. وَمَن يَتَوَلَّهُمْ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الظَّالِمُونَ — *wa man yatawallahum fa-ulāʾika humu'ẓ-ẓālimūn*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *yatawallahum* — ⭐ **ALLIES with them**. *ẓālimūn* — ⭐⭐ **WRONGDOERS** (*ẓulm* = *ẓulmah*, darkness; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:5** — *lā yahdi'l-qawma'ẓ-ẓālimīn*; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:17** — *jazāʾu'ẓ-ẓālimīn*!).
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever makes allies of them — then it is those who are the wrongdoers.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***HUMU'Ẓ-ẒĀLIMŪN* — "THEY are THE WRONGDOERS"** — ⭐ **allying with active enemies = *ẓulm* (putting something in the wrong place — the classical definition of *ẓulm*: *waḍʿ ash-shayʾ fī ghayri mawḍiʿih*)** — ⭐ **putting your LOYALTY where it doesn't belong.** ⭐⭐ ***Ẓulm* = *ẓulmah* (DARKNESS)** — ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:11** — *mina'ẓ-ẓulumāti ila'n-nūr*. ⭐ **The *ẓālimīn* thread: Jumuʿah 62:5, Ḥashr 59:17, Mumtaḥanah 60:9.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** + jussive + ***fa-ulāʾika*** (answer). ⭐ ***humu'ẓ-ẓālimūna*** = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** *ẓulm* — ⭐ **misplaced loyalty as the very definition of injustice.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***HUMU'Ẓ-ẒĀLIMŪN* = allying with active enemies = *ẒULM* — "putting a thing in the WRONG PLACE" (loyalty misplaced).** ⭐ ***Ẓulm* = *ẓulmah* (DARKNESS) — Ṭalāq 65:11.** ⭐ ***Ẓālimīn*: Jumuʿah 62:5, Ḥashr 59:17, Mumtaḥanah 60:9.**

## §5 — The Women Who Emigrated (v.10–11)

*⭐ Background: under the Treaty of Ḥudaybiyah (6 AH), anyone who came from Makkah to the Muslims had to be returned. Then believing WOMEN began escaping to Madīnah — among them Umm Kulthūm bint ʿUqbah. This verse clarified: the treaty's return clause does not apply to believing women.*

**26. يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا جَاءَكُمُ الْمُؤْمِنَاتُ مُهَاجِرَاتٍ فَامْتَحِنُوهُنَّ — *yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū idhā jāʾakumu'l-muʾminātu muhājirātin fa'mtaḥinūhunn*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *muʾmināt* — ⭐ **BELIEVING WOMEN**. *muhājirāt* — ⭐ **EMIGRATING (women)** (*hijrah*; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:8** *al-muhājirīn*!). *imtaḥinū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**TRIAL / ORDEAL / testing**" family — *maḥana* = to test, to try (originally: to beat leather / to strike); ⭐ ***miḥnah* = an ORDEAL / severe TRIAL** (⭐ *al-Miḥnah* = the famous **INQUISITION** of the ʿAbbāsid era — Imām Aḥmad's ordeal over "the created Qurʾān"!); ⭐⭐⭐ ***imtiḥān* = an EXAM** (⭐ everyday!: *imtiḥān nihāʾī* = **FINAL EXAM**; *imtiḥān al-qabūl* = **ENTRANCE EXAM**!); ⭐ ***mumtaḥin* = an EXAMINER**; ⭐⭐ ***al-MUMTAḤANAH*** = **the surah's NAME** — "the [woman] who is EXAMINED" (with *fatḥah* on the *ḥāʾ*) — or "the [surah] that EXAMINES" (with *kasrah* — *mumtaḥinah*).
- **Here it means:** "**O you who believe, when believing women come to you as emigrants, examine them.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SURAH'S NAME — *FA'MTAḤINŪHUNNA* — "EXAMINE them!"** ⭐ ***IMTIḤĀN* = an EXAM** — ⭐ **the everyday school word, straight from this verse.** ⭐⭐ **What was examined?** ⭐ **Their MOTIVE: did they come for Allah and His Messenger — or to escape a bad marriage, for money, for a man?** ⭐ **Classical (Ibn ʿAbbās): they were asked to swear "*by Allah, I did not leave out of hatred for my husband, nor seeking a new land, nor for worldly gain — but only for love of Allah and His Messenger.*"** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the surah's two "exams": v.1 — Ḥāṭib's loyalty was EXAMINED (and he slipped); v.10 — the women's loyalty is EXAMINED (and they passed — leaving husbands, homes, families).** ⭐ **The women emigrants: an *uswah* of loyalty like Ibrāhīm's.**
  ⭐⭐ ***MIḤNAH* = an ORDEAL — *al-Miḥnah* = the ʿAbbāsid INQUISITION (Imām Aḥmad's ordeal).** ⭐ ***Imtiḥān nihāʾī* = FINAL EXAM; *mumtaḥin* = EXAMINER.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Muhājirāt* — Ḥashr 59:8** — *al-fuqarāʾi'l-**muhājirīn*** (the men who emigrated) ⭐ **→ Mumtaḥanah 60:10** — ***al-muʾminātu muhājirāt*** (the WOMEN who emigrated). ⭐ **Consecutive surahs — men and women of the hijrah.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***muhājirātin*** = *ḥāl* ("**as** emigrants"). ⭐⭐ ***fa'mtaḥinūhunna*** = **Form VIII** imperative (*iftaʿala* — "put them to the test").
- **Why this exact word:** *imtiḥān* — ⭐ **the exam of motive — and the surah's name.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA'MTAḤINŪHUNNA* = EXAMINE them — the surah's NAME (*al-Mumtaḥanah* = the woman EXAMINED).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***IMTIḤĀN* = an EXAM (*imtiḥān nihāʾī* = FINAL exam); *mumtaḥin* = EXAMINER; *miḥnah* = ORDEAL (Imām Aḥmad's *Miḥnah*).** ⭐⭐ **The exam: their MOTIVE — for Allah, not a husband, money, or a man (Ibn ʿAbbās).** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO EXAMS: Ḥāṭib (v.1 — slipped) / the women (v.10 — passed).** ⭐⭐ ***Muhājirīn* (Ḥashr 59:8 — men) / *muhājirāt* (60:10 — women).** ⭐ **Treaty of Ḥudaybiyah.**

**27. اللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ بِإِيمَانِهِنَّ ۖ فَإِنْ عَلِمْتُمُوهُنَّ مُؤْمِنَاتٍ فَلَا تَرْجِعُوهُنَّ إِلَى الْكُفَّارِ — *Allāhu aʿlamu bi-īmānihinn · fa-in ʿalimtumūhunna muʾminātin fa-lā tarjiʿūhunna ila'l-kuffār*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *aʿlam* — ⭐ **KNOWS BEST**. *ʿalimtumūhunna* — ⭐ **you KNOW them to be**. *tarjiʿūhunna* — ⭐ **RETURN them / send them back** (*rajaʿa*; ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:8** — *inna ilā rabbika'r-**rujʿā***; ⭐ ***marjiʿ* = a REFERENCE / authority**!).
- **Here it means:** "**Allah knows best about their faith. Then if you know them to be believers, do not return them to the disbelievers.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE HUMILITY OF THE EXAMINER: *ALLĀHU AʿLAMU BI-ĪMĀNIHINN*** — ⭐ **"ALLAH knows BEST their faith"** — ⭐ **you examine the OUTSIDE (words, oath, behaviour); only Allah sees the INSIDE.** ⭐⭐ **A principle for every human judgment of faith: judge by the apparent, leave the heart to Allah** (⭐ the ḥadīth of Usāmah — "*did you split open his heart?*" — Muslim). ⭐ ***Fa-in ʿALIMTUMŪHUNNA muʾmināt*** — ⭐ **"if YOU know them to be believers" — your knowledge = reasonable assessment, not certainty.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Fa-lā TARJIʿŪHUNNA ila'l-kuffār*** — ⭐ **"DON'T SEND THEM BACK"** — ⭐ **the treaty's return clause doesn't apply to believing women** — ⭐ **protecting the most vulnerable from being forced back into disbelief.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Rajaʿa* — *marjiʿ* = a REFERENCE / religious AUTHORITY** — ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:8 — *ila rabbika'r-rujʿā* (to your Lord is the RETURN).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***Allāhu aʿlamu*** = parenthetical (*iʿtirāḍ*). ⭐ ***ʿalimtumūhunna muʾminātin*** — verb of knowing + two objects. ⭐ ***fa-lā tarjiʿūhunna*** = prohibition (Form I *rajaʿa* used **transitively** — "send back").
- **Why this exact word:** *aʿlam* — ⭐ **the examiner's limit — outward assessment, inward knowledge left to Allah.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ALLĀHU AʿLAMU BI-ĪMĀNIHINN* = ALLAH knows their faith BEST — you judge the OUTSIDE, He sees the INSIDE ("did you split open his heart?" — Muslim).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA-LĀ TARJIʿŪHUNNA ILA'L-KUFFĀR* = DON'T SEND THEM BACK — the treaty's clause doesn't cover believing women.** ⭐ ***Marjiʿ* = REFERENCE / AUTHORITY; ʿAlaq 96:8.**

**28. لَا هُنَّ حِلٌّ لَّهُمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحِلُّونَ لَهُنَّ — *lā hunna ḥillun lahum wa lā hum yaḥillūna lahunn*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *ḥill* — ⭐⭐⭐ **LAWFUL / permissible** (*ḥalla* = to untie; ⭐ ***ḥalāl***!; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:1–2** — *mā **aḥalla**'llāhu lak* / *taḥillata aymānikum*!; ⭐ ***ḥall* = a SOLUTION**; ⭐ ***taḥlīl* = ANALYSIS** — *taḥlīl ad-dam* = **BLOOD TEST**!).
- **Here it means:** "**They are not lawful [wives] for them, nor are they lawful [husbands] for them.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A PERFECT CHIASMUS — the marriage dissolved from BOTH sides.** ⭐ ***Lā HUNNA ḥillun LAHUM*** — ⭐ **"THEY (the women) aren't lawful for THEM (the men)"** ⭐ ***wa lā HUM yaḥillūna LAHUNNA*** — ⭐ **"nor are THEY (the men) lawful for THEM (the women)."** ⭐⭐ **The mirror structure (*hunna… lahum / hum… lahunna*) says: the bond is cut COMPLETELY, on both sides.** ⭐ **A believing woman and a disbelieving husband — the marriage no longer stands.** ⭐⭐ **And note: the first uses a NOUN (*ḥillun* — a permanent state), the second a VERB (*yaḥillūna* — ongoing)** — ⭐ classically: **"she is not lawful for him now, and he will never become lawful for her (while he disbelieves)."**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤILL* — the *Ḥ-L-L* (UNTYING) root — TAḤRĪM 66:1–2!** ⭐ ***ḥalāl* = UNTIED (permissible); *ḥarām* = TIED (forbidden)** — ⭐ **Taḥrīm: *aḥalla* (made lawful), *taḥillah* (the untying of an oath).** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah: *ḥill* — the marriage-tie no longer "untied" for them.** ⭐ ***Ḥall* = SOLUTION; *taḥlīl ad-dam* = BLOOD TEST.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐⭐ **CHIASMUS: *hunna… lahum / hum… lahunna*** — ⭐ **NOUN (*ḥillun*) then VERB (*yaḥillūna*)** — ⭐ different emphases.
- **Why this exact word:** **the chiasmus** — ⭐ **a mirror sentence for a bond cut from both ends.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **CHIASMUS: *LĀ HUNNA ḤILLUN LAHUM WA LĀ HUM YAḤILLŪNA LAHUNNA* — not lawful for THEM, nor THEY for THEM — the bond cut from BOTH sides.** ⭐⭐ **NOUN (*ḥillun* — permanent) then VERB (*yaḥillūna* — ongoing).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥ-L-L*: Taḥrīm 66:1–2 (*aḥalla*, *taḥillah*); *ḥall* = SOLUTION; *taḥlīl ad-dam* = BLOOD TEST.**

**29. وَآتُوهُم مَّا أَنفَقُوا ۚ وَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْكُمْ أَن تَنكِحُوهُنَّ إِذَا آتَيْتُمُوهُنَّ أُجُورَهُنَّ — *wa ātūhum mā anfaqū · wa lā junāḥa ʿalaykum an tankiḥūhunna idhā ātaytumūhunna ujūrahunn*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *ātūhum* — ⭐ **GIVE them** (the disbelieving husbands). *anfaqū* — ⭐ **they SPENT** (⭐ the *nafaqa* root — **Munāfiqūn 63:7, 10**!). *junāḥ* — ⭐⭐ the "**WING / leaning (to one side)**" family — ***janāḥ* = a WING** (⭐ *janāḥ* = a **WING** of a bird / a building — *janāḥ al-wilādah* = **MATERNITY WING**!); ⭐ ***junāḥ* = a SIN / blame** (a "leaning" away from the right!); ⭐ ***janaḥa* = to INCLINE** (⭐ *wa in janaḥū li's-salmi fa'jnaḥ lahā* — 8:61 — "if they INCLINE to peace, incline to it"!); ⭐ ***junḥah* = a MISDEMEANOUR** (legal!). *tankiḥūhunna* — ⭐ **MARRY them** (*nikāḥ*). *ujūr* — ⭐⭐ **WAGES → MAHR (dowries)** (*ajr*; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:6** — *fa-ātūhunna ujūrahunna* (nursing wages)!).
- **Here it means:** "**And give them [the husbands] what they spent. And there is no blame upon you if you marry them when you have given them their dowries.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **JUSTICE EVEN TO THE ENEMY — refund the dowry.** ⭐ ***Wa ĀTŪHUM MĀ ANFAQŪ*** — ⭐ **"GIVE them [the disbelieving husbands] what they SPENT"** (the mahr they'd paid) — ⭐ **even though they're the enemy side, they're not cheated.** ⭐⭐ **v.8: *tuqsiṭū* (be JUST) — here it's applied in law.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Wa lā JUNĀḤA ʿalaykum an tankiḥūhunna*** — ⭐ **"NO BLAME on you if you MARRY them"** — ⭐ **the emigrant women can start new lives** — ⭐ ***idhā ātaytumūhunna UJŪRAHUNNA*** — ⭐ **"when you give them THEIR dowries"** — ⭐ **a NEW mahr, their own right.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***JUNĀḤ* (SIN / BLAME) ← *JANĀḤ* (a WING)** — ⭐ **a sin "leans" you to one side, like a tilted wing** — ⭐ ***lā junāḥa*** = "**no LEANING [from right]**" = no blame. ⭐ ***Janāḥ al-wilādah* = MATERNITY WING** (hospital!); ⭐ ***junḥah* = MISDEMEANOUR**; ⭐ **8:61 — *in janaḥū li's-salm* (if they INCLINE to peace).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***UJŪR* — WAGES = MAHR** — ⭐ **ṬALĀQ 65:6** — *fa-ātūhunna **ujūrahunna*** (pay nursing mothers their WAGES) ⭐ **→ Mumtaḥanah 60:10** — *ātaytumūhunna **ujūrahunna*** (give them their DOWRIES). ⭐ **The same word: a woman's RIGHT — whether for nursing or for marriage.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā junāḥa*** = ***lā an-nāfiyah li'l-jins*** ("**NO blame at all**"). ⭐ ***an tankiḥūhunna*** = *maṣdar* ("in your marrying them"). ⭐ ***idhā*** = condition.
- **Why this exact word:** *junāḥ* — ⭐ **blame as a tilt off course — lifted, so the women can begin again.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA ĀTŪHUM MĀ ANFAQŪ* = REFUND the disbelieving husbands' DOWRY — justice even to the enemy side (v.8 *tuqsiṭū* in action).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ JUNĀḤA* = NO BLAME — *junāḥ* (SIN) ← *janāḥ* (WING) — a sin "TILTS" you; *janāḥ al-wilādah* = MATERNITY WING; 8:61 *janaḥū* (incline).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***UJŪR* = WAGES = MAHR — Ṭalāq 65:6 (nursing wages) / 60:10 (dowries) — a woman's RIGHT.** ⭐ ***Junḥah* = MISDEMEANOUR.**

**30. وَلَا تُمْسِكُوا بِعِصَمِ الْكَوَافِرِ — *wa lā tumsikū bi-ʿiṣami'l-kawāfir*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *tumsikū* — ⭐ **HOLD ON TO** (*amsaka*; ⭐ **Mulk 67:19** — *mā yumsikuhunna illa'r-Raḥmān*!; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:2** — *fa-amsikūhunna bi-maʿrūf*!; ⭐ ***imsāk***). *ʿiṣam* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**PROTECTIVE TIE / safeguard**" family — *ʿaṣama* = **to PROTECT, guard, hold back**; ⭐ ***ʿiṣmah* (pl. *ʿiṣam*) = a protective TIE / bond** (here: the MARRIAGE bond!); ⭐ ***ʿiṣmah* = INFALLIBILITY** (of prophets — ⭐ ***maʿṣūm* = INFALLIBLE / protected from sin**!); ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿāṣimah* = a CAPITAL CITY** (⭐ the "protecting" city — the stronghold!; ⭐ *ʿāṣimat Bākistān* = the **CAPITAL of Pakistan**!); ⭐ ***miʿṣam* = a WRIST** (where a bracelet/strap is "tied" — ⭐ clinical: *miʿṣam* = the **WRIST**!); ⭐ ***iʿtiṣām* = HOLDING FAST** (⭐ *wa'ʿtaṣimū bi-ḥabli'llāh* — 3:103!; ⭐ *iʿtiṣām* = a **SIT-IN** protest!). *kawāfir* — ⭐ **DISBELIEVING WOMEN** (plural of *kāfirah*).
- **Here it means:** "**And do not hold on to marriage-bonds with disbelieving women.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE OTHER DIRECTION — men too must let go.** ⭐ **v.10a: believing WOMEN aren't sent back to disbelieving husbands.** ⭐ **v.10b: believing MEN shouldn't keep marriages with idolatrous WIVES who stayed in Makkah.** ⭐⭐ **The rule applies equally both ways — fairness in the law.** ⭐ **(ʿUmar divorced two wives who remained idolaters in Makkah after this verse — reported.)**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿIṢAM* — the PROTECTIVE TIE — and its surprising family:** ⭐ ***ʿIṢMAH* = INFALLIBILITY (*maʿṣūm*)** — ⭐ **a prophet is "protected" from sin.** ⭐ ***ʿĀṢIMAH* = a CAPITAL CITY** — ⭐ **the "stronghold" that protects.** ⭐ ***MIʿṢAM* = the WRIST** (clinical!) — ⭐ **where a bond/bracelet is tied.** ⭐ ***IʿTIṢĀM* = HOLDING FAST — *wa'ʿtaṣimū bi-ḥabli'llāh* (3:103)** — ⭐ **and modern: a SIT-IN.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Marriage = a protective TIE (*ʿiṣmah*) — "don't HOLD ON (*tumsikū*) to a tie that no longer protects."** ⭐ **Hold fast instead to the ROPE of Allah (*iʿtiṣām*, 3:103).**
  ⭐⭐ ***TUMSIKŪ* — the *IMSĀK* thread:** ⭐ **Mulk 67:19** — *mā **yumsikuhunna** illa'r-Raḥmān* (only the Merciful HOLDS the birds). ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:2** — *fa-**amsikūhunna** bi-maʿrūf* (KEEP them decently — a valid marriage). ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:10** — *wa **lā tumsikū** bi-ʿiṣami'l-kawāfir* (DON'T keep an invalid one). ⭐⭐ **Keep the valid bond kindly (Ṭalāq); release the invalid one (Mumtaḥanah).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā tumsikū*** = prohibition (Form IV + *bi-*). ⭐ ***ʿiṣami*** = plural of *ʿiṣmah*. ⭐ ***al-kawāfiri*** = plural of *kāfirah* (*fawāʿil*) — ⚠️ diptote, but genitive *-i* with *al-*.
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿiṣmah* — ⭐ **marriage as a protective tie — the same root as infallibility and capital cities.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA LĀ TUMSIKŪ BI-ʿIṢAMI'L-KAWĀFIR* = don't HOLD ON to marriage-ties with idolatrous wives — the rule applies BOTH WAYS (fairness).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿIṢMAH* = a PROTECTIVE TIE → *MAʿṢŪM* (INFALLIBLE), *ʿĀṢIMAH* (CAPITAL CITY), *MIʿṢAM* (WRIST — clinical), *IʿTIṢĀM* (HOLDING FAST — 3:103 / a SIT-IN).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***IMSĀK*: Allah HOLDS the birds (Mulk 67:19), KEEP a valid marriage (Ṭalāq 65:2), DON'T keep an invalid one (60:10).** ⭐ **ʿUmar divorced two idolatrous wives (reported).**

**31. وَاسْأَلُوا مَا أَنفَقْتُمْ وَلْيَسْأَلُوا مَا أَنفَقُوا ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ حُكْمُ اللَّهِ ۖ يَحْكُمُ بَيْنَكُمْ — *wa's'alū mā anfaqtum wal-yasʾalū mā anfaqū · dhālikum ḥukmu'llāh · yaḥkumu baynakum*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *isʾalū* — ⭐ **ASK / claim**. *wal-yasʾalū* — ⭐ **and LET THEM ask** (*lām al-amr*). *ḥukm* — ⭐⭐ the "**BRIDLE → JUDGMENT / rule**" family — ***ḥukm* = a RULING / JUDGMENT** (⭐ ***ḥukūmah* = a GOVERNMENT**!; ⭐ ***maḥkamah* = a COURT**!; ⭐ ***ḥakam* = a REFEREE / arbiter**!; ⭐ ***ḥikmah* = WISDOM**; ⭐ ← ***ḥakamah* = the BRIDLE** — **Tīn 95:8**!; ⭐ **Qalam 68:36, 39** — *kayfa **taḥkumūn***!).
- **Here it means:** "**And ask for what you spent, and let them ask for what they spent. That is the judgment of Allah; He judges between you.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **RECIPROCAL JUSTICE — both sides claim their dowries back.** ⭐ ***Wa'sʾalū mā anfaqtum*** — ⭐ **YOU claim back the mahr you paid (to wives who stayed in Makkah).** ⭐ ***Wal-yasʾalū mā anfaqū*** — ⭐ **THEY claim back the mahr they paid (to wives who emigrated).** ⭐⭐ **Perfectly balanced — like a treaty clause. Even with an enemy, the accounts are squared.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHĀLIKUM ḤUKMU'LLĀH*** — ⭐ **"THAT is ALLAH's JUDGMENT"** — ⭐ **not human negotiation — divine ruling.** ⭐ ***Yaḥkumu baynakum*** — ⭐ **"He JUDGES between you"** — ⭐ **between Muslims and their enemies — fairly.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤUKM* ← *ḤAKAMAH* (BRIDLE)** — ⭐ **a ruling "bridles" the parties, holding them to justice** — ⭐ ***ḥukūmah* = GOVERNMENT; *maḥkamah* = COURT; *ḥakam* = REFEREE.** ⭐ **QALAM 68:36** — *mā lakum kayfa **taḥkumūn*** (what's wrong with you — how do you JUDGE?) — ⭐ **their bad judgment vs Allah's *ḥukm*.** ⭐ **Tīn 95:8** — *a-laysa'llāhu bi-**aḥkami'l-ḥākimīn*** (isn't Allah the most JUST of JUDGES?).
  ⭐ ⭐ ***ANFAQTUM / ANFAQŪ*** — ⭐ **the *nafaqa* root again — spending, refunded fairly.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa'sʾalū*** = imperative; ⭐ ***wal-yasʾalū*** = ***lām al-amr*** (3rd person command — "**let them** ask"). ⭐ ***yaḥkumu baynakum*** = new sentence (*istiʾnāf*) or *ḥāl*.
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥukm* — ⭐ **a balanced ruling between enemies, called Allah's own judgment.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **RECIPROCAL JUSTICE: YOU claim back your mahr; LET THEM claim back theirs — accounts squared even with an enemy.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHĀLIKUM ḤUKMU'LLĀH* = THAT is ALLAH's JUDGMENT — not negotiation.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤUKM* ← BRIDLE: *ḥukūmah* = GOVERNMENT; *maḥkamah* = COURT; *ḥakam* = REFEREE; Qalam 68:36 (*kayfa taḥkumūn*), Tīn 95:8 (*aḥkam al-ḥākimīn*).** ⚠️ ***Wal-yasʾalū* = *lām al-amr*.**

**32. وَاللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ — *wa'llāhu ʿalīmun ḥakīm*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *ʿalīm* — ⭐ **ALL-KNOWING**. *ḥakīm* — ⭐ **ALL-WISE** (⭐ ← BRIDLE).
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***ʿALĪMUN ḤAKĪM*** — ⭐ **KNOWING (the women's true faith — v.10a *Allāhu aʿlamu bi-īmānihinn*) + WISE (the balanced ruling — v.10b *ḥukm*).** ⭐ ***Ḥakīm* and *ḥukm* — the SAME root, side by side** — ⭐ **the WISE One gives the RULING.** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:2** — *wa huwa'l-ʿalīmu'l-ḥakīm* — ⭐ **the same pair after the oath-ruling.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿalīmun ḥakīmun*** — two *faʿīl* attributes.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **knowledge of hearts + wisdom of rulings — the two halves of v.10.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***ʿALĪMUN ḤAKĪM* = KNOWS their faith (v.10a) + WISE in the ruling (v.10b).** ⭐ ***Ḥakīm* & *ḥukm* — same root, side by side.** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:2 — same pair after a ruling.**

**33. وَإِن فَاتَكُمْ شَيْءٌ مِّنْ أَزْوَاجِكُمْ إِلَى الْكُفَّارِ فَعَاقَبْتُمْ فَآتُوا الَّذِينَ ذَهَبَتْ أَزْوَاجُهُم مِّثْلَ مَا أَنفَقُوا — *wa in fātakum shayʾun min azwājikum ila'l-kuffāri fa-ʿāqabtum fa-ātu'lladhīna dhahabat azwājuhum mithla mā anfaqū*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *fātakum* — ⭐⭐ the "**MISSING / slipping away**" family — *fāta* = **to ESCAPE, slip away, be MISSED**; ⭐ ***fawt* = MISSING (a chance)**; ⭐ ***tafāwut* = DISPARITY / discrepancy** (⭐ **Mulk 67:3** — *mā tarā fī khalqi'r-raḥmāni min **tafāwut***!); ⭐ ***fāʾit* = PAST** (⭐ *al-ʿām al-fāʾit* = **LAST year**!). *ʿāqabtum* — ⭐⭐ **you got your TURN / you took [spoils] in return** (Form III of *ʿaqaba* — ⭐ ***ʿuqbah* = a TURN** (riding in turns!); ⭐ ***ʿiqāb* = PUNISHMENT** (Ḥashr 59:4!); ⭐ ***ʿāqibah* = OUTCOME**). *dhahabat* — ⭐ **WENT (away)**. *mithl* — ⭐ **the EQUIVALENT**.
- **Here it means:** "**And if any of your wives go over to the disbelievers [and they don't refund the dowry], and you then gain [in your turn], give those whose wives have gone the equivalent of what they spent.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A SAFETY NET — when the enemy WON'T refund.** ⭐ **If a Muslim's wife left for Makkah and the Quraysh REFUSED to return his mahr (as v.10 required)** — ⭐ **then when the Muslims "got their TURN" (*ʿāqabtum*) — gained spoils or funds** — ⭐ **compensate that husband from it.** ⭐⭐ **No one is left out of pocket because the other side cheated.** ⭐ **Justice that doesn't depend on the enemy's goodwill.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĀQABTUM* — the *ʿAQIB* (HEEL) root — "your TURN came"** — ⭐ ***ʿuqbah* = a TURN** (classically: two people riding one camel in TURNS — one rides, the other follows at the HEEL!) — ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:4, 7** — *shadīd al-**ʿiqāb*** (penalty — on the heels); ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:17** — ***ʿāqibatahumā***; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:9** — ***ʿāqibatu** amrihā*. ⭐ **Here: your TURN to receive.**
  ⭐⭐ ***FĀTAKUM* — "slipped away from you"** — ⭐ **Mulk 67:3** — *min **tafāwut*** (no DISPARITY / flaw in creation). ⭐ ***Al-ʿām al-fāʾit* = LAST YEAR.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Shayʾun min azwājikum*** — ⭐ **"ANYTHING / anyone of your wives"** — ⭐ ***shayʾ*** used for persons — a delicate, non-specific phrasing.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***in*** + past (condition) + ***fa-ʿāqabtum*** + ***fa-ātū*** (answer). ⭐ ***mithla*** = object (*mithla mā* = "the equivalent of what").
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿāqabtum* — ⭐ **"your turn" — compensation arriving in sequence, so no one is left wronged.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A SAFETY NET: if the enemy WON'T refund the dowry, compensate the husband when YOUR TURN comes (*ʿāqabtum*).** ⭐ **Justice not dependent on the enemy's goodwill.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĀQABTUM* ← *ʿuqbah* (a TURN — riding at the HEEL) — the *ʿaqib* thread: Ḥashr 59:4, 7, 17; Ṭalāq 65:9.** ⭐⭐ ***FĀTA* = slipped away — Mulk 67:3 *tafāwut*; *al-ʿām al-fāʾit* = LAST YEAR.**

**34. وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ الَّذِي أَنتُم بِهِ مُؤْمِنُونَ — *wa'ttaqu'llāha'lladhī antum bihi muʾminūn*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *ittaqū* — ⭐ **SHIELD yourselves** (*taqwā*). *muʾminūn* — ⭐ **BELIEVERS**.
- **Here it means:** "**And fear Allah, in whom you are believers.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***ALLADHĪ ANTUM BIHI MUʾMINŪN*** — ⭐ **"the One you BELIEVE in"** — ⭐ **an appeal to consistency: you believe in Him — so act on it in money matters too.** ⭐ ***Bihi* FRONTED** — ⭐ **"in HIM [specifically] you believe."** ⭐⭐ **The *taqwā* — again where MONEY is involved** (⭐ like Ṭalāq's divorce finances, Ḥashr 59:7's *fayʾ*).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***bihi*** fronted before ***muʾminūna*** = emphasis.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **faith invoked as the reason for fairness in settlements.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***ALLADHĪ ANTUM BIHI MUʾMINŪN* = "the One you BELIEVE in" — be consistent in MONEY matters too.** ⭐ ***Taqwā* where money is at stake (Ṭalāq, Ḥashr 59:7).**

## §6 — The Women's Pledge (v.12)

**35. يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ إِذَا جَاءَكَ الْمُؤْمِنَاتُ يُبَايِعْنَكَ — *yā ayyuha'n-nabiyyu idhā jāʾaka'l-muʾminātu yubāyiʿnak*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *yubāyiʿnaka* — ⭐⭐⭐ **PLEDGE ALLEGIANCE to you** (Form III of *bāʿa* — to sell!; ⭐ ***bayʿah* = a PLEDGE of allegiance** (sealed like a SALE!); ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:9** — *wa dharu'l-**bayʿ***!; ⭐ ***mubāyaʿah* = swearing allegiance**; ⭐ ***Bayʿat ar-Riḍwān*** (48:18); ⭐ ***Bayʿat al-ʿAqabah***).
- **Here it means:** "**O Prophet, when the believing women come to you pledging allegiance to you…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE WOMEN'S *BAYʿAH* — and the SALE root.** ⭐ ***Yubāyiʿnaka*** — ⭐ **from *bayʿ* (SALE)** — ⭐ **a pledge of allegiance = a TRANSACTION: you "sell" your obedience to Allah for Paradise** (⭐ 9:111 — *inna'llāha'shtarā mina'l-muʾminīna anfusahum… fa'stabshirū bi-bayʿikum*!). ⭐⭐⭐ **JUMUʿAH 62:9** — *wa dharu'l-**BAYʿ*** — ⭐ **LEAVE the small SALE (market trade)** ⭐ **→ Mumtaḥanah 60:12** — *yu**BĀYIʿ**naka* — ⭐ **make the GREAT sale (allegiance to Allah's Messenger).** ⭐⭐ **And AṢ-ṢAFF 61:10 (next surah!) — *hal adullukum ʿalā TIJĀRAH*** — ⭐ **"shall I show you a TRADE that saves you?"** ⭐ **The commerce of faith across the set.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **WOMEN pledging DIRECTLY — as full, independent members of the ummah** — ⭐ **their own *bayʿah*, their own commitment** — ⭐ **ʿĀʾishah: the Prophet's ﷺ hand never touched a woman's hand in the *bayʿah* — he took it by WORD** (Bukhārī).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yubāyiʿnaka*** = **Form III** (mutual — a two-sided pact), **feminine plural** (*nūn al-niswah*) + *-ka*. ⭐ = *ḥāl*.
- **Why this exact word:** *bayʿah* — ⭐ **allegiance as a sale — women making their own transaction with Allah.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUBĀYIʿNAKA* = the WOMEN'S PLEDGE — *bayʿah* from *bayʿ* (SALE): selling obedience for Paradise (9:111).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:9 LEAVE the small SALE (*bayʿ*) → Mumtaḥanah 60:12 make the GREAT sale (*bayʿah*) → Ṣaff 61:10 the TRADE that saves (*tijārah*).** ⭐⭐ **Women pledging DIRECTLY — by WORD, not handshake (ʿĀʾishah — Bukhārī).** ⭐ ***Bayʿat ar-Riḍwān*, *Bayʿat al-ʿAqabah*.**

**36. عَلَىٰ أَن لَّا يُشْرِكْنَ بِاللَّهِ شَيْئًا وَلَا يَسْرِقْنَ وَلَا يَزْنِينَ وَلَا يَقْتُلْنَ أَوْلَادَهُنَّ — *ʿalā an lā yushrikna bi'llāhi shayʾan wa lā yasriqna wa lā yaznīna wa lā yaqtulna awlādahunn*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *yushrikna* — ⭐ **ASSOCIATE (partners)** (*shirk*; ⭐ ***sharikah* = a COMPANY**; ⭐ ***sharīk* = a PARTNER**). *yasriqna* — ⭐⭐ the "**STEALING / sneaking**" family — *saraqa* = to steal; ⭐ ***sariqah* = THEFT**; ⭐ ***sāriq* = a THIEF**; ⭐ ***istaraqa'l-samʿ* = to EAVESDROP (steal a hearing)** (⭐ **Jinn 72:9** — the jinn "eavesdropping" on heaven!; ⭐ 15:18); ⭐ ***sariqah adabiyyah* = PLAGIARISM** (literary theft!). *yaznīna* — ⭐ **commit ADULTERY / fornication** (*zinā*). *yaqtulna* — ⭐ **KILL** (*qatl*). *awlādahunna* — ⭐ **their CHILDREN**.
- **Here it means:** "**…on the condition that they will not associate anything with Allah, nor steal, nor commit unlawful sexual acts, nor kill their children…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SIX CONDITIONS OF THE PLEDGE — first four here:** ⭐ **(1) *LĀ YUSHRIKNA* — no SHIRK** (the root of all — belief). ⭐ **(2) *LĀ YASRIQNA* — no THEFT** (property). ⭐ **(3) *LĀ YAZNĪNA* — no ZINĀ** (family / lineage). ⭐ **(4) *LĀ YAQTULNA AWLĀDAHUNNA* — no KILLING their children** (life). ⭐⭐⭐ **A complete moral charter: GOD, PROPERTY, CHASTITY, LIFE** — ⭐ **the same "essential objectives" (*maqāṣid*) later formalized in Islamic law.** ⭐ **And the Prophet ﷺ took the SAME pledge from the MEN at ʿAqabah** (Bukhārī — ʿUbādah ibn aṣ-Ṣāmit) — ⭐ **identical conditions for men and women.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ YAQTULNA AWLĀDAHUNNA* — TAKWĪR 81:8–9!** ⭐ ***wa idha'l-mawʾūdatu suʾilat · bi-ayyi dhanbin qutilat*** — ⭐ **"when the girl BURIED ALIVE is asked: for what sin was she KILLED?"** ⭐ **Takwīr: the Day she's asked; Mumtaḥanah: the pledge that ends it.** ⭐ **Classically includes: infanticide, burying daughters, abortion without right, and (by extension) neglect that kills.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Sariqah adabiyyah* = PLAGIARISM; *istaraqa's-samʿ* = EAVESDROPPING (Jinn 72:9).** ⭐ ***Sharikah* = COMPANY.**
  ⭐ ⭐ **Background (reported): HIND bint ʿUtbah — Abū Sufyān's wife, once a fierce enemy — was among the women at this *bayʿah* after the Conquest of Makkah; at "no theft" she asked about taking from her stingy husband's money; at "no zinā" she said "*does a free woman commit zinā?*"; at "no killing children" she said "*we raised them small, and you killed them big* (at Badr)!" — and the Prophet ﷺ smiled.** ⭐ **v.7's promise — *mawaddah* between enemies — fulfilled in a single scene.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿalā an lā*** = "**on [condition] that… not**." ⭐ ***yushrikna, yasriqna, yaznīna, yaqtulna*** = **feminine plural** present (*nūn al-niswah* — no change for subjunctive). ⭐ ***shayʾan*** = "**anything at all**."
- **Why this exact word:** **the four** — ⭐ **a complete charter: God, property, chastity, life.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **PLEDGE CONDITIONS 1–4: no SHIRK (God), no THEFT (property), no ZINĀ (chastity/lineage), no KILLING CHILDREN (life) — the *maqāṣid* in a pledge.** ⭐⭐ **SAME pledge from the MEN at ʿAqabah (Bukhārī).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ YAQTULNA AWLĀDAHUNNA* — TAKWĪR 81:8–9 (*al-mawʾūdah* — the buried girl ASKED; the pledge ENDS it).** ⭐⭐ **HIND bint ʿUtbah at the *bayʿah* (reported) — v.7's *mawaddah* FULFILLED.** ⭐ ***Sariqah adabiyyah* = PLAGIARISM; *istaraqa's-samʿ* = EAVESDROP (Jinn 72:9).**

**37. وَلَا يَأْتِينَ بِبُهْتَانٍ يَفْتَرِينَهُ بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِنَّ وَأَرْجُلِهِنَّ — *wa lā yaʾtīna bi-buhtānin yaftarīnahu bayna aydīhinna wa arjulihinn*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *buhtān* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**STUNNING / DUMBFOUNDING**" family — *bahita* = **to be STUNNED, dumbfounded, speechless** (⭐ *fa-buhita'lladhī kafar* — 2:258 — "the disbeliever was DUMBFOUNDED" — by Ibrāhīm's argument!); ⭐ ***buhtān* = a SLANDER / outrageous LIE** (one that STUNS the accused!); ⭐ ***bahth* = slander** (⭐ ḥadīth: backbiting is saying what's TRUE; if false, it's ***buhtān***!); ⭐⭐ ***bāhit* = FADED / PALE (colour)** (⭐ *lawn bāhit* = a **PALE / dull colour**!; ⭐ — "stunned" of its brightness!); ⭐ ***mabhūt* = ASTONISHED**. *yaftarīnahu* — ⭐⭐ **FABRICATE it** (Form VIII of *farā* — to cut (leather) → to INVENT a lie; ⭐ ***iftirāʾ* = FABRICATION / slander**; ⭐ ***muftarī* = a SLANDERER**; ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:7** — *mimmani'**ftarā** ʿala'llāhi'l-kadhib*!). *aydīhinna wa arjulihinna* — ⭐ **their HANDS and FEET**.
- **Here it means:** "**…nor bring forth a slander they have fabricated between their hands and their feet…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **CONDITION 5 — and a DELICATE IDIOM.** ⭐ ***BUHTĀN yaftarīnahu BAYNA AYDĪHINNA WA ARJULIHINNA*** — ⭐ **"a slander fabricated BETWEEN their HANDS and their FEET"** — ⭐ classical (Ibn ʿAbbās and most): **attributing to her husband a CHILD that isn't his** — ⭐ **the child is carried "between her hands" and born "between her feet."** ⭐ **The Qurʾān names the gravest betrayal of lineage with the most modest possible words.** ⭐⭐ **Others add: any slander/false accusation a woman invents (lying about others' honour).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***BUHTĀN* ← *BAHITA* (to be STUNNED / DUMBFOUNDED)** — ⭐ **a slander so outrageous it STUNS the accused speechless.** ⭐ **2:258** — *fa-**buhita**'lladhī kafar* (Ibrāhīm DUMBFOUNDED the king!). ⭐ ***Bāhit* = a FADED / PALE colour** — ⭐ **slander "fades" a person's honour.** ⭐ **The ḥadīth: backbiting = saying what's TRUE of him; if it's FALSE — that's *buhtān*** (Muslim).
  ⭐⭐ ***YAFTARĪNAHU* — *IFTIRĀʾ* = FABRICATION** — ⭐ ***farā* = to CUT (leather)** — **a lie "cut out" and shaped** — ⭐ **AṢ-ṢAFF 61:7 (next surah!)** — *wa man aẓlamu mimmani'**ftarā** ʿala'llāhi'l-kadhib* — ⭐ **FABRICATING lies against ALLAH.** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah: fabrication against PEOPLE; Ṣaff: against ALLAH.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yaʾtīna bi-*** = "**bring / come with**." ⭐ ***yaftarīnahu*** = *ṣifah* of *buhtān* (feminine plural + *-hu*). ⭐ ***bayna aydīhinna wa arjulihinna*** = *ḥāl* / idiom.
- **Why this exact word:** *buhtān* — ⭐ **a stunning lie — named with the most modest idiom for the gravest betrayal.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **CONDITION 5: no *BUHTĀN* "fabricated BETWEEN their HANDS and FEET" = attributing to the husband a CHILD not his (Ibn ʿAbbās) — gravest betrayal, most modest words.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BUHTĀN* ← *BAHITA* (STUNNED — 2:258); *bāhit* = FADED colour; backbiting = TRUE, *buhtān* = FALSE (Muslim).** ⭐⭐ ***IFTIRĀʾ* ← *farā* (CUT leather) — a lie "cut out" — Ṣaff 61:7 (*iftarā ʿala'llāh*).** ⭐ **Against PEOPLE (60) / against ALLAH (61).**

**38. وَلَا يَعْصِينَكَ فِي مَعْرُوفٍ ۙ فَبَايِعْهُنَّ وَاسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُنَّ اللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ — *wa lā yaʿṣīnaka fī maʿrūfin fa-bāyiʿhunna wa'staghfir lahunna'llāh · inna'llāha ghafūrun raḥīm*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *yaʿṣīnaka* — ⭐ **DISOBEY you** (*ʿiṣyān* = MUTINY; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:6** — *lā yaʿṣūna'llāh*; ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:21** — *ʿaṣawnī*). *maʿrūf* — ⭐⭐ **what is RECOGNIZED as GOOD** (⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:2, 6** — *bi-maʿrūf*!; ⭐ ***al-amr bi'l-maʿrūf***). *bāyiʿhunna* — ⭐ **ACCEPT their pledge**. *istaghfir* — ⭐ **SEEK forgiveness**.
- **Here it means:** "**…nor disobey you in what is right — then accept their pledge and ask Allah to forgive them. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **CONDITION 6 — and a REVOLUTIONARY limit: *FĪ MAʿRŪF*.** ⭐ ***Wa lā yaʿṣīnaka FĪ MAʿRŪF*** — ⭐ **"nor disobey you IN WHAT IS RIGHT"** — ⭐ **even obedience to the PROPHET ﷺ is phrased as obedience in *maʿrūf* (good)** — ⭐ **he would only ever command good — but the wording sets the PRINCIPLE for every leader after him.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The ḥadīth: "*There is NO obedience in disobedience to Allah — obedience is ONLY in MAʿRŪF*" (Bukhārī, Muslim).** ⭐ **If the Prophet's ﷺ own pledge says "in *maʿrūf*" — no ruler, husband, or boss can demand obedience in wrong.** ⭐⭐ ***Maʿrūf* — Ṭalāq 65:2** (*amsikūhunna bi-maʿrūf aw fāriqūhunna bi-maʿrūf*), **65:6** (*iʾtamirū bi-maʿrūf*) — ⭐ **the decency-word.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA'STAGHFIR LAHUNNA'LLĀH*** — ⭐ **"SEEK FORGIVENESS for them"** — ⭐ **MUNĀFIQŪN 63:5–6** — the Prophet ﷺ offered *istighfār* to the hypocrites — they REFUSED (*lawwaw ruʾūsahum*), and it made no difference (*sawāʾun ʿalayhim*). ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:12** — the believing women CAME, pledged — and he seeks forgiveness for them. ⭐⭐ **The same *istighfār* — refused by the hypocrites, received by the believing women.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fī maʿrūfin*** — indefinite (**any** good). ⭐ ***fa-bāyiʿhunna*** = answer of *idhā* (v.12a). ⭐ ***wa'staghfir lahunna'llāha*** — *istaghfara* + *li-* + **Allāh as object** ("ask ALLAH's forgiveness for them").
- **Why this exact word:** *maʿrūf* — ⭐ **obedience bounded by good — even in the Prophet's ﷺ own pledge.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **CONDITION 6: *LĀ YAʿṢĪNAKA FĪ MAʿRŪF* = no disobedience IN WHAT IS RIGHT — even the PROPHET's ﷺ pledge is bounded by *maʿrūf*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **"No obedience in disobedience to Allah — obedience ONLY in *maʿrūf*" (Bukhārī, Muslim) — no ruler/husband/boss can demand obedience in wrong.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ISTIGHFĀR*: OFFERED to the hypocrites — REFUSED (Munāfiqūn 63:5–6) / given to the believing women — RECEIVED (60:12).** ⭐ ***Maʿrūf* — Ṭalāq 65:2, 6.**

## §7 — The Closing Warning (v.13)

**39. يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تَتَوَلَّوْا قَوْمًا غَضِبَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِمْ — *yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū lā tatawallaw qawman ghaḍiba'llāhu ʿalayhim*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *lā tatawallaw* — ⭐ **do not TAKE AS ALLIES** (*tawallā* + object; ⭐ v.9!). *ghaḍiba* — ⭐⭐ the "**ANGER**" family — ***ghaḍab* = ANGER** (⭐ ***ghaḍbān* = ANGRY**; ⭐ ***al-maghḍūbi ʿalayhim*** — **FĀTIḤAH 1:7**!).
- **Here it means:** "**O you who believe, do not take as allies a people with whom Allah is angry…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BOOKEND — the surah ends where it began.** ⭐ **v.1** — *lā **tattakhidhū** ʿaduwwī wa ʿaduwwakum **awliyāʾ*** (don't take My enemies as ALLIES). ⭐ **v.13** — *lā **tatawallaw** qawman ghaḍiba'llāhu ʿalayhim* (don't take as ALLIES those under Allah's anger). ⭐⭐ **Opening and closing — the same command, framing the whole surah.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAḌIBA'LLĀHU ʿALAYHIM* — FĀTIḤAH 1:7!** ⭐ ***ghayri'l-maghḍūbi ʿalayhim wa la'ḍ-ḍāllīn*** — ⭐ **"not [the path] of those who EARNED [Your] ANGER, nor of those astray"** — ⭐ **you recite it in EVERY rakʿah.** ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah closes with the Fātiḥah's warning applied to loyalty.** ⭐ **(Classical identifications of "the people under anger" here vary: some say hostile groups among the People of the Book, others say all disbelievers — the verse's lesson is general.)**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā tatawallaw*** = prohibition (Form V, weak-final). ⭐ ***qawman*** = object; ***ghaḍiba'llāhu ʿalayhim*** = *ṣifah*.
- **Why this exact word:** *ghaḍab* — ⭐ **the Fātiḥah's word, closing the surah's loyalty frame.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **BOOKEND: v.1 *lā tattakhidhū… awliyāʾ* → v.13 *lā tatawallaw qawman…* — the same command opens and closes.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAḌIBA'LLĀHU ʿALAYHIM* = FĀTIḤAH 1:7 (*al-maghḍūbi ʿalayhim*) — recited every rakʿah.** ⭐ **Identifications vary; the lesson is general.**

**40. قَدْ يَئِسُوا مِنَ الْآخِرَةِ كَمَا يَئِسَ الْكُفَّارُ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ الْقُبُورِ — *qad yaʾisū mina'l-ākhirati kamā yaʾisa'l-kuffāru min aṣḥābi'l-qubūr*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *yaʾisū* — ⭐⭐ **DESPAIRED / gave up hope** (*yaʾs*; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:4** — *wa'llāʾī **yaʾisna** mina'l-maḥīḍ* (menopause — *sinn al-yaʾs*)!). *ākhirah* — ⭐ **the HEREAFTER**. *qubūr* — ⭐⭐ **GRAVES** (plural of *qabr*; ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:21** — *thumma amātahu **fa-aqbarah***!; ⭐ **Takāthur 102:2** — *ḥattā zurtumu'l-**maqābir***!; ⭐ **Infiṭār 82:4** — *wa idha'l-**qubūru** buʿthirat*!; ⭐ **ʿĀdiyāt 100:9** — *idhā buʿthira mā fi'l-**qubūr***!).
- **Here it means:** "**They have despaired of the Hereafter, just as the disbelievers have despaired of the people of the graves.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A HAUNTING SIMILE — with TWO classical readings:** ⭐ **(1) *min aṣḥābi'l-qubūr* = the DEAD in the graves** — ⭐ **"they despair of the Hereafter, as the disbelievers despair of their DEAD ever coming back"** — ⭐ **total disbelief in resurrection.** ⭐ **(2) *min* = explaining *al-kuffār*: "the disbelievers WHO ARE IN the graves"** — ⭐ **"as the disbelievers who've already died despair (having SEEN their fate)"** — ⭐ **they lie in their graves knowing there's no hope left.** ⭐⭐ **Either way: no hope in the Hereafter = why you shouldn't ally with them — their whole worldview is hopeless.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAʾS* — DESPAIR — and ṬALĀQ 65:4** — *yaʾisna mina'l-maḥīḍ* — ⭐ **"despaired of menstruation" = MENOPAUSE (*sinn al-yaʾs*)** — ⭐ **a neutral, biological "end of hope."** ⭐ **Here: a spiritual "end of hope" — despair of the Hereafter.** ⭐⭐ ***Lā tayʾasū min rawḥi'llāh*** (12:87) — ⭐ **"DON'T despair of Allah's mercy" — for believers, despair is forbidden.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-QUBŪR* — the GRAVES thread:** ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:21** — *fa-**aqbarah*** (He BURIED him — honour). ⭐ **Takāthur 102:2** — *ḥattā zurtumu'l-**maqābir*** (until you VISIT the graveyards). ⭐ **Infiṭār 82:4 / ʿĀdiyāt 100:9** — the graves SCATTERED / OVERTURNED. ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:13** — ***aṣḥāb al-qubūr*** (the people of the graves — despairing). ⭐⭐ **The graves: buried, visited, overturned — and here, a picture of hopelessness.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***qad yaʾisū*** = "**they have ALREADY despaired**" (*qad* + past = established). ⭐⭐ ***min aṣḥābi'l-qubūri*** — ⚠️ ***min*** = **"of" (object of despair)** OR **"among" (explaining *al-kuffār*)** — two readings.
- **Why this exact word:** *yaʾs* — ⭐ **total hopelessness about the Hereafter — the last word on why such people can't be your allies.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They DESPAIR of the Hereafter as disbelievers despair of the DEAD in their GRAVES — TWO readings: (1) despair of the dead returning; (2) the dead disbelievers themselves, hopeless.** ⭐⭐ ***Yaʾs*: MENOPAUSE (*sinn al-yaʾs* — Ṭalāq 65:4) / despair of the Hereafter (60:13) — "DON'T despair of Allah's mercy" (12:87).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QUBŪR*: BURIED (80:21), VISITED (102:2), OVERTURNED (82:4, 100:9), HOPELESS (60:13).** ⚠️ ***Min* — "of" or "among".**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْمُقْسِطِينَ (v.8) vs الْقَاسِطُونَ (Jinn 72:14)** — ⚠️ **Form IV = JUST; Form I = UNJUST** — the "stripping" hamzah.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **تَوَلَّوْهُمْ (v.9) vs يَتَوَلَّ (v.6)** — ⚠️ ***tawallā* + OBJECT = BEFRIEND; alone = TURN AWAY.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **إِلَّا قَوْلَ إِبْرَاهِيمَ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **an EXCEPTION from the model** — not to be imitated (9:114).
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **لَا هُنَّ حِلٌّ لَّهُمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحِلُّونَ لَهُنَّ (v.10)** — ⚠️ **CHIASMUS** — noun then verb.
5. ⭐⭐⭐ **بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِنَّ وَأَرْجُلِهِنَّ (v.12)** — ⚠️ **idiom** — falsely attributing a child to the husband.
6. ⭐⭐ **الْمُمْتَحَنَة** — ⚠️ ***mumtaḥanah* (the woman EXAMINED)** / ***mumtaḥinah* (the surah that EXAMINES)** — both said.
7. ⭐⭐ **عَدُوِّي وَعَدُوَّكُمْ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **singular *ʿaduww* for a group.**
8. ⭐⭐ **إِن كُنتُمْ خَرَجْتُمْ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **answer DELETED.**
9. ⭐⭐ **وَوَدُّوا (v.2)** — ⚠️ **PAST** after jussives — their wish already exists.
10. ⭐⭐ **يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **attaches to EITHER side** (pause debate); ⭐ **variants of *yafṣilu*.**
11. ⭐⭐ **بَدَا ... الْعَدَاوَةُ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **masculine verb, feminine subject** (allowed); ⚠️ ***badā* (APPEAR) ≠ *badaʾa* (BEGIN).**
12. ⭐⭐ **أَبَدًا حَتَّىٰ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **"forever — UNTIL"** — the door stays open.
13. ⭐⭐ **أُسْوَةٌ** — ⚠️ **variant *iswah*.**
14. ⭐⭐ **بُرَآءُ (v.4)** — ⚠️ ***fuʿalāʾ* plural** of *barīʾ*.
15. ⭐⭐ **لَا جُنَاحَ (v.10)** — ⚠️ ***lā* of total negation** — NO blame at all.
16. ⭐⭐ **وَلْيَسْأَلُوا (v.10)** — ⚠️ ***lām al-amr*** ("LET them ask").
17. ⭐⭐ **مِنْ أَصْحَابِ الْقُبُورِ (v.13)** — ⚠️ ***min* = "of" or "among"** — two readings.
18. ⭐ **اسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُنَّ اللَّهَ (v.12)** — ⚠️ **Allāh as the OBJECT** of *istaghfir* ("ask Allah's forgiveness").
19. ⭐ **شَيْءٌ مِّنْ أَزْوَاجِكُمْ (v.11)** — ⚠️ ***shayʾ* used for persons** — delicate phrasing.
