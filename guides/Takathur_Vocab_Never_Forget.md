# Surah At-Takāthur (102) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–8)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It climbs a ladder of certainty — and you're standing on the bottom rung.**

⭐ **v.5:** *law taʿlamūna **ʿilma'l-yaqīn*** — "if only you knew with **the KNOWLEDGE of certainty**"
⭐ **v.7:** *la-tarawunnahā **ʿayna'l-yaqīn*** — "you will see it with **the EYE of certainty**"

⭐⭐ **Two different kinds of certainty, named one after the other:**
- ⭐ ***ʿilm al-yaqīn*** = certainty by **INFORMATION** — you've been *told*, and you believe it.
- ⭐ ***ʿayn al-yaqīn*** = certainty by **SIGHT** — you've *seen* it with your own eyes.

⭐ **Scholars name a third rung found elsewhere in the Qurʾān: ⭐ ***ḥaqq al-yaqīn*** — certainty by **EXPERIENCE**, being *inside* it.**

⭐⭐ **Classic illustration: ⭐ **being told there's a fire (*ʿilm*) → seeing the fire (*ʿayn*) → being burned by it (*ḥaqq*).** ⭐ **The surah's point: you could have climbed to the top rung by *knowing*. Instead you'll get there by *seeing*.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **Told → Seen → Touched.** ⭐ **The surah begs you to be convinced at "told."**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The distraction** (v.1–2) — ⭐ *competing for more distracted you — until you visited the graves.*
2. **The double warning** (v.3–4) — ⭐ *No! You will know. Then no! You will know.*
3. **The ladder** (v.5–7) — ⭐ **knowledge of certainty → eye of certainty.**
4. **The question** (v.8) — ⭐ *then you will be asked about the blessings.*

## §1 — The Distraction (v.1–2)

**1. أَلْهَاكُمُ — *alhākum*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**distraction / amusement**" family — ***lahw*** = diversion, entertainment, the stuff that pulls your attention off what matters; ⭐ ***malhā* = a place of amusement** (modern: a nightclub/amusement hall).
- **Here it means:** "**[Competing for] more has distracted you.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **you own this root from ʿAbasa 80:10** — *fa-anta ʿanhu **talahhā*** ("you were **distracted** from him").
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the comparison is sobering:** ⭐ **in ʿAbasa, even da'wah to powerful chiefs was filed as *lahw* when it cost a sincere seeker.** ⭐ **Here the *lahw* is something far less noble — ⭐ **just wanting MORE.**
  
  ⭐ **And notice the grammar: ⭐ *alhā-**KUM*** — the distraction is the **subject**, and **you** are the object.** ⭐ **You didn't pursue the distraction — ⭐ **it took you.** ⭐ **Form IV = it *caused* you to be distracted.**
  
  ⭐ **And the verb has no object for *what* you were distracted *from*.** ⭐ **Deleted on purpose — ⭐ **from everything that mattered.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV past** (*alhā*), weak-final, with *-kum* (you, plural). ⭐ **Past tense = it has already happened to you.**
- **Why this word:** *lahw* (**frivolous diversion**) rather than "occupied" — ⭐ **it judges the pursuit as *trivial*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Malhā* = an AMUSEMENT HALL.* ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:10: even da'wah to VIPs was *lahw* when misplaced. Here: just wanting MORE.** ⭐ **And YOU are the object — it took you.**

**2. التَّكَاثُرُ — *at-takāthur*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**muchness / abundance**" family — *kathura* = to be many; ⭐ ***kathīr* = much/many***; ⭐ ***al-Kawthar* = abundance** (Sūrah 108!). ⭐ **Form VI (*takāthara*) = ⭐ **competing with EACH OTHER in having more.**
- **Here it means:** "**the mutual rivalry for more.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the FORM is the whole diagnosis.**
  
  ⭐ **It's not *kathrah* (having much).** ⭐ **It's ***takāthur*** — ⭐ **Form VI, the MUTUAL form: "competing with ONE ANOTHER for more."**
  
  ⭐⭐ **So the problem isn't wealth. ⭐ **It's comparison.** ⭐ **You don't want more — ⭐ **you want more *than him*.**
  
  ⭐ **And note: ⭐ **WHAT you compete in isn't named.** ⭐ **Money? Children? Followers? Status? Square footage?** ⭐ **The deletion makes it universal — whatever your scoreboard is.**
  
  ⭐ **You own Form VI from FOUR places now:**
  - **Nabaʾ 78:1** — ***yatasāʾalūn*** (asking **each other**)
  - **Fajr 89:18** — ***taḥāḍḍūn*** (urging **each other** — they didn't)
  - **Balad 90:17 / ʿAṣr 103:3** — ***tawāṣaw*** (enjoining **each other** — the good version)
  - ⭐ **Takāthur 102:1** — ***takāthur*** (out-competing **each other**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **Form VI shows what a society does *together*.** ⭐ **Fajr's people wouldn't urge each other to feed the poor; ⭐ **Takāthur's people *will* compete with each other for more.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form VI verbal noun** (*tafāʿul*) — ⭐ **spot the *ta-* + long *ā*.** ⭐ **Definite (*at-*) = "THE well-known rivalry."**
- **Why this word:** *takāthur* (Form VI) over *kathrah* — ⭐ **it names the **social engine** of greed: rivalry.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NOT "having much" — "out-competing EACH OTHER for more."** ⭐ **The disease is COMPARISON.** ⭐⭐ **Form VI = mutual: *yatasāʾalūn* (78:1), *taḥāḍḍūn* (89:18), *tawāṣaw* (90:17), *takāthur* (102:1).** ⭐ **And *al-Kawthar* = same root.**

**3. حَتَّىٰ زُرْتُمُ الْمَقَابِرَ — *ḥattā zurtumu'l-maqābir*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *zurtum* — ⭐ the "**visiting**" family — *zāra* = to visit; ⭐ ***ziyārah* = A VISIT*** (⭐ **you use it for visiting Madīnah, visiting family**); *zāʾir* = a visitor. *maqābir* — ⭐ **plural of *maqbarah* = a GRAVEYARD** — from *qabr* (⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:21 *aqbarah*, Infiṭār 82:4, ʿĀdiyāt 100:9**).
- **Here it means:** "**until you visited the graveyards.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the most quietly profound verb in the surah — and a bedouin is said to have caught it instantly.**
  
  ⭐ **It doesn't say "until you were buried" or "until you died."** ⭐ **It says ***zurtum*** — "**until you VISITED**."**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And what's the defining feature of a visit? ⭐ **YOU LEAVE.** ⭐ **A *zāʾir* doesn't stay.** ⭐ **There's a well-known report of an Arab who heard this verse and said: ⭐ ***"By Allah — a visitor does not remain. They will be raised."*** ⭐ **The grave is a stop, not a destination.**
  
  ⭐ **Two classical readings of the whole verse:** ⭐ **(a) you competed until death took you to the graves; ⭐ (b) the tribes literally went to the graveyards to **count their dead** as part of the boasting — "we have more graves than you!"** ⭐ **Either way, the competition doesn't stop until the cemetery.**
  
  ⭐ **The *qabr* root across four surahs: ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:21** (He *gave* you a grave — a blessing), **Infiṭār 82:4** (graves *overturned*), **ʿĀdiyāt 100:9** (what's in them *tipped out*), ⭐ **Takāthur 102:2** (you only *visit* them).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ḥattā*** = "**until**" — a limit. ⭐ ***Maqābir*** is a **diptote** (*mafāʿil* plural) — ⚠️ **fatḥah, no tanwīn.** ⭐ ***Zurtum*** — hollow verb (z-w-r), the long vowel shortens before the suffix.
- **Why this word:** *zurtum* (**visited**) over *dakhaltum* (entered) — ⭐ **because a visit implies **departure** — the resurrection hidden inside one verb.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ziyārah* = A VISIT. And A VISITOR DOESN'T STAY.** ⭐ **The resurrection is hidden in one verb.** ⭐⭐ **The *qabr* thread: GIVEN a grave (80:21) → graves OVERTURNED (82:4) → contents TIPPED OUT (100:9) → you only VISIT (102:2).**

## §2 — The Double Warning (v.3–4)

**4. كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ ثُمَّ كَلَّا — *kallā sawfa taʿlamūn, thumma kallā…*** · v.3–4
- **Root feelings:** *kallā* — ⭐ **the stop-sign particle**: "No! Absolutely not!" *sawfa* — ⭐ **the future particle** ("will"). *taʿlamūn* — **you will know**.
- **Here it means:** "**No! You will come to know. Then — no! You will come to know.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **you own this almost word-for-word from Nabaʾ 78:4–5.**
  
  ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:4–5 — *kallā **sa**-yaʿlamūn · thumma kallā **sa**-yaʿlamūn*** ("No! **THEY** will know. Then no! **THEY** will know.")
  ⭐ **Takāthur 102:3–4 — *kallā **sawfa** taʿlamūn · thumma kallā **sawfa** taʿlamūn*** ("No! **YOU** will know. Then no! **YOU** will know.")
  
  ⭐⭐ **Two shifts:**
  - ⭐ **THEY → YOU.** ⭐ **Nabaʾ talked *about* the deniers. Takāthur turns and talks *to* you.**
  - ⭐ ***sa-* → *sawfa*.** ⭐ ***Sa-*** = near future; ⭐ ***sawfa*** = a touch **further** future. ⭐ **Some scholars read it as: you'll know at death (v.3), and again at resurrection (v.4).** ⭐ **Two moments of knowing.**
  
  ⭐ **And the *thumma* escalation is the same:** ⭐ **say it, pause, say it harder.** ⭐ **Like a parent: "You'll see." … "Oh, you will SEE."**
  
  ⭐ **And note — the threat is deliberately **unfinished**: ⭐ **know *what*?** ⭐ **Not said.** ⭐ **An open threat is scarier than a stated one.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **سَوْفَ + present = future** ("will"). ⚠️ ⭐ **Compare *sa-* (Nabaʾ 78:4) — both future markers, *sawfa* often slightly more distant.**
- **Why this word:** the **repeated, unfinished** threat ⭐ **forces you to supply what you'll know.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:4–5 = "THEY will know" (*sa-*). ⭐ **Takāthur 102:3–4 = "YOU will know" (*sawfa*).** ⭐ **Same double warning — now pointed at you.** ⭐ **Possibly: you'll know at DEATH, then again at RESURRECTION.**

## §3 — The Ladder of Certainty (v.5–7)

**5. لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عِلْمَ الْيَقِينِ — *law taʿlamūna ʿilma'l-yaqīn*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *law* — ⭐ **"if only / if"** — for a condition that **didn't happen**. *ʿilm* — **knowledge**. *yaqīn* — ⭐ the "**certainty / settled conviction**" family — *yaqina* = **to be certain, for doubt to settle out like still water**; ⭐ ***al-yaqīn* is also a Qurʾānic name for DEATH*** (15:99 — "worship your Lord until *al-yaqīn* comes to you" — the one certain thing).
- **Here it means:** "**No! If only you knew with the knowledge of certainty…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **two masterstrokes in five words.**
  
  ⭐ **ONE — THE DELETED ANSWER.** ⭐ ***Law*** introduces a condition: "**If you knew…**" — ⭐ **and then the sentence just… stops.** ⭐ **"If you knew with certainty — [then ___]."** ⭐ **The *jawāb* (answer) of *law* is deliberately DELETED.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Why?** ⭐ **Because no filler would be big enough.** ⭐ ***If you truly knew — you'd never have competed. You'd never have laughed. You'd never have slept.*** ⭐ **The reader's mind supplies the answer, and it's always bigger than anything written.** ⭐ ***Ḥadhf jawāb ash-sharṭ*** — a classic Qurʾānic technique.
  
  ⭐ **TWO — *ʿilm al-yaqīn*, the first rung.** ⭐ **Knowledge-certainty: ⭐ **certainty by INFORMATION.** ⭐ **You were told. That *should* have been enough.**
  
  ⭐ **And *al-yaqīn* = DEATH elsewhere** — ⭐ **because death is the one thing nobody doubts.** ⭐ **Free, haunting hook.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***law*** = **hypothetical condition** (contrary to fact). ⚠️ ⭐ **Its answer is DELETED here.** ⭐ ***ʿilma'l-yaqīn*** is a cognate-style accusative (*maf'ūl muṭlaq*) — "know **a knowing of** certainty."
- **Why this word:** ⭐ **deleting the answer** is far more powerful than any stated consequence.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"If only you knew with certainty — [ ]."** ⭐ **The ANSWER IS DELETED.** ⭐ **Your mind fills it, and always with something bigger.** ⭐⭐ **Rung 1: *ʿilm al-yaqīn* = TOLD.** ⭐ **And *al-yaqīn* = DEATH in 15:99 — the one certainty.**

**6. لَتَرَوُنَّ الْجَحِيمَ — *la-tarawunna'l-jaḥīm*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *tarawunna* — ⭐ **"you WILL see"** (from *raʾā*) with heavy emphasis. *jaḥīm* — ⭐ the "**fiercely blazing fire**" family — you own it from **Nāziʿāt 79:36, 39**, **Takwīr 81:12**, **Infiṭār 82:14**, **Muṭaffifīn 83:16**.
- **Here it means:** "**You will surely see the Blaze.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the grammar carries the force — and it's a construction you've seen once before in its light form.**
  
  ⭐ ***La-tarawunna*** = ⭐ ***lām* of oath + verb + HEAVY NŪN OF EMPHASIS (*nūn at-tawkīd ath-thaqīlah*, ـنَّ).** ⭐ **The strongest "you WILL" Arabic can produce.** ⭐ **Like an oath: "**By Allah, you WILL see it.**"
  
  ⭐⭐ **Compare ʿAlaq 96:15** — *la-nasfaʿan* — ⭐ **the LIGHT nūn of emphasis (written as an alif).** ⭐ **Here: the HEAVY nūn (doubled).** ⭐ **Two surahs, both emphatic nūns — learn them as a pair.**
  
  ⭐ **And the shift is crucial:** ⭐ **v.5 was ***taʿlamūn*** (KNOW). ⭐ **v.6 is ***tarawunna*** (SEE).** ⭐ **You refused to *know* it — so you'll *see* it.**
  
  ⭐ **The "seeing" thread you own: ⭐ **Balad 90:7** (*"does he think no one SAW him?"*), **ʿAlaq 96:14** (*"Allah SEES"*), **Zalzalah 99:7–8** (*"he will SEE it"* — his deeds), ⭐ **Takāthur 102:6** (*"you will SEE the Blaze"*).** ⭐ **First you see your deeds, then their destination.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ ***lām al-qasam* + *nūn at-tawkīd ath-thaqīlah*** = maximum emphasis. ⚠️ ⭐ **The original *tarawūna* loses its *nūn* of plural and gains the doubled emphatic *nūn*; the wāw takes a ḍammah to avoid two sukūns.** **A tricky form — recognize it, don't parse it from scratch.**
- **Why this word:** the **heavy nūn** converts a prediction into ⭐ **something sworn**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ـنَّ = THE HEAVY NŪN OF EMPHASIS** — "you **WILL**, by Allah." ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:15's *la-nasfaʿan* = the LIGHT nūn.** ⭐ **Learn them as a pair.** ⭐⭐ **v.5 = KNOW. v.6 = SEE.** ⭐ **You refused to know; you'll see.**

**7. عَيْنَ الْيَقِينِ — *ʿayna'l-yaqīn*** · v.7
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ ***ʿayn*** = **an EYE** — ⭐ **and also a SPRING** (*ʿayn jāriyah*, Ghāshiyah 88:12), ⭐ **and the ESSENCE/very self of a thing** (*ʿayn ash-shayʾ* = the thing itself).
- **Here it means:** "**Then you will surely see it with the eye of certainty.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the second rung of the ladder — and the word *ʿayn* has three meanings that all fit.**
  
  ⭐ **Rung 1 (v.5) — *ʿilm al-yaqīn* — certainty by **HEARING about it**.**
  ⭐ **Rung 2 (v.7) — *ʿayn al-yaqīn* — certainty by **SEEING it**.**
  ⭐ ***(Rung 3, elsewhere — *ḥaqq al-yaqīn* — certainty by **BEING IN it**.)***
  
  ⭐⭐ **And *ʿayn* means:**
  - ⭐ **an EYE** — you see it with your own eyes
  - ⭐ **the ESSENCE** — you see **the thing itself**, not a description
  - ⭐ **a SPRING** — (and the irony: the Blaze is the opposite of a spring)
  
  ⭐⭐ **The doctor's version (useful for you):** ⭐ ***ʿilm al-yaqīn* = reading the lab report.** ⭐ ***ʿayn al-yaqīn* = seeing the scan yourself.** ⭐ ***ḥaqq al-yaqīn* = being the patient.** ⭐ **Same fact, three degrees of certainty.**
  
  ⭐ **And note the *thumma* (then) — ⭐ **v.6 "you'll see the Blaze," v.7 "THEN you'll see it with the eye of certainty."** ⭐ **First a sighting; then a sighting that leaves zero doubt.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **same heavy-nūn structure as v.6** (*la-tarawunna-hā*), now with the pronoun *-hā* attached. ⭐ ***ʿAyna'l-yaqīn*** is accusative — a *maf'ūl muṭlaq*-like phrase ("a seeing **of** certainty").
- **Why this word:** *ʿayn* (**the eye / the thing itself**) — ⭐ **the shortest word for "no more intermediaries."**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TOLD (*ʿilm*, v.5) → SEEN (*ʿayn*, v.7) → BEING IN IT (*ḥaqq*, elsewhere).** ⭐⭐ **Doctor's version: the lab REPORT → the SCAN → being the PATIENT.** ⭐ ***ʿAyn* = eye, spring, and the THING ITSELF.**

## §4 — The Question (v.8)

**8. ثُمَّ لَتُسْأَلُنَّ يَوْمَئِذٍ عَنِ النَّعِيمِ — *thumma la-tusʾalunna yawmaʾidhin ʿani'n-naʿīm*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *tusʾalunna* — ⭐ the "**asking**" family (*suʾāl* = a question) — ⭐ **PASSIVE**: "you WILL BE ASKED." *naʿīm* — ⭐ the "**blessing / softness / comfort**" family — *niʿmah* = favour; ⭐ ***naʿam* = YES***; *nāʿim* = **soft** — you own it from **Insān 76:20**, **Infiṭār 82:13**, **Muṭaffifīn 83:22, 24**, **Ghāshiyah 88:8**.
- **Here it means:** "**Then you will surely be asked, that Day, about the blessings.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the twist ending — and it's a word you've only ever seen as a REWARD.**
  
  ⭐ **Every previous appearance of *naʿīm* in your study was **PARADISE**:**
  - **Insān 76:20** — *raʾayta **naʿīman*** (you see **bliss**)
  - **Infiṭār 82:13** — *la-fī **naʿīm*** (the righteous are in **bliss**)
  - **Muṭaffifīn 83:22, 24** — ***naḍrata'n-naʿīm*** (the **glow of bliss** on their faces)
  - ⭐ **Takāthur 102:8** — *you will be **ASKED ABOUT** the naʿīm*
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Here, for the first time, *naʿīm* isn't a reward — ⭐ **it's an EXAMINATION TOPIC.** ⭐ **The blessings you competed for (*takāthur*, v.1) become the subject of the questioning.**
  
  ⭐ **And it's *naʿīm* in DUNYA — every blessing.** ⭐ **A well-known hadith: the Prophet ﷺ, after a simple meal of dates, meat, and cool water, said this is among the *naʿīm* you will be asked about.** ⭐ **Not just mansions — ⭐ **a cold drink of water.**
  
  ⭐ **And the full circle: ⭐ **v.1 — you were distracted by **getting MORE**.** ⭐ **v.8 — you'll be asked about **everything you GOT**.** ⭐ **The surah opens with acquisition and closes with the audit.**
  
  ⭐ **And *tusʾalunna* is PASSIVE** — ⭐ **you don't get to choose the questions.** ⭐ **Compare Takwīr 81:8** — *wa idha'l-mawʾūdatu **suʾilat*** (the buried girl **was asked**). ⭐ **There the victim was asked; here you are.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **THIRD heavy nūn in three verses** (*la-tarawunna*, *la-tarawunnahā*, *la-tusʾalunna*). ⭐⭐ **Triple oath-emphasis.** ⭐ **Passive + *ʿan* = "asked **ABOUT**."**
- **Why this word:** *naʿīm* (**the reward-word**) — ⭐ **turning the language of Paradise into the language of the exam is the surah's sting.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Naʿīm* was PARADISE in four surahs (76:20, 82:13, 83:22, 88:8).** ⭐ **Here it's the EXAM QUESTION.** ⭐⭐ **Even a COLD DRINK OF WATER counts** (the hadith). ⭐ **v.1: you chased MORE. v.8: you're AUDITED on all of it.** ⭐ **And THREE heavy nūns in three verses.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **the ANSWER of *law* is DELETED.** ⭐ **"If only you knew —" and the sentence stops.** **Don't look for the missing clause; the silence is the rhetoric.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **النَّعِيمِ (v.8)** — ⚠️ **here it's what you're QUESTIONED about**, not a reward. ⭐ **The only time in your study it's flipped.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **عِلْمَ الْيَقِينِ (v.5) vs عَيْنَ الْيَقِينِ (v.7)** — ⚠️ **two DIFFERENT certainties**: by being TOLD vs by SEEING. ⭐ **Don't merge them.**
4. ⭐⭐ **لَتَرَوُنَّ / لَتُسْأَلُنَّ (v.6–8)** — ⚠️ **HEAVY nūn of emphasis** (ـنَّ). ⭐ **Compare the LIGHT nūn in *la-nasfaʿan* (ʿAlaq 96:15).**
5. ⭐⭐ **التَّكَاثُرُ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **Form VI = competing with EACH OTHER**, not merely "having much." ⭐ **The disease is comparison.**
6. ⭐⭐ **زُرْتُمُ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **"VISITED," not "entered."** ⭐ **A visitor leaves — the resurrection is implied.**
7. ⭐ **سَوْفَ (v.3–4) vs سَـ (Nabaʾ 78:4–5)** — ⚠️ **both future markers**; *sawfa* often a touch more distant.
8. ⭐ **الْمَقَابِرَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **a DIPTOTE** (*mafāʿil*): fatḥah, no tanwīn.
9. ⭐ **أَلْهَاكُمُ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **YOU are the object** — the rivalry distracted *you*. ⭐ **And what you were distracted FROM is deleted.**
10. ⭐ **الْيَقِينِ** — ⚠️ **elsewhere (15:99) *al-yaqīn* = DEATH**, the one thing no one doubts.
