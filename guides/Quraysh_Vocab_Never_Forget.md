# Surah Quraysh (106) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–4)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**Its two caravan routes go to the two countries whose names you learned in Surah Al-Balad.**

⭐ **v.2:** *riḥlata'**sh-shitāʾi** wa'**ṣ-ṣayf*** — "the journey of **WINTER** and of **SUMMER**."

⭐⭐ **Quraysh ran two great trade caravans a year:**
- ⭐ **WINTER → YEMEN** (south, warm)
- ⭐ **SUMMER → ASH-SHĀM** (north, Greater Syria, cooler)

⭐⭐⭐ **NOW RECALL BALAD 90:18–19:**
- ⭐ ***aṣḥābu'l-**maymanah*** — "people of the RIGHT" — ⭐ **from *y-m-n*, the root of *AL-YAMAN***
- ⭐ ***aṣḥābu'l-**mashʾamah*** — "people of the LEFT" — ⭐ **from *sh-ʾ-m*, the root of *ASH-SHĀM***

⭐⭐⭐ **The two destinations of Quraysh's caravans are the two countries named after the RIGHT hand and the LEFT hand.** ⭐ **Face east from Makkah: Yemen is on your right, Shām on your left.** ⭐ **Their entire economy ran between the two words Balad used for the saved and the damned.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **WINTER → Yemen (*yamīn*, RIGHT). SUMMER → Shām (*shimāl*, LEFT).** ⭐ **Two caravans, two hands, one House in the middle.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The security** (v.1–2) — ⭐ **for the familiar safety of Quraysh — their winter and summer journeys.**
2. **The command** (v.3) — ⭐ ***so let them worship the Lord of THIS House.***
3. **The two gifts** (v.4) — ⭐ **who FED them against hunger and SECURED them against fear.**

⭐ **Context worth knowing: many classical commentators connect this surah's opening *lām* ("**for**…") directly to Sūrat al-Fīl just before it** — ⭐ ***"We made the Elephant army like chewed straw… FOR the security of Quraysh."*** ⭐ **They remain two separate surahs in the muṣḥaf, but read in sequence they form one argument: ⭐ **the House was protected → so its people prospered → so they owe its Lord.**

## §1 — The Security (v.1–2)

**1. لِإِيلَافِ — *li-īlāf*** · v.1, 2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**FAMILIARITY / becoming accustomed / bonding**" family — *alifa* = **to become familiar with, to grow used to, to feel at home with**; ⭐ ***ulfah* = INTIMACY, close companionship***; ⭐ ***taʾlīf* = COMPOSING / authoring a book*** (⭐ **putting things together so they belong!**); ⭐ ***muʾallif* = an AUTHOR***; ⭐ **and *allafa bayna qulūbihim* = "He joined their hearts together."** ⭐ ***Īlāf* (Form IV verbal noun) = making something familiar and secure; also, historically, **the trade covenants/safe-passage pacts** Quraysh made with surrounding powers.**
- **Here it means:** "**For the [accustomed] security of Quraysh — their security in the journey of winter and summer.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the word is repeated in two consecutive verses, and its root makes it far richer than "protection."**
  
  ⭐ ***Īlāf* isn't "safety" in the military sense.*** ⭐ **It's ⭐ **the safety of THE FAMILIAR** — ⭐ **routine, habit, the comfort of something that always works.** ⭐ **Quraysh's caravans crossed bandit-filled desert, and nobody touched them — ⭐ **because they were the guardians of the Kaʿbah.** ⭐ **The protection had become so routine they stopped noticing it.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And THAT is the surah's critique.** ⭐ **The danger of *īlāf* is that familiarity breeds forgetfulness.** ⭐ **You stop seeing the blessing because you've never been without it.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the historical sense — ⭐ ***īlāf* = the PACTS of safe passage** Quraysh negotiated (with Byzantium, Persia, Abyssinia, Yemen)** — ⭐ **they thought **their diplomacy** kept them safe.** ⭐ **The surah says: ⭐ **no — the House did.**
  
  ⭐ **Free hooks you use: ⭐ ***muʾallif* = an AUTHOR***, ⭐ ***taʾlīf* = composing a book***, ⭐ ***ulfah* = intimacy.***
  
  ⭐ **Why REPEATED (v.1 and v.2)?** ⭐ **v.2 is a *badal* — it restates and **specifies** v.1: ⭐ **"for their *īlāf* — [that is,] their *īlāf* of the winter and summer journey."** ⭐ **Name it, then zoom in. ⭐ **Same technique as Takwīr 81:15–16, Aʿlā 87:18–19.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ **the opening *li-* ("FOR") has a debated attachment** — ⭐ **(a) to Sūrat al-Fīl ("We did that FOR Quraysh's security"), ⭐ (b) to v.3 ("FOR their security, LET THEM worship"), ⭐ (c) a *lām* of wonder ("Marvel at Quraysh's security!").** ⭐ **All classical.**
- **Why this word:** *īlāf* (**the familiar**) rather than *amn* (security) — ⭐ **because the point is a blessing so regular it became invisible.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUʾALLIF* = AN AUTHOR; *ulfah* = INTIMACY.** ⭐ ***Īlāf* = the safety of THE FAMILIAR.** ⭐⭐⭐ **A blessing so routine they STOPPED SEEING IT.** ⭐ **They credited their PACTS; the surah credits the HOUSE.** ⭐ **And the *li-* may link straight back to Sūrat al-Fīl.**

**2. قُرَيْشٍ — *Quraysh*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ **the tribe's name — a DIMINUTIVE form (*fuʿayl*)** — with two classical etymologies: ⭐ **(a) from *qarasha* = to GATHER / to earn by trade** (⭐ **they gathered together and lived by commerce!**); ⭐ **(b) from *qirsh* = a SHARK** — ⭐ **the dominant creature of the sea, which eats and is not eaten.**
- **Here it means:** "**Quraysh**" — the Prophet's ﷺ own tribe, custodians of the Kaʿbah.
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **one of the very few tribes NAMED in the Qurʾān — and both etymologies fit the surah.**
  
  ⭐ **Etymology (a) — *qarasha* = to gather/earn by trade:** ⭐ **perfect for a surah about **trade caravans**.** ⭐ **The name itself means "traders."**
  
  ⭐ **Etymology (b) — *qirsh* = SHARK:** ⭐ **the creature no one dares attack.** ⭐ **Exactly like the caravans: ⭐ **untouched in the desert.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the DIMINUTIVE (*Quraysh*, not *Qarsh*) adds affection or smallness** — ⭐ **"the little gatherers" or "the little shark."** ⭐ **Small tribe, untouchable — ⭐ **but only because of the House.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note: the modern coin *qirsh* (piastre) shares these letters** — ⭐ **a fun echo for a trading tribe.**
  
  ⭐ **And notice the surah's intimacy: ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ's OWN tribe is addressed by name** — ⭐ **the people who knew the Kaʿbah best and owed it most.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fuʿayl*** = the **diminutive** pattern (*taṣghīr*). ⭐ **Genitive by *iḍāfah* after *īlāf*.**
- **Why this word:** naming the tribe directly ⭐ **makes the surah a personal letter**, not a general sermon.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Quraysh* = a DIMINUTIVE** — ⭐ **from *qarasha* (to GATHER/TRADE) or *qirsh* (a SHARK).** ⭐ **Traders, and untouchable.** ⭐ **One of the very few tribes NAMED in the Qurʾān — the Prophet's ﷺ own.**

**3. رِحْلَةَ الشِّتَاءِ وَالصَّيْفِ — *riḥlata'sh-shitāʾi wa'ṣ-ṣayf*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *riḥlah* — ⭐ **a JOURNEY / trip** — from *raḥala* = **to set out, to saddle up and depart**; ⭐ ***raḥl* = a camel saddle***; ⭐ ***riḥlah* = a TRIP / voyage*** (⭐ **modern: *riḥlah jawwiyyah* = a FLIGHT!**). *shitāʾ* — **WINTER**. *ṣayf* — **SUMMER**.
- **Here it means:** "**the journey of winter and summer.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the BEST cross-surah payoff in this batch — the geography connects to Balad.**
  
  ⭐ **The winter caravan went SOUTH to YEMEN (warm); the summer caravan went NORTH to ASH-SHĀM (cooler).** ⭐ **Seasonal logistics, perfectly planned.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And Balad 90:18–19 gave you the roots:**
  - ⭐ ***maymanah*** (right/blessed) ← ***y-m-n*** ← ⭐ ***AL-YAMAN***
  - ⭐ ***mashʾamah*** (left/ill-omened) ← ***sh-ʾ-m*** ← ⭐ ***ASH-SHĀM***
  
  ⭐⭐ **Facing the sunrise from Makkah: Yemen is on your RIGHT, Shām on your LEFT.** ⭐ **The countries are literally named "Right-land" and "Left-land."**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **So Quraysh's entire livelihood shuttled between ⭐ **the right and the left** — ⭐ **the same two words Balad used for **the companions of Paradise and the companions of Fire**.** ⭐ **And the question the surah leaves open: ⭐ **which hand will Quraysh end up on?**
  
  ⭐ **And the pairing *shitāʾ / ṣayf* is a *ṭibāq* (opposite-pair) forming a *merism*** — ⭐ **"winter and summer" = ALL YEAR ROUND.** ⭐ **The protection never took a season off.**
  
  ⭐ **Free hook: ⭐ ***riḥlah* = a TRIP*** — ⭐ **you'd use it for any journey.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***riḥlata*** is accusative (object of the verbal noun *īlāf*). ⚠️ ⭐ ***Riḥlah* is SINGULAR** though there were two journeys — ⭐ **read as "the journey-system of winter and summer," or a generic singular.**
- **Why this word:** naming **both seasons** ⭐ **makes the security **year-round** and therefore invisible.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WINTER → YEMEN (*yamīn*, RIGHT). SUMMER → SHĀM (*shimāl*, LEFT).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Balad 90:18–19: *maymanah* (Yemen's root) = the SAVED; *mashʾamah* (Shām's root) = the DAMNED.** ⭐ **Their trade ran between the two hands.** ⭐⭐ ***Riḥlah jawwiyyah* = A FLIGHT.** ⭐ **Winter + summer = ALL YEAR.**

## §2 — The Command (v.3)

**4. فَلْيَعْبُدُوا رَبَّ هَٰذَا الْبَيْتِ — *fal-yaʿbudū rabba hādha'l-bayt*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *yaʿbudū* — ⭐ the "**servitude / worship**" family — ***ʿabd* = a SERVANT***; *ʿibādah* = worship (⭐ **servanthood**). *bayt* — ⭐ **a HOUSE** (⭐ ***al-Bayt al-Ḥarām*, *Bayt Allāh* — the Kaʿbah; and *bayt* = a line of POETRY!**).
- **Here it means:** "**So let them worship the Lord of THIS House.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the demonstrative "THIS" is the key — and you've now seen it three times.**
  
  ⭐ **The *hādhā* ("THIS") thread:**
  - **Balad 90:1** — *lā uqsimu bi-**hādha'l-balad*** (⭐ **"I swear by THIS city"**)
  - **Tīn 95:3** — *wa **hādha'l-baladi'l-amīn*** (⭐ **"by THIS secure city"**)
  - ⭐ **Quraysh 106:3** — *rabba **hādha'l-bayt*** (⭐ **"the Lord of THIS House"**)
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Three surahs, and the camera ZOOMS IN: ⭐ **this CITY → this SECURE city → this HOUSE.** ⭐ **From the town to the building at its centre.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And "THIS" makes it physical and immediate** — ⭐ **the building they could see from their doorsteps, the one they guarded, profited from, and filled with idols.** ⭐ **Not "a house" — ⭐ ***the one right there***.
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the *fa-* is CAUSAL: ⭐ **"SO let them worship."** ⭐ **Because of v.1–2.** ⭐ **The argument is a straight line: ⭐ **the House protected you → your trade prospered → SO worship the House's Lord.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **And note the precise object: ⭐ **"the LORD of this House" — not the House itself.** ⭐ **They honoured the building and worshipped idols inside it.** ⭐ **The surah redirects: ⭐ **the House is not the point; its Owner is.**
  
  ⭐ **The *lām* of command (*fal-yaʿbudū*)** — ⭐ **you own it from ʿAbasa 80:24 (*fal-yanẓur*), Ṭāriq 86:5 (*fal-yanẓur*), ʿAlaq 96:17 (*fal-yadʿu*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **لام الأمر** on a third-person verb — "**LET them** worship." ⚠️ ⭐ **Jussive: *yaʿbudū* drops its final nūn.** ⭐ **The *fa-* is causal ("so / therefore").**
- **Why this word:** "**the Lord of THIS House**" rather than "Allah" — ⭐ **it ties the obligation to **the exact source of their prosperity**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ZOOM: "THIS city" (90:1) → "THIS SECURE city" (95:3) → "THIS HOUSE" (106:3).** ⭐ **Three surahs, closing in on the Kaʿbah.** ⭐⭐ **"The LORD of this House" — not the House itself.** ⭐ **The *fa-* = "SO" — because of everything before.** ⭐ **And *bayt* also = a line of POETRY.**

## §3 — The Two Gifts (v.4)

**5. أَطْعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٍ — *aṭʿamahum min jūʿ*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *aṭʿama* — ⭐ **Form IV of *ṭaʿima* (to taste/eat)** = **to FEED someone**; ⭐ ***ṭaʿām* = FOOD***; ⭐ ***maṭʿam* = A RESTAURANT*** (⭐ **a place of eating!**). *jūʿ* — ⭐ **HUNGER** (*jāʾiʿ* = hungry; ⭐ ***majāʿah* = FAMINE***).
- **Here it means:** "**who fed them against hunger.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the first gift — and it answers hunger threads from FOUR surahs.**
  
  ⭐ **Makkah sits in a barren valley — ⭐ **no farms, no rivers, nothing grows.** ⭐ **Yet its people ate well, because the caravans (v.2) brought the world's food to them.** ⭐ **Food in a place that produces none.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The hunger/feeding thread you own:**
  - **Insān 76:8** — *wa **yuṭʿimūna'ṭ-ṭaʿāma** ʿalā ḥubbihi* (⭐ **the righteous FEED others despite loving the food**)
  - **Ghāshiyah 88:7** — *lā yusminu wa **lā yughnī min jūʿ*** (⭐ **Hell's "food" does NOT avail against HUNGER**)
  - **Fajr 89:18** — *wa lā taḥāḍḍūna ʿalā **ṭaʿāmi'l-miskīn*** (⭐ **you don't urge FEEDING the poor**)
  - **Balad 90:14** — *aw **iṭʿāmun** fī yawmin dhī **masghabah*** (⭐ **FEEDING on a day of HUNGER** — the steep climb)
  - ⭐ **Quraysh 106:4** — ***aṭʿamahum min jūʿ*** (⭐ **HE fed THEM against hunger**)
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And here's the sting: ⭐ **Allah FED Quraysh against hunger.** ⭐ **Fajr 89:18 said they wouldn't even urge each other to feed the poor.** ⭐ **Balad 90:14 said feeding the hungry is the steep climb they didn't attempt.** ⭐⭐ **They received the very thing they refused to pass on.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And compare Ghāshiyah 88:7 exactly: ⭐ ***lā yughnī MIN JŪʿ*** (Hell's food **doesn't** protect against hunger) vs. ⭐ ***aṭʿamahum MIN JŪʿ*** (Allah **did** protect them against hunger).** ⭐ **Same phrase *min jūʿ*, opposite outcome.**
  
  ⭐ **Free hook: ⭐ ***maṭʿam* = A RESTAURANT***.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV** (*aṭʿama*) = **causative** (to cause to eat = to feed). ⭐ ***Min*** here = "**from / against**" — ⭐ **rescuing them FROM hunger.** ⭐ ***Jūʿin*** is **indefinite** — ⭐ **"from (any) hunger."**
- **Why this word:** *aṭʿama* (**He fed**) with Allah as subject — ⭐ **it credits the food to Him, not to the caravans.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Maṭʿam* = A RESTAURANT; *majāʿah* = FAMINE.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:7: Hell's food does NOT protect *min jūʿ*. ⭐ **Quraysh 106:4: Allah DID feed them *min jūʿ*.** ⭐ **Same phrase.** ⭐⭐ **And Fajr 89:18 / Balad 90:14: they wouldn't feed the hungry — ⭐ **yet THEY were fed.**

**6. وَآمَنَهُم مِّنْ خَوْفٍ — *wa āmanahum min khawf*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *āmana* — ⭐ **Form IV of *amina* (to be safe)** = **to MAKE SAFE, to grant security**; ⭐⭐ **the root of *amn* (security), *amānah* (a trust), *ĪMĀN* (faith), *al-Amīn* (the Prophet's ﷺ title), and *āmīn*.** *khawf* — ⭐ **FEAR** (⚠️ **plain fear — compare *khashyah*, awe-filled fear**).
- **Here it means:** "**and made them safe from fear.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the final gift — and it completes the most important thread in the last three surahs.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **TĪN 95:3 — *wa hādha'l-baladi'l-**AMĪN*** — ⭐ "**by this SECURE city**."
  ⭐⭐⭐ **QURAYSH 106:4 — *wa **ĀMANAHUM** min khawf*** — ⭐ "**and He MADE THEM SECURE** from fear."
  
  ⭐⭐ **Tīn called the city *amīn* (secure). ⭐ **Quraysh tells you WHO made it secure.** ⭐ **Same root, and the second answers the first.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **AND THIS IS IBRĀHĪM'S DUʿĀʾ, ANSWERED.** ⭐ **When Ibrāhīm (peace be upon him) left his family in this barren valley, he prayed (2:126): ⭐ ***"My Lord, make this a SECURE land (balad āmin) and provide its people with FRUITS."*** ⭐⭐ **TWO requests: ⭐ **SECURITY** and **FOOD**.** ⭐ **And Quraysh 106:4 lists exactly **TWO gifts**: ⭐ ***aṭʿamahum* (He FED them) + *āmanahum* (He made them SECURE).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ibrāhīm asked for two things; the surah confirms both were granted — to his descendants, who then forgot Whom to thank.**
  
  ⭐ **And the root's link to faith is the final, quiet point: ⭐ ***amn* (security) and *īmān* (faith) are the SAME ROOT.*** ⭐ **The One who gave them *amn* is asking for *īmān* in return.** ⭐ **Safety and faith are one word-family; ⭐ **the gift and its proper response share three letters.**
  
  ⭐ **And *āmīn*** — ⭐ **you say it after al-Fātiḥah: "**make it secure / so be it**."** ⭐ **Free hook.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV** (*āmana*) = **causative** (to cause to be safe). ⭐ ***Min khawfin*** — ⭐ **indefinite: "from (any) fear."** ⭐ **Perfectly parallel with *min jūʿin*: ⭐ **two Form IV verbs, two *min*'s, two indefinite threats.**
- **Why this word:** *āmana* (**made secure**) — ⭐ **because *amn* and *īmān* share a root, the gift points straight at the response.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Tīn 95:3: the city is *AMĪN* (secure). ⭐ **Quraysh 106:4: *ĀMANAHUM* — HE made it secure.** ⭐⭐⭐ **IBRĀHĪM'S DUʿĀʾ (2:126) asked for TWO things: SECURITY + FOOD. ⭐ **Quraysh 106:4 lists TWO gifts: FED + SECURED.** ⭐ **Answered.** ⭐⭐ ***Amn* (security) and *ĪMĀN* (faith) = SAME ROOT.** ⭐ **The gift of safety asks for the response of faith.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **الشِّتَاءِ وَالصَّيْفِ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **Winter → YEMEN, Summer → SHĀM.** ⭐ **And those two countries are named from the roots of *maymanah* (right) and *mashʾamah* (left) in Balad 90:18–19.** **Don't miss the geography.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **آمَنَهُم (v.4)** — ⚠️ **same root as *ĪMĀN* (faith) and *amīn* (Tīn 95:3).** ⭐ **The gift of security points at the response of faith.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **v.4's TWO gifts (food + security)** — ⚠️ **match exactly the TWO requests in Ibrāhīm's duʿāʾ (2:126).** ⭐ **Read v.4 as the answer to that prayer.**
4. ⭐⭐ **لِإِيلَافِ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **the *li-* has a DEBATED attachment** — to Sūrat al-Fīl, to v.3, or as a *lām* of wonder. ⭐ **All classical.**
5. ⭐⭐ **إِيلَافِ** — ⚠️ **means the safety of the FAMILIAR**, not military protection. ⭐ **The blessing became so routine it became invisible.** ⭐ **Same root as *muʾallif* (author).**
6. ⭐⭐ **رَبَّ هَٰذَا الْبَيْتِ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **"the LORD of this House," not the House itself.** ⭐ **They honoured the building and filled it with idols.**
7. ⭐ **إِيلَافِهِمْ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **a *badal* (appositive)**: v.2 restates and specifies v.1.
8. ⭐ **رِحْلَةَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **SINGULAR** though there were two journeys — ⭐ **read as a generic/system singular.**
