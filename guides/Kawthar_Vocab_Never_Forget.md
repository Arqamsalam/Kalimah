# Surah Al-Kawthar (108) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–3)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It is the SHORTEST surah in the Qurʾān — and it is the exact mirror-image of the surah before it.**

⭐ **Classical scholars (notably ar-Rāzī) observed that Kawthar answers Māʿūn point by point:**

| **Māʿūn 107 — the failure** | **Kawthar 108 — the cure** |
|---|---|
| *yamnaʿūna'l-māʿūn* — **WITHHOLD** the smallest thing | *aʿṭaynāka'l-**kawthar*** — **We GAVE you the MOST** |
| *ʿan ṣalātihim sāhūn* — **heedless OF prayer** | ***fa-ṣalli*** — **so PRAY** |
| *yurāʾūn* — **for SHOW** (for people) | ***li-rabbika*** — **for your LORD** (for Him) |
| *yamnaʿūn* — **withhold from others** | ***wa'nḥar*** — **and SACRIFICE** (give to others) |
| *yukadhdhibu bi'd-dīn* — the **denier** is cut off from good | ***shāniʾaka huwa'l-abtar*** — **your hater is the cut-off one** |

⭐⭐⭐ **Every disease in Māʿūn has its medicine in Kawthar.** ⭐ **Read them together; they're one argument across two surahs.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **Māʿūn: won't lend a POT. Kawthar: was given an OCEAN.** ⭐ **Three verses, ten words, the whole answer.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The gift** (v.1) — ⭐ **We have given you *al-Kawthar*.**
2. **The response** (v.2) — ⭐ **so pray to your Lord, and sacrifice.**
3. **The reversal** (v.3) — ⭐ **your hater — HE is the cut-off one.**

⭐ **Context: when the Prophet's ﷺ sons died in infancy, his enemies mocked him as *al-abtar* — "the cut-off one," a man with no male heir, whose name would die with him.** ⭐ **This surah is the reply.**

## §1 — The Gift (v.1)

**1. إِنَّا أَعْطَيْنَاكَ — *innā aʿṭaynāka*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**GIVING**" family — *aʿṭā* = to give; ⭐ ***ʿaṭāʾ* = a GIFT*** (⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:36**); ⭐ ***ʿaṭiyyah* = a present.**
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, We have given you…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the tense is a promise kept — and you own the promise from four surahs ago.**
  
  ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:5 — *wa la-sawfa **yuʿṭīka** rabbuka fa-tarḍā*** — ⭐ "**your Lord WILL GIVE you**, and you will be pleased." — ⭐ **FUTURE tense.**
  ⭐ **Kawthar 108:1 — *innā **aʿṭaynāka**'l-kawthar*** — ⭐ "**We HAVE GIVEN you** al-Kawthar." — ⭐ **PAST tense.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME VERB, same recipient.** ⭐ **Ḍuḥā promised; Kawthar says it's done.** ⭐ **The "will give" became "have given."**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the *ʿaṭāʾ* thread across four surahs:**
  - **Nabaʾ 78:36** — *jazāʾan min rabbika **ʿaṭāʾan** ḥisābā* (⭐ **a GIFT, well-calculated**)
  - **Layl 92:5** — *fa-ammā man **aʿṭā*** (⭐ **the man who GIVES**)
  - **Ḍuḥā 93:5** — ***yuʿṭīka*** (⭐ **He WILL give you**)
  - ⭐ **Kawthar 108:1** — ***aʿṭaynāka*** (⭐ **We HAVE given you**)
  
  ⭐ **And the emphasis: *INNĀ* — "Indeed, WE"** — ⭐ **the plural of majesty + *inna*.** ⭐ **The Giver names Himself first, before the gift.**
  
  ⭐ **Note the *-ka* ("you", singular) — ⭐ **addressed directly to the Prophet ﷺ**, at the moment his enemies called him cut off.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV past** (*aʿṭā*) with *-nā* (We) + *-ka* (you). ⭐ ***aʿṭā*** takes **two objects**: you (*-ka*) and the gift (*al-kawthar*).
- **Why this word:** the **past tense** turns a promise into ⭐ **a completed fact** — at the exact moment of mockery.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:5: "He WILL give you" (*yuʿṭīka*). ⭐ **Kawthar 108:1: "We HAVE given you" (*aʿṭaynāka*).** ⭐ **Promise → fulfilled.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿAṭāʾ* thread: a gift (78:36), a giver (92:5), will give (93:5), have given (108:1).**

**2. الْكَوْثَرَ — *al-kawthar*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**MUCHNESS / abundance**" family — *kathura* = to be many; *kathīr* = much; ⭐ ***kawthar* is on the *fawʿal* intensive pattern** = ⭐ **OVERWHELMING, limitless abundance** — the most that "much" can be.
- **Here it means:** "**al-Kawthar**" — ⭐ **abundant good; and in authentic hadith, specifically **a river in Paradise** given to the Prophet ﷺ, with the **Ḥawḍ** (his pool) on the Day of Judgment fed from it.**
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BEST CROSS-SURAH ROOT-PAYOFF IN THIS BATCH.**
  
  ⭐ **Takāthur 102:1 — *alhākumu't-**TAKĀTHUR*** — ⭐ "**competing with each other for MORE** distracted you."
  ⭐ **Kawthar 108:1 — *aʿṭaynāka'l-**KAWTHAR*** — ⭐ "**We gave you the MOST.**"
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME ROOT (*k-th-r*).** ⭐ **Takāthur: people exhausting themselves **chasing** more, until the grave.** ⭐ **Kawthar: the most there is, **simply given** — no chasing.** ⭐⭐ **One surah is the rat-race; the other is the gift that ends it.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the *fawʿal* pattern makes it superlative:** ⭐ ***kathīr* = much; *akthar* = more; *kawthar* = ⭐ **abundance beyond measure.** ⭐ **The word itself is the biggest form of "much" Arabic has.**
  
  ⭐ **Classical scholars include under *al-Kawthar*: ⭐ **the river in Paradise (the primary meaning in hadith), the Ḥawḍ, prophethood, the Qurʾān, his followers, his name raised high** (⭐ **Sharḥ 94:4 *wa rafaʿnā laka dhikrak*!**).** ⭐ **All forms of "abundant good."**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And it answers the mockery perfectly:** ⭐ **they said he had **NO** descendants — *abtar*, cut off, **too little**.** ⭐ **The reply: he was given ⭐ ***KAWTHAR*** — **too MUCH to count.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fawʿal*** intensive pattern. ⭐ **Definite (*al-*)** — "**THE** Abundance" — a specific, known thing.
- **Why this word:** *kawthar* — ⭐ **the most extreme possible form of "abundance,"** chosen to answer a charge of **lack**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TAKĀTHUR (102:1) = CHASING more. KAWTHAR (108:1) = GIVEN the most.** ⭐ **Same root — the rat-race and the gift that ends it.** ⭐⭐ ***Fawʿal* = the BIGGEST form of "much."** ⭐ **The river in Paradise + the Ḥawḍ.** ⭐ **They said "too little" (*abtar*) → He gave "too much" (*kawthar*).**

## §2 — The Response (v.2)

**3. فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ — *fa-ṣalli li-rabbika*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *ṣalli* — ⭐ **imperative of *ṣallā* = PRAY.** ⭐ ***li-rabbika* = FOR your Lord** (the *lām* of purpose/dedication).
- **Here it means:** "**So pray to [for] your Lord.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the cure for Māʿūn's disease, in three words.**
  
  ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:5–6 — they were *ʿan ṣalātihim sāhūn* (heedless OF prayer) and *yurāʾūn* (praying FOR SHOW).**
  ⭐ **Kawthar 108:2 — *fa-ṣalli* (so PRAY) *li-rabbika* (FOR YOUR LORD).**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Li-rabbika* is the antidote to *riyāʾ*.** ⭐ **Māʿūn's people prayed **for the audience**; ⭐ **Kawthar says pray **for your Lord** — and only Him.** ⭐ **The *lām* specifies the audience.**
  
  ⭐ **And the *fa-* = "SO" — CAUSAL:** ⭐ **because We gave you *al-Kawthar*, ⭐ **SO pray.** ⭐ **Gratitude, not obligation, drives the prayer.** ⭐ **Compare Quraysh 106:3** — ***fal-yaʿbudū*** rabba hādha'l-bayt — ⭐ **"SO let them worship"** — ⭐ **same causal *fa*, same logic: you were given → so worship.**
  
  ⭐ **The *ṣalāh* thread across your surahs:**
  - **Qiyāmah 75:31** — *wa lā **ṣallā*** (⭐ **he did NOT pray**)
  - **Aʿlā 87:15** — ***fa-ṣallā*** (⭐ **the successful one prayed**)
  - **ʿAlaq 96:10** — *ʿabdan idhā **ṣallā*** (⭐ **a servant STOPPED from praying**)
  - **Māʿūn 107:4** — *li'l-**muṣallīn*** (⭐ **those who pray EMPTILY**)
  - ⭐ **Kawthar 108:2** — ***fa-ṣalli** li-rabbika* (⭐ **PRAY — for your Lord**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **Didn't pray → prayed → prevented → prayed emptily → ⭐ **pray sincerely.** ⭐ **The thread resolves.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **imperative of a weak-final Form II verb** — ⚠️ ***ṣalli*** has **dropped its final yāʾ** (from *ṣallī*). ⭐ ***Li-*** = the *lām* of dedication.
- **Why this word:** *li-rabbika* — ⭐ **one preposition specifying the audience** — the exact repair for *yurāʾūn*.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Māʿūn 107:6: *yurāʾūn* — praying FOR PEOPLE. ⭐ **Kawthar 108:2: *li-rabbika* — praying FOR YOUR LORD.** ⭐ **The *lām* specifies the audience.** ⭐⭐ ***Ṣalāh* thread: didn't (75:31) → did (87:15) → prevented (96:10) → empty (107:4) → SINCERE (108:2).** ⭐ **And the *fa-* = "SO" — because you were GIVEN.**

**4. وَانْحَرْ — *wa'nḥar*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**THROAT / upper chest**" family — ***naḥr* = the upper part of the chest where it meets the neck***; ⭐ ***naḥara* = to SACRIFICE a camel** (by stabbing at the base of the throat — the *naḥr*!); ⭐⭐ ***Yawm an-Naḥr* = THE DAY OF SACRIFICE*** — ⭐ **10th of Dhu'l-Ḥijjah, the day of ʿĪd al-Aḍḥā!**
- **Here it means:** "**and sacrifice.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **one word, and it's the answer to Māʿūn's final line.**
  
  ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:7 — *wa **yamnaʿūna**'l-māʿūn*** — ⭐ "**they WITHHOLD even small things.**"
  ⭐ **Kawthar 108:2 — *wa'**nḥar*** — ⭐ "**and SACRIFICE**" — ⭐ **give something of real value, slaughtered and distributed to the poor.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Māʿūn's man won't lend a pot; Kawthar commands giving a CAMEL.** ⭐ **From the smallest withheld to the largest given.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the pairing *ṣalli* + *anḥar* is the complete religion in two words:** ⭐ ***ṣalāh*** = your duty **to Allah** (body, worship); ⭐ ***naḥr*** = your duty **to people** (wealth, feeding).** ⭐ **Compare Bayyinah 98:5** — ***yuqīmu'ṣ-ṣalāta wa yuʾtu'z-zakāh*** — ⭐ **prayer + giving, the same two limbs.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **The everyday hook: ⭐ ***Yawm an-NAḤR* = the Day of Sacrifice*** — ⭐ **the ʿĪd day when the sacrifice is made, directly after ʿĪd prayer.** ⭐ **The order in the verse is the order of the day: ⭐ **PRAY, then SACRIFICE.** ⭐ **Many scholars connect the verse to exactly this sequence.**
  
  ⭐ **(A minority reading interprets *naḥr* as placing the hands on the chest in prayer — *wa'nḥar* = "and place [your hands] at the *naḥr*." The sacrifice reading is the widely held one.)**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **imperative, Form I** (*inḥar*, with connecting hamzah). ⭐ **No object stated** — ⭐ **"sacrifice" generally.**
- **Why this word:** *naḥr* — ⭐ **the most costly offering in that world**, answering Māʿūn's most trivial refusal.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAWM AN-NAḤR* = ʿĪD AL-AḌḤĀ, the Day of Sacrifice.** ⭐ ***Naḥr* = the THROAT** where the camel is sacrificed. ⭐⭐⭐ **Māʿūn's man won't lend a POT → Kawthar commands giving a CAMEL.** ⭐⭐ **PRAY (to Allah) + SACRIFICE (for people) = the two limbs** (like Bayyinah 98:5's *ṣalāh* + *zakāh*). ⭐ **And the ʿĪd order: pray, THEN sacrifice.**

## §3 — The Reversal (v.3)

**5. إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ — *inna shāniʾaka*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**intense, bitter HATRED**" family — *shaniʾa* = **to hate fiercely, with enmity**; ⭐ ***shanaʾān* = deep animosity** (⭐ **5:2, 5:8: *lā yajrimannakum **shanaʾānu** qawmin* — "do not let **hatred** of a people…"**).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, your hater…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the word is RARE, and its rarity marks the intensity.**
  
  ⭐ ***Shāniʾ* isn't ordinary dislike.*** ⭐ **It's ⭐ **active, bitter, lasting enmity** — the kind that makes you mock someone's grief.** ⭐ **And that's exactly what happened: they mocked the Prophet ﷺ when his infant sons died.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And notice: the hater is **NOT NAMED**.** ⭐ **Classical reports name several individuals (al-ʿĀṣ ibn Wāʾil is commonly mentioned).** ⭐ **The Qurʾān names none of them — ⭐ **only the trait.** ⭐ **Compare Fīl 105 — Abrahah never named.** ⭐ **And contrast Masad 111 — Abū Lahab IS named (in three surahs' time).**
  
  ⭐ **And the *-ka* ("YOUR hater") keeps it personal** — ⭐ **the whole surah speaks directly to him: *aʿṭaynā-**ka***, *rabbi-**ka***, *shāniʾa-**ka***.** ⭐ **Three *-ka*'s in three verses.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **active participle** (*fāʿil*) with possessive *-ka*. ⚠️ ⭐ **Note the hamzah on the middle root letter (ش-ن-أ).** ⭐ **Subject of *inna*.**
- **Why this word:** *shāniʾ* (**bitter hater**) rather than *ʿaduww* (enemy) — ⭐ **it names the **emotion** behind the mockery.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Shanaʾān* = bitter hatred** (5:8: "don't let hatred of a people…"). ⭐ **The hater is NOT NAMED — only the trait** (like Abrahah, Fīl 105). ⭐ **But Abū Lahab WILL be named (Masad 111).** ⭐ **Three *-ka*'s in three verses — a personal letter.**

**6. هُوَ الْأَبْتَرُ — *huwa'l-abtar*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**CUTTING OFF / severing**" family — *batara* = **to cut off** (especially **a tail**); ⭐ ***abtar* = an animal whose TAIL has been cut off*** — ⭐ **and by extension, a man **cut off from posterity**, from good, from remembrance; ⭐ **and in hadith, any important matter begun without Allah's name is ***abtar*** — "cut off from blessing."**
- **Here it means:** "**— HE is the one cut off.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the insult is returned to sender — and the grammar does it with one pronoun.**
  
  ⭐ **They called the Prophet ﷺ ***al-abtar*** — "**the tailless one**," ⭐ **a man with no surviving son, whose line and name would end with him.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The reply: ⭐ ***inna shāniʾaka **HUWA**'l-abtar*** — "**your hater — HE is the cut-off one.**"
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the ***huwa*** is crucial: ⭐ **it's the *ḍamīr al-faṣl* (separating pronoun)**, ⭐ **creating RESTRICTION: "**HE — and not you — is the cut-off one.**"** ⭐ **You own this construction from Nāziʿāt 79:39, 41 (*hiya'l-maʾwā*) and Bayyinah 98:6–7 (*ulāʾika hum*).**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And history confirmed it word for word:** ⭐ **the Prophet's ﷺ name is spoken in every adhān, every shahādah, every tashahhud, by billions, fourteen centuries later** (⭐ **Sharḥ 94:4 — *wa rafaʿnā laka dhikrak*, "We RAISED your mention"**). ⭐ **His mockers? ⭐ **Most people couldn't name one.** ⭐ **The "tailless" man has the longest tail in history; the mockers were cut off.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And now read the whole surah as a single contrast:** ⭐ ***KAWTHAR*** (v.1, the MOST) vs ***ABTAR*** (v.3, CUT OFF). ⭐ **Abundance vs. severance — ⭐ **first word and last word of the shortest surah.**
  
  ⭐ **The rhyme: *kawthar, wa'nḥar, abtar*** — ⭐ **all *-ar*.** ⭐ **The insult rhymes with the gift.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***huwa* = *ḍamīr al-faṣl*** — restriction. ⭐ ***Abtar*** — the ***afʿal*** pattern used for **physical defects/characteristics** (like *aʿmā*, blind — ʿAbasa 80:2).
- **Why this word:** returning **their exact word** (*abtar*) ⭐ **is more devastating than any new insult.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ABTAR* = a TAILLESS animal** — ⭐ **they called him "cut off" when his sons died.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HUWA* = "HE — not you"** (restriction). ⭐ **Returned to sender.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And history agrees: his name is in every adhān (Sharḥ 94:4); theirs are forgotten.** ⭐⭐ **First word *KAWTHAR* (the most) ↔ last word *ABTAR* (cut off).**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **Kawthar is the MIRROR of Māʿūn** — ⚠️ **read them together**: withhold/give, heedless/pray, for show/for your Lord, deny/cut off.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْكَوْثَرَ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **same root as *at-takāthur* (102:1).** ⭐ ***Fawʿal* = the most extreme "abundance."** ⭐ **Primary hadith meaning: a river in Paradise.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَعْطَيْنَاكَ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **PAST tense** — ⭐ **the fulfilment of Ḍuḥā 93:5's FUTURE *yuʿṭīka*.**
4. ⭐⭐ **هُوَ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **the SEPARATING PRONOUN** = "HE, and not you." ⭐ **Not a filler word.**
5. ⭐⭐ **الْأَبْتَرُ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **originally a TAILLESS animal** — ⭐ **their insult, returned to them.**
6. ⭐⭐ **وَانْحَرْ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **SACRIFICE** (at the *naḥr*, the throat) — ⭐ ***Yawm an-Naḥr* = ʿĪd al-Aḍḥā.** ⭐ **(A minority reads it as hand placement in prayer.)**
7. ⭐ **فَصَلِّ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **imperative of a weak-final verb: the final yāʾ is DROPPED.**
8. ⭐ **لِرَبِّكَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **the *lām* specifies the AUDIENCE** — the precise cure for *riyāʾ*.
9. ⭐ **شَانِئَكَ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **hamzah on the middle letter** (ش-ن-أ). ⭐ **Bitter hatred, not mere dislike.**
10. ⭐ **The shortest surah** — ⚠️ **three verses, ten words** — and it ends on the word they used to mock him.
