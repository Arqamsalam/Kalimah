# Surah Aṣ-Ṣaff (61) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–14) · **Complete coverage — every important word**

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It opens with a question that stings — "why do you SAY what you don't DO?" — and ends with a TRADE that saves you, and a call to be ALLAH'S HELPERS.**

⭐ **v.2–3:** ***lima taqūlūna mā lā tafʿalūn*** — "**why do you SAY what you don't DO?**" — ⭐ **the gap between words and deeds — "most hateful to Allah."**
⭐ **v.4:** the answer — ***ṣaffan ka-annahum bunyānun marṣūṣ*** — **in RANKS, like a building SOLDERED with LEAD** — ⭐ **words matched by solid action.**
⭐ **v.10–11:** ***hal adullukum ʿalā tijāratin tunjīkum*** — "**Shall I show you a TRADE that will SAVE you?**"
⭐ **v.14:** ***kūnū anṣāra'llāh*** — "**Be HELPERS of Allah**" — like ʿĪsā's disciples.

⭐⭐⭐ **The famous story (Tirmidhī, Aḥmad — authentic): some Companions said "if only we knew the deed Allah loves most, we'd do it" — then they hesitated when it came. The surah was revealed — and the Prophet ﷺ recited ALL of it to them.** ⭐ **This surah has one of the most famous "chained" narrations in ḥadīth: each narrator recited the whole surah to the next — *al-musalsal bi-qirāʾat Sūrat aṣ-Ṣaff*.**

⭐⭐ **And it names the Prophet ﷺ as "*AḤMAD*" — the only place in the Qurʾān (v.6) — in ʿĪsā's prophecy.**

⭐⭐ **It closes the *Musabbiḥāt* trio of this set: Ḥashr 59:1 and Ṣaff 61:1 open IDENTICALLY — *sabbaḥa li'llāhi mā fi's-samāwāti wa mā fi'l-arḍ, wa huwa'l-ʿazīzu'l-ḥakīm*.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐⭐ **SAY vs DO (v.2) → SOLDERED RANKS (v.4) → the TRADE that saves (v.10) → HELPERS of Allah (v.14).** ⭐ ***AḤMAD* — the only Qurʾānic use (v.6).**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **Glorification** (v.1) — identical to Ḥashr's opening.
2. **Say what you do** (v.2–4) — Allah loves those who fight in ranks like a soldered building.
3. **Mūsā and ʿĪsā** (v.5–6) — Mūsā's people hurt him; ʿĪsā announced a messenger named Aḥmad.
4. **The light they can't blow out** (v.7–9) — they want to extinguish Allah's light with their mouths; He will complete it.
5. **The trade** (v.10–13) — faith and striving → forgiveness, gardens, the great win — and a near victory.
6. **Helpers of Allah** (v.14) — like the disciples of ʿĪsā; the believers prevailed.

## §1 — Glorification (v.1)

**1. سَبَّحَ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۖ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ — *sabbaḥa li'llāhi mā fi's-samāwāti wa mā fi'l-arḍ · wa huwa'l-ʿazīzu'l-ḥakīm*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *sabbaḥa* — ⭐ **GLORIFIED** (past; *sabaḥa* = to swim far; ⭐ ***masbaḥ* = POOL**; ⭐ ***misbaḥah* = PRAYER BEADS**). *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* — ⭐ **ALMIGHTY, WISE**.
- **Here it means:** "**Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth has glorified Allah, and He is the Almighty, the Wise.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD IDENTICAL to ḤASHR 59:1.** ⭐ **Two surahs, the same first verse — *sabbaḥa* (past).** ⭐⭐ **And in between: MUMTAḤANAH (60) — the only surah of the three WITHOUT a glorification opening** — ⭐ **as if the surah of the loyalty EXAM sits between two declarations that all creation is loyal to Allah.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *MUSABBIḤĀT* — complete:** ⭐ ***sabbaḥa* (past): Ḥadīd 57, Ḥashr 59, Ṣaff 61** ⭐ ***yusabbiḥu* (present): Jumuʿah 62, Taghābun 64** ⭐ ***sabbiḥ* (command): Aʿlā 87** ⭐ ***subḥāna* (timeless): Isrāʾ 17.** ⭐ ***Al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*** — ⭐ **ALMIGHTY (He'll make His religion prevail — v.9) + WISE (He chooses the trade that saves — v.10).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***sabbaḥa*** = Form II past (established). ⭐ ***huwa'l-ʿazīzu'l-ḥakīmu*** = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the same opening as Ḥashr — the universe's loyalty framing a surah about human consistency.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= ḤASHR 59:1 WORD-FOR-WORD (*sabbaḥa* — past).** ⭐⭐ **Mumtaḥanah (the loyalty EXAM) sits between two glorifications.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUSABBIḤĀT*: *sabbaḥa* (57, 59, 61), *yusabbiḥu* (62, 64), *sabbiḥ* (87), *subḥāna* (17).** ⭐ **ALMIGHTY (v.9 prevail) + WISE (v.10 the trade).**

## §2 — Say What You Do (v.2–4)

**2. يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفْعَلُونَ — *yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū lima taqūlūna mā lā tafʿalūn*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *lima* — ⭐⭐ **WHY?** (*li* + *mā*, alif dropped; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:1** — *yā ayyuha'n-nabiyyu **lima** tuḥarrimu*!). *taqūlūna* — ⭐ **you SAY** (*qawl*). *tafʿalūna* — ⭐ **you DO** (*fiʿl*; ⭐ ***fiʿl* = a VERB** (grammar!) / an ACT; ⭐ ***faʿʿāl* = EFFECTIVE**; ⭐ ***tafāʿul* = a REACTION** (chemistry!); ⭐ ***infiʿāl* = EMOTION / agitation**).
- **Here it means:** "**O you who believe, why do you say what you do not do?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE QUESTION THAT STINGS — *LIMA*, "WHY?"** ⭐ **Addressed to BELIEVERS (*yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū*)** — ⭐ **not hypocrites, not disbelievers — believers who PROMISED and didn't follow through.** ⭐ **The background: Companions said "if we knew the most beloved deed, we'd do it" — when told (striving in Allah's path), some hesitated.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TAQŪLŪNA* (SAY) vs *TAFʿALŪNA* (DO)** — ⭐ **the gap between words and deeds** — ⭐ **the gap that defined the HYPOCRITES in MUNĀFIQŪN** (*nashhadu innaka la-rasūlu'llāh* — perfect words, false lives) — ⭐ **here the believers are warned not to drift toward it.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***LIMA* — TAḤRĪM 66:1!** ⭐ ***yā ayyuha'n-nabiyyu LIMA tuḥarrimu mā aḥalla'llāhu lak*** — ⭐ **"O Prophet, WHY forbid what Allah allowed?" (gentle).** ⭐ ***yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū LIMA taqūlūna mā lā tafʿalūn*** — ⭐ **"O believers, WHY say what you don't do?" (sharp).** ⭐⭐ **The same "*lima*" — gentle correction to the Prophet ﷺ; a sharper one to the believers.** ⚠️ ***Lima* = *li-mā* — alif dropped** (like *ʿamma*, Nabaʾ 78:1).
  ⭐⭐ ***FIʿL* = a VERB (grammar!)** — ⭐ **"why do you have the *qawl* (statement) without the *fiʿl* (verb/action)?"** ⭐ ***Tafāʿul* = a chemical REACTION; *infiʿāl* = EMOTION.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***lima*** = ***li-mā*** (alif dropped in a question). ⭐ ***mā*** = relative ("**what**"). ⭐ **The question is *istinkārī*** (a rebuke, not a request for information).
- **Why this exact word:** *lima* — ⭐ **a rebuking "why" — the same word Taḥrīm used gently, now sharp.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LIMA TAQŪLŪNA MĀ LĀ TAFʿALŪN* = WHY do you SAY what you don't DO? — to BELIEVERS.** ⭐ **The gap that defined the HYPOCRITES (Munāfiqūn 63:1).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LIMA*: Taḥrīm 66:1 (gentle — to the Prophet ﷺ) / Ṣaff 61:2 (sharp — to the believers).** ⭐⭐ ***Fiʿl* = a VERB / ACT — "a statement without a verb"; *tafāʿul* = REACTION; *infiʿāl* = EMOTION.** ⚠️ ***Lima* = *li-mā*, alif dropped.**

**3. كَبُرَ مَقْتًا عِندَ اللَّهِ أَن تَقُولُوا مَا لَا تَفْعَلُونَ — *kabura maqtan ʿinda'llāhi an taqūlū mā lā tafʿalūn*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *kabura* — ⭐ **is GREAT / grave / enormous** (*k-b-r*; ⭐ ***kabīrah* = a MAJOR SIN**!; ⭐ ***Allāhu akbar***; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:23** *al-Mutakabbir*!). *maqt* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**INTENSE LOATHING / detestation**" family — ***maqt* = the most INTENSE HATRED / detestation** (stronger than *bughḍ*!; ⭐ ***maqīt* = DETESTABLE**; ⭐ ***nikāḥ al-maqt*** — "the DETESTABLE marriage" — marrying one's father's ex-wife — **4:22** — *innahu kāna fāḥishatan wa **maqtan***!; ⭐ ***mamqūt* = LOATHED**). *ʿinda'llāh* — ⭐ **in Allah's SIGHT**.
- **Here it means:** "**Most hateful it is in the sight of Allah that you say what you do not do.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KABURA MAQTAN* — "ENORMOUS in DETESTATION"** — ⭐ ***MAQT* = the STRONGEST word for hatred in Arabic** — ⭐ **the same word for the most shameful marriage (*nikāḥ al-maqt*, 4:22)!** ⭐⭐⭐ **Saying-without-doing is called by the word for the most loathsome deed.** ⭐ **Not just "disliked" — *MAQT* — DETESTED.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿINDA'LLĀH* — "in ALLAH's sight"** — ⭐ **people may admire your promises; Allah sees the gap.** ⭐ ***Kabura* — the *k-b-r* root: *kabīrah* = a MAJOR sin** — ⭐ **saying-without-doing as a "major."**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE REPETITION: v.2 *mā lā tafʿalūn* — v.3 *mā lā tafʿalūn*** — ⭐ **the phrase REPEATED in back-to-back verses** — ⭐ **to hammer it in — "say what you don't DO… say what you don't DO."** ⭐ **The repetition IS the rebuke.**
  ⭐⭐ ***ʿInda'llāh* — Mumtaḥanah/Taghābun's *ʿindahu* (what's WITH Him — reward); here: what's DETESTED in His sight.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***kabura*** = verb of **wonder/blame** (like *biʾsa*); ***maqtan*** = ***tamyīz*** ("great **in terms of** detestation"); ⭐ ***an taqūlū*** = the subject (*maṣdar* — "your SAYING"). ⭐ **The *mā lā tafʿalūn* repeated (v.2, v.3).**
- **Why this exact word:** *maqt* — ⭐ **the strongest word for hatred — applied to the gap between words and deeds.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KABURA MAQTAN ʿINDA'LLĀH* = ENORMOUS DETESTATION in Allah's sight.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAQT* = the STRONGEST hatred — the word for *NIKĀḤ AL-MAQT* (4:22 — marrying a father's ex-wife)!** ⭐ **Say-without-do called by the word for the most loathsome deed.** ⭐⭐ ***Mā lā tafʿalūn* REPEATED (v.2, v.3) — the repetition IS the rebuke.** ⭐ ***Kabura* — *kabīrah* = MAJOR sin.**

**4. إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ الَّذِينَ يُقَاتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِهِ صَفًّا — *inna'llāha yuḥibbu'lladhīna yuqātilūna fī sabīlihi ṣaffā*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *yuḥibbu* — ⭐ **LOVES** (*ḥubb*). *yuqātilūna* — ⭐ **FIGHT** (Form III). *ṣaff* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**ROW / RANK / LINING UP**" family — ***ṣaff* = a ROW, a LINE, a RANK** (⭐ ***ṣaff* = a CLASS / GRADE** in school — *aṣ-ṣaff ath-thālith* = **THIRD GRADE**!; ⭐ ***ṣufūf aṣ-ṣalāh* = prayer ROWS**!); ⭐ ***ṣaffa* = to LINE UP / arrange in rows**; ⭐ ***ṣāffāt* = (birds) with wings SPREAD in a line** (⭐ **Mulk 67:19** — *a-wa lam yaraw ila'ṭ-ṭayri fawqahum **ṣāffātin***!); ⭐ ***aṣ-Ṣāffāt*** = Sūrah 37 (angels in RANKS!); ⭐ ***taṣfīf* = hair STYLING / arranging** (⭐ *taṣfīf ash-shaʿr* = **hairdressing**!); ⭐ ***ṣaff* = a TYPESETTING** (printing!).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, Allah loves those who fight in His cause in rows…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SURAH'S NAME — *AṢ-ṢAFF* — THE ROW / RANK.** ⭐ **After the rebuke (v.2–3): what DOES Allah love? — those who fight *ṢAFFAN* — "in RANKS" — side by side, ordered, disciplined.** ⭐ **Not individual heroics — a UNITED, ORDERED body.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *ṢAFF* THREAD — rows everywhere in your study:** ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:38** — *yawma yaqūmu'r-rūḥu wa'l-malāʾikatu **ṣaffā*** — ⭐ **the Spirit and angels standing in a ROW on the Day.** ⭐ **Fajr 89:22** — *wa jāʾa rabbuka wa'l-malaku **ṣaffan ṣaffā*** — ⭐ **angels, RANK upon RANK.** ⭐ **Mulk 67:19** — *aṭ-ṭayri fawqahum **ṣāffātin*** — ⭐ **birds with wings SPREAD in line.** ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:4** — *yuqātilūna fī sabīlihi **ṣaffā*** — ⭐ **believers in RANKS.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Angels in rows, birds in line, believers in ranks — and every day: *ṢUFŪF AṢ-ṢALĀH* (prayer ROWS)** — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ: "*straighten your rows — for straight rows are part of the perfection of prayer*" (Bukhārī, Muslim).** ⭐ **The prayer row is rehearsal for unity.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ṣaff* = a school CLASS / GRADE (*aṣ-ṣaff ath-thālith* = THIRD GRADE); *taṣfīf ash-shaʿr* = HAIRDRESSING.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yuḥibbu*** = present (ongoing love). ⭐ ***ṣaffan*** = ***ḥāl*** ("**in** rows") — ⭐ **a masdar as *ḥāl*** (= *ṣāffīn* — "lined up").
- **Why this exact word:** *ṣaff* — ⭐ **ordered unity — the opposite of empty individual promises.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AṢ-ṢAFF* = the ROW / RANK — the surah's NAME — Allah LOVES those who strive in RANKS.** ⭐ **Not individual heroics — ORDERED unity.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṢAFF* thread: angels in a ROW (Nabaʾ 78:38), RANK upon RANK (Fajr 89:22), birds in LINE (Mulk 67:19), believers in RANKS (Ṣaff 61:4).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṢUFŪF AṢ-ṢALĀH* — "straighten your rows" (Bukhārī, Muslim) — rehearsal for unity.** ⭐⭐ ***Ṣaff* = school GRADE; *taṣfīf ash-shaʿr* = HAIRDRESSING.**

**5. كَأَنَّهُم بُنْيَانٌ مَّرْصُوصٌ — *ka-annahum bunyānun marṣūṣ*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *bunyān* — ⭐⭐ the "**BUILDING / structure**" family — *banā* = to build; ⭐ ***bunyān* = a BUILDING / structure**; ⭐ ***bināʾ* = CONSTRUCTION**; ⭐ ***bunyah* = a STRUCTURE / physique** (⭐ *bunyah jasadiyyah* = **BUILD / physique** — clinical!; ⭐ *al-bunyah at-taḥtiyyah* = **INFRASTRUCTURE**!); ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:11** — *rabbi'**bni** lī ʿindaka baytan*!; ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:12** — *wa **banaynā** fawqakum sabʿan shidādā*. *marṣūṣ* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**SOLDERED / packed tight with LEAD**" family — *raṣṣa* = **to PACK tightly, to join firmly — classically, to solder with LEAD**; ⭐⭐⭐ ***raṣāṣ* = LEAD (the metal)** — and ⭐⭐⭐ ***raṣāṣ* = BULLETS** (modern — made of lead!; ⭐ *raṣāṣah* = a **BULLET**!; ⭐ *qalam raṣāṣ* = a **PENCIL** ("lead pen")!; ⭐ *at-tasammum bi'r-raṣāṣ* = **LEAD POISONING** — clinical!); ⭐ ***tarāṣṣ* = to stand SHOULDER TO SHOULDER, packed tight** (⭐ the Prophet ﷺ: "*tarāṣṣū* — pack your rows tight [in prayer]"!); ⭐ ***raṣīf* = a PAVEMENT / sidewalk** (⭐ related *r-ṣ-f* — stones packed tight!).
- **Here it means:** "**…as though they were a building soldered together with lead.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST VIVID IMAGE OF UNITY IN THE QURʾĀN.** ⭐ ***BUNYĀNUN MARṢŪṢ*** — ⭐ **a building whose stones are SOLDERED with molten LEAD** — ⭐ **no gaps, no weak points — a single solid mass.** ⭐ **You can't pull one stone out; you can't push the wall over.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MARṢŪṢ* ← *RAṢĀṢ* = LEAD — and modern Arabic *RAṢĀṢ* = BULLETS!** ⭐ ***Raṣāṣah* = a BULLET; *qalam raṣāṣ* = a PENCIL ("lead pen"); *at-tasammum bi'r-raṣāṣ* = LEAD POISONING (clinical).** ⭐⭐ ***TARĀṢṢŪ!*** — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ in prayer: "*TARĀṢṢŪ* — pack your rows TIGHT — don't leave gaps for Shayṭān"** (Abū Dāwūd, Nasāʾī) — ⭐ **the same root — the PRAYER ROW soldered like the battle rank!**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ḤASHR CONTRAST — the surahs talk to each other:** ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:14** — *taḥsabuhum jamīʿan **wa qulūbuhum SHATTĀ*** — ⭐ **the enemies LOOKED united — their hearts were SCATTERED.** ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:4** — *ka-annahum **bunyānun MARṢŪṢ*** — ⭐ **the believers ARE united — SOLDERED like lead.** ⭐⭐⭐ **SCATTERED hearts behind walls (59) vs SOLDERED ranks in the open (61).** ⭐ **And the ḥadīth: "*The believer to the believer is like a BUILDING — each part SUPPORTS the other*" — and he interlaced his fingers** (Bukhārī, Muslim). ⭐ ***Al-bunyah at-taḥtiyyah* = INFRASTRUCTURE; *bunyah* = PHYSIQUE.**
  ⭐⭐ ***BUNYĀN* — the *banā* thread:** ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:12** — *wa **banaynā** fawqakum sabʿan shidādā* (We BUILT seven strong [heavens]). ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:11** — Āsiyah: *rabbi'**bni** lī ʿindaka baytan* (BUILD me a house near You). ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:4** — ***bunyānun** marṣūṣ* (believers as a BUILDING). ⭐ **Allah builds the sky; Āsiyah asks for a house; believers BECOME a building.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ka-anna*** = "**as if**." ⭐ ***marṣūṣun*** = **passive participle** (soldered — BY something/someone). ⭐ ***bunyānun*** = indefinite (a building **of this kind**).
- **Why this exact word:** *marṣūṣ* — ⭐ **lead-soldered — unity with no gaps, the answer to Ḥashr's scattered hearts.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BUNYĀNUN MARṢŪṢ* = a BUILDING SOLDERED with LEAD — no gaps, one solid mass.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RAṢĀṢ* = LEAD → BULLETS (*raṣāṣah*); *qalam raṣāṣ* = PENCIL; *at-tasammum bi'r-raṣāṣ* = LEAD POISONING (clinical).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TARĀṢṢŪ!* — "pack your PRAYER ROWS tight" — same root.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥashr 59:14 hearts SCATTERED (*shattā*) vs Ṣaff 61:4 SOLDERED (*marṣūṣ*).** ⭐⭐⭐ **"Believers are like a BUILDING — each part supports the other" (fingers interlaced — Bukhārī, Muslim).** ⭐⭐ ***Banā*: Allah BUILDS the sky (78:12), Āsiyah's HOUSE (66:11), believers BECOME a building (61:4).** ⭐ ***Al-bunyah at-taḥtiyyah* = INFRASTRUCTURE; *bunyah* = PHYSIQUE.**

## §3 — Mūsā and ʿĪsā (v.5–6)

**6. وَإِذْ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوْمِهِ يَا قَوْمِ لِمَ تُؤْذُونَنِي وَقَد تَّعْلَمُونَ أَنِّي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ إِلَيْكُمْ — *wa idh qāla Mūsā li-qawmihi yā qawmi lima tuʾdhūnanī wa qad taʿlamūna annī rasūlu'llāhi ilaykum*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *Mūsā* — ⭐ **MOSES** (⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:15**, **Aʿlā 87:19**, **Muzzammil 73:15**!). *lima* — ⭐ **WHY?** (⭐ v.2!). *tuʾdhūnanī* — ⭐⭐ the "**HARM / HURT / annoyance**" family — *adhā* = harm, hurt, annoyance (⭐ ***adhā* = HARM / injury** — clinical: *adhā* = **damage / injury**!; ⭐ ***īdhāʾ* = HARMING**; ⭐ ***muʾdhī* = HARMFUL**; ⭐ ***imāṭat al-adhā ʿani'ṭ-ṭarīq*** — **REMOVING HARM from the road** — the lowest branch of faith (Muslim)!). *taʿlamūna* — ⭐ **you KNOW**.
- **Here it means:** "**And [remember] when Mūsā said to his people: 'O my people, why do you hurt me, when you know that I am the Messenger of Allah to you?'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ANOTHER *LIMA* — and the SAME problem as v.2!** ⭐ **v.2: *LIMA taqūlūna mā lā tafʿalūn*** (believers — say but don't do). ⭐ **v.5: Mūsā: *LIMA tuʾdhūnanī wa qad taʿlamūna*** (his people — KNOW but act against it). ⭐⭐⭐ **The gap again: KNOWING he's a messenger — yet HURTING him.** ⭐ **Knowledge without action = the disease of the surah.** ⭐⭐ **How did they hurt Mūsā?** ⭐ **Disobedience (refusing to enter the land, the calf), complaints, and accusations** (⭐ Aḥzāb 33:69 — *lā takūnū ka'lladhīna ādhaw Mūsā* — "don't be like those who HURT Mūsā"!). ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ, when hurt: "*May Allah have mercy on Mūsā — he was hurt with more than this, and was patient*" (Bukhārī).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA QAD TAʿLAMŪNA* — "when you KNOW"** — ⭐ **the worst kind of hurt: from people who KNOW better.** ⭐ ***Qad* + present = certainty here ("you DO know").**
  ⭐⭐ ***ADHĀ* — HARM** — ⭐ ***imāṭat al-adhā ʿani'ṭ-ṭarīq*** — ⭐ **removing HARM from the road = a branch of faith (Muslim)** — ⭐ **the opposite of causing harm to a prophet.** ⭐ ***Adhā* = INJURY (clinical).**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Yā QAWMI*** — ⭐ **"O MY people"** — ⭐ **the tender address (the *yāʾ* of possession dropped)** — ⭐ **like Nūḥ (71:2 — *yā qawmi*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa idh*** = "**and [recall] when**." ⭐ ***yā qawmi*** = vocative + possessive *yāʾ* dropped. ⭐ ***tuʾdhūnanī*** = Form IV + *-nī* (the *nūn al-wiqāyah*!). ⭐⭐ ***wa qad taʿlamūna*** = *ḥāl* — ***qad* + present = certainty**.
- **Why this exact word:** *lima* — ⭐ **the second "why" — knowing and still harming.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SECOND *LIMA*: v.2 SAY but don't DO / v.5 KNOW but HURT — knowledge without action, the surah's disease.** ⭐⭐ ***WA QAD TAʿLAMŪNA* = when you KNOW — hurt from those who know better.** ⭐⭐ **Aḥzāb 33:69 "don't be like those who HURT Mūsā"; "Mūsā was hurt more — and was patient" (Bukhārī).** ⭐⭐ ***Adhā* = HARM / INJURY; *imāṭat al-adhā ʿani'ṭ-ṭarīq* = removing harm from the road (Muslim).** ⭐ ***Yā qawmi* — Nūḥ 71:2.**

**7. فَلَمَّا زَاغُوا أَزَاغَ اللَّهُ قُلُوبَهُمْ ۚ وَاللَّهُ لَا يَهْدِي الْقَوْمَ الْفَاسِقِينَ — *fa-lammā zāghū azāgha'llāhu qulūbahum · wa'llāhu lā yahdi'l-qawma'l-fāsiqīn*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *zāghū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**SWERVING / deviating / tilting**" family — *zāgha* = **to SWERVE, deviate, tilt away from the straight**; ⭐ ***zaygh* = DEVIATION** (⭐ classically: *zaygh al-baṣar* = the gaze SWERVING — **Najm 53:17** — *mā **zāgha**'l-baṣaru wa mā ṭaghā* — the Prophet's ﷺ sight didn't SWERVE!); ⭐ ***azāgha* (Form IV) = to cause to SWERVE**; ⭐ **Āl ʿImrān 3:7** — *fa-ammā'lladhīna fī qulūbihim **zayghun*** (those with DEVIATION in their hearts); ⭐ **Āl ʿImrān 3:8** — *rabbanā **lā tuzigh** qulūbanā baʿda idh hadaytanā* — ⭐ **the famous DUʿĀʾ!** *fāsiqīn* — ⭐ **DEFIANT** (a date bursting its skin; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:6**!).
- **Here it means:** "**So when they swerved, Allah made their hearts swerve. And Allah does not guide the defiantly disobedient people.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST IMPORTANT LAW OF HEARTS IN THE QURʾĀN: THEY swerved FIRST → THEN Allah let their hearts swerve.** ⭐ ***FA-LAMMĀ ZĀGHŪ*** — ⭐ **"WHEN they SWERVED" (their choice — Form I)** — ⭐ ***AZĀGHA'LLĀHU QULŪBAHUM*** — ⭐ **"Allah CAUSED their hearts to SWERVE" (His consequence — Form IV).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same root, two forms: their action (Form I) → His response (Form IV).** ⭐ **Allah didn't mislead them first; He CONFIRMED the direction they CHOSE.** ⭐ **Measure for measure.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE "CONSEQUENCE, NOT CAUSE" THREAD — now FOUR surahs:** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:3** — *āmanū thumma kafarū **fa-ṭubiʿa** ʿalā qulūbihim* (they disbelieved → hearts SEALED). ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:19** — *nasu'llāha **fa-ansāhum** anfusahum* (they forgot Allah → made to FORGET themselves). ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:5** — *fa-lammā zāghū **azāgha'llāhu** qulūbahum* (they SWERVED → hearts made to SWERVE). ⭐ **(and Muṭaffifīn 83:14 — *rāna* — the RUST of their deeds).** ⭐⭐ **In every case: THEIR choice first, His consequence second.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE DUʿĀʾ — ĀL ʿIMRĀN 3:8:** ⭐ ***RABBANĀ LĀ TUZIGH QULŪBANĀ BAʿDA IDH HADAYTANĀ*** — ⭐ **"Our Lord, DON'T let our hearts SWERVE after You've guided us"** — ⭐ **the protection against exactly what happened here.** ⭐ **Recite it knowing this verse.** ⭐⭐ **NAJM 53:17** — *mā **zāgha**'l-baṣaru wa mā ṭaghā* — ⭐ **the Prophet's ﷺ gaze did NOT SWERVE (at the Miʿrāj).**
  ⭐⭐ ***LĀ YAHDI'L-QAWMA'L-FĀSIQĪN* — MUNĀFIQŪN 63:6 IDENTICAL** — ⭐ **the *fāsiq* thread: 63:6, 59:5, 59:19, 61:5.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-lammā*** + past = "**when**" (⚠️ not "not yet"!). ⭐⭐⭐ ***zāghū*** (Form I — **their act**) → ***azāgha*** (Form IV — **causative: His response**).
- **Why this exact word:** *zāgha / azāgha* — ⭐ **one root, two forms: human choice and divine consequence.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA-LAMMĀ ZĀGHŪ AZĀGHA'LLĀHU QULŪBAHUM* = THEY SWERVED first (Form I) → Allah made their hearts SWERVE (Form IV) — CONSEQUENCE, not cause.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREAD: SEALED after disbelief (63:3), made to FORGET after forgetting (59:19), made to SWERVE after swerving (61:5), RUST (83:14).** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE DUʿĀʾ (3:8): *RABBANĀ LĀ TUZIGH QULŪBANĀ BAʿDA IDH HADAYTANĀ*.** ⭐⭐ **Najm 53:17: the Prophet's ﷺ gaze did NOT swerve.** ⭐⭐ ***Lā yahdi'l-qawma'l-fāsiqīn* = Munāfiqūn 63:6.**

**8. وَإِذْ قَالَ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ يَا بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ إِنِّي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ إِلَيْكُم — *wa idh qāla ʿĪsa'bnu Maryama yā banī Isrāʾīla innī rasūlu'llāhi ilaykum*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *ʿĪsā ibn Maryam* — ⭐⭐⭐ **JESUS SON OF MARY** (⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:12** — *Maryama'bnata ʿImrān*!). *banī Isrāʾīl* — ⭐ **the CHILDREN OF ISRAEL** (Yaʿqūb's descendants).
- **Here it means:** "**And [remember] when ʿĪsā son of Maryam said: 'O Children of Israel, indeed I am the Messenger of Allah to you…'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĪSĀ IBN MARYAM* — always named by his MOTHER.** ⭐ **Every other prophet is named alone or by his father — ʿĪsā is "son of MARYAM"** — ⭐ **because he had NO father — the name itself is a creed** (⭐ against both those who called him "son of God" and those who slandered his mother). ⭐⭐⭐ **TAḤRĪM 66:12 — the LAST verse of Taḥrīm** — *wa Maryama'bnata ʿImrāna'llatī aḥṣanat farjahā fa-nafakhnā fīhi min rūḥinā* — ⭐ **Maryam, who guarded her chastity, into whom We breathed** — ⭐ **→ Ṣaff 61:6 — her SON speaks.** ⭐ **Mother (66:12) → son (61:6).**
  ⭐⭐ **NOTE: Mūsā says *YĀ QAWMI* ("O MY people") — ʿĪsā says *YĀ BANĪ ISRĀʾĪL* ("O Children of Israel")** — ⭐ classically: **ʿĪsā had no father, so no paternal "tribe" to call "my people"** — ⭐ **a precise difference.** ⭐ **The Qurʾān never has ʿĪsā say "*yā qawmi*."**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Innī rasūlu'llāhi ilaykum*** — ⭐ **the same words as Mūsā (v.5: *annī rasūlu'llāhi ilaykum*)** — ⭐ **two prophets, same claim, both rejected.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿĪsa'bnu Maryama*** — ⭐ ***ibn*** in apposition (no initial *alif* written between two names). ⚠️ ⭐ ***Maryama*** = **diptote**.
- **Why this exact word:** *ibn Maryam* — ⭐ **a name that is itself a statement of his miraculous birth.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĪSĀ IBN MARYAM* — always named by his MOTHER — no father — the name IS a creed.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:12 (Maryam — the breath) → Ṣaff 61:6 (her son speaks).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Mūsā: *YĀ QAWMI* (my people) / ʿĪsā: *YĀ BANĪ ISRĀʾĪL* — no father, no paternal "people" — the Qurʾān never has ʿĪsā say *yā qawmi*.** ⭐ **Same claim as Mūsā (*innī rasūlu'llāhi ilaykum*).**

**9. مُّصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَيْنَ يَدَيَّ مِنَ التَّوْرَاةِ — *muṣaddiqan li-mā bayna yadayya mina't-tawrāh*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *muṣaddiq* — ⭐⭐ **CONFIRMING** (Form II of *ṣadaqa* — ⭐ ***taṣdīq* = CONFIRMATION / certification**; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:12** — *wa **ṣaddaqat** bi-kalimāti rabbihā* (his MOTHER confirmed!)). *bayna yadayya* — ⭐⭐ **"between my hands" = BEFORE me** (an idiom for what came EARLIER / is in front). *at-Tawrāh* — ⭐ **the TORAH** (⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:5** — *ḥummilu't-tawrāh*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…confirming what came before me of the Torah…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUṢADDIQAN* — CONFIRMING — the family thread!** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:12** — Maryam: *wa **ṢADDAQAT** bi-kalimāti rabbihā wa kutubih* — ⭐ **the MOTHER CONFIRMED her Lord's words and books.** ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:6** — ʿĪsā: ***MUṢADDIQAN** li-mā bayna yadayya mina't-tawrāh* — ⭐ **the SON CONFIRMS the Torah.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Mother and son — both *muṣaddiq*.** ⭐ **And Maryam's title: *ṣiddīqah* (5:75).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AT-TAWRĀH* — JUMUʿAH 62:5!** ⭐ ***mathalu'lladhīna ḥummilu't-TAWRĀTA thumma lam yaḥmilūhā ka-mathali'l-ḥimār*** — ⭐ **those who carried the Torah without living it = a DONKEY carrying books.** ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:6** — ⭐ **ʿĪsā CONFIRMS that same Torah** — ⭐ **he came to revive what they'd been carrying uselessly.**
  ⭐⭐ ***BAYNA YADAYYA* — "between my HANDS" = what came BEFORE me** — ⭐ **an idiom: what's "between your hands" is IN FRONT of you in time = already present / earlier.** ⭐ **(The Torah preceded ʿĪsā — it was "before him.")**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***muṣaddiqan*** = ***ḥāl*** (of the speaker). ⭐ ***yadayya*** = dual of *yad* + *yāʾ* (the dual *nūn* drops, *yāʾ*s merge — *yadayya*).
- **Why this exact word:** *muṣaddiq* — ⭐ **continuity: the son confirms, as his mother confirmed.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUṢADDIQAN* = CONFIRMING — Maryam *ṢADDAQAT* (Taḥrīm 66:12) → ʿĪsā *MUṢADDIQ* (Ṣaff 61:6) — mother and son.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AT-TAWRĀH* — Jumuʿah 62:5 (the DONKEY carrying it) → ʿĪsā CONFIRMS it (61:6).** ⭐⭐ ***BAYNA YADAYYA* = "between my hands" = BEFORE me (in time).** ⚠️ ***Yadayya* = dual + *yāʾ*.**

**10. وَمُبَشِّرًا بِرَسُولٍ يَأْتِي مِن بَعْدِي اسْمُهُ أَحْمَدُ — *wa mubashshiran bi-rasūlin yaʾtī min baʿdi'smuhu Aḥmad*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *mubashshir* — ⭐⭐⭐ **BRINGER OF GOOD NEWS** (Form II of *bashara*; ⭐ ***bushrā / bishārah* = GOOD NEWS** — it shows on the SKIN of the face!; ⭐ ***basharah* = SKIN (epidermis)**; ⭐ ***bashar* = HUMANS**; ⭐ **Taghābun 64:6** — *a-basharun yahdūnanā*!; ⭐ ***tabshīr* = EVANGELISM** — "spreading good news"!). *yaʾtī min baʿdī* — ⭐ **COMES AFTER me**. *ismuhu* — ⭐ **his NAME** (⭐ **Ḥashr 59:24** — *al-asmāʾu'l-ḥusnā*!). *Aḥmad* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**PRAISE**" family — ***ḥamd* = PRAISE** (⭐ **Taghābun 64:1** — *lahu'l-ḥamd*!); ⭐ ***Aḥmad* = "MOST praiseworthy" / "MOST praising [of Allah]"** (*afʿal* — superlative!); ⭐ ***Muḥammad* = "praised AGAIN and AGAIN"** (Form II passive); ⭐ ***Maḥmūd* = "praised"**; ⭐ ***al-maqām al-maḥmūd*** (17:79).
- **Here it means:** "**…and bringing good news of a messenger to come after me, whose name is Aḥmad.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ONLY TIME THE QURʾĀN CALLS THE PROPHET ﷺ "AḤMAD."** ⭐ **Everywhere else: *Muḥammad* (4 times — 3:144, 33:40, 47:2, 48:29).** ⭐ **Here, in ʿĪsā's prophecy: *ISMUHU AḤMAD*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The ḥadīth (Bukhārī, Muslim): "*I have [several] names: I am MUḤAMMAD, and I am AḤMAD, and I am al-MĀḤĪ (through whom Allah ERASES disbelief), and I am al-ḤĀSHIR (at whose feet people are GATHERED), and I am al-ʿĀQIB (who comes LAST)*."** ⭐⭐⭐ **AND THREE OF THOSE ROOTS ARE IN THIS SET:** ⭐ ***AḤMAD*** — **Ṣaff 61:6** (here!). ⭐ ***AL-ḤĀSHIR*** — **Ḥashr 59:2** (*li-awwali'l-ḤASHR*!). ⭐ ***AL-ʿĀQIB*** — **Ḥashr 59:4, 7** (*shadīd al-ʿIQĀB*), **59:17** (*ʿĀQIBATAHUMĀ*). ⭐ **The Names of the Prophet ﷺ woven through the surahs.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AḤMAD* vs *MUḤAMMAD* — same root, different shades:** ⭐ ***Muḥammad*** (Form II passive, *mufaʿʿal*) = **the one PRAISED again and again** (by others — people praise him). ⭐ ***Aḥmad*** (*afʿal* — superlative) = **the MOST praising [of Allah]** OR **the MOST praiseworthy** — ⭐ classically: **he praised Allah more than anyone (*aḥmad* = most praising) — and is praised more than anyone (*muḥammad*).** ⭐ **On the Day: he'll be given the *Liwāʾ al-Ḥamd* (Banner of Praise) and *al-Maqām al-Maḥmūd* (the Praised Station).** ⭐ **Al-Fātiḥah: *al-ḥamdu li'llāh*.** ⭐⭐ **And TAGHĀBUN 64:1 — *lahu'l-ḥamd* — ALL praise belongs to ALLAH; and His Messenger is named *AḤMAD* — the most PRAISING of Him.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUBASHSHIR* — the *BASHAR* (SKIN) root — and TAGHĀBUN 64:6!** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:6** — the earlier nations: *a-BASHARUN yahdūnanā?* (mere HUMANS guiding us?!) — ⭐ **contempt for the human messenger.** ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:6** — ʿĪsā: *wa MUBASHSHIRAN bi-rasūl* — ⭐ **bringing GOOD NEWS (*bushrā*) of a human messenger.** ⭐⭐ **Same root: *bashar* (human — despised) / *bushrā* (good news — announced).** ⭐ **The good news WAS a human.** ⭐ ***Tabshīr* = EVANGELISM.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mubashshiran*** = second ***ḥāl*** (joined to *muṣaddiqan*). ⭐ ***yaʾtī min baʿdī*** = *ṣifah* of *rasūl*. ⚠️⭐⭐ ***Aḥmadu*** = **DIPTOTE** (*afʿal* pattern + proper name) — no tanwīn. ⚠️ ⭐ ***baʿdi'smuhu*** — the *yāʾ* of *baʿdī* elided / or read *baʿdiya'smuhu* (variant).
- **Why this exact word:** *Aḥmad* — ⭐ **the superlative of praise — the Prophet's ﷺ name in the mouth of ʿĪsā.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ISMUHU AḤMAD* — the ONLY Qurʾānic use of "AḤMAD" (elsewhere *Muḥammad* ×4).** ⭐⭐⭐ **ḤADĪTH (Bukhārī, Muslim): MUḤAMMAD, AḤMAD, al-MĀḤĪ, al-ḤĀSHIR, al-ʿĀQIB — and *Ḥashr* (59:2) + *ʿiqāb/ʿāqibah* (59:4, 7, 17) + *Aḥmad* (61:6) are ALL in this set!** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUḤAMMAD* = PRAISED again and again (by others) / *AḤMAD* = MOST PRAISING (of Allah) & most praiseworthy.** ⭐⭐ ***Liwāʾ al-Ḥamd*, *al-Maqām al-Maḥmūd*; Taghābun 64:1 *lahu'l-ḥamd*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BASHAR* (human — despised, Taghābun 64:6) / *MUBASHSHIR* (good news — 61:6) — the good news WAS a human.** ⚠️ ***Aḥmadu* = DIPTOTE.** ⭐ ***Tabshīr* = EVANGELISM.**

**11. فَلَمَّا جَاءَهُم بِالْبَيِّنَاتِ قَالُوا هَٰذَا سِحْرٌ مُّبِينٌ — *fa-lammā jāʾahum bi'l-bayyināti qālū hādhā siḥrun mubīn*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *bayyināt* — ⭐ **CLEAR PROOFS** (⭐ **Taghābun 64:6**, **Bayyinah 98**!). *siḥr* — ⭐⭐ the "**MAGIC / bewitching / early dawn**" family — ***siḥr* = MAGIC, sorcery** (⭐ ***sāḥir* = a MAGICIAN**; ⭐ ***saḥḥār* = a master magician**); ⭐ ***saḥar* = the LAST part of the night before dawn** (⭐ ***suḥūr* = the PRE-DAWN MEAL** in Ramaḍān!) — ⭐ classically linked: magic is "hidden" like the dim pre-dawn; ⭐ ***siḥr al-bayān* = the "magic" of eloquence** (⭐ *inna mina'l-bayāni la-siḥrā* — "some eloquence is MAGIC" — Bukhārī!); ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:24** — *in hādhā illā **siḥrun** yuʾthar*!. *mubīn* — ⭐ **CLEAR / obvious**.
- **Here it means:** "**But when he came to them with clear proofs, they said: 'This is obvious magic.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE OLDEST INSULT: "IT'S MAGIC!"** ⭐ ***Fa-lammā jāʾahum bi'l-BAYYINĀT*** — ⭐ **"when he came with CLEAR proofs"** — ⭐ ***qālū hādhā SIḤRUN MUBĪN*** — ⭐ **"they said: this is CLEAR magic!"** ⭐⭐⭐ **The irony: CLEAR proofs (*bayyināt*) called CLEAR magic (*siḥrun mubīn*)** — ⭐ **the same clarity, denied by renaming it.** ⭐⭐⭐ **MUDDATHTHIR 74:24** — al-Walīd about the Qurʾān: *in hādhā illā **siḥrun yuʾthar*** — ⭐ **"this is just MAGIC handed down!"** ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:6** — about ʿĪsā's miracles (or the Prophet ﷺ — "he" can refer to either!): ***siḥrun mubīn***. ⭐⭐ **Every messenger got the same label: "MAGICIAN!"** (⭐ *kadhālika mā ataʾl-ladhīna min qablihim min rasūlin illā qālū sāḥirun aw majnūn* — 51:52 — "no messenger came except they said: MAGICIAN or MADMAN" — ⭐ **Qalam 68:2 — *majnūn*!**).
  ⭐⭐ ***Fa-lammā JĀʾAHUM*** — ⭐ classically: **"he" = ʿĪsā (with his miracles) — OR AḤMAD (the Prophet ﷺ, who came with the Qurʾān)** — ⭐ **both readings: both were called magicians.**
  ⭐⭐ ***SAḤAR* = the PRE-DAWN darkness — *SUḤŪR* = the PRE-DAWN MEAL** — ⭐ **magic "hides" like that dim hour.** ⭐ ***Inna mina'l-bayāni la-siḥrā*** — **"some ELOQUENCE is MAGIC" (Bukhārī).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-lammā*** + past = "**when**." ⭐ ***hādhā siḥrun mubīnun*** — nominal sentence (dismissive certainty). ⭐ ⚠️ **Variant: *hādhā sāḥirun mubīn*** ("this is a clear MAGICIAN").
- **Why this exact word:** *siḥr* — ⭐ **the standard label for every messenger's proofs — clarity renamed as trickery.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **CLEAR PROOFS (*bayyināt*) → called CLEAR MAGIC (*siḥrun mubīn*) — clarity denied by renaming.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir 74:24 (*siḥrun yuʾthar* — al-Walīd) / Ṣaff 61:6 (*siḥrun mubīn*) — every messenger: "MAGICIAN or MADMAN" (51:52; Qalam 68:2).** ⭐⭐ **"He" = ʿĪsā OR Aḥmad ﷺ — both called magicians.** ⭐⭐ ***Saḥar* = PRE-DAWN; *SUḤŪR* = PRE-DAWN MEAL; "some eloquence is MAGIC" (Bukhārī).** ⚠️ **Variant *sāḥirun*.**

## §4 — The Light They Can't Blow Out (v.7–9)

**12. وَمَنْ أَظْلَمُ مِمَّنِ افْتَرَىٰ عَلَى اللَّهِ الْكَذِبَ وَهُوَ يُدْعَىٰ إِلَى الْإِسْلَامِ — *wa man aẓlamu mimmani'ftarā ʿala'llāhi'l-kadhiba wa huwa yudʿā ila'l-islām*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *aẓlam* — ⭐ **MORE unjust** (*ẓulm* = *ẓulmah*, darkness). *iftarā* — ⭐⭐ **FABRICATED** (Form VIII of *farā* = to CUT leather → to invent a lie; ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:12** — *bi-buhtānin **yaftarīnah***!; ⭐ ***iftirāʾ* = SLANDER**). *kadhib* — ⭐ **a LIE**. *yudʿā* — ⭐ **is INVITED / called** (passive of *daʿā*; ⭐ ***daʿwah* = an INVITATION**). *al-islām* — ⭐⭐ **SUBMISSION** (*s-l-m*; ⭐ ***salām* = PEACE**; ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:23** — *as-Salām*!).
- **Here it means:** "**And who is more unjust than one who fabricates a lie about Allah while he is being invited to Islam?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE WORST INJUSTICE — inventing lies about Allah WHILE being invited to submit.** ⭐ ***Man AẒLAMU*** — ⭐ **"who is MORE unjust?"** — ⭐ **a rhetorical question = NO ONE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Wa huwa YUDʿĀ ila'l-ISLĀM*** — ⭐ **"WHILE he's being INVITED to Islam"** — ⭐ **the irony: someone offers him peace (*islām* ← *salām*) and he answers with a LIE (calling the proofs "magic," v.6).** ⭐ **Invited to the light → fabricates darkness.**
  ⭐⭐ ***IFTARĀ* — FABRICATION** — ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:12** — *buhtānin **yaftarīnah*** (a slander fabricated against PEOPLE) ⭐ **→ Ṣaff 61:7** — *iftarā ʿala'llāhi'l-kadhib* (a lie fabricated against ALLAH). ⭐ **Against people (60) / against Allah (61) — the second is worse.** ⭐ ***Farā* = to CUT leather — a lie "cut and shaped."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man aẓlamu*** = rhetorical question (*istifhām inkārī*). ⭐ ***mimmani*** = *min* + *man*. ⭐⭐ ***wa huwa yudʿā*** = ***ḥāl*** (passive — "**while** being invited").
- **Why this exact word:** *yudʿā ila'l-islām* — ⭐ **the invitation that makes the lie unforgivable.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAN AẒLAMU…* = who is MORE unjust? — NO ONE — than one who fabricates lies about Allah WHILE being INVITED to Islam.** ⭐ **Offered PEACE (*salām*) → answers with a LIE.** ⭐⭐ ***IFTIRĀʾ*: against PEOPLE (Mumtaḥanah 60:12) / against ALLAH (Ṣaff 61:7).** ⭐ ***Farā* = CUT leather.**

**13. وَاللَّهُ لَا يَهْدِي الْقَوْمَ الظَّالِمِينَ — *wa'llāhu lā yahdi'l-qawma'ẓ-ẓālimīn*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *yahdī* — ⭐ **GUIDES**. *ẓālimīn* — ⭐ **WRONGDOERS**.
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= JUMUʿAH 62:5 WORD-FOR-WORD.** ⭐ **AND Ṣaff has BOTH formulas:** ⭐ **v.5** — *lā yahdi'l-qawma'l-**FĀSIQĪN*** (= Munāfiqūn 63:6) ⭐ **v.7** — *lā yahdi'l-qawma'**Ẓ-ẒĀLIMĪN*** (= Jumuʿah 62:5). ⭐⭐ **The two "not-guided" groups — DEFIANT (Mūsā's people who swerved) and WRONGDOERS (those who fabricate against Allah).** ⭐ **Not guided — because they REFUSED guidance first** (v.5's *zāghū → azāgha*).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā yahdī*** = negated present (ongoing law).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the Jumuʿah formula, completing the pair in one surah.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ṣaff holds BOTH: *lā yahdi'l-qawma'l-FĀSIQĪN* (v.5 = Munāfiqūn 63:6) and *Ẓ-ẒĀLIMĪN* (v.7 = Jumuʿah 62:5).** ⭐ **Not guided because they REFUSED first (v.5).**

**14. يُرِيدُونَ لِيُطْفِئُوا نُورَ اللَّهِ بِأَفْوَاهِهِمْ — *yurīdūna li-yuṭfiʾū nūra'llāhi bi-afwāhihim*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *yurīdūna* — ⭐ **they WANT / intend**. *yuṭfiʾū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**EXTINGUISHING**" family — *ṭafiʾa* = to go OUT (fire, light); ⭐ ***aṭfaʾa* (Form IV) = to EXTINGUISH, put out**; ⭐⭐ ***iṭfāʾ* = EXTINGUISHING** (⭐ ***al-iṭfāʾiyyah* = the FIRE BRIGADE**!; ⭐ ***rajul al-iṭfāʾ* = a FIREFIGHTER**; ⭐ ***muṭfiʾat ḥarīq* = a FIRE EXTINGUISHER**!); ⭐ ***inṭifāʾ* = going out / EXTINCTION**. *nūr* — ⭐⭐⭐ **LIGHT** (⭐ **Taghābun 64:8** — *an-nūr alladhī anzalnā* (the Qurʾān)!; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — *nūruhum yasʿā*!; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:11**). *afwāh* — ⭐⭐ **MOUTHS** (plural of *fam / fūh*; ⭐ ***fūhah* = a NOZZLE / vent / opening** (⭐ *fūhat al-burkān* = a **volcano's VENT**!); ⭐ ***shafawī* = ORAL / verbal** (different root, same idea); ⭐ ***mushāfahah* = face-to-face, oral**).
- **Here it means:** "**They want to extinguish Allah's light with their mouths…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST MEMORABLE IMAGE IN THE SURAH — trying to BLOW OUT the SUN.** ⭐ ***Li-yuṭfiʾū NŪRA'LLĀHI BI-AFWĀHIHIM*** — ⭐ **"to EXTINGUISH ALLAH'S LIGHT with their MOUTHS"** — ⭐ **picture someone puffing at the SUN, trying to blow it out like a candle.** ⭐⭐⭐ **"With their MOUTHS" — their only weapons are WORDS: lies (v.7 *iftirāʾ*), insults (v.6 "magic!"), propaganda.** ⭐ **You can blow out a candle — not the sun.** ⭐ **And Allah's light (the Qurʾān, Islam) is the sun.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***IṬFĀʾ* = EXTINGUISHING — *AL-IṬFĀʾIYYAH* = the FIRE BRIGADE; *muṭfiʾat ḥarīq* = FIRE EXTINGUISHER.** ⭐ **They tried to be "firefighters" against Allah's light.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *NŪR* THREAD — now FOUR surahs:** ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:11** — *mina'ẓ-ẓulumāti **ila'n-nūr*** (brought INTO the light). ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — ***nūruhum** yasʿā* (CARRYING light on the Day — *atmim lanā nūranā*). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:8** — ***an-nūr** alladhī anzalnā* (the LIGHT = the Qurʾān). ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:8** — *li-yuṭfiʾū **nūra'llāh*** (they try to BLOW IT OUT — and can't).
  ⭐⭐ ***AFWĀH* (MOUTHS) — *fūhah* = a VENT / NOZZLE (*fūhat al-burkān* = a volcano's VENT)** — ⭐ **their mouths: vents of hot air.** ⭐ **(At-Tawbah 9:32 — nearly identical: *yurīdūna an yuṭfiʾū nūra'llāhi bi-afwāhihim wa yaʾba'llāhu illā an yutimma nūrah*.)**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***yurīdūna LI-yuṭfiʾū*** — ⚠️ **the *lām* after *yurīdu*** (= *an*) — ⭐ classically: **"they want [to do what they do] in order to extinguish"** — ⭐ vs 9:32's ***an yuṭfiʾū*** (no *lām*). ⭐ ***bi-afwāhihim*** = instrumental ***bi-*** ("**with** their mouths").
- **Why this exact word:** *iṭfāʾ* + *afwāh* — ⭐ **the futility of puffing at the sun.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LI-YUṬFIʾŪ NŪRA'LLĀHI BI-AFWĀHIHIM* = trying to BLOW OUT Allah's light with their MOUTHS — puffing at the SUN.** ⭐ **Their only weapons: WORDS (lies v.7, "magic!" v.6).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***IṬFĀʾ* → *AL-IṬFĀʾIYYAH* = FIRE BRIGADE; *muṭfiʾat ḥarīq* = FIRE EXTINGUISHER.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***NŪR* ×4: INTO the light (65:11), CARRYING it (66:8), the light = the QURʾĀN (64:8), they try to BLOW IT OUT (61:8).** ⭐⭐ ***Afwāh* → *fūhah* = VENT (*fūhat al-burkān*).** ⭐ **≈ Tawbah 9:32.** ⚠️ ***Yurīdūna li-* (vs 9:32 *an*).**

**15. وَاللَّهُ مُتِمُّ نُورِهِ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ الْكَافِرُونَ — *wa'llāhu mutimmu nūrihi wa law kariha'l-kāfirūn*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *mutimm* — ⭐⭐⭐ **COMPLETING / perfecting** (Form IV participle of *tamma*; ⭐ ***tamām!* = PERFECT / OK!**; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — *rabbanā **atmim** lanā nūranā*!; ⭐ ***itmām* = COMPLETION**; ⭐ ***al-yawma… atmamtu ʿalaykum niʿmatī*** — 5:3!). *kariha* — ⭐⭐ the "**DISLIKE / aversion / compulsion**" family — *kariha* = to DISLIKE, hate; ⭐ ***karāhiyah* = HATRED / aversion** (⭐ *ḥaqd wa karāhiyah* = **hatred / "hate speech"**!); ⭐⭐ ***makrūh* = DISLIKED** — ⭐ **a FIQH CATEGORY: *makrūh* (disliked but not forbidden)**!; ⭐ ***kurh* = AVERSION / unwillingness**; ⭐ ***ikrāh* = COMPULSION** (⭐ *lā **ikrāha** fi'd-dīn* — 2:256 — "no COMPULSION in religion"!); ⭐ ***mukrah* = COERCED** (⭐ fiqh: the *mukrah*'s statements may not count!).
- **Here it means:** "**…but Allah will complete His light, even if the disbelievers dislike it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA'LLĀHU MUTIMMU NŪRIH* — "but ALLAH will COMPLETE His light"** — ⭐ **the answer to the puffing: not just "it won't go out" — it will be COMPLETED, perfected, full.** ⭐ ***MUTIMM* — an active PARTICIPLE (noun)** — ⭐ **not "will complete" (verb) but "IS the Completer" — a settled, permanent fact.** ⭐⭐⭐ **TAḤRĪM 66:8** — the believers' duʿāʾ: ***rabbanā ATMIM lanā nūranā*** — ⭐ **"Our Lord, COMPLETE our light"** — ⭐ **and here: *Allāhu MUTIMMU nūrih* — "Allah IS the COMPLETER of His light."** ⭐⭐ **The believers ASK for their light to be completed; Allah DECLARES He'll complete His.** ⭐ **The prayer and its guarantee.** ⭐ ***Tamām!* = "PERFECT!"; 5:3 *atmamtu ʿalaykum niʿmatī* — the religion COMPLETED.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA LAW KARIHA'L-KĀFIRŪN* — "EVEN IF the disbelievers HATE it"** — ⭐ **their dislike changes nothing.** ⭐ ***KARIHA* → *MAKRŪH* (the fiqh category — "DISLIKED")** — ⭐ ***IKRĀH* = COMPULSION — *lā ikrāha fi'd-dīn* (2:256)** — ⭐ **Allah won't COMPEL them to believe — but their HATRED won't stop His light.** ⭐ ***Karāhiyah* = HATRED.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***mutimmu nūrihi*** = **active participle + *iḍāfah*** (permanent state) — ⚠️ **variant *mutimmun nūrahu*** (tanwīn + object). ⭐⭐ ***wa law*** = "**EVEN IF**" (concessive).
- **Why this exact word:** *mutimm* — ⭐ **completion as a settled fact — the guarantee behind the believers' prayer.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA'LLĀHU MUTIMMU NŪRIH* = ALLAH IS the COMPLETER of His light — a settled FACT (noun, not verb).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8: believers PRAY *atmim lanā nūranā* → Ṣaff 61:8: Allah DECLARES *mutimmu nūrih*.** ⭐ ***Tamām!* = PERFECT; 5:3 *atmamtu*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA LAW KARIHA* = EVEN IF they HATE it — *MAKRŪH* (fiqh: DISLIKED); *IKRĀH* = COMPULSION (2:256 *lā ikrāha*); *karāhiyah* = HATRED.** ⚠️ **Variant *mutimmun nūrahu*.**

**16. هُوَ الَّذِي أَرْسَلَ رَسُولَهُ بِالْهُدَىٰ وَدِينِ الْحَقِّ لِيُظْهِرَهُ عَلَى الدِّينِ كُلِّهِ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ الْمُشْرِكُونَ — *huwa'lladhī arsala rasūlahu bi'l-hudā wa dīni'l-ḥaqqi li-yuẓhirahu ʿala'd-dīni kullihi wa law kariha'l-mushrikūn*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *arsala* — ⭐ **SENT** (*risālah* = a MESSAGE / letter!). *hudā* — ⭐ **GUIDANCE**. *dīn al-ḥaqq* — ⭐ **the RELIGION OF TRUTH**. *yuẓhirahu* — ⭐⭐⭐ **to make it PREVAIL / manifest / uppermost** (Form IV of *ẓahara* ← ***ẓahr* = the BACK**; ⭐ ***aẓhara ʿalā* = to make PREVAIL over / to REVEAL to** — ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:3** — *wa **aẓharahu**'llāhu ʿalayh* (REVEALED to him)!; ⭐ **Jinn 72:26** — *fa-lā **yuẓhiru** ʿalā ghaybihi aḥadā*!; ⭐ ***ẓuhr* = NOON** (when the sun is UPPERMOST!)). *mushrikūn* — ⭐ **POLYTHEISTS** (*shirk*).
- **Here it means:** "**It is He who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth, to make it prevail over all religion, even if the polytheists dislike it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE PROMISE OF PREVALENCE.** ⭐ ***Li-YUẒHIRAHU ʿala'd-dīni kullih*** — ⭐ **"to make it PREVAIL over ALL religion"** — ⭐ ***aẓhara ʿalā* = to put ON TOP / make UPPERMOST** (⭐ ***ẓuhr* = NOON — when the sun is at its HIGHEST**!) — ⭐ **Islam rising like the noon sun — after v.8's attempt to blow it out.** ⭐ Classical: **prevailing by PROOF (always) and by power (at times, as Allah wills).** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *Ẓ-H-R* root — the set's thread:** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:3** — *aẓharahu'llāhu ʿalayh* (REVEALED a secret to him). ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:4** — *taẓāharā* (two wives BACKING each other). ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:9** — *ẓāharū ʿalā ikhrājikum* (enemies BACKING your expulsion). ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:9** — *li-yuẓhirahu ʿala'd-dīn* (to make it PREVAIL). ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:14** — *fa-aṣbaḥū **ẓāhirīn*** (they became DOMINANT). ⭐⭐ **One root: reveal, back up, prevail, dominate.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **v.8 *KĀFIRŪN* → v.9 *MUSHRIKŪN*** — ⭐ **two parallel verses ending *wa law kariha…*** — ⭐ **v.8: about the LIGHT (the message) — the DISBELIEVERS (who cover it) hate it.** ⭐ **v.9: about the RELIGION prevailing over others — the POLYTHEISTS (whose idols it replaces) hate it.** ⭐ **Precise matching of word to theme.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Bi'l-HUDĀ wa DĪNI'L-ḤAQQ*** — ⭐ **GUIDANCE (knowledge) + the RELIGION of TRUTH (practice)** — ⭐ **the two halves of the message.** ⭐ ***Risālah* = a MESSAGE / LETTER** (*arsala*).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***huwa'lladhī*** = restriction ("**HE** is the one who"). ⭐ ***li-yuẓhirahu*** = purpose (*lām at-taʿlīl*). ⭐ ***kullihi*** = emphasis ("**ALL** of it").
- **Why this exact word:** *aẓhara* — ⭐ **prevailing like the noon sun — the answer to those who tried to blow out its light.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LI-YUẒHIRAHU ʿALA'D-DĪNI KULLIH* = to make it PREVAIL over ALL religion — rising like the NOON sun (*ẓuhr*) after they tried to blow it out.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ẓ-H-R*: REVEALED (66:3), BACKED (66:4, 60:9), PREVAIL (61:9), DOMINANT (61:14).** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.8 *KĀFIRŪN* (about the LIGHT) / v.9 *MUSHRIKŪN* (about the RELIGION over idols) — both *wa law kariha*.** ⭐⭐ ***Hudā* (knowledge) + *dīn al-ḥaqq* (practice).** ⭐ ***Risālah* = LETTER / MESSAGE.**

## §5 — The Trade (v.10–13)

**17. يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا هَلْ أَدُلُّكُمْ عَلَىٰ تِجَارَةٍ تُنجِيكُم مِّنْ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ — *yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū hal adullukum ʿalā tijāratin tunjīkum min ʿadhābin alīm*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *adullukum* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**POINTING THE WAY / GUIDING / indicating**" family — *dalla* = **to POINT TO, show the way, indicate**; ⭐⭐ ***dalīl* = a GUIDE / EVIDENCE / PROOF / MANUAL** (⭐ ***dalīl al-mustakhdim* = USER MANUAL**!; ⭐ *dalīl siyāḥī* = a **TOURIST GUIDE**; ⭐ ***ad-dalīl*** = the **EVIDENCE** in fiqh!); ⭐ ***dalālah* = SIGNIFICANCE / meaning / indication** (⭐ *dalālah* = **semantics / connotation**!); ⭐ ***dallāl* = a BROKER / auctioneer** (who "points" buyers to goods!); ⭐ ***dalāl* = coquettishness / pampering** (⭐ *mudallal* = **SPOILED**!); ⭐ ***istidlāl* = DEDUCTION / inference**. *tijārah* — ⭐⭐⭐ **TRADE / COMMERCE** (⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:11** — *tijāratan aw lahwā*!; ⭐ ***matjar* = SHOP**). *tunjīkum* — ⭐⭐ **SAVES / rescues you** (*najā*; ⭐ ***najāt* = SALVATION**; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:11** — *wa **najjinī** min Firʿawn*!; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:14** — *thumma **yunjīh***). *alīm* — ⭐ **PAINFUL**.
- **Here it means:** "**O you who believe, shall I guide you to a trade that will save you from a painful punishment?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE GREATEST SALES PITCH EVER — *HAL ADULLUKUM ʿALĀ TIJĀRAH?*** ⭐ **"Shall I SHOW you a TRADE?"** — ⭐ **in a merchant society (Makkah! Madīnah's markets!) — Allah speaks their language: a DEAL, a TRANSACTION.** ⭐ **And the return? Not profit — *TUNJĪKUM* — SALVATION from painful punishment.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE COMMERCE THREAD — completed across the set:** ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:9** — *dharu'l-**bayʿ*** (LEAVE the market sale). ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:11** — they left the Prophet ﷺ for a ***tijārah*** (a caravan's TRADE). ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:9** — *humu'l-**khāsirūn*** (the LOSERS). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:9** — *yawm at-**taghābun*** (the AUDIT of mutual loss/gain). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:17** — ***qarḍan ḥasanā*** (a LOAN to Allah). ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:12** — ***yubāyiʿnak*** (the women's PLEDGE — a sale). ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:10** — ***hal adullukum ʿalā TIJĀRAH*** — ⭐⭐⭐ **the TRADE that SAVES — answering Jumuʿah 62:11's trade that DISTRACTED.** ⭐ **The caravan's trade pulled them from the khuṭbah; THIS trade pulls them to Paradise.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ADULLUKUM* — *DALĪL* = a GUIDE / EVIDENCE / USER MANUAL** — ⭐ ***dallāl* = a BROKER** — ⭐ **Allah as the "broker" pointing you to the best deal!** ⭐ ***Dalālah* = SIGNIFICANCE; *istidlāl* = DEDUCTION; *mudallal* = SPOILED.**
  ⭐⭐ ***TUNJĪKUM* — SALVATION** — ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:11** — Āsiyah: *najjinī* (RESCUE me) ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:14** — the criminal's ransom fails to *yunjīh* ⭐ **→ here: the TRADE that DOES rescue.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***hal adullukum*** = question (*ʿarḍ* — an OFFER/invitation, not a real question). ⭐ ***tunjīkum*** = *ṣifah* of *tijāratin*. ⚠️ ⭐ **Variant: *tunajjīkum*** (Form II).
- **Why this exact word:** *tijārah* — ⭐ **faith offered in the vocabulary of the marketplace — the trade that saves.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***HAL ADULLUKUM ʿALĀ TIJĀRATIN TUNJĪKUM* = "Shall I SHOW you a TRADE that SAVES you?" — Allah in merchants' language.** ⭐⭐⭐ **COMMERCE THREAD: *bayʿ* (62:9), *tijārah* that DISTRACTED (62:11), *khāsirūn* (63:9), *taghābun* (64:9), *qarḍ* (64:17), *bayʿah* (60:12) → the *TIJĀRAH* that SAVES (61:10).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DALĪL* = GUIDE / EVIDENCE / USER MANUAL; *dallāl* = BROKER; *dalālah* = SIGNIFICANCE; *istidlāl* = DEDUCTION.** ⭐⭐ ***Tunjīkum*: Āsiyah's *najjinī* (66:11); the ransom that failed (70:14).** ⚠️ **Variant *tunajjīkum*.**

**18. تُؤْمِنُونَ بِاللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ وَتُجَاهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ بِأَمْوَالِكُمْ وَأَنفُسِكُمْ — *tuʾminūna bi'llāhi wa rasūlihi wa tujāhidūna fī sabīli'llāhi bi-amwālikum wa anfusikum*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *tuʾminūna* — ⭐ **you BELIEVE** (*amn* = safety). *tujāhidūna* — ⭐⭐ **you STRIVE** (Form III of *jahada*; ⭐ ***juhd* = EFFORT**; ⭐ ***jihād***; ⭐ ***ijtihād***; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:9** — *jāhid*!; ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:1** — *jihādan fī sabīlī*!). *amwāl* — ⭐ **WEALTH**. *anfus* — ⭐ **SELVES / LIVES**.
- **Here it means:** "**[It is that] you believe in Allah and His Messenger and strive in the cause of Allah with your wealth and your lives.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TRADE'S TERMS — TWO things you "PAY":** ⭐ **(1) *TUʾMINŪNA* — FAITH (inner).** ⭐ **(2) *TUJĀHIDŪNA bi-AMWĀLIKUM wa ANFUSIKUM* — STRIVING with WEALTH and LIVES (outer).** ⭐⭐⭐ **The answer to v.2's rebuke: *lima taqūlūna mā lā tafʿalūn* — here's what to DO, not just say.** ⭐ **Faith (said) + striving (done) = the complete trade.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **A GRAMMAR GEM — *TUʾMINŪNA* looks like a STATEMENT ("you believe") — but MEANS a COMMAND ("believe!").** ⭐ **Proof: v.12 — *YAGHFIR lakum* — is JUSSIVE (the *rāʾ* has sukūn)** — ⭐ **a jussive needs a COMMAND before it to answer** — ⭐ **so *tuʾminūna* = *āminū* (imperative in meaning)!** ⭐ **ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd's reading was actually *āminū bi'llāhi… wa jāhidū*.** ⭐⭐ **Classical (az-Zamakhsharī): the statement-form is MORE gentle and MORE emphatic — "you WILL believe" — as if it's already happening.** ⭐ **A command dressed as a description of who you are.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***BI-AMWĀLIKUM WA ANFUSIKUM* — WEALTH before LIVES** — ⭐ **almost always in this order in the Qurʾān** — ⭐ classically: **wealth is given more often (every day) than life (rarely); wealth is also the first test (Taghābun 64:15 — *amwālukum wa awlādukum fitnah*!).** ⭐ **(Except 9:111 — *anfusahum wa amwālahum* — "PURCHASED their lives and wealth" — lives first in the SALE!)** ⭐⭐ **And the *infāq* / *nifāq* thread: Munāfiqūn — "don't spend"; Ṣaff — strive with your WEALTH.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐⭐ ***tuʾminūna, tujāhidūna*** = **present indicative in FORM, IMPERATIVE in MEANING** (*khabar bi-maʿnā al-amr*) — ⭐ proven by the **jussive answer *yaghfir*** (v.12). ⭐ **Variant (Ibn Masʿūd): *āminū… wa jāhidū*.** ⭐ ***amwālikum*** before ***anfusikum***.
- **Why this exact word:** **the indicative-as-command** — ⭐ **the deal's terms stated as if you're already keeping them.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TRADE'S TERMS: BELIEVE (inner) + STRIVE with WEALTH and LIVES (outer) — the answer to v.2's "say but don't do."** ⭐⭐⭐ **GRAMMAR GEM: *TUʾMINŪNA* = statement in FORM, COMMAND in MEANING — proven by v.12's JUSSIVE *yaghfir*; Ibn Masʿūd read *āminū*.** ⭐ **A command dressed as who you ARE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AMWĀL* before *ANFUS* (wealth is given more often, the first test — 64:15) — except 9:111 (*anfusahum wa amwālahum*).** ⭐ ***Jihād* thread: Taḥrīm 66:9, Mumtaḥanah 60:1.**

**19. ذَٰلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ — *dhālikum khayrun lakum in kuntum taʿlamūn*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *khayr* — ⭐ **BETTER / good**. *taʿlamūn* — ⭐ **you KNOW**.
- **Here it means:** "**That is better for you, if you only knew.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD with JUMUʿAH 62:9!** ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:9** — *fa'sʿaw ilā dhikri'llāhi wa dharu'l-bayʿ · **dhālikum khayrun lakum in kuntum taʿlamūn*** — ⭐ **LEAVE the small trade → better for you.** ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:11** — *tuʾminūna… wa tujāhidūna… · **dhālikum khayrun lakum in kuntum taʿlamūn*** — ⭐ **MAKE the great trade → better for you.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The same sentence — LEAVING one trade (62) and TAKING another (61).** ⭐ ***In kuntum taʿlamūn* — "IF you only KNEW"** — ⭐ **the *law taʿlamūn* family (Takāthur 102:5, Nūḥ 71:4)** — ⭐ **knowledge is the difference between the good trade and the bad.** ⭐ **And Taghābun 64:16** — *wa anfiqū **khayran li-anfusikum*** (spend — BETTER for yourselves).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***dhālikum*** = "**that** (for you all)." ⭐ ***in kuntum taʿlamūna*** — answer implied ("[you'd do it]").
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the Jumuʿah sentence, now attached to the great trade.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= JUMUʿAH 62:9 WORD-FOR-WORD: LEAVE the small trade (62) / MAKE the great trade (61) — *dhālikum khayrun lakum in kuntum taʿlamūn*.** ⭐⭐ ***In kuntum taʿlamūn* — Takāthur 102:5, Nūḥ 71:4.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:16 *khayran li-anfusikum*.**

**20. يَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ ذُنُوبَكُمْ — *yaghfir lakum dhunūbakum*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *yaghfir* — ⭐⭐ **He will FORGIVE** (⚠️ **JUSSIVE**; *ghafara* = to COVER; ⭐ ***mighfar* = a HELMET**!). *dhunūb* — ⭐⭐ the "**TAIL / consequence**" family — ***dhanb* = a SIN** (⭐ classically linked to ***dhanab* = a TAIL** — a sin's consequence FOLLOWS you like a tail!; ⭐ ***dhanab* = TAIL** — *dhanab al-kalb* = a dog's tail!; ⭐ ***mudhnib* = GUILTY / a sinner**; ⭐ ***dhunāb* = a COMET's tail**!).
- **Here it means:** "**He will forgive you your sins…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TRADE'S RETURN #1 — FORGIVENESS.** ⭐ ***YAGHFIR*** — ⭐ **JUSSIVE (sukūn on the *rāʾ*)** — ⭐ **the proof (v.11) that *tuʾminūna* is really a COMMAND — "BELIEVE… [and] He WILL forgive."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHANB* (SIN) ← *DHANAB* (TAIL)** — ⭐ **a sin "TRAILS" behind you, a consequence that follows you like a tail** — ⭐ **forgiveness = Allah CUTTING OFF the tail.** ⭐ ***Dhunāb* = a COMET's TAIL; *mudhnib* = GUILTY.** ⭐⭐ ***Ghafara* = to COVER — *mighfar* = a HELMET** — ⭐ **He COVERS your sins as a helmet covers your head.** ⭐ **Takwīr 81:9** — *bi-ayyi **dhanbin** qutilat* (for what SIN was she killed?).
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐⭐ ***yaghfir*** = **JUSSIVE** — **answer of an IMPLIED imperative** (*āminū* hidden in *tuʾminūna*). ⭐ ***dhunūbakum*** = object.
- **Why this exact word:** *yaghfir* — ⭐ **the jussive that reveals the grammar of v.11 — and the first return on the trade.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAGHFIR LAKUM DHUNŪBAKUM* = RETURN #1: FORGIVENESS — JUSSIVE (proof that v.11's *tuʾminūna* = a COMMAND).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHANB* (SIN) ← *DHANAB* (TAIL) — a sin TRAILS you; forgiveness CUTS the tail; *dhunāb* = COMET's tail; *mudhnib* = GUILTY.** ⭐⭐ ***Ghafara* = COVER — *mighfar* = HELMET.** ⭐ **Takwīr 81:9 *dhanb*.**

**21. وَيُدْخِلْكُمْ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ وَمَسَاكِنَ طَيِّبَةً فِي جَنَّاتِ عَدْنٍ — *wa yudkhilkum jannātin tajrī min taḥtiha'l-anhāru wa masākina ṭayyibatan fī jannāti ʿadn*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *yudkhilkum* — ⭐ **ADMIT you** (jussive). *masākin* — ⭐⭐ **DWELLINGS** (plural of *maskan*; *sakana* = to be STILL, settle; ⭐ ***sakan* = HOUSING**; ⭐ ***sakīnah* = TRANQUILLITY**; ⭐ ***miskīn* = the POOR**!; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:6** — *askinūhunna*!). *ṭayyibah* — ⭐⭐ **PURE / GOOD / pleasant** (*ṭayyib*; ⭐ ***ṭayyibāt* = GOOD things**; ⭐ ***ṭīb* = PERFUME**!; ⭐ ***ṭabīb* — a DOCTOR** — ⚠️ different root *ṭ-b-b*!). *ʿadn* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**SETTLING PERMANENTLY / staying put**" family — *ʿadana* = **to STAY, settle permanently in a place**; ⭐ ***ʿadn* = PERMANENT residence / EDEN**; ⭐⭐⭐ ***maʿdin* (pl. *maʿādin*) = a MINERAL / METAL / MINE** (⭐ what "settles" permanently in the earth!; ⭐ *maʿdin* = **METAL** — *ʿumlah maʿdiniyyah* = a **COIN**!; ⭐ *miyāh maʿdiniyyah* = **MINERAL WATER**!; ⭐ *at-taʿdīn* = **MINING**!); ⭐ ***ʿAdan*** = the city of **ADEN** (Yemen).
- **Here it means:** "**…and admit you into gardens beneath which rivers flow, and pleasant dwellings in Gardens of Eden [permanent residence].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TRADE'S RETURN #2 — and the most beautiful property in existence.** ⭐ ***JANNĀT tajrī min taḥtiha'l-anhār*** — ⭐ **gardens with flowing rivers** ⭐ ***wa MASĀKINA ṬAYYIBAH*** — ⭐ **PLEASANT, PURE dwellings** ⭐ ***fī JANNĀTI ʿADN*** — ⭐ **in Gardens of PERMANENT RESIDENCE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿADN* = PERMANENCE — from *ʿadana* (to SETTLE for good)** — ⭐ **and the same root gives *MAʿDIN* = a MINERAL / METAL** — ⭐ **what settles PERMANENTLY in the earth** — ⭐ ***miyāh maʿdiniyyah* = MINERAL WATER; *ʿumlah maʿdiniyyah* = a COIN; *taʿdīn* = MINING; *ʿAdan* = ADEN.** ⭐ **Paradise = the "Gardens of Settling-Forever" — no eviction, no moving out.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***MASĀKIN* — the *SAKANA* root (STILLNESS / DWELLING)** — ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:6** — *askinūhunna min ḥaythu sakantum* (HOUSE them where you live) ⭐ **→ here: Allah HOUSES you in Paradise** — ⭐ ***sakīnah* = TRANQUILLITY** — **dwellings of peace** — ⭐ **and the *miskīn* (the poor — "made still" by poverty) — in Paradise, everyone has a *maskan*.** ⭐ **The Muhājirūn (Ḥashr 59:8) lost their HOMES (*diyār*) for Allah → here: *masākin ṭayyibah*.**
  ⭐⭐ ***ṬAYYIBAH* — PURE / pleasant** — ⭐ ***ṭīb* = PERFUME** — ⚠️ **not *ṭabīb* (doctor — *ṭ-b-b*)!**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yudkhilkum*** = jussive (joined to *yaghfir*). ⭐ ***masākina*** = **diptote** (*mafāʿil*), joined to *jannātin* (object). ⭐ ***ʿadnin*** = genitive (*iḍāfah*).
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿadn* — ⭐ **permanent settlement — the root of minerals that never move.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **RETURN #2: gardens, rivers, PLEASANT DWELLINGS (*masākin ṭayyibah*) in *JANNĀTI ʿADN* (Gardens of PERMANENT RESIDENCE).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿADN* ← *ʿadana* (SETTLE for good) → *MAʿDIN* = MINERAL / METAL (*miyāh maʿdiniyyah* = MINERAL WATER; *taʿdīn* = MINING); *ʿAdan* = ADEN.** ⭐ **No eviction, ever.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MASĀKIN* ← *sakana*: Ṭalāq 65:6 (*askinūhunna*) → Allah houses YOU; the Muhājirūn lost their *diyār* (59:8) → given *masākin*.** ⭐ ***Sakīnah* = TRANQUILLITY.** ⚠️ ***Ṭayyib* (ṭ-y-b) ≠ *ṭabīb* (ṭ-b-b).**

**22. ذَٰلِكَ الْفَوْزُ الْعَظِيمُ — *dhālika'l-fawzu'l-ʿaẓīm*** · v.12
- **Root feeling:** *fawz* — ⭐⭐ **WIN / success** (⭐ ***fāʾiz* = WINNER**; ⭐ ***mafāzah* = DESERT** (place of winning!)).
- **Here it means:** "**That is the tremendous success.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= TAGHĀBUN 64:9 WORD-FOR-WORD** — *dhālika'l-fawzu'l-ʿaẓīm*. ⭐ **And Ḥashr 59:20** — *aṣḥābu'l-jannati humu'l-**fāʾizūn***. ⭐⭐ **The trade (*tijārah*) ends in the WIN (*fawz*)** — ⭐ **the merchant's dream: a deal that can't lose.** ⭐ **Taghābun: the Day of loss and gain — here: the guaranteed GAIN.** ⭐ ***Mafāzah* = DESERT (Nabaʾ 78:31 *mafāzā*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***dhālika*** (far — exalted) + definite + adjective = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the winner's word closing the trade.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHĀLIKA'L-FAWZU'L-ʿAẒĪM* = Taghābun 64:9 WORD-FOR-WORD; Ḥashr 59:20 *al-fāʾizūn*.** ⭐ **The TRADE ends in the WIN — a deal that can't lose.** ⭐ ***Mafāzah* = DESERT (78:31).**

**23. وَأُخْرَىٰ تُحِبُّونَهَا ۖ نَصْرٌ مِّنَ اللَّهِ وَفَتْحٌ قَرِيبٌ ۗ وَبَشِّرِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ — *wa ukhrā tuḥibbūnahā · naṣrun mina'llāhi wa fatḥun qarīb · wa bashshiri'l-muʾminīn*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *ukhrā* — ⭐ **ANOTHER [thing]** (feminine of *ākhar*; ⚠️ ≠ *ākhirah*). *tuḥibbūnahā* — ⭐ **you LOVE it**. *naṣr* — ⭐⭐⭐ **HELP / VICTORY** (⭐ **SŪRAT AN-NAṢR 110:1** — *idhā jāʾa **naṣru'llāhi wa'l-fatḥ***!; ⭐ ***Anṣār***; ⭐ v.14 *anṣāra'llāh*!). *fatḥ* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**OPENING**" family — ***fatḥ* = OPENING → VICTORY / CONQUEST** (⭐ ***Fatḥ Makkah* = the CONQUEST of Makkah**!; ⭐ ***miftāḥ* = a KEY**!; ⭐ ***al-Fātiḥah* = the OPENING [surah]**!; ⭐ ***iftitāḥ* = an INAUGURATION / opening ceremony**; ⭐ ***al-Fattāḥ*** = the Opener / Judge; ⭐ ***fatḥah*** — the vowel "a" (mouth OPENS)!). *qarīb* — ⭐ **NEAR**. *bashshir* — ⭐ **GIVE GOOD NEWS** (*bushrā*; ⭐ v.6 *mubashshiran*!).
- **Here it means:** "**And [there is] another [favour] that you love: victory from Allah and a near conquest. And give good news to the believers.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A BONUS — and an honest acknowledgment of human nature.** ⭐ ***Wa UKHRĀ TUḤIBBŪNAHĀ*** — ⭐ **"and ANOTHER thing that YOU LOVE"** — ⭐ **Allah acknowledges: yes, you love to see victory in THIS life too.** ⭐ **The main return = forgiveness + Paradise (v.12); the bonus = victory now (v.13).** ⭐⭐ **Not shamed for wanting it — promised it.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***NAṢRUN MINA'LLĀHI WA FATḤUN QARĪB* — and SŪRAT AN-NAṢR 110:1!** ⭐ ***idhā jāʾa NAṢRU'LLĀHI wa'l-FATḤ*** — ⭐ **"when the HELP of Allah and the OPENING/CONQUEST come"** — ⭐ **the SAME PAIR (*naṣr + fatḥ*).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ṣaff PROMISES it (*fatḥun QARĪB* — "near"); Naṣr ANNOUNCES it has come (*idhā jāʾa*).** ⭐ **The conquest of Makkah (8 AH) — the promise fulfilled.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***FATḤ* = OPENING → CONQUEST** — ⭐ **a conquest in Islam is an "OPENING" — of hearts and lands to guidance** — ⭐ ***miftāḥ* = a KEY; *al-Fātiḥah* = the OPENING surah; *iftitāḥ* = INAUGURATION; *fatḥah* = the vowel "a" (mouth OPENS).**
  ⭐⭐ ***BASHSHIR* — the *bushrā* root** — ⭐ **v.6 — ʿĪsā *mubashshiran* (good news of Aḥmad) → v.13 — Aḥmad ﷺ told to *bashshir* (give good news to the believers).** ⭐ **The announced one becomes the announcer.**
  ⭐ ⚠️ ***Ukhrā* (ANOTHER, fem.) ≠ *ākhirah* (the HEREAFTER)!**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***wa ukhrā*** = subject of an implied predicate ("**and** [you have] another") — or object ("[He'll give you] another"). ⭐ ***naṣrun*** = ***badal*** (explaining *ukhrā*). ⭐ ***wa bashshiri*** = imperative (new sentence).
- **Why this exact word:** *ukhrā tuḥibbūnahā* — ⭐ **a bonus that honours the human love of visible victory.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA UKHRĀ TUḤIBBŪNAHĀ* = ANOTHER thing YOU LOVE — a BONUS: victory NOW (main return = Paradise, v.12).** ⭐ **Human nature acknowledged, not shamed.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***NAṢRUN MINA'LLĀHI WA FATḤUN QARĪB* — SŪRAT AN-NAṢR 110:1 (*naṣru'llāhi wa'l-fatḥ*): Ṣaff PROMISES (near), Naṣr ANNOUNCES (it came) — the Conquest of Makkah.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FATḤ* = OPENING → CONQUEST; *MIFTĀḤ* = KEY; *AL-FĀTIḤAH* = the OPENING; *iftitāḥ* = INAUGURATION; *fatḥah* = vowel "a".** ⭐⭐ ***Bashshir*: v.6 ʿĪsā *mubashshir* of Aḥmad → v.13 Aḥmad ﷺ told to *bashshir*.** ⚠️ ***Ukhrā* ≠ *ākhirah*.**

## §6 — Helpers of Allah (v.14)

**24. يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُونُوا أَنصَارَ اللَّهِ — *yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū kūnū anṣāra'llāh*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *kūnū* — ⭐ **BE**. *anṣār* — ⭐⭐⭐ **HELPERS / supporters** (plural of *nāṣir* / *naṣīr*; ⭐ ***al-Anṣār*** — the Madīnan Helpers — **Ḥashr 59:9**!; ⭐ ***naṣr* = VICTORY** (v.13!); ⭐ ***nāṣir* = SUPPORTER**; ⭐ ***intiṣār* = TRIUMPH**; ⭐ ***manṣūr* = VICTORIOUS** (a common name!)).
- **Here it means:** "**O you who believe, be helpers of Allah…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KŪNŪ ANṢĀRA'LLĀH* — "BE ALLAH'S HELPERS"** — ⭐ **Allah needs no help** (⭐ *wa'llāhu huwa'l-ghaniyyu'l-ḥamīd* — Mumtaḥanah 60:6, Taghābun 64:6!) — ⭐ **but He HONOURS you by calling your service to His religion "helping Allah."** ⭐ **An honour, not a need.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *ANṢĀR* — ḤASHR 59:9** — ⭐ **the Madīnan HELPERS who settled in faith, loved the emigrants, preferred them over themselves (*īthār*).** ⭐ **Ḥashr 59:8** — the Muhājirūn ***yanṣurūna'llāha wa rasūlah*** (SUPPORTING Allah and His Messenger). ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:14 — *kūnū anṣāra'llāh*: the title of the Anṣār — open to EVERY believer.** ⭐⭐ **v.13 *NAṢR* (victory FROM Allah) → v.14 *ANṢĀR* (helpers OF Allah)** — ⭐ **help Him, and He helps you** (⭐ *in tanṣuru'llāha yanṣurkum* — 47:7!). ⭐ ***Manṣūr* = VICTORIOUS (a name).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kūnū*** = imperative of *kāna*. ⭐ ***anṣāra'llāhi*** = *khabar* of *kūnū* (*iḍāfah*). ⚠️ ⭐ **Variant: *anṣāran li'llāh*** (with tanwīn + *li-*).
- **Why this exact word:** *anṣār* — ⭐ **the honoured title of Madīnah's Helpers, offered to all believers.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KŪNŪ ANṢĀRA'LLĀH* = BE ALLAH's HELPERS — He needs no help (60:6, 64:6) — an HONOUR, not a need.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The title of the ANṢĀR (Ḥashr 59:9) — open to EVERY believer; Muhājirūn *yanṣurūna'llāh* (59:8).** ⭐⭐ **v.13 *NAṢR* FROM Allah → v.14 *ANṢĀR* OF Allah — "help Allah, He helps you" (47:7).** ⭐ ***Manṣūr* = VICTORIOUS.** ⚠️ **Variant *anṣāran li'llāh*.**

**25. كَمَا قَالَ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ لِلْحَوَارِيِّينَ مَنْ أَنصَارِي إِلَى اللَّهِ ۖ قَالَ الْحَوَارِيُّونَ نَحْنُ أَنصَارُ اللَّهِ — *kamā qāla ʿĪsa'bnu Maryama li'l-ḥawāriyyīna man anṣārī ila'llāh · qāla'l-ḥawāriyyūna naḥnu anṣāru'llāh*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *ḥawāriyyūn* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**WHITENESS / PURITY — and RETURNING / conversing**" family — ***ḥawar* = intense WHITENESS** (⭐ ***ḥūr* (sing. *ḥawrāʾ*) = the maidens of Paradise** — with eyes of intense white and black!); ⭐⭐ ***ḥawāriyy* = a DISCIPLE / sincere close supporter** — ⭐ classically: **(a) "pure-hearted" (white, clean of hypocrisy); (b) they were WASHERS / BLEACHERS of clothes (*ḥawwarū* = to whiten cloth!); (c) they wore WHITE**; ⭐ ***al-Ḥawāriyy*** — the Prophet ﷺ: "*every prophet has a ḥawāriyy, and my ḥawāriyy is az-ZUBAYR*" (Bukhārī)!; ⭐⭐ same letters ***ḥ-w-r***: ***ḥiwār* = DIALOGUE / conversation** (⭐ ***muḥāwarah* = a DIALOGUE / debate**; ⭐ *ḥiwār* = an **INTERVIEW**!) — ⭐ from ***ḥāra* = to RETURN** (words going back and forth!); ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:14** — *innahu ẓanna an lan **yaḥūr*** (he thought he'd never RETURN)!.
- **Here it means:** "**…as ʿĪsā son of Maryam said to the disciples: 'Who are my helpers [on the way] to Allah?' The disciples said: 'We are Allah's helpers.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MODEL OF THE ANṢĀR — the DISCIPLES of ʿĪsā.** ⭐ **ʿĪsā: *MAN ANṢĀRĪ ila'llāh?*** — ⭐ **"Who are MY helpers [toward] Allah?"** ⭐ **The disciples: *NAḤNU ANṢĀRU'LLĀH*** — ⭐ **"WE are ALLAH's helpers!"** ⭐⭐⭐ **Notice the shift: ʿĪsā asks for "MY helpers" — they answer "ALLAH's helpers"** — ⭐ **they understood: helping the messenger IS helping Allah.** ⭐ **And v.14a: *kūnū anṣāra'llāh* — the believers told to give the SAME answer.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤAWĀRIYY* — WHITENESS / PURITY** — ⭐ **pure-hearted, or WASHERS who WHITENED clothes, or dressed in WHITE** (all classical) — ⭐ ***ḥūr* (Paradise maidens) — same root (intense white of the eye).** ⭐⭐ ***AZ-ZUBAYR* — "MY *ḥawāriyy*" (Bukhārī)** — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ gave az-Zubayr ibn al-ʿAwwām the title of ʿĪsā's disciples** — ⭐ **(and az-Zubayr was one of the three sent to intercept Ḥāṭib's letter — Mumtaḥanah 60:1!)** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤIWĀR* = DIALOGUE — same letters (*ḥ-w-r*)** — ⭐ **from *ḥāra* (to RETURN — words go back and forth)** — ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:14** — *ẓanna an lan **yaḥūr*** (he thought he'd never RETURN). ⭐ ***Muḥāwarah* = DEBATE; *ḥiwār* = INTERVIEW.**
  ⭐⭐ ***ILA'LLĀH* — "TOWARD Allah"** — ⭐ **"who will help me on the JOURNEY to Allah?"** — ⭐ **helping = walking together toward Him.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kamā*** = "**just as**." ⭐ ***man anṣārī*** = question. ⭐ ***ila'llāhi*** — ⭐ ***ilā*** = "**toward / [in the way] to**" (or = *maʿa*, "together with Allah's help"). ⭐ ***naḥnu anṣāru'llāhi*** = nominal (firm declaration).
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥawāriyy* — ⭐ **pure-hearted helpers — a title the Prophet ﷺ gave az-Zubayr.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ʿĪSĀ: "Who are MY helpers?" → DISCIPLES: "We are ALLAH's helpers!" — helping the messenger IS helping Allah.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤAWĀRIYY* ← *ḥawar* (WHITENESS): pure-hearted / cloth-WHITENERS / dressed in WHITE; *ḥūr* (Paradise maidens).** ⭐⭐⭐ **"My *ḥawāriyy* is AZ-ZUBAYR" (Bukhārī) — who intercepted Ḥāṭib's letter (60:1)!** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤIWĀR* = DIALOGUE (same letters) ← *ḥāra* (RETURN) — Inshiqāq 84:14 *lan yaḥūr*; *muḥāwarah* = DEBATE; *ḥiwār* = INTERVIEW.** ⭐ ***Ila'llāh* = TOWARD Allah.**

**26. فَآمَنَت طَّائِفَةٌ مِّن بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ وَكَفَرَت طَّائِفَةٌ — *fa-āmanat ṭāʾifatun min banī Isrāʾīla wa kafarat ṭāʾifah*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *ṭāʾifah* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**CIRCLING / going around**" family — *ṭāfa* = **to go AROUND, circle**; ⭐⭐ ***ṭawāf* = CIRCUMAMBULATION of the Kaʿbah**!; ⭐ ***ṭāʾifah* = a GROUP / faction / party** (people "gathered around" something!); ⭐ ***ṭāʾifiyyah* = SECTARIANISM**!; ⭐ ***aṭ-Ṭāʾif*** — the city (classically: its wall "went around" it); ⭐ ***ṭawāfa* — patrolling**; ⭐ ***ṭūfān* = a FLOOD / DELUGE** (⭐ water that SURROUNDS everything — **Nūḥ's flood** — 29:14!); ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** — *wa **ṭāʾifatun** mina'lladhīna maʿak*!; ⭐ **Qalam 68:19** — *fa-**ṭāfa** ʿalayhā **ṭāʾifun** min rabbik* (a calamity "went around" the garden at night)!.
- **Here it means:** "**Then a group of the Children of Israel believed, and a group disbelieved.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SPLIT — one people, two groups.** ⭐ ***Fa-āmanat ṬĀʾIFATUN… wa kafarat ṬĀʾIFAH*** — ⭐ **a perfect *ṭibāq* (BELIEVED / DISBELIEVED) with the same noun repeated** — ⭐ **the Children of Israel divided over ʿĪsā** — ⭐ **like Taghābun 64:2: *fa-minkum kāfirun wa minkum muʾmin*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṬĀʾIFAH* ← *ṬĀFA* (to CIRCLE)** — ⭐ **a group = people who "circle around" a leader or idea** — ⭐ ***ṬAWĀF* — circling the Kaʿbah!** ⭐ ***Ṭāʾifiyyah* = SECTARIANISM** (circling around factions instead of truth!); ⭐ ***ṬŪFĀN* = a FLOOD (water surrounding all — Nūḥ!)**; ⭐ **Qalam 68:19** — *fa-ṭāfa ʿalayhā ṭāʾifun* (a night calamity "circled" the garden) — ⭐ **the same root: circling in worship, in faction, in flood, in calamity.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-āmanat*** / ***wa kafarat*** — feminine verbs (for *ṭāʾifah*). ⭐ ***ṭāʾifatun*** ×2 — **indefinite** (a group — not all).
- **Why this exact word:** *ṭāʾifah* — ⭐ **the split of one people into believing and disbelieving circles.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ONE people, TWO groups: *āmanat ṭāʾifah… kafarat ṭāʾifah* — like Taghābun 64:2 (*kāfir / muʾmin*).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṬĀʾIFAH* ← *ṬĀFA* (CIRCLE): *ṬAWĀF* (around the Kaʿbah), *ṭāʾifiyyah* (SECTARIANISM), *ṬŪFĀN* (FLOOD — Nūḥ), *aṭ-Ṭāʾif* (the city); Qalam 68:19 (calamity circled the garden); Muzzammil 73:20.**

**27. فَأَيَّدْنَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا عَلَىٰ عَدُوِّهِمْ فَأَصْبَحُوا ظَاهِرِينَ — *fa-ayyadna'lladhīna āmanū ʿalā ʿaduwwihim fa-aṣbaḥū ẓāhirīn*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *ayyadnā* — ⭐⭐ the "**STRENGTH / SUPPORT**" family — ***ayd* = STRENGTH, power** (⭐ *wa's-samāʾa banaynāhā **bi-aydin*** — 51:47 — "We built the heaven with STRENGTH"!); ⭐ ***ayyada* (Form II) = to SUPPORT, strengthen**; ⭐ ***taʾyīd* = SUPPORT / ENDORSEMENT** (⭐ *taʾyīd* = **ENDORSEMENT** — political!); ⭐ ***muʾayyid* = a SUPPORTER** (⭐ *muʾayyidūn wa muʿāriḍūn* = **SUPPORTERS and OPPONENTS**!). *ʿaduwwihim* — ⭐⭐⭐ **their ENEMY** (⭐ the ***ʿaduww*** thread!). *aṣbaḥū* — ⭐ **they BECAME / woke up as** (*ṣubḥ* = MORNING; ⭐ **Qalam 68:17, 21** — *muṣbiḥīn*!). *ẓāhirīn* — ⭐⭐⭐ **DOMINANT / UPPERMOST / victorious** (*ẓahara* — ⭐ v.9 *li-yuẓhirahu*!).
- **Here it means:** "**So We supported those who believed against their enemy, and they became dominant.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SURAH'S LAST WORDS — the PROMISE of v.9 FULFILLED.** ⭐ **v.9** — *li-**YUẒHIRAHU** ʿala'd-dīni kullih* — ⭐ **to make it PREVAIL (promise).** ⭐ **v.14** — *fa-aṣbaḥū **ẒĀHIRĪN*** — ⭐ **they BECAME DOMINANT (fulfilment).** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *ẓ-h-r* root bookends the second half of the surah: promise → fulfilment.** ⭐ ***FA-AṢBAḤŪ*** — ⭐ **"they WOKE UP / BECAME [by morning]"** — ⭐ **like waking to a new dawn — the night of persecution over** (⭐ vs **Qalam 68:17–20**: the garden owners *muṣbiḥīn* — woke to RUIN!). ⭐⭐ **Classical: (a) the believers in ʿĪsā prevailed by PROOF; (b) his true followers ultimately prevailed in history; (c) or: with the coming of Muḥammad ﷺ, who confirmed ʿĪsā — the believers prevailed.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *ʿADUWW* THREAD — COMPLETE across FIVE surahs:** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4** — *humu'l-**ʿaduww** fa'ḥdharhum* (the enemy in the COMMUNITY). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:14** — *ʿaduwwan lakum fa'ḥdharūhum* (in the HOME). ⭐ **Mumtaḥanah 60:1** — *ʿaduwwī wa ʿaduwwakum* (at WAR outside). ⭐ **Ṣaff 61:14** — *fa-ayyadna'lladhīna āmanū ʿalā **ʿaduwwihim*** — ⭐⭐⭐ **SUPPORTED AGAINST their enemy — and VICTORIOUS.** ⭐ **The thread ends in victory.**
  ⭐⭐ ***AYYADNĀ* — *TAʾYĪD* = SUPPORT / ENDORSEMENT; *muʾayyid* = SUPPORTER** — ⭐ **51:47 — *bi-aydin* (built with STRENGTH).** ⭐ **The Anṣār helped Allah (v.14a) → Allah SUPPORTED them (v.14c)** — ⭐ **47:7 — *in tanṣuru'llāha yanṣurkum*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-ayyadnā*** = Form II past (majestic "We"). ⭐⭐ ***fa-aṣbaḥū ẓāhirīna*** — ***aṣbaḥa*** = "**became**" (a *kāna*-sister) + ***ẓāhirīna*** (*khabar*, accusative).
- **Why this exact word:** *ẓāhirīn* — ⭐ **the fulfilled promise — the same root as v.9's *li-yuẓhirahu*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA-AṢBAḤŪ ẒĀHIRĪN* = they BECAME DOMINANT — v.9 PROMISED *li-yuẓhirahu* → v.14 FULFILLED *ẓāhirīn*.** ⭐⭐ ***Aṣbaḥū* = WOKE UP AS — a new dawn (vs Qalam 68:17–20 — woke to RUIN).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿADUWW* COMPLETE: COMMUNITY (63:4), HOME (64:14), WAR (60:1), VICTORY OVER them (61:14).** ⭐⭐ ***TAʾYĪD* = SUPPORT / ENDORSEMENT; *muʾayyid* = SUPPORTER; 51:47 *bi-aydin*.** ⭐ **Helped Allah (v.14a) → SUPPORTED by Allah (v.14c) — 47:7.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **تُؤْمِنُونَ ... وَتُجَاهِدُونَ (v.11)** — ⚠️ **indicative in FORM, imperative in MEANING** — proven by jussive ***yaghfir*** (v.12); Ibn Masʿūd: ***āminū***.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **زَاغُوا (Form I) → أَزَاغَ (Form IV) (v.5)** — ⚠️ **their act → His consequence.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَحْمَدُ (v.6)** — ⚠️ **DIPTOTE**, and the **only** Qurʾānic use of the name.
4. ⭐⭐ **لِمَ (v.2, 5)** — ⚠️ ***li + mā*, alif dropped.**
5. ⭐⭐ **يُرِيدُونَ لِيُطْفِئُوا (v.8)** — ⚠️ ***lām*** after *yurīdu* (vs ***an*** in 9:32).
6. ⭐⭐ **مُتِمُّ نُورِهِ (v.8)** — ⚠️ **participle = settled fact**; variant ***mutimmun nūrahu***.
7. ⭐⭐ **الْكَافِرُونَ (v.8) / الْمُشْرِكُونَ (v.9)** — ⚠️ **not interchangeable** — each fits its verse.
8. ⭐⭐ **صَفًّا (v.4)** — ⚠️ **maṣdar as *ḥāl*** ("lined up").
9. ⭐⭐ **يَا قَوْمِ (Mūsā) vs يَا بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ (ʿĪsā)** — ⚠️ **ʿĪsā never says *yā qawmi*.**
10. ⭐⭐ **وَأُخْرَىٰ (v.13)** — ⚠️ ***ukhrā* (ANOTHER) ≠ *ākhirah* (HEREAFTER).**
11. ⭐⭐ **مَسَاكِنَ (v.12)** — ⚠️ **diptote.**
12. ⭐⭐ **طَيِّبَةً** — ⚠️ ***ṭ-y-b* (pure) ≠ *ṭ-b-b* (*ṭabīb*, doctor).**
13. ⭐⭐ **فَلَمَّا (v.5, 6)** — ⚠️ **+ past = "when"**, not "not yet."
14. ⭐ **سِحْرٌ مُّبِينٌ (v.6)** — ⚠️ **variant *sāḥirun*.**
15. ⭐ **تُنجِيكُم (v.10)** — ⚠️ **variant *tunajjīkum*.**
16. ⭐ **أَنصَارَ اللَّهِ (v.14)** — ⚠️ **variant *anṣāran li'llāh*.**
17. ⭐ **بَيْنَ يَدَيَّ (v.6)** — ⚠️ **idiom = BEFORE me** (in time).
