# Surah Az-Zalzalah (99) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–8)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**The earth becomes a witness — and the verb used for its testimony is the same one you were commanded to use about Allah's favour.**

⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:11** — *wa ammā bi-niʿmati rabbika **fa-ḥaddith*** — ⭐ **"as for your Lord's favour — **NARRATE IT**."**
⭐ **Zalzalah 99:4** — *yawmaʾidhin ***tuḥaddithu** akhbārahā* — ⭐ **"that Day it will **NARRATE its news**."**

⭐⭐ **SAME ROOT, SAME FORM II VERB.** ⭐ **You are told to narrate what was done *for* you. ⭐ **The earth will narrate what was done *on* it.**

⭐ **And think about what that means: ⭐ **the ground you're standing on right now is taking notes.** ⭐ **Every place you've ever prayed, every place you've ever sinned — ⭐ **the location itself is a witness with a voice.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **The earth is going to *talk*.** ⭐ **And the word for what it does is the root of ***ḥadīth*** — a narration.

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The shaking** (v.1–3) — ⭐ **the earth quakes, throws out its weights, and man asks "what's wrong with it?"**
2. **The testimony** (v.4–5) — ⭐ **it narrates its news, because your Lord inspired it.**
3. **The procession** (v.6) — ⭐ **people issue forth SCATTERED, to be shown their deeds.**
4. **The atom's weight** (v.7–8) — ⭐ **the most famous couplet in the juzʾ.**

## §1 — The Shaking (v.1–3)

**1. زُلْزِلَتِ ... زِلْزَالَهَا — *zulzilati… zilzālahā*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ a **REDUPLICATED root** (*z-l-z-l*) — the "**violent, repeated shaking**" family — *zalzala* = **to shake convulsively, again and again**; ⭐ ***zilzāl* = AN EARTHQUAKE*** (still the everyday Arabic word).
- **Here it means:** "**When the earth is shaken with its [own] quaking.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the doubled root is the earthquake, and you own this pattern from three surahs.**
  
  ⭐ **Arabic uses **reduplicated roots** for actions that are **repeated, quivering, or relentless**:**
  - ***waswasa*** — to whisper repeatedly (⭐ **Sūrat an-Nās**)
  - ***ʿasʿasa*** — the night creeping (⭐ **Takwīr 81:17**)
  - ***damdama*** — to crush utterly, blow on blow (⭐ **Shams 91:14**)
  - ⭐ ***zalzala*** — to shake convulsively (⭐ **here**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **Say it out loud: *zul-zi-lat… zil-zā-la-hā*.** ⭐ **The word IS the tremor.** ⭐ **You can hear the ground moving in the syllables.**
  
  ⭐ **And *zilzālahā* is a ⭐ **cognate accusative** (*maf'ūl muṭlaq*)** — ⭐ **the verb repeated as a noun for emphasis.** ⭐ **You own this from Nāziʿāt 79:1–4, ʿAbasa 80:25–26, Fajr 89:19–22, Ṭāriq 86:15–16.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **But here it has a possessive: ***zilzāla-HĀ*** — "**ITS quaking**."** ⭐ **Not just "a shaking" — ⭐ **the specific, final, appointed quake that belongs to it.** ⭐ **The earth has one earthquake with its name on it.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare Fajr 89:21** — *idhā **dukkati**'l-arḍu **dakkan dakkā*** ("when the earth is **pounded, pounding upon pounding**"). ⭐ **Two surahs, two doubled-emphasis destructions of the earth.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **passive verb** (*zulzilat*) + **cognate accusative with a possessive pronoun**. ⚠️ ⭐ **The passive means **no doer is named** — standard for cosmic events.**
- **Why this word:** the **reduplication** makes the verse ⭐ **onomatopoeic** — the sound performs the meaning.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Say it: *zul-zi-lat zil-zā-la-hā*. ⭐ **The word IS the earthquake.** ⭐⭐ **Same doubling as *waswasa*, *ʿasʿasa* (81:17), *damdama* (91:14).** ⭐ **And *zilzāla-**HĀ*** = "**ITS** quake" — the one with its name on it.**

**2. أَثْقَالَهَا — *athqālahā*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**heaviness / weight**" family — *thaqula* = to be heavy; ⭐ ***thiql* = a burden/weight***; ⭐ ***mīthqāl* = a unit of weight*** (⭐ **coming in v.7!**); ⭐ **and *ath-Thaqalān* = "the two weighty ones"** (jinn and mankind).
- **Here it means:** "**and the earth throws out its burdens.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the word is deliberately ambiguous, and both meanings land.**
  
  ⭐ **Classical readings of *athqāl*:** ⭐ **(a) **the dead** buried in it — the bodies it has been carrying; ⭐ **(b) **its treasures** — the gold, minerals, and hidden things in its depths.
  
  ⭐⭐ **Either way, the image is: ⭐ **the earth has been CARRYING something heavy for a very long time, and now it puts it down.**
  
  ⭐ **And that's the emotional logic of the surah: ⭐ **the earth is treated as a **burdened creature**.** ⭐ **v.2 it unloads. ⭐ **v.4 it speaks.** ⭐ **It's been holding weight and staying silent — and both end on the same day.**
  
  ⭐ **You own this root from Insān 76:27** — *wa yadharūna warāʾahum yawman **thaqīlā*** ("they leave behind them a **HEAVY** Day"). ⭐⭐ **Insān: a heavy DAY. Zalzalah: the earth's heavy LOAD.** ⭐ **Same root.**
  
  ⭐ **And it comes back in **v.7** as ***mithqāl*** — ⭐ **a weight so small it's the opposite of *athqāl*.** ⭐⭐ **The surah opens with the earth's GREATEST weights and closes with the SMALLEST weight imaginable.** ⭐ **Same root, both ends.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **broken plural of *thiql*; accusative object of *akhrajat*.** ⭐ **Note *akhrajat* (Form IV) = "brought OUT" — ⭐ **the same verb used in Nāziʿāt 79:31 (*akhraja minhā māʾahā*) and Aʿlā 87:4 (*akhraja'l-marʿā*) for the earth producing **life**.** ⭐⭐ **Same verb: the earth brings out pasture, and brings out its dead.**
- **Why this word:** *athqāl* frames the dead as ⭐ **a load the earth has been carrying**, which makes the release feel like relief.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Mīthqāl* = a unit of WEIGHT** (⭐ **coming in v.7!**). ⭐⭐ **The surah opens with the earth's HEAVIEST loads (*athqāl*) and closes with the LIGHTEST weight (*mithqāl dharrah*).** ⭐ **Same root, both extremes.** ⭐ **And Insān 76:27 had a "**heavy** Day."**

**3. مَا لَهَا — *mā lahā*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ **an idiom**: *mā la-* + pronoun = "**what's wrong with it? / what's the matter with it?**"
- **Here it means:** "**And man says, 'What is wrong with it?'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **three words, and they're doing something psychologically exact.**
  
  ⭐ **This is the ⭐ **only human speech in the entire surah** — ⭐ **and it's a **question about the earth**, not about himself.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Think about that.** ⭐ **The graves are opening, the ground is convulsing, and man's reaction is: ⭐ **"what's wrong with *it*?"** ⭐ **He's still treating himself as the observer and the earth as the problem.**
  
  ⭐ **And the surah answers by ⭐ **letting the earth speak instead of him** (v.4).** ⭐ **He asks a question about the earth; the earth gives testimony about *him*.** ⭐⭐ **The roles invert in a single verse.**
  
  ⭐ **Note also the word for man is ***al-insān*** — ⭐ **the same generic term used in Qiyāmah 75:3, 75:13, Nabaʾ 78:40 (*al-marʾ*), ʿAbasa 80:17, Infiṭār 82:6, ʿAlaq 96:6.** ⭐ **Always the same: the camera narrows to one unnamed human.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mā*** here is interrogative; ***lahā*** = *li-* + *hā*. ⭐ **A very common Arabic idiom for "what's up with…?"**
- **Why this word:** the **idiom's casualness** is the point — ⭐ **it sounds almost like confusion rather than terror**, which makes it worse.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The ONLY human speech in the surah is ⭐ **a question about the EARTH**, not about himself.** ⭐ **"What's wrong with *it*?"** ⭐⭐ **And the earth answers by testifying about *him*.** ⭐ **The roles invert in one verse.**

## §2 — The Testimony (v.4–5)

**4. تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا — *tuḥaddithu akhbārahā*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *tuḥaddithu* — ⭐ **Form II of the "newness / relating" root** — *ḥadatha* = to happen, to be new; ⭐ ***ḥadīth* = (a) a NARRATION and (b) NEW***; ⭐ **Form II *ḥaddatha* = TO NARRATE, to tell about.** *akhbār* — ⭐ **plural of *khabar* = news, a report** (⭐ **modern *akhbār* = THE NEWS on TV!**); ⭐ **and *khabīr* = one deeply informed** (⭐ **coming in ʿĀdiyāt 100:11!**).
- **Here it means:** "**That Day it will narrate its news.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BEST CROSS-SURAH LINK IN THIS SURAH — and it's a direct command answered by the earth.**
  
  ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:11 — *wa ammā bi-niʿmati rabbika **FA-ḤADDITH*** — ⭐ **"as for your Lord's favour — **NARRATE IT**."**
  ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:4 — *yawmaʾidhin ***TUḤADDITHU** akhbārahā* — ⭐ **"that Day it will **NARRATE** its news."**
  
  ⭐⭐ **SAME ROOT, SAME FORM II.** ⭐ **You were commanded to narrate the favour. ⭐ **The earth will narrate the record.** ⭐ **Two narrations, one verb.**
  
  ⭐ **And the *khabar* / *nabaʾ* distinction is worth knowing:**
  - ⭐ ***nabaʾ*** (⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:2**) = ⭐ **MOMENTOUS, weighty news** — the kind that changes everything
  - ⭐ ***khabar*** (here) = ⭐ **an ordinary report** — the everyday word for news
  
  ⭐⭐ **And that's devastating.** ⭐ **The earth doesn't deliver a **grand announcement** — ⭐ **it just gives its **report**, plainly, like a witness reciting facts.** ⭐ **No drama. Just: here's what happened here.**
  
  ⭐ **And *ḥadīth* also means NEW** — ⭐ **so the earth's testimony arrives **fresh**, as if it all happened yesterday.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form II present tense.** ⚠️ ⭐ **Note *akhbārahā* is a **broken plural** of *khabar*** — ⭐ **plural, because there are many reports, not one.**
- **Why this word:** *tuḥaddithu* (**narrate**) rather than *tashhadu* (testify) — ⭐ **narration is *telling the story*, which is more complete than answering charges.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:11: ***YOU* narrate the favour** (*fa-ḥaddith*). ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:4: **the EARTH narrates the record** (*tuḥaddithu*).** ⭐ **Same root, same form.** ⭐⭐ **And *akhbār* = THE NEWS** (⚠️ **ordinary *khabar*, not weighty *nabaʾ*** — Nabaʾ 78:2). ⭐ **A plain report, which is worse.**

**5. أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا — *awḥā lahā*** · v.5
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**swift, subtle communication**" family — *waḥā* = **to signal quickly and secretly**; ⭐ ***waḥy* = REVELATION*** — ⭐ **the word used for what comes to prophets!**; the root carries **speed and hiddenness** (a message passed so fast no one else catches it).
- **Here it means:** "**because your Lord inspired / commanded it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the astonishing thing here is *which word* is used.**
  
  ⭐ ***Waḥy*** is ⭐ **the technical term for REVELATION to prophets.** ⭐ **And here it's used for ⭐ **Allah communicating with the EARTH**.
  
  ⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān uses the prophetic-revelation word for the ground.** ⭐ **Which tells you the earth's testimony isn't guesswork or memory — ⭐ **it's **commanded, directed, and authorized** from above.**
  
  ⭐ **And the root's core sense — ⭐ **swift, subtle signalling** — ⭐ **explains why you can't see it happening.** ⭐ **The earth is receiving instructions right now, imperceptibly.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note the preposition: *awḥā **LAHĀ*** (with *li-*), not *awḥā ilayhā*.** ⭐ **Classical grammarians note this shade: *li-* can carry a sense of **command/permission given TO** something, slightly different from *ilā* (**to** someone as a recipient).** ⭐ **Both are used in the Qurʾān.**
  
  ⭐ **And the *bi-anna* ("because") makes v.5 ⭐ **the *reason* for v.4**: ⭐ **the earth speaks **because** it was told to.** ⭐ **Not because it's angry, not because it's alive in a pagan sense — ⭐ **because it was given the order.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV** (*awḥā*), weak-final. ⭐ ***Bi-anna*** = "**because**," giving the cause of the narration.
- **Why this word:** *awḥā* (**the revelation word**) rather than *amara* (commanded) — ⭐ **it dignifies the earth's testimony as **divinely authorized speech**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAḤY* = REVELATION — the word used for PROPHETS.** ⭐⭐ **And here it's used for **the EARTH**.** ⭐ **The ground receives instructions.** ⭐ **The root means **swift, subtle signalling** — which is why you can't see it.**

## §3 — The Procession (v.6)

**6. يَصْدُرُ النَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا — *yaṣduru'n-nāsu ashtātā*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *yaṣduru* — ⭐ the "**issuing forth / proceeding out**" family — *ṣadara* = **to come out, to proceed from**; ⭐ ***ṣadr* = the CHEST / the FRONT of something*** (⭐ **you own it from Sharḥ 94:1**, *nashraḥ laka **ṣadrak***); ⭐ **and *maṣdar* = A SOURCE** (⭐ **also the grammar term for a verbal noun — "the thing everything issues from"!**). *ashtāt* — ⭐ the "**scattering / dispersal**" family — ⭐ **the plural of *shatt*.**
- **Here it means:** "**That Day people will issue forth, scattered in groups, to be shown their deeds.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **two words, two payoffs — and one of them closes a thread from Layl.**
  
  ⭐ **FIRST — *ashtātā*.** ⭐ **You own this root from Layl 92:4** — *inna **saʿyakum la-SHATTĀ*** ("indeed, **your efforts are SCATTERED** in different directions").
  
  ⭐⭐ **Layl 92:4 said your **strivings** go in scattered directions.** ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:6 says on that Day **you** come out scattered.** ⭐⭐ **You scatter in life; you scatter at the exit.** ⭐ **Same root, and the second is the consequence of the first.**
  
  ⭐ **SECOND — *yaṣduru*.** ⭐ **The root of *ṣadr* (chest) and ***maṣdar* (a SOURCE)**.** ⭐ **The image is of people **issuing from a source**, like water from a spring — ⭐ **coming out in streams, not marching in formation.**
  
  ⭐ **And *maṣdar* is your free grammar hook:** ⭐ **you already know it as the term for a **verbal noun** — "the source from which verbs derive."**
  
  ⭐ **And note ***li-yurau aʿmālahum*** — ⭐ **"to BE SHOWN their deeds" — PASSIVE.** ⭐ **They don't look; they are **shown**.** ⭐ **No choosing what to review.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ashtātā*** is an accusative *ḥāl* ("in a state of being scattered"). ⚠️ ⭐ ***li-yurau*** is a **passive subjunctive** after the *lām* of purpose — ⭐ **note the dropped nūn.**
- **Why this word:** *ashtāt* (**scattered**) rather than a word for ordered ranks — ⭐ **because the surah is about **individual** accounting, not collective.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Layl 92:4: your **EFFORTS** are *shattā* (scattered). ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:6: **YOU** come out *ashtātā* (scattered).** ⭐ **Same root — the second follows from the first.** ⭐⭐ **And *maṣdar* = A SOURCE** (the grammar term!). ⭐ **People **issue forth** like water from a spring.** ⭐ **And they are **SHOWN** their deeds — passive.**

## §4 — The Atom's Weight (v.7–8)

**7. مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ — *mithqāla dharrah*** · v.7, 8
- **Root feelings:** *mithqāl* — ⭐ **a UNIT OF WEIGHT** — ⭐ **the same root as *athqāl* in v.2!** (*thaqula* = to be heavy). *dharrah* — ⭐ **the tiniest possible particle** — classically described as ⭐ **a speck of dust visible in a shaft of sunlight**, or **the smallest ant**; ⭐ **modern Arabic: *dharrah* = AN ATOM**, and ***dharrī* = ATOMIC*** (⭐ *al-qunbulah adh-dharriyyah* = the atomic bomb!).
- **Here it means:** "**the weight of an atom / a mote.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the surah's masterstroke — and it's an internal root-echo across six verses.**
  
  ⭐ **v.2 — the earth throws out its ***ATHQĀL*** — ⭐ **its GREATEST weights** (the dead, the treasures, everything it has carried).
  ⭐ **v.7 — ***MITHQĀLA dharrah*** — ⭐ **the SMALLEST conceivable weight.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **SAME ROOT, opposite ends of the scale.** ⭐ **The surah opens by unloading mountains of weight and closes by weighing a speck of dust.** ⭐ **And the message is: **both are measured.**
  
  ⭐ **And ***dharrah* = AN ATOM** in modern Arabic** — ⭐ **a free, unforgettable hook.** ⭐ **Classical scholars described it as the dust you see floating in a sunbeam, which is the same idea: ⭐ **the smallest thing your eye can register.**
  
  ⭐ **The genius of the choice: ⭐ **a *dharrah* is not "small" — it's **the threshold of visibility**.** ⭐ **Below that, you literally cannot see it.** ⭐⭐ **So the verse says: **even what you couldn't see yourself doing is on the scale.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mithqāla*** is accusative (object of *yaʿmal*); ***dharrah*** genitive by *iḍāfah*. ⭐ **The *mifʿāl* pattern here names an **instrument/unit of measure**.**
- **Why this word:** *dharrah* is ⭐ **the smallest unit the audience could picture**, which makes the claim maximally comprehensive.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Dharrah* = AN ATOM** in modern Arabic (*al-qunbulah adh-**dharriyyah***). ⭐ **Classically: the dust in a sunbeam.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And v.2's *ATHQĀL* (greatest weights) vs v.7's *MITHQĀL* (smallest weight) — ⭐ **SAME ROOT, both ends of the scale.** ⭐ **Both get measured.**

**8. خَيْرًا يَرَهُ / شَرًّا يَرَهُ — *khayran yarah / sharran yarah*** · v.7, 8
- **Root feelings:** *khayr* / *sharr* — ⭐ **good / evil** — ⚠️ **both IRREGULAR** (serving as noun and comparative). *yarah* — ⭐ **"he will SEE it"** — from *raʾā* (to see).
- **Here it means:** "**So whoever does an atom's weight of good will SEE it. And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will SEE it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the most perfectly balanced couplet in the juzʾ — and the verb choice is everything.**
  
  ⭐ **Look at the symmetry:**
  
  | v.7 | v.8 |
  |---|---|
  | *fa-**man** yaʿmal mithqāla dharratin **KHAYRAN** yarah* | *wa **man** yaʿmal mithqāla dharratin **SHARRAN** yarah* |
  
  ⭐⭐ **IDENTICAL word for word — except *khayran* / *sharran*.** ⭐ **The same technique as Layl 92:7/92:10** (*yusrā* / *ʿusrā*) — ⭐ **two verses that differ by one word.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the verb: ***YARAH* — "he will SEE it."*** ⭐ **Not "he will be told," not "he will be judged," not "he will be repaid."** ⭐ ***SEE it.*** ⭐ **With his own eyes.**
  
  ⭐ **And that closes a thread running through FOUR surahs:**
  - **Balad 90:7** — *a-yaḥsabu an **lam yarahu** aḥad* (⭐ **"does he think NO ONE SAW him?"**)
  - **ʿAlaq 96:14** — *a-lam yaʿlam bi-anna'llāha **YARĀ*** (⭐ **"does he not know that Allah SEES?"**)
  - **Zalzalah 99:6** — *li-**yurau** aʿmālahum* (⭐ **"to BE SHOWN their deeds"**)
  - ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:7–8** — ***YARAH*** (⭐ **"he will SEE it"**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **He thought no one saw (Balad). Allah sees (ʿAlaq). He is shown (99:6). And he sees it himself (99:7–8).** ⭐ **Four surahs, one closing argument.**
  
  ⭐ **And note *yarah* is **jussive with a short vowel** — ⚠️ **read carefully; the pronoun *-hu* becomes *-h*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** = "whoever" (conditional), so *yaʿmal* and *yarah* are both **jussive** — ⚠️ ⭐ **note *yarah* has lost its final alif (from *yarā*).** ⭐ **A conditional sentence: *man* + jussive + jussive.**
- **Why this word:** *yarah* (**SEE**) rather than *yujzā* (be repaid) — ⭐ **because **seeing your own deed** is a more total accountability than being told about it.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.7 and v.8 are IDENTICAL except *khayran* / *sharran*.** ⭐ **Same technique as Layl 92:7/92:10.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the verb is ***YARAH* — "he will SEE it"** — not be told, not be judged. ⭐ **FOUR surahs: Balad 90:7 ("no one saw me"), ʿAlaq 96:14 ("Allah SEES"), Zalzalah 99:6 (**shown**), 99:7–8 (**he sees it**).**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَثْقَالَهَا (v.2) and مِثْقَالَ (v.7)** — ⚠️ **SAME ROOT, six verses apart.** ⭐ **The earth's **heaviest** loads and the **lightest** conceivable weight.** **Both measured.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **تُحَدِّثُ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **the same Form II verb as Ḍuḥā 93:11's *fa-ḥaddith*.** ⭐ **You narrate the favour; the earth narrates the record.**
3. ⭐⭐ **أَخْبَارَهَا (v.4)** — ⚠️ **ordinary *khabar* (a report), NOT *nabaʾ* (momentous news, Nabaʾ 78:2).** ⭐ **The plainness is the point.**
4. ⭐⭐ **أَوْحَىٰ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **this is the **PROPHETIC REVELATION** word (*waḥy*), used here for **the earth**.** ⭐ **Don't downgrade it to "told."**
5. ⭐⭐ **أَشْتَاتًا (v.6)** — ⚠️ **same root as *shattā* in Layl 92:4.** ⭐ **Efforts scattered in life → people scattered at the exit.**
6. ⭐⭐ **يَرَهُ (v.7–8)** — ⚠️ **jussive: the final alif of *yarā* is DROPPED**, and the pronoun becomes *-h*. ⭐ **Read carefully.**
7. ⭐ **v.7 and v.8 are word-for-word identical except *khayran*/*sharran*** — ⚠️ **read them as a matched pair**, like Layl 92:7/92:10.
8. ⭐ **زُلْزِلَتِ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **PASSIVE** ("**it is shaken**"), not active. ⭐ **No doer named — standard for cosmic events.**
9. ⭐ **لِّيُرَوْا (v.6)** — ⚠️ **PASSIVE subjunctive**: "to **BE SHOWN**," not "to see." ⭐ **They don't choose what to review.**
10. ⭐ **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا (v.5)** — ⚠️ **note the preposition *li-*, not *ilā*.** ⭐ **A recognized shade: command/permission given TO it.**
