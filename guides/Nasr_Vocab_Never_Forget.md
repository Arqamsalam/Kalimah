# Surah An-Naṣr (110) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–3)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It's a victory announcement — and its only instruction is to ask for FORGIVENESS.**

⭐ **v.1–2:** ⭐ **the help of Allah comes, Makkah opens, people enter the religion in crowds** — ⭐ **the greatest triumph imaginable.**
⭐ **v.3:** *fa-sabbiḥ bi-ḥamdi rabbika **wa'staghfirh*** — ⭐ "**So glorify with the praise of your Lord, and SEEK HIS FORGIVENESS.**"

⭐⭐ **No celebration. No victory parade. No "you did it."** ⭐ **The response to total success is: ⭐ **glorify Him, praise Him, ask forgiveness.**

⭐⭐⭐ **And the companions read something deeper in it.** ⭐ **When ʿUmar (رضي الله عنه) asked the senior companions about this surah, most said it commands praise at victory. ⭐ **Ibn ʿAbbās (رضي الله عنهما), still young, said: ⭐ *"It is the announcement of the Messenger's ﷺ approaching death"* — ⭐ **his mission complete.** ⭐ ʿUmar said: *"I know of it only what you know."*** ⭐ **It is reported to be among the last complete surahs revealed.**

⭐ **And ʿĀʾishah (رضي الله عنها) reported that after it, the Prophet ﷺ would often say in his rukūʿ and sujūd: ⭐ ***"Subḥānaka'llāhumma rabbanā wa bi-ḥamdik, Allāhumma'ghfir lī"*** — ⭐ **"carrying out [the command of] the Qurʾān."**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **The work is DONE → so the worker prepares to go home.** ⭐ **Victory isn't the end of the story; it's the signal that the story is ending.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The arrival** (v.1) — ⭐ **when Allah's help comes, and the Opening.**
2. **The sight** (v.2) — ⭐ **people entering Allah's religion in crowds.**
3. **The response** (v.3) — ⭐ **glorify, praise, seek forgiveness — He is ever Relenting.**

---

## §1 — The Arrival (v.1)

**1. نَصْرُ اللَّهِ — *naṣru'llāh*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**HELP / aid that brings victory**" family — *naṣara* = **to help someone against an opponent**; ⭐ ***nāṣir* = a helper/supporter***; ⭐⭐ ***al-Anṣār* = THE HELPERS of Madīnah*** (who gave the Prophet ﷺ refuge!); ⭐ ***intiṣār* = victory/triumph.***
- **Here it means:** "**When the help of Allah comes…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the possessive is the theology — and the root closes a thread from Ṭāriq.**
  
  ⭐ **It's ***naṣru'LLĀH*** — "**the help OF ALLAH**."** ⭐ **Not "your victory," not "the Muslims' victory."** ⭐⭐ **The triumph is attributed to Him in the first two words.** ⭐ **Which is exactly why v.3 commands humility, not pride: ⭐ **you didn't win it.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And you own the root from Ṭāriq 86:10** — *fa-mā lahu min quwwatin **wa lā NĀṢIR*** — ⭐ "**he will have no strength and NO HELPER**" — ⭐ **describing the denier on the Day of Judgment.** ⭐⭐ **Ṭāriq: the denier has NO *nāṣir*. Naṣr: the believers receive *naṣr Allāh*.** ⭐ **Same root, opposite fates.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And *al-Anṣār* is your free hook: ⭐ **the people of Madīnah who HELPED** — ⭐ **the human *naṣr* that preceded the divine one.** ⭐ **They gave refuge in hardship; Allah gave victory in the end.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare Muṭaffifīn/Burūj-style scenes of persecution** — ⭐ **Burūj 85 told of believers burned in a ditch with no visible help.** ⭐ **Naṣr tells of help arriving.** ⭐ **The arc of the juzʾ bends here.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***idhā* + past (*jāʾa*) = a certain future** ("**when** it comes" — guaranteed). ⭐ ***Naṣru'llāh*** = *iḍāfah* (attribution).
- **Why this word:** *naṣr* (**help that wins**) rather than *fawz* (success) — ⭐ **because *naṣr* always implies **someone else** doing the helping.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-ANṢĀR* = THE HELPERS of Madīnah.** ⭐ **Same root.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:10: the denier has NO *nāṣir*. ⭐ **Naṣr 110:1: *naṣr ALLĀH* arrives.** ⭐⭐ **"The help OF ALLAH" — not yours — which is why v.3 commands humility.**

**2. وَالْفَتْحُ — *wa'l-fatḥ*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**OPENING**" family — *fataḥa* = **to open**; ⭐⭐ ***miftāḥ* = A KEY***; ⭐⭐ ***al-Fātiḥah* = THE OPENER*** (the first surah!); ⭐ ***fatḥ* = an opening AND a conquest*** (⭐ **a city "opened"**); ⭐ ***al-Fattāḥ* = a NAME OF ALLAH** (The Opener, The Judge).
- **Here it means:** "**…and the Opening [the Conquest of Makkah].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **in Arabic, a city isn't "conquered" — it's "OPENED."**
  
  ⭐ ***Fatḥ Makkah* = "the OPENING of Makkah"*** — ⭐ **the standard term for its conquest (8 AH).** ⭐⭐ **And the word choice is perfect for what happened: ⭐ **the city was entered almost without bloodshed; the Prophet ﷺ declared a general amnesty — ⭐ **"go, you are free."** ⭐ **Less a conquest than an **unlocking**.
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the root gives you two hooks you use daily:**
  - ⭐ ***MIFTĀḤ* = A KEY*** — ⭐ **the thing that opens.**
  - ⭐ ***AL-FĀTIḤAH* = THE OPENER*** — ⭐ **the surah that opens the Qurʾān and every rakʿah.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **So the Qurʾān OPENS with *al-Fātiḥah* and, near its end, announces *al-Fatḥ*.** ⭐ **The book that begins with "the Opening" ends its revelation with "the Opening."**
  
  ⭐ **And note the DEFINITE article: *AL-fatḥ*** — ⭐ **"**THE** Opening"** — ⭐ **a specific, known, long-awaited one.**
  
  ⭐ **Classical note: ⭐ *fatḥ* also means a JUDGMENT/decision* (*al-Fattāḥ* = the Judge who "opens" a case) — ⭐ **the Opening of Makkah was a verdict between the two sides.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***al-fatḥ*** joined to *naṣr* by *wa* — ⭐ **both subjects of *jāʾa*.** ⭐ **The help comes FIRST, the opening SECOND — cause, then effect.**
- **Why this word:** *fatḥ* (**opening**) rather than *ghalabah* (overpowering) — ⭐ **it describes the conquest as an **unlocking**, which matches its mercy.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIFTĀḤ* = A KEY. *AL-FĀTIḤAH* = THE OPENER.** ⭐ **Same root.** ⭐⭐⭐ **In Arabic a city isn't "conquered" — it's "OPENED."** ⭐ **And Makkah was opened with an amnesty.** ⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān OPENS with *al-Fātiḥah* and announces *al-Fatḥ* near the end.**

## §2 — The Sight (v.2)

**3. يَدْخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ اللَّهِ — *yadkhulūna fī dīni'llāh*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *yadkhulūna* — ⭐ **ENTERING** (*dakhala* = to enter; *madkhal* = an entrance). *dīn* — ⭐ **here: RELIGION** — from the **obligation** root.
- **Here it means:** "**and you see the people entering into the religion of Allah…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the BEST consecutive-surah link in this batch — the previous surah set it up.**
  
  ⭐ **Kāfirūn 109:6 — *lakum **dīnukum** wa liya dīn*** — ⭐ "**to you YOUR religion, and to me mine**" — ⭐ **total separation, the door closed.**
  ⭐ **Naṣr 110:2 — *yadkhulūna fī **dīni'llāh** afwājā*** — ⭐ "**entering into ALLAH'S religion in crowds**."
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Back to back.** ⭐ **Kāfirūn: "YOUR religion" (*dīnukum*) — theirs, separate. ⭐ **Naṣr: "ALLAH'S religion" (*dīnu'llāh*) — and they are walking INTO it.** ⭐ **The door Kāfirūn closed to compromise, Naṣr shows opening to sincerity.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the *dīn* sense-switch is now complete across three surahs:**
  - ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:1** — *bi'd-**dīn*** = ⭐ **the RECKONING**
  - ⭐ **Kāfirūn 109:6** — ***dīnukum*** = ⭐ **YOUR religion**
  - ⭐ **Naṣr 110:2** — ***dīni'llāh*** = ⭐ **ALLAH'S religion**
  
  ⭐ **And note the verb: *yadkhulūna fī* — "entering INTO."** ⭐ **Religion described as **a space you walk into** — a house with a door.** ⭐ **Compare Fajr 89:29–30** — *fa'**dkhulī** fī ʿibādī · wa'**dkhulī** jannatī* — ⭐ **"ENTER among My servants, ENTER My Garden."** ⭐ **Enter the religion in dunya → enter the Garden in the Hereafter. Same verb.**
  
  ⭐ ***Wa raʾayta*** ("and you SEE") — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ is promised he'll **witness** it with his own eyes.** ⭐ **The seeing thread (90:7, 96:14, 99:7, 102:6) ends on joy.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***raʾayta*** + *an-nāsa* (object) + *yadkhulūna* (a *ḥāl*, describing them: "**while** entering"). ⭐ ***Fī dīni'llāh*** = *iḍāfah*.
- **Why this word:** *dīnu'llāh* — ⭐ **attributing the religion to Allah**, right after Kāfirūn's "your religion / my religion."
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Kāfirūn 109:6: "YOUR religion" (separate). ⭐ **Naṣr 110:2: "ALLAH'S religion" — they ENTER it.** ⭐ **Back-to-back surahs.** ⭐⭐ ***Dīn*: Reckoning (107:1) → your religion (109:6) → Allah's religion (110:2).** ⭐ **And *yadkhulūna* → Fajr 89:29's *udkhulī* — enter the religion, then the Garden.**

**4. أَفْوَاجًا — *afwājā*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ ***fawj*** = **a TROOP, a throng, a wave of people moving together** — ⭐ ***afwāj* = throngs upon throngs** (⭐ **modern: *fawj* = a regiment/battalion!**).
- **Here it means:** "**…in crowds / in multitudes.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **you own this word EXACTLY from Nabaʾ 78:18 — and the contrast is one of the most beautiful in the juzʾ.**
  
  ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:18 — *yawma yunfakhu fi'ṣ-ṣūri fa-taʾtūna **AFWĀJĀ*** — ⭐ "**the Day the Trumpet is blown, and you come forth in CROWDS**" — ⭐ **to JUDGMENT.**
  ⭐ **Naṣr 110:2 — *yadkhulūna fī dīni'llāhi **AFWĀJĀ*** — ⭐ "**entering Allah's religion in CROWDS**."
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **IDENTICAL WORD, same position (verse-ending), same image of streaming multitudes.** ⭐ **Nabaʾ: crowds **summoned** to Judgment. ⭐ **Naṣr: crowds **choosing** to enter faith.** ⭐⭐ **The same picture of humanity in waves — ⭐ **once in terror, once in faith.** ⭐ **And the first group that enters in crowds now won't need to fear the crowds of that Day.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And historically exact:** ⭐ **after the Opening of Makkah, delegations came from tribes across Arabia — ⭐ **the year 9 AH was called *ʿĀm al-Wufūd*, "the Year of Delegations."** ⭐ **Whole tribes at once, *afwājan*.**
  
  ⭐ **Free hook: ⭐ ***fawj* = a REGIMENT*** in modern Arabic.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***afwājan*** — accusative *ḥāl* ("**in a state of** crowds"). ⭐ **Broken plural of *fawj*.**
- **Why this word:** *afwāj* (**crowds of crowds**) — ⭐ **after years of individuals converting one by one in secret, now whole tribes at once.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **IDENTICAL to Nabaʾ 78:18 — *afwājā*.** ⭐ **Nabaʾ: crowds SUMMONED to Judgment. ⭐ **Naṣr: crowds ENTERING faith.** ⭐ **Same image — terror vs. faith.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿĀm al-Wufūd* = the Year of Delegations** (9 AH). ⭐ ***Fawj* = a REGIMENT.**

## §3 — The Response (v.3)

**5. فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ — *fa-sabbiḥ bi-ḥamdi rabbika*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *sabbiḥ* — ⭐ the "**swimming / gliding**" family → ***tasbīḥ*** = **declaring Allah FAR ABOVE every flaw** (⭐ **moving swiftly away from any imperfection**). *ḥamd* — ⭐ **PRAISE / grateful acknowledgement** (⭐ ***al-ḥamdu lillāh*; *Muḥammad*, *Aḥmad* — "the praised one"!**).
- **Here it means:** "**Then glorify with the praise of your Lord…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **two words you say every day — joined in a formula you also say every day.**
  
  ⭐ ***Sabbiḥ bi-ḥamdi rabbik*** = ⭐ **the root of *subḥāna'llāhi wa bi-ḥamdih*** — ⭐ **"Glory be to Allah, and with His praise."** ⭐ **You say it in rukūʿ (*subḥāna rabbiya'l-ʿaẓīm wa bi-ḥamdih*), in morning and evening adhkār.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the two words do different jobs:**
  - ⭐ ***TASBĪḤ*** = ⭐ **NEGATION — declaring Him free of every flaw** ("He is not lacking, not weak, not in need")
  - ⭐ ***ḤAMD*** = ⭐ **AFFIRMATION — declaring every perfection His** ("all good, all praise belongs to Him")
  
  ⭐⭐ **Together: ⭐ **remove every defect, affirm every perfection.** ⭐ **The complete description in two words.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And at the moment of VICTORY, why *tasbīḥ*?** ⭐ **Because the danger of victory is thinking ⭐ **you** did it.** ⭐ ***Tasbīḥ* clears that thought: ⭐ **He alone is perfect; your role was always lacking.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **You own *sabbiḥ* EXACTLY from Aʿlā 87:1** — ***sabbiḥi**'sma rabbika'l-aʿlā* ⭐ ("**GLORIFY** the name of your Lord, the Most High"). ⭐ **Same imperative.** ⭐ **Aʿlā commanded it at the start of the journey; ⭐ **Naṣr commands it at the end.**
  
  ⭐ **And the *s-b-ḥ* root's origin: ⭐ **swimming** — you own it from **Nāziʿāt 79:3** (*as-sābiḥāt*, those who **glide**).**
  
  ⭐ ***Ḥamd* → *MUḤAMMAD*** — ⭐ **"the much-praised one."** ⭐ **The command to praise is given to the man whose name means "praised."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form II imperative** (*sabbiḥ*). ⭐ ***Bi-ḥamdi*** — the *bi-* = "**with / accompanied by**" praise. ⭐ **The *fa-* = "SO / then" — the response to v.1–2.**
- **Why this word:** *tasbīḥ* + *ḥamd* — ⭐ **the formula that most directly cancels self-congratulation.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SUBḤĀNA'LLĀHI WA BI-ḤAMDIH*** — ⭐ **the formula you say daily, commanded here.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Tasbīḥ* = NO flaw; *ḥamd* = ALL perfection.** ⭐⭐ **Aʿlā 87:1: *sabbiḥ* at the START of the journey. ⭐ **Naṣr 110:3: *sabbiḥ* at the END.** ⭐ ***Ḥamd* → MUḤAMMAD = "the praised one."**

**6. وَاسْتَغْفِرْهُ — *wa'staghfirh*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**COVERING / shielding**" family — *ghafara* = **to cover over, to shield**; ⭐⭐⭐ ***mighfar* = A HELMET*** (⭐ **the armour that covers and protects the head!**); ⭐ ***maghfirah* = FORGIVENESS*** (⭐ **sin covered over, and you shielded from its consequence**); ⭐ ***al-Ghafūr* = a NAME OF ALLAH.** ⭐ **Form X *istaghfara* = to SEEK covering / seek forgiveness.**
- **Here it means:** "**…and seek His forgiveness.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the most surprising command in the surah — and Form X tells the whole story.**
  
  ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ has just been given total victory.** ⭐ **The command isn't "rejoice" — ⭐ **it's "SEEK FORGIVENESS."** ⭐ **The most honoured human, at his moment of greatest success.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Why?** ⭐ **Classical answers: ⭐ **(a) humility at victory, so no trace of pride remains; ⭐ (b) completion of a lifetime's mission — every great task ends in *istighfār*, as every prayer ends in *istighfār* (*astaghfiru'llāh* ×3 after ṣalāh!); ⭐ (c) preparation to meet his Lord** (Ibn ʿAbbās's reading).** ⭐ **Note (b): ⭐ ***istighfār* closes the ṣalāh, closes the Hajj (2:199), closes the night (51:18) — and here, closes a mission.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the *mighfar* (HELMET) image is unforgettable:** ⭐ ***maghfirah* isn't just "erasing" sin — it's ⭐ **covering you so its consequences can't strike.** ⭐ **Like a helmet.** ⭐ **And notice: he'd just come out of years of battle, wearing an actual *mighfar*.** ⭐ **Now: seek the *maghfirah* that protects in a different way.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE FORM X CONTRAST — the best grammar payoff in your collection:**
  - ⭐ ***ISTAGHNĀ*** (Form X) — ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:5, Layl 92:8, ʿAlaq 96:7** — ⭐ **"to consider oneself NEEDING NOTHING"** — ⭐ **the root of all transgression.**
  - ⭐ ***ISTAGHFIR*** (Form X) — ⭐ **Naṣr 110:3** — ⭐ **"SEEK covering"** — ⭐ **admitting you NEED.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME FORM. Opposite souls.** ⭐ **The *mustaghnī* says "I need nothing." ⭐ **The *mustaghfir* says "I need forgiveness."** ⭐ **The surah of victory ends on the posture of need — ⭐ **the exact cure for *istighnāʾ*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form X imperative** (*istaghfir*) + *-hu* (Him). ⭐⭐ **Form X = SEEKING** (*istaghfara* = seek forgiveness; *istaʿāna* = seek help) **or CONSIDERING** (*istaghnā* = consider oneself rich).
- **Why this word:** *istighfār* at the moment of victory — ⭐ **the clearest possible signal that success belongs to Allah.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIGHFAR* = A HELMET.** ⭐ ***Maghfirah* = being COVERED so the consequence can't strike.** ⭐⭐⭐ **FORM X: *ISTAGHNĀ* (80:5, 92:8, 96:7) = "I NEED NOTHING." ⭐ *ISTAGHFIR* (110:3) = "I NEED FORGIVENESS."** ⭐ **Same form, opposite souls.** ⭐⭐ **And *istighfār* closes the ṣalāh (*astaghfiru'llāh* ×3) — and here, a whole mission.**

**7. إِنَّهُ كَانَ تَوَّابًا — *innahu kāna tawwābā*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**RETURNING / turning back**" family — *tāba* = **to turn back, to return**; ⭐ ***tawbah* = REPENTANCE*** (⭐ **"turning back" to Allah**); ⭐ **when said of ALLAH, *tāba ʿalā* = He **turns back toward** the servant in mercy;** ⭐ ***at-Tawwāb* = a NAME OF ALLAH** — The Ever-Relenting.
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, He is ever Accepting of repentance.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the final word of the surah — and it's on the intensive pattern you've tracked through the whole juzʾ.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Tawwāb* is on the *faʿʿāl* INTENSIVE pattern** — ⭐ **"one who does it A LOT, constantly, by nature."** ⭐ **You own this shape from:**
  - **Qiyāmah 75:2** — *al-**lawwāmah*** (⭐ the soul that blames **relentlessly**)
  - **Nabaʾ 78:13** — ***wahhājā*** (⭐ **blazing** fiercely)
  - **Nabaʾ 78:14** — ***thajjājā*** (⭐ **pouring** in torrents)
  - **Nabaʾ 78:25** — ***ghassāqā*** (⭐ foul, freezing)
  - **Nabaʾ 78:28** — ***kidhdhābā*** (⭐ **lying** relentlessly)
  - **Burūj 85:16** — ***faʿʿālun** li-mā yurīd* (⭐ **doer** of whatever He wills)
  - ⭐ **Naṣr 110:3** — ***TAWWĀBĀ*** (⭐ **RELENTING, again and again**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ used the pattern for blazing suns, pouring rain, freezing pus, and relentless lies.** ⭐ **The juzʾ's last use of it is ⭐ **relentless mercy**.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And *tāba* = to TURN BACK** — ⭐ **so *tawbah* is the servant turning back to Allah, ⭐ **and *at-Tawwāb* is Allah turning back to the servant.** ⭐ **Every time you return, ⭐ **He returns** — and the *faʿʿāl* pattern says He does it **without limit**.
  
  ⭐ **And note ***KĀNA*** ("He WAS / has always been")** — ⭐ **not "He will be" — ⭐ **He has ALWAYS been Tawwāb.** ⭐ **The mercy isn't new; it's His permanent nature.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the return-root thread: ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:8** — *inna ilā rabbika'r-**rujʿā*** (⭐ **"to your Lord is the RETURN"**) ⭐ **— and Ghāshiyah 88:25** — *iyābahum* ⭐ **(their RETURN)**.** ⭐ **Different roots, same idea: ⭐ **everything returns to Him — and He returns to whoever returns.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kāna*** with Allah's attributes = **permanence** ("has always been"). ⭐ ***Tawwāban*** — *faʿʿāl* intensive, accusative as *khabar* of *kāna*.
- **Why this word:** ending on ***Tawwāb*** — ⭐ **so that the command to seek forgiveness is immediately followed by the certainty it's granted.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TAWWĀB* = *faʿʿāl* — RELENTING AGAIN AND AGAIN, without limit.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ used *faʿʿāl* for blazing suns and relentless LIES; ⭐ **the juzʾ's last *faʿʿāl* is relentless MERCY.** ⭐⭐ ***Tawbah* = YOU turn back; *at-Tawwāb* = HE turns back to you.** ⭐ ***KĀNA* = He has ALWAYS been this.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **وَاسْتَغْفِرْهُ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **the command at VICTORY is to seek FORGIVENESS.** ⭐ **Not a sign of wrongdoing — humility, completion, and (per Ibn ʿAbbās) the signal of the Prophet's ﷺ approaching end.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **اسْتَغْفِرْ vs اسْتَغْنَىٰ** — ⚠️ **SAME Form X, opposite meanings.** ⭐ **"Seek forgiveness" (I NEED) vs. "consider oneself self-sufficient" (I need NOTHING).**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَفْوَاجًا (v.2)** — ⚠️ **IDENTICAL to Nabaʾ 78:18** — ⭐ **crowds to Judgment there, crowds into faith here.**
4. ⭐⭐ **دِينِ اللَّهِ (v.2)** — ⚠️ ***dīn* = RELIGION** — ⭐ **and set directly against Kāfirūn 109:6's "your religion."**
5. ⭐⭐ **الْفَتْحُ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **"the OPENING" = the Conquest of Makkah.** ⭐ **Arabic says a city is OPENED, not conquered.**
6. ⭐⭐ **تَوَّابًا (v.3)** — ⚠️ ***faʿʿāl* intensive = relenting AGAIN AND AGAIN.** ⭐ **And *kāna* = He has ALWAYS been so.**
7. ⭐ **نَصْرُ اللَّهِ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **"the help OF ALLAH"** — the victory is attributed to Him, not to the Muslims.
8. ⭐ **إِذَا جَاءَ (v.1)** — ⚠️ ***idhā* + past = a CERTAIN future** ("when [not if] it comes").
9. ⭐ **سَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ (v.3)** — ⚠️ ***tasbīḥ* = negation of every flaw; *ḥamd* = affirmation of every perfection.** ⭐ **Two different jobs.**
10. ⭐ **يَدْخُلُونَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **a *ḥāl*: "WHILE entering"** — describing what he sees them doing.
