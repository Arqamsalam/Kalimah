# Surah Nūḥ (71) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–28) · **Complete coverage — every important word**

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It's a prophet's REPORT to his Lord after 950 years — every method he tried, and every way they refused.**

⭐ **The methods (v.5–9):**
- ⭐ ***laylan wa nahārā*** — **NIGHT and DAY** (v.5)
- ⭐ ***jihārā*** — **OPENLY, publicly** (v.8)
- ⭐ ***aʿlantu… wa asrartu*** — **in PUBLIC and in PRIVATE** (v.9)

⭐ **The responses (v.6–7):**
- ⭐ ***firārā*** — they **FLED** (v.6)
- ⭐ ***aṣābiʿahum fī ādhānihim*** — **FINGERS IN THEIR EARS** (v.7)
- ⭐ ***istaghshaw thiyābahum*** — **COVERED THEMSELVES WITH THEIR CLOTHES** (v.7)
- ⭐ ***aṣarrū wa'stakbarū'stikbārā*** — **PERSISTED and grew arrogant — utterly** (v.7)

⭐⭐⭐ **And look at the rhyme: almost every verse ends in *-ārā*** — *firārā, istikbārā, jihārā, isrārā, ghaffārā, midrārā, anhārā, waqārā, aṭwārā, khasārā, kubbārā, anṣārā, dayyārā, kaffārā, tabārā.* ⭐ **A long, open, mournful "*-āā-rā*" — the sound of a call echoing for nine and a half centuries.**

⭐⭐ **And the surah's most famous promise — v.10–12:** ⭐ ***istaghfirū rabbakum innahu kāna ghaffārā · yursili's-samāʾa ʿalaykum midrārā · wa yumdidkum bi-amwālin wa banīn…*** ⭐ **"Seek your Lord's forgiveness — He is ever-Forgiving — He will send rain, wealth, children, gardens, rivers."** ⭐ **Istighfār as the key to provision.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐⭐ **950 years: NIGHT, DAY, PUBLIC, PRIVATE — and they FLED, BLOCKED their ears, HID in their clothes.** ⭐ **Echo of *-ārā*.** ⭐ **And the cure for drought, poverty, and childlessness: *ISTIGHFĀR*.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The mission** (v.1–4) — warn your people; worship, fear Allah, obey me; He'll forgive and give you respite.
2. **Nūḥ's report** (v.5–9) — night and day, public and private — they fled, blocked their ears, covered themselves.
3. **The promise of istighfār** (v.10–12) — rain, wealth, sons, gardens, rivers.
4. **The signs** (v.13–20) — your stages of creation, the layered heavens, moon and sun, growth from earth, the earth as a carpet.
5. **Their response** (v.21–24) — they obeyed the rich, plotted hugely, clung to five idols.
6. **The end** (v.25) — drowned, then admitted into Fire.
7. **Nūḥ's two prayers** (v.26–28) — leave no dweller of the disbelievers; forgive me and my parents and the believers.

## §1 — The Mission (v.1–4)

**1. إِنَّا أَرْسَلْنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِ — *innā arsalnā nūḥan ilā qawmih*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *arsalnā* — **We SENT** (*rasūl* = messenger; *risālah* = a message). *Nūḥ* — ⭐ **the prophet NŪḤ** (peace be upon him) — ⚠️ a non-Arabic name, but **fully declined** (*Nūḥan*) because it has three letters with a quiet middle. *qawm* — ⭐ **a PEOPLE / nation** (from *qāma* — those who **stand** together; ⭐ ***qawmī* = NATIONAL**; ⭐ ***qawmiyyah* = NATIONALISM**).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, We sent Nūḥ to his people…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The surah named after a man who preached for 950 years** (*fa-labitha fīhim alfa sanatin illā khamsīna ʿāmā*, 29:14). ⭐ **And you met him in the previous surahs' shadow:** ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:11** — *innā lammā **ṭagha'l-māʾu** ḥamalnākum fi'l-jāriyah* — ⭐ **"when the WATER overflowed, We carried you in the SHIP"** — ⭐ **that was HIS flood, HIS ark.** ⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah gave you the flood in one verse; Nūḥ gives you the 950 years that led to it.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ilā qawmihi* — "to HIS people"** — ⭐ **his own people, his own family and tribe.** ⭐ **The hardest audience: those who knew him before he was a prophet** — ⭐ **exactly the Prophet's ﷺ situation with Quraysh.** ⭐ **This surah is comfort: Nūḥ endured 950 years; your 13 in Makkah are not in vain.**
  ⭐ ***Qawm* from *qāma* (to STAND)** — ⭐ **a people = those who STAND together.** ⭐ ***Qawmiyyah* = NATIONALISM.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***innā*** = "**indeed We**" (majestic plural + emphasis). ⭐ ***Nūḥan*** — ⚠️ **fully declined** despite being foreign (three letters, quiet middle — like *ʿĀdun*, Ḥāqqah 69:4).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **opening with Allah's own sending — establishing that the 950-year mission was His.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NŪḤ — 950 YEARS of preaching (29:14).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:11 gave you his FLOOD and SHIP in one verse; Sūrat Nūḥ gives the 950 years before it.** ⭐⭐ ***Ilā qawmihi* = to HIS OWN people — like the Prophet ﷺ with Quraysh.** ⭐ ***Qawm* ← *qāma* (STAND together); *qawmiyyah* = NATIONALISM.** ⚠️ ***Nūḥan* = fully declined.**

**2. أَنْ أَنذِرْ قَوْمَكَ مِن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ — *an andhir qawmaka min qabli an yaʾtiyahum ʿadhābun alīm*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *andhir* — ⭐ **WARN!** (⭐ ***indhār* = an ALARM**; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:2** — *qum fa-**andhir***!). *alīm* — ⭐ the "**PAIN**" family — *alima* = to feel pain; ⭐ ***alam* = PAIN** (everyday!); ⭐ ***alīm* = PAINFUL** (*faʿīl* — intense).
- **Here it means:** "**…[saying]: 'Warn your people before a painful punishment comes to them.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SAME FIRST COMMAND AS MUDDATHTHIR.** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:2** — *qum fa-**andhir*** — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ's first public command: "rise and WARN."** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:1** — *an **andhir** qawmak* — ⭐ **Nūḥ's first command: "WARN your people."** ⭐⭐⭐ **The first and the last (major) messengers — given the same first word.** ⭐ ***Andhir*** — ⭐ **the alarm.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Min qabli an yaʾtiyahum*** — ⭐ **"BEFORE it comes"** — ⭐ **a warning is only useful BEFORE.** ⭐ **The urgency is in the timing.** ⭐ ⭐ ***ʿAdhābun alīm*** — ⭐ **a PAINFUL punishment** — ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:13** — *wa ʿadhāban **alīmā***. ⭐ ***Alam* = PAIN** — your free hook.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***an*** = *an at-tafsīriyyah* (the "**namely / saying**" *an*, explaining the content of the sending). ⭐ ***andhir*** = Form IV imperative.
- **Why this exact word:** *indhār* — ⭐ **the alarm-call shared by the first and last messengers.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir 74:2 *qum fa-ANDHIR* (Prophet ﷺ) = Nūḥ 71:1 *an ANDHIR qawmak* (Nūḥ).** ⭐ **First and last messengers — same first command.** ⭐⭐ ***Min qabli* = BEFORE — a warning is only useful before.** ⭐ ***Alam* = PAIN.**

**3. قَالَ يَا قَوْمِ إِنِّي لَكُمْ نَذِيرٌ مُّبِينٌ — *qāla yā qawmi innī lakum nadhīrun mubīn*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *yā qawmi* — ⭐ **"O my people!"** (⚠️ the possessive *yāʾ* — *qawmī* — **dropped**: *yā qawmi*). *nadhīr* — ⭐ **a WARNER**. *mubīn* — ⭐ the "**CLARITY / making distinct**" family — *bāna* = to become clear; ⭐ ***mubīn* = CLEAR, making things plain** (⭐ ***bayn* = between**; ⭐ **Takwīr 81:23** *al-ufuq al-mubīn*; ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:1** *al-bayyinah*!).
- **Here it means:** "**He said: 'O my people, indeed I am a clear warner to you.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YĀ QAWMI* — "O MY people"** — ⭐ **with the possessive "my"** — ⭐ **tender, familial.** ⭐ **He doesn't say "O disbelievers!" (as in Kāfirūn 109:1) — he says "O MY PEOPLE."** ⭐ **For 950 years, he called them HIS.** ⭐⭐ **And the dropped *yāʾ* (*qawmi* not *qawmī*)** — ⭐ **a common feature in vocative address, softening the sound** (⭐ like *dīn[ī]*, Kāfirūn 109:6).
  ⭐⭐ ***Nadhīrun MUBĪN* — a CLEAR warner** — ⭐ ***mubīn* from *bayn* (BETWEEN)** — ⭐ **clarity = separating truth from falsehood** (⭐ **Bayyinah 98:1**). ⭐ **Nothing vague, nothing hidden: plain warning.** ⭐ **And compare Ḥāqqah 69:41–42 — not a poet, not a soothsayer (whose speech was VAGUE)** — ⭐ **a prophet's warning is *mubīn*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***yā qawmi*** = vocative with **dropped possessive *yāʾ*** (kasrah remains as the trace). ⭐ ***lakum*** fronted ("**to you** [specifically]").
- **Why this exact word:** *yā qawmi* — ⭐ **a warner who never stopped calling them his own.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YĀ QAWMI* = "O MY people" — 950 years, and he still called them HIS.** ⭐ **Not "O disbelievers" (109:1).** ⚠️⭐⭐ **The possessive *yāʾ* is DROPPED (kasrah trace) — like *dīn[ī]* (109:6).** ⭐⭐ ***MUBĪN* ← *bayn* (between) — Bayyinah 98:1.** ⭐ **Clear — unlike the soothsayers' riddles (69:42).**

**4. أَنِ اعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ وَاتَّقُوهُ وَأَطِيعُونِ — *ani'ʿbudu'llāha wa'ttaqūhu wa aṭīʿūn*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *uʿbudū* — **WORSHIP / serve** (*ʿabd*). *ittaqūhu* — ⭐ **SHIELD yourselves [from His displeasure]** (*taqwā* ← *wiqāyah* — **ARMOUR**). *aṭīʿūn* — ⭐ the "**OBEDIENCE / compliance**" family — *ṭāʿa* = to obey; ⭐ ***ṭāʿah* = OBEDIENCE**; ⭐ ***istiṭāʿah* = ABILITY / capacity** (⭐ *mustaṭāʿ* = possible!); ⭐ *taṭawwuʿ* = **VOLUNTEERING** (doing more than obliged — ⭐ *ṣalāt at-taṭawwuʿ* = **voluntary prayer**!).
- **Here it means:** "**[Saying]: 'Worship Allah, fear Him, and obey me.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE COMMANDS — the whole message in three words:** ⭐ **(1) *uʿbudū'llāh* — WORSHIP Allah** (the relationship); ⭐ **(2) *ittaqūhu* — SHIELD yourselves** (the inner guard); ⭐ **(3) *aṭīʿūn* — OBEY ME** (the practical following of the messenger). ⭐⭐ **Belief, God-consciousness, and following the messenger** — ⭐ **the same three pillars every prophet brought.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Aṭīʿū[nī]* — the hidden *yāʾ*!** ⭐ **The full form is *aṭīʿūnī* ("obey ME") — but the *yāʾ* is DROPPED in the muṣḥaf, leaving the kasrah** — ⭐ **to match the rhyme / for brevity.** ⭐ **You own this feature: *dīn[ī]* (Kāfirūn 109:6), *yasr[i]* (Fajr 89:4), *qawm[ī]* (v.2 just now).** ⭐ **Two dropped *yāʾ*s in two verses.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ittaqūhu* — the *taqwā* root (ARMOUR):** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:17** — *fa-kayfa **tattaqūna** in kafartum yawmā* (how will you SHIELD yourselves from a Day?). ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:56** — *huwa ahlu't-**taqwā*** (He is worthy of being shielded from).
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Istiṭāʿah* = ABILITY** — ⭐ **obedience (*ṭāʿah*) and ability share a root: you obey what you're ABLE to.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***an*** = *tafsīriyyah* again. ⚠️⭐⭐ ***aṭīʿūni*** — **possessive/object *yāʾ* dropped** (*aṭīʿūnī*). ⭐ **Three imperatives joined by *wa*.**
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the three-part creed: worship, guard, follow.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE COMMANDS: WORSHIP Allah · SHIELD yourselves (*taqwā*) · OBEY me.** ⭐ **Every prophet's message.** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***Aṭīʿū[nī]* — the "ME" *yāʾ* is DROPPED (like *qawm[ī]*, v.2).** ⭐⭐ ***Istiṭāʿah* = ABILITY; *taṭawwuʿ* = VOLUNTEERING; *ṣalāt at-taṭawwuʿ* = VOLUNTARY prayer.**

**5. يَغْفِرْ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمْ — *yaghfir lakum min dhunūbikum*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *yaghfir* — ⭐ **He will FORGIVE / cover** (⭐ ***mighfar* = a HELMET**). *dhunūb* — ⭐⭐ the "**TAIL / what follows behind**" family — ***dhanab* = a TAIL**!; ⭐ ***dhanb* = a SIN** (classically: **a sin is something that FOLLOWS you like a tail** — its consequences trail behind you!); ⭐ ***mudhnib* = a sinner**; ⭐ ***dhanab* also = the end / last part**.
- **Here it means:** "**He will forgive you some of your sins…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHANB* (SIN) shares a root with *DHANAB* (TAIL).** ⭐ **Why?** ⭐ Classical: **a sin is like a TAIL — it trails BEHIND you, following you wherever you go** — ⭐ **its consequences drag after the deed.** ⭐⭐ **Forgiveness (*maghfirah* — from *mighfar*, a HELMET) COVERS it — cuts off the tail.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Min dhunūbikum* — "SOME of your sins" / "FROM your sins"** — ⭐ classical readings: ⭐ **(a) *min* = "some" — the sins of the past, before you believed** (later sins need fresh repentance); ⭐ **(b) *min* = extra, emphatic — "ALL your sins."** ⭐ **Both classical.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Yaghfir* — JUSSIVE** (sukūn) — ⭐ **because it's the ANSWER to the commands of v.3** ("worship, fear, obey **[and then]** He will forgive"). ⭐ **The forgiveness is the CONSEQUENCE of obeying.** ⭐ (Like Maʿārij 70:42 — *dharhum yakhūḍū*.)
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***yaghfir*** = **JUSSIVE** (answer of a command — *jawāb aṭ-ṭalab*). ⭐ ***min*** = partitive or emphatic (debated).
- **Why this exact word:** *dhanb* — ⭐ **sin pictured as a tail that follows; forgiveness as its removal.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHANB* (SIN) ← *DHANAB* (TAIL) — a sin TRAILS BEHIND you like a tail.** ⭐ **Forgiveness (*mighfar* = HELMET) covers it.** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***Yaghfir* = JUSSIVE — the answer to v.3's commands.** ⭐ ***Min* = "some" or emphatic — both classical.**

**6. وَيُؤَخِّرْكُمْ إِلَىٰ أَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى — *wa yuʾakhkhirkum ilā ajalin musammā*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *yuʾakhkhir* — ⭐ the "**DELAYING / putting back**" family — *akhkhara* = to postpone; ⭐ ***taʾkhīr* = DELAY**; ⭐ ***taʾakhkhur* = LATENESS** (⭐ **Muddaththir 74:37** *yataʾakhkhar*!); ⭐ ***ākhir* = LAST**; ⭐ ***ākhirah* = the Hereafter**. *ajal* — ⭐⭐ the "**TERM / deadline / appointed time**" family — ***ajal* = an appointed TERM, a deadline — and the END of a lifespan** (death); ⭐ ***ājil* = DEFERRED / later** (⭐ ***ʿājil* vs *ājil*** = **now vs later** — ⭐ *ad-dunyā ʿājilah wa'l-ākhirah ājilah*!); ⭐ ***taʾjīl* = POSTPONEMENT**. *musammā* — ⭐ **NAMED, specified** (*ism* = a name; ⭐ *tasmiyah* = naming / saying *bismillāh*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and give you respite until a named term.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO TERMS — and the difference is the key.** ⭐ **v.4a** — *wa yuʾakhkhirkum ilā **ajalin musammā*** — ⭐ **"He will DELAY you to a NAMED term"** — ⭐ **i.e., you'll live out your natural lifespan, not be destroyed early by punishment.** ⭐ **v.4b** — *inna **ajala'llāhi** idhā jāʾa **lā yuʾakhkhar*** — ⭐ **"Allah's term, when it comes, will NOT be delayed."** ⭐⭐⭐ **Two *ajal*s:** ⭐ **(1) a NAMED, natural term — which belief can earn you (escaping the early, collective punishment); ⭐ (2) Allah's FINAL term — death, which NOTHING delays.** ⭐ **Belief can save you from the flood; nothing saves you from death.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Yuʾakhkhir* (delay) — the *akhkhara* root** — ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:37** — *an yataqaddama aw **yataʾakhkhar*** (to step forward or **fall behind**). ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:13, Infiṭār 82:5** — *qaddama wa **akhkhara***. ⭐⭐ **Here Allah does the delaying — giving TIME.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ajal* = a DEADLINE** — ⭐ ***ājil* = DEFERRED**, ⭐ ***ʿājil* = IMMEDIATE** — ⭐ **the Qurʾān's contrast of this world (*al-ʿājilah*, 75:20 — *bal tuḥibbūna'l-**ʿājilah***!) and the next.** ⭐ ***Musammā* — NAMED** — ⭐ **the term already has a name; it's written** (⭐ *tasmiyah* = saying *bismillāh*).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yuʾakhkhirkum*** — jussive (joined to *yaghfir*). ⭐ ***musamman*** — passive participle, weak-final (*musammā*).
- **Why this exact word:** *ajal musammā* — ⭐ **distinguishes the delay belief earns from the death no one escapes.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO *AJAL*s: v.4a a NAMED natural term (belief EARNS it — escape the flood) vs v.4b Allah's FINAL term (NOTHING delays it — death).** ⭐⭐ ***ʿĀJIL* (now) vs *ĀJIL* (later) — Qiyāmah 75:20 *tuḥibbūna'l-ʿājilah*.** ⭐ ***Ajal* = a DEADLINE; *taʾjīl* = POSTPONEMENT.** ⭐ ***Musammā* = NAMED (*tasmiyah*).** ⭐ ***Yataʾakhkhar* (74:37).**

**7. إِنَّ أَجَلَ اللَّهِ إِذَا جَاءَ لَا يُؤَخَّرُ ۖ لَوْ كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ — *inna ajala'llāhi idhā jāʾa lā yuʾakhkhar · law kuntum taʿlamūn*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *ajal Allāh* — ⭐ **Allah's TERM** (death / the final appointed time). *lā yuʾakhkhar* — ⭐ **is NOT DELAYED** (passive). *law kuntum taʿlamūn* — ⭐ **"IF ONLY you knew"**.
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, Allah's term, when it comes, is not delayed — if only you knew.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The second *ajal* — and a deleted answer you know.** ⭐ ***Lā yuʾakhkhar*** — ⭐ **NOT DELAYED — not by a moment** (⭐ *fa-idhā jāʾa ajaluhum lā yastaʾkhirūna sāʿatan wa lā yastaqdimūn*, 7:34). ⭐⭐⭐ ***Law kuntum taʿlamūn* — "IF ONLY you knew…"** — ⭐ **TAKĀTHUR 102:5** — *kallā **law taʿlamūna** ʿilma'l-yaqīn* — ⭐ **"If only you knew with the knowledge of certainty —"** ⭐ **and the answer is DELETED — the sentence just stops.** ⭐⭐ **Same device here: "if only you knew [ — you would believe / you would hurry / you would weep]."** ⭐ **The silence is bigger than any filler.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ajala'LLĀH*** — ⭐ **"ALLAH'S term"** — ⭐ **attributed directly to Him** — ⭐ **no one negotiates with it.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***inna*** + subject + ***idhā jāʾa*** (condition) + ***lā yuʾakhkharu*** (passive present). ⭐⭐ ***law*** — **answer DELETED** (*ḥadhf jawāb ash-sharṭ*).
- **Why this exact word:** *law… taʿlamūn* — ⭐ **the deleted answer forces the listener to supply the consequence.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ YUʾAKHKHAR* — Allah's term is NOT DELAYED, not by a moment (7:34).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LAW KUNTUM TAʿLAMŪN* — "IF ONLY you knew —" ANSWER DELETED, like TAKĀTHUR 102:5.** ⭐ **The silence is bigger than any filler.**

## §2 — Nūḥ's Report (v.5–9)

**8. قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي دَعَوْتُ قَوْمِي لَيْلًا وَنَهَارًا — *qāla rabbi innī daʿawtu qawmī laylan wa nahārā*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *rabbi* — ⭐ **"My Lord!"** (⚠️ *rabbī* with the *yāʾ* dropped — vocative). *daʿawtu* — ⭐ **I CALLED / invited** (*daʿwah* = a call, an invitation — ⭐ **the root of "daʿwah"**!). *laylan wa nahārā* — ⭐ **NIGHT and DAY** (a *merism* = **all the time**).
- **Here it means:** "**He said: 'My Lord, I have called my people night and day.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE REPORT BEGINS — a prophet accounting to his Lord.** ⭐ ***Rabbi*** — ⭐ **"MY Lord"** — ⭐ **intimate, direct.** ⭐ **Compare v.2: *yā qawmi* (O MY people) → v.5: *rabbi* (MY Lord).** ⭐⭐ **He belongs to both — his people and his Lord — and he's caught between them.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Laylan wa nahārā* — NIGHT and DAY** — ⭐ **a *merism* (two opposites meaning ALL)** — ⭐ **he never stopped.** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:2, 7 — the night for PRAYER, the day for SWIMMING through tasks** (*sabḥan ṭawīlā*). ⭐ **Nūḥ used BOTH night and day for *daʿwah*.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Daʿawtu* — the root of *DAʿWAH*** — ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:17** — *tadʿū man adbara wa tawallā* — ⭐ **the FIRE calls those who turned away.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nūḥ CALLED them to Paradise for 950 years; the Fire will call them in the end.** ⭐ **Jinn 72:19–20** — *qāma ʿabdu'llāhi **yadʿūhu**… qul innamā **adʿū** rabbī* — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ calling (on) his Lord.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐ ***rabbi*** = vocative with dropped *yāʾ* (*rabbī*). ⭐ ***laylan wa nahāran*** = **adverbs of time** (accusative).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **a merism of time — nothing left out.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LAYLAN WA NAHĀRĀ* = NIGHT and DAY — a merism: ALL the time.** ⭐⭐ ***Yā qawmi* (v.2, MY people) → *rabbi* (v.5, MY Lord) — caught between both.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Daʿawtu* = the root of DAʿWAH — Nūḥ called them 950 years; the FIRE calls them at the end (70:17).** ⚠️ ***Rabbi* = *rabbī*, yāʾ dropped.**

**9. فَلَمْ يَزِدْهُمْ دُعَائِي إِلَّا فِرَارًا — *fa-lam yazidhum duʿāʾī illā firārā*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *yazidhum* — ⭐ **INCREASE them** (*zāda*; ⚠️ jussive after *lam* — the long *ī* shortens: *yazīd* → *yazid*). *duʿāʾ* — ⭐ **my CALLING** (here = **calling them**, not supplication). *firār* — ⭐⭐ the "**FLEEING in panic**" family — *farra* = to flee; ⭐ ***mafarr* = an escape** (⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:10**!); ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:34** — *yawma **yafirru**'l-marʾ*; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:51** — ***farrat** min qaswarah*!; ⭐ ***firār* = flight / desertion** (⭐ modern: *firār* = **ESCAPE** — from prison!).
- **Here it means:** "**But my calling only increased them in flight.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TRAGIC IRONY — the more he called, the faster they ran.** ⭐ **Not "my calling didn't work" — ⭐ "it INCREASED their fleeing."** ⭐ **The call itself drove them away.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And *firār* — FLEEING — you own it from Muddaththir 74:50–51:** ⭐ ***ka-annahum ḥumurun mustanfirah · farrat min qaswarah*** — ⭐ **"like WILD DONKEYS bolting, FLEEING from a lion"** — ⭐ **the Quraysh fleeing the Prophet's ﷺ reminder.** ⭐⭐ **Nūḥ's people FLED from his call (71:6); Quraysh FLED from the reminder (74:51).** ⭐ **The same reaction, separated by thousands of years.** ⭐ **The comfort to the Prophet ﷺ: this has always happened.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE "*ZĀDA… ILLĀ*" PATTERN — appearing FOUR times in this surah:** ⭐ **v.6** — *lam yazidhum duʿāʾī **illā firārā*** (my call only increased them in **FLEEING**) ⭐ **v.21** — *lam yazidhu māluhu wa waladuhu **illā khasārā*** (his wealth only increased him in **LOSS**) ⭐ **v.24** — *wa lā tazidi'ẓ-ẓālimīna **illā ḍalālā*** (increase them only in **STRAYING**) ⭐ **v.28** — *wa lā tazidi'ẓ-ẓālimīna **illā tabārā*** (increase them only in **RUIN**). ⭐⭐⭐ **Fleeing → loss → straying → ruin. The surah's whole tragedy in one repeated structure.** ⭐ **And Jinn 72:6** — *fa-**zādūhum** rahaqā* (the false refuge only increased them in burden); ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:30** — *fa-lan **nazīdakum** illā ʿadhābā*.
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***firār* = an ESCAPE** (from prison).
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ ***lam yazid*** — **jussive of a hollow verb: the long vowel shortens** (*yazīdu* → *yazid*). ⭐ ***illā*** = restriction ("**nothing but**").
- **Why this exact word:** *firār* — ⭐ **makes their rejection active flight, not passive indifference.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The more he CALLED, the more they FLED — the call itself drove them away.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nūḥ 71:6 *FIRĀRĀ* = Muddaththir 74:51 *FARRAT* (donkeys fleeing a lion).** ⭐ **Same reaction, thousands of years apart.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ZĀDA… ILLĀ* ×4: FLEEING (v.6) → LOSS (v.21) → STRAYING (v.24) → RUIN (v.28).** ⭐ ***Firār* = an ESCAPE from prison.**

**10. وَإِنِّي كُلَّمَا دَعَوْتُهُمْ لِتَغْفِرَ لَهُمْ — *wa innī kullamā daʿawtuhum li-taghfira lahum*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *kullamā* — ⭐ **"EVERY TIME / whenever"** (*kull* + *mā*). *li-taghfira lahum* — ⭐ **"so that YOU might forgive them"**.
- **Here it means:** "**And indeed, every time I called them so that You might forgive them…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **LOOK AT THE PURPOSE: *li-taghfira lahum* — "so that YOU might FORGIVE them."** ⭐ **He didn't call them to win an argument, or to gain followers, or for his own honour** — ⭐ **he called them so ALLAH would FORGIVE them.** ⭐⭐ **The whole 950 years were an act of mercy toward them.** ⭐ **And they responded by blocking their ears (next).** ⭐ ⭐ ***Kullamā* — "EVERY SINGLE TIME"** — ⭐ **not once; every time, without exception, the same reaction.**
  ⭐ **And notice: he speaks TO ALLAH (*taghfira* — "YOU forgive")** — ⭐ **the report is a conversation.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kullamā*** = "**whenever**" (repetition). ⭐ ***li-*** + subjunctive (*taghfira*) = purpose. ⭐ **2nd person (*taghfira*) — addressing Allah.**
- **Why this exact word:** *li-taghfira* — ⭐ **reveals the motive behind the warning: forgiveness, not condemnation.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY did he call? *LI-TAGHFIRA LAHUM* — "so that YOU might FORGIVE them."** ⭐ **950 years of MERCY.** ⭐⭐ ***Kullamā* = EVERY SINGLE TIME.**

**11. جَعَلُوا أَصَابِعَهُمْ فِي آذَانِهِمْ — *jaʿalū aṣābiʿahum fī ādhānihim*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *aṣābiʿ* — ⭐ **FINGERS** (plural of *iṣbaʿ*; ⭐ *buṣmat al-iṣbaʿ* = **a FINGERPRINT**!). *ādhān* — ⭐⭐ **EARS** (plural of *udhun*) — ⭐ **the root of *adhān*** (the call to prayer!), ***idhn*** (permission — **Qadr 97:4**), ⭐ ***udhunun wāʿiyah*** (the retaining ear — **Ḥāqqah 69:12**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…they put their fingers in their ears…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE EARS — and the contrast with Ḥāqqah is the best in this batch.** ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:12** — *wa taʿiyahā **udhunun wāʿiyah*** — ⭐ **"so that a RETAINING EAR might hold it"** — ⭐ **an ear like a VESSEL, open and keeping.** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:7** — *jaʿalū **aṣābiʿahum fī ādhānihim*** — ⭐ **"they put their FINGERS IN THEIR EARS"** — ⭐ **ears PLUGGED.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Two surahs apart: the ear that RETAINS vs the ear that's BLOCKED.** ⭐ **Same organ, opposite choices.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Aṣābiʿ* — FINGERS — but you can't fit a whole finger in your ear!** ⭐ Classical: **this is *majāz* — "fingers" for "FINGERTIPS"** — ⭐ **the whole is named for the part, to show how FORCEFULLY they jammed them in, as if they'd push the entire finger in.** ⭐ **Exaggeration that reveals their desperation not to hear.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Udhun* → *ADHĀN*** — ⭐ **the call to prayer you hear five times a day.** ⭐ **They blocked their ears to the call; Muslims rise to the *adhān*.**
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***buṣmat al-iṣbaʿ* = a FINGERPRINT** (⭐ and **Qiyāmah 75:4** — *ʿalā an nusawwiya **banānah*** — restoring the FINGERTIPS!).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***jaʿalū*** = "**they placed**." ⭐⭐ ***aṣābiʿ*** — ***majāz mursal*** (the whole named for the part — fingers for fingertips).
- **Why this exact word:** *aṣābiʿ* — ⭐ **the hyperbole shows a frantic refusal to hear.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:12: the ear that RETAINS (*udhunun wāʿiyah*). Nūḥ 71:7: FINGERS IN EARS — BLOCKED.** ⭐ **Same organ, opposite choices.** ⭐⭐ **"FINGERS" for FINGERTIPS — *majāz*: they JAMMED them in.** ⭐⭐ ***Udhun* → *ADHĀN* (call to prayer), *idhn* (permission).** ⭐ ***Buṣmat al-iṣbaʿ* = a FINGERPRINT (Qiyāmah 75:4 *banān*).**

**12. وَاسْتَغْشَوْا ثِيَابَهُمْ — *wa'staghshaw thiyābahum*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *istaghshaw* — ⭐⭐⭐ **Form X of *ghashiya*** = **to COVER oneself completely, to wrap oneself up** — ⭐ the "**COVERING / enveloping**" family — ⭐ ***al-Ghāshiyah* = the ENVELOPER** (**Sūrah 88!**); ⭐ **Layl 92:1** — *wa'l-layli idhā **yaghshā*** (the night when it **COVERS**); ⭐ **Shams 91:4** — *yaghshāhā*; ⭐ ***ghishāwah* = a COVERING over the eyes** (2:7); ⭐ ***ghashsh* = CHEATING** (covering the truth!). *thiyāb* — ⭐ **GARMENTS** (⭐ **Muddaththir 74:4** — *wa **thiyābaka** fa-ṭahhir*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and wrapped themselves in their garments…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They didn't just block their EARS — they covered their EYES and FACES too.** ⭐ ***Istaghshaw*** — ⭐ **Form X = they SOUGHT to cover themselves** — ⭐ **pulled their cloaks over their heads** — ⭐ classically: **so they wouldn't SEE Nūḥ, wouldn't HEAR him, and so he wouldn't recognize them.** ⭐ **Total sensory shutdown: fingers in ears (hearing), garments over heads (sight).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the *gh-sh-y* root — the root of AL-GHĀSHIYAH:** ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:1** — *hal atāka ḥadīthu'l-**ghāshiyah*** — ⭐ **the ENVELOPING Day.** ⭐ **Layl 92:1** — *wa'l-layli idhā **yaghshā*** — ⭐ **the night that COVERS.** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:7** — ***istaghshaw** thiyābahum* — ⭐ **they COVERED themselves.** ⭐⭐⭐ **They wrapped themselves to escape the warning (71:7); the Enveloping Day (*al-Ghāshiyah*) will wrap THEM (88:1).** ⭐ **Self-covering now, being covered then.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE GARMENT CONTRAST — Muddaththir!** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:1, 4** — ***yā ayyuha'l-muddaththir*… *wa thiyābaka fa-ṭahhir*** — ⭐ **the Prophet ﷺ was COVERED in a cloak — and told to RISE and PURIFY his garments.** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:7** — ***istaghshaw thiyābahum*** — ⭐ **Nūḥ's people COVERED themselves in their garments — to HIDE from the truth.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ threw off his covering to WARN; they pulled on theirs to AVOID the warning.** ⭐ **Same garments, opposite purposes.**
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***ghashsh* = CHEATING** (covering the truth); ⭐ ***ghishāwah* = a VEIL** over the eyes.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form X** (*istaghshā*) = **seeking / doing to oneself** — weak-final, plural (*istaghshayū* → *istaghshaw*). ⭐ ***thiyābahum*** = object.
- **Why this exact word:** *istaghshā* — ⭐ **the root of *al-Ghāshiyah*, so their self-covering foreshadows their enveloping.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ISTAGHSHAW* = they WRAPPED themselves — fingers in EARS + cloaks over EYES = total shutdown.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Gh-sh-y* = AL-GHĀSHIYAH (88:1): they covered themselves now → the Enveloping Day covers them then.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir 74:1, 4: the Prophet ﷺ THREW OFF his cloak to WARN. Nūḥ 71:7: they PULLED ON theirs to AVOID.** ⭐ ***Ghashsh* = CHEATING.**

**13. وَأَصَرُّوا وَاسْتَكْبَرُوا اسْتِكْبَارًا — *wa aṣarrū wa'stakbarū'stikbārā*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *aṣarrū* — ⭐ the "**PERSISTING / insisting stubbornly**" family — *aṣarra* = to persist, to insist; ⭐ ***iṣrār* = PERSISTENCE / INSISTENCE / DETERMINATION** (⭐ modern: ***iṣrār* = determination** — positive or negative!); ⭐ ***al-iṣrār ʿala'dh-dhanb* = PERSISTING in sin** (a fiqh/akhlāq term!). *istakbarū* — ⭐⭐ **Form X of *kabura*** = **to CONSIDER ONESELF GREAT** (⭐ **Muddaththir 74:23** — *thumma adbara **wa'stakbar***!; ⭐ **Iblīs**: *abā **wa'stakbara***, 2:34).
- **Here it means:** "**…and persisted, and grew arrogant with [great] arrogance.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE FOUR RESPONSES complete:** ⭐ **(1) fingers in ears, (2) cloaks over heads, (3) PERSISTED, (4) ARROGANCE.** ⭐⭐ ***Aṣarrū* — PERSISTED** — ⭐ **the ḥadīth/saying: "*No sin is minor if PERSISTED in (maʿa'l-iṣrār), and no sin is major with istighfār.*"** ⭐ **And v.10 is ISTIGHFĀR!** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Iṣrār* (persistence, v.7) vs *istighfār* (seeking forgiveness, v.10)** — ⭐ **the two roads, three verses apart.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Istakbarū'STIKBĀRĀ* — the cognate accusative** — ⭐ **"grew arrogant with an ARROGANCE"** — ⭐ **Arabic's underline: TOTAL arrogance.** ⭐⭐ **The Form X ego family:** ⭐ ***istaghnā*** (I need nothing — 80:5, 92:8, 96:7), ⭐ ***istakthara*** (seek more — 74:6), ⭐ ***istakbara*** (I'm great — 74:23, 71:7). ⭐ **vs *istaghfara* (I need forgiveness — 110:3, 73:20, 71:10).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nūḥ's people: *istakbarū* (v.7). Nūḥ's cure: *istaghfirū* (v.10). Same form, opposite souls — in one surah.**
  ⭐⭐ **And Muddaththir 74:23** — *thumma adbara **wa'stakbar*** — ⭐ **al-Walīd** — ⭐ **the same verb across the ages.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***aṣarrū*** = Form IV, doubled root (*ṣ-r-r*). ⭐⭐ ***istakbarū'stikbāran*** = **Form X + cognate accusative** (*maf'ūl muṭlaq*) = maximum emphasis.
- **Why this exact word:** *iṣrār* + *istikbār* — ⭐ **the twin roots of refusal: stubbornness and pride.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FOUR RESPONSES: fingers in EARS, cloaks over HEADS, PERSISTED, ARROGANCE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***IṢRĀR* (persistence, v.7) vs *ISTIGHFĀR* (forgiveness, v.10) — "No sin is minor with *iṣrār*, none major with *istighfār*."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ISTAKBARŪ* (v.7) vs *ISTAGHFIRŪ* (v.10) — same Form X, opposite souls, one surah.** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:23 *wa'stakbar*.** ⭐ ***Iṣrār* = DETERMINATION.**

**14. ثُمَّ إِنِّي دَعَوْتُهُمْ جِهَارًا — *thumma innī daʿawtuhum jihārā*** · v.8
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**OPENNESS / loudness / publicity**" family — *jahara* = **to make public, to speak aloud**; ⭐ ***jahr* = LOUDNESS** (⭐ ***ṣalāh jahriyyah* = an AUDIBLE prayer** — Fajr, Maghrib, ʿIshāʾ, recited aloud!); ⭐ ***jihāran* = OPENLY, publicly**; ⭐ ***mijhar* = a MICROSCOPE** (makes things **visible**!); ⭐ ***jahwarī* = (a voice) LOUD / resonant**.
- **Here it means:** "**Then I called them openly.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***THUMMA* — "THEN" — a CHANGE OF STRATEGY.** ⭐ **Private calling failed (v.7) → *thumma* → PUBLIC calling (v.8).** ⭐ ***Jihārā*** — ⭐ **openly, loudly, in the gathering places** — ⭐ **no more one-on-one.** ⭐⭐ ***Ṣalāh jahriyyah* — the AUDIBLE prayers** — ⭐ **Fajr, Maghrib, ʿIshāʾ, where the imam recites ALOUD** — ⭐ **same root.** ⭐ ***Mijhar* = a MICROSCOPE** — ⭐ **makes the hidden visible.**
  ⭐⭐ **The *thumma*-escalation (v.8, v.9):** ⭐ **(1) night and day (v.5) → (2) *thumma* — OPENLY (v.8) → (3) *thumma* — openly AND secretly (v.9).** ⭐ **Every method, in sequence.** ⭐ **Compare Muddaththir 74:21–23's *thumma*s — the man producing a slander step by step.** ⭐ **Nūḥ's *thumma*s: a prophet trying every door.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***thumma*** = sequence + **escalation** (*tarākhī*). ⭐ ***jihāran*** = *maf'ūl muṭlaq* or *ḥāl* ("**openly**").
- **Why this exact word:** *jihār* — ⭐ **the shift from private to public — a new tactic after the old one failed.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***THUMMA* = a CHANGE OF STRATEGY: private failed → *JIHĀRĀ* (OPENLY).** ⭐⭐ ***ṢALĀH JAHRIYYAH* = AUDIBLE prayers (Fajr, Maghrib, ʿIshāʾ).** ⭐ ***MIJHAR* = a MICROSCOPE.** ⭐⭐ **Nūḥ's *thumma*s: a prophet trying every door. Muddaththir 74:21–23's *thumma*s: a man building a lie.**

**15. ثُمَّ إِنِّي أَعْلَنتُ لَهُمْ وَأَسْرَرْتُ لَهُمْ إِسْرَارًا — *thumma innī aʿlantu lahum wa asrartu lahum isrārā*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *aʿlantu* — ⭐ the "**ANNOUNCING / making public**" family — *ʿalana* = to become public; ⭐ ***iʿlān* = an ANNOUNCEMENT / an ADVERTISEMENT** (everyday!); ⭐ ***ʿalanan* = publicly**; ⭐ ***ʿalāniyah* = openness**. *asrartu* — ⭐ the "**SECRET**" family — *sarra* = to conceal; ⭐ ***sirr* = a SECRET**; ⭐ ***sarāʾir* = SECRETS** (⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:9** — *yawma **tublā's-sarāʾir***!); ⭐ ***isrār* = speaking secretly / confiding**; ⭐ ***sirrī* = CONFIDENTIAL**; ⚠️ ***surūr* = JOY** (same letters, *s-r-r*! — ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:9** *masrūrā*!).
- **Here it means:** "**Then I announced to them publicly, and I confided to them privately.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE THIRD STRATEGY — BOTH at once: PUBLIC and PRIVATE.** ⭐ ***Aʿlantu* — PUBLIC announcement** (⭐ ***iʿlān* = an ADVERTISEMENT**!). ⭐ ***Asrartu… isrārā* — PRIVATE, secret, whispered** — ⭐ **one-on-one counsel, in confidence.** ⭐⭐ **A *ṭibāq* (opposite pair) — public vs secret** — ⭐ **he tried EVERY channel: day/night (time), open/hidden (manner).** ⭐ **A complete da'wah strategy, 950 years old.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Isrārā* — the cognate accusative (*asrartu… isrārā*)** — ⭐ **"confided with real confidentiality"** — ⭐ **genuine, heartfelt private counsel.** ⭐⭐ **And the *s-r-r* root's DOUBLE life:** ⭐ ***sirr* = SECRET** (Ṭāriq 86:9 — the Day SECRETS are tested) ⭐ and ⭐ ***surūr* = JOY** (Inshiqāq 84:9 — returning to his family ***masrūrā***, JOYFUL!). ⭐ **Classical link: joy is something felt in the SECRET depths of the heart.** ⭐ **One root: secret and joy.**
  ⭐ **And Ṭāriq 86:9** — ***yawma tublā's-sarāʾir*** — ⭐ **"the Day SECRETS are tested."** ⭐ **Nūḥ spoke to them in SECRET (*isrār*); their secrets will be exposed (*sarāʾir*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ two Form IV verbs (*aʿlana*, *asarra*) — ⭐ ***ṭibāq***. ⭐ ***isrāran*** = cognate accusative.
- **Why this exact word:** *iʿlān / isrār* — ⭐ **every possible manner of address, exhausted.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **PUBLIC (*aʿlantu*) + PRIVATE (*asrartu isrārā*) — every channel tried.** ⭐ **Time: night/day (v.5). Manner: open/secret (v.8–9).** ⭐⭐ ***IʿLĀN* = an ADVERTISEMENT; *SIRRĪ* = CONFIDENTIAL.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***S-r-r*: *SIRR* (SECRET — Ṭāriq 86:9) & *SURŪR* (JOY — Inshiqāq 84:9) — joy lives in the heart's secret depths.**

## §3 — The Promise of Istighfār (v.10–12)

**16. فَقُلْتُ اسْتَغْفِرُوا رَبَّكُمْ إِنَّهُ كَانَ غَفَّارًا — *fa-qultu'staghfirū rabbakum innahu kāna ghaffārā*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *istaghfirū* — ⭐ **Form X of *ghafara*** = **SEEK covering / forgiveness** (⭐ ***mighfar* = a HELMET**). *ghaffār* — ⭐⭐ ***faʿʿāl* INTENSIVE** = **ONE WHO FORGIVES AGAIN AND AGAIN, endlessly** — ⭐ the highest of three forgiveness-intensities: ***ghāfir* (one who forgives) → *ghafūr* (abundantly forgiving) → *ghaffār* (forgiving over and over without limit)**; ⭐ ***al-Ghaffār* = a NAME OF ALLAH**; ⭐ ***ʿAbd al-Ghaffār*** (a name!).
- **Here it means:** "**And I said: 'Seek your Lord's forgiveness — indeed, He is ever Forgiving.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE HEART OF THE SURAH — and the Form X turnaround.** ⭐ **v.7: *wa'STAKBARŪ'stikbārā*** — **they grew ARROGANT (Form X — "I'm great").** ⭐ **v.10: *ISTAGHFIRŪ rabbakum*** — **SEEK FORGIVENESS (Form X — "I need").** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same form, three verses apart: the disease (*istikbār*) and the cure (*istighfār*).** ⭐ **Nūḥ's whole message in one Form X swap.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the *istighfār* thread you've built:** ⭐ **Naṣr 110:3** — *wa'**staghfirh** innahu kāna **tawwābā*** — ⭐ **VICTORY ends in istighfār.** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** — *wa'**staghfiru**'llāh inna'llāha **ghafūrun** raḥīm* — ⭐ **WORSHIP ends in istighfār.** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:10** — ***istaghfirū** rabbakum innahu kāna **ghaffārā*** — ⭐ **istighfār as the KEY to PROVISION** (v.11–12). ⭐⭐⭐ **Three surahs: istighfār after victory, after worship, and BEFORE provision.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAFFĀR* — the *faʿʿāl* intensive:** ⭐ **Naṣr 110:3 *kāna TAWWĀBĀ* (He turns back again and again) — Nūḥ 71:10 *kāna GHAFFĀRĀ* (He forgives again and again).** ⭐ **Same structure (*innahu kāna* + *faʿʿāl*).** ⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE ONE-LETTER CONTRAST WITHIN THIS SURAH: v.10 *GHAFFĀRĀ* (Allah — forgiving endlessly) vs v.27 *KAFFĀRĀ* (the disbelievers — ungrateful endlessly).** ⭐ **GH vs K — one letter, the two poles of the surah.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Kāna*** — "**He has ALWAYS been**" — ⭐ **permanence: forgiveness is His nature, not a mood.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form X imperative** (*istaghfirū*). ⭐ ***ghaffāran*** = *faʿʿāl* intensive, *khabar* of *kāna*.
- **Why this exact word:** *ghaffār* (**endlessly forgiving**) — ⭐ **because after 950 years of rejection, the door is STILL open.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.7 *ISTAKBARŪ* (arrogance — Form X) → v.10 *ISTAGHFIRŪ* (forgiveness — Form X). Disease and cure, same form.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHĀFIR* → *GHAFŪR* → *GHAFFĀR* — three intensities; *Ghaffār* = endlessly.** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.10 *GHAFFĀRĀ* (Allah) vs v.27 *KAFFĀRĀ* (them) — ONE LETTER.** ⭐⭐ **Istighfār: after VICTORY (110:3), after WORSHIP (73:20), before PROVISION (71:10).** ⭐ **Naṣr 110:3 *kāna tawwābā* = 71:10 *kāna ghaffārā*.**

**17. يُرْسِلِ السَّمَاءَ عَلَيْكُم مِّدْرَارًا — *yursili's-samāʾa ʿalaykum midrārā*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *yursil* — ⭐ **He will SEND** (Form IV; jussive). *as-samāʾ* — ⭐ **the SKY** — ⭐ **here = RAIN** (the sky named for what comes from it). *midrār* — ⭐⭐ the "**ABUNDANT FLOWING (of milk / rain)**" family — *darra* = **for milk to flow abundantly from the udder**; ⭐ ***dirr* = flowing MILK**; ⭐ ***midrār* = POURING ABUNDANTLY, copious** (*mifʿāl* intensive — rain that keeps coming); ⭐ ***lillāhi darruh!*** = "**How excellent he is!**" (lit. "to Allah belongs his milk-flow!" — an idiom of praise!); ⭐ ***mudirr li'l-bawl* = a DIURETIC** (clinical — "causing flow"!).
- **Here it means:** "**He will send the sky [rain] upon you in abundant downpours.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **RAIN pictured as MILK flowing from an udder.** ⭐ ***Midrār* — from *dirr*, flowing MILK** — ⭐ **the sky becomes a nursing udder, pouring generously.** ⭐ **For a desert people: rain = milk = life.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the cross-surah promise:** ⭐ **Jinn 72:16** — *wa an lawi'staqāmū ʿala'ṭ-ṭarīqati **la-asqaynāhum māʾan ghadaqā*** — ⭐ **"if they stood upright on the Way, We'd give them ABUNDANT water."** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:11** — *istaghfirū… **yursili's-samāʾa ʿalaykum midrārā*** — ⭐ **"seek forgiveness… He'll send ABUNDANT rain."** ⭐⭐⭐ **Two surahs, one law: uprightness and istighfār → RAIN.** ⭐ ***Ghadaq* (72:16) and *midrār* (71:11) — two words for abundant water.**
  ⭐⭐ **And Nabaʾ 78:14** — *wa anzalnā mina'l-muʿṣirāti māʾan **thajjājā*** (rain **pouring in torrents**). ⭐ **Rain words: *thajjāj* (torrents), *ghadaq* (copious), *midrār* (flowing like milk).**
  ⭐ Clinical hook: ⭐ ***mudirr li'l-bawl* = a DIURETIC** — **"causing flow."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***yursili*** = **JUSSIVE** (answer to *istaghfirū*) — ⭐ **the kasrah is added to avoid two sukūns** before *as-samāʾ*. ⭐ ***midrāran*** = *ḥāl* (*mifʿāl* intensive — usable for masculine and feminine).
- **Why this exact word:** *midrār* — ⭐ **rain as milk: nourishment, generosity, life.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIDRĀR* ← *DIRR* = flowing MILK — the sky becomes a nursing UDDER.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Jinn 72:16 *ghadaqā* (upright → ABUNDANT water) = Nūḥ 71:11 *midrārā* (istighfār → ABUNDANT rain).** ⭐⭐ **Rain words: *thajjāj* (78:14), *ghadaq* (72:16), *midrār* (71:11).** ⭐ ***Lillāhi darruh!* = "How excellent!"; *mudirr* = a DIURETIC.** ⚠️ **Jussive.**

**18. وَيُمْدِدْكُم بِأَمْوَالٍ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ جَنَّاتٍ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ أَنْهَارًا — *wa yumdidkum bi-amwālin wa banīna wa yajʿal lakum jannātin wa yajʿal lakum anhārā*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *yumdid* — ⭐⭐ the "**EXTENDING / SUPPLYING**" family — *madda* = to extend; ⭐ ***amadda* (Form IV) = to SUPPLY, reinforce**; ⭐ ***imdād* = SUPPLY** (⭐ ***imdādāt* = SUPPLIES** — military/logistics!; ⭐ *imdād kahrabāʾī* = **power supply**!); ⭐ ***madd*** — tajwīd lengthening; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:12** — *mālan **mamdūdā***!; ⭐ **Humazah 104:9** — *ʿamadin **mumaddadah***. *amwāl* — **WEALTH**. *banīn* — **SONS**. *jannāt* — **GARDENS**. *anhār* — ⭐ **RIVERS** (⭐ *nahr*; ⭐ ***nahār* = DAY** — same root? *n-h-r* — ⭐ **both = flowing broadness, the day "flowing" with light!**).
- **Here it means:** "**…and supply you with wealth and sons, and make for you gardens, and make for you rivers.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FIVE GIFTS for istighfār:** ⭐ **(1) RAIN (v.11), (2) WEALTH, (3) SONS, (4) GARDENS, (5) RIVERS.** ⭐⭐⭐ **A famous application — al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī:** ⭐ **one man complained to him of DROUGHT, another of POVERTY, another of having NO CHILDREN, another that his GARDEN was dry** — ⭐ **he told every one of them: "*Seek Allah's forgiveness*" — and recited these verses.** ⭐ **When asked why he gave the same answer to all, he said: "I said nothing from myself — Allah says *istaghfirū rabbakum…*"** ⭐⭐ **(And ʿUmar (رضي الله عنه) is reported to have gone out for the rain-prayer and done nothing but ISTIGHFĀR — saying he sought rain through "the keys of the sky.")**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MUDDATHTHIR CONTRAST:** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:12–13** — *wa jaʿaltu lahu **mālan mamdūdā · wa banīna** shuhūdā* — ⭐ **al-Walīd was GIVEN extended WEALTH and SONS — and he was ungrateful.** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:12** — *wa **yumdidkum bi-amwālin wa banīn*** — ⭐ **Nūḥ's people were PROMISED extended wealth and sons — for istighfār.** ⭐⭐ **SAME *m-d-d* root, SAME pair (wealth + sons).** ⭐ **One received them and denied; the others were offered them and refused.**
  ⭐⭐ **And v.21 completes the irony:** ⭐ *wa'ttabaʿū man lam yazidhu **māluhu wa waladuhu** illā khasārā* — ⭐ **they followed the man whose WEALTH and CHILDREN only increased him in LOSS.** ⭐ **Wealth and children from istighfār = blessing; wealth and children without it = loss.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Yajʿal lakum… wa yajʿal lakum*** — ⭐ **"make FOR YOU… make FOR YOU"** — ⭐ **repeated *lakum*: personal, generous.**
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***imdādāt* = SUPPLIES**; ⭐ ***nahr* = a river / *nahār* = day.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***yumdidkum*** — **jussive of a DOUBLED verb, UNMERGED** (*yumdid*, not *yumidd*) — ⭐ **like Muddaththir 74:6 *tamnun*.** ⭐ ***yajʿal*** ×2 — jussive (answer to *istaghfirū*).
- **Why this exact word:** *imdād* (**supply / extension**) — ⭐ **the same root used for al-Walīd's wealth, now offered as a gift for repentance.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FIVE GIFTS for istighfār: RAIN, WEALTH, SONS, GARDENS, RIVERS.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī: drought, poverty, childlessness, dry garden → "ISTIGHFĀR" for all four.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir 74:12–13: al-Walīd GIVEN *māl mamdūd* + *banīn* (ungrateful). Nūḥ 71:12: OFFERED *yumdidkum bi-amwālin wa banīn* (refused).** ⭐ **Same *m-d-d*, same pair.** ⭐ ***Imdādāt* = SUPPLIES.** ⚠️ ***Yumdid* = unmerged doubled jussive (like *tamnun*, 74:6).**

## §4 — The Signs (v.13–20)

**19. مَّا لَكُمْ لَا تَرْجُونَ لِلَّهِ وَقَارًا — *mā lakum lā tarjūna li'llāhi waqārā*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *mā lakum* — ⭐ **"WHAT IS WRONG WITH YOU?"** (⭐ **Muddaththir 74:49** — *fa-mā lahum*; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:36** — *fa-māli'lladhīna*). *tarjūna* — ⭐ the "**HOPE / EXPECT (and sometimes FEAR)**" family — *rajā* = to hope, to expect; ⭐ ***rajāʾ* = HOPE** (⭐ ***ar-rajāʾ!* = "PLEASE!"** — on signs: *ar-rajāʾ ʿadam at-tadkhīn* = **please don't smoke**!); ⭐ classically, *rajā* **in a negative sentence** can mean **to FEAR / to reckon with**. *waqār* — ⭐⭐ the "**HEAVINESS / GRAVITY → DIGNITY**" family — *waqura* = to be heavy, grave, dignified; ⭐ ***waqr* = a heavy LOAD**; ⭐⭐ ***waqr* fi'l-udhun = DEAFNESS** (heaviness in the ear! — ⭐ *fī ādhāninā **waqr***, 41:5!); ⭐ ***waqār* = DIGNITY, gravitas, solemn respect**; ⭐ ***tawqīr* = RESPECT, reverence** (⭐ *muwaqqar* = **respected**!).
- **Here it means:** "**What is wrong with you that you do not hold Allah in due reverence?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAQĀR* — DIGNITY / GRAVITY — from HEAVINESS.** ⭐ **To give someone *waqār* = to give them WEIGHT in your heart** — ⭐ **treat them as weighty, serious, to be reckoned with.** ⭐ **They gave Allah NO WEIGHT.** ⭐⭐ ***Lā tarjūna li'llāhi waqārā*** — ⭐ classical readings: ⭐ **(a) "you don't HOPE for any dignity from Allah"** (no hope of honour from Him); ⭐ **(b) "you don't FEAR Allah's majesty"** (*rajā* in negation = fear); ⭐ **(c) "you don't attribute to Allah the greatness He deserves."** ⭐ **All: they treated Him as WEIGHTLESS.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE ROOT'S HIDDEN LINK TO v.7:** ⭐ ***WAQR* fi'l-udhun = DEAFNESS** — **"heaviness in the ear"** — ⭐ **Fuṣṣilat 41:5** — *wa fī ādhāninā **waqr*** — "**in our ears is DEAFNESS**." ⭐ **v.7: they put FINGERS IN THEIR EARS.** ⭐⭐⭐ **They made their ears "heavy" (*waqr* — deaf) — and gave Allah no "weight" (*waqār* — reverence).** ⭐ **The same root: heavy ears, weightless God.** ⭐ **Deafness and disrespect, one root.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Mā lakum* — "WHAT'S WRONG with you?"** — ⭐ **astonished question** — ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:3 (man asks about the earth), Muddaththir 74:49, Maʿārij 70:36 (Allah asks about them).** ⭐ **Here Nūḥ asks his people.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***ar-rajāʾ* = "PLEASE"** (on signs); ⭐ ***tawqīr* = RESPECT**; ⭐ ***muwaqqar* = RESPECTED**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mā lakum*** + ***lā tarjūna*** (*ḥāl*: "**what's with you, not hoping…**"). ⭐ ***waqāran*** = object.
- **Why this exact word:** *waqār* (**weight → reverence**) — ⭐ **they gave Allah no weight, while making their own ears heavy.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAQĀR* = DIGNITY ← *waqr* = HEAVINESS — giving someone WEIGHT in your heart.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WAQR* in the ear = DEAFNESS (41:5) — they plugged their ears (v.7) and gave Allah no *waqār* (v.13).** ⭐ **Heavy ears, weightless God — one root.** ⭐⭐ ***Rajā* in negation can mean FEAR.** ⭐ ***Ar-rajāʾ* = "PLEASE"; *tawqīr* = RESPECT.** ⭐ ***Mā lakum*: 99:3, 74:49, 70:36.**

**20. وَقَدْ خَلَقَكُمْ أَطْوَارًا — *wa qad khalaqakum aṭwārā*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *qad* + past — ⭐ **"He has INDEED…"** (certainty). *aṭwār* — ⭐⭐ the "**STAGE / phase / boundary**" family — ***ṭawr* = a STAGE, a phase, a state** (⭐ also ***ṭawr* = a time / an occasion**); ⭐⭐ ***taṭawwur* = DEVELOPMENT / EVOLUTION** (modern!); ⭐ ***taṭwīr* = DEVELOPING / upgrading** (⭐ *taṭwīr al-barāmij* = **software development**!); ⭐ ***aṭwār* = stages**.
- **Here it means:** "**…when He created you in stages?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE EMBRYO IN ONE WORD.** ⭐ ***Aṭwārā* = in STAGES** — ⭐ classical: **drop (*nuṭfah*) → clot (*ʿalaqah*) → lump (*muḍghah*) → bones → flesh → a new creation** (*al-Muʾminūn 23:12–14*) — ⭐ **and then infancy → childhood → youth → maturity → old age.** ⭐⭐ **And the argument: ⭐ "He took you through STAGES you didn't choose — and you don't give Him *waqār* (v.13)?"** ⭐ **The proof of His power is written in your own body's history.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The CREATION-STAGES thread across your surahs:** ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:2** — *khalaqa'l-insāna min **ʿalaq*** (from a CLINGING clot). ⭐ **Insān 76:2** — *min nuṭfatin **amshāj*** (from a MIXED drop). ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:19** — *min nuṭfatin khalaqahu **fa-qaddarah*** (from a drop — then PROPORTIONED). ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:37–39** — *nuṭfah → ʿalaqah → fa-khalaqa fa-sawwā*. ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:6** — *māʾin dāfiq*. ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:14** — ***aṭwārā*** — **all the stages in ONE WORD.** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:39** — *mimmā yaʿlamūn* (from what they KNOW).
  ⭐ Modern hook: ⭐ ***taṭawwur* = DEVELOPMENT** — ⭐ ***taṭwīr al-barāmij* = SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT** — ⭐ **you are His "development," stage by stage.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa qad*** = **a circumstantial clause (*ḥāl*)** — "**WHEN [in fact] He created you…**" ⭐ ***aṭwāran*** = *ḥāl* (in stages).
- **Why this exact word:** *aṭwār* — ⭐ **compresses the entire embryology and life-cycle into a single argument.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AṬWĀRĀ* = in STAGES — the embryo (*nuṭfah → ʿalaqah → muḍghah → bones → flesh*) + the life-cycle, in ONE WORD.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Creation thread: *ʿalaq* (96:2), *amshāj* (76:2), *qaddarah* (80:19), *ʿalaqah* (75:38), *dāfiq* (86:6), *AṬWĀR* (71:14).** ⭐⭐ ***TAṬAWWUR* = DEVELOPMENT; *taṭwīr al-barāmij* = SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT.** ⭐ **He developed you — give Him *waqār*.**

**21. أَلَمْ تَرَوْا كَيْفَ خَلَقَ اللَّهُ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ طِبَاقًا — *a-lam taraw kayfa khalaqa'llāhu sabʿa samāwātin ṭibāqā*** · v.15
- **Root feelings:** *a-lam taraw* — ⭐ **"Have you not SEEN…?"** (⭐ **Fajr 89:6, Fīl 105:1** — *a-lam tara kayfa…*). *ṭibāq* — ⭐⭐ the "**LAYERING / matching / covering**" family — *ṭabaqa* = to cover, to fit exactly over; ⭐ ***ṭabaq* = a LAYER, a STAGE — and a PLATE / dish** (⭐ everyday: *ṭabaq* = **a plate** of food!; *aṭbāq* = **dishes**); ⭐⭐ ***ṭābiq* = a FLOOR / STOREY of a building** (⭐ *aṭ-ṭābiq ath-thālith* = **the third floor**!); ⭐ ***ṭabaqah* = a CLASS / social stratum** (⭐ *ṭabaqah wusṭā* = **middle class**!); ⭐ ***muṭābiq* = MATCHING / identical**; ⭐ ***taṭbīq* = an APPLICATION** (⭐ **an APP** on your phone!).
- **Here it means:** "**Have you not seen how Allah created seven heavens in layers?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE HEAVENS AS FLOORS OF A BUILDING.** ⭐ ***Ṭibāqā* = in LAYERS, one above another** — ⭐ **like the STOREYS (*ṭawābiq*) of a tower, or stacked PLATES.** ⭐ **And *muṭābiq* = MATCHING** — ⭐ classical: **layered AND fitting perfectly, each matching the next** — ⭐ **no gaps, no flaws** (⭐ **Mulk 67:3** — *alladhī khalaqa sabʿa samāwātin **ṭibāqā** mā tarā fī khalqi'r-raḥmāni min tafāwut* — "**you see no FLAW**"!).
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE INSHIQĀQ CONNECTION:** ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:19** — *la-tarkabunna **ṭabaqan ʿan ṭabaq*** — ⭐ **"you will surely ride STAGE after STAGE"** (layer upon layer of your life's journey). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:15** — *sabʿa samāwātin **ṭibāqā*** — ⭐ **seven heavens LAYER upon LAYER.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And v.14 — *aṭwārā* (STAGES of YOU) → v.15 — *ṭibāqā* (LAYERS of the HEAVENS).** ⭐ **Your stages (*aṭwār*) and the sky's layers (*ṭibāq*) — side by side.** ⭐ **The same Designer layered both.**
  ⭐⭐ ***A-lam taraw kayfa* — "have you not SEEN HOW"** — ⭐ **Fajr 89:6** — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka bi-ʿĀd*; ⭐ **Fīl 105:1** — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka bi-aṣḥābi'l-fīl*. ⭐ **There: see how He DESTROYED; here: see how He CREATED.** ⭐ **The same formula for His power to make and to unmake.**
  ⭐ Modern hooks: ⭐ ***ṭābiq* = a FLOOR**; ⭐ ***ṭabaqah wusṭā* = MIDDLE CLASS**; ⭐ ***taṭbīq* = an APP**; ⭐ ***ṭabaq* = a PLATE**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***a-lam*** + jussive (*taraw*, nūn dropped). ⭐ ***ṭibāqan*** = *ḥāl* or adjective (verbal noun of *ṭābaqa*, Form III — "matching layers").
- **Why this exact word:** *ṭibāq* — ⭐ **layers that also MATCH — order and perfection in one word.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṬIBĀQ* = LAYERS like the FLOORS (*ṭawābiq*) of a building — and *muṭābiq* = MATCHING (no flaw — Mulk 67:3).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:19: *ṭabaqan ʿan ṭabaq* (stage after stage).** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.14 *AṬWĀR* (your STAGES) → v.15 *ṬIBĀQ* (the sky's LAYERS).** ⭐⭐ ***A-lam tara kayfa*: see how He DESTROYED (89:6, 105:1) / how He CREATED (71:15).** ⭐ ***Ṭabaq* = a PLATE; *taṭbīq* = an APP; *ṭabaqah wusṭā* = MIDDLE CLASS.**

**22. وَجَعَلَ الْقَمَرَ فِيهِنَّ نُورًا وَجَعَلَ الشَّمْسَ سِرَاجًا — *wa jaʿala'l-qamara fīhinna nūran wa jaʿala'sh-shamsa sirājā*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *qamar* — **the MOON**. *nūr* — ⭐ the "**LIGHT (gentle, reflected)**" family — ***nūr* = LIGHT** (⭐ ***an-Nūr*** = a Name of Allah; ⭐ ***munawwar* = ILLUMINATED** — *al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah*!). *shams* — **the SUN**. *sirāj* — ⭐⭐ **a LAMP — one that BURNS and produces its own light** (⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:13** — *wa jaʿalnā **sirājan wahhājā***!; ⭐ *sirāj munīr* — the Prophet ﷺ, 33:46!; ⭐ ***sirāj* = an oil LAMP** that burns its own fuel).
- **Here it means:** "**And made the moon therein a light, and made the sun a lamp.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE PRECISE DISTINCTION — and it's astronomically exact.** ⭐ **MOON = *NŪR* (light)** — ⭐ **a gentle, REFLECTED light** (it has no fire of its own). ⭐ **SUN = *SIRĀJ* (a LAMP)** — ⭐ **a BURNING source that PRODUCES its own light and heat.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān never calls the moon a *sirāj* or the sun merely *nūr*** — ⭐ **one SOURCES light, the other RECEIVES and REFLECTS it.** ⭐ ⭐ **Compare Yūnus 10:5** — *jaʿala'sh-shamsa **ḍiyāʾan** wa'l-qamara **nūrā*** — ⭐ **the sun = *ḍiyāʾ* (radiance, from its own source), the moon = *nūr* (reflected light).** ⭐ **Consistent across the Qurʾān.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And SHAMS 91:2 — *wa'l-qamari idhā TALĀHĀ*** — ⭐ **"the moon when it FOLLOWS it [the sun]"** — ⭐ **I noted there: the moon follows / recites the sun, transmitting light it didn't make.** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:16 states it outright: moon = *nūr*, sun = *sirāj*.** ⭐⭐ **And Nabaʾ 78:13** — *wa jaʿalnā **sirājan wahhājā*** — ⭐ **"a BLAZING LAMP"** — ⭐ **the sun as *sirāj* in both surahs.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the Prophet ﷺ himself is called *sirājan munīrā* (33:46) — "a LIGHT-GIVING LAMP"** — ⭐ **a source of guidance, not a reflection.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Fīhinna* — "IN THEM" (the heavens)** — ⭐ classical: **the moon is in the nearest heaven, but "in them" = in their collective set** (like saying "in the house" for one room).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ two parallel clauses (*jaʿala X Y*) — ***jaʿala*** takes two objects. ⭐ ***fīhinna*** = feminine plural pronoun (the heavens).
- **Why this exact word:** *nūr* vs *sirāj* — ⭐ **a precise physical distinction between reflected and generated light.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **MOON = *NŪR* (REFLECTED light). SUN = *SIRĀJ* (a burning LAMP — its OWN light).** ⭐ **Never swapped in the Qurʾān.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Shams 91:2: the moon FOLLOWS (*talāhā*) the sun — transmitting borrowed light.** ⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:13: *sirājan wahhājā*. Yūnus 10:5: sun = *ḍiyāʾ*, moon = *nūr*.** ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ = *sirājan munīrā* (33:46).** ⭐ ***Al-Madīnah al-MUNAWWARAH*.**

**23. وَاللَّهُ أَنبَتَكُم مِّنَ الْأَرْضِ نَبَاتًا — *wa'llāhu anbatakum mina'l-arḍi nabātā*** · v.17
- **Root feelings:** *anbata* — ⭐ the "**SPROUTING / growing (plants)**" family — *nabata* = to sprout; ⭐ ***anbata* (Form IV) = to make GROW**; ⭐ ***nabāt* = PLANTS, vegetation** (⭐ ***nabātī* = VEGETARIAN / VEGETABLE**! — *zayt nabātī* = **vegetable oil**!); ⭐ ***manbat* = a place of growth / origin**; ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:15** — *li-nukhrija bihi ḥabban wa **nabātā***; ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:27** — *fa-**anbatnā** fīhā ḥabbā*.
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah caused you to grow from the earth like plants.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **HUMANS GROWN LIKE PLANTS.** ⭐ ***Anbatakum… nabātā*** — ⭐ **"He made YOU sprout — a sprouting"** — ⭐ **as if you were a CROP.** ⭐ Classical: **Ādam from earth; and every human's body built from the earth's produce** (food → blood → the drop → you). ⭐⭐ **You are, literally, grown from soil.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **A GRAMMAR GEM — MIXED FORMS:** ⭐ **The verb is Form IV (*anbata*, "caused to grow")** — ⭐ **but the cognate accusative is the Form I noun (*nabāt*, "growing / plants")** — ⭐ **not the expected Form IV maṣdar *inbāt*.** ⭐ **Classical explanation: the mixture means BOTH — "He caused you to grow, and you grew like plants grow" — combining His action and your natural growth.** ⭐⭐⭐ **You've seen this EXACT device: Muzzammil 73:8 — *wa TABATTAL ilayhi TABTĪLĀ*** — ⭐ **Form V verb + Form II maṣdar.** ⭐ **Two surahs, the same deliberate mixing of forms to double the meaning.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Nabāt* — the plant thread:** ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:15** (*ḥabban wa nabātā* — grain and **plants**), ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:27–31** (*fa-anbatnā fīhā ḥabbā…* — We **made grow** grain, grapes, olives…), ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:17** — ***anbatakum*** — ⭐ **"We made YOU grow."** ⭐⭐⭐ **ʿAbasa: He grows your FOOD. Nūḥ: He grows YOU.**
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***nabātī* = VEGETARIAN**; ⭐ ***zayt nabātī* = VEGETABLE OIL**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***anbatakum*** (Form IV) + ***nabātan*** (Form I maṣdar) = ⭐ **mixed-form cognate accusative** (like 73:8). ⭐ ***wa'llāhu*** fronted (emphasis: **ALLAH** did this).
- **Why this exact word:** *nabāt* — ⭐ **pictures human life as plant life — dependent, grown, and returning to the soil (v.18).**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **HUMANS GROWN LIKE PLANTS — from soil, through food, to you.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ANBATA* (Form IV) + *NABĀTĀ* (Form I noun) = MIXED FORMS — like Muzzammil 73:8 *tabattal tabtīlā*.** ⭐⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:27: He grows your FOOD. Nūḥ 71:17: He grows YOU.** ⭐ ***Nabātī* = VEGETARIAN; *zayt nabātī* = VEGETABLE OIL.**

**24. ثُمَّ يُعِيدُكُمْ فِيهَا وَيُخْرِجُكُمْ إِخْرَاجًا — *thumma yuʿīdukum fīhā wa yukhrijukum ikhrājā*** · v.18
- **Root feelings:** *yuʿīdu* — ⭐ the "**RETURNING / repeating**" family — *ʿāda* = to return; ⭐ ***aʿāda* = to RETURN something, to REPEAT** (⭐ ***iʿādah* = a REPLAY / repetition**; ⭐ ***ʿīd* = a FESTIVAL** — the day that RETURNS each year!; ⭐ ***ʿādah* = a HABIT** — what you return to!; ⭐ ***al-Muʿīd* = the Restorer — a Name of Allah**). *yukhriju* — ⭐ **to BRING OUT** (⭐ ***ikhrāj* = EXTRACTION** — and ⭐ ***ikhrāj* = DIRECTING (a film)**!; ⭐ ***mukhrij* = a FILM DIRECTOR**!).
- **Here it means:** "**Then He will return you into it, and bring you out [again] — a bringing out.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE COMPLETE CYCLE: grown FROM the earth (v.17) → returned INTO it (v.18a) → brought OUT of it again (v.18b).** ⭐ ***Minhā khalaqnākum wa fīhā nuʿīdukum wa minhā nukhrijukum tāratan ukhrā*** (20:55) — ⭐ **"from it We created you, into it We return you, from it We bring you out again."** ⭐⭐ **The plant image continues:** ⭐ **a seed goes into the soil and comes out as new growth** — ⭐ **resurrection pictured as a second sprouting.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ikhrājā* — the cognate accusative** — ⭐ **"bring you out with a BRINGING OUT"** — ⭐ **emphatic: a REAL, certain emergence.** ⭐ ⭐ **And the *kh-r-j* thread:** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:43** — *yawma **yakhrujūna** mina'l-ajdāthi sirāʿā* (the Day they COME OUT of the graves rushing) — ⭐ **the previous surah describes the very event Nūḥ promises.** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:31** — *akhraja minhā māʾahā wa marʿāhā* (brought out its water and pasture). ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:2** — *wa akhrajati'l-arḍu athqālahā* (the earth brought out its burdens).
  ⭐⭐ ***Yuʿīdukum* — RETURN** — ⭐ ***ʿĪD* = a FESTIVAL** (the day that comes BACK each year) — ⭐ **and *al-Muʿīd* (the Restorer)** — ⭐ **Burūj 85:13** — *innahu huwa yubdiʾu **wa yuʿīd*** — "**He originates AND RESTORES**." ⭐ **Same verb.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***mukhrij* = a FILM DIRECTOR** (*ikhrāj* = directing — "bringing out" a film!); ⭐ ***ʿādah* = a HABIT**; ⭐ ***iʿādah* = a REPLAY**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***thumma*** = sequence. ⭐ ***ikhrājan*** = **cognate accusative** (Form IV maṣdar — ⚠️ **here the verb and maṣdar MATCH**, unlike v.17).
- **Why this exact word:** *iʿādah* + *ikhrāj* — ⭐ **the burial and the resurrection, completing the plant-cycle of v.17.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE CYCLE: GROWN from earth (v.17) → RETURNED into it (v.18a) → BROUGHT OUT again (v.18b) — resurrection as a second sprouting.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Maʿārij 70:43: *yakhrujūna mina'l-ajdāth* — the very event.** ⭐⭐ ***Yuʿīd* — Burūj 85:13 *yubdiʾu wa yuʿīd*; *ʿĪD* = the festival that RETURNS.** ⭐ ***Mukhrij* = a FILM DIRECTOR; *ʿādah* = a HABIT.** ⚠️ **Here verb + maṣdar MATCH (unlike v.17).**

**25. وَاللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ الْأَرْضَ بِسَاطًا — *wa'llāhu jaʿala lakumu'l-arḍa bisāṭā*** · v.19
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**SPREADING OUT / flattening / simplicity**" family — *basaṭa* = **to spread out, to stretch flat**; ⭐ ***bisāṭ* = a CARPET, a rug** (⭐ *bisāṭ aḥmar* = **the RED CARPET**!); ⭐⭐ ***basīṭ* = SIMPLE** (everyday!); ⭐ ***al-Bāsiṭ* = the Expander (a Name of Allah)**; ⭐ ***inbisāṭ* = CHEERFULNESS / ease** (feeling "spread out"!); ⭐ ***basṭ* = the numerator of a fraction** (math!).
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah made the earth for you a spread-out carpet.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE EARTH AS A CARPET.** ⭐ ***Bisāṭā*** — ⭐ **a CARPET rolled out for a guest** — ⭐ **flat enough to walk, sit, build, farm.** ⭐ **Hospitality: the host lays the carpet before the guest arrives.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The "earth spread out" thread — now FOUR images:** ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:6** — *a-lam najʿali'l-arḍa **mihādā*** — **a CRADLE** (soft bed). ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:20** — *wa ila'l-arḍi kayfa **suṭiḥat*** — **SURFACED / flattened** (⭐ *saṭḥ* = a roof!). ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:30** — *wa'l-arḍa baʿda dhālika **daḥāhā*** — **SPREAD out** (like an egg / an ostrich nest). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:19** — ***bisāṭā*** — **a CARPET.** ⭐⭐ **Cradle, roof-surface, spread nest, carpet — four homely images for one planet.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Basīṭ* = SIMPLE** — ⭐ **the earth is laid out "simply," flat to the walker** — ⭐ **and *al-Bāsiṭ* = He who EXPANDS** (provision, hearts — paired with *al-Qābiḍ*, who constricts).
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***bisāṭ aḥmar* = RED CARPET**; ⭐ ***basīṭ* = SIMPLE**; ⭐ ***inbisāṭ* = CHEERFULNESS**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa'llāhu*** fronted (emphasis — the second time, v.17 and v.19). ⭐ ***jaʿala*** + two objects (*al-arḍa*, *bisāṭan*).
- **Why this exact word:** *bisāṭ* — ⭐ **domestic, welcoming: the earth as a host's carpet, laid for you.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BISĀṬ* = a CARPET — the earth rolled out like a host's rug.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Earth images: CRADLE (*mihād*, 78:6), SURFACED (*suṭiḥat*, 88:20), SPREAD (*daḥāhā*, 79:30), CARPET (*bisāṭ*, 71:19).** ⭐⭐ ***BASĪṬ* = SIMPLE; *bisāṭ aḥmar* = RED CARPET; *al-Bāsiṭ* = the Expander.** ⭐ ***Inbisāṭ* = CHEERFULNESS.**

**26. لِّتَسْلُكُوا مِنْهَا سُبُلًا فِجَاجًا — *li-taslukū minhā subulan fijājā*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *taslukū* — ⭐⭐ the "**THREADING / travelling a path**" family — *salaka* = to thread, to travel along; ⭐ ***silk* = a WIRE / thread** (⭐ ***lā-silkī* = WIRELESS**!); ⭐ ***maslak* = a PATH / track**; ⭐ ***sulūk* = BEHAVIOUR**. *subul* — ⭐ **plural of *sabīl* = ROADS, ways** (⭐ ***ibn as-sabīl* = a TRAVELLER**; ⭐ *fī sabīli'llāh*). *fijāj* — ⭐ **plural of *fajj* = a WIDE MOUNTAIN PASS, a broad valley-road** (⭐ **Ḥajj 22:27** — *min kulli **fajjin** ʿamīq* — "**from every DEEP mountain pass**" — the pilgrims!).
- **Here it means:** "**…so that you may travel along it on wide roads.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *SALAKA* THREAD — and here, at last, its MERCIFUL use.** ⭐ **You've seen *salaka* as CONFINEMENT and PUNISHMENT:** ⭐ **Jinn 72:17** — *yaslukhu ʿadhāban ṣaʿadā* (THREADED into a climbing punishment); ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:42** — *mā salakakum fī saqar* (what THREADED you into Saqar?); ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:32** — *fī silsilatin… fa'slukūhu* (THREAD him through the chain). ⭐ **And once as PROTECTION:** **Jinn 72:27** — *yasluku… raṣadā* (guards threaded around revelation). ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW: Nūḥ 71:20 — *li-TASLUKŪ minhā subulan fijājā* — "so you may TRAVEL along WIDE roads."** ⭐ **The same verb: threaded through a chain (punishment) vs travelling broad roads (blessing).** ⭐ **Narrow passages for the deniers; WIDE ones for you — if you're grateful.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Fijāj* — WIDE mountain passes** — ⭐ **the opposite of a needle's eye!** ⭐ **Ḥajj 22:27** — ***min kulli fajjin ʿamīq*** — ⭐ **the pilgrims come "from every DEEP mountain PASS"** — ⭐ **same word.** ⭐ **The roads Allah opened through the mountains are the roads pilgrims walk to His House.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Subul* — ROADS** — ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:19** — *fa-man shāʾa'ttakhadha ilā rabbihi **sabīlā*** — ⭐ **ONE road to your Lord; many roads (*subul*) across the earth.**
  ⭐ ⭐ **The carpet (v.19) has ROADS on it (v.20)** — ⭐ **a furnished home, with pathways.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-*** + subjunctive (*taslukū*, nūn dropped) = purpose. ⭐ ***subulan fijājan*** — noun + adjective (both plural).
- **Why this exact word:** *salaka* + *fijāj* — ⭐ **the confinement-verb of the punishment surahs, turned into the freedom of wide roads.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SALAKA* — THREADED into punishment (72:17, 74:42, 69:32) → here: TRAVEL along WIDE roads (71:20).** ⭐ **Narrow chains for deniers; WIDE passes for the grateful.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FIJĀJ* = WIDE mountain PASSES — Ḥajj 22:27 *min kulli fajjin ʿamīq* (the pilgrims' roads).** ⭐⭐ ***Ibn as-sabīl* = a TRAVELLER; *lā-silkī* = WIRELESS.** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:19: ONE *sabīl* to your Lord.**

## §5 — Their Response (v.21–24)

**27. قَالَ نُوحٌ رَّبِّ إِنَّهُمْ عَصَوْنِي — *qāla nūḥun rabbi innahum ʿaṣawnī*** · v.21
- **Root feeling:** *ʿaṣaw* — ⭐ the "**DISOBEYING**" family — *ʿaṣā* = to disobey (⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:21**, **Muzzammil 73:16** — Pharaoh *ʿaṣā*; ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:10** — *fa-ʿaṣaw rasūla rabbihim*; ⭐ **Jinn 72:23** — *wa man yaʿṣi'llāha*); ⭐ ***ʿaṣā* = a STAFF** (same letters!); ⭐ ***maʿṣiyah* = a SIN / act of disobedience**; ⭐ ***ʿiṣyān* = REBELLION / mutiny** (modern!).
- **Here it means:** "**Nūḥ said: 'My Lord, indeed they have disobeyed me…'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The report resumes — after the signs (v.13–20), back to the verdict.** ⭐ ***ʿAṣawnī* — "they DISOBEYED ME"** — ⭐ **answering v.3: *wa aṭīʿūn*** ("and OBEY me"). ⭐⭐⭐ **v.3 *AṬĪʿŪN* (obey me) → v.21 *ʿAṢAWNĪ* (they disobeyed me).** ⭐ **The command and its refusal, bookending the argument.** ⭐ ⭐ **The *ʿaṣā* thread: Pharaoh (79:21, 73:16), the nations (69:10), whoever (72:23), Nūḥ's people (71:21).** ⭐ ***ʿIṣyān* = REBELLION / mutiny.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Qāla NŪḤUN*** — ⭐ **his NAME, in the third person — after speaking in the first person (v.5–20)** — ⭐ **a narrative shift (*iltifāt*): the narrator re-introduces him for the final section.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿaṣawnī*** = *ʿaṣaw* + *-nī* (me). ⭐ ***rabbi*** = vocative, *yāʾ* dropped.
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿaṣā* — ⭐ **answers v.3's *aṭīʿūn* directly.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.3 *AṬĪʿŪN* (OBEY me) → v.21 *ʿAṢAWNĪ* (they DISOBEYED me).** ⭐⭐ ***ʿAṣā* thread: Pharaoh (79:21, 73:16), nations (69:10), Jinn 72:23, Nūḥ's people (71:21).** ⭐ ***ʿIṣyān* = MUTINY; *ʿaṣā* = a STAFF.**

**28. وَاتَّبَعُوا مَن لَّمْ يَزِدْهُ مَالُهُ وَوَلَدُهُ إِلَّا خَسَارًا — *wa'ttabaʿū man lam yazidhu māluhu wa waladuhu illā khasārā*** · v.21
- **Root feelings:** *ittabaʿū* — ⭐ **Form VIII of *tabiʿa*** = **to FOLLOW** (⭐ ***tābiʿ* = a FOLLOWER**; ⭐ ***at-tābiʿūn*** = the generation after the Companions!; ⭐ ***mutābaʿah* = FOLLOW-UP**!). *māl* / *walad* — **WEALTH / CHILDREN**. *khasār* — ⭐ the "**LOSS — trade loss**" family — *khasira*; ⭐ ***khusr*** (**ʿAṣr 103:2**!); ⭐ ***khasārah* = financial LOSS**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and followed the one whose wealth and children increased him only in loss.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THEY FOLLOWED THE RICH — not the prophet.** ⭐ ***Man lam yazidhu māluhu wa waladuhu illā khasārā*** — ⭐ **the chiefs, the wealthy, the men with many sons** — ⭐ **whose wealth and children brought them only LOSS.** ⭐⭐ **The people chose the rich man's way over the messenger's way** — ⭐ **because wealth LOOKS like success.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE IRONY WITH v.12:** ⭐ **v.12 — Nūḥ PROMISED: *yumdidkum bi-AMWĀLIN wa BANĪN*** — ⭐ **istighfār would bring wealth and sons as BLESSING.** ⭐ **v.21 — they FOLLOWED a man whose *MĀLUHU wa WALADUHU* brought only LOSS.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Wealth and children WITH Allah = blessing; WITHOUT Him = loss.** ⭐ **Same goods, opposite outcomes.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And *KHASĀR* — ʿAṢR 103:2** — *inna'l-insāna la-fī **khusr*** — ⭐ **every human is in LOSS (by default, as time drains).** ⭐ **Here: their wealth and children only ADDED to the loss.** ⭐⭐ **And the "*mā aghnā*" thread:** ⭐ **Layl 92:11** (*mā yughnī ʿanhu māluhu*), **Masad 111:2** (*mā aghnā ʿanhu māluhu wa mā kasab* — "what he EARNED" = his **children**!), **Ḥāqqah 69:28** (*mā aghnā ʿannī māliyah*). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:21 goes further: wealth didn't just fail to help — it INCREASED the loss.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the *zāda… illā* pattern — #2 of 4** (v.6 *firārā*, v.21 *khasārā*, v.24 *ḍalālā*, v.28 *tabārā*).
  ⭐ ⭐ **And Muddaththir 74:12–13** — al-Walīd's ***mālan mamdūdā wa banīna shuhūdā*** — ⭐ **exactly the type they followed.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** = "**the one who**" (relative). ⭐ ***lam yazid*** — jussive, hollow (long *ī* shortened). ⭐ ***waladuhu*** — ⚠️ **recitation variant *wulduhu*** (plural sense — "his children").
- **Why this exact word:** *khasār* — ⭐ **a trade word: wealth that should have profited, instead compounding the loss.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They FOLLOWED THE RICH — whose wealth and children brought only LOSS.** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.12 PROMISED: *amwāl + banīn* as BLESSING (with Allah). v.21: *māl + walad* = only LOSS (without Him).** ⭐⭐ ***KHASĀR* = ʿAṢR 103:2 *khusr*.** ⭐⭐ **Wealth thread: won't avail (92:11, 111:2, 69:28) → here it ADDS to loss.** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:12–13: al-Walīd = the type they followed.** ⭐ ***Zāda… illā* #2.**

**29. وَمَكَرُوا مَكْرًا كُبَّارًا — *wa makarū makran kubbārā*** · v.22
- **Root feelings:** *makarū* — ⭐ the "**SCHEMING / cunning plotting**" family — *makara* = to plot craftily, to scheme; ⭐ ***makr* = a SCHEME, a cunning plot**; ⭐ ***makkār* = CRAFTY / sly**; ⭐ ***wa makarū wa makara'llāh*** (3:54) — they plotted, and Allah plotted. *kubbār* — ⭐⭐⭐ **the *faʿʿāl* intensive of *kabīr*** = **ENORMOUSLY, HUGELY great** — ⭐ **the GRADED ladder of "big": *kabīr* (big) → *kubār* (very big) → *kubbār* (ENORMOUS!)**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and they plotted a tremendously great plot.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KUBBĀR* — a RARE intensive: the "BIGGEST big."** ⭐ **Arabic grades bigness:** ⭐ ***kabīr*** = big ⭐ ***kubār*** = very big (*fuʿāl*) ⭐ ***kubbār*** = ENORMOUSLY, overwhelmingly big (*fuʿʿāl*). ⭐⭐ **The plot was the BIGGEST possible** — ⭐ classical: **inciting the masses against Nūḥ, mocking him, threatening his followers, and organizing the whole society to cling to the idols** (v.23 is the plot in words).
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Makr / kayd* — the plotting thread:** ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:15–16** — *innahum yakīdūna **kaydā** · wa akīdu kaydā* (they plot; I plot). ⭐ **Fīl 105:2** — *a-lam yajʿal **kaydahum** fī taḍlīl* (their plot sent astray — Abrahah). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:22** — *wa makarū **makran kubbārā*** (an ENORMOUS plot). ⭐⭐ **Abrahah's plot became a lost camel; Nūḥ's people's ENORMOUS plot ended under the flood.** ⭐ **The bigger the plot, the bigger the fall.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the *k-b-r* root — Muddaththir:** ⭐ **v.3 *fa-kabbir* (MAGNIFY Allah), v.23 *istakbara* (made himself great), v.35 *iḥda'l-kubar* (one of the GREAT calamities).** ⭐ **And Nūḥ 71:7 — *istakbarū'stikbārā*** (arrogance), ⭐ **71:22 — *makran kubbārā*** (an ENORMOUS plot). ⭐ **Arrogance (*istikbār*) breeds enormous plots (*makr kubbār*).**
  ⭐ ⭐ **Cognate accusative *makarū makran*** — **"plotted a plot"** + the adjective ***kubbārā***.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***kubbāran*** = **the *fuʿʿāl* intensive** (rarer than *faʿʿāl*) — **maximum bigness**. ⭐ ***makran*** = cognate accusative.
- **Why this exact word:** *kubbār* — ⭐ **the rarest, biggest form of "big," matching the scale of their opposition.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KABĪR* (big) → *KUBĀR* (very big) → *KUBBĀR* (ENORMOUS) — Arabic GRADES bigness.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Plot thread: Ṭāriq 86:15 (*kayd*), Fīl 105:2 (Abrahah's plot → a LOST CAMEL), Nūḥ 71:22 (*makr KUBBĀR* → the FLOOD).** ⭐ **The bigger the plot, the bigger the fall.** ⭐⭐ ***K-b-r*: *istakbarū* (v.7, arrogance) → *kubbārā* (v.22, an enormous plot).** ⭐ ***Makkār* = CRAFTY.**

**30. وَقَالُوا لَا تَذَرُنَّ آلِهَتَكُمْ — *wa qālū lā tadharunna ālihatakum*** · v.23
- **Root feelings:** *tadharunna* — ⭐⭐ **the DEFECTIVE verb *wadhara*** (to leave — no past tense in use!) + ⭐ **the HEAVY *nūn* of emphasis** (⭐ **Takāthur 102:6** *la-tarawunna*; ⭐ **Humazah 104:4** *la-yunbadhanna*). *ālihah* — ⭐ **GODS** (plural of *ilāh* — ⭐ **Nās 114:3** *ilāhi'n-nās*!).
- **Here it means:** "**And they said: 'Never abandon your gods…'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE PLOT IN WORDS — and the *dhar* verb completes its arc.** ⭐ ***Lā TADHARUNNA*** — ⭐ **"Do NOT — EVER — LEAVE your gods!"** — ⭐ **the heavy *nūn* makes it a sworn, desperate command.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *DHAR* THREAD — now FIVE uses:** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:11** — *wa **dharnī** wa'l-mukadhdhibīn* (Allah: "LEAVE ME with the deniers"). ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:11** — ***dharnī** wa man khalaqtu waḥīdā* (Allah: "LEAVE ME with him"). ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:28** — *lā tubqī **wa lā tadhar*** (Saqar LEAVES nothing). ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:42** — ***fa-dharhum** yakhūḍū* (Allah: "LEAVE THEM to wade"). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:23** — ***lā tadharunna** ālihatakum* (the chiefs: "NEVER LEAVE your gods!"). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:26–27** — ***lā tadhar** ʿala'l-arḍ… in **tadharhum*** (Nūḥ: "LEAVE no dweller… if You LEAVE them"). ⭐⭐⭐ **The chiefs said "never LEAVE your gods" — and Nūḥ prayed "LEAVE not one of them on earth."** ⭐ **The same verb, thrown back.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Āliha* — GODS (plural)** — ⭐ **the opposite of NĀS 114:3's *ILĀHI'n-nās* (THE God of mankind — singular)** ⭐ **and IKHLĀṢ 112:1 — *Allāhu aḥad*.** ⭐ **Many *āliha* vs ONE *Ilāh*.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Ālihatakum* — "YOUR gods"** — ⭐ **possessive, tribal: "OUR heritage, YOUR identity"** — ⭐ **the appeal to tradition** (⭐ *innā wajadnā ābāʾanā ʿalā ummah*, 43:22 — "we found our fathers on a way").
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***lā tadharunna*** = **prohibition + DEFECTIVE verb + HEAVY nūn** — ⭐ the plural *nūn* drops before the emphatic *nūn* (*tadharūna* → *tadharunna*). ⭐ ***ālihah*** = broken plural of *ilāh*.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ ***dhar* + heavy nūn — a desperate oath to cling to idols, answered by Nūḥ's *lā tadhar* (v.26).**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ TADHARUNNA* = "NEVER EVER LEAVE your gods!" — defective verb + HEAVY nūn.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHAR* thread: Allah "leave Me" (73:11, 74:11), Saqar "leaves nothing" (74:28), "leave them" (70:42), chiefs "never LEAVE your gods" (71:23) → Nūḥ "LEAVE not one" (71:26).** ⭐⭐ ***ĀLIHAH* (many gods) vs *ILĀH* (Nās 114:3) / *AḤAD* (Ikhlāṣ 112:1).** ⭐ **The appeal to TRADITION (43:22).**

**31. وَلَا تَذَرُنَّ وَدًّا وَلَا سُوَاعًا وَلَا يَغُوثَ وَيَعُوقَ وَنَسْرًا — *wa lā tadharunna waddan wa lā suwāʿan wa lā yaghūtha wa yaʿūqa wa nasrā*** · v.23
- **Root feelings:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FIVE IDOLS — each name has a meaning:** ⭐ ***Wadd*** — ⭐ **"LOVE"** (*wadda* = to love; ⭐ ***al-Wadūd* — Burūj 85:14**; ⭐ *mawaddah*; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:11** *yawaddu*). ⭐ ***Suwāʿ*** — ⭐ (name; meaning uncertain — classically linked to *sāʿah*, an hour / time). ⭐ ***Yaghūth*** — ⭐ **"HE HELPS / rescues"** (*ghāth* = to rescue; ⭐ ***ghawth* = RESCUE**; ⭐ ***istighāthah* = a CRY FOR HELP**; ⭐ ***ghayth* = RAIN** — the rescue from drought!). ⭐ ***Yaʿūq*** — ⭐ **"HE HINDERS / prevents"** (*ʿāqa* = to hinder; ⭐ ***ʿāʾiq* = an OBSTACLE**; ⭐ ***muʿawwaq* = DISABLED** — modern!; ⭐ ***iʿāqah* = DISABILITY**!). ⭐ ***Nasr*** — ⭐ **"VULTURE / EAGLE"** (⭐ *nasr* = vulture / eagle; ⭐ *an-Nasr al-Wāqiʿ* = the star **Vega**!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and never abandon Wadd, nor Suwāʿ, nor Yaghūth, Yaʿūq, and Nasr.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ORIGIN OF IDOLATRY — in five names.** ⭐ **Bukhārī (from Ibn ʿAbbās):** ⭐ **these were the names of RIGHTEOUS MEN among Nūḥ's people.** ⭐ **When they died, Shayṭān whispered to the people: "set up STATUES in their gathering-places and name them after them" — to remember them.** ⭐ **They did — but didn't worship them.** ⭐ **When THAT generation died and knowledge faded, ⭐ the next generation WORSHIPPED them.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Idolatry began as RESPECT for the righteous, turned into memorials, then into gods** — ⭐ **in a few generations.** ⭐ **That's why Islam guards so carefully against exaggerating reverence for the pious and against images in worship.**
  ⭐⭐ **And these idols survived to pre-Islamic Arabia** — ⭐ **Ibn ʿAbbās reported that the Arab tribes later worshipped idols with these same names: Wadd (Kalb tribe at Dūmat al-Jandal), Suwāʿ (Hudhayl), Yaghūth (Murād), Yaʿūq (Hamdān), Nasr (Ḥimyar).** ⭐ **Nūḥ's idols, inherited by the Prophet's ﷺ world.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE IRONY OF THE NAMES:** ⭐ ***WADD* = "LOVE" — but *al-WADŪD* (the Loving) is ALLAH** (Burūj 85:14). ⭐ ***YAGHŪTH* = "HE RESCUES" — but no idol rescued them from the flood** (⭐ v.25 — *falam yajidū lahum… anṣārā*). ⭐ ***YAʿŪQ* = "HE HINDERS" — ⭐ and the idol truly HINDERED them — from the truth.** ⭐ ***NASR* = a VULTURE** — ⭐ **the bird that feeds on the dead.** ⭐ **Names promising love, rescue, power — delivering none.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Yaghūth* → *ISTIGHĀTHAH* (a cry for help)** — ⭐ **in Islam, *istighāthah* belongs to Allah alone** (⭐ *idh tastaghīthūna rabbakum*, 8:9). ⭐ ***Ghayth* = RAIN** — ⭐ **v.11's *midrār* (rain) came from istighfār to Allah, not from Yaghūth.**
  ⭐ Modern hooks: ⭐ ***muʿawwaq* = DISABLED / *iʿāqah* = DISABILITY** (from *ʿāqa*, hinder); ⭐ ***istighāthah* = a CRY FOR HELP**; ⭐ ***nasr* = an EAGLE** (on several national emblems!).
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***Yaghūtha* and *Yaʿūqa* = DIPTOTES** (verb-shaped proper names — *wazn al-fiʿl*) — ⭐ **NO tanwīn** — ⭐ **while *Waddan*, *Suwāʿan*, *Nasran* HAVE tanwīn.** ⭐ **The repeated *wa lā* breaks off after *Yaghūth* — *wa Yaʿūqa wa Nasrā* (no more *lā*) — classical: rhythm / they were grouped.**
- **Why this exact word:** **naming the idols** — ⭐ **preserves the history of how idolatry began, as a warning for all time.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FIVE IDOLS = five RIGHTEOUS MEN → STATUES to remember them → WORSHIPPED by the next generation (Bukhārī, Ibn ʿAbbās).** ⭐ **Idolatry began as RESPECT.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WADD* = LOVE (but *al-WADŪD* is Allah, 85:14); *YAGHŪTH* = HE RESCUES (no rescue from the flood); *YAʿŪQ* = HE HINDERS (it did — from truth); *NASR* = a VULTURE.** ⭐⭐ **Survived into pre-Islamic Arabia.** ⭐ ***Muʿawwaq* = DISABLED; *istighāthah* = CRY FOR HELP; *ghayth* = RAIN.** ⚠️ ***Yaghūtha, Yaʿūqa* = DIPTOTES.**

**32. وَقَدْ أَضَلُّوا كَثِيرًا ۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ الظَّالِمِينَ إِلَّا ضَلَالًا — *wa qad aḍallū kathīrā · wa lā tazidi'ẓ-ẓālimīna illā ḍalālā*** · v.24
- **Root feelings:** *aḍallū* — ⭐ **Form IV of *ḍalla*** = **to LEAD ASTRAY** (⭐ ***ḍāllah* = a LOST CAMEL** — **Ḍuḥā 93:7**; ⭐ **Fīl 105:2** *taḍlīl*; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:31** *yuḍillu*). *kathīr* — **MANY** (*k-th-r* — ⭐ **Takāthur, Kawthar**!). *ẓālimīn* — ⭐ the "**DARKNESS / wrongdoing**" family — *ẓalama* = **to wrong, to put something in the wrong place**; ⭐ ***ẓulm* = INJUSTICE**; ⭐ ***ẓulmah* = DARKNESS** (same root!); ⭐ ***ẓālim* = a WRONGDOER / tyrant**. *ḍalāl* — **STRAYING / error**.
- **Here it means:** "**And they have led many astray. And [my Lord], increase the wrongdoers in nothing but straying.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO HALVES:** ⭐ **(a) *aḍallū kathīrā* — "they [the idols / the chiefs] have misled MANY"** — ⭐ **the five idols misled countless generations** (all the way to pre-Islamic Arabia!). ⭐ **(b) *wa lā tazidi'ẓ-ẓālimīna illā ḍalālā* — Nūḥ's first prayer AGAINST them:** ⭐ **"increase the wrongdoers only in straying."** ⭐⭐ **After 950 years, the prophet of patience finally prays against them** — ⭐ **because Allah told him: *lan yuʾmina min qawmika illā man qad āman*** (11:36 — "**none of your people will believe except those who already have**"). ⭐ **He prayed against them only once the door was confirmed shut.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ẓulm* (INJUSTICE) and *ẓulmah* (DARKNESS) — ONE ROOT** — ⭐ **the ḥadīth: "*Aẓ-ẓulmu ẓulumātun yawma'l-qiyāmah*" — ⭐ "INJUSTICE will be DARKNESSES on the Day of Resurrection."** ⭐ **Wrongdoing is darkness; the wrongdoer walks in the dark.** ⭐ ***Ẓalama* = putting a thing in the WRONG PLACE** — ⭐ **and the greatest *ẓulm* is *shirk* (31:13) — putting worship in the wrong place (in Wadd and Nasr).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Zāda… illā* #3: *ḌALĀLĀ*** — ⭐ **v.6 fleeing → v.21 loss → v.24 STRAYING → v.28 ruin.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ḍalla* thread:** ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:7** — *wa wajadaka **ḍāllan** fa-hadā* (He found you SEARCHING → guided). ⭐ **Fīl 105:2** — their plot sent **astray**. ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:31** — *yuḍillu man yashāʾ*. ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:24** — they **misled**, and stray further.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***wa qad*** + past = "**and indeed they have**." ⚠️⭐ ***lā tazidi*** = **jussive (prohibition/supplication) of a hollow verb** (*tazīd* → *tazid*), ⭐ **kasrah added before *aẓ-ẓālimīn*.** ⭐ **The shift from report ("they misled") to prayer ("increase them…") = *iltifāt*.**
- **Why this exact word:** ***ẓālimīn*** — ⭐ **names them by the darkness they chose, before praying they stay in it.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They MISLED many — the five idols, all the way to pre-Islamic Arabia.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nūḥ's FIRST prayer AGAINST them — only after Allah said "none will believe except those who have" (11:36).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ẒULM* (injustice) = *ẒULMAH* (DARKNESS) — "injustice will be DARKNESSES on that Day."** ⭐ **Greatest *ẓulm* = *shirk* (31:13).** ⭐⭐ ***Zāda… illā* #3: *ḌALĀLĀ*.** ⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:7 *ḍāllan*.**

## §6 — The End (v.25)

**33. مِّمَّا خَطِيئَاتِهِمْ أُغْرِقُوا — *mimmā khaṭīʾātihim ughriqū*** · v.25
- **Root feelings:** *mimmā* — ⭐ ***min* + *mā*** — ⚠️ **here the *mā* is EXTRA (*mā ṣilah / zāʾidah*) for EMPHASIS** — "**because of their sins — their very sins**." *khaṭīʾāt* — ⭐ **SINS** (deliberate — *khaṭiʾa*; ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:9, 37** — *al-khāṭiʾah, al-khāṭiʾūn*; ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:16** — *khāṭiʾah*). *ughriqū* — ⭐ the "**DROWNING / sinking**" family — *ghariqa* = to drown; ⭐ ***gharaq* = DROWNING**; ⭐ ***gharīq* = DROWNED** (⭐ *mustaghriq* = **absorbed / engrossed** — "drowned" in a task!; ⭐ ***istighrāq* = total absorption**); ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:1** — *wa'n-nāziʿāti **gharqā*** (pulling out souls **"drowningly"** — violently!).
- **Here it means:** "**Because of their sins they were drowned…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE FLOOD — in ONE WORD: *ughriqū* (they were DROWNED).** ⭐ **950 years of warning → one word of ending.** ⭐ **Compare Ḥāqqah 69:11** — *innā lammā **ṭagha'l-māʾu** ḥamalnākum fi'l-jāriyah* — ⭐ **the water OVERFLOWED (*ṭaghā*); the believers were CARRIED in the ship.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:11 shows the ARK side (saved); Nūḥ 71:25 shows the WATER side (drowned).** ⭐ **The same flood, from both sides, in surahs two apart.**
  ⭐⭐ ***MIMMĀ khaṭīʾātihim*** — ⭐ **"because of their SINS" — FRONTED** — ⭐ **the cause comes FIRST, before the punishment.** ⭐ **Emphasis: "it was their SINS — nothing else — that drowned them."** ⭐ **And the extra *mā* doubles the emphasis.** ⭐ **Not bad luck, not nature — their deliberate sins (*khaṭīʾāt*, Form I).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Gharqā* — Nāziʿāt 79:1** — ⭐ **the angels pulling out wicked souls "DROWNINGLY" (with violent force)** — ⭐ **and here, literal drowning.** ⭐ ***Mustaghriq* = ENGROSSED** — **"drowned" in something.**
  ⭐ **And PHARAOH was drowned too** (Muzzammil 73:16 *akhdhan wabīlā* — the "downpour" seizure). ⭐ **Two great drownings: Nūḥ's people and Pharaoh.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***mimmā*** = *min* + **extra *mā*** (*ṣilah* — emphasis). ⭐⭐ ***mimmā khaṭīʾātihim*** FRONTED before the verb = **restriction/emphasis on the cause**. ⭐ ***ughriqū*** = Form IV passive.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **fronting the SINS before the DROWNING — cause before effect, unmistakably.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***UGHRIQŪ* = they were DROWNED — 950 years of warning, one word of ending.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:11: the ARK side (CARRIED). Nūḥ 71:25: the WATER side (DROWNED).** ⭐ **Same flood, both sides.** ⭐⭐ ***MIMMĀ KHAṬĪʾĀTIHIM* FRONTED + extra *mā* = "because of their SINS — nothing else."** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:1 *gharqā*; *mustaghriq* = ENGROSSED.**

**34. فَأُدْخِلُوا نَارًا — *fa-udkhilū nārā*** · v.25
- **Root feelings:** *udkhilū* — ⭐ **they were MADE TO ENTER** (Form IV passive of *dakhala*). *nār* — **FIRE**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and then [immediately] made to enter a Fire.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WATER → then FIRE — and the *fa* is the evidence.** ⭐ ***Ughriqū FA-udkhilū nārā*** — ⭐ **"they were drowned, and THEN IMMEDIATELY (*fa*) made to enter a Fire."** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *fa* means IMMEDIATE sequence — no gap** — ⭐ **but the Day of Judgment is still to come!** ⭐ **So classical scholars cite this verse as evidence for punishment in the GRAVE / the *BARZAKH*** — ⭐ **the interval between death and resurrection.** ⭐ **They drowned in WATER and immediately entered FIRE.**
  ⭐⭐ **The paradox: drowning in water → burning in fire** — ⭐ **opposites, back to back** — ⭐ classical: **"they drowned in water and burned in fire" — a striking *ṭibāq*.** ⭐ ⭐ ***Nāran*** — **INDEFINITE** — ⭐ **"A fire" — unspecified, vast.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Udkhilū* — MADE TO ENTER (passive)** — ⭐ **contrast Fajr 89:29–30** — *fa'**dkhulī** fī ʿibādī · wa'**dkhulī** jannatī* — ⭐ **the content soul is INVITED to enter (active imperative); Nūḥ's people are FORCED to enter (passive).** ⭐ **And Maʿārij 70:38** — *an **yudkhala** jannata naʿīm* — ⭐ **the greedy COVETED being admitted to Paradise.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***fa*** = **immediate sequence** (*taʿqīb*) — ⭐ **the key to the barzakh reading.** ⭐ ***udkhilū*** = Form IV passive.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the *fa* compresses drowning and burning into one moment — and opens a window on the barzakh.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **DROWNED → *FA* (IMMEDIATELY) → FIRE.** ⭐ **The *fa* = no gap → evidence for punishment in the BARZAKH (the grave).** ⭐⭐ **Drowned in WATER, burned in FIRE — opposites back to back.** ⭐⭐ **Fajr 89:29–30: INVITED to enter (*udkhulī*). Nūḥ 71:25: FORCED to enter (*udkhilū*).** ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:38: COVETED being admitted.**

**35. فَلَمْ يَجِدُوا لَهُم مِّن دُونِ اللَّهِ أَنصَارًا — *fa-lam yajidū lahum min dūni'llāhi anṣārā*** · v.25
- **Root feelings:** *yajidū* — **they FOUND**. *min dūni'llāh* — ⭐ **"BESIDES Allah / apart from Allah"**. *anṣār* — ⭐ **HELPERS** (⭐ ***al-Anṣār*** of Madīnah; ⭐ **Naṣr 110:1** *naṣru'llāh*; ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:10** *wa lā nāṣir*; ⭐ **Jinn 72:24** *aḍʿafu nāṣirā*).
- **Here it means:** "**…and they found for themselves no helpers besides Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHERE WERE THE FIVE IDOLS?** ⭐ **v.23: "never abandon Wadd, Suwāʿ, Yaghūth, Yaʿūq, Nasr!"** ⭐ **v.25: "they found NO HELPERS."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAGHŪTH* — "HE RESCUES" — didn't rescue.** ⭐ **Not one idol lifted a finger as the water rose.** ⭐ **The names promised help; the reality was silence.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The *NĀṢIR* thread:** ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:10** — *fa-mā lahu min quwwatin **wa lā nāṣir*** (no strength, NO HELPER). ⭐ **Jinn 72:24** — *man **aḍʿafu nāṣiran** wa aqallu ʿadadā* (who is WEAKER in helpers). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:25** — ***lam yajidū lahum… anṣārā*** (found NO helpers). ⭐ **Naṣr 110:1** — *idhā jāʾa **naṣru'llāh*** (when ALLAH'S HELP comes). ⭐⭐⭐ **The deniers: NO *nāṣir*. The believers: *naṣr Allāh*.** ⭐ **Help is His alone to give.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Min dūni'llāh*** — ⭐ **"besides Allah"** — ⭐ **every helper except Him failed** — ⭐ **compare Jinn 72:22** — *wa lan ajida min dūnihi multaḥadā* (I'll find no REFUGE besides Him).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lam*** + jussive (*yajidū*, nūn dropped). ⭐ ***anṣāran*** = object (plural of *nāṣir*).
- **Why this exact word:** *anṣār* — ⭐ **the idols' entire purpose (help, rescue) shown to be empty.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHERE WERE THE IDOLS? *YAGHŪTH* ("he RESCUES") — didn't rescue.** ⭐ **NO helpers.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Nāṣir* thread: no helper (86:10), weaker in helpers (72:24), NO HELPERS (71:25) vs *NAṢR ALLĀH* (110:1).** ⭐ **Help is His alone.** ⭐ **Jinn 72:22: no refuge besides Him.**

## §7 — Nūḥ's Two Prayers (v.26–28)

**36. وَقَالَ نُوحٌ رَّبِّ لَا تَذَرْ عَلَى الْأَرْضِ مِنَ الْكَافِرِينَ دَيَّارًا — *wa qāla nūḥun rabbi lā tadhar ʿala'l-arḍi mina'l-kāfirīna dayyārā*** · v.26
- **Root feelings:** *lā tadhar* — ⭐ **"do not LEAVE"** (the **defective verb *wadhara*** — again!). *dayyār* — ⭐⭐ the "**DWELLING / house**" family — ***dār* = a HOUSE, an abode** (⭐ ***dār al-ākhirah*** = the Abode of the Hereafter; ⭐ ***diyār* = homes / lands**; ⭐ ***dāʾirah* = a CIRCLE / a department** — from *dāra*, to go around!); ⭐ ***dayyār* = a DWELLER, an inhabitant — "anyone at all who lives in a house"** (used ONLY in negation: *mā fi'd-dāri dayyār* = "**there's not a soul in the house**").
- **Here it means:** "**And Nūḥ said: 'My Lord, do not leave upon the earth any dweller from among the disbelievers.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *DHAR* VERB THROWN BACK.** ⭐ **v.23 — the chiefs: *lā TADHARUNNA ālihatakum*** — ⭐ "**NEVER LEAVE your gods!**" ⭐ **v.26 — Nūḥ: *lā TADHAR ʿala'l-arḍi… dayyārā*** — ⭐ "**LEAVE not one of them on earth!**" ⭐⭐⭐ **They swore never to LEAVE their idols — Nūḥ prays that THEY not be LEFT.** ⭐ **The same defective verb, turned against them.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Dayyār* — "a single DWELLER"** — ⭐ **a word used only in NEGATION** — ⭐ ***mā fi'd-dāri dayyār*** = "**not a SOUL in the house**" — ⭐ **total emptiness.** ⭐ **Like *aḥad* in negation (Ikhlāṣ 112:4, Ḥāqqah 69:47).** ⭐ **Not one house left inhabited by a denier.**
  ⭐⭐ **And why such a severe prayer from the most patient prophet?** ⭐ **v.27 explains — and Allah had already told him (11:36) that no more would believe.** ⭐ **It's a prayer born of knowledge, not anger.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Dāʾirah* = a CIRCLE / a government DEPARTMENT** — ⭐ **same root as *dār* (what "goes around" a space).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā tadhar*** = prohibition/supplication + jussive of the **defective verb**. ⭐ ***dayyāran*** = object — ⚠️ **only used in negative contexts.**
- **Why this exact word:** *dayyār* — ⭐ **a negation-only word for total absence, matching the totality of the flood.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.23 chiefs: "NEVER LEAVE your gods" (*lā tadharunna*) → v.26 Nūḥ: "LEAVE not one of them" (*lā tadhar*).** ⭐ **The verb thrown back.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***DAYYĀR* = a single DWELLER — NEGATION-ONLY: *mā fi'd-dāri dayyār* = "not a SOUL in the house."** ⭐ **Like *aḥad* in negation (112:4, 69:47).** ⭐⭐ **Severe — but born of KNOWLEDGE (11:36), not anger.** ⭐ ***Dār* = HOUSE; *dāʾirah* = CIRCLE / DEPARTMENT.**

**37. إِنَّكَ إِن تَذَرْهُمْ يُضِلُّوا عِبَادَكَ — *innaka in tadharhum yuḍillū ʿibādak*** · v.27
- **Root feelings:** *tadharhum* — ⭐ **[if] You LEAVE them** (the defective verb, again). *yuḍillū* — ⭐ **they will LEAD ASTRAY** (Form IV of *ḍalla*). *ʿibād* — ⭐ **SERVANTS** (*ʿabd*; ⭐ **Fajr 89:29** *fa'dkhulī fī **ʿibādī***!).
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, if You leave them, they will mislead Your servants…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE REASON for the prayer — protection of the believers.** ⭐ ***In tadharhum yuḍillū ʿibādak*** — ⭐ **"if You LEAVE them, they will mislead YOUR SERVANTS."** ⭐ **Nūḥ isn't praying out of revenge — he's protecting the few believers and future generations.** ⭐⭐ **He's seen 950 years of evidence: they mislead (v.24 — *aḍallū kathīrā*), they'll keep misleading.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Dhar* — FOURTH use in this surah:** ⭐ **v.23 *lā tadharunna* (×2 — "never leave your gods"), v.26 *lā tadhar* (leave not one), v.27 *in tadharhum* (if You leave them).** ⭐ **The whole ending pivots on who LEAVES whom.**
  ⭐⭐ ***ʿIbādak* — "YOUR servants"** — ⭐ **possessive, protective — "Your own"** — ⭐ **Fajr 89:29** — *fa'dkhulī fī **ʿibādī*** — ⭐ **"enter among MY servants"** — ⭐ **the same honoured group.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***in*** (condition) + jussive (*tadhar*) + jussive answer (*yuḍillū*, nūn dropped). ⭐ ***ʿibādaka*** = object.
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿibādak* — ⭐ **reveals the motive: protecting Allah's servants.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY the prayer? "If You leave them, they'll MISLEAD YOUR SERVANTS."** ⭐ **Protection, not revenge.** ⭐⭐ ***Dhar* ×4 in this surah's end: v.23 (×2), v.26, v.27.** ⭐ ***ʿIbādak* — Fajr 89:29 *fī ʿibādī*.**

**38. وَلَا يَلِدُوا إِلَّا فَاجِرًا كَفَّارًا — *wa lā yalidū illā fājiran kaffārā*** · v.27
- **Root feelings:** *yalidū* — ⭐ **they BEGET** (*walada* — ⭐ **Ikhlāṣ 112:3** *lam yalid*!; ⭐ **Balad 90:3** *wālid wa mā walad*). *fājir* — ⭐⭐ the "**BURSTING through limits**" family — *fajara* = to burst open; ⭐ ***fujūr* = wickedness** (bursting moral limits); ⭐ ***fajr* = DAWN** (light bursting through!); ⭐ ***fujjār* — Infiṭār 82:14**; ⭐ ***fujūrahā* — Shams 91:8**. *kaffār* — ⭐⭐ **the *faʿʿāl* INTENSIVE of *kāfir*** = **one who DISBELIEVES / is UNGRATEFUL again and again, endlessly** (⭐ *kafara* = to bury a seed / cover over; ⭐ ***kaffārah* = EXPIATION**).
- **Here it means:** "**…and they will not beget except wicked, utterly ungrateful [children].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TWO ROOTS YOU'VE TRACKED LONGEST — together, at the end.**
  ⭐ ***FĀJIR* — the *f-j-r* root** — ⭐ **your longest thread (EIGHT appearances):** sin (Qiyāmah 75:5), a spring (Insān 76:6), the wicked (ʿAbasa 80:42, Infiṭār 82:14), seas bursting (82:3), dawn (Fajr 89:1, Qadr 97:5), the soul's capacity (Shams 91:8) — ⭐ **and now: *fājiran* (Nūḥ 71:27).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***KAFFĀR* — the *faʿʿāl* intensive — and the ONE-LETTER CONTRAST:** ⭐ **v.10 — *innahu kāna GHAFFĀRĀ*** — ⭐ **ALLAH: forgiving AGAIN AND AGAIN.** ⭐ **v.27 — *fājiran KAFFĀRĀ*** — ⭐ **THEM: ungrateful AGAIN AND AGAIN.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAFFĀR* vs *KAFFĀR* — GH vs K — ONE LETTER, same *faʿʿāl* pattern.** ⭐ **Endless forgiveness offered; endless ingratitude returned.** ⭐ **The whole surah in two words.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Kaffār* — the *k-f-r* intensive ladder:** ⭐ ***kāfir*** (one who disbelieves) → ***kafūr*** (Insān 76:3 — *immā shākiran wa immā **kafūrā*** — utterly ungrateful) → ***kaffār*** (Nūḥ 71:27 — endlessly so). ⭐ ***ʿĀdiyāt 100:6*** — *la-**kanūd*** (ungrateful — barren ground).
  ⭐⭐ ***Lā yalidū* — "they will not BEGET"** — ⭐ **the *w-l-d* root of Ikhlāṣ** (*lam yalid* — Allah does NOT beget) ⭐ **and Balad 90:3** (*wa wālidin wa mā walad*). ⭐ **Nūḥ knew from experience: 950 years, generation after generation, the children turned out like the parents** — ⭐ **the corruption had become hereditary culture.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā yalidū*** = jussive (joined to *yuḍillū*). ⭐⭐ ***fājiran kaffāran*** = two descriptions — ***faʿʿāl*** intensive for *kaffār*.
- **Why this exact word:** *kaffār* — ⭐ **the exact *faʿʿāl* mirror of *ghaffār* in v.10.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.10 *GHAFFĀRĀ* (Allah — forgiving ENDLESSLY) vs v.27 *KAFFĀRĀ* (them — ungrateful ENDLESSLY). ONE LETTER, same *faʿʿāl*.** ⭐ **The whole surah in two words.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FĀJIR* — the *f-j-r* thread: NINE appearances now.** ⭐⭐ ***K-f-r* ladder: *kāfir* → *kafūr* (76:3) → *kaffār* (71:27).** ⭐ ***Lā yalidū* — *w-l-d* of Ikhlāṣ 112:3.** ⭐ **Corruption had become hereditary.**

**39. رَّبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ — *rabbi'ghfir lī wa li-wālidayya*** · v.28
- **Root feelings:** *ighfir* — ⭐ **FORGIVE** (imperative — ⭐ *mighfar* = helmet). *wālidayya* — ⭐ **MY TWO PARENTS** (dual of *wālid* + *-ya*, my — ⭐ *wālidayn* = parents; ⭐ ***birr al-wālidayn* = dutifulness to PARENTS**!).
- **Here it means:** "**My Lord, forgive me and my parents…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST BELOVED DUʿĀʾ IN THE SURAH — and the prophet begins with HIMSELF.** ⭐ ***Rabbi'ghfir LĪ*** — ⭐ **"forgive ME"** — ⭐ **after 950 years of tireless da'wah, the first person he asks forgiveness for is HIMSELF.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Exactly like NAṢR 110:3 — after the Prophet's ﷺ completed mission: *wa'staghfirh*** — ⭐ **seek forgiveness.** ⭐ **Every great task ends in *istighfār* — Nūḥ's after 950 years, Muhammad's ﷺ after 23.** ⭐⭐ **And v.10 — he told his people *istaghfirū* (seek forgiveness); v.28 — he does it himself.** ⭐ **The preacher practises what he preached.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Wa li-WĀLIDAYYA* — "and my PARENTS"** — ⭐ **second in line** — ⭐ classical note: **Nūḥ's parents were believers** (unlike his son — 11:42–46). ⭐ **This duʿāʾ is used constantly by Muslims** — ⭐ **compare Ibrāhīm's: *rabbana'ghfir lī wa li-wālidayya wa li'l-muʾminīna yawma yaqūmu'l-ḥisāb*** (14:41) — ⭐ **nearly IDENTICAL** — ⭐ **two great prophets, the same prayer for themselves, their parents, and all believers.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Wālidayya* — the *w-l-d* root again** — ⭐ **v.27: the disbelievers BEGET (*yalidū*) only wicked children → v.28: Nūḥ prays for those who BEGOT HIM (*wālidayya*).** ⭐ **The same root: bad offspring vs blessed parents.** ⭐ ***Birr al-wālidayn* = honouring PARENTS.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ighfir*** = imperative (supplication). ⚠️⭐⭐ ***wālidayya*** = **dual *wālidayn* + *-ya*** — ⭐ **the *nūn* drops and the two *yāʾ*s merge with a shaddah** (*wālidayni* + *ya* → *wālidayya*).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **self first, parents second — the humble order of every prophetic duʿāʾ.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RABBI'GHFIR LĪ* — after 950 YEARS, he asks forgiveness for HIMSELF FIRST.** ⭐⭐⭐ **= Naṣr 110:3 *wa'staghfirh*: every great mission ends in ISTIGHFĀR (Nūḥ after 950 years, Muhammad ﷺ after 23).** ⭐⭐ **v.10 he TAUGHT *istighfirū*; v.28 he PRACTISED it.** ⭐⭐⭐ **= Ibrāhīm 14:41: *rabbana'ghfir lī wa li-wālidayya* — NEARLY IDENTICAL.** ⭐ **v.27 they BEGET wicked → v.28 he prays for those who BEGOT him.** ⚠️ ***Wālidayya* = dual + yāʾ, merged.** ⭐ ***Birr al-wālidayn*.**

**40. وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيْتِيَ مُؤْمِنًا وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ — *wa li-man dakhala baytiya muʾminan wa li'l-muʾminīna wa'l-muʾmināt*** · v.28
- **Root feelings:** *dakhala* — **ENTERED**. *bayt* — ⭐ **a HOUSE** (⭐ ***al-Bayt*** — the Kaʿbah, **Quraysh 106:3** *rabba hādha'l-bayt*!; ⭐ *bayt* = a line of POETRY!). *muʾmin* — ⭐ **a BELIEVER** (*īmān* ← *amn*, security). *muʾmināt* — ⭐ **believing WOMEN**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and whoever enters my house as a believer, and the believing men and the believing women…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE CIRCLES OF MERCY — widening outward:** ⭐ **(1) ME (*lī*)** → ⭐ **(2) my PARENTS (*wālidayya*)** → ⭐ **(3) whoever ENTERS MY HOUSE as a believer** → ⭐ **(4) ALL believing men and women** — ⭐ **for all time.** ⭐⭐ **From the self to the whole ummah of believers across history** — ⭐ **including YOU, thousands of years later.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nūḥ prayed for you.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Man dakhala BAYTIYA MUʾMINAN*** — ⭐ **"whoever enters my HOUSE as a BELIEVER"** — ⭐ classical readings: **his house / his mosque / his ARK** (the ship as his house!) — ⭐ **and the condition *muʾminan* (as a believer) excludes his disbelieving son and wife** (66:10). ⭐ **Being in the prophet's house isn't enough — you must enter it AS A BELIEVER.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Al-muʾminīna wa'l-muʾmināt*** — ⭐ **believing men AND believing women, named separately** — ⭐ **equally included in the prayer** (⭐ compare 33:35 — the famous verse listing men and women side by side).
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***Bayt* — the HOUSE thread:** ⭐ **Quraysh 106:3** — *fal-yaʿbudū rabba **hādha'l-bayt*** (the Lord of THIS HOUSE — the Kaʿbah). ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:28** — *li-man dakhala **baytiya** muʾminā* (whoever enters MY house as a believer). ⭐ **And *dayyār* (v.26) — a DWELLER in a house** — ⭐ **no disbelieving dweller left; every believing guest forgiven.**
  ⭐ ⚠️ ***Baytiya*** — ⭐ **the possessive *yāʾ* carries a FATḤAH here** (*baytiya*, not *baytī*) — ⭐ **to avoid two sukūns / for flow before *muʾminan*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***muʾminan*** = ***ḥāl*** ("**as** a believer" — the condition). ⚠️ ⭐ ***baytiya*** — *yāʾ* with fatḥah.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the condition *muʾminan* — so proximity to a prophet isn't mistaken for faith.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WIDENING CIRCLES: ME → my PARENTS → whoever enters my HOUSE as a believer → ALL believing men and women, for all time.** ⭐ **NŪḤ PRAYED FOR YOU.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUʾMINAN* (AS a believer) = the CONDITION — excludes his son and wife (66:10).** ⭐ **Proximity ≠ faith.** ⭐⭐ ***Bayt*: Quraysh 106:3 (the Kaʿbah) / 71:28 (Nūḥ's house — or ark).** ⭐ **Men AND women named separately.** ⚠️ ***Baytiya* — yāʾ with fatḥah.**

**41. وَلَا تَزِدِ الظَّالِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارًا — *wa lā tazidi'ẓ-ẓālimīna illā tabārā*** · v.28
- **Root feelings:** *tazid* — ⭐ **INCREASE** (jussive — supplication). *ẓālimīn* — ⭐ **WRONGDOERS** (*ẓulm* = injustice = ⭐ *ẓulmah*, darkness). *tabār* — ⭐⭐ the "**DESTRUCTION / ruin**" family — *tabbara* = **to destroy, shatter**; ⭐ ***tabār* = RUIN, destruction**; ⭐ ***mutabbar* = destroyed**; ⭐ ***tibr* = GOLD DUST / unminted gold** (gold that's been "broken" into fragments!). ⚠️ **NOT the same root as *tabbat* (Masad 111:1 — *t-b-b*, to perish)** — **this is *t-b-r*.**
- **Here it means:** "**…and increase the wrongdoers in nothing but ruin.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SURAH'S LAST WORD — *TABĀRĀ* (RUIN) — and the *zāda… illā* pattern completes.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Zāda… illā* — FOUR times:** ⭐ **v.6** — *lam yazidhum duʿāʾī illā **FIRĀRĀ*** (my call increased them only in **FLEEING**). ⭐ **v.21** — *lam yazidhu māluhu wa waladuhu illā **KHASĀRĀ*** (wealth increased him only in **LOSS**). ⭐ **v.24** — *wa lā tazidi'ẓ-ẓālimīna illā **ḌALĀLĀ*** (increase them only in **STRAYING**). ⭐ **v.28** — *wa lā tazidi'ẓ-ẓālimīna illā **TABĀRĀ*** (increase them only in **RUIN**). ⭐⭐⭐ **FLEEING → LOSS → STRAYING → RUIN.** ⭐ **The four-step descent of a people who refused 950 years of mercy.** ⭐ **And v.24 and v.28 are IDENTICAL except the last word: *ḍalālā* → *tabārā*.** ⭐ **Straying ends in ruin.**
  ⭐⭐ **The surah's shape: it opens with *andhir* (WARN, v.1) and ends with *tabārā* (RUIN, v.28)** — ⭐ **the warning ignored, the ruin arrived.** ⭐ **But the verse just before (v.28a) is FORGIVENESS for all believers** — ⭐ **so the surah ends on two destinies: forgiveness for those who entered "the house" believing, ruin for those who wronged.**
  ⭐⭐ ⚠️ ***Tabār* (t-b-r, RUIN) ≠ *tabbat* (t-b-b, PERISH — Masad 111:1)** — ⭐ **look-alike roots, similar meanings, different words.** ⭐ ***Tibr* = GOLD DUST** — ⭐ **"shattered" gold.**
  ⭐ **And note the *-ārā* rhyme closes the surah: *tabārā*** — ⭐ **the long, open echo ends on ruin.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lā tazidi*** = jussive supplication (hollow verb shortened; kasrah for two sukūns). ⭐ ***illā tabāran*** = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** *tabār* — ⭐ **completes the four-step *zāda… illā* descent with its final stage.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ZĀDA… ILLĀ* ×4: FLEEING (v.6) → LOSS (v.21) → STRAYING (v.24) → RUIN (v.28).** ⭐ **The four-step descent.** ⭐⭐ **v.24 and v.28 IDENTICAL except *ḌALĀLĀ* → *TABĀRĀ*: straying ends in ruin.** ⭐⭐ **Opens *ANDHIR* (warn, v.1) → closes *TABĀRĀ* (ruin, v.28).** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***TABĀR* (t-b-r) ≠ *TABBAT* (t-b-b, Masad 111:1).** ⭐ ***Tibr* = GOLD DUST.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **غَفَّارًا (v.10) vs كَفَّارًا (v.27)** — ⚠️ **ONE LETTER apart, same *faʿʿāl* pattern** — ⭐ **Allah endlessly forgiving / them endlessly ungrateful.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **اسْتَكْبَرُوا (v.7) vs اسْتَغْفِرُوا (v.10)** — ⚠️ **SAME Form X, opposite meanings** — ⭐ **the disease and the cure, three verses apart.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَنبَتَكُم ... نَبَاتًا (v.17)** — ⚠️ **MIXED forms** (Form IV verb + Form I noun) — ⭐ **deliberate, like Muzzammil 73:8.** ⭐ **But v.18 *yukhrijukum ikhrājā* MATCHES.**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **نُورًا / سِرَاجًا (v.16)** — ⚠️ **moon = REFLECTED light; sun = a burning LAMP.** ⭐ **Never swapped.**
5. ⭐⭐⭐ **فَأُدْخِلُوا (v.25)** — ⚠️ **the *fa* = IMMEDIATELY** — ⭐ **classical evidence for punishment in the BARZAKH.**
6. ⭐⭐ **يَغُوثَ / يَعُوقَ (v.23)** — ⚠️ **DIPTOTES** (verb-shaped names) — ⭐ **no tanwīn, unlike *Waddan, Suwāʿan, Nasran*.**
7. ⭐⭐ **يَا قَوْمِ · أَطِيعُونِ · رَبِّ (v.2, 3, 5)** — ⚠️ **the "my/me" *yāʾ* is DROPPED** (kasrah remains).
8. ⭐⭐ **مِّمَّا (v.25)** — ⚠️ **the *mā* is EXTRA (emphasis)** — ⭐ "because of their sins — their very sins." ⭐ **And it's FRONTED.**
9. ⭐⭐ **دَيَّارًا (v.26)** — ⚠️ **used ONLY in negation** — "not a single dweller."
10. ⭐⭐ **يُمْدِدْكُم (v.12)** — ⚠️ **unmerged doubled jussive** (like *tamnun*, 74:6).
11. ⭐⭐ **يُرْسِلِ / يَغْفِرْ / يُمْدِدْ / يَجْعَلْ (v.4, 11–12)** — ⚠️ **all JUSSIVE** — the answer to the commands (*jawāb aṭ-ṭalab*).
12. ⭐⭐ **تَبَارًا (v.28)** — ⚠️ ***t-b-r* (ruin)**, ⭐ **NOT *t-b-b* (*tabbat*, Masad 111:1).**
13. ⭐⭐ **وَالِدَيَّ (v.28)** — ⚠️ **dual + "my" — the *yāʾ*s merge with a shaddah.** ⭐ **And *baytiya* — *yāʾ* with fatḥah.**
14. ⭐⭐ **وَقَارًا (v.13)** — ⚠️ **from *waqr* (HEAVINESS)** — ⭐ **same root as *waqr* = DEAFNESS.** ⭐ **And *tarjūna* in negation may mean FEAR.**
15. ⭐ **كُبَّارًا (v.22)** — ⚠️ ***fuʿʿāl* — rarer and stronger than *kubār* and *kabīr*.**
16. ⭐ **نُوحًا (v.1)** — ⚠️ **foreign but FULLY DECLINED** (three letters, quiet middle — like *ʿĀdun*).
17. ⭐ **وَلَدُهُ (v.21)** — ⚠️ **recitation variant *wulduhu*.**
18. ⭐ **أَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى (v.4a) vs أَجَلَ اللَّهِ (v.4b)** — ⚠️ **two different "terms"** — ⭐ **one belief can earn; one nothing delays.**

## 🌙 The Three Surahs Together (69 · 70 · 71)

⭐ **Read in mushaf order, they form one argument about TRUTH, HUMAN NATURE, and a PROPHET'S ENDURANCE:**

| | **Al-Ḥāqqah (69)** | **Al-Maʿārij (70)** | **Nūḥ (71)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core image** | The TRUE Day — the bill that must be paid | The STAIRWAYS — ascent to the Lord | A prophet's 950-year REPORT |
| **The flood** | *ṭagha'l-māʾ… fi'l-jāriyah* (v.11) — the ARK side | — | *ughriqū* (v.25) — the WATER side |
| **The ear** | *udhunun wāʿiyah* (v.12) — RETAINS | *jamaʿa fa-awʿā* (v.18) — a vessel of GOLD | *aṣābiʿahum fī ādhānihim* (v.7) — BLOCKED |
| ***Ḥamīm*** | no *ḥamīm* (FRIEND) for him (v.35) | *lā yasʾalu ḥamīmun ḥamīmā* (v.10) | — |
| **Feeding the poor** | *lā yaḥuḍḍu ʿalā ṭaʿāmi'l-miskīn* (v.34) | *fī amwālihim ḥaqqun maʿlūm* (v.24) | — |
| **Wealth** | *mā aghnā ʿannī māliyah* (v.28) | *jamaʿa fa-awʿā* (v.18) | *lam yazidhu māluhu… illā khasārā* (v.21) |
| **Certainty / prayer** | *ḥaqq al-yaqīn* (v.51) → *subḥāna rabbiya'l-ʿaẓīm* | *ʿalā ṣalātihim dāʾimūn* (v.23) | *istaghfirū rabbakum* (v.10) |
| **The close** | *fa-sabbiḥ bi'smi rabbika'l-ʿaẓīm* | *dhālika'l-yawmu'lladhī kānū yūʿadūn* | *rabbi'ghfir lī wa li-wālidayya…* |

⭐⭐⭐ **Ḥāqqah shows the flood from the SHIP; Nūḥ shows it from the WATER.** ⭐ **Ḥāqqah praises the EAR that retains; Nūḥ shows ears BLOCKED with fingers; Maʿārij shows a man whose only "vessel" holds gold.** ⭐ **And all three end by turning to Allah — in glorification, in the promised Day, and in a prophet's prayer for every believer who would ever live.**
