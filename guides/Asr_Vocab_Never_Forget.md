# Surah Al-ʿAṣr (103) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–3)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**Three verses. Fourteen words. And the whole human condition, with the only exit, in one sentence.**

⭐ **The structure is mathematical:**
- ⭐ **v.1** — an oath: **TIME**.
- ⭐ **v.2** — the verdict on **EVERYONE**: *in loss.*
- ⭐ **v.3** — the exception, with **FOUR conditions**:
  1. ⭐ ***āmanū*** — they **believed**
  2. ⭐ ***ʿamilu'ṣ-ṣāliḥāt*** — they **did righteous deeds**
  3. ⭐ ***tawāṣaw bi'l-ḥaqq*** — they **urged each other to truth**
  4. ⭐ ***tawāṣaw bi'ṣ-ṣabr*** — they **urged each other to patience**

⭐⭐ **Two are personal (believe, act). Two are social (urge each other).** ⭐ **You can't escape loss alone — ⭐ **half the exit is helping others out.**

⭐ **A saying commonly attributed to Imām ash-Shāfiʿī: ⭐ *"If people reflected on this surah alone, it would suffice them."***

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **Time is passing → everyone is losing → except four things.** ⭐ **Two you do alone, two you do together.**

## The Big Picture

1. **The oath** (v.1) — ⭐ **by Time.**
2. **The verdict** (v.2) — ⭐ **man is immersed in loss.**
3. **The exit** (v.3) — ⭐ **four conditions.**

---

## §1 — The Oath (v.1)

**1. وَالْعَصْرِ — *wa'l-ʿaṣr*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**SQUEEZING / PRESSING**" family — *ʿaṣara* = **to squeeze, to press out**; ⭐ ***ʿaṣīr* = JUICE*** (what you squeeze out of fruit!); ⭐ ***miʿṣarah* = a PRESS*** (olive press); ⭐ **and *ʿaṣr* = time / an era / the afternoon.**
- **Here it means:** "**By Time.**" ⭐ *(Classical readings: time itself, the age/era, or the afternoon — the time of ʿAṣr prayer.)*
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **one of the most beautiful root-connections in the whole juzʾ — and you already own the other half.**
  
  ⭐ **Why would "time" and "juice" share a root?** ⭐ **Because ***time is squeezed***.** ⭐ **Every day presses something out of your life — ⭐ **and what's left is either juice or pulp.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the afternoon (ʿaṣr) is ⭐ **the squeezed end of the day** — the light is compressed, the hours remaining are few.** ⭐ **The day is being wrung out.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW THE PAYOFF: ⭐ **you own this root from Nabaʾ 78:14** — *wa anzalnā mina'l-**MUʿṢIRĀTI** māʾan thajjājā* ("We sent down from the **RAIN-CLOUDS** abundant water") — ⭐ **clouds named "**the squeezed ones**," wrung out like a wet towel.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ: clouds are squeezed → rain → life.** ⭐ **ʿAṣr: time is squeezed → and what comes out of YOU?** ⭐ **Same root. The question is what your life yields when pressed.**
  
  ⭐ **The everyday hooks are free: ⭐ ***ʿaṣīr burtuqāl* = orange JUICE***; ⭐ ***Ṣalāt al-ʿAṣr*** — you pray in the squeezed hour.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **the *wāw* is *wāw al-qasam*** (the "by" of oaths) — *al-ʿaṣri* is genitive.
- **Why this word:** *ʿaṣr* over *zamān* or *dahr* — ⭐ **because only *ʿaṣr* carries the idea of **pressure and yield**.** ⭐ **(Compare *dahr* in Insān 76:1 — time as a vast force.)**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿAṢĪR* = JUICE.** ⭐ **TIME IS SQUEEZED.** ⭐ **What does your life yield when pressed?** ⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:14: *muʿṣirāt* = clouds SQUEEZED into rain.** ⭐ **And you pray *ʿAṣr* in the squeezed end of the day.**

## §2 — The Verdict (v.2)

**2. لَفِي خُسْرٍ — *la-fī khusr*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**LOSS — specifically in TRADE**" family — *khasira* = **to lose money in a deal, to lose capital**; ⭐ ***khasārah* = a financial LOSS*** (modern Arabic: *rabḥ wa khasārah* = **profit and loss**!); ⭐ ***khāsir* = a loser.**
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, man is in loss.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the verse is a BUSINESS STATEMENT — and that's what makes it devastating.**
  
  ⭐ ***Khusr* is a TRADER'S word.*** ⭐ **It means losing your **capital** — not just failing to profit, but **your principal shrinking**.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And what is the capital? ⭐ **v.1 just told you: TIME.** ⭐ **Every hour is capital being spent.** ⭐ **If it buys nothing lasting, ⭐ **the account is running down, automatically.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Which means loss is the DEFAULT.** ⭐ **You don't have to *do* anything wrong to lose — ⭐ **you just have to do nothing right.** ⭐ **Time is melting like ice; doing nothing is losing.**
  
  ⭐ **Three grammatical details make it heavier:**
  - ⭐ ***inna* + *la-*** — ⭐ **double emphasis ("most certainly").**
  - ⭐ ***fī* — "IN" loss** — ⭐ **immersed in it, surrounded by it.** ⭐ **Compare Balad 90:4 (*fī kabad* — created **IN** toil) and Tīn 95:4 (*fī aḥsani taqwīm* — **IN** the best form).** ⭐ **Three surahs: you're IN toil, IN the best form, IN loss.**
  - ⭐ ***khusrin* is INDEFINITE** — ⭐ **"a loss" = an enormous, unspecified loss.**
  
  ⭐ **You own this root from TWO surahs:** ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:12** — *tilka idhan karratun **khāsirah*** ("that would be a **losing** return" — the mockers, accidentally right) ⭐ **and Muṭaffifīn 83:3** — ***yukhsirūn*** (the cheaters who **cause loss** by short-measuring). ⭐⭐ **The fraudsters made others lose; ʿAṣr says everyone is losing.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***inna* + emphatic *lām*** on *fī*. ⭐ ***Al-insān*** = **all of mankind** (definite article of genus — *al-jins*), ⭐ **which is why v.3 can make an exception from it.**
- **Why this word:** *khusr* (**trade loss**) rather than *ḍalāl* (being astray) — ⭐ **it frames life as a **transaction already in progress**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Rabḥ wa KHASĀRAH* = PROFIT AND LOSS.** ⭐ **Your CAPITAL is TIME (v.1), and it's draining.** ⭐⭐⭐ **LOSS IS THE DEFAULT — you don't have to do wrong; you just have to do nothing right.** ⭐ ***Fī* = IMMERSED** (like *fī kabad* 90:4, *fī aḥsani taqwīm* 95:4). ⭐ **And Nāziʿāt 79:12, Muṭaffifīn 83:3.**

## §3 — The Exit (v.3)

**3. الصَّالِحَاتِ — *aṣ-ṣāliḥāt*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**being sound / fit / repairing**" family — *ṣalaḥa* = **to be good, sound, fit for purpose**; ⭐ ***iṣlāḥ* = REFORM / repair***; ⭐ ***maṣlaḥah* = benefit/public interest***; ⭐ ***ṣulḥ* = reconciliation, a peace treaty***.
- **Here it means:** "**except those who believed and did righteous deeds.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the root means *fit for purpose*, and that reframes "righteous."**
  
  ⭐ ***Ṣāliḥ* isn't "nice."*** ⭐ **It's ⭐ **sound, functioning, doing what it was built to do** — like a *ṣāliḥ* tool or a road *ṣāliḥ* for travel.** ⭐ **And *iṣlāḥ* = REPAIR.** ⭐ **So *ʿamal ṣāliḥ* = ⭐ **action that WORKS, that fixes, that's fit for the purpose you were made for.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And this connects to v.2's trade metaphor:** ⭐ **if time is draining capital, ⭐ **ṣāliḥāt are the investments that actually return something.**
  
  ⭐ **And the phrase ***āmanū wa ʿamilu'ṣ-ṣāliḥāt*** is a Qurʾānic signature** ⭐ **you own from three places:** ⭐ **Inshiqāq 84:25, Tīn 95:6 (*illa'lladhīna āmanū wa ʿamilu'ṣ-ṣāliḥāt*), Bayyinah 98:7.** ⭐⭐ **Tīn 95:6 even has the SAME "except" structure** — ⭐ **everyone falls to the lowest of the low *except* these.** ⭐ **ʿAṣr: everyone is in loss *except* these.** ⭐ **Two surahs, same exit.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note: belief comes FIRST, deeds SECOND.** ⭐ **Always that order.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***aṣ-ṣāliḥāt*** — **feminine plural** active participle used as a noun (the word *aʿmāl*, deeds, is understood). ⭐ ***Illā*** = **exception** — ⭐ **from *al-insān* (all mankind) in v.2.**
- **Why this word:** *ṣāliḥ* (**fit for purpose / repairing**) rather than *khayr* (good) — ⭐ **it's about **function**, not just virtue.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Iṣlāḥ* = REPAIR; *ṣulḥ* = a PEACE TREATY.** ⭐ ***Ṣāliḥ* = FIT FOR PURPOSE.** ⭐ **Righteous deeds = the investments that actually return.** ⭐⭐ **Tīn 95:6: everyone falls *EXCEPT* these. ʿAṣr 103:3: everyone loses *EXCEPT* these.** ⭐ **Same exit.**

**4. وَتَوَاصَوْا — *wa tawāṣaw*** · v.3 (×2)
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**enjoining / advising / bequeathing**" family — *waṣiyyah* = ⭐ **A WILL / last testament*** (instructions you leave behind!); *awṣā* = to enjoin; ⭐ **Form VI (*tawāṣā*) = to enjoin ONE ANOTHER.**
- **Here it means:** "**and urged one another to truth, and urged one another to patience.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **you own this EXACT verb from Balad 90:17 — and the comparison is the best cross-surah gem in this batch.**
  
  ⭐ **Balad 90:17 — *wa **tawāṣaw** bi'**ṣ-ṣabri** wa **tawāṣaw** bi'l-**marḥamah*** — ⭐ **urged each other to **PATIENCE** and to **COMPASSION**.**
  ⭐ **ʿAṣr 103:3 — *wa **tawāṣaw** bi'l-**ḥaqqi** wa **tawāṣaw** bi'**ṣ-ṣabr*** — ⭐ **urged each other to **TRUTH** and to **PATIENCE**.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Same verb, used TWICE in each verse, in BOTH surahs.** ⭐ **And ***ṣabr* appears in both*** — ⭐ **it's the constant.** ⭐ **Balad pairs it with **mercy**; ʿAṣr pairs it with **truth**.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Put them together and you get the complete social ethic: ⭐ **TRUTH + PATIENCE + MERCY.** ⭐ **Truth without patience becomes harsh; truth without mercy becomes cruel.** ⭐ **The two surahs supply each other's missing piece.**
  
  ⭐ ***Waṣiyyah* = A WILL*** — ⭐ **the instructions a dying person leaves.** ⭐ **So *tawāṣī* has a gravity to it: ⭐ **you urge each other the way a dying man advises his children — **seriously, lovingly, with nothing to gain**.
  
  ⭐ **And Form VI = MUTUAL** — ⭐ **you both give and receive the reminder.** ⭐ **Nobody is only the preacher; everybody is also preached to.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare Fajr 89:18** — *wa lā **taḥāḍḍūna*** ("you do NOT urge one another") — ⭐ **the failure. ʿAṣr and Balad show the cure.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form VI past, weak-final** — ⚠️ **the original *tawāṣayū* contracts to *tawāṣaw*.** ⭐ **Note the verb is REPEATED rather than saying "urged one another to truth and patience" — ⭐ **each gets its own full verb, its own weight.**
- **Why this word:** *tawāṣā* (**mutual bequeathing**) rather than *amara* (commanding) — ⭐ **it's peer-to-peer, not top-down.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Waṣiyyah* = A WILL** — ⭐ **urge each other like a dying man advises his children.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Balad 90:17: PATIENCE + MERCY. ʿAṣr 103:3: TRUTH + PATIENCE.** ⭐ **Together: TRUTH + PATIENCE + MERCY.** ⭐ **And Form VI = you both GIVE and RECEIVE the reminder.** ⭐ **Fajr 89:18 = the failure (*lā taḥāḍḍūn*).**

**5. بِالْحَقِّ — *bi'l-ḥaqq*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**firmly established / binding / real**" family — *ḥaqqa* = **to be true, to be established, to be due**; ⭐ ***ḥaqq* = TRUTH AND A RIGHT*** (⭐ **"ḥuqūq" = RIGHTS** — human rights = *ḥuqūq al-insān*!); ⭐ ***al-Ḥaqq* — a NAME OF ALLAH***; ⭐ ***taḥqīq* = investigation/verification.**
- **Here it means:** "**[urged one another] to the truth.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the root holds TRUTH and RIGHTS together — and that's exactly why it's the first thing to urge.**
  
  ⭐ ***Ḥaqq* means both what is TRUE and what is OWED.*** ⭐ ***Ḥuqūq* = rights** — ⭐ **what you're owed and what you owe.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **So urging each other *bi'l-ḥaqq* covers: ⭐ **(a) the TRUTH of belief, and ⭐ (b) giving everyone their DUE.** ⭐ **Doctrine and justice, one word.**
  
  ⭐ **You own the root from Nabaʾ 78:39** — *dhālika'l-yawmu'l-**ḥaqq*** ("that is **the TRUE Day**" — the established, certain Day), ⭐ **and Inshiqāq 84:2, 5** — *wa **ḥuqqat*** (the sky "**was made fit / rightly obliged**").
  
  ⭐ **And the order matters: ⭐ **TRUTH first, then PATIENCE.** ⭐ **Because the moment you stand for the truth, ⭐ **you will need patience.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bi-*** after *tawāṣaw* = "**to / with**" (what is enjoined). ⭐ **Definite = "**THE** truth."**
- **Why this word:** *ḥaqq* (**truth + right**) rather than *ṣidq* (honesty) — ⭐ **because it covers both belief and obligation.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥuqūq al-insān* = HUMAN RIGHTS.** ⭐ ***Ḥaqq* = TRUTH **and** what is OWED.** ⭐ **Doctrine and justice in one word.** ⭐⭐ **TRUTH first, then PATIENCE — because standing for truth REQUIRES patience.** ⭐ **And *al-Ḥaqq* is a Divine Name.**

**6. بِالصَّبْرِ — *bi'ṣ-ṣabr*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**RESTRAINING / BINDING / HOLDING FIRM**" family — *ṣabara* = **originally: to CONFINE, to hold something in check, to tie it down**; ⭐ ***ṣabr* = patience/steadfastness***; ⭐ ***ṣabir* = ALOES*** (a famously **bitter** plant! — patience is bitter); ⭐ ***al-Ṣabūr* — a NAME OF ALLAH.**
- **Here it means:** "**[urged one another] to patience/steadfastness.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the final word of the surah — and the root is a word of RESTRAINT, which closes a loop you've been tracking.**
  
  ⭐ ***Ṣabr* doesn't mean "waiting calmly."*** ⭐ **It originally means ⭐ **to HOLD YOURSELF IN, to confine, to tie down.** ⭐ **Restraining the tongue from complaint, the limbs from panic, the heart from despair.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And *ṣabir* = ALOES — the bitter plant.** ⭐ **Classical Arabic links them: ⭐ **patience tastes bitter, but it heals.** ⭐ **Free, unforgettable hook.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW THE THREAD: ⭐ **restraint as wisdom** runs through your surahs:**
  - **Fajr 89:5** — *li-dhī **ḥijr*** — ⭐ **intellect = a WALL** (restraint)
  - **Fajr 89:11** — ***ṭaghaw*** — ⭐ **the nations OVERFLOWED** (no restraint)
  - **Tīn 95:8** — ***aḥkami'l-ḥākimīn*** — ⭐ ***ḥikmah* from the BRIDLE** (restraint)
  - ⭐ **ʿAṣr 103:3** — ***ṣabr*** — ⭐ **holding yourself in** (restraint)
  
  ⭐⭐ **Arabic keeps defining virtue as the ability to HOLD BACK.** ⭐ **Intelligence is a wall; wisdom is a bridle; patience is a tether.**
  
  ⭐ **And *ṣabr* is in BOTH Balad 90:17 and ʿAṣr 103:3** — ⭐ **the only word the two "urge one another" verses share.** ⭐ **It's the constant.**
  
  ⭐ **And note: the surah ENDS on *ṣabr*.** ⭐ **The last instruction for escaping loss is: **hold on**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bi-*** again after the repeated *tawāṣaw*. ⭐ **Definite = "**THE** patience."**
- **Why this word:** *ṣabr* (**restraint**) — ⭐ **because truth (the previous word) provokes resistance, and only restraint survives it.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ṣabir* = ALOES — a BITTER plant that HEALS.** ⭐ ***Ṣabr* originally = TO TIE DOWN / CONFINE.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Virtue as RESTRAINT: *ḥijr* = a WALL (89:5), *ḥikmah* = a BRIDLE (95:8), *ṣabr* = a TETHER (103:3).** ⭐ **And the surah ENDS on "hold on."**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْعَصْرِ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **root = SQUEEZING** (*ʿaṣīr* = juice). ⭐ **Same root as *al-muʿṣirāt* (Nabaʾ 78:14).** **Time is pressed; what does your life yield?**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **خُسْرٍ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **a TRADE word: losing CAPITAL.** ⭐ **And loss is the DEFAULT — doing nothing is losing.**
3. ⭐⭐ **الصَّبْرِ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **originally means RESTRAINT / tying down**, not "waiting." ⭐ ***Ṣabir* = ALOES (bitter, healing).**
4. ⭐⭐ **تَوَاصَوْا (v.3)** — ⚠️ **Form VI = MUTUAL**: you both give and receive. ⭐ **Same verb as Balad 90:17.**
5. ⭐⭐ **الْحَقِّ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **means TRUTH AND what is OWED** (*ḥuqūq* = rights). **Don't narrow it to one.**
6. ⭐ **الْإِنسَانَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **the *al-* of GENUS = all mankind**, which is why v.3 can carve an exception.
7. ⭐ **خُسْرٍ is INDEFINITE** — ⚠️ **"a loss" = an enormous, unspecified loss.**
8. ⭐ **The verb is REPEATED** (*tawāṣaw… wa tawāṣaw*) — ⚠️ **each object gets its own full verb**, its own weight.
9. ⭐ **Belief before deeds** — ⚠️ **always this order** in *āmanū wa ʿamilu'ṣ-ṣāliḥāt*.
10. ⭐ **Four conditions, not one** — ⚠️ **two personal (believe, act), two SOCIAL (urge truth, urge patience).** **Half the exit is collective.**
