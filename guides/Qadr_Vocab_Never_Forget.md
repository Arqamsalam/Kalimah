# Surah Al-Qadr (97) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–5)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**Five verses — and the phrase *Laylat al-Qadr* appears THREE times in them.**

⭐ **v.1:** *innā anzalnāhu fī **laylati'l-qadr***
⭐ **v.2:** *wa mā adrāka mā **laylatu'l-qadr***
⭐ **v.3:** ***laylatu'l-qadr** khayrun min alfi shahr*

⭐⭐ **Three mentions in three consecutive verses — in a surah of only five.** ⭐ **That density is unusual even for the Qurʾān.** ⭐ **The repetition does what repetition always does in Arabic: **it fixes the thing in place** — appropriate for a night whose name means *the night of DECREE and MEASURE*.

⭐ **And notice the opening pronoun: *anzalnā-**HU*** — "**We sent IT down**."** ⭐ **"It" is never named.** ⭐ **The Qurʾān is so obviously the subject that it doesn't need saying.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **A five-verse surah that says one name three times — the name meaning "the night of MEASURING."** ⭐ **And the thing measured, in v.3, is **a thousand months**.

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The sending down** (v.1) — ⭐ *We sent it down on the Night of Decree.*
2. **The unanswerable question** (v.2) — ⭐ *and what could convey to you what it is?*
3. **The measure** (v.3) — ⭐ **better than a thousand months.**
4. **The descent** (v.4) — ⭐ **angels and the Spirit come down, by their Lord's permission.**
5. **The single word** (v.5) — ⭐ ***Peace*** — until dawn breaks.

## §1 — The Sending Down (v.1)

**1. أَنزَلْنَاهُ — *anzalnāhu*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**coming down / descending**" family — *nazala* = to descend; ⭐ ***manzil* = a HOUSE/stopping-place*** (where you **come down** and settle!); *nāzil* = descending; ⭐ **Form IV *anzala* = to SEND DOWN.**
- **Here it means:** "**Indeed, We sent it down on the Night of Decree.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **you own this root from two surahs, and the *form* difference matters.**
  - **Insān 76:23** — *innā naḥnu ***nazzalnā** ʿalayka'l-qurʾāna **tanzīlā*** — ⭐ **Form II (*nazzala*) = sending down **gradually, in stages**.**
  - **Nabaʾ 78:14** — *wa ***anzalnā** mina'l-muʿṣirāti māʾan thajjājā* — ⭐ **Form IV, used for **rain**.**
  - ⭐ **Qadr 97:1** — ***anzalnāhu*** — ⭐ **Form IV = a **single, complete** sending-down.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Classical scholars connect exactly this distinction to the two descents of the Qurʾān:** ⭐ **the whole revelation descended at once (*anzala*, Form IV) on this night, and then came to the Prophet ﷺ **piece by piece** over 23 years (*nazzala*, Form II).** ⭐ **Two forms of one root, two modes of revelation.**
  
  ⭐ **And *manzil* = a HOUSE** is your free hook — ⭐ **the place where you *come down* and stay.**
  
  ⭐ **Note also the parallel with Nabaʾ 78:14: ⭐ **rain sent down (*anzalnā*) → grain, gardens, life.** ⭐ **Here: revelation sent down (*anzalnāhu*) → guidance, life of a different kind.** ⭐ **Same verb, two rains.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV** (*anzala*), past, with **-nā** (We) and **-hu** (it). ⚠️ ⭐ **The *-hu* has no antecedent in the surah** — ⭐ **a deliberate omission (*iḍmār*): the Qurʾān is too obvious to name.**
- **Why this word:** *anzala* (**a single complete descent**) rather than *nazzala* — ⭐ **it marks this night as the moment of **wholesale** revelation.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Manzil* = A HOUSE** (where you come DOWN and settle). ⭐⭐ **Form IV *anzala* = ALL AT ONCE (Qadr 97:1). Form II *nazzala* = IN STAGES (Insān 76:23).** ⭐ **Two forms, two descents.** ⭐ **And the "it" is never named.**

**2. الْقَدْرِ — *al-qadr*** · v.1, 2, 3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ **a spectacular double root** — *q-d-r* means **BOTH**: ⭐ **(a) to MEASURE OUT precisely, to decree, to apportion** (*qadar* = **destiny/measure**; *taqdīr* = precise estimation) — ⭐ **AND (b) to have POWER/capacity** (*qudrah* = power; *qādir* = able). ⭐ **And a third sense: *qadr* = WORTH/rank** (*qadruhu ʿaẓīm* = his worth is great).
- **Here it means:** "**the Night of Decree / Measure / Worth**" — ⭐ **all three readings are classical.**
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SEVENTH+ APPEARANCE of this root in your study — and here it becomes a surah title.**
  
  ⭐ **Track it across your surahs:**
  - **Qiyāmah 75:4** — *bālā **qādirīna** ʿalā an nusawwiya banānah* (⭐ **POWER + precision** to remake fingertips)
  - **Insān 76:16** — *qawārīra min fiḍḍatin ***qaddarūhā** taqdīrā* (⭐ **drinks measured EXACTLY**)
  - **ʿAbasa 80:19** — *min nuṭfatin khalaqahu ***fa-qaddarah*** (⭐ **proportioned him**)
  - **Aʿlā 87:3** — *wa'lladhī ***qaddara** fa-hadā* (⭐ **measured, then guided**)
  - **Fajr 89:16** — *fa-**qadara** ʿalayhi rizqah* (⭐ **restricted his provision**)
  - **Balad 90:5** — *an lan **yaqdira** ʿalayhi aḥad* (⭐ **no one has POWER over him**)
  - ⭐ **Qadr 97:1 — *laylati'l-**QADR**.***
  
  ⭐⭐ **And ALL THREE meanings fit this night perfectly:**
  - ⭐ **The night of **MEASURING** — decrees for the year are apportioned.
  - ⭐ **The night of **POWER** — revelation descends, angels fill the earth.
  - ⭐ **The night of **WORTH** — better than a thousand months.
  
  ⭐ **The Qurʾān names the night with a word that says all three at once.** ⭐ **That's not ambiguity — it's compression.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***iḍāfah*** ("the night **of** decree"). ⭐ **Note *laylat* becomes *laylati* in genitive as the second term of the oath-less construction.**
- **Why this word:** ⭐ **no other Arabic word could carry decree, power, and worth simultaneously.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Qadr* = (a) MEASURE/decree, (b) POWER, (c) WORTH — ALL THREE.** ⭐⭐ **SEVEN+ appearances across your surahs: 75:4, 76:16, 80:19, 87:3, 89:16, 90:5, 97:1.** ⭐ **And all three meanings fit this night.**

## §2 — The Unanswerable Question (v.2)

**3. وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ — *wa mā adrāka*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** the "**knowing by perception**" family — *darā* = to become aware; ⭐ **Form IV *adrā* = to make someone know.**
- **Here it means:** "**And what can make you know what the Night of Decree is?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **you own this formula across SIX surahs now — and the *adrāka* / *yudrīka* distinction is high-yield:**
  
  ⭐ ***wa mā **YUDRĪKA*** (PRESENT tense) — ⭐ **introduces something you **could not know** and **won't be told**.** ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:3.**
  ⭐ ***wa mā **ADRĀKA*** (PAST tense) — ⭐ **introduces something **so immense** that an answer follows, but no description could be adequate.**
  - **Infiṭār 82:17–18** (twice!) · **Muṭaffifīn 83:8, 19** · **Ṭāriq 86:2** · **Balad 90:12** · ⭐ **Qadr 97:2**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And here, unusually, the answer DOES come (v.3–5).** ⭐ **But notice what the answer is: **a comparison** (better than a thousand months), **a description** (angels descend), and **one word** (peace).** ⭐ **Never a direct definition.**
  
  ⭐ **The question is doing something specific: ⭐ **it pauses you.** ⭐ **v.1 stated a fact. v.2 stops and says *do you actually grasp what that means?* ⭐ **Then v.3–5 try.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ **note the TWO *mā*'s** — ⭐ **the first is interrogative ("what?"), the second introduces the subject-clause.** ⭐ **Same structure in all six surahs.**
- **Why this word:** the **question form** conveys immensity ⭐ **more effectively than any adjective could.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***yudrīka* (present, ʿAbasa 80:3) = "you couldn't know" — NO answer follows.** ⭐ ***adrāka* (past) = "what could convey it to you?" — an answer follows.** ⭐ **SIX surahs: 82:17–18, 83:8, 83:19, 86:2, 90:12, 97:2.**

## §3 — The Measure (v.3)

**4. خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ — *khayrun min alfi shahr*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *khayr* — ⭐ **"better"** — ⚠️ **an IRREGULAR comparative** (no *afʿal* form) — ⭐ **you own it from Aʿlā 87:17** (*wa'l-ākhiratu **khayrun** wa abqā*) and **Ḍuḥā 93:4**. *alf* — **a thousand**. *shahr* — ⭐ **a MONTH** — ⭐ **from *shahara* = to make well-known/visible** (⭐ **a month is named after the **sighting** of the new moon — *ishhār* = making public!**).
- **Here it means:** "**The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the number is doing precise work, and so is the word for "month."**
  
  ⭐ **A thousand months is roughly **83 years** — ⭐ **more than a typical human lifetime.** ⭐ **So the comparison is: ⭐ **this ONE night outweighs a whole life of nights.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And notice it doesn't say "equal to" — it says ***khayrun min*** — **BETTER THAN**.** ⭐ **Not equivalent. **Superior.**
  
  ⭐ **And *shahr* (month) coming from *shahara* (to make visible/known)** is a lovely hook: ⭐ **a month is literally "the sighting" — named after the crescent you look for.** ⭐ **Same root as *mashhūr* (famous) and *ishhār* (publicity).**
  
  ⭐ **The *khayr* thread across three surahs:** ⭐ **Aʿlā 87:17 (the Hereafter is better and more lasting), Ḍuḥā 93:4 (the last is better for you), Qadr 97:3 (this night is better than a thousand months).** ⭐ **Three comparisons, all about **what actually outweighs what**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ ***khayr* and *sharr* are IRREGULAR comparatives*** — ⭐ **they don't take the *afʿal* pattern.** ⭐ **Memorize them as exceptions.** ⭐ **The *min* here is *min at-tafḍīl* (the "than" of comparison).**
- **Why this word:** ⭐ **a thousand months ≈ a lifetime** — ⭐ **the comparison is calibrated to the human span.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ **A thousand months ≈ **83 years** ≈ **a whole lifetime**.** ⭐ **And it says "**BETTER than**," not "equal to."** ⭐⭐ ***Shahr* (month) ← *shahara* = TO MAKE VISIBLE** — a month is "the sighting." ⚠️ **And *khayr* is an IRREGULAR comparative.**

## §4 — The Descent (v.4)

**5. تَنَزَّلُ — *tanazzalu*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**descending**" family again — ⭐ **but here in **Form V** (*tanazzala*).**
- **Here it means:** "**The angels and the Spirit descend therein.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the FORM is the whole picture, and it contrasts beautifully with v.1.**
  
  ⭐ **v.1 — *anzalnā* (Form IV) = ⭐ **ONE decisive sending-down, done by Allah.**
  ⭐ **v.4 — *tanazzalu* (Form V) = ⭐ **CONTINUOUS, repeated, wave-after-wave descending, done by the angels.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **One thing came down once (the revelation); many beings come down repeatedly (the angels).** ⭐ **Two verbs, two motions, three verses apart.**
  
  ⭐ **And Form V here carries a sense of ⭐ **gradual, successive arrival** — ⭐ **not a single event but a stream.** ⭐ **Picture it: the descent doesn't happen *at* a moment; it happens *throughout* the night.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note: the original form is *tatanazzalu* — ⭐ **one *tāʾ* has been DROPPED**, a standard contraction in Form V/VI present tense.** ⭐ **You met the same dropping in Layl 92:14 (*talaẓẓā* from *tatalaẓẓā*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form V present, with a **dropped *tāʾ***.** ⚠️ ⭐ **Recognize this: *tanazzalu* ← *tatanazzalu*.** ⭐ **Very common.**
- **Why this word:** *tanazzalu* (**continuous descending**) over *tanzilu* — ⭐ **it makes the night *full* of traffic, not marked by one arrival.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.1 *anzalnā* (Form IV) = ONE decisive descent. ⭐ **v.4 *tanazzalu* (Form V) = CONTINUOUS, wave after wave.** ⭐ ⚠️ **And *tanazzalu* ← *tatanazzalu* with a DROPPED tāʾ** (like *talaẓẓā*, Layl 92:14).

**6. وَالرُّوحُ — *wa'r-rūḥ*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**spirit / breath / relief**" family — *rūḥ* = spirit; ⭐ **related to *rīḥ* (wind) and *rāḥah* (REST/relief)** — ⭐ **all sharing an idea of *moving air* and *ease*.**
- **Here it means:** "**the angels and the Spirit**" — ⭐ **most commentators identify this as **Jibrīl**, mentioned separately for honour.**
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **you own this exact usage from Nabaʾ 78:38** — *yawma yaqūmu'r-**Rūḥu** wa'l-malāʾikatu ṣaffā* ("the Day **the Spirit** and the angels stand in rank").
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the contrast between the two scenes is extraordinary:**
  - **Nabaʾ 78:38** — ⭐ **the Rūḥ stands SILENT, in ranks, awaiting permission to speak.** ⭐ **A scene of awe and stillness.**
  - **Qadr 97:4** — ⭐ **the Rūḥ DESCENDS, with permission, bringing decrees.** ⭐ **A scene of motion and mercy.**
  
  ⭐ **Same being, two appearances: ⭐ **on Judgment Day he cannot move or speak; on this night he comes down freely.**
  
  ⭐ **And in BOTH, he's named **separately from** "the angels"** — ⭐ **even though he is one.** ⭐ **The separate mention is the honour.**
  
  ⭐ **And note *rāḥah* = REST** — ⭐ **the same root as the *Rūḥ*.** ⭐ **Fitting for a night that ends on the word ***salām***.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **joined to *al-malāʾikah* by *wa*** — ⭐ **a construction called *ʿaṭf al-khāṣṣ ʿala'l-ʿāmm* (joining the specific to the general) for **emphasis and honour**.**
- **Why this word:** *ar-Rūḥ* (a **majestic title**) rather than the name Jibrīl — ⭐ **it maximizes the weight of the descent.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Rūḥ*, *rīḥ* (wind), *rāḥah* (REST)** — one root. ⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:38: the Rūḥ stands SILENT on Judgment Day. Qadr 97:4: the Rūḥ DESCENDS on this night.** ⭐ **Same being, opposite postures.**

**7. بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم — *bi-idhni rabbihim*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**permission / listening**" family — *adhina* = ⭐ **BOTH "to permit" AND "to listen/give ear"** — ⭐ ***udhun* = AN EAR***; ⭐ ***adhān* = THE CALL TO PRAYER*** (⭐ **the announcement you give ear to!**); *idhn* = permission.
- **Here it means:** "**by the permission of their Lord.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the root's double life is the hook — and it's one you hear five times a day.**
  
  ⭐ ***Udhun* = an EAR. *Adhān* = the CALL TO PRAYER. *Idhn* = PERMISSION.*** ⭐ **All one root.** ⭐ **Because permission is something you **give ear to** — you listen for the go-ahead.**
  
  ⭐ **And the theological weight of *bi-idhn* here:** ⭐ **even the angels — even the Rūḥ — descend only **by permission**.** ⭐ **Nothing moves on its own authority.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare Nabaʾ 78:38** — *lā yatakallamūna **illā man adhina** lahu'r-Raḥmān* ("**none speak except whom the Most Merciful permits**"). ⭐⭐ **SAME ROOT, same restriction, and again involving the Rūḥ!** ⭐ **In Nabaʾ, permission is needed to **speak**; here, permission is needed to **descend**.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **the *bi-* is instrumental/circumstantial** ("by means of / with"). ⭐ ***Idhni*** is genitive after *bi-*, and *rabbihim* genitive by *iḍāfah*.
- **Why this word:** *idhn* keeps ⭐ **the hierarchy visible** even in a scene of overwhelming honour.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Udhun* = AN EAR. *ADHĀN* = the call to prayer. *Idhn* = PERMISSION.** ⭐ **All one root — permission is what you *give ear to*.** ⭐⭐ **And Nabaʾ 78:38 used the same root: none SPEAK except by permission. Here: none DESCEND except by permission.**

**8. مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ — *min kulli amr*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**command / matter / affair**" family — *amara* = to command; *amr* = **a command** AND **a matter/affair**; ⭐ ***amīr* = a commander***; *imārah* = leadership.
- **Here it means:** "**with every matter / from every command.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐ **the phrase is deliberately comprehensive.**
  
  ⭐ ***Kulli amr* = "EVERY matter"*** — ⭐ **no category excluded.** ⭐ **The decrees descending cover **all affairs**, not a selection.**
  
  ⭐ **And this is where the *qadr* (measure/decree) of v.1 becomes concrete:** ⭐ **the night is named "the Night of Measuring," and here you see **the measuring being delivered**.** ⭐ **The title and the action match.**
  
  ⭐ **You own *amr* from Infiṭār 82:19** — *wa'l-**amru** yawmaʾidhin lillāh* ("**the command**, that Day, belongs to Allah"). ⭐ **Infiṭār: the command is His on Judgment Day. Qadr: His commands descend on this night.** ⭐ **Same word, both directions.**
  
  ⭐ **And from Nāziʿāt 79:5** — *fa'l-**mudabbirāti amrā*** ("those who **arrange the affair**"). ⭐⭐ **The angels who *manage the amr* (Nāziʿāt) are the ones who *descend with every amr* (Qadr).** ⭐ **Same beings, same word, two surahs.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ **the *min* here is debated** — ⭐ **read as *bi-* ("with every matter") or as partitive/causal ("concerning every matter").** ⭐ **Both are classical; the sense of comprehensiveness is constant.**
- **Why this word:** *amr* (**command AND matter**) covers ⭐ **both the instruction and the thing instructed about.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Amīr* = A COMMANDER.* ⭐ **Same root.** ⭐⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:5: the angels who ARRANGE the *amr*. Qadr 97:4: they descend WITH every *amr*.** ⭐ **Same beings, same word.** ⭐ **And Infiṭār 82:19: the *amr* belongs to Allah.**

## §5 — The Single Word (v.5)

**9. سَلَامٌ هِيَ — *salāmun hiya*** · v.5
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**wholeness / soundness / safety**" family — *salima* = **to be safe, whole, free from defect**; ⭐ ***islām*** = submission that brings wholeness; ⭐ ***muslim*** = one who submits; ⭐ ***salām*** = peace; ⭐ **and ***as-Salām* — a NAME OF ALLAH** (The Source of Peace!).
- **Here it means:** "**Peace it is** — until the emergence of dawn."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the most striking word-order in the surah, and it's easy to miss in translation.**
  
  ⭐ **The Arabic is ***salāmun hiya*** — ⭐ **"PEACE — it is."** ⭐ **The predicate comes **FIRST**, before the subject.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **In Arabic, fronting = EMPHASIS and RESTRICTION.** ⭐ **So it isn't "it is peaceful" — ⭐ **it's "**PEACE** is what it is," or "**nothing but peace**."** ⭐ **The night is *defined* by the word, not merely described by it.**
  
  ⭐ **And think about how much the surah packs into one word:** ⭐ **after angels, the Spirit, decrees for every matter, a night worth a lifetime — ⭐ **the summary is one syllable: *salām*.**
  
  ⭐ **And the root means **WHOLENESS**, not just absence of conflict.** ⭐ ***Salima* = to be intact, sound, undamaged.*** ⭐ **So *salām* is a night with **nothing broken in it**.
  
  ⭐ **You say this root constantly: ⭐ ***as-salāmu ʿalaykum***, ***islām***, ***muslim***, ⭐ **and *as-Salām* is one of the Divine Names.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***khabar muqaddam*** — **a fronted predicate.** ⚠️ ⭐ **Normal order would be *hiya salāmun*; the reversal is deliberate emphasis.** ⭐ **You own this fronting technique from Qiyāmah 75:12, 75:30, Nāziʿāt 79:44, Ghāshiyah 88:25, Sharḥ 94:8, ʿAlaq 96:8.**
- **Why this word:** ⭐ **one word, fronted, is the most economical possible summary** of everything v.1–4 described.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Salāmun hiya* = "PEACE — it is."** ⭐ **The predicate is FRONTED = "nothing BUT peace."** ⭐⭐ **And *salima* = TO BE WHOLE/INTACT** — ⭐ **not just "no conflict" but "nothing broken."** ⭐ **Same root as *islām*, *muslim*, *as-Salām*.**

**10. حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ — *ḥattā maṭlaʿi'l-fajr*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *maṭlaʿ* — ⭐ the "**rising / emerging / coming into view**" family — *ṭalaʿa* = **to rise** (of the sun), **to appear**; ⭐ ***ṭulūʿ ash-shams* = SUNRISE***; ⭐ **and *iṭṭalaʿa* = to look into / become informed** (to have something **rise** into your view!). *fajr* — ⭐ **DAWN** — ⭐ **from *fajara* = TO SPLIT OPEN** (light **cleaving** the dark).
- **Here it means:** "**until the emergence of dawn.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the final word closes a root-thread that runs through SEVEN of your surahs.**
  
  ⭐ **The *f-j-r* root, tracked:**
  - **Qiyāmah 75:5** — *li-**yafjura** amāmah* (⭐ **bursting into sin**)
  - **Insān 76:6** — ***yufajjirūnahā*** (⭐ **a spring made to gush**)
  - **ʿAbasa 80:42** — *al-**fajarah*** (⭐ **the wicked**)
  - **Infiṭār 82:3** — ***fujjirat*** (⭐ **seas burst their bounds**)
  - **Infiṭār 82:14** — *al-**fujjār*** (⭐ **the wicked**)
  - **Fajr 89:1** — *wa'l-**fajr*** (⭐ **the DAWN**, as an oath)
  - **Shams 91:8** — ***fujūrahā*** (⭐ **the soul's capacity to burst limits**)
  - ⭐ **Qadr 97:5 — *maṭlaʿi'l-**FAJR*** (⭐ **dawn ENDS the night of peace**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **EIGHT appearances across seven surahs.** ⭐ **One root: sinning, a heavenly spring, the wicked (twice), the seas, the soul's dark potential, and **daybreak** (twice).**
  
  ⭐ **And note how the surah ENDS:** ⭐ **not at midnight, not at a fixed hour — ⭐ **at the moment light **splits** the dark.** ⭐ **The night of peace is bounded by the first crack of dawn.**
  
  ⭐ **And *maṭlaʿ* is a **noun of time/place*** — ⭐ **"the *point* of rising."** ⭐ **A precise boundary, not a vague ending.**
  
  ⭐ ***Ṭalaʿa* → *iṭṭalaʿa* (to become informed)*** ⭐ **is a lovely bonus: knowledge *rises* into view, like the sun.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ḥattā*** here is a preposition of limit ("**until**"), so *maṭlaʿi* is **genitive**. ⭐ **Noun of time on the *mafʿal* pattern.**
- **Why this word:** *maṭlaʿ al-fajr* gives ⭐ **an exact, observable, natural boundary** — ⭐ **one anyone can verify by looking east.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Fajr* = dawn, because light SPLITS the dark** — ⭐ **EIGHT appearances across SEVEN surahs: 75:5, 76:6, 80:42, 82:3, 82:14, 89:1, 91:8, 97:5.** ⭐⭐ **And *ṭulūʿ ash-shams* = SUNRISE; *iṭṭalaʿa* = to become informed** (knowledge **rises** into view). ⭐ **The night of peace is bounded by the first crack of light.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **أَنزَلْنَا (v.1, Form IV) vs. نَزَّلْنَا (Insān 76:23, Form II)** — ⚠️ **same root, different forms, different modes.** ⭐ **Form IV = ALL AT ONCE. Form II = IN STAGES.** **Classical scholars tie this to the two descents of the Qurʾān.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْقَدْرِ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **carries THREE meanings at once: MEASURE/decree, POWER, and WORTH.** ⭐ **Don't pick one — all three are classical and all three fit.**
3. ⭐⭐ **تَنَزَّلُ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **originally *tatanazzalu*, with one *tāʾ* DROPPED.** ⭐ **Same contraction as *talaẓẓā* (Layl 92:14).**
4. ⭐⭐ **سَلَامٌ هِيَ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **the predicate is FRONTED.** ⭐ **Not "it is peace" but "**PEACE** is what it is" — a restriction.**
5. ⭐⭐ **أَنزَلْنَا**هُ** (v.1)** — ⚠️ **the "it" is NEVER named.** ⭐ **A deliberate omission — the Qurʾān is too obvious to specify.**
6. ⭐ **مَا أَدْرَاكَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **PAST tense = an answer follows.** ⭐ **Compare *mā yudrīka* (ʿAbasa 80:3, present) = no answer follows. **The tense changes the function.**
7. ⭐ **بِإِذْنِ (v.4)** — from the root of ***udhun* (EAR) and *ADHĀN*.** ⚠️ **Permission is what you "give ear to."**
8. ⭐ **مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **the *min* is debated** (as *bi-*, or partitive). ⭐ **Both classical; the comprehensiveness is the constant.**
9. ⭐ **خَيْرٌ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **IRREGULAR comparative** (no *afʿal* form). Same for *sharr*. **A memorized exception.**
10. ⭐ **مَطْلَعِ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **a noun of TIME/PLACE** ("the point of rising"), not a verb. ⭐ **From *ṭalaʿa* — the same root as *ṭulūʿ ash-shams* and *iṭṭalaʿa* (to become informed).**
