# Surah Al-Jinn (72) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–28) · **Complete coverage — every important word**

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It's a review of the Qurʾān written by a non-human audience — and their creed is Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ, told as a story.**

⭐ **v.1:** *innā samiʿnā **qurʾānan ʿajabā*** — "We have heard a **WONDROUS** Qurʾān."

⭐ The jinn listened once, and immediately: believed (v.2), declared His majesty (v.3), denied He has a consort or child (v.3), admitted they had been fooled (v.4–5), and said "we will never associate **anyone** with our Lord" (v.2).

⭐⭐⭐ **Now count the word *aḥad* in this surah — it appears SIX times** (v.2, 7, 18, 20, 22, 26). ⭐ **And v.3 is Ikhlāṣ in other words:** *mā'ttakhadha ṣāḥibatan wa lā waladā* — "He has taken **no consort and no child**" = *lam yalid wa lam yūlad*. ⭐ **You just finished Ikhlāṣ; Al-Jinn is the same creed, spoken by the first beings who heard it and believed on the spot.**

⭐⭐ **And the shame in it:** Quraysh heard the Qurʾān for years and called it magic (Muddaththir 74:24). ⭐ **A band of jinn heard it ONCE and called it a wonder.**

⭐ **The rhyme:** almost every verse ends in **-adā** — *ʿajabā, aḥadā, waladā, shaṭaṭā, rahaqā, raṣadā, rashadā, qidadā, ḥaṭabā, ghadaqā, ṣaʿadā, libadā, multaḥadā, amadā, ʿadadā* — ⭐ **a surah full of verse-ending nouns, exactly the category you study.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **Humans heard it for years and called it magic. Jinn heard it once and called it *ʿajab*.** ⭐ ***Aḥad* ×6 — Ikhlāṣ, narrated.**

## The Big Picture

*⭐ Context (Bukhārī, from Ibn ʿAbbās): the Prophet ﷺ was travelling with companions toward the market of ʿUkāẓ. The jinn had suddenly found the sky sealed with meteors and went searching for the cause. At Nakhlah they found him leading Fajr prayer, listened — and believed. He did not see them; he learned of it **by revelation** (v.1).*

1. **The listening** (v.1–2) — a wondrous Qurʾān that guides to maturity.
2. **Their creed** (v.3–7) — His majesty, no consort or child, the lies they once believed.
3. **The sealed sky** (v.8–10) — guards, meteors, and a humble "we don't know."
4. **Two kinds of jinn** (v.11–15) — righteous and otherwise; submitters and deviators.
5. **The promise of rain** (v.16–17) — uprightness brings abundance — and a test.
6. **The mosques and the crowd** (v.18–19) — worship belongs to Allah; they crowded around His servant.
7. **The Messenger's limits** (v.20–23) — *say:* I own no harm or guidance for you; I only convey.
8. **Who is weaker?** (v.24–25) — they'll see; and *I don't know when.*
9. **The guarded Unseen** (v.26–28) — revelation escorted by guards; everything counted.

## §1 — The Listening (v.1–2)

**1. قُلْ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ — *qul ūḥiya ilayya*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** the "**swift, subtle communication**" family — *waḥā* = to signal quickly and secretly; ***waḥy* = REVELATION**.
- **Here it means:** "**Say: It has been revealed to me…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ the **passive** *ūḥiya* is the whole point of the opening. The Prophet ﷺ **did not see** the jinn listening — **he was told.** ⭐ The surah is itself a piece of news about the unseen, delivered through the unseen channel. ⭐ You own *awḥā* from **Zalzalah 99:5** (*bi-anna rabbaka **awḥā** lahā*) — the revelation-word used for **the earth**. ⭐⭐ The same word: revelation to the **earth** (so it testifies), and revelation to the **Prophet ﷺ** (so he knows who listened).
- **Grammar bite:** *ūḥiya* = **Form IV passive**, weak-final. ⭐ *ilayya* = *ilā* + *-ya* (to me), the *yāʾ* doubled.
- **Why this exact word:** *waḥy* rather than "I learned" — it marks the jinn story as **revealed knowledge**, not observation.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ **He didn't SEE them — it was REVEALED to him (*ūḥiya*).** ⭐ ***Waḥy* to the earth (99:5) and to the Prophet ﷺ (72:1).**

**2. اسْتَمَعَ — *istamaʿa*** · v.1 (and v.9)
- **Root feeling:** the "**hearing**" family — *samiʿa* = to hear; **Form VIII *istamaʿa* = to LISTEN ATTENTIVELY, deliberately, with effort**; *istimāʿ* = listening; *mustamiʿ* = a listener.
- **Here it means:** "**…that a band of jinn listened [attentively].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Hearing vs. listening.** *Samiʿa* (Form I) is sound reaching your ears; ***istamaʿa*** (Form VIII) is **turning your whole attention toward it**. ⭐ The jinn didn't overhear — they **listened**. ⭐⭐ And the **same verb returns in v.9** — *fa-man **yastamiʿi**'l-āna* — for jinn trying to **eavesdrop** on the heavens. ⭐⭐⭐ **One Form VIII verb, two kinds of listening: stealing secrets from the sky (v.9), and receiving the Qurʾān (v.1).** The first earned a meteor; the second earned guidance.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form VIII = effort/for oneself** (the infixed *-t-*). ⭐ *Anna-hu'stamaʿa* — the *-hu* is *ḍamīr ash-shaʾn* ("**that** [the matter is]…").
- **Why this exact word:** *istamaʿa* rather than *samiʿa* — because their **attentiveness** is the contrast with Quraysh's turning away.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Samiʿa* = HEARD. *Istamaʿa* = LISTENED (on purpose).** ⭐⭐ **v.1: listening to the Qurʾān → guidance. v.9: listening to steal from the sky → a meteor.** Same verb.

**3. نَفَرٌ — *nafar*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** the "**rushing out / setting off in a group**" family — *nafara* = to go out, to rush forth; ***nafar* = a small band (classically 3 to 10)**; ***nafīr* = a CALL TO ARMS / general mobilization**; ***nufūr* = aversion** (rushing away from something).
- **Here it means:** "**a small band [of jinn]**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ the root holds **both directions of rushing** — *toward* (a *nafar* setting out, a *nafīr* mobilizing) and *away* (*nufūr*, aversion). ⭐⭐⭐ And it returns in **Muddaththir 74:50**: *ka-annahum ḥumurun **mustanfirah*** — the deniers as **wild donkeys bolting in panic**. ⭐ **Same root: a *nafar* of jinn rushing TOWARD the Qurʾān (72:1); humans *mustanfir* — bolting AWAY from it (74:50).** ⭐ And the number: just **a small band** — not a nation. Small, sincere, and changed.
- **Grammar bite:** *nafarun* = **indefinite** ("*a* band"), subject of *istamaʿa*. ⭐ A **collective noun** (no singular from its root).
- **Why this exact word:** *nafar* names a **small group on the move**, which is exactly what they were — travellers searching.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Nafīr* = a CALL TO ARMS; *nufūr* = AVERSION.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Jinn 72:1: a *nafar* rushes TOWARD the Qurʾān. Muddaththir 74:50: humans *mustanfirah* — bolt AWAY.** Same root.

**4. الْجِنِّ — *al-jinn*** · v.1 (and throughout)
- **Root feeling:** the "**COVERING / concealing**" family — *janna* = to cover; *jinn* = the **hidden** beings; *jannah* = a garden (covered by foliage); *janīn* = a fetus (covered in the womb); *majnūn* = one whose mind is covered; *junnah* = a shield.
- **Here it means:** "**[a band] of the jinn**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ You own this root from **Takwīr 81:22** (*bi-**majnūn***), **Fajr 89:30** (***jannatī***), and **Nās 114:6** (*mina'l-**jinnah** wa'n-nās*). ⭐⭐⭐ **Nās ended the Qurʾān with whisperers "from the jinn and people" — Al-Jinn shows you jinn who became believers.** ⭐ The hidden beings aren't all enemies — some heard and submitted (v.14).
- **Grammar bite:** *al-jinn* = collective; genitive after *min*. ⭐ Compare *al-jinnah* (Nās 114:6) — same meaning, collective with *tāʾ*.
- **Why this exact word:** the surah is named after them because ⭐ **the hidden world's testimony is the argument**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Janna* = TO COVER: jinn (hidden), jannah (shaded garden), janīn (fetus), majnūn (covered mind), junnah (shield).** ⭐ **Nās 114:6 warned of whispering jinn; Al-Jinn shows believing ones.**

**5. قُرْآنًا عَجَبًا — *qurʾānan ʿajabā*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** the "**WONDER / astonishment**" family — *ʿajiba* = to marvel; ***ʿajab* = wonder (the noun itself)**; *ʿajīb* = wonderful; *taʿajjub* = astonishment; ⭐ ***ʿajab adh-dhanab* = the tailbone** (the "wonder" from which man is rebuilt, per hadith!).
- **Here it means:** "**We have heard a wondrous Qurʾān.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The grammar is the praise.** They don't say *qurʾānan **ʿajīban*** (a wonderful Qurʾān, using an adjective). They say *qurʾānan **ʿajabā*** — using **the verbal noun "wonder" itself** as the description. ⭐ This is ***al-waṣf bi'l-maṣdar*** — describing something by a noun = **"a Qurʾān that IS wonder itself."** ⭐ Like saying "he is *justice*" instead of "he is just." ⭐⭐ And note they call it **by name — *Qurʾān***. Their very first word about it is its title: the **Recitation** (ʿAlaq 96:1, *iqraʾ*).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***waṣf bi'l-maṣdar*** — a *maṣdar* used as an adjective = **maximum intensity**. Both accusative (object of *samiʿnā*).
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿajab* (the noun) instead of *ʿajīb* (the adjective) — ⭐ **because the adjective would allow degrees; the noun says it is wonder itself.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Not "a WONDERFUL Qurʾān" — "a Qurʾān that IS WONDER."** ⭐ **A noun used as an adjective = intensity.** ⭐ ***ʿAjab adh-dhanab* = the TAILBONE** — the "wonder" bone from which man is rebuilt.

**6. يَهْدِي إِلَى الرُّشْدِ — *yahdī ila'r-rushd*** · v.2 (and *rashad* in v.10, 14, 21)
- **Root feeling:** the "**RIGHT CONDUCT / maturity / sound judgment**" family — *rashada* = to be rightly guided, to reach sound judgment; ***rushd* = maturity of mind, right guidance**; ⭐ ***sinn ar-rushd* = the age of legal majority**; ⭐ ***al-Khulafāʾ ar-Rāshidūn* = the Rightly-Guided Caliphs**; *murshid* = a guide/mentor; *irshād* = guidance (⭐ *irshādāt* = **instructions**, e.g. on a medicine box!).
- **Here it means:** "**It guides to right conduct.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Rushd* isn't just "guidance" — it's MATURITY.** ⭐ The Qurʾān, in the jinn's words, **grows you up**: it brings you to the age of sound judgment. ⭐ Its opposite is ***ghayy*** — reckless error (*qad tabayyana'r-**rushdu** mina'l-**ghayy***, 2:256). ⭐⭐⭐ **The *r-sh-d* root appears FOUR times in this surah** — v.2 (*rushd*), v.10 (*rashadā*), v.14 (*taḥarraw **rashadā***), v.21 (*ḍarran wa lā **rashadā***). ⭐ It's the surah's thread: **what is maturity, who gives it, who seeks it, and who can't give it.**
- **Grammar bite:** *yahdī* (present = ongoing) + *ilā*. ⭐ *Rushd* (*fuʿl*) and *rashad* (*faʿal*) are **two verbal-noun forms of one root**, same meaning — the second carries the *-adā* rhyme.
- **Why this exact word:** *rushd* (maturity) rather than *hudā* — ⭐ **because the jinn experienced it as waking up, growing up.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Sinn ar-RUSHD* = the AGE OF MAJORITY; *ar-RĀSHIDŪN* = the Rightly-Guided Caliphs; *irshādāt* = INSTRUCTIONS.** ⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān GROWS YOU UP.** ⭐ ***R-sh-d* ×4 in this surah (v.2, 10, 14, 21).**

**7. فَآمَنَّا بِهِ ۖ وَلَن نُّشْرِكَ بِرَبِّنَا أَحَدًا — *fa-āmannā bihi wa lan nushrika bi-rabbinā aḥadā*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *āmannā* — the "**safety / trust**" family — *amn* (security), ***īmān* (faith)** (you own it from Tīn 95:3, Quraysh 106:4). *nushrik* — the "**PARTNERSHIP / sharing**" family — *sharika* = to share; ***sharīk* = a PARTNER**; ⭐ ***sharikah* = a COMPANY** (business!); *shirk* = associating partners with Allah. *aḥad* — **anyone / the One**.
- **Here it means:** "**So we believed in it, and we will never associate anyone with our Lord.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Look at the speed.** *Fa-āmannā* — the ***fa*** means "**and so, immediately**." ⭐ Heard → believed. No debate, no delay. ⭐⭐ And ***lan*** = "**will NEVER**" — the strongest future negation. ⭐⭐⭐ And ***aḥadā*** — **the first of SIX** in this surah. ⭐ You just learned from **Ikhlāṣ 112:4** that *aḥad* in a **negative** sentence = "**anyone at all**." ⭐ **Their first creed: "we will never share our Lord with ANYONE."** ⭐ And ***sharikah* = a COMPANY** — *shirk* is treating Allah like a business with **shareholders**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lan* + subjunctive = emphatic future negation** ("never"). ⚠️ *nushrika* takes **bi-** for the One associated with.
- **Why this exact word:** *shirk* — ⭐ **the exact sin the Qurʾān was sent to remove**, named in their very first sentence.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHARIKAH* = a COMPANY** — *shirk* = treating Allah like a firm with shareholders. ⭐⭐ ***Fa-āmannā* = heard → IMMEDIATELY believed.** ⭐ ***Aḥadā* — first of SIX in this surah** (Ikhlāṣ narrated).

## §2 — Their Creed (v.3–7)

*⭐ A note on v.1–14: each statement begins *wa annahu / wa annā* ("and that…"). There are **two established recitations** — *wa **a**nnā* (fatḥah: continuing "it was revealed to me THAT…") and *wa **i**nnā* (kasrah: the jinn's own words, "and indeed we…"). Both are authentic; the meaning is the same either way.*

**8. وَأَنَّهُ تَعَالَىٰ جَدُّ رَبِّنَا — *wa annahu taʿālā jaddu rabbinā*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ ***jadd*** — a word with three famous meanings: **(a) MAJESTY / grandeur**; **(b) a GRANDFATHER** (⭐ *jadd* = grandfather in everyday Arabic!); **(c) good FORTUNE / luck**. *Taʿālā* — **Form VI of *ʿalā* = to be exalted, high above** (⭐ *al-Aʿlā*, Aʿlā 87:1).
- **Here it means:** "**And exalted is the majesty of our Lord.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **You say this phrase in every prayer.** ⭐ The opening supplication of ṣalāh (*duʿāʾ al-istiftāḥ / thanāʾ*): *subḥānaka'llāhumma wa bi-ḥamdik, wa tabāraka'smuk, **wa taʿālā jadduk**, wa lā ilāha ghayruk* — ⭐⭐ "**and exalted is Your MAJESTY**." ⭐ Same two words the jinn used.
  ⭐⭐ And the post-prayer *dhikr*: *wa lā yanfaʿu **dha'l-jaddi** minka'l-**jadd*** — "**the FORTUNE of the fortunate cannot avail him against You.**" ⭐ There *jadd* = **fortune/wealth**; here *jadd* = **majesty**. ⭐ **Same word, two senses, both in your daily prayers.**
  ⭐ The idea: the jinn are saying His **greatness** is far too high to need a consort or child (next word).
- **Grammar bite:** *taʿālā* = Form VI past, weak-final. ⭐ *jaddu* = subject (nominative), in *iḍāfah* with *rabbinā*.
- **Why this exact word:** *jadd* (majesty) — ⭐ **a rare, weighty word**, chosen by beings who had just been stunned into awe.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"*Wa taʿālā JADDUK*" — the OPENING DUʿĀʾ of your prayer.** Same phrase. ⭐⭐ ***Jadd* = MAJESTY / GRANDFATHER / FORTUNE.** ⭐ **Post-prayer: "the *jadd* (fortune) of the fortunate won't help against You."**

**9. مَا اتَّخَذَ صَاحِبَةً وَلَا وَلَدًا — *ma'ttakhadha ṣāḥibatan wa lā waladā*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *ittakhadha* — **Form VIII of *akhadha* (to take)** = **to TAKE FOR ONESELF, to adopt**. *ṣāḥibah* — **a female companion / wife** (*ṣāḥib* = companion). *walad* — **a child** (*walada* = to beget).
- **Here it means:** "**He has not taken a wife or a child.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **This is Ikhlāṣ, in the jinn's words.** ⭐ **Ikhlāṣ 112:3** — *lam **yalid** wa lam yūlad* (He neither begets nor was begotten). ⭐ **Jinn 72:3** — *ma'ttakhadha ṣāḥibatan wa lā **waladā*** (He has taken no wife and no child). ⭐⭐ **Same root (*w-l-d*), same denial** — and the jinn add the logical step: no **wife** either, because a child needs a mother. ⭐ You own *ṣāḥibah* from **ʿAbasa 80:36** (*wa **ṣāḥibatihi** wa banīh* — the wife you flee on that Day) and *ittakhadha* from **Insān 76:29** (*fa-man shāʾa'**ttakhadha** ilā rabbihi sabīlā*).
- **Grammar bite:** *mā* + past = negation. ⭐ ***Ittakhadha*** = Form VIII, with the *hamzah* of *akhadha* assimilated into a doubled *tāʾ*.
- **Why this exact word:** *ittakhadha* ("**take** for oneself") — ⭐ **it denies even adoption**, not only biological begetting.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Ikhlāṣ 112:3: *lam yalid*. Jinn 72:3: no wife, no *walad*.** ⭐ **The jinn's creed = Ikhlāṣ.** ⭐ ***Ittakhadha* = take FOR ONESELF** — denies even adoption.

**10. سَفِيهُنَا — *safīhunā*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** the "**LIGHTNESS / flimsiness → foolishness**" family — *safiha* = to be light-headed, foolish; ⭐ originally ***thawb safīh* = a FLIMSY, thinly-woven cloth**; ***safah* = folly**; *sufahāʾ* = fools (2:13, 2:142).
- **Here it means:** "**And that our foolish one used to say about Allah an outrageous falsehood.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ The root pictures **thin, flimsy cloth** — a mind with **no weight to it**, that anything can blow through. ⭐ Classical readings: the "*safīh*" is **Iblīs**, or the foolish leaders among the jinn. ⭐⭐ **Notice the jinn's honesty:** they call their own former leader "**OUR fool**." No defending their tribe — immediate disavowal. ⭐ Compare **Kāfirūn 109** — disavowal of false worship. ⭐ And note *kāna yaqūlu* = **used to say, habitually** (past continuous).
- **Grammar bite:** *safīh* = *faʿīl* adjective used as a noun + *-nā* (our). ⭐ ***Kāna + present = habitual past*** ("used to say").
- **Why this exact word:** *safīh* (**flimsy-minded**) rather than *kādhib* (liar) — ⭐ **it diagnoses lightness of mind, not only dishonesty.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Thawb safīh* = FLIMSY CLOTH.** ⭐ ***Safah* = a mind with NO WEIGHT.** ⭐ **They call their own leader "OUR FOOL" — instant disavowal.**

**11. شَطَطًا — *shaṭaṭā*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** the "**going FAR beyond the limit**" family — *shaṭṭa* = to go far away, to exceed; ⭐ ***shaṭṭ* = a RIVERBANK / shore** (the far edge — ⭐ ***Shaṭṭ al-ʿArab***, the river in Iraq!); ***shaṭaṭ* = excess, going way beyond the truth**.
- **Here it means:** "**…an outrageous / extreme falsehood [about Allah].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ The image: **a statement so far from the truth it has reached the far bank.** ⭐ Not a small error — a claim that has **crossed all the way over**. ⭐ Compare the *ṭaghā* thread (water **bursting its banks**: Nabaʾ 78:22, Fajr 89:11, ʿAlaq 96:6). ⭐⭐ **Two water-and-bank images for excess: *ṭaghā* (overflowing the bank) and *shaṭaṭ* (going to the far bank).**
- **Grammar bite:** *shaṭaṭan* = a verbal noun as **cognate-type accusative** ("a saying of extreme excess"), carrying the *-adā*-family rhyme.
- **Why this exact word:** *shaṭaṭ* (**distance** from truth) — ⭐ **the falsehood is measured by how FAR it went.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***SHAṬṬ al-ʿArab* — a river's far BANK.** ⭐ ***Shaṭaṭ* = a claim gone all the way to the far bank of falsehood.** ⭐ **Compare *ṭaghā*: water OVER the bank.**

**12. وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّا ... كَذِبًا — *wa annā ẓanannā… kadhibā*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *ẓanna* — the "**supposing / thinking**" family — **ranges from assumption to certainty depending on context**. *kadhib* — **a LIE**.
- **Here it means:** "**And we had assumed that humans and jinn would never speak a lie about Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A confession of NAÏVETY.** ⭐ The jinn explain why they believed the *safīh*: ⭐ "**we assumed no one would DARE lie about Allah.**" ⭐ They trusted because the subject was too sacred to lie about. ⭐⭐ It's a deeply human (and jinn) mistake: **assuming that anything said in God's name must be true.** ⭐ ⚠️ **TRAP — *ẓanna* changes meaning:** here it's **assumption** (and a wrong one); in **Qiyāmah 75:28** — *wa **ẓanna** annahu'l-firāq* — it means **certainty** (the dying man *knows* it's the parting). ⭐ **Context decides.**
- **Grammar bite:** *ẓanannā* = past, 1st plural (doubled *nūn*). ⭐ *lan taqūla* = **lan + subjunctive** ("would never say").
- **Why this exact word:** *ẓann* — ⭐ **admitting their belief was a mere assumption** is part of their repentance.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"We ASSUMED nobody would lie about Allah" — the trust that misled them.** ⚠️ ⭐ ***Ẓann* here = ASSUMPTION; in Qiyāmah 75:28 = CERTAINTY.**

**13. الْإِنسُ — *al-ins*** · v.5, 6
- **Root feeling:** the "**familiarity / intimacy / being seen**" family — *anisa* = to be familiar, at ease with; ***uns* = intimacy, companionship**; ***ins* = humankind**; *insān* = a human; ⭐ *istiʾnās* = seeking familiarity / (modern) **domestication** of animals; ⭐ and the classical play: *insān* ← ***nisyān*** (forgetting) — from Nās.
- **Here it means:** "**humans**" (paired with *al-jinn*)
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ins* and *jinn* are named as OPPOSITES:** ⭐ ***ins* = the SEEN, the familiar** (from *uns*, being at ease with); ⭐ ***jinn* = the HIDDEN** (from *janna*, to cover). ⭐ **Visible vs. invisible creation, in two words.** ⭐ And in v.6 the jinn reveal a human failing: humans sought refuge in **them** — the seen begging the unseen.
- **Grammar bite:** *al-ins* = collective noun (nominative, subject of *taqūla*).
- **Why this exact word:** *ins* (not *nās*) — ⭐ **because it pairs with *jinn* as seen vs. hidden.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INS* = the SEEN, the familiar (*uns* = intimacy). *JINN* = the HIDDEN (*janna* = to cover).** ⭐ **Visible vs. invisible creation.**

**14. يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالٍ مِّنَ الْجِنِّ — *yaʿūdhūna bi-rijālin mina'l-jinn*** · v.6
- **Root feeling:** the "**SEEKING REFUGE / clinging for safety**" family — *ʿādha* = to take refuge, to cling like a young animal to its mother; ***maʿādha'llāh!*** = "God forbid!" (literally "refuge in Allah!").
- **Here it means:** "**And there were men of humankind who used to seek refuge in men of the jinn…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE PERFECT PAYOFF from the surah you just finished.** ⭐ **Falaq 113:1 & Nās 114:1** — ***qul aʿūdhu** bi-rabbi'l-falaq / bi-rabbi'n-nās* — "**seek refuge in the LORD**." ⭐ **Jinn 72:6** — ***yaʿūdhūna** bi-rijālin mina'l-jinn* — "**they sought refuge in JINN**." ⭐⭐ **Same verb — the true refuge vs. the false one.** ⭐ The historical practice: a pre-Islamic traveller stopping in a valley at night would say, "**I seek refuge in the master of this valley from the foolish of his people**" — addressing the jinn believed to own the place. ⭐ And the jinn themselves testify: **it didn't protect them** (next word). ⭐⭐ **The Muʿawwidhatān are the correction: seek refuge in the Lord of the jinn, not in the jinn.**
- **Grammar bite:** *kāna… yaʿūdhūna* = **habitual past** ("used to seek refuge"). ⭐ Hollow verb (ʿ-w-dh). ⭐ *bi-* = **in** (the refuge sought).
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿādha* — ⭐ **so the reader instantly hears Falaq and Nās against it.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Falaq/Nās: *aʿūdhu* bi-RABB. Jinn 72:6: *yaʿūdhūna* bi-JINN.** ⭐ **True refuge vs. false refuge — same verb.** ⭐⭐ **Pre-Islamic travellers: "I seek refuge in the master of this valley."**

**15. فَزَادُوهُمْ رَهَقًا — *fa-zādūhum rahaqā*** · v.6 (and *rahaqā* in v.13)
- **Root feelings:** *zāda* — **to INCREASE** (*ziyādah*). *rahaq* — the "**overtaking / burdening / covering with distress**" family — *rahiqa* = to overtake, to come upon; ***rahaq* = burden, fear, oppression, folly**; ⭐ ***murāhiq* = an ADOLESCENT** (one "**approaching**/overtaking" maturity!); ⭐ ***murhaq* = EXHAUSTED** (modern: *anā murhaq* = "I'm worn out").
- **Here it means:** "**…so they only increased them in burden / fear / folly.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The refuge backfired.** ⭐ They sought safety in the jinn — and the jinn (or the practice) **increased** their fear and oppression. ⭐ Classical readings: the jinn grew **arrogant** ("even humans seek refuge in us!") and humans grew more **afraid**. ⭐⭐ **False refuge doesn't just fail — it adds weight.** ⭐ You own *rahaq* from **ʿAbasa 80:41** — *tarhaquhā qatarah* (blackness **overtakes** their faces), and it returns in **v.13** (*fa-lā yakhāfu bakhsan wa lā **rahaqā*** — the believer fears no burden) and **Muddaththir 74:17** (*sa-**urhiquhu** ṣaʿūdā*). ⭐⭐⭐ **v.6: the false refuge ADDED *rahaq*. v.13: the believer has NO *rahaq*.** Same word, seven verses apart.
  ⭐ And *zāda* — you own it from **Nabaʾ 78:30** (*fa-lan **nazīdakum** illā ʿadhābā* — "We'll only INCREASE you in punishment").
- **Grammar bite:** *zāda* takes **two objects** (*-hum* and *rahaqan*). ⭐ Hollow verb.
- **Why this exact word:** *rahaq* — ⭐ **a heavy, overtaking burden — the exact opposite of the relief they sought.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Murhaq* = EXHAUSTED; *murāhiq* = an ADOLESCENT.** ⭐⭐ **False refuge ADDED weight (v.6); true faith removes it (v.13: "fears no *rahaq*").** ⭐ **Also ʿAbasa 80:41, Muddaththir 74:17.**

**16. أَن لَّن يَبْعَثَ اللَّهُ أَحَدًا — *an lan yabʿatha'llāhu aḥadā*** · v.7
- **Root feeling:** *baʿatha* — the "**raising up / sending forth**" family — ***baʿth* = RESURRECTION**; also **to send a messenger** (*baʿatha rasūlan*); ⭐ *biʿthah* = a delegation / (modern) **a scholarship abroad**; *inbaʿatha* (Shams 91:12).
- **Here it means:** "**And they [humans] assumed, as you [jinn] assumed, that Allah would never raise anyone.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Two things.** ⭐ **(1) The jinn turn and ADDRESS their own people — and humans:** *kamā **ẓanantum*** ("as **you** assumed"). ⭐ They're now preaching. ⭐ **(2) *yabʿatha* has two classical readings — both true:** ⭐ "would never **resurrect** anyone" or "would never **send** anyone (as a messenger)." ⭐⭐ Both were denied by jinn and humans alike — and both were just disproven: **a Messenger HAS been sent**, and his message is **resurrection**. ⭐ And ***aḥadā*** — **the second of six**.
- **Grammar bite:** *lan + subjunctive* = "would **never**." ⭐ *kamā* = "**just as**" (comparison).
- **Why this exact word:** *baʿatha* — ⭐ **one verb holding both denials they had held: no Messenger, no Resurrection.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Baʿatha* = RESURRECT **and** SEND a messenger.** ⭐ **Both were denied; both just came true.** ⭐ ***Biʿthah* = a SCHOLARSHIP abroad.** ⭐ ***Aḥadā* #2.**

## §3 — The Sealed Sky (v.8–10)

**17. وَأَنَّا لَمَسْنَا السَّمَاءَ — *wa annā lamasna's-samāʾ*** · v.8
- **Root feeling:** the "**TOUCHING / feeling for / seeking**" family — *lamasa* = to touch; ***lams* = touch** (⭐ *shāshat lams* = a **touchscreen**!); ⭐ ***iltamasa* = to SEEK / request** (*iltimās* = a petition); *malmūs* = tangible.
- **Here it means:** "**And we reached for / sought [to reach] the heaven…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***Lamasa* is "touching to FEEL for something"** — like reaching into the dark. ⭐ The jinn describe **groping toward the sky** — trying to get close enough to overhear. ⭐ The image is of **reaching up and feeling** — and finding it **full of guards** (next).
- **Grammar bite:** past, 1st plural. ⭐ *as-samāʾa* = direct object.
- **Why this exact word:** *lamasa* (feeling for) — ⭐ **captures their stealth: not marching up, but feeling their way.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Shāshat LAMS* = a TOUCHSCREEN.** ⭐ ***Iltimās* = a PETITION.** ⭐ **They "felt for" the sky — and found guards.**

**18. فَوَجَدْنَاهَا مُلِئَتْ حَرَسًا شَدِيدًا — *fa-wajadnāhā muliʾat ḥarasan shadīdā*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *muliʾat* — the "**FILLING**" family — *malaʾa* = to fill; *milʾ* = fill/fullness; *malīʾ* = full; ⭐ *al-malaʾ* = the chiefs/council (those who "fill" the eye). *ḥaras* — the "**GUARDING**" family — ***ḥaras* = guards** (collective); ***ḥāris* = a GUARD** (⭐ *ḥāris al-marmā* = a **GOALKEEPER**!); ⭐ ***al-ḥaras al-waṭanī* = the NATIONAL GUARD**. *shadīd* — **severe, strong**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and found it filled with strong guards and flaming meteors.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***Muliʾat* — FILLED.** Not "had some guards" — **packed**. ⭐ The sky became suddenly **sealed** at the moment of revelation, so nothing could interfere with it. ⭐⭐ And ***shadīd*** — you own it in four places: the **firm** heavens (Nabaʾ 78:12), your **firm** frame (Insān 76:28), His **severe** grip (Burūj 85:12), and man's **intense** love of wealth (ʿĀdiyāt 100:8). ⭐ **Here: the heavens' guards are *shadīd* — and they guard revelation.**
- **Grammar bite:** *muliʾat* = **passive** (filled — by Allah, unnamed). ⭐ *ḥarasan* = **tamyīz** (specification: "filled **in terms of** guards").
- **Why this exact word:** *malaʾa* (**filled**) — ⭐ **total coverage: no gap to slip through.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥāris al-marmā* = a GOALKEEPER; *al-ḥaras al-waṭanī* = the NATIONAL GUARD.** ⭐ **The sky was FILLED (*muliʾat*) — no gaps.** ⭐ ***Shadīd* thread: sky, bones, His grip, love of money — and now the guards.**

**19. وَشُهُبًا / شِهَابًا — *wa shuhubā / shihābā*** · v.8, 9
- **Root feeling:** the "**FLAME / blazing brightness**" family — ***shihāb* = a shooting star, a METEOR, a flaming brand**; *shuhub* = its plural; ⭐ *ashhab* = grey-white (bright-coloured); ⭐ the name ***Shihāb ad-Dīn*** = "**Flame of the Religion**."
- **Here it means:** "**…and flaming meteors**" (v.8); "**…will find a meteor lying in wait for him**" (v.9)
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ The **shooting stars** you see at night are, in this surah's picture, **guards' weapons** — fired at eavesdroppers. ⭐ The jinn learned it the hard way: **every attempt to listen now meets a flame.** ⭐ And the change happened **at the Prophet's ﷺ mission** — that's what sent the jinn searching for the cause and led them to the Qurʾān.
- **Grammar bite:** *shuhuban* = broken plural, accusative (joined to *ḥarasan*). ⭐ *shihāban* (v.9) = singular, object of *yajid*.
- **Why this exact word:** *shihāb* — ⭐ **the visible night-sky event the audience already knew, given its hidden meaning.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***SHIHĀB* = a METEOR / shooting star.** ⭐ ***Shihāb ad-Dīn* = "Flame of the Religion."** ⭐ **Shooting stars = guards' fire at eavesdroppers.**

**20. نَقْعُدُ مِنْهَا مَقَاعِدَ لِلسَّمْعِ — *naqʿudu minhā maqāʿida li's-samʿ*** · v.9
- **Root feeling:** the "**SITTING**" family — *qaʿada* = to sit; ***maqʿad* = a SEAT** (⭐ modern: **a car seat, a plane seat**, *maqʿad* in parliament!); *qāʿidah* = a base / a **rule** (what something "sits" on); *quʿūd* = sitting (⭐ **Burūj 85:6**!). *samʿ* — **hearing**.
- **Here it means:** "**And we used to sit there in positions for listening.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ The image is almost comic: jinn with **reserved SEATS** in the sky, like an audience at a performance, **listening in** on the angels. ⭐ ***Maqāʿid li's-samʿ* = "listening seats."** ⭐ **That was then.** Now (v.9): ***fa-man yastamiʿi'l-ĀNA*** — "**whoever listens NOW**" finds a meteor. ⭐⭐ And compare **Burūj 85:6** — *idh hum ʿalayhā **quʿūd*** — the persecutors **SITTING** by the fire-ditch watching. ⭐ **Two kinds of sitting: spectators of cruelty (Burūj), and eavesdroppers on heaven (Jinn).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ *maqāʿida* = **diptote** (*mafāʿil* pattern) — fatḥah, no tanwīn. ⭐ *kunnā naqʿudu* = **habitual past** ("used to sit").
- **Why this exact word:** *maqāʿid* (**seats**) — ⭐ **pictures it as an established, habitual position — which made its sudden loss so striking.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAQʿAD* = a SEAT** (car, plane, parliament). ⭐ **Jinn had "listening SEATS" in the sky — then lost them.** ⚠️ ***Maqāʿid* = DIPTOTE.** ⭐ **Burūj 85:6: persecutors SITTING (*quʿūd*) by the ditch.**

**21. فَمَن يَسْتَمِعِ الْآنَ يَجِدْ لَهُ شِهَابًا رَّصَدًا — *fa-man yastamiʿi'l-āna yajid lahu shihāban raṣadā*** · v.9 (and *raṣadā* in v.27)
- **Root feelings:** *al-ān* — **NOW** (the root *ʾ-n-y*: reaching its moment — ⭐ the same root as *āniyah*, boiling at its peak, **Ghāshiyah 88:5**). *raṣad* — the "**WATCHING / lying in wait**" family — *raṣada* = to observe, ambush; ***marṣad* = an OBSERVATORY**; ⭐ *raṣīd* = a **(bank) BALANCE** — what's been watched/recorded!
- **Here it means:** "**But whoever listens now will find a meteor lying in wait for him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *raṣad* thread is one of your best:**
  - **Nabaʾ 78:21** — *inna jahannama kānat **mirṣādā*** (**Hell** lies in wait)
  - **Fajr 89:14** — *inna rabbaka la-**bi'l-mirṣād*** (**your Lord** is at the lookout)
  - ⭐ **Jinn 72:9** — *shihāban **raṣadā*** (**a meteor** lies in wait for eavesdroppers)
  - ⭐ **Jinn 72:27** — *yasluku min bayni yadayhi wa min khalfihi **raṣadā*** (**guards** escort revelation)
  ⭐⭐ **In this one surah, *raṣad* appears TWICE with two jobs:** ⭐ **v.9 — guards lying in wait AGAINST thieves of revelation; v.27 — guards escorting and PROTECTING revelation.** ⭐ The same watching serves to **block** and to **protect**.
  ⭐ And note the **Form VIII *yastamiʿ*** again (from v.1): **good listening vs. stolen listening.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man* + jussive + jussive** = conditional ("whoever **listens**, **finds**"). ⚠️ *yastamiʿi* gets a **kasrah** to avoid two sukūns before *al-ān*. ⭐ *raṣadan* = verbal noun used **as an adjective** ("an ambushing meteor") — *waṣf bi'l-maṣdar* again (like *ʿajabā*, v.1).
- **Why this exact word:** *raṣad* — ⭐ **the meteor isn't random; it is **waiting**, positioned, watching.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MARṢAD* = an OBSERVATORY.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Raṣad* thread: Hell (78:21), your Lord (89:14), a meteor (72:9), revelation's escort (72:27).** ⭐ **v.9 BLOCKS thieves; v.27 PROTECTS the message.** ⭐ ***Al-ān* = NOW** — same root as *āniyah* (boiling, 88:5).

**22. لَا نَدْرِي أَشَرٌّ أُرِيدَ ... أَمْ أَرَادَ بِهِمْ رَبُّهُمْ رَشَدًا — *lā nadrī a-sharrun urīda… am arāda bihim rabbuhum rashadā*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *nadrī* — the "**knowing by perception**" family (*darā*; you own *yudrīka/adrāka*). *sharr* — **evil/harm**. *urīda / arāda* — the "**WILLING / intending**" family — *irādah* = will; *murīd* = a disciple (one who **wills** the path).
- **Here it means:** "**And we do not know whether harm is intended for those on earth, or whether their Lord intends for them right guidance.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST REFINED VERSE IN THE SURAH — a lesson in *adab* (courtesy toward Allah), from jinn.**
  ⭐ Look at the two halves:
  - ⭐ ***a-sharrun **URĪDA*** — "is **harm intended**" — **PASSIVE, no doer named.**
  - ⭐ ***am **ARĀDA** bihim **RABBUHUM** rashadā*** — "or **their Lord INTENDS** guidance" — **ACTIVE, Allah named as doer.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Harm: no subject. Good: Allah as subject.** ⭐ The jinn will not attach **evil** to Allah's name — but gladly attach **guidance** to Him.
  ⭐⭐⭐ **You saw the IDENTICAL courtesy in Falaq 113:2** — *min sharri **mā khalaq*** ("the evil **of what He created**," not "the evil He created"). ⭐ **And in Ibrāhīm's words (26:80):** *wa idhā mariḍtu fa-huwa yashfīn* — "when **I** fall ill, **He** heals me." ⭐ **Three voices — the Qurʾān, Ibrāhīm, and the jinn — all keep evil off His name.**
  ⭐ And the **humility**: *lā nadrī* — "**we don't know**." ⭐ After the sky was suddenly sealed, they didn't pretend to understand. ⭐ **Admitting ignorance of the unseen** — the theme of the surah's end (v.25–26).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***urīda* = PASSIVE; *arāda* = ACTIVE with explicit subject.** ⭐ *a-… am…* = "**whether… or…**" (alternative question).
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the contrast of voices — passive for evil, active for good — is the entire point.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **EVIL: passive, no doer (*urīda*). GOOD: active, "their LORD intends" (*arāda rabbuhum*).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same adab as Falaq 113:2 ("evil of what He CREATED") and Ibrāhīm (26:80: "when I fall ill, HE heals").** ⭐ ***Lā nadrī* = "we DON'T KNOW" — humility before the unseen.**

## §4 — Two Kinds of Jinn (v.11–15)

**23. مِنَّا الصَّالِحُونَ وَمِنَّا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ — *minna'ṣ-ṣāliḥūna wa minnā dūna dhālik*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *ṣāliḥūn* — the "**sound / fit for purpose / repairing**" family — *ṣalaḥa* = to be sound; *iṣlāḥ* = **repair/reform**; *ṣulḥ* = a peace treaty (you own it from **ʿAṣr 103:3**). *dūna* — **below / short of / other than**.
- **Here it means:** "**And among us are the righteous, and among us are those below that.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **Remarkable honesty about their own kind.** ⭐ The jinn don't say "we're all good now" — they say **some of us are righteous, some are *dūna dhālik*** ("**short of that**"). ⭐ *Dūna* is a gentle word — not "evil," but "**below** the mark" — a spectrum, not two boxes. ⭐ It answers a question humans often ask: **are all jinn evil?** The jinn themselves say: no — **like humans, they vary.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***minnā* (fronted) + subject** = "**among us** [are]…" — a nominal sentence with fronted predicate. ⭐ *dūna* = an adverb of place used for rank.
- **Why this exact word:** *dūna dhālik* (**below that**) — ⭐ **refuses to flatten their community into one verdict.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ **"Some of us righteous, some *DŪNA DHĀLIK* (short of that)."** ⭐ **Jinn vary, like humans — a spectrum, not a single category.** ⭐ ***Iṣlāḥ* = REPAIR** (ʿAṣr 103:3).

**24. طَرَائِقَ قِدَدًا — *ṭarāʾiqa qidadā*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *ṭarāʾiq* — plural of ***ṭarīqah* = a WAY, a path, a method** (⭐ *ṭarīq* = a road; ⭐ ***ṭarīqah* = a Sufi ORDER**; ⭐ *ṭarīqat al-istikhdām* = **instructions for use**!). *qidad* — the "**CUTTING LENGTHWISE / slicing into strips**" family — *qadda* = to cut lengthwise, to slit; ***qidd* = a strip of leather**; ⭐ ***qadd* = STATURE / figure** (a person's lengthwise outline!); *qidad* = **pieces cut apart, separate factions**.
- **Here it means:** "**We were [on] divided paths / of different ways.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The image is a single hide CUT INTO STRIPS** — one thing sliced into separate, diverging pieces. ⭐ The jinn community wasn't just "diverse" — it was **cut apart** into factions going different ways. ⭐⭐ **Compare Layl 92:4** — *inna saʿyakum la-**shattā*** — "your strivings are **SCATTERED**." ⭐ And **Zalzalah 99:6** — *yaṣduru'n-nāsu **ashtātā*** (people come out **in scattered groups**). ⭐ **Jinn 72:11: *ṭarāʾiqa **qidadā*** — paths **sliced apart**.** ⭐ Three surahs, one truth: **creatures diverge.** ⭐ And ***ṭarīqah*** returns in **v.16** — ***istaqāmū ʿala'ṭ-ṭarīqah*** — "**stand straight on THE path**." ⭐⭐ **v.11: many sliced paths. v.16: THE one path — and standing straight on it.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ *ṭarāʾiqa* = **DIPTOTE** (*faʿāʾil* plural) — fatḥah, no tanwīn. ⭐ It's the *khabar* of *kunnā* (with an implied "**of**": "we were [people of] divided ways"). ⭐ *qidadan* = adjective.
- **Why this exact word:** *qidad* (**strips cut lengthwise**) — ⭐ **shows the division as an act of tearing apart, not natural variety.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QADD* = STATURE (a lengthwise outline); *qidd* = a LEATHER STRIP.** ⭐ ***Qidad* = one hide SLICED into separate strips.** ⭐⭐ **v.11: many sliced paths (*ṭarāʾiq*). v.16: THE path (*aṭ-ṭarīqah*).** ⭐ ***Ṭarīqat al-istikhdām* = INSTRUCTIONS for use.** ⚠️ **DIPTOTE.**

**25. لَّن نُّعْجِزَ اللَّهَ — *lan nuʿjiza'llāh*** · v.12
- **Root feeling:** the "**INABILITY / incapacity**" family — *ʿajaza* = to be unable; ***ʿajz* = incapacity, weakness**; ⭐⭐ ***muʿjizah* = A MIRACLE** (literally "**that which renders others incapable**" — of matching it!); ⭐ ***ʿajūz* = an old person** (one who has become unable); ⭐ **Form IV *aʿjaza* = to make someone unable / to escape someone's power.**
- **Here it means:** "**And we became certain that we could never escape Allah on earth, nor escape Him by flight.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The root of the word for MIRACLE.** ⭐ A ***muʿjizah*** is something that **leaves you unable** to reproduce it. ⭐ The Qurʾān is the *muʿjizah* — it renders every challenger *ʿājiz* (incapable). ⭐⭐ And here the jinn confess the reverse: **WE can never render ALLAH incapable** (*lan nuʿjiza'llāh*). ⭐⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān makes creatures *ʿājiz*; no creature can make Allah *ʿājiz*.** One root, both directions. ⭐ Two scenarios covered: **"on earth"** (staying and resisting) and **"by flight"** (running). ⭐ **Neither works.** ⭐ ⚠️ And note *ẓanannā* here = **certainty** (the realization of believers), not the mistaken assumption of v.5!
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lan* + subjunctive** ×2 = "**never**." ⭐ **Form IV** *aʿjaza* = to put out of power / escape. ⚠️ ***Ẓann* in v.12 = CERTAINTY; in v.5 = mistaken ASSUMPTION** — same word, same surah.
- **Why this exact word:** *aʿjaza* — ⭐ **shares a root with *muʿjizah*, quietly setting the miracle of the Qurʾān against the powerlessness of its hearers.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUʿJIZAH* = A MIRACLE = "that which makes others UNABLE."** ⭐ ***ʿAjūz* = an OLD person (became unable).** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān makes creatures *ʿājiz*; no creature can make Allah *ʿājiz*.** ⚠️ ***Ẓanannā* v.5 = wrong ASSUMPTION; v.12 = CERTAINTY.**

**26. هَرَبًا — *harabā*** · v.12
- **Root feeling:** the "**FLEEING / escaping**" family — *haraba* = to flee; ***harab* = flight**; ⭐ ***hārib* = a FUGITIVE**; ⭐ *tahrīb* = **SMUGGLING** (making things escape the law!); *mahrab* = an escape route.
- **Here it means:** "**…nor escape Him by flight.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The jinn — beings who can move fast and hide — admit even THEY can't outrun Allah.** ⭐ Compare **Qiyāmah 75:10** — *ayna'l-**mafarr*** ("where is the **escape**?") — and **ʿAbasa 80:34** (*yawma **yafirru'l-marʾ***). ⭐ ***Farra* = to flee in panic; *haraba* = to flee / escape.** ⭐ **Humans will ask "where's the escape?" on that Day; jinn already concluded: there is none.**
- **Grammar bite:** *haraban* = **verbal noun as ḥāl or tamyīz** ("by **way of** flight").
- **Why this exact word:** *harab* — ⭐ **the most natural plan for a hidden, fast being, explicitly ruled out.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Hārib* = a FUGITIVE; *tahrīb* = SMUGGLING.** ⭐ **Even the fast, hidden jinn can't flee Him.** ⭐ **Compare Qiyāmah 75:10: "where is the ESCAPE (*mafarr*)?"**

**27. سَمِعْنَا الْهُدَىٰ آمَنَّا بِهِ — *samiʿna'l-hudā āmannā bih*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *samiʿa* — **to hear**. *hudā* — the "**guidance**" family (*hadā* = to guide; *hadiyyah* = a gift — something guided to you).
- **Here it means:** "**And when we heard the guidance, we believed in it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They call the Qurʾān simply "*AL-HUDĀ*" — "THE GUIDANCE."** ⭐ Not "a book," not "a recitation" this time — **its function as its name**. ⭐ Compare **v.1** (*qurʾānan ʿajabā* — its **wonder**) and **v.2** (*yahdī ila'r-rushd* — its **effect**). ⭐ **Three descriptions in thirteen verses: wonder, maturity, guidance.** ⭐⭐ And ***lammā** samiʿnā… āmannā* — "**the moment** we heard, we believed." ⭐ ***Lammā* + past = "as soon as / when."** No gap.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lammā* + past = "as soon as"** (temporal). ⚠️ **Compare ʿAbasa 80:23's *lammā yaqḍi* = "has NOT YET"** — *lammā* + **jussive** = negation. ⭐⭐ **Same word, two functions: with PAST = "when"; with JUSSIVE = "not yet."**
- **Why this exact word:** *al-hudā* — ⭐ **naming the Qurʾān by what it does for you.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **They call the Qurʾān "*AL-HUDĀ*" — THE GUIDANCE.** ⭐ **Wonder (v.1), maturity (v.2), guidance (v.13).** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***Lammā* + PAST = "as soon as" (here). *Lammā* + JUSSIVE = "not yet" (ʿAbasa 80:23).**

**28. فَلَا يَخَافُ بَخْسًا وَلَا رَهَقًا — *fa-lā yakhāfu bakhsan wa lā rahaqā*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *bakhs* — the "**UNDERVALUING / short-changing / cheating on weight**" family — *bakhasa* = to reduce someone's due, to give less than deserved; ⭐ ***thaman bakhs* = a paltry PRICE** (⭐ Yūsuf 12:20: his brothers sold him *bi-thamanin **bakhs*** — for a **cheap** price!); ⭐ *lā tabkhasū'n-nāsa ashyāʾahum* (7:85) — "don't **short-change** people." *rahaq* — **burden / oppression** (v.6).
- **Here it means:** "**So whoever believes in his Lord fears neither being short-changed nor burdened.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Two fears removed — and both connect to earlier surahs.**
  ⭐ ***Bakhs* = being SHORT-CHANGED.** ⭐ **Muṭaffifīn 83:1–3** cursed those who **short-measure** others (*al-muṭaffifīn*). ⭐ **Here: the believer will NEVER be short-measured by Allah** — every good deed paid in full. ⭐ Compare **Zalzalah 99:7** — an **atom's weight** of good will be seen.
  ⭐ ***Rahaq* = burden** — ⭐⭐⭐ **v.6: those who sought refuge in jinn got *rahaq* ADDED. v.13: the believer fears NO *rahaq*.** ⭐ **Same word, the two refuges compared.**
  ⭐ The memorable hook: **Yūsuf was sold *bi-thamanin bakhs*** — the most precious boy, at a **bargain** price. ⭐ **Allah never does *bakhs*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa-*** in the answer of a conditional with *man*. ⭐ *lā yakhāfu* — the present is **raised** (not jussive) because of the *fa* + implied subject ("then **he** does not fear").
- **Why this exact word:** *bakhs* (**short-changing**) — ⭐ **a market word, promising full, honest payment.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Thaman BAKHS* = a CHEAP PRICE — Yūsuf was sold for one (12:20).** ⭐ **Allah never short-changes.** ⭐⭐ **Muṭaffifīn cursed the short-measurers; here the believer is never short-measured.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Rahaq*: ADDED by false refuge (v.6), REMOVED by faith (v.13).**

**29. الْمُسْلِمُونَ وَمِنَّا الْقَاسِطُونَ — *al-muslimūna wa minna'l-qāsiṭūn*** · v.14 (and v.15)
- **Root feelings:** *muslimūn* — the "**wholeness / submission / safety**" family (*salima*; *islām*; *salām* — Qadr 97:5). *qāsiṭūn* — ⭐⭐⭐ **a root with OPPOSITE meanings depending on the form:** **Form I *qasaṭa* = to DEVIATE, to be UNJUST**; ⭐ **Form IV *aqsaṭa* = to be JUST, equitable**; ⭐ ***qisṭ* = JUSTICE, equity**; ⭐ ***al-Muqsiṭ* = THE JUST — a Name of Allah**; ⭐ ***qisṭ* also = an INSTALMENT** (modern: paying in *aqsāṭ* — fair portions!).
- **Here it means:** "**And among us are the Muslims, and among us are the deviators.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BEST GRAMMAR TRAP IN THE SURAH.**
  ⭐ ***QĀSIṬ*** (Form I participle) = **UNJUST, one who deviates from what's right.**
  ⭐ ***MUQSIṬ*** (Form IV participle) = **JUST, one who acts with equity.** *(Allah loves al-muqsiṭīn — 5:42, 49:9, 60:8.)*
  ⭐⭐ **Same three letters (*q-s-ṭ*). Opposite people.** ⭐ The Form IV prefix doesn't add — it **reverses**: *aqsaṭa* = "**removed** the *qasṭ* (injustice)" → became just. ⭐ (Arabic's *hamzat as-salb* — the "**removing**" *hamzah*.)
  ⭐⭐ **Compare the Form X contrast you own: *istaghnā* ("I need nothing") vs. *istaghfir* ("I need forgiveness") — same form, opposite souls.** ⭐ **Here: same ROOT, different form, opposite souls.**
  ⭐ And note the pairing: **muslimūn vs qāsiṭūn** — not "muslims vs. disbelievers" but "**those who submitted** vs. **those who swerved**." ⭐ Submission is defined against **deviation**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***qāsiṭ* (Form I) = unjust; *muqsiṭ* (Form IV) = just** — ⭐ **the *hamzat as-salb* (privative hamzah) reverses the meaning.**
- **Why this exact word:** *qāsiṭ* (**swerving from justice**) — ⭐ **names their failure as a deviation from the straight path of v.16.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QĀSIṬ* = UNJUST (Form I). *MUQSIṬ* = JUST (Form IV) — *al-Muqsiṭ* is a NAME OF ALLAH.** ⭐ **Same root, OPPOSITE meanings.** ⭐⭐ ***Qisṭ* = JUSTICE — and an INSTALMENT** (paying in fair portions). ⚠️ **The single best trap in Al-Jinn.**

**30. تَحَرَّوْا رَشَدًا — *taḥarraw rashadā*** · v.14
- **Root feeling:** the "**SEEKING CAREFULLY / aiming deliberately for the most fitting**" family — ***taḥarrā* = to seek out with care, to aim for precisely**; ⭐ ***taḥarrī* = INVESTIGATION** (⭐ modern: *taḥarrī khāṣṣ* = a **PRIVATE DETECTIVE**!); ⭐ *ḥarī* = fitting, worthy ("*ḥarīy bi-* = worthy of"); ⭐ in fiqh, ***taḥarrī al-qiblah*** = **carefully determining the qiblah direction** when unsure!
- **Here it means:** "**So whoever has submitted — those have sought out right guidance [carefully].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Islam described as an INVESTIGATION.** ⭐ The submitters didn't stumble into guidance — they ***taḥarraw*** it: **searched carefully, aimed precisely**, like someone working out the qiblah in an unfamiliar place. ⭐⭐ **And that's exactly what these jinn did** — they noticed the sky change, **went searching** across the land (Bukhārī), found the Prophet ﷺ, listened (*istamaʿū*), and believed. ⭐ **Their own story is *taḥarrī*.** ⭐ And ***rashadā*** — the third *r-sh-d* in the surah.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form V past, weak-final** — the plural *taḥarrayū* contracts to *taḥarraw*. ⭐ Form V = **effort applied to oneself**.
- **Why this exact word:** *taḥarrā* — ⭐ **makes guidance the result of a deliberate, careful search.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Taḥarrī khāṣṣ* = a PRIVATE DETECTIVE.** ⭐ ***Taḥarrī al-qiblah* = carefully working out the qiblah direction.** ⭐⭐ **Submission = a careful SEARCH for *rushd*** — ⭐ **exactly what these jinn did.** ⭐ ***R-sh-d* #3.**

**31. فَكَانُوا لِجَهَنَّمَ حَطَبًا — *fa-kānū li-jahannama ḥaṭabā*** · v.15
- **Root feeling:** ***ḥaṭab* = FIREWOOD, kindling** — ⭐ *ḥaṭṭāb* = a **woodcutter**.
- **Here it means:** "**And as for the deviators — they will be firewood for Hell.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Two stunning connections.**
  ⭐ **ONE — Masad 111:4** — *wa'mraʾatuhu **ḥammālata'l-ḥaṭab*** — Abū Lahab's wife, "**the carrier of FIREWOOD**." ⭐⭐ **There: she CARRIES the firewood. Here: the deviators ARE the firewood.** ⭐ The talebearer who fed fires between people, and the jinn who fuel the Fire itself.
  ⭐ **TWO — the irony of FIRE fuelling FIRE.** ⭐ The jinn are **created from fire** (*mārij min nār*, 55:15). ⭐⭐ **And the unjust among them become FUEL for fire.** ⭐ **Fire burning fire** — classical scholars noted: Allah can make even beings of fire suffer by fire. ⭐ Being made of a thing doesn't make you immune to it.
  ⭐ And ***kānū*** ("they **will be** / have become") — **past tense for a certain future**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-jahannama*** = "**for** Hell" (*lām* of purpose); ⚠️ *jahannama* = **diptote** (foreign/feminine proper noun) — fatḥah in the genitive. ⭐ *ḥaṭaban* = *khabar* of *kāna*.
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥaṭab* (**firewood**) rather than "people of the Fire" — ⭐ **reduces them to fuel — a thing consumed, not a person present.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Masad 111:4: she CARRIES the firewood (*ḥammālat al-ḥaṭab*). Jinn 72:15: they ARE the firewood (*ḥaṭabā*).** ⭐⭐ **Beings made of FIRE become FUEL for fire.** ⭐ ***Ḥaṭṭāb* = a WOODCUTTER.** ⚠️ ***Jahannama* = diptote.**

## §5 — The Promise of Rain (v.16–17)

**32. اسْتَقَامُوا عَلَى الطَّرِيقَةِ — *istaqāmū ʿala'ṭ-ṭarīqah*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *istaqāmū* — **Form X of *qāma* (to stand)** = **to stand UPRIGHT and STAY straight** (*istiqāmah* = steadfastness); ⭐ ***aṣ-ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm*** — the straight path you ask for 17× a day. *aṭ-ṭarīqah* — **THE way** (v.11).
- **Here it means:** "**And [it was revealed] that if they had kept straight on the Way…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The *q-w-m* thread you've built across the juzʾ:**
  - **Takwīr 81:28** — *li-man shāʾa minkum an **yastaqīm*** (whoever wills to go **straight**)
  - **Tīn 95:4** — *fī aḥsani **taqwīm*** (the best **upright** form)
  - **Bayyinah 98:3, 5** — *kutubun **qayyimah**… dīnu'l-**qayyimah*** (**upright** writings/religion)
  - ⭐ **Jinn 72:16** — ***istaqāmū** ʿala'ṭ-ṭarīqah*
  ⭐⭐ And the internal echo: **v.11** — *kunnā **ṭarāʾiqa** qidadā* (we were **many sliced paths**) → **v.16** — *ʿala'**ṭ-ṭarīqah*** (on **THE path**). ⭐ **Plural and scattered → singular and definite.** ⭐ **Form X = seeking/maintaining** — not one upright moment, but **staying** upright.
  ⭐ ⚠️ Note: the voice shifts here — v.16 onward is generally read as **Allah's words** (continuing "it was revealed to me that…"), no longer the jinn's quoted speech.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***law*** + past = **hypothetical condition**; its answer is *la-asqaynāhum* (with the *lām* of the answer). ⭐ **Form X** hollow verb.
- **Why this exact word:** *istiqāmah* — ⭐ **the condition for blessing is not perfection but STAYING upright.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Aṣ-ṣirāṭ al-MUSTAQĪM*** — 17× a day. ⭐⭐ ***Q-w-m* thread: *yastaqīm* (81:28), *taqwīm* (95:4), *qayyimah* (98:3), *istaqāmū* (72:16).** ⭐⭐ **v.11: many SLICED paths. v.16: THE path.**

**33. لَأَسْقَيْنَاهُم مَّاءً غَدَقًا — *la-asqaynāhum māʾan ghadaqā*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *asqā* — the "**giving to drink / irrigating**" family — *saqā* = to give drink; ⭐ ***istisqāʾ* = THE PRAYER FOR RAIN**; ⭐ *suqyā* (Shams 91:13 — the she-camel's **drinking-turn**); ⭐ *saqqāʾ* = a water-carrier. *ghadaq* — ⭐ **ABUNDANT, copious (water)** — rain pouring plentifully.
- **Here it means:** "**…We would have given them abundant water to drink.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Uprightness → RAIN.** ⭐ To desert people, **abundant water** is the image of **total prosperity** — crops, herds, wealth, life. ⭐⭐ It echoes the promise of Nūḥ (71:10–12): *istaghfirū rabbakum… yursili's-samāʾa ʿalaykum midrārā* — "**seek forgiveness… He will send rain upon you in abundance.**" ⭐ And it connects to **Nabaʾ 78:14** — *māʾan **thajjājā*** (water **pouring in torrents**). ⭐⭐ **Spiritual straightness and physical rain are linked** throughout the Qurʾān.
  ⭐ ***Istisqāʾ*** — the rain prayer — is your hook.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV** (*asqā* = to provide drink — often of **irrigation**, giving water for land/crops). ⭐ ***la-*** = the *lām* in the answer of *law*. ⭐ *ghadaqan* = adjective.
- **Why this exact word:** *ghadaq* — ⭐ **the most generous word for water, matching the generosity of the promise.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ISTISQĀʾ* = the PRAYER FOR RAIN.** ⭐⭐ **Upright on the Way → ABUNDANT water** (*ghadaq*). ⭐ **Compare Nūḥ 71:10–12: seek forgiveness → abundant rain.** ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:14: *thajjājā* (torrents).**

**34. لِّنَفْتِنَهُمْ فِيهِ — *li-naftinahum fīh*** · v.17
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**TESTING BY FIRE**" family — *fatana* = originally **to put GOLD INTO FIRE to test its purity** — melting away what's false; ⭐ ***fitnah* = a TRIAL, a test** (and by extension, temptation, discord); ⭐ *fattān* = an enchanter, a **charming** temptress (*fātinah* = charming!); ⭐ *maftūn* = infatuated.
- **Here it means:** "**…so that We might test them by it.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The twist: even the BLESSING is a TEST.** ⭐ v.16 promised abundant rain; v.17 adds — **so We can test them with it**. ⭐ Prosperity is **gold in the fire**: it shows whether the gratitude is real. ⭐⭐ You saw this exact principle in **Fajr 89:15–16** — *idhā mā **ibtalāhu** rabbuhu fa-akramahu…* — **wealth is a TEST**, not a verdict. ⭐ And the root image: ⭐ **gold is put into fire not to destroy it, but to show what's real.**
  ⭐ The *f-t-n* root across your surahs: ⭐ **Burūj 85:10** — *inna'lladhīna **fatanu**'l-muʾminīn* (those who **persecuted / tried** the believers — with literal fire, the Ditch!) ⭐⭐ **Burūj: humans put believers through FIRE. Jinn: Allah puts people through ABUNDANCE.** ⭐ And **Muddaththir 74:31** — *wa mā jaʿalnā ʿiddatahum illā **fitnah*** (even a number is a test).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-*** = *lām of purpose* + subjunctive (*naftina*). ⭐ *fīhi* = "**by / in** it" (the water).
- **Why this exact word:** *fitnah* (**gold in fire**) — ⭐ **shows the purpose of blessing: to reveal what's genuine.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Fatana* = putting GOLD IN FIRE to test its purity.** ⭐⭐ **Even ABUNDANCE is a *fitnah*** — like Fajr 89:15 (wealth = a test). ⭐⭐ **Burūj 85:10: humans tried believers with literal FIRE.** ⭐ ***Fātinah* = CHARMING** (a temptress).

**35. وَمَن يُعْرِضْ عَن ذِكْرِ رَبِّهِ — *wa man yuʿriḍ ʿan dhikri rabbih*** · v.17
- **Root feeling:** the "**WIDTH / showing the side**" family — *ʿarḍ* = **width, breadth**; ***aʿraḍa ʿan* = to TURN AWAY from** (literally, to **show your side/breadth** to something instead of facing it); *muʿriḍ* = one who turns away; ⭐ *ʿarḍ* also = **an exhibition / an offer** (*maʿraḍ* = **an EXHIBITION**!); ⭐ *ʿarīḍ* = wide.
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever turns away from the remembrance of his Lord…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The image is BODILY** — like **Aʿlā 87:11**'s *yatajannabuhā* (**sidestepping**, from *janb*, a side). ⭐ ***Aʿraḍa* = to turn your SIDE toward something** instead of your face. ⭐ You're not running away — you're **standing sideways**, refusing to face it. ⭐⭐ **Two "side" roots for rejecting the reminder: *janb* (Aʿlā 87:11) and *ʿarḍ* (Jinn 72:17).** ⭐ And ***dhikr rabbih*** — the juzʾ's master thread: **the reminder**. ⭐ It returns in **Muddaththir 74:49** — *fa-mā lahum ʿani't-tadhkirati **muʿriḍīn*** — "**what's wrong with them, turning away from the reminder?**"
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man* + jussive** (*yuʿriḍ*, sukūn) = conditional; answer *yaslukhu* (also jussive). ⭐ **Form IV** + *ʿan*.
- **Why this exact word:** *iʿrāḍ* (**turning your side**) — ⭐ **a posture of refusal, not mere absence.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAʿRAḌ* = an EXHIBITION; *ʿarḍ* = WIDTH.** ⭐ ***Aʿraḍa* = turning your SIDE instead of your FACE.** ⭐⭐ **Two "side" roots for rejection: *janb* (87:11), *ʿarḍ* (72:17).** ⭐ **Returns as *muʿriḍīn* in Muddaththir 74:49.**

**36. يَسْلُكْهُ عَذَابًا صَعَدًا — *yaslukhu ʿadhāban ṣaʿadā*** · v.17
- **Root feelings:** *yasluk* — ⭐⭐ the "**THREADING through / inserting / travelling a path**" family — *salaka* = to make something go into, to **thread** (a needle!); ⭐ ***silk* = a THREAD, a WIRE** (⭐ *silk kahrabāʾī* = **an electric wire**; *lā-silkī* = **WIRELESS**!); ⭐ ***maslak* = a path/track**; ⭐ ***sulūk* = BEHAVIOUR, conduct** (the "path" you walk!). *ṣaʿad* — ⭐ the "**ASCENDING / climbing**" family — *ṣaʿida* = to go up; ⭐ ***miṣʿad* = an ELEVATOR / LIFT**; *ṣuʿūd* = ascent; *ṣaʿīd* = elevated ground, also **surface earth** (*tayammum* with *ṣaʿīd*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…He will thread him into an ever-mounting, severe punishment.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO vivid images in three words.**
  ⭐ ***Yaslukhu* = He THREADS him into it** — like **thread through a needle's eye**: ⭐ forced into a narrow, tight passage, with no room around it. ⭐⭐ And it returns in **v.27** (*yasluku… raṣadā* — guards **threaded** around revelation) and **Muddaththir 74:42** — *mā **salakakum** fī saqar* ("what **threaded** you into Saqar?").
  ⭐ ***Ṣaʿad* = a punishment that keeps CLIMBING** — rising, steepening, **harder every moment**, like walking up an endless slope. ⭐⭐ Compare **Balad 90:11** — *fa-la'qtaḥama'l-**ʿaqabah*** — the **steep climb** of good deeds they refused. ⭐⭐⭐ **They wouldn't climb the *ʿaqabah* of good (Balad); they're made to climb the *ṣaʿad* of punishment (Jinn).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE CROSS-SURAH SYMMETRY: Muddaththir 74:17 — *sa-urhiquhu **ṣaʿūdā*** ("I will burden him with a **steep climb**").** ⭐ **Jinn 72:17 and Muddaththir 74:17 — BOTH VERSE 17, both the same root.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yaslukhu*** — jussive (answer of *man*), takes **two objects** (*-hu* and *ʿadhāban*). ⭐ *ṣaʿadan* = **verbal noun used as adjective** (*waṣf bi'l-maṣdar*) — "a **climbing** punishment."
- **Why this exact word:** *ṣaʿad* (**endless ascent**) — ⭐ **makes punishment feel like exhausting effort that never reaches a top.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LĀ-SILKĪ* = WIRELESS; *silk* = a WIRE/THREAD; *sulūk* = BEHAVIOUR.** ⭐ **Threaded into it like a needle's eye.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIṢʿAD* = an ELEVATOR.** ⭐ ***Ṣaʿad* = a punishment that keeps CLIMBING.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Jinn 72:17 *ṣaʿadā* = Muddaththir 74:17 *ṣaʿūdā* — BOTH VERSE 17.** ⭐ **Refused the climb of good (Balad 90:11) → made to climb this.**

## §6 — The Mosques and the Crowd (v.18–19)

**37. وَأَنَّ الْمَسَاجِدَ لِلَّهِ — *wa anna'l-masājida lillāh*** · v.18
- **Root feeling:** the "**PROSTRATION**" family — *sajada* = to prostrate; ***masjid* = "the place of prostration"** (a mosque); ⭐ *sajjādah* = a **PRAYER MAT**; *sujūd*.
- **Here it means:** "**And the places of prostration belong to Allah, so do not call upon anyone alongside Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Classical readings of *al-masājid* — and they're all rich:**
  ⭐ **(a) MOSQUES** — buildings of worship belong to Allah alone; ⭐ **(b) the SEVEN BODY PARTS of prostration** (forehead, two hands, two knees, two feet) — ⭐ **your forehead on the ground belongs to Him**; ⭐ **(c) all acts of *sujūd*** — the worship itself.
  ⭐⭐ Reading (b) is unforgettable: ⭐ **every limb that touches the floor in *sajdah* is His.** ⭐ So don't put that forehead down for anyone else.
  ⭐ You own the root from **ʿAlaq 96:19** — ***wa'sjud** wa'qtarib* (**prostrate and draw near** — a sajdah verse). ⭐ And ***fa-lā tadʿū maʿa'llāhi aḥadā*** — ***aḥadā* #3**.
  ⭐ **Note the fronting: *lillāh*** — "**to ALLAH**" (only).
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ *al-masājida* = **diptote** (*mafāʿil*). ⭐ ***fa-lā tadʿū*** = prohibition + jussive (*tadʿū* drops the *nūn*).
- **Why this exact word:** *masājid* (**places of prostration**) — ⭐ **ties the oneness of worship to the most physical act of worship.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SAJJĀDAH* = a PRAYER MAT; *masjid* = place of prostration.** ⭐⭐ ***Al-masājid* may mean the SEVEN LIMBS of sajdah — your forehead on the ground is HIS.** ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:19: *usjud*.** ⭐ ***Aḥadā* #3.**

**38. لَمَّا قَامَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ يَدْعُوهُ — *lammā qāma ʿabdu'llāhi yadʿūh*** · v.19
- **Root feelings:** *qāma* — **to stand** (the *q-w-m* root — *qiyām* in prayer!). *ʿabd* — **a servant**. *yadʿū* — the "**calling / supplicating**" family (*duʿāʾ*).
- **Here it means:** "**And when the servant of Allah stood up, calling upon Him…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ is called "*ʿABD ALLĀH*" — "the SERVANT of Allah" — at the moment of his greatest honour.** ⭐ Not "the Messenger," not by name. ⭐⭐ The Qurʾān does this at his highest moments: **the Night Journey** (*subḥāna'lladhī asrā bi-**ʿabdih***, 17:1), **receiving revelation** (*fa-awḥā ilā **ʿabdih***, 53:10), **the Qurʾān's descent** (*nazzala'l-furqāna ʿalā **ʿabdih***, 25:1). ⭐ **Servanthood is the title of honour.** ⭐ And the scene is the **prayer** the jinn saw: he **stood (*qāma*)** — the *qiyām* of ṣalāh — **calling on Him**.
  ⭐ Compare **ʿAlaq 96:10** — *ʿabdan idhā ṣallā* — "**a servant when he prays**" — someone tried to stop him. ⭐ **Here, crowds pressed in to hear him.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lammā* + past** = "**when**." ⭐ *yadʿūhu* = *ḥāl* ("**while** calling Him").
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿabd Allāh* — ⭐ **because standing in prayer is servanthood at its peak.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **He's called *ʿABD ALLĀH* (servant) at his HIGHEST moments — Isrāʾ (17:1), revelation (53:10), here.** ⭐ **Servanthood IS the honour.** ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:10: someone tried to STOP the praying servant; here crowds pressed in.**

**39. كَادُوا يَكُونُونَ عَلَيْهِ لِبَدًا — *kādū yakūnūna ʿalayhi libadā*** · v.19
- **Root feelings:** *kādū* — ⭐ **"they ALMOST / nearly"** (*kāda* = to be on the verge). *libad* — ⭐⭐ the "**layering / sticking together in a dense mass**" family — *labada* = to cling together, to mat; ⭐ ***libdah* = FELT** (wool pressed into dense layers!) and ⭐ **a felt cap**; *lubad* = heaped up.
- **Here it means:** "**…they almost became layered upon him in crowds.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **A ROOT YOU OWN — and the contrast is beautiful.**
  ⭐ **Balad 90:6** — *yaqūlu ahlaktu mālan **lubadā*** — the arrogant man boasting of wealth **"piled in a solid mass"** that he **destroyed**. ⭐ **Jinn 72:19** — *kādū yakūnūna ʿalayhi **libadā*** — beings pressing around the Prophet ﷺ **layered densely** to **hear** him.
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Same root (*l-b-d*): MONEY piled up to be squandered (Balad); LISTENERS piled up to hear the Qurʾān (Jinn).** ⭐ What you **heap around yourself** reveals you.
  ⭐ Classical readings of who crowded: ⭐ **(a) the jinn**, so eager to hear they almost climbed over each other; ⭐ **(b) the hostile Quraysh**, crowding to block him. ⭐ Both are classical.
  ⭐ ***Libdah* = FELT** — wool fibres **pressed so tightly they become one sheet**. ⭐ That's the density of the crowd.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***kāda* + present** = "**almost**" did. ⭐ *libadan* = *khabar* of *yakūnūna* (plural of *libdah*).
- **Why this exact word:** *libad* (**felt-like layers**) — ⭐ **pictures bodies pressed together until they seem one mass.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LIBDAH* = FELT** (fibres pressed into one sheet). ⭐⭐⭐ **Balad 90:6: WEALTH piled *lubadā* (to squander). Jinn 72:19: LISTENERS piled *libadā* (to hear).** ⭐ **Same root — what you heap around you reveals you.** ⭐ ***Kādū* = they ALMOST.**

## §7 — The Messenger's Limits (v.20–23)

*⭐ Notice the rhythm: **qul** (v.20), **qul** (v.21), **qul** (v.22), **qul** (v.25). Four "Say:"s — each one a statement of what the Prophet ﷺ is **not** and **cannot** do. The surah that began with jinn submitting ends with the Messenger submitting too.*

**40. قُلْ إِنَّمَا أَدْعُو رَبِّي وَلَا أُشْرِكُ بِهِ أَحَدًا — *qul innamā adʿū rabbī wa lā ushriku bihi aḥadā*** · v.20
- **Root feelings:** *adʿū* — **to call upon / supplicate** (*duʿāʾ*). *ushrik* — **to associate partners** (*shirk*, *sharīk*; v.2).
- **Here it means:** "**Say: I only call upon my Lord, and I associate no one with Him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Messenger repeats the jinn's own creed.** ⭐ **v.2 (the jinn):** *wa lan **nushrika** bi-rabbinā **aḥadā*** ⭐ **v.20 (the Prophet ﷺ):** *wa lā **ushriku** bihi **aḥadā*** ⭐⭐ **Almost word for word.** ⭐ The new believers and the Messenger say the same sentence — ⭐ **one creed, for jinn and humans alike.** ⭐ And ***innamā*** = "**ONLY**" — restriction. ⭐ ***Aḥadā* #4.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***innamā* = restriction** ("nothing but"). ⭐ *lā* + present = negation of present/future.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **echoing v.2 exactly — so the reader hears the jinn and the Prophet ﷺ in unison.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.2 (JINN): *lan nushrika… aḥadā*. v.20 (PROPHET ﷺ): *lā ushriku… aḥadā*.** ⭐ **Same sentence — one creed for both.** ⭐ ***Aḥadā* #4.**

**41. لَا أَمْلِكُ لَكُمْ ضَرًّا وَلَا رَشَدًا — *lā amliku lakum ḍarran wa lā rashadā*** · v.21
- **Root feelings:** *amlik* — the "**OWNING / having power over**" family (*malik* = king — **Nās 114:2**; *lā tamliku* — **Infiṭār 82:19**). *ḍarr* — the "**HARM**" family — *ḍarra* = to harm; ⭐ ***ḍarar* = damage** (⭐ *ḍarūrah* = **NECESSITY** — a pressing harm that forces you!); ⭐ *ḍarīr* = blind. *rashad* — **right guidance**.
- **Here it means:** "**Say: I possess no power to harm you or to guide you aright.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Look at the PAIR — it's deliberately uneven.** ⭐ You'd expect "**harm or BENEFIT**" (*ḍarr wa nafʿ*) — the usual pairing. ⭐ Instead: "**harm or GUIDANCE**" (*ḍarr wa rashad*). ⭐⭐ Classical note: **the opposite of each is implied** — "I can't harm you **or benefit you**; I can't **misguide** you **or guide** you." ⭐ **One word from each pair, the other implied** (*iḥtibāk* — a recognized balāgha device). ⭐⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ — the most powerful guide in history — says he OWNS no guidance.** ⭐ He **conveys** it (v.23); **Allah gives** it. ⭐ Compare **Ghāshiyah 88:22** — *lasta ʿalayhim bi-**muṣayṭir*** ("you are **not a controller** over them") — ⭐ **same limit.**
  ⭐ And *rashadā* = **#4 of the *r-sh-d* thread.** ⭐ ⭐ **The thread completes: the Qurʾān GUIDES to *rushd* (v.2); the jinn don't know if Allah intends *rashad* (v.10); the submitters SEARCH for *rashad* (v.14); the Prophet ﷺ cannot OWN *rashad* (v.21).** ⭐ **Only Allah gives it.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***iḥtibāk*** — mentioning one half of two pairs and leaving the other halves implied. ⭐ *lā amliku* + *lām* (for you).
- **Why this exact word:** *ḍarr / rashad* — ⭐ **the most humble statement of prophetic limits possible.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḌARŪRAH* = NECESSITY** (a pressing harm). ⭐⭐⭐ **"No HARM, no GUIDANCE" — each implying its opposite (*iḥtibāk*).** ⭐ **He CONVEYS guidance; Allah GIVES it.** ⭐⭐ ***R-sh-d* thread complete: guides to it (v.2) → unknown (v.10) → sought (v.14) → not owned by the Prophet ﷺ (v.21).** ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:22: "not a controller."**

**42. لَن يُجِيرَنِي مِنَ اللَّهِ أَحَدٌ — *lan yujīranī mina'llāhi aḥad*** · v.22
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐ the "**NEIGHBOURHOOD / protection**" family — *jāwara* = to be next to; ⭐ ***jār* = a NEIGHBOUR**; ⭐ ***jiwār* = protection, asylum** (in Arab custom, a neighbour was **under your protection**); ***ajāra* = to grant protection, to give asylum**; ⭐ *istijārah* = seeking protection; ⭐ *mujāwir* = one who stays near the Ḥaram! ⚠️ **NOT the root of *ajr* (a wage) or *ījār* (rent).**
- **Here it means:** "**Say: None can protect me from Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The root is the NEIGHBOUR — and Arab culture made neighbours protectors.** ⭐ In Makkah, a man could enter another's ***jiwār*** — his **protection** — and be safe from enemies. ⭐ (The Prophet ﷺ himself returned from Ṭāʾif under the *jiwār* of al-Muṭʿim ibn ʿAdī.) ⭐⭐ **Here: no *jiwār* exists that could protect anyone FROM Allah.** ⭐ **Even the Messenger has no "neighbour" to hide behind.** ⭐ And ***aḥad* #5** — ⚠️ here **nominative** (*aḥadun*), as the **subject**: "**no one** will protect me."
  ⭐ ⚠️ **TRAP: *yujīr* (j-w-r, protection) ≠ *ajr* (ʾ-j-r, wage) ≠ *ījār* (rent).** Look-alikes, different roots.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***lan* + subjunctive** ("will never"). ⭐ *-nī* = me (with the *nūn al-wiqāyah*). ⭐ Hollow verb, Form IV.
- **Why this exact word:** *ajāra* (**to grant neighbourly protection**) — ⭐ **evokes the strongest human safety-net of that society — and declares it useless against Allah.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***JĀR* = a NEIGHBOUR; *jiwār* = PROTECTION/asylum.** ⭐ **No neighbour can shelter you FROM Allah.** ⚠️ ⭐⭐ ***Yujīr* (protection) ≠ *ajr* (wage) ≠ *ījār* (rent).** ⭐ ***Aḥad* #5 — here as SUBJECT (nominative).**

**43. مُلْتَحَدًا — *multaḥadā*** · v.22
- **Root feeling:** ⭐⭐⭐ the "**TURNING ASIDE / inclining to one side**" family — *laḥada* = to incline, to turn aside; ⭐⭐ ***laḥd* = the SIDE-NICHE dug into the wall of a GRAVE** (where the body is laid — "turned aside" from the main shaft!); ⭐⭐ ***ilḥād* = DEVIATION → ATHEISM**; ⭐ ***mulḥid* = an ATHEIST** (one who turns aside from the truth); ***multaḥad* = a place to turn aside into, a refuge**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and I will never find any refuge besides Him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ONE ROOT: a grave, atheism, and refuge.**
  ⭐ ***Laḥd*** — the **niche in the grave**, cut **into the side**, where you'll be laid. ⭐ ***Mulḥid*** — the **atheist**, who **turns aside** from the straight. ⭐ ***Multaḥad*** — a **hiding place you turn aside into**.
  ⭐⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ says: there is NO *multaḥad* — no side-pocket in all of existence to tuck into away from Allah.** ⭐ The only "side niche" you're certain to enter is the ***laḥd*** — and there too, you're with Him.
  ⭐⭐ Compare **Qiyāmah 75:11** — *kallā **lā wazar*** ("No! There is no **refuge**" — a mountain stronghold) and **75:10** (*ayna'l-**mafarr***). ⭐ **Three refuge-words denied: *wazar* (mountain fort), *mafarr* (escape route), *multaḥad* (side-niche).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form VIII noun of place** (*multaḥad* = place of *iltiḥād*). ⭐ *min dūnihi* = "**besides** Him."
- **Why this exact word:** *multaḥad* — ⭐ **the image of ducking aside into a niche — and the root's grave-and-atheism echoes make it haunting.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LAḤD* = the GRAVE'S SIDE-NICHE. *MULḤID* = an ATHEIST. *Multaḥad* = a HIDING-NICHE.** ⭐ **All "turning aside."** ⭐⭐ **No side-pocket in existence to hide from Him.** ⭐ **Refuges denied: *wazar* (75:11), *mafarr* (75:10), *multaḥad* (72:22).**

**44. إِلَّا بَلَاغًا مِّنَ اللَّهِ وَرِسَالَاتِهِ — *illā balāghan mina'llāhi wa risālātih*** · v.23 (and *ablaghū* v.28)
- **Root feelings:** *balāgh* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**REACHING / arriving at the target**" family — *balagha* = to reach; ***balāgh* = conveying, delivering a message**; ⭐ *tablīgh* = **proclamation, da'wah**; ⭐⭐ ***bāligh* = an ADULT** (one who has **reached** maturity — *bulūgh* = puberty!); ⭐⭐⭐ ***BALĀGHAH* = ELOQUENCE — the science of rhetoric** (speech that **reaches** its target!); ⭐ *mablagh* = **a SUM of money** (the amount "reached"). *risālāt* — **messages** (*risālah* = a letter, a message; *rasūl* = messenger).
- **Here it means:** "**[My only power is] conveying from Allah, and His messages.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE WORD FOR WHAT YOU'VE BEEN STUDYING ALL ALONG.**
  ⭐ Every entry in these guides has a "**Word magic (balāgha)**" layer. ⭐⭐ ***Balāghah*** — Arabic rhetoric — comes from ***balagha*, TO REACH**: ⭐ **eloquent speech is speech that REACHES its target** — the listener's mind and heart. ⭐ And here the Prophet's ﷺ whole job is ***balāgh*** — **making the message reach**.
  ⭐⭐ **And *bāligh* = an ADULT** — one who has **reached** maturity. ⭐ ⭐ **Connect to *rushd* (v.2) — the Qurʾān brings you to maturity (*rushd*); the Messenger's job is *balāgh* (reaching).** ⭐ Two "maturity" roots in one surah.
  ⭐ **The restriction is total:** ⭐ "I own no harm, no guidance (v.21), no refuge (v.22) — ***illā balāghan*** — **EXCEPT delivery**." ⭐ **His only power is to make it reach you.** ⭐ Compare **Ghāshiyah 88:21** — *innamā anta **mudhakkir*** (you are only a **reminder**) and **Nāziʿāt 79:45** (*innamā anta **mundhir***). ⭐⭐ **Reminder, warner, deliverer — the Prophet's ﷺ three job titles, all limited to transmission.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***illā*** — exception from v.22 (*lā amliku… illā balāghan*: "I own nothing except delivery"). ⭐ *balāghan* = accusative (exception).
- **Why this exact word:** *balāgh* — ⭐ **defines prophethood as making the message ARRIVE, not forcing its acceptance.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BALĀGHAH* (rhetoric) = speech that REACHES its target** — ⭐ **the "Word magic" layer of every guide you've read!** ⭐⭐ ***Bāligh* = an ADULT (reached maturity); *mablagh* = a SUM of money.** ⭐⭐ **His only power: *balāgh* — making it REACH you.** ⭐ **Job titles: *mudhakkir* (88:21), *mundhir* (79:45), *balāgh* (72:23).**

**45. وَمَن يَعْصِ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ ... خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا — *wa man yaʿṣi'llāha wa rasūlahu… khālidīna fīhā abadā*** · v.23
- **Root feelings:** *yaʿṣi* — the "**DISOBEYING**" family — *ʿaṣā* = to disobey (⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:21**, Pharaoh *kadhdhaba wa **ʿaṣā***; ⭐ and *ʿaṣā* = **a staff** — same letters!). *khālidīn* — the "**ETERNITY**" family (*Khālid*; **Bayyinah 98:6, 8**; **Humazah 104:3** *akhladah*). *abad* — **FOREVER** (*abadī* = eternal; ⭐ *ilā'l-abad* = **forever**).
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger — for him is the Fire of Hell, abiding therein forever.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **Allah and His Messenger mentioned TOGETHER** — ⭐ disobeying the conveyed message *is* disobeying its Sender. ⭐ That's the logic of *balāgh* (v.23): the courier carries the King's word. ⭐⭐ **A grammar detail worth noticing:** ⭐ *fa-inna **lahu*** (singular — "for **him**") → *khālidīna* (**plural** — "abiding"). ⭐ Why? ⭐ ***Man* ("whoever") is singular in FORM but plural in MEANING** — ⭐ the Qurʾān honours both: singular pronoun first, then plural. ⭐⭐ And **Bayyinah 98:8** used the same pair — ***khālidīna fīhā abadā*** — for **the believers in Paradise**. ⭐ **Identical phrase, opposite destinations.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yaʿṣi*** — jussive, **final yāʾ dropped** (from *yaʿṣī*). ⭐⭐ ***Man*: singular form, plural meaning** — hence *lahu* (sg.) then *khālidīna* (pl.). ⭐ *khālidīna* = *ḥāl*.
- **Why this exact word:** pairing Allah **and** His Messenger — ⭐ **closes any loophole of "I obey God but not the messenger."**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ **Disobeying the MESSENGER = disobeying the SENDER** (the courier carries the King's word). ⭐⭐ ***Man* = singular FORM, plural MEANING** → *lahu* then *khālidīna*. ⭐⭐⭐ ***Khālidīna fīhā abadā* — the SAME PHRASE as Bayyinah 98:8 (Paradise). Opposite destinations.** ⭐ ***Yaʿṣi* drops its yāʾ.**

## §8 — Who Is Weaker? (v.24–25)

**46. فَسَيَعْلَمُونَ مَنْ أَضْعَفُ نَاصِرًا وَأَقَلُّ عَدَدًا — *fa-sa-yaʿlamūna man aḍʿafu nāṣiran wa aqallu ʿadadā*** · v.24
- **Root feelings:** *aḍʿaf* — ⭐ **WEAKER** (*ḍaʿufa* = to be weak; *ḍuʿf* = weakness; ⭐ *ḍiʿf* = **DOUBLE** — a different sense of the same root!). *nāṣir* — **a HELPER** (*naṣr*; ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:10**, *wa lā nāṣir*; ⭐ **Naṣr 110:1**; ⭐ *al-Anṣār*). *aqall* — **FEWER** (*qalīl* = few/little; ⭐ *aqalliyyah* = a **MINORITY**!). *ʿadad* — **NUMBER** (⭐ **Humazah 104:2** *ʿaddadah*; ⭐ *ʿaddād* = a **meter**).
- **Here it means:** "**…then they will know who is weaker in helpers and fewer in number.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The surah answers Quraysh's favourite boast.** ⭐ Quraysh mocked the early Muslims for being **few and weak** — a handful of slaves, the poor, the young. ⭐⭐ **The verse throws two comparatives back: *aḍʿafu* (weaker) and *aqallu* (fewer).** ⭐ "**You'll find out** who's actually weak and few — when you see what you were promised." ⭐ And it came true historically: ⭐ **Naṣr 110:2** — people entering the religion ***afwājā*** (in **crowds**). ⭐⭐ **The "few" became crowds; the boasters had no *nāṣir*** (Ṭāriq 86:10). ⭐ ⭐ And ***ʿadad*** — **Humazah 104:2**: the man who **counted** (*ʿaddada*) his wealth. ⭐ **Counting money, counting followers — both will be recounted.** ⭐ And note *ʿadadā* returns in **v.28** — *wa aḥṣā kulla shayʾin **ʿadadā*** — **Allah counts EVERYTHING.** ⭐⭐ **v.24: they boast of their numbers. v.28: He has numbered all things.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***afʿal*** comparatives ×2. ⭐ *nāṣiran*, *ʿadadan* = **tamyīz** (specification: "weaker **in terms of** helpers"). ⭐ ***sa-*** = near future.
- **Why this exact word:** two comparatives — ⭐ **because the boast was itself comparative: "we have more."**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AQALLIYYAH* = a MINORITY.** ⭐ **Quraysh mocked the "FEW and WEAK" — "you'll see who's weaker and fewer."** ⭐⭐ **The few became CROWDS (Naṣr 110:2, *afwājā*).** ⭐⭐ ***ʿAdad*: v.24 they boast numbers; v.28 He has numbered ALL things.** ⭐ ***Ḍiʿf* = DOUBLE** — same root as weakness!

**47. قُلْ إِنْ أَدْرِي — *qul in adrī*** · v.25
- **Root feelings:** *in* — ⭐ **here = NEGATION ("not")**, not "if." *adrī* — **I know** (*darā*).
- **Here it means:** "**Say: I do not know…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ says "I DON'T KNOW."** ⭐ The most knowledgeable of creation, asked when the Hour comes, answers with plain honesty. ⭐⭐ **And it mirrors the jinn exactly:** ⭐ **v.10 (jinn):** *wa annā **lā nadrī*** — "**we don't know**." ⭐ **v.25 (Prophet ﷺ):** *qul **in adrī*** — "**I don't know**." ⭐ **Same root, same humility, jinn and Messenger together.** ⭐⭐ Compare **Nāziʿāt 79:42–44** — asked *ayyāna mursāhā* ("when does it drop anchor?"), the answer was *fīma anta min dhikrāhā · ilā rabbika muntahāhā* — ⭐ **"that's not yours to know — its end is with your Lord."** ⭐ **Same truth, two surahs.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***in* = NEGATION** here (*in an-nāfiyah*), equal to *mā*. ⭐ **You met this trap in Takwīr 81:27** (*in huwa illā dhikr* = "it is **nothing but** a reminder"). ⭐ **Don't read it as "if."**
- **Why this exact word:** *in adrī* — ⭐ **a short, bare admission — with no softening.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.10 JINN: "*lā nadrī*" (we don't know). v.25 PROPHET ﷺ: "*in adrī*" (I don't know).** ⭐ **Same humility.** ⚠️⭐⭐ ***IN* = NOT here** (like Takwīr 81:27). ⭐ **Nāziʿāt 79:44: "its end is with your Lord."**

**48. أَقَرِيبٌ مَّا تُوعَدُونَ أَمْ يَجْعَلُ لَهُ رَبِّي أَمَدًا — *a-qarībun mā tūʿadūna am yajʿalu lahu rabbī amadā*** · v.25
- **Root feelings:** *qarīb* — **NEAR** (*qurb*; ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:19** *iqtarib*). *tūʿadūn* — **you are PROMISED/threatened** (*waʿd* = promise; *waʿīd* = threat; passive). *amad* — ⭐ **a SPAN of time, a set term, a far limit** — the distance to an end-point.
- **Here it means:** "**…whether what you are promised is near, or whether my Lord will set for it a distant term.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The two options are left OPEN — on purpose.** ⭐ **Near, or far?** The Prophet ﷺ doesn't know — and that **uncertainty is itself the warning**: ⭐ it could be **today**. ⭐⭐ ***Amad* = the distance to a limit** — ⭐ the word implies there **is** an end-point, just not a known one. ⭐ Like **Nāziʿāt 79:42**'s *mursāhā* (**its anchoring**) — ⭐ **the Hour has a harbour; the date is hidden.**
  ⭐ And note *tūʿadūn* is **passive** — "**you are promised**" — the Promiser unnamed, the promise certain.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***a-… am…*** = "**whether… or…**" (the same alternative-question as v.10!). ⭐ *tūʿadūna* = passive present of *waʿada* (the initial *wāw* becomes the long *ū*).
- **Why this exact word:** *amad* (**a span to a limit**) — ⭐ **affirms the Hour has a fixed end while hiding its distance.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ **NEAR or FAR? Unknown — and that's the warning: it could be TODAY.** ⭐ ***Amad* = the distance to a fixed LIMIT.** ⭐ **Like Nāziʿāt 79:42's *mursāhā* — the Hour has a harbour; the date is hidden.** ⭐ ***A-… am…* — same structure as v.10.**

## §9 — The Guarded Unseen (v.26–28)

**49. عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ فَلَا يُظْهِرُ عَلَىٰ غَيْبِهِ أَحَدًا — *ʿālimu'l-ghaybi fa-lā yuẓhiru ʿalā ghaybihi aḥadā*** · v.26
- **Root feelings:** *ghayb* — the "**ABSENCE / the hidden**" family — ***ghayb* = the UNSEEN**; *ghāʾib* = absent (⭐ **Infiṭār 82:16** *bi-ghāʾibīn*); ⭐ *ghībah* = **backbiting** (talking about the absent!); ⭐ *ghaybūbah* = **a COMA / unconsciousness** (being "absent"!). *yuẓhir* — the "**APPEARING / the back / prominence**" family — *ẓahara* = to appear; ⭐ ***aẓhara ʿalā* = to DISCLOSE to, to give mastery over**; ⭐ *ẓahr* = the back (⭐ **Sharḥ 94:3**); ⭐ *ẓuhr* = **NOON** (when the sun is most apparent — **Ṣalāt aẓ-Ẓuhr**!).
- **Here it means:** "**[He is] the Knower of the Unseen, and He does not disclose His Unseen to anyone.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **This verse answers the whole first half of the surah.** ⭐ The jinn **used to sit in listening-seats** (v.9) trying to **steal glimpses of the unseen**. ⭐ Soothsayers (*kuhhān*) claimed jinn told them secrets. ⭐⭐ **v.26 closes the door completely: ⭐ He discloses His *ghayb* to NO ONE.** ⭐ And ***aḥadā*** — **#6, the last**. ⭐⭐⭐ **SIX *aḥad*s — and the last one says: no one gets the unseen except by His choice.**
  ⭐ ***Ẓuhr* (noon)** is your hook: ⭐ ***aẓhara* = to make apparent** — like the noon sun showing everything. ⭐ **He doesn't put His *ghayb* in the noon light for anyone.**
  ⭐ The clinical hook: ⭐ ***ghaybūbah* = a COMA** — the patient is *ghāʾib*, "absent."
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿālimu*** = *khabar* of an implied "He is." ⭐ ***aẓhara ʿalā*** = "to disclose **to** / let someone overpower" — ⚠️ the preposition *ʿalā* is fixed.
- **Why this exact word:** *ghayb* (**the absent/hidden**) — ⭐ **the exact thing the jinn tried to steal and the soothsayers claimed to sell.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHAYBŪBAH* = a COMA (being "absent"); *ghībah* = BACKBITING (about the absent).** ⭐⭐ ***ẒUHR* = NOON** — *aẓhara* = make apparent. ⭐ **He puts His *ghayb* in nobody's noon light.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Aḥadā* #6 — the last: NO ONE gets the unseen except by His choice.**

**50. إِلَّا مَنِ ارْتَضَىٰ مِن رَّسُولٍ — *illā mani'rtaḍā min rasūl*** · v.27
- **Root feeling:** the "**CONTENTMENT / approval**" family — *raḍiya* = to be pleased; ***irtaḍā* (Form VIII) = to APPROVE AND CHOOSE for oneself**; *murtaḍā* = the approved/chosen one; ⭐ you own *riḍā* across **seven surahs** (88:9, 89:28, 92:21, 93:5, 98:8, 101:7).
- **Here it means:** "**…except a messenger whom He has approved [and chosen].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The one exception — and it's chosen by APPROVAL.** ⭐ Not by effort, not by sitting in "listening seats" (v.9), not by magic. ⭐ ***Irtaḍā*** — Form VIII = ⭐ **He selected with satisfaction, for Himself.** ⭐ The unseen isn't **stolen**; it's **entrusted** — to whom He's pleased with. ⭐⭐ **The riḍā thread gets a new face: not the servant pleased with Allah (Fajr 89:28), but Allah pleased with a messenger — enough to trust him with the unseen.**
  ⭐ *Murtaḍā* — you may know it as a name (⭐ **ʿAlī al-Murtaḍā** — "the approved one").
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***illā man*** = exception from v.26's *aḥadā*. ⭐ **Form VIII** of *raḍiya*, weak-final. ⭐ *min rasūlin* = *min bayāniyyah* ("**namely** a messenger").
- **Why this exact word:** *irtaḍā* — ⭐ **makes access to the unseen a matter of divine approval, not technique.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***MURTAḌĀ* = "the APPROVED one"** (ʿAlī al-Murtaḍā). ⭐⭐ **The unseen isn't STOLEN (v.9) — it's ENTRUSTED to whom He approves (v.27).** ⭐ ***Riḍā* thread: now ALLAH approving a messenger.**

**51. فَإِنَّهُ يَسْلُكُ مِن بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِ رَصَدًا — *fa-innahu yasluku min bayni yadayhi wa min khalfihi raṣadā*** · v.27
- **Root feelings:** *yasluk* — **to THREAD / insert / send along a path** (*silk* = wire/thread — v.17). *bayna yadayhi* — ⭐ **"between his two hands" = IN FRONT OF him** (an idiom). *khalf* — **BEHIND** (⭐ *khalīfah* = a successor, one who comes **after**). *raṣad* — **watchers / guards lying in wait** (v.9).
- **Here it means:** "**…then He sends, in front of him and behind him, guards [to watch over him].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SURAH'S TWO HALVES MEET IN ONE WORD.**
  ⭐ **v.9:** *yajid lahu shihāban **raṣadā*** — a **guard lying in wait** to **BLOCK** the thief.
  ⭐ **v.27:** *yasluku… **raṣadā*** — **guards threaded around** the messenger to **PROTECT** the message.
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Same word, same surah: *raṣad* that STOPS the stealing of revelation, and *raṣad* that ESCORTS the delivery of revelation.** ⭐ The jinn learned (v.8–9) that the sky was guarded; ⭐ **now we learn why**: **the message is under armed escort** from heaven to the messenger's lips, so that nothing is added or stolen.
  ⭐⭐ And ***yasluku*** — the **thread** verb from **v.17** (*yaslukhu ʿadhāban ṣaʿadā*): ⭐ **v.17: the denier THREADED into a climbing punishment. v.27: guards THREADED around the messenger.** ⭐ **Same verb — one is confinement, the other is protection.**
  ⭐ And ***bayna yadayhi*** — "between his two hands" = **in front** — ⭐ a lovely idiom: **what's in front of you is what's between your hands.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bayna yadayhi*** — **dual** (*yadayn*), genitive, nūn dropped in *iḍāfah*. ⭐ *raṣadan* = object of *yasluku* (a collective/verbal noun: "a guard-band").
- **Why this exact word:** *raṣad* again — ⭐ **so the opening's "guarded sky" and the ending's "guarded messenger" are revealed as one system.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RAṢAD* v.9 = guards BLOCKING thieves of revelation. *RAṢAD* v.27 = guards ESCORTING revelation.** ⭐ **One system: the message under armed escort.** ⭐⭐ ***Yasluk*: v.17 threaded INTO punishment; v.27 threaded AROUND for protection.** ⭐ ***Bayna yadayhi* = "between his hands" = IN FRONT.** ⭐ ***Khalīfah* = one who comes AFTER (*khalf*).**

**52. لِّيَعْلَمَ أَن قَدْ أَبْلَغُوا رِسَالَاتِ رَبِّهِمْ — *li-yaʿlama an qad ablaghū risālāti rabbihim*** · v.28
- **Root feelings:** *ablaghū* — **Form IV of *balagha*** = **to make reach, to deliver** (v.23 *balāgh*; ⭐ *balāghah*, rhetoric!). *risālāt* — **messages** (⭐ *risālah* = a letter).
- **Here it means:** "**…so that He may make evident that they have delivered the messages of their Lord.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **The purpose of the guards (v.27): ⭐ to ensure the messages are DELIVERED intact.** ⭐ *Li-yaʿlama* = "so that He may **know**" — ⭐ classical note: not that Allah learns something new, but that it **becomes manifest / established in reality** (*ʿilm ẓuhūr*). ⭐⭐ And ***qad ablaghū*** — "**they HAVE delivered**" — ⭐ ***qad* + past = certainty / completion.** ⭐ **The messengers' job (*balāgh*, v.23) is confirmed DONE.** ⭐ Compare **Naṣr 110** — the **completion** of the Prophet's ﷺ mission. ⭐ ***Balāgh* in v.23 (the duty) → *ablaghū* in v.28 (the duty fulfilled).**
  ⭐ ⚠️ Note the shift from **singular** (*rasūl*, v.27) to **plural** (*ablaghū*, *risālāt*) — ⭐ **the principle covers every messenger, not just one.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***li-*** = purpose + subjunctive. ⭐ ***qad* + past** = "**have indeed**." ⭐ **Form IV** (causative: made it reach).
- **Why this exact word:** *ablagha* — ⭐ **closes the *balāgh* thread: commanded (v.23), guarded (v.27), completed (v.28).**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Balāgh* thread: the DUTY (v.23) → GUARDED (v.27) → COMPLETED (v.28: *qad ablaghū*).** ⭐ ***Qad* + past = HAVE indeed.** ⭐ **Singular *rasūl* → plural *ablaghū*: every messenger.** ⭐ ***Risālah* = a LETTER.**

**53. وَأَحَاطَ بِمَا لَدَيْهِمْ — *wa aḥāṭa bi-mā ladayhim*** · v.28
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**SURROUNDING / encircling**" family — *ḥāṭa* = to surround; ***aḥāṭa bi-* = to ENCOMPASS fully, to surround on all sides**; ⭐⭐ ***muḥīṭ* = an OCEAN** (⭐ *al-Muḥīṭ al-Hādī* = **the PACIFIC Ocean**; *al-Muḥīṭ al-Aṭlasī* = the Atlantic!) — the water that surrounds the land; ⭐ ***muḥīṭ* also = a CIRCUMFERENCE / perimeter** (math!) and **an ENVIRONMENT** (surroundings); ⭐ *ḥāʾiṭ* = **a WALL** (that encloses!).
- **Here it means:** "**And He has encompassed whatever is with them.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Aḥāṭa* = to surround completely — like an OCEAN surrounds land.** ⭐ Nothing the messengers carry is outside His encirclement. ⭐ ***Ladayhim*** = "**with them / in their possession**" — ⭐ every word, every detail. ⭐⭐ You own *muḥīṭ* from **Burūj 85:20** — *wa'llāhu min warāʾihim **muḥīṭ*** — ⭐ "**Allah encompasses them from behind**" — ⭐ **said of the persecutors.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Burūj: He encompasses the ENEMIES (so they can't escape). Jinn: He encompasses the MESSENGERS (so nothing is lost).** ⭐ **The same encircling — a trap for one, a safeguard for the other.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***al-muḥīṭ* = the OCEAN**; ⭐ ***ḥāʾiṭ* = a WALL**; ⭐ ***muḥīṭ ad-dāʾirah* = a circle's circumference**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***aḥāṭa bi-*** — the *bi-* is fixed. ⭐ *ladā* = "**with / in the presence of**" (more immediate than *ʿinda*).
- **Why this exact word:** *aḥāṭa* — ⭐ **total encirclement, with no gap for loss or tampering.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-MUḤĪṬ AL-HĀDĪ* = the PACIFIC OCEAN.** ⭐ **The ocean SURROUNDS land — He surrounds all they carry.** ⭐ ***Ḥāʾiṭ* = a WALL.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Burūj 85:20: He encompasses the PERSECUTORS (trap). Jinn 72:28: He encompasses the MESSENGERS (safeguard).**

**54. وَأَحْصَىٰ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ عَدَدًا — *wa aḥṣā kulla shayʾin ʿadadā*** · v.28
- **Root feelings:** *aḥṣā* — ⭐⭐ the "**COUNTING with PEBBLES**" family — ***ḥaṣā* = PEBBLES**; ***aḥṣā* = to count precisely, item by item** (the ancient way: a **pebble for each thing**); ⭐ ***iḥṣāʾ* = STATISTICS** (⭐ modern: *al-iḥṣāʾ* = statistics; *iḥṣāʾiyyāt* = **figures/statistics**!); ⭐ *ḥaṣāh* = **a kidney/gall STONE** (clinical!). *ʿadad* — **NUMBER** (v.24).
- **Here it means:** "**…and has counted every single thing in number.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SURAH'S LAST WORDS — nearly identical to a verse you own.**
  ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:29** — *wa kulla shayʾin **aḥṣaynāhu** kitābā* — "**and every thing We have counted in a record**."
  ⭐ **Jinn 72:28** — *wa **aḥṣā** kulla shayʾin **ʿadadā*** — "**and has counted every thing in number**."
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Same verb, same phrase *kulla shayʾ*.** ⭐ **Nabaʾ: counted into a BOOK (*kitābā*). Jinn: counted by NUMBER (*ʿadadā*).** ⭐ **Every thing written AND numbered.**
  ⭐⭐ **And the pebbles:** ⭐ ***ḥaṣā* = pebbles** — the ancient tally: drop a **pebble for each item**. ⭐ **Not an estimate. A pebble each.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the *ʿadad* closes the surah's argument:** ⭐ **v.24 — the boasters thought they had the bigger NUMBERS (*aqallu ʿadadā*). v.28 — He has NUMBERED every single thing (*ʿadadā*).** ⭐ **They counted their followers; He counts everything — including them.**
  ⭐ The clinical hook: ⭐ ***ḥaṣāh* = a kidney/gall STONE** (*ḥaṣā kulwiyyah*). ⭐ And ***iḥṣāʾ* = STATISTICS** — the science of counting.
  ⭐ **The final rhyme: *ʿadadā*** — ⭐ **the last of 28 verses ending in *-adā*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV** (*aḥṣā*), weak-final. ⭐ *ʿadadan* = **tamyīz** or *maf'ūl muṭlaq*-like ("counted **in terms of** number"). ⭐ *kulla shayʾin* = object, fronted in Nabaʾ, here after the verb.
- **Why this exact word:** *aḥṣā* (**pebble-counting**) — ⭐ **total, item-by-item precision — the surah's final word on the unseen: nothing escapes the count.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***IḤṢĀʾ* = STATISTICS; *ḥaṣā* = PEBBLES; *ḥaṣāh* = a KIDNEY STONE.** ⭐ **A PEBBLE for every single thing.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:29: counted into a BOOK. Jinn 72:28: counted by NUMBER.** ⭐⭐ **v.24: they boasted NUMBERS. v.28: He has numbered EVERYTHING.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْقَاسِطُونَ (v.14)** — ⚠️ ***qāsiṭ* (Form I) = UNJUST**; ⭐ ***muqsiṭ* (Form IV) = JUST** (*al-Muqsiṭ* is a Divine Name). **Same root, opposite meanings.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **ظَنَنَّا in v.5 vs v.12** — ⚠️ **v.5 = a mistaken ASSUMPTION; v.12 = CERTAINTY.** ⭐ **Same word, same surah.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **رَصَدًا in v.9 vs v.27** — ⚠️ **v.9 guards BLOCK thieves; v.27 guards ESCORT revelation.** **Read them together.**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **v.10 adab** — ⚠️ **evil is PASSIVE (*urīda*, no doer); good is ACTIVE with "their Lord" as subject.** ⭐ **Don't miss the asymmetry.**
5. ⭐⭐ **يُجِيرَنِي (v.22)** — ⚠️ **from *j-w-r* (neighbour/protection)**, ⭐ **NOT *ajr* (wage) or *ījār* (rent).**
6. ⭐⭐ **إِنْ أَدْرِي (v.25)** — ⚠️ ***in* = NOT**, not "if." ⭐ **Same as Takwīr 81:27.**
7. ⭐⭐ **لَمَّا (v.13, 19)** — ⚠️ **+ PAST = "when / as soon as."** ⭐ **Compare ʿAbasa 80:23: *lammā* + JUSSIVE = "not yet."**
8. ⭐⭐ **عَجَبًا (v.1)** — ⚠️ **a NOUN used as an adjective** (*waṣf bi'l-maṣdar*) = "**wonder itself**," not merely "wonderful." ⭐ **Same device in *raṣadā* (v.9) and *ṣaʿadā* (v.17).**
9. ⭐⭐ **اسْتَمَعَ (v.1, 9)** — ⚠️ **Form VIII = LISTEN attentively** (≠ *samiʿa*, hear). ⭐ **Used for faithful listening (v.1) AND eavesdropping (v.9).**
10. ⭐⭐ **لَهُ ... خَالِدِينَ (v.23)** — ⚠️ ***man* = singular FORM, plural MEANING** — hence the switch from *lahu* to *khālidīna*.
11. ⭐ **وَأَنَّا / وَإِنَّا (v.1–14)** — ⚠️ **two authentic recitations** (fatḥah / kasrah). ⭐ **Same meaning.**
12. ⭐ **Diptotes** — ⚠️ ***maqāʿida* (v.9), *ṭarāʾiqa* (v.11), *al-masājida* (v.18), *jahannama* (v.15, 23)** — ⭐ **no tanwīn, fatḥah in the genitive.**
13. ⭐ **v.16 onward** — ⚠️ **generally read as Allah's words**, no longer the jinn's quoted speech.
14. ⭐ **يَعْصِ (v.23)** — ⚠️ **jussive drops the final yāʾ.**
