# Surah Al-Falaq (113) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–5)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**ONE Name of Allah against FOUR evils — while the very next surah does the reverse.**

⭐ **Al-Falaq (113):** ⭐ **seek refuge in ONE attribute — *Rabb al-Falaq* (Lord of the Daybreak) — ⭐ **from FOUR evils**: everything created, the settling dark, the knot-blowers, the envier.**

⭐ **An-Nās (114):** ⭐ **seek refuge in THREE attributes — *Rabb*, *Malik*, *Ilāh* of mankind — ⭐ **from ONE evil**: the whisperer.**

⭐⭐⭐ **Classical scholars (notably Ibn al-Qayyim) explain the imbalance:** ⭐ **Falaq's evils threaten your BODY and WORLDLY life** (darkness, magic, envy) — ⭐ **Nās's evil threatens your FAITH** (whispering into the heart). ⭐⭐ **The harm to your religion is greater — so you call on MORE of His Names against it.** ⭐ **Four evils need one Name; one whisper needs three.**

⭐ **These two are ***al-Muʿawwidhatān*** — "the two [surahs] of seeking refuge."** ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ would recite them (with al-Ikhlāṣ) into his cupped hands each night before sleep and wipe them over his body; ⭐ **and he advised reciting them after every prayer.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **FALAQ = 1 Name vs 4 evils (to your BODY). NĀS = 3 Names vs 1 evil (to your FAITH).** ⭐ **The bigger danger gets more Names.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The refuge** (v.1) — ⭐ **in the Lord of the Daybreak.**
2. **The general** (v.2) — ⭐ **from the evil of what He created.**
3. **Three specifics** (v.3–5) — ⭐ **the settling dark · those who blow on knots · the envier when he envies.**

## §1 — The Refuge (v.1)

**1. أَعُوذُ — *aʿūdhu*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**seeking refuge / clinging for protection**" family — *ʿādha* = **to take refuge with, to cling to someone for safety**; ⭐ ***maʿādh* = a refuge*** (⭐ ***maʿādha'llāh!* = "Allah forbid!" — literally "refuge with Allah!"**); ⭐ ***taʿawwudh* = saying *aʿūdhu billāh*.** ⭐ **Classical lexicographers connect it to ⭐ **a young animal pressing itself against its mother**.
- **Here it means:** "**Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of the daybreak.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **you say this word before every recitation — and the image is a child running to its mother.**
  
  ⭐ ***Aʿūdhu* isn't just "I ask for protection."*** ⭐ **It's ⭐ **I CLING** — like a frightened child pressing against a parent, ⭐ **body to body.** ⭐ **Not a request filed from a distance — a run toward safety.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And you already say it constantly:**
  - ⭐ ***aʿūdhu billāhi mina'sh-shayṭāni'r-rajīm*** — ⭐ **before every recitation** (⭐ **and *rajīm* you own from Takwīr 81:25!**)
  - ⭐ ***maʿādha'llāh!*** — ⭐ **"God forbid!" — literally "refuge in Allah!"**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the PRESENT tense *aʿūdhu*** — ⭐ **"I AM seeking refuge," now, ongoing.** ⭐ **Not "I sought" — ⭐ **a refuge you live in continuously.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Compare Qāriʿah 101:9** — *fa-**ummuhu** hāwiyah* — ⭐ **"his MOTHER [refuge] is the Abyss."** ⭐ **There, the refuge-image was horrifying: the only place left to run was a pit.** ⭐⭐ **Here, the refuge is ⭐ **the Lord of the Daybreak.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **hollow verb** (ع-و-ذ), present 1st person. ⭐ ***aʿūdhu bi-*** = "I seek refuge **IN/WITH**" — ⚠️ **the *bi-* is fixed.**
- **Why this word:** *ʿādha* (**clinging for refuge**) — ⭐ **more intimate and urgent than *istaʿāna* (seeking help).**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAʿĀDHA'LLĀH!* = "God forbid!" — literally "REFUGE in Allah!"** ⭐ **And *aʿūdhu billāh* before every recitation.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿĀdha* = a young animal PRESSING against its mother.** ⭐ **Compare Qāriʿah 101:9: his "mother" was the ABYSS — here it's the Lord of the Daybreak.**

**2. بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ — *bi-rabbi'l-falaq*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**SPLITTING / cleaving apart**" family — *falaqa* = **to split, to cleave** (⭐ ***falaqa'l-ḥabba* = He split the seed**); ⭐ ***al-falaq* = DAYBREAK** — ⭐ **the dawn SPLITTING the night**; ⭐ **modern Arabic: *falaq* also = a split, a cleft.**
- **Here it means:** "**the Lord of the Daybreak.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE THIRD "SPLITTING" ROOT in your study — and the logic of the refuge is perfect.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **You now own THREE different Arabic roots that all mean "to split" — and all used of dawn or creation:**
  - ⭐ ***F-J-R*** — ***fajr*** (Fajr 89:1, Qadr 97:5) — ⭐ **dawn BURSTING through**
  - ⭐ ***F-Ṭ-R*** — ***infaṭarat*** (Infiṭār 82:1) — ⭐ **the sky SPLITTING; *Fāṭir* = the Originator who split creation into being**
  - ⭐ ***F-L-Q*** — ***al-falaq*** (Falaq 113:1) — ⭐ **daybreak CLEAVING the night**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And elsewhere the Qurʾān says: ⭐ ***inna'llāha fāliqu'l-ḥabbi wa'n-nawā*** — "**Allah is the SPLITTER of the seed and the date-stone**" (6:95), ⭐ **and *fāliqu'l-iṣbāḥ* — "**SPLITTER of the dawn**" (6:96).** ⭐ **The same verb for a seed cracking open in soil and the sun cracking open the sky.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW THE LOGIC OF THE REFUGE:** ⭐ **why seek protection in "the Lord of the Daybreak"** specifically? ⭐ **Because the evils that follow are DARK: ⭐ **the settling night (v.3), hidden sorcery (v.4), concealed envy (v.5).** ⭐⭐ **And who do you go to against darkness? ⭐ **The One who SPLITS darkness every single morning.** ⭐ **Every dawn is evidence that He can tear open whatever night has closed over you.**
  
  ⭐ **And *Rabb* = the NURTURER, the Sustainer** — ⭐ **not "the Mighty" — ⭐ **refuge in His care.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bi-rabbi*** — *bi-* after *aʿūdhu*; ⭐ ***iḍāfah*** ("Lord **of** the daybreak").
- **Why this word:** *falaq* (**splitting dawn**) — ⭐ **chosen precisely because every evil named next is a form of darkness.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE "SPLITTING" ROOTS: *f-j-r* (FAJR, 89:1), *f-ṭ-r* (INFAṬARAT/FĀṬIR, 82:1), *f-l-q* (FALAQ, 113:1).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Why "Lord of the DAYBREAK"? Because every evil that follows is DARK — ⭐ **and He splits darkness every morning.** ⭐ ***Fāliq al-ḥabb* = He splits the SEED (6:95).**

## §2 — The General (v.2)

**3. مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ — *min sharri mā khalaq*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *sharr* — ⭐ **EVIL / harm** (⚠️ **also an irregular comparative "worse"** — you own it from **Bayyinah 98:6**, *sharru'l-bariyyah*, and **Zalzalah 99:8**, *sharran yarah*). *khalaqa* — **to create**.
- **Here it means:** "**from the evil of what He created.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **one grammatical choice here is a lesson in *adab* (courtesy) toward Allah.**
  
  ⭐ **It doesn't say "**from the evil He created**."** ⭐ **It says ***min sharri MĀ KHALAQ*** — "**from the evil OF what He created**."** ⭐⭐ **The evil is attributed to **creation**, not to the act of creating.** ⭐ **He created things; ⭐ **some created things cause harm.** ⭐ **Evil is never attached to His name directly.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **This is a consistent Qurʾānic courtesy: ⭐ **Ibrāhīm says "*when I am ill, He heals me*" (26:80)** — ⭐ **illness attributed to himself, healing to Allah.** ⭐ **Same adab here.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And the STRUCTURE: general, then specific.** ⭐ **v.2 = ⭐ **ALL harm from ALL creation** (the umbrella).** ⭐ **v.3–5 = ⭐ **three particular evils singled out** (because they're hidden and especially dangerous).** ⭐ **Like a prescription: a broad-spectrum cover, then three targeted ones.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note *sharr* repeats FOUR times** — ⭐ ***min sharri… wa min sharri… wa min sharri… wa min sharri*** — ⭐ **the four evils, each introduced by the same phrase, like four doors being bolted one by one.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***min sharri*** = "**from** the evil **of**" — ⭐ ***iḍāfah***. ⭐ ***mā khalaq*** — relative ("what He created").
- **Why this word:** *mā khalaq* (**what He created**) — ⭐ **broad enough to cover everything, while keeping evil off His Name.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"The evil OF what He created" — NOT "the evil He created."** ⭐ **Evil is attached to CREATION, never to His Name.** ⭐ **Like Ibrāhīm (26:80): "when I am ill, HE heals me."** ⭐⭐ **General (v.2) → three specifics (v.3–5)** — ⭐ **broad-spectrum cover, then targeted.** ⭐ ***Sharr* ×4 = four doors bolted.**

## §3 — Three Specific Evils (v.3–5)

**4. غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ — *ghāsiqin idhā waqab*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *ghāsiq* — ⭐ the "**DARKNESS deepening / intense gloom**" family — *ghasaqa* = **for the night to become fully dark**; ⭐ ***ghasaq al-layl* = the darkness of night** (⭐ ***ṣalāh… ilā ghasaqi'l-layl*, 17:78** — prayer times "until the darkness of night"). *waqaba* — ⭐ the "**ENTERING / settling in / penetrating**" family — ⭐ ***waqb* = a HOLLOW in rock where water collects***; *waqaba* = **to enter and settle, to come down into**.
- **Here it means:** "**and from the evil of darkness when it settles.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the root gives you a stunning link to Nabaʾ — and the night here is the opposite of Ḍuḥā's.**
  
  ⭐ ***Ghāsiq* = darkness that has deepened fully.*** ⭐ ***Waqaba* = SETTLED IN, like water pooling in a hollow.** ⭐⭐ **So: ⭐ **the dark when it has fully **sunk in and pooled** around you.** ⭐ **Why fear it?** ⭐ **Because darkness is when harm moves unseen — ⭐ **predators, thieves, and the hidden evils that follow in v.4–5.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And you own this root from Nabaʾ 78:25** — *illā ḥamīman wa **GHASSĀQĀ*** — ⭐ **the dark, freezing, foul drink of Hell.** ⭐⭐ **Same root (*gh-s-q*):** ⭐ **Nabaʾ: the *ghassāq* of Hell; ⭐ **Falaq: the *ghāsiq* of night — and you seek refuge from both.**
  
  ⭐ **(A narration from ʿĀʾishah reports the Prophet ﷺ pointing to the MOON and saying "seek refuge from this, for it is the *ghāsiq* when it *waqab*" — ⭐ **read as the moon when it eclipses/darkens.** ⭐ **Both readings: darkness descending.**)
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the night-verb thread across your surahs now has a dark pole:**
  - **Takwīr 81:17** — *ʿasʿas* (⭐ **creeping**)
  - **Fajr 89:4** — *yasr* (⭐ **travelling on**)
  - **Shams 91:4 / Layl 92:1** — *yaghshā* (⭐ **covering**)
  - **Ḍuḥā 93:2** — ***sajā*** (⭐ **going peacefully STILL** — no threat at all)
  - ⭐ **Falaq 113:3** — ***waqab*** (⭐ **SETTLING IN as a threat**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **Ḍuḥā's still night is a comfort; Falaq's settled night is a danger.** ⭐ **Same darkness, two experiences — ⭐ **and the difference is whether you're under His protection.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note *ghāsiqin* is INDEFINITE** — ⭐ **"*a* darkness" — any darkness, any night, any eclipse.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***idhā* + past** = "**when** it [does]" — ⭐ **the evil is tied to a CONDITION: when it settles.** ⭐ ***Ghāsiq*** — active participle.
- **Why this word:** *waqaba* (**settling like water in a hollow**) — ⭐ **pictures darkness as something that **fills a space** and stays.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Waqb* = a HOLLOW where water POOLS.** ⭐ **Darkness that has SETTLED around you.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:25: *GHASSĀQ* (Hell's dark foul drink). Falaq 113:3: *GHĀSIQ* (night's darkness). Same root.** ⭐⭐ **Ḍuḥā 93:2: night goes STILL (comfort). Falaq 113:3: night SETTLES IN (danger).**

**5. النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ — *an-naffāthāti fi'l-ʿuqad*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *naffāthāt* — ⭐ the "**BLOWING with a light spray**" family — *nafatha* = **to blow with a little spittle** (⭐ **between *nafkh*, pure blowing, and *tafl*, spitting**); ⭐ ***nafth* is exactly what the Prophet ﷺ did into his hands with these very surahs!** ⭐ ***naffāthāt* = *faʿʿālāt* intensive, feminine plural = those who HABITUALLY blow.** *ʿuqad* — ⭐ **plural of *ʿuqdah* = A KNOT** — ⭐⭐ **the root of *ʿaqd* = A CONTRACT** and ⭐⭐ ***ʿaqīdah* = CREED/BELIEF** (⭐ **what's firmly TIED in the heart!**).
- **Here it means:** "**and from the evil of those who blow on knots**" — ⭐ **sorcerers, tying knots and blowing on them with spells.**
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the root of "knot" is the root of "creed" — and the juzʾ has taught you about untying.**
  
  ⭐ **Sorcery in that world involved ⭐ **tying KNOTS in a cord and BLOWING spells into each one** — ⭐ **binding the victim symbolically.** ⭐ **Reports connect the revelation of the Muʿawwidhatān to an act of sorcery against the Prophet ﷺ involving a knotted cord; ⭐ **as each verse was recited, a knot loosened.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the *ʿ-q-d* root is enormous:**
  - ⭐ ***ʿuqdah* = A KNOT*** (here)
  - ⭐ ***ʿaqd* = A CONTRACT*** (⭐ **a binding tie between parties** — *ʿaqd az-zawāj*, a marriage contract!)
  - ⭐ ***ʿaqīdah* = CREED*** (⭐ **belief firmly TIED in the heart**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **So the sorcerer TIES knots to harm; ⭐ **the believer's protection is an *ʿaqīdah* — a different kind of knot, tied to Allah.** ⭐ **Knots against knots.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the UNTYING thread you own:**
  - **Balad 90:13** — ***fakku** raqabah* (⭐ **UNTIE a slave's neck** — the steep climb)
  - **Bayyinah 98:1** — ***munfakkīn*** (⭐ **not UNTIED from disbelief** until the Evidence came)
  - **Nāziʿāt 79:2** — ***an-nāshiṭāt*** (⭐ the soul **UNTIED like a knot** — gently drawn out)
  - ⭐ **Falaq 113:4** — ***fi'l-ʿuqad*** (⭐ **knots TIED for harm**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **The juzʾ praises untying — necks, hearts, souls — ⭐ **and seeks refuge from those who tie knots to hurt.**
  
  ⭐⭐ ⚠️ **The FEMININE plural (*naffāthāt*)** — ⭐ **classical readings: ⭐ **(a) *an-nufūs* — "SOULS" (feminine in Arabic) that blow — i.e. any practitioner, male or female; ⭐ (b) female practitioners specifically, as reported in the incident.** ⭐ **Reading (a) is widely held — it's about the **act**, not the gender.**
  
  ⭐ **And the *faʿʿāl* intensive — ⭐ **those who do it HABITUALLY** — ⭐ **same shape as *tawwāb* (110:3), *ḥammālah* (111:4), *khannās* (114:4).**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The irony that heals: ⭐ ***nafth* — the light blowing — is also exactly what the Prophet ﷺ did with these surahs into his palms.** ⭐ **The same action: the sorcerer's *nafth* binds; ⭐ **the believer's *nafth* with the Qurʾān unbinds.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***an-naffāthāt*** — **definite** ("**THE** knot-blowers," a known category) vs v.3's indefinite *ghāsiq*. ⭐ ***Fi'l-ʿuqad*** — "**ON/INTO** the knots."
- **Why this word:** *ʿuqad* (**knots**) — ⭐ **names the actual mechanism of sorcery, and shares a root with the believer's counter-weapon (*ʿaqīdah*).**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿUqdah* = A KNOT; *ʿAQD* = A CONTRACT; *ʿAQĪDAH* = CREED** — ⭐ **ALL "tying."** ⭐ **Sorcerers tie knots to harm; the believer's *ʿaqīdah* is a knot tied to Allah.** ⭐⭐⭐ **UNTYING thread: *fakk* (90:13), *munfakkīn* (98:1), *nāshiṭāt* (79:2) — ⭐ **the juzʾ praises untying.** ⭐⭐ ***Nafth* = light blowing — what the sorcerer does, and what the Prophet ﷺ did with these surahs into his palms.**

**6. حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ — *ḥāsidin idhā ḥasad*** · v.5
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**ENVY**" family — *ḥasada* = **to envy — to wish that someone LOSE a blessing they have** (⚠️ **distinct from *ghibṭah*, wishing to have the same blessing yourself, which is permitted**); ⭐ ***ḥasad* = envy.**
- **Here it means:** "**and from the evil of an envier when he envies.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the final evil — and the *idhā* condition is a mercy hidden in grammar.**
  
  ⭐ **It doesn't say "**from the evil of an envier**."** ⭐ **It says ***ḥāsidin IDHĀ ḤASAD*** — "**an envier WHEN HE ENVIES**."** ⭐⭐ **The harm is tied to the moment he ACTS on it — ⭐ **when the envy moves from feeling to deed (or to the evil eye).** ⭐ **A passing feeling suppressed harms no one but its owner.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And notice the pairing of conditions:** ⭐ ***ghāsiqin IDHĀ waqab*** (v.3) **and** ***ḥāsidin IDHĀ ḥasad*** (v.5). ⭐ **Both evils are dangerous ⭐ **when ACTIVATED**: ⭐ **darkness when it settles, envy when it acts.** ⭐ **The middle one (v.4) has no *idhā* — sorcery is always active by definition.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And envy is ⭐ **the sin that sits at the root of the juzʾ's earlier stories**:** ⭐ **the rivalry of *takāthur* (102:1) — ⭐ **competing for more** — is envy's soil.** ⭐ **The *humazah* (104:1) jabs at others because their worth threatens his.** ⭐ **Iblīs's first sin was envy of Ādam.** ⭐ **Falaq ends on the evil that begins so many others.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note *ḥāsidin* is INDEFINITE** — ⭐ **"*an* envier" — any envier, anywhere.** ⭐ **The surah's pattern: v.3 indefinite (*ghāsiq*), v.4 definite (*an-naffāthāt*), v.5 indefinite (*ḥāsid*).** ⭐ **Definite for the known practitioners; indefinite for the darkness and envy that can come from anywhere.**
  
  ⭐ **The rhyme: *falaq, khalaq, waqab, ʿuqad, ḥasad*** — ⭐ **a sharp *-aq / -ad / -ab*** — ⭐ **clipped, guarding sounds.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***idhā* + past** = conditional "**when**." ⭐ ***Ḥāsidin*** — active participle, indefinite.
- **Why this word:** *ḥasad* with *idhā ḥasad* — ⭐ **names envy precisely and limits the harm to its **activation**.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥasad* = wishing someone LOSES their blessing.** ⚠️ ***Ghibṭah* = wishing to HAVE it too (permitted).** ⭐⭐⭐ **"An envier WHEN HE ENVIES" — ⭐ **harm only when ACTIVATED.** ⭐⭐ **Paired with v.3: darkness WHEN it settles, envy WHEN it acts.** ⭐ **Envy is the soil of *takāthur* (102:1) and *humazah* (104:1) — and Iblīs's first sin.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **"the evil OF what He created,"** ⭐ **NOT "the evil He created."** ⭐ **Evil is attached to creation, never to His Name — a deliberate courtesy.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **الْعُقَدِ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **KNOTS — same root as *ʿaqd* (contract) and *ʿaqīdah* (creed).** ⭐ **Tying for harm vs. tying in faith.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **Falaq vs Nās** — ⚠️ **1 Name / 4 evils (bodily) vs 3 Names / 1 evil (faith).** ⭐ **Read them as a matched pair.**
4. ⭐⭐ **حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **"WHEN he envies"** — the harm is tied to **activation**. ⭐ **And *ḥasad* ≠ *ghibṭah*.**
5. ⭐⭐ **النَّفَّاثَاتِ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **feminine plural widely read as "SOULS that blow"** — ⭐ **any practitioner, not a statement about women.**
6. ⭐⭐ **غَاسِقٍ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **same root as *ghassāq* (Nabaʾ 78:25).** ⭐ **And *waqab* = settling like water in a hollow.**
7. ⭐⭐ **الْفَلَقِ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **the THIRD splitting root** (with *fajr* and *faṭara*). ⭐ **And chosen because every evil named is a form of DARKNESS.**
8. ⭐ **أَعُوذُ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **present tense = an ongoing refuge** — ⭐ **and the image is CLINGING, not filing a request.**
9. ⭐ **Definite vs indefinite** — ⚠️ ***ghāsiq* (indef.), *an-naffāthāt* (def.), *ḥāsid* (indef.).** ⭐ **Known practitioners vs. dangers from anywhere.**
10. ⭐ **إِذَا appears twice (v.3, v.5) but NOT in v.4** — ⚠️ **sorcery is always active; darkness and envy become harmful when activated.**
