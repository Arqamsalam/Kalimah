# Surah Al-Māʿūn (107) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–7)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It says "woe to those who PRAY" — and one preposition is the difference between you and them.**

⭐ **v.4:** *fa-waylun li'l-**muṣallīn*** — "**So woe to those who pray —**"
⭐ **v.5:** *alladhīna hum **ʿAN** ṣalātihim sāhūn* — "**who are heedless OF their prayer.**"

⭐⭐⭐ **The word is ***ʿAN*** (of/away from), not ***FĪ*** (in).** ⭐ **A famous early observation (reported from Anas and others): ⭐ ***"Praise be to Allah it says ʿan ṣalātihim and not fī ṣalātihim."***
- ⭐ ***sāhūn FĪ ṣalātihim*** = absent-minded **IN** their prayer — ⭐ **a lapse every human has** (which is why ***sujūd as-sahw*** exists!).
- ⭐ ***sāhūn ʿAN ṣalātihim*** = heedless **OF** their prayer — ⭐ **neglecting it, delaying it, not caring about it at all.**

⭐⭐ **One preposition separates the normal human from the condemned.**

⭐ **And the surah's structure is a mirror:**
- ⭐ **v.1–3** — ⭐ **failure toward PEOPLE** (shoves the orphan, doesn't urge feeding the poor)
- ⭐ **v.4–7** — ⭐ **failure toward ALLAH** (heedless prayer, showing off) — ⭐ **ending back on people (withholding small kindnesses)**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ ***ʿAN*, not *FĪ*.** ⭐ **Forgetting IN prayer = *sujūd as-sahw*. Forgetting the prayer itself = *wayl*.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The question** (v.1) — ⭐ *have you seen the one who denies the Reckoning?*
2. **The answer is behaviour** (v.2–3) — ⭐ **he shoves the orphan, doesn't urge feeding the poor.**
3. **The shock** (v.4–6) — ⭐ **woe to those who pray — heedlessly, for show.**
4. **The smallest failure** (v.7) — ⭐ **they withhold even the small things.**

## §1 — Denial Defined by Behaviour (v.1–3)

**1. أَرَأَيْتَ الَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّينِ — *a-raʾayta'lladhī yukadhdhibu bi'd-dīn*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *a-raʾayta* — ⭐ **"have you seen…?"** = an idiom: ⭐ **"consider / tell me about."** *dīn* — ⭐ **here: the RECKONING** (from *dayn*, a **DEBT** — Settlement Day).
- **Here it means:** "**Have you seen the one who denies the Reckoning?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the surah defines a theological crime by a social behaviour — and that's revolutionary.**
  
  ⭐ **You'd expect the description of a Reckoning-denier to be about **beliefs**: he doubts resurrection, mocks scripture.** ⭐⭐ **Instead v.2: ⭐ ***"That is the one who SHOVES THE ORPHAN."***
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The Qurʾān identifies denial of the Afterlife **by how you treat orphans**.** ⭐ **Because if you really believed in Settlement Day, ⭐ **you couldn't push a child away.** ⭐ **Your behaviour IS your creed.**
  
  ⭐ **You own *a-raʾayta* from ʿAlaq 96:9, 11, 13** — ⭐ **three unanswered "have you seen?"s about the man who forbids prayer.** ⭐ **Next surah-group, same idiom: ⭐ **ʿAlaq's man stops others' prayer; Māʿūn's man neglects his own.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And *yukadhdhibu bi'd-dīn* you own EXACTLY:**
  - **Infiṭār 82:9** — *bal **tukadhdhibūna bi'd-dīn*** (⭐ **you deny the Reckoning**)
  - **Tīn 95:7** — *fa-mā **yukadhdhibuka** baʿdu **bi'd-dīn*** (⭐ **what makes you deny the Reckoning?**)
  - ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:1** — *alladhī **yukadhdhibu bi'd-dīn***
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ ***Dīn* here = the RECKONING.*** ⭐ **Not "religion"** (as in Bayyinah 98:5 and Kāfirūn 109:6). ⭐ **Watch the sense switch two surahs from now.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **أَرَأَيْتَ** = idiom ("tell me"). ⭐ ***kadhdhaba bi-*** = "deny **something**" (fixed preposition).
- **Why this word:** defining the denier through **v.2–3's actions** ⭐ **refuses to let belief stay abstract.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"Who denies the Reckoning?" → "the one who SHOVES THE ORPHAN."** ⭐ **Behaviour IS creed.** ⭐⭐ ***Yukadhdhibu bi'd-dīn*: Infiṭār 82:9, Tīn 95:7, Māʿūn 107:1.** ⚠️ ***Dīn* = RECKONING here** (*dayn* = debt). ⭐ **And *a-raʾayta* from ʿAlaq 96:9.**

**2. يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ — *yaduʿʿu'l-yatīm*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**SHOVING violently / pushing roughly away**" family — *daʿʿa* = **to push someone forcefully, to repel with harsh shoves** — ⭐ **used elsewhere for people being shoved into Hell (52:13: *yawma yudaʿʿūna ilā nāri jahannama daʿʿā*).**
- **Here it means:** "**That is the one who shoves the orphan away.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the verb is PHYSICAL and VIOLENT — and it's the very verb used for how sinners are shoved into Hell.**
  
  ⭐ ***Daʿʿa* isn't "neglects" or "ignores."*** ⭐ **It's ⭐ **to SHOVE, to drive away roughly with your hands.** ⭐ **The orphan comes asking — and gets pushed.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And elsewhere (52:13), the SAME VERB describes sinners being ***shoved*** into Jahannam.** ⭐ **The man who shoves the orphan away will himself be shoved.** ⭐ **Measure for measure.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **The orphan thread across FIVE surahs — every possible attitude:**
  - **Insān 76:8** — ***yuṭʿimūna*** — the righteous **FEED** him despite their own hunger
  - **Fajr 89:17** — ***lā tukrimūna*** — you don't **HONOUR** him
  - **Balad 90:15** — ***yatīman dhā maqrabah*** — feeding him is **THE STEEP CLIMB**
  - **Ḍuḥā 93:9** — ***fa-lā taqhar*** — do not **CRUSH** him
  - ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:2** — ***yaduʿʿu*** — he **SHOVES** him
  
  ⭐⭐ **Feed → honour → climb for → don't crush → (he) shoves.** ⭐ **Māʿūn is the lowest rung.**
  
  ⭐ **Note *fa-dhālika* ("SO THAT is the one") — ⭐ ***dhālika* is the FAR demonstrative** ("that one, over there") — ⭐ **distancing, contemptuous.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **doubled root** (د-ع-ع) — ⭐ ***yaduʿʿu*** — the doubled letter merges with a *shaddah*. ⭐ **Present tense = HABITUAL: this is what he does.**
- **Why this word:** *daʿʿa* (**violent shoving**) — ⭐ **to name the cruelty physically, so it can't be softened.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Daʿʿa* = TO SHOVE VIOLENTLY** — ⭐ **the SAME VERB used for sinners SHOVED into Hell (52:13).** ⭐ **He shoves; he'll be shoved.** ⭐⭐ **Orphan ladder: FEED (76:8) → HONOUR (89:17) → CLIMB (90:15) → don't CRUSH (93:9) → he SHOVES (107:2).**

**3. وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ — *wa lā yaḥuḍḍu ʿalā ṭaʿāmi'l-miskīn*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**URGING / spurring on**" family — *ḥaḍḍa* = **to urge strongly, to incite someone toward something.**
- **Here it means:** "**and does not urge the feeding of the poor.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **you own this verse almost WORD-FOR-WORD from Fajr 89:18 — but one change in FORM tells you something.**
  
  ⭐ **Fajr 89:18 — *wa lā **taḥāḍḍūna** ʿalā ṭaʿāmi'l-miskīn*** — ⭐ **Form VI: "you don't urge **ONE ANOTHER**"** (a **society's** failure)
  ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:3 — *wa lā **yaḥuḍḍu** ʿalā ṭaʿāmi'l-miskīn*** — ⭐ **Form I: "**HE** doesn't urge"** (an **individual's** failure)
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Same root, same object, same preposition.** ⭐ **Fajr indicted the COMMUNITY; Māʿūn indicts the PERSON.** ⭐ **No hiding in the crowd.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And remember Fajr's insight: ⭐ **the bar is shockingly low.** ⭐ **It doesn't say "he doesn't FEED the poor" — ⭐ **"he doesn't even URGE it."** ⭐ **Urging costs NOTHING.** ⭐ **He fails at the free part.**
  
  ⭐ **And note the wording: *ṭaʿāmi'l-miskīn* — "**the FOOD OF the poor**."** ⭐ **Not "food FOR the poor" — ⭐ **food that BELONGS to them.** ⭐ **Their right, held in your hands.** ⭐ **(Compare Bayyinah/ʿAṣr's *ḥaqq* — what is owed.)**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form I** (*ḥaḍḍa*) vs Fajr's **Form VI** (*taḥāḍḍa*). ⭐ **Doubled root** — note *yaḥuḍḍu*.
- **Why this word:** ⭐ **moving from Form VI to Form I** personalizes the charge.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Fajr 89:18 = *taḥāḍḍūna* (Form VI — "you don't urge EACH OTHER"). ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:3 = *yaḥuḍḍu* (Form I — "HE doesn't urge").** ⭐ **Society → individual. No hiding in the crowd.** ⭐⭐ ***Ṭaʿām al-miskīn* = food that BELONGS to the poor.**

## §2 — The Shock: Woe to Those Who Pray (v.4–6)

**4. فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ — *fa-waylun li'l-muṣallīn*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *wayl* — ⭐ **a cry of doom / woe** — you own it from **Muṭaffifīn 83:1, 10** and **Humazah 104:1**. *muṣallīn* — ⭐ **those who PRAY** — Form II participle of *ṣallā*.
- **Here it means:** "**So woe to those who pray —**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **one of the most shocking phrases in the Qurʾān — and it's deliberately left hanging for a moment.**
  
  ⭐ **"WOE… to those who PRAY."** ⭐ **Read alone, it's almost impossible.** ⭐ **Woe has been for cheaters (83:1), deniers (83:10), slanderers (104:1).** ⭐⭐ **Now: people who PRAY?**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **The suspense is the teaching.** ⭐ **v.5–6 resolve it: woe to those who pray — ⭐ **heedlessly, and for show.** ⭐ **But for one breath, you hear "woe to the praying" and it shocks you into checking your own prayer.**
  
  ⭐ **⚠️ Note: a well-known warning about this verse — ⭐ **never quote v.4 alone**, just as you'd never quote "do not approach prayer" (4:43) without its continuation.** ⭐ **The meaning lives in v.5.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **The *wayl* thread now covers FOUR kinds of people:**
  - **Muṭaffifīn 83:1** — the **CHEATERS** (short-measuring)
  - **Muṭaffifīn 83:10** — the **DENIERS**
  - **Humazah 104:1** — the **SLANDERERS**
  - ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:4** — the **CARELESS PRAYERS**
  
  ⭐ **And the *fa-* ("SO woe") connects it to v.1–3** — ⭐ **the same man who shoves the orphan is the man whose prayer is empty.** ⭐ **The two halves are one portrait.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***waylun*** — indefinite noun starting a sentence, ⭐ **allowed because it's a curse/supplication** (as in Humazah 104:1).
- **Why this word:** *muṣallīn* (**people who pray**) — ⭐ **the most unexpected target, chosen precisely to shock.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **"WOE… to those who PRAY" — ⭐ **a deliberate shock.** ⚠️ **Never quote it without v.5.** ⭐⭐ ***Wayl* targets: CHEATERS (83:1), DENIERS (83:10), SLANDERERS (104:1), CARELESS PRAYERS (107:4).** ⭐ **And the *fa-* joins them to the orphan-shover.**

**5. عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ — *ʿan ṣalātihim sāhūn*** · v.5
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**heedlessness / forgetful neglect / inattention**" family — *sahā* = **to be heedless, absent-minded, to forget**; ⭐⭐ ***sujūd as-SAHW* = the PROSTRATION OF FORGETFULNESS*** (⭐ **the two extra prostrations you make when you forget something in prayer!**).
- **Here it means:** "**who are heedless OF their prayer.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST IMPORTANT PREPOSITION IN THE JUZʾ.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **It says *ʿAN ṣalātihim*, NOT *FĪ ṣalātihim*.**
  - ⭐ ***sāhūn FĪ ṣalātihim*** = ⭐ **absent-minded WITHIN prayer** — losing count, forgetting a rakʿah, your mind wandering.** ⭐ **Every human does this.** ⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ himself did it — which is WHY ***sujūd as-sahw*** was legislated.**
  - ⭐ ***sāhūn ʿAN ṣalātihim*** = ⭐ **heedless OF prayer itself** — ⭐ **delaying it out of its time, skipping it, treating it as unimportant.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **A famous early remark (reported from Anas, and others): ⭐ ***"Praise be to Allah who said 'ʿan ṣalātihim' and not 'fī ṣalātihim.'"*** ⭐ **Because if it had said *fī*, every one of us would be condemned.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the free hook is perfect: ⭐ ***SUJŪD AS-SAHW***.** ⭐ **The same root names the forgiven kind of forgetting (with a built-in fix) and the condemned kind (without one).** ⭐ **One gets two prostrations; the other gets *wayl*.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare ʿAbasa 80:10** — *fa-anta ʿanhu **talahhā*** — ⭐ **distracted FROM the blind man (*ʿan*).** ⭐ **And Takāthur 102:1** — ***alhākum*** — ⭐ **the rivalry DISTRACTED you.** ⭐ **Three heedlessness words: *lahw* twice, *sahw* here.**
  
  ⭐ **Note: *sāhūn* is a PARTICIPLE** — ⭐ **a settled STATE, not a one-off lapse.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐⭐ ***sahā ʿan*** = heedless **OF/AWAY FROM**; ⭐ ***sahā fī*** = absent-minded **IN**. ⭐ **The preposition is the meaning.** ⭐ ***Sāhūn*** — active participle, plural (weak-final: *sāhūna*).
- **Why this word:** *sahw* (**heedlessness**) with *ʿan* — ⭐ **precise enough to exclude the ordinary human lapse.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿAN*, NOT *FĪ*.** ⭐ ***Fī* = forgetting IN prayer (everyone does it). *ʿAn* = neglecting the prayer ITSELF.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SUJŪD AS-SAHW*** — ⭐ **same root: the forgiven forgetting gets 2 prostrations; the condemned kind gets *wayl*.** ⭐ **"Praise Allah it says *ʿan* and not *fī*."**

**6. يُرَاءُونَ — *yurāʾūn*** · v.6
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**SEEING**" family — *raʾā* = to see; ⭐⭐ **Form III *rāʾā* = to make sure others SEE you, to act FOR an audience** → ⭐ ***riyāʾ* = SHOWING OFF / ostentation*** — ⭐ **called "the minor shirk" in hadith.**
- **Here it means:** "**those who make a show [of their deeds].**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the SEEING thread reaches its darkest point — and it's a grammatical inversion.**
  
  ⭐ ***Riyāʾ* literally means ⭐ **"arranging to BE SEEN."** ⭐ **The worship is real in form, but its **audience is people**, not Allah.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Track the seeing thread through your surahs:**
  - **Balad 90:7** — *a-yaḥsabu an **lam yarahu** aḥad* (⭐ **"does he think NO ONE SEES him?"** — he wants to be UNSEEN in sin)
  - **ʿAlaq 96:14** — *bi-anna'llāha **yarā*** (⭐ **"ALLAH SEES"**)
  - **Zalzalah 99:7–8** — ***yarah*** (⭐ **"he will SEE" his deeds**)
  - **Takāthur 102:6–7** — ***la-tarawunna*** (⭐ **"you WILL SEE" the Blaze**)
  - ⭐ **Māʿūn 107:6** — ***yurāʾūn*** (⭐ **he wants PEOPLE to see him in worship**)
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE INVERSION: ⭐ **Balad's man wanted to be UNSEEN while sinning.** ⭐ **Māʿūn's man wants to be SEEN while worshipping.** ⭐ **Both have forgotten the one Viewer who matters (ʿAlaq 96:14).** ⭐ **Hide the sin from people, display the prayer to people — ⭐ **the same blindness, two directions.**
  
  ⭐ **And Form III often carries a sense of **directing an action at others**:** ⭐ ***rāʾā* = "to show [yourself] TO people."**
  
  ⭐⭐ **The antidote is in the very next surah: ⭐ **Kawthar 108:2** — *fa-ṣalli **LI-RABBIKA*** — ⭐ **"pray FOR YOUR LORD"** — ⭐ **to Him, not to the audience.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form III present** (*yurāʾūna*). ⚠️ ⭐ **Note the hamzah (ء) — the root is ر-أ-ي, and Form III produces *yurāʾūn*.** ⭐ **No object stated — ⭐ **they show off, full stop. Generally.**
- **Why this word:** *riyāʾ* (**performing to be seen**) — ⭐ **names the corruption of an act that looks perfect from outside.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RIYĀʾ* = arranging to BE SEEN** — "the minor shirk." ⭐⭐⭐ **INVERSION: Balad 90:7's man wants to be UNSEEN in SIN; Māʿūn 107:6's man wants to be SEEN in WORSHIP.** ⭐ **Both forget ʿAlaq 96:14: "Allah SEES."** ⭐⭐ **And the cure is next surah: *fa-ṣalli LI-RABBIKA* (108:2).**

## §3 — The Smallest Failure (v.7)

**7. وَيَمْنَعُونَ الْمَاعُونَ — *wa yamnaʿūna'l-māʿūn*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *yamnaʿūn* — ⭐ the "**preventing / withholding**" family — *manaʿa* = **to withhold, to prevent**; ⭐ ***mamnūʿ* = FORBIDDEN*** (⭐ **"*mamnūʿ at-tadkhīn*" = no smoking!**); ⭐⭐ **and *mamnūʿ min aṣ-ṣarf* = A DIPTOTE** (⭐ **literally "PREVENTED from full declension" — the grammar term you've been learning all juzʾ!**). *māʿūn* — ⭐ **small, everyday household items lent between neighbours** — ⭐ **a pot, a bucket, salt, an axe, a needle, water, fire to light a lamp** — ⭐ **from *maʿn* = something small, easy, ordinary.**
- **Here it means:** "**and they withhold [even] small kindnesses.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the surah is NAMED after the SMALLEST thing in it — and that's the whole point.**
  
  ⭐ ***Al-Māʿūn* = the little things*** — ⭐ **the cup of sugar, the borrowed ladder, the pinch of salt.** ⭐ **Things that cost nearly nothing to lend.** ⭐ **Classical scholars list: a pot, a bucket, an axe, a sieve, water, salt, fire.** ⭐ **(Some also read it as *zakāh*.)**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the surah's descent is now complete:**
  ⭐ **He denies the Reckoning (v.1) → ⭐ shoves the orphan (v.2) → ⭐ doesn't urge feeding the poor (v.3) → ⭐ neglects his prayer (v.5) → ⭐ shows off (v.6) → ⭐ **won't even lend a pot (v.7).**
  
  ⭐⭐ **The last item is the SMALLEST — and that's what makes it damning.** ⭐ **A man who won't lend salt to a neighbour has a heart that's closed at every level.** ⭐ **The tiny refusal reveals everything.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And notice the parallel with Zalzalah 99:7–8** — ⭐ ***mithqāla dharrah*** — **an ATOM'S weight is counted.** ⭐ **Māʿūn shows you what atom-sized deeds look like in daily life: ⭐ **lending a bucket.**
  
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the grammar hook is a gift: ⭐ ***MAMNŪʿ min aṣ-ṣarf* = a DIPTOTE** — ⭐ **a noun "WITHHELD" from full case-endings.** ⭐ **You've learned *ḥadāʾiq*, *zarābiyy*, *abābīl*, *maqābir* as diptotes — ⭐ **every one of them is *mamnūʿ*, "withheld," the same word as v.7.**
  
  ⭐ **The rhyme: *dīn, yatīm, miskīn, muṣallīn, sāhūn, yurāʾūn, māʿūn*** — ⭐ **closing on *-ūn*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yamnaʿūna*** — present = **habitual**. ⭐ ***Al-māʿūn*** — definite: "**THE** [known] small necessities." ⭐ **Note: *manaʿa* takes the thing withheld as a direct object.**
- **Why this word:** naming the surah after ***māʿūn*** ⭐ **makes the smallest kindness the measure of the whole person.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MĀʿŪN* = a borrowed POT, BUCKET, SALT, AXE.** ⭐ **The surah is NAMED after the smallest kindness.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAMNŪʿ min aṣ-ṣarf* = A DIPTOTE — "WITHHELD" from full declension.** ⭐ **Same root as *yamnaʿūn*!** ⭐⭐ **Descent: deny → shove → don't urge → neglect prayer → show off → won't lend a pot.** ⭐ **Zalzalah 99:7's atom, in daily life.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ (v.5)** — ⚠️ ***ʿAN*, not *FĪ*.** ⭐ ***Fī* = forgetting IN prayer (human, fixed by *sujūd as-sahw*); *ʿan* = neglecting prayer itself (condemned).** **One preposition is the whole verse.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ (v.4)** — ⚠️ **NEVER quote it alone.** ⭐ **The meaning is completed in v.5–6.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **الدِّينِ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **means the RECKONING here** (*dayn* = debt). ⭐ **In Kāfirūn 109:6, two surahs later, *dīn* = RELIGION.** **Watch the switch.**
4. ⭐⭐ **يَحُضُّ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **Form I (HE doesn't urge)** vs Fajr 89:18's **Form VI (you don't urge EACH OTHER).** ⭐ **Same verse, sharpened to the individual.**
5. ⭐⭐ **يَدُعُّ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **means SHOVE VIOLENTLY**, not "neglect." ⭐ **Same verb as being shoved into Hell (52:13).**
6. ⭐⭐ **يُرَاءُونَ (v.6)** — ⚠️ **Form III of *raʾā*** = arranging to BE SEEN (*riyāʾ*). ⭐ **Hamzah-carrying root.**
7. ⭐⭐ **الْمَاعُونَ (v.7)** — ⚠️ **SMALL household items** (pot, bucket, salt, axe), not "charity" in general. ⭐ **(Some also read it as zakāh.)**
8. ⭐ **يَمْنَعُونَ (v.7)** — ⚠️ **same root as *mamnūʿ min aṣ-ṣarf* (DIPTOTE).** ⭐ **A free grammar anchor.**
9. ⭐ **فَذَٰلِكَ (v.2)** — ⚠️ **the FAR demonstrative ("THAT one")** — distancing, contemptuous.
10. ⭐ **سَاهُونَ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **a PARTICIPLE = a settled state**, not a single lapse.
