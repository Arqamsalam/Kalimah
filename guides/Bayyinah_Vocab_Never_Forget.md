# Surah Al-Bayyinah (98) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–8)

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**It contains the Qurʾān's shortest complete definition of religion — and it's five items in one verse.**

⭐ **v.5:** *wa mā umirū **illā** li-yaʿbudu'llāha **mukhliṣīna** lahu'd-dīna **ḥunafāʾa** wa **yuqīmu'ṣ-ṣalāta** wa **yuʾtu'z-zakāta** — wa dhālika **dīnu'l-qayyimah***

> ⭐ **"They were commanded **nothing except** to worship Allah, **making the religion purely His**, **inclining to truth**, and to **establish the prayer** and **give the zakāh** — and **that** is the upright religion."**

⭐⭐ **Note the restriction: ***mā umirū illā*** — "they were commanded **NOTHING EXCEPT**."** ⭐ **Not "among other things" — ⭐ **this is the whole list.**

⭐ **Five items: ⭐ worship Him · purely · inclining to truth · establish prayer · give zakāh.** ⭐ **Three about the heart, two about the limbs.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐ **The surah is named "The Clear Evidence" — and its own most famous verse *is* the clear evidence: a definition so tight nothing can be added to it.**

---

## The Big Picture

1. **The state before** (v.1) — ⭐ *they were not going to break off until the Clear Evidence came.*
2. **What the Evidence is** (v.2–3) — ⭐ **a Messenger reciting purified scrolls, containing upright writings.**
3. **The split** (v.4) — ⭐ *they only divided AFTER the Evidence came.*
4. **The definition** (v.5) — ⭐ **the five items.**
5. **Two verdicts** (v.6–7) — ⭐ ***the worst of creation*** and ***the best of creation***.
6. **The reward** (v.8) — ⭐ *He is pleased with them, and they with Him.*

## §1 — The State Before (v.1)

**1. مُنفَكِّينَ — *munfakkīn*** · v.1
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**undoing / unfastening / separating apart**" family — *fakka* = **to untie, to release, to break something apart at its joint**; ⭐ ***fakk* = also the JAW*** (the joint that opens!); *infikāk* = coming loose, detachment.
- **Here it means:** "**were not going to break off / be released [from their state]** until the Clear Evidence came to them."
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **you own this root from Balad 90:13 — and the connection is remarkable.**
  
  ⭐ **Balad 90:13 — *fakku **raqabah*** — "**the FREEING of a neck**" (⭐ **the first answer to "what is the steep pass?"**).
  ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:1 — ***munfakkīn*** — "**[not] being released**."
  
  ⭐⭐ **Same root: untying a slave's neck, and being untied from a state of disbelief.** ⭐ **Both are **releases from bondage** — one physical, one spiritual.**
  
  ⭐ **And the word here is a *Form VII passive-sense participle*** — ⭐ **they weren't going to come loose **by themselves**.** ⭐ **Something external had to arrive: the *Bayyinah*.**
  
  ⭐ **The verse describes a **stuck** condition** — ⭐ **a knot that wasn't going to untie on its own.** ⭐ **The Messenger is the hand that unties it.**
  
  ⭐ ⚠️ **Note: this verse has several classical interpretations** (about what exactly they weren't separating from, and whether it describes them before or after). ⭐ **The **root image — being stuck until released** — holds across all of them.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form VII** (*infakka*) active participle, plural accusative as *khabar* of *lam yakun*. ⭐ **You own Form VII from Takwīr 81:2 (*inkadarat*), Infiṭār 82:1 (*infaṭarat*), Shams 91:12 (*inbaʿatha*).**
- **Why this word:** *munfakkīn* frames disbelief as ⭐ **a knot**, which makes revelation ⭐ **a release** rather than an imposition.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Fakka* = TO UNTIE.* ⭐⭐ **Balad 90:13: ***fakku raqabah*** — untying a SLAVE'S NECK. ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:1: *munfakkīn* — not being untied from disbelief.** ⭐ **Same root, two bondages.** ⭐ **And *fakk* also = THE JAW (the joint that opens).**

**2. الْبَيِّنَةُ — *al-bayyinah*** · v.1, 4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**clarity / separating and making distinct**" family — *bāna* = to become clear; ⭐ ***bayn* = BETWEEN*** (⭐ **clarity comes from *separating* one thing from another!**); *bayyana* = to clarify; ⭐ ***bayān* = eloquent exposition*** (⭐ **you own it from Qiyāmah 75:19**); ⭐ ***mubīn* = self-evidently clear*** (⭐ **Takwīr 81:23**).
- **Here it means:** "**the Clear Evidence**" — ⭐ **the surah's name.**
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the root's core idea explains what "evidence" means in Arabic.**
  
  ⭐ ***Bayn* = BETWEEN.*** ⭐ **So *bayyinah* = ⭐ **something that puts distance BETWEEN truth and falsehood** — it **separates** them so you can tell which is which.** ⭐ **Clarity, in Arabic, is *distinguishing*, not brightness.
  
  ⭐ **You own the root across three surahs:**
  - **Qiyāmah 75:19** — *thumma inna ʿalaynā ***bayānah*** ("upon Us is its **clarification**")
  - **Takwīr 81:23** — *bi'l-ufuqi'l-**mubīn*** ("the **clear** horizon")
  - ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:1, 4** — ***al-bayyinah*** ("the **Clear Evidence**")
  
  ⭐⭐ **And here is the surah's sharpest irony:** ⭐ **v.1 says they were stuck **until the Clear Evidence came**.** ⭐ **v.4 says they **divided only AFTER** the Clear Evidence came.** ⭐⭐ **The thing that was supposed to unite them by clarifying is what split them.** ⭐ **Not because it was unclear — ⭐ **because clarity forces a choice.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bayyinah*** is an intensive adjective (*fayʿilah*) used as a noun; **feminine**, which is why *tāʾtiyahum* (v.1) and *jāʾathum* (v.4) are feminine verbs.
- **Why this word:** *bayyinah* over "proof" — ⭐ **because it names the *function* (separating) rather than the *form*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Bayn* = BETWEEN.* ⭐ **A *bayyinah* puts distance **between** truth and falsehood.** ⭐⭐ **And the irony: v.1 they were stuck UNTIL it came; v.4 they SPLIT only AFTER it came.** ⭐ **Clarity forces a choice.**

## §2 — What the Evidence Is (v.2–3)

**3. يَتْلُو — *yatlū*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**following after**" family — *talā* = **to follow**; ⭐ **and from that: *talā* = TO RECITE** (⭐ **reciting is *following* the words in order!**); ⭐ ***tilāwah* = recitation.***
- **Here it means:** "**a Messenger from Allah, reciting purified scrolls.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **you own this exact root from Shams 91:2** — *wa'l-qamari idhā **talāhā*** ("the moon when it **FOLLOWS** it").
  
  ⭐⭐ **Same root: the moon **following** the sun, and the Messenger **reciting** revelation.** ⭐ **And the connection is beautiful: ⭐ **the moon has no light of its own; it faithfully transmits what it received.** ⭐ **That's exactly what *tilāwah* is — **following along, transmitting without altering.**
  
  ⭐ **And note the word choice against *iqraʾ* (ʿAlaq 96:1):** ⭐ ***iqraʾ*** = the **command** to read; ⭐ ***yatlū*** = the **ongoing action** of reciting.** ⭐ **ʿAlaq gave the order; Bayyinah shows it being carried out.**
  
  ⭐ **The present tense (*yatlū*) makes it **continuous** — ⭐ **this is what he *does*, not something he did once.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ **weak-final verb** (ت-ل-و) — ⭐ **note the final wāw appears as the long *ū*.** ⭐ ***Rasūlun*** is indefinite, in apposition to *al-bayyinah* — ⭐ **the Evidence IS a Messenger.**
- **Why this word:** *yatlū* (**follow/recite**) rather than *yaqraʾ* — ⭐ **it stresses **faithful transmission**, not performance.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Talā* = TO FOLLOW **and** TO RECITE.** ⭐⭐ **Shams 91:2: the moon FOLLOWS the sun.** ⭐ **The moon RECITES the sun — transmitting light it didn't make.** ⭐ **And ʿAlaq 96:1 gave the command *iqraʾ*; here it's being done.**

**4. صُحُفًا مُّطَهَّرَةً — *ṣuḥufan muṭahharah*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *ṣuḥuf* — ⭐ **scrolls/pages** (*ṣaḥīfah*; ⭐ **modern *ṣaḥāfah* = JOURNALISM**, *ṣaḥīfah* = **a newspaper**). *muṭahharah* — ⭐ the "**purity**" family — ⭐ **you own it from Insān 76:21** (*sharāban **ṭahūrā***, a **purifying** drink) and **ʿAbasa 80:14**.
- **Here it means:** "**purified scrolls.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **this is nearly a verbatim echo of ʿAbasa 80:13–14.**
  
  ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:13–14 — *fī **ṣuḥufin mukarramatin · marfūʿatin muṭahharah*** ("in scrolls **honoured, exalted, purified**").
  ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:2 — *yatlū **ṣuḥufan muṭahharah*** ("reciting **purified scrolls**").
  
  ⭐ **Same noun, same adjective, two surahs.** ⭐ **Free vocabulary.**
  
  ⭐ **And *muṭahharah* is a **passive participle** — ⭐ **they were *made* pure. Someone purified them.** ⭐ **Not "clean by nature" but **actively protected from contamination**.
  
  ⭐ **The *ṣuḥuf* root now appears across FOUR surahs:**
  - **ʿAbasa 80:13** — the scrolls of **revelation** (honoured, in angels' hands)
  - **Takwīr 81:10** — the scrolls of **your deeds** (spread open)
  - **Aʿlā 87:18–19** — the **earliest** scrolls (of Ibrāhīm and Mūsā)
  - ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:2** — the **purified** scrolls being recited
  
  ⭐⭐ **Four archives, one word.**
  
  ⭐ **And remember the everyday hook: ⭐ ***ṣaḥāfah* = JOURNALISM***, *ṣaḥīfah* = a **newspaper**.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **passive participle, Form II** (*muṭahharah*) — ⭐ **recognize the *mu-...-a-* shape.** ⭐ **Both accusative as objects of *yatlū*.**
- **Why this word:** *muṭahharah* (**made pure**) rather than "true" — ⭐ **because purity is about **what's been kept out**, which is the transmission claim.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Ṣaḥāfah* = JOURNALISM**; *ṣaḥīfah* = a NEWSPAPER. ⭐⭐ **Nearly identical to ʿAbasa 80:13–14.** ⭐ **FOUR archives, one word: revelation's scrolls (80:13), YOUR scrolls (81:10), the ancient scrolls (87:18), these purified scrolls (98:2).**

**5. كُتُبٌ قَيِّمَةٌ — *kutubun qayyimah*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *kutub* — ⭐ **writings/books** (*kataba* = to write; ⭐ **you own *kātibīn* from Infiṭār 82:11** — the **writing** scribes). *qayyimah* — ⭐ the "**standing / uprightness**" family — *qāma* = to stand; ⭐ ***qayyim* = upright, correct, well-established, self-sustaining***; ⭐ **and *al-Qayyūm* — a NAME OF ALLAH** (The Self-Subsisting, who **stands** and sustains all).
- **Here it means:** "**within them are upright writings.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the *q-w-m* root is one of your best-tracked, and *qayyimah* is its sharpest form here.**
  
  ⭐ **Track it:**
  - **Takwīr 81:28** — *an ***yastaqīm*** ("to **go straight**")
  - **Tīn 95:4** — *fī aḥsani ***taqwīm*** ("the best **standing/form**")
  - **Bayyinah 98:3** — *kutubun ***qayyimah*** ("**upright** writings")
  - ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:5** — *dīnu'l-***qayyimah*** ("the **upright** religion")
  - ⭐ **And *aṣ-ṣirāṭ al-MUSTAQĪM*** — ⭐ **which you say 17× a day.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **The root means *standing upright AND making others stand*.** ⭐ **So *kutub qayyimah* aren't just "correct books" — ⭐ **they're books that **hold things up**, that keep a person and a society **standing straight**.
  
  ⭐ **And *al-Qayyūm*** (a Divine Name, in Āyat al-Kursī) ⭐ **is the same root**: the One who **stands** by Himself and **sustains** everything else. ⭐ **Free hook from a verse you likely know by heart.**
  
  ⭐ **Note the structure of v.2–3:** ⭐ **a Messenger → reciting scrolls → containing writings.** ⭐ **Three layers: **the man, the medium, the content.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***qayyimah*** is an intensive adjective (*fayʿil* pattern), feminine agreeing with *kutub*. ⭐ **Note *kutubun* is a broken plural treated as feminine singular — ⭐ **a standard Arabic agreement rule for non-human plurals.**
- **Why this word:** *qayyimah* (**upright/sustaining**) rather than *ṣaḥīḥah* (correct) — ⭐ **it names what the books **do to you**, not just what they are.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Al-QAYYŪM*** — the Divine Name in **Āyat al-Kursī** — ⭐ **same root.** ⭐⭐ **And *aṣ-ṣirāṭ al-MUSTAQĪM* — 17× a day.** ⭐ **Also *taqwīm* (Tīn 95:4) and *yastaqīm* (Takwīr 81:28).** ⭐ **These books don't just state truth — they HOLD YOU UP.**

## §3 — The Split, and the Definition (v.4–5)

**6. تَفَرَّقَ — *tafarraqa*** · v.4
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**separating / splitting into groups**" family — *faraqa* = to separate; ⭐ ***firqah* = a sect/faction***; ⭐ ***al-Furqān* = a name of the Qurʾān*** (⭐ **"The Criterion" — that which SEPARATES truth from falsehood!**); ⭐ **and *yawm al-**faṣl**, a different root with the same job** (Nabaʾ 78:17).
- **Here it means:** "**And those given the Scripture did not become divided until AFTER the Clear Evidence came to them.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the verse's timing is the whole indictment.**
  
  ⭐ **It doesn't say they were divided and the Evidence failed to unite them.** ⭐ **It says the division **began after** it arrived.** ⭐⭐ **The clarity itself was the trigger.**
  
  ⭐ **And the root-irony is sharp: ⭐ **the Qurʾān is called ***al-Furqān*** — **the Separator** — ⭐ **because it separates **truth from falsehood**.** ⭐ **Here, the same root describes people separating **from each other**.** ⭐⭐ **The book that separates truth from error caused people who wouldn't accept it to separate from those who would.**
  
  ⭐ **Form V (*tafarraqa*) = ⭐ **they split themselves** — reflexive, self-inflicted.** ⭐ **Nobody divided them; **they came apart.**
  
  ⭐ **Compare Nabaʾ 78:3** — *alladhī hum fīhi ***mukhtalifūn*** ("about which they **DIFFER**"). ⭐ **Nabaʾ: differing about the News. Bayyinah: splitting after the Evidence.** ⭐ **Two surahs on the same human failure.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form V** (*tafaʿʿala*) = **reflexive** — doing it to themselves. ⭐ ***Illā min baʿdi mā*** = "**not except after**" — an emphatic time-restriction.
- **Why this word:** *tafarraqa* (**they split themselves**) rather than a passive — ⭐ **it places the fault entirely with them.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Al-FURQĀN* — a name of the Qurʾān = "THE SEPARATOR."** ⭐ **Same root.** ⭐⭐ **The book that separates TRUTH from FALSEHOOD caused PEOPLE to separate from each other.** ⭐ **And they split only **AFTER** clarity arrived.**

**7. مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ الدِّينَ — *mukhliṣīna lahu'd-dīn*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *mukhliṣ* — ⭐ the "**purifying / making something unmixed**" family — *khalaṣa* = **to become pure, to be free of admixture**; ⭐ ***khāliṣ* = pure, unadulterated*** (⭐ **used of pure gold, pure milk!**); ⭐ ***ikhlāṣ* = SINCERITY*** — ⭐ **and *Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ*!**; *khalāṣ* = deliverance. *dīn* — ⭐ **here: devotion/religion** ⚠️ **(a different sense from Infiṭār 82:9!)**.
- **Here it means:** "**worshipping Allah, making the religion purely His.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the root is a *purity-of-substance* word, and that's what makes it the right word for sincerity.**
  
  ⭐ ***Khāliṣ* is what you say of **pure gold** or **unmixed milk*** — ⭐ **something with **nothing else in it**.** ⭐ **So *ikhlāṣ* isn't "trying hard" — ⭐ **it's **removing every other ingredient** from your intention.**
  
  ⭐ **And *Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ* (112) is named for this** — ⭐ **the surah of **pure, unmixed** monotheism.** ⭐ **Free hook.**
  
  ⭐⭐ ⚠️ **MAJOR TRAP: *dīn* here means RELIGION/DEVOTION — not "the Reckoning."**
  - **Infiṭār 82:9, 15, 17, 18** — *yawm ad-**dīn*** = ⭐ **SETTLEMENT DAY** (from *dayn*, a **debt**)
  - **Tīn 95:7** — *bi'd-**dīn*** = ⭐ **the Reckoning**
  - ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:5 — *lahu'd-**dīn*** = ⭐ **RELIGION / devotion**
  
  ⭐ **Same word, and the root unifies them: ⭐ ***d-y-n* = obligation.*** ⭐ **A *dayn* is a debt you owe; a *dīn* is a devotion you owe.** ⭐⭐ **Both are things you're **obligated** to.** ⭐ **Context decides which.**
  
  ⭐ **And note *lahu* is FRONTED: *mukhliṣīna **LAHU**'d-dīn*** — ⭐ **"making the religion **TO HIM** purely" = **to Him ALONE**.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **Form IV** active participle, plural accusative as *ḥāl* ("**while** making it pure"). ⭐ **The fronting of *lahu* = restriction.**
- **Why this word:** *mukhliṣ* (**unmixed**) rather than a word for honesty — ⭐ **because the test is **what else is in there**, not whether you mean it.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Khāliṣ* = PURE GOLD / UNMIXED MILK.** ⭐ ***Ikhlāṣ* = removing every OTHER ingredient** — ⭐ **and *Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ* is named for it.** ⭐⭐ ⚠️ **And *dīn* HERE = RELIGION, not "the Reckoning" (Infiṭār 82:9). ⭐ **One root: *dayn* = a debt you owe; *dīn* = a devotion you owe.**

**8. حُنَفَاءَ — *ḥunafāʾ*** · v.5
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**inclining / turning away from crookedness toward straightness**" family — *ḥanafa* = **to incline**; ⭐ ***ḥanīf*** = **one who turns away from all false worship toward pure monotheism** — ⭐ **the title given to **Ibrāhīm** (peace be upon him) repeatedly in the Qurʾān.
- **Here it means:** "**inclining to truth / as pure monotheists.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the word's *direction* is the point.**
  
  ⭐ ***Ḥanīf* means someone who has **turned** — ⭐ **away from something, toward something else.** ⭐ **It's a word of **motion and preference**, not just a label.
  
  ⭐⭐ **And it carries enormous weight because it's ***Ibrāhīm's title***.** ⭐ **So v.5 is saying: ⭐ **what you were commanded is exactly what Ibrāhīm did.** ⭐ **Which answers the whole context — the People of the Scripture are being told this isn't a new religion but **the original one**.
  
  ⭐ **Compare Aʿlā 87:18–19** — *inna hādhā la-fi'ṣ-**ṣuḥufi'l-ūlā · ṣuḥufi IBRĀHĪMA wa Mūsā*** ("this is in the earliest scrolls — **the scrolls of IBRĀHĪM** and Mūsā"). ⭐⭐ **Aʿlā names Ibrāhīm's scrolls; Bayyinah names Ibrāhīm's posture.** ⭐ **Both say: this message is the oldest thing there is.**
  
  ⭐ **And note the pairing in v.5: ⭐ ***mukhliṣīn*** (purity of **content** — nothing else mixed in) + ⭐ ***ḥunafāʾ*** (purity of **direction** — turned away from all else).** ⭐ **Inward and outward.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ ***ḥunafāʾa* is a DIPTOTE*** (*mamnūʿ min aṣ-ṣarf*) — ⭐ **it takes **fatḥah without tanwīn** in the accusative.** ⭐ **Plural patterns ending in *-āʾ* usually are.** ⭐ **You own diptotes from *ḥadāʾiq* (78:32), *zarābiyy* (88:16), *Irama* (89:7).**
- **Why this word:** *ḥanīf* carries ⭐ **Ibrāhīm's authority** in a single word.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ḥanīf* = IBRĀHĪM'S TITLE** — one who **TURNS AWAY** from all false worship. ⭐⭐ **And Aʿlā 87:19 named *the scrolls of IBRĀHĪM*.** ⭐ **Both say: this is the ORIGINAL religion.** ⭐ **And *mukhliṣīn* (pure content) + *ḥunafāʾ* (pure direction) = inward and outward.**

**9. يُقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَيُؤْتُوا الزَّكَاةَ — *yuqīmu'ṣ-ṣalāta wa yuʾtu'z-zakāh*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *yuqīmū* — ⭐ **Form IV of *qāma* (to stand)** = ⭐ **to MAKE something stand, to establish it properly** — ⭐ **the same root as *qayyimah* two verses earlier!** *yuʾtū* — **Form IV of "to come"** = **to give/hand over** (⭐ **you own it from Layl 92:18**, *yuʾtī mālahu*). *zakāh* — ⭐ the "**purify + grow**" family.
- **Here it means:** "**and to establish the prayer and give the zakāh.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the verb for prayer is the most under-appreciated word here.**
  
  ⭐ **It does NOT say *yuṣallū* ("they pray").** ⭐ **It says ***yuqīmū*** — **"they MAKE IT STAND."** ⭐⭐ **Prayer isn't something you *perform*; it's something you **erect**, like a structure — ⭐ **built properly, upright, and kept standing.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And here's the payoff: ⭐ **v.3 said the writings are *QAYYIMAH* (upright). ⭐ **v.5 says you must *YUQĪMŪ* the prayer (make it stand).** ⭐ **SAME ROOT, two verses apart.** ⭐ **Upright books produce an upright prayer.** ⭐ **And the verse ends *dīnu'l-**QAYYIMAH*** — ⭐ **THREE appearances of *q-w-m* in three verses.**
  
  ⭐ **And *zakāh* is your most-tracked reward-root, now across SEVEN surahs:**
  - **Nāziʿāt 79:18** (offered to **Pharaoh**) · **ʿAbasa 80:3, 7** (the **blind man**) · **Aʿlā 87:14** · **Shams 91:9** · **Layl 92:18** · ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:5**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And Bayyinah is where it becomes an **institution**.** ⭐ **The other surahs used it as a verb (*purify yourself*); ⭐ **here it's a **noun with the definite article** — ⭐ ***az-Zakāh*, the obligation.**
  
  ⭐ **Note the pairing: ⭐ ***ṣalāh*** (your relationship with Allah) + ⭐ ***zakāh*** (your relationship with people).** ⭐ **Two limbs of the same religion.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **both are **subjunctive** after the *lām* of purpose** (*li-yaʿbudū… wa yuqīmū… wa yuʾtū*) — ⚠️ ⭐ **note they DROP their final nūn.** ⭐ **A standard subjunctive marker for these plural forms.**
- **Why this word:** *yuqīmū* (**make it stand**) rather than *yuṣallū* — ⭐ **because a prayer can be performed and still not be *standing*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **NOT "they pray" — ⭐ **"they MAKE IT STAND."*** ⭐⭐ **And *q-w-m* appears THREE times in three verses: *qayyimah* (v.3), *yuqīmū* (v.5), *al-qayyimah* (v.5).** ⭐ **Upright books → upright prayer → upright religion.** ⭐⭐ **And *zakāh* is now in SEVEN surahs — here as an INSTITUTION, not just a verb.**

## §4 — Two Verdicts, and the Reward (v.6–8)

**10. شَرُّ الْبَرِيَّةِ / خَيْرُ الْبَرِيَّةِ — *sharru'l-bariyyah / khayru'l-bariyyah*** · v.6, 7
- **Root feelings:** *sharr* / *khayr* — ⭐ **worst / best** — ⚠️ **BOTH are IRREGULAR comparatives/superlatives** (no *afʿal* form). *bariyyah* — ⭐ the "**creating from nothing**" family — *baraʾa* = **to create, to originate**; ⭐ ***al-Bāriʾ* — a NAME OF ALLAH*** (The Originator!); ⭐ **and *barāʾah* = innocence/acquittal** (being **clear** of something).
- **Here it means:** "**those are the worst of creation**" (v.6) / "**those are the best of creation**" (v.7).
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **a perfect *muqābala* (mirror-antithesis) — and the word for "creation" is the key.**
  
  ⭐ **The two verses are **grammatically identical** apart from the contents:**
  
  | v.6 | v.7 |
  |---|---|
  | *inna'lladhīna **kafarū*** | *inna'lladhīna **āmanū wa ʿamilu'ṣ-ṣāliḥāt*** |
  | *ulāʾika hum **SHARRU**'l-bariyyah* | *ulāʾika hum **KHAYRU**'l-bariyyah* |
  
  ⭐ **Same frame, opposite superlative.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **And *bariyyah* (from *baraʾa*, to create from nothing) is doing something specific:** ⭐ **it doesn't say "the worst of *people*" — ⭐ **it says "the worst of **everything that was created**."** ⭐ **Including animals, including everything.**
  
  ⭐ **And ***al-Bāriʾ* is a Divine Name*** — ⭐ **so *bariyyah* quietly reminds you **who made them** even as it ranks them.**
  
  ⭐ **Note *ulāʾika **hum*** in both — ⭐ **the *ḍamīr al-faṣl* (separating pronoun)** = ⭐ **restriction: "those, **they specifically**, are…"** ⭐ **You own this from Nāziʿāt 79:39, 41 (*hiya'l-maʾwā*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️ ⭐ ***khayr* and *sharr* are IRREGULAR*** — ⭐ **they serve as comparative AND superlative with no *afʿal* form.** ⭐ **Memorize as exceptions.** ⭐ **You met *khayr* in Aʿlā 87:17, Ḍuḥā 93:4, Qadr 97:3.**
- **Why this word:** *bariyyah* (**all created things**) rather than *nās* (people) — ⭐ **it makes the ranking universal.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Al-BĀRIʾ* — a NAME OF ALLAH** (The Originator). ⭐ **Same root as *bariyyah*.** ⭐⭐ **"Worst/best of ALL CREATION," not just of people.** ⭐ ⚠️ **And *khayr*/*sharr* are IRREGULAR — no *afʿal* form.**

**11. جَنَّاتُ عَدْنٍ ... خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا — *jannātu ʿadnin… khālidīna fīhā abadā*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *ʿadn* — ⭐ the "**permanent residence / staying put**" family — *ʿadana* = **to remain, to dwell permanently**; ⭐ ***maʿdin* = A MINE*** (⭐ **the place where a mineral **permanently resides** in the earth! — and modern *maʿdinī* = mineral**). *khālidīn* — ⭐ the "**eternity**" family — ⭐ **the same root as the name *KHĀLID*** (⭐ **you own it from Insān 76:19**, *wildānun **mukhalladūn***). *abadā* — **forever** (*abadī* = eternal).
- **Here it means:** "**Gardens of perpetual residence… abiding therein forever.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **THREE eternity-words stacked in one verse — and *ʿadn* is the best of them.**
  
  ⭐ ***Jannātu ʿadn*** = ⭐ **"Gardens of PERMANENT RESIDENCE."** ⭐ **Not "Gardens of Eden" as a place-name — ⭐ **the word describes the **nature of the stay**.
  
  ⭐⭐ **And ***maʿdin* = A MINE*** is the perfect hook: ⭐ **a mine is where gold or iron has **settled permanently** in the rock.** ⭐ **It doesn't move. It's *from* there.** ⭐ **That's *ʿadn*.**
  
  ⭐ **Then the verse stacks two more: ⭐ ***khālidīna*** (abiding) + ⭐ ***abadā*** (forever).** ⭐ **Three words for permanence in one clause** — ⭐ **because the surah has just said the disbelievers are *khālidīna fīhā* in Hell (v.6).** ⭐ **The same eternity, two addresses.**
  
  ⭐ **And *Khālid*** is a name you've likely heard all your life — ⭐ **it literally means "the eternal one."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***khālidīna*** is a **plural active participle in the accusative as *ḥāl*** ("**in a state of** abiding"). ⭐ ***Abadā*** is an accusative adverb of time.
- **Why this word:** *ʿadn* names the garden by ⭐ **the permanence of residence**, which is the actual reward.
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Maʿdin* = A MINE** — ⭐ **where a mineral resides PERMANENTLY in the rock.** ⭐ **That's *ʿadn*.** ⭐⭐ **And *KHĀLID* = "the eternal one"** (Insān 76:19's *mukhalladūn*). ⭐ **THREE permanence-words in one verse: *ʿadn*, *khālidīn*, *abadā*.**

**12. رَّضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ — *raḍiya'llāhu ʿanhum wa raḍū ʿanh*** · v.8
- **Root feeling:** the "**contentment / pleasure**" family — *riḍā* = satisfaction; ⭐ **you own it from Ghāshiyah 88:9** (*li-saʿyihā **rāḍiyah***), **Fajr 89:28** (***rāḍiyatan marḍiyyah***), **Layl 92:21** (*la-sawfa **yarḍā***), **Ḍuḥā 93:5** (*fa-**tarḍā***), **Qāriʿah 101:7** (*ʿīshatin **rāḍiyah***).
- **Here it means:** "**Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **the same mutual-satisfaction idea as Fajr 89:28 — but expressed with **two full verbs** instead of two participles.**
  
  ⭐ **Fajr 89:28 — ***rāḍiyatan marḍiyyah*** — ⭐ **active participle + passive participle** (she is pleased / she is pleased-with).
  ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:8 — ***raḍiya'llāhu ʿanhum wa raḍū ʿanh*** — ⭐ **two complete verbs, both directions spelled out.**
  
  ⭐⭐ **Fajr compressed it into two words; Bayyinah expands it into a full sentence.** ⭐ **Same mutual *riḍā*, two grammatical strategies.**
  
  ⭐ **And notice the ORDER: ⭐ **HIS pleasure is stated FIRST.** ⭐ ***Raḍiya'llāhu ʿanhum*** comes before *wa raḍū ʿanh*.** ⭐ **His satisfaction precedes and causes theirs.**
  
  ⭐ **And *riḍā* is now across SIX surahs** — ⭐ **making it one of the most consistent reward-words in the juzʾ.** ⭐ **Track it: Ghāshiyah 88:9 (pleased with their **striving**), Fajr 89:28 (**mutual**), Layl 92:21 (he **will** be pleased), Ḍuḥā 93:5 (**you** will be pleased), Bayyinah 98:8 (**mutual, spelled out**), Qāriʿah 101:7 (a **pleasing life**).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***raḍiya ʿan*** = "to be pleased **with**" — ⚠️ **note the preposition *ʿan*, not *bi-*.** ⭐ **Learn the verb with its preposition.**
- **Why this word:** stating **both directions** explicitly ⭐ **rules out a one-sided reward.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **Fajr 89:28 compressed it: *rāḍiyatan marḍiyyah* (two participles). ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:8 spells it out: two full verbs.** ⭐ **Same mutual pleasure.** ⭐⭐ **And HIS pleasure is stated FIRST.** ⭐ ⚠️ **The verb takes *ʿan*, not *bi-*.**

**13. لِمَنْ خَشِيَ رَبَّهُ — *li-man khashiya rabbah*** · v.8
- **Root feeling:** ⭐ the "**awe-filled fear**" family — ***khashyah*** = **fear that comes from KNOWING** something's greatness (⚠️ **compare *khawf*, plain fear**).
- **Here it means:** "**That is for whoever fears his Lord in awe.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **the surah's final words — and they name the **one qualifying condition**.**
  
  ⭐ **After everything — the Evidence, the definition, the two verdicts, the eternal gardens — ⭐ **the whole thing is filed under a single filter: ***khashyah***.
  
  ⭐ **And *khashyah* is now across FOUR surahs, functioning as **the same filter every time**:**
  - **Nāziʿāt 79:19** — *wa ahdiyaka ilā rabbika fa-**takhshā*** (⭐ **Pharaoh could have had it**)
  - **Nāziʿāt 79:26** — *la-ʿibratan li-man **yakhshā*** (⭐ **the lesson is for whoever has it**)
  - **Nāziʿāt 79:45** — *innamā anta mundhiru man **yakhshāhā*** (⭐ **you warn whoever has it**)
  - **Aʿlā 87:10** — *sa-yadhdhakkaru man **yakhshā*** (⭐ **whoever has it WILL take heed**)
  - ⭐ **Bayyinah 98:8** — *li-man **khashiya** rabbah* (⭐ **the reward is for whoever has it**)
  
  ⭐⭐ **FIVE verses across THREE surahs, and the word always does the same job: ⭐ **it separates who the message lands on.**
  
  ⭐ **And remember the crucial nuance from Nāziʿāt 79:19: ⭐ **guidance comes FIRST, awe SECOND.** ⭐ ***Khashyah* is the result of knowledge**, not a substitute for it.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** here = "**whoever**" (relative), not "who?". ⭐ **Past tense *khashiya* after *man* = a general, timeless condition.**
- **Why this word:** *khashyah* over *khawf* — ⭐ **because this is **reverence from knowledge**, which is the only kind that produces the five items of v.5.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Khashyah* = fear that comes from KNOWING** (not *khawf*, plain fear). ⭐⭐ **FIVE verses, THREE surahs: 79:19, 79:26, 79:45, 87:10, 98:8.** ⭐ **Always the same filter.** ⭐ **And Nāziʿāt 79:19: **guidance FIRST, awe SECOND.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **الدِّينَ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **means RELIGION/DEVOTION here**, ⭐ **NOT "the Reckoning"** as in Infiṭār 82:9 and Tīn 95:7. ⭐ **One root (*obligation*), two senses. **Context decides.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **مُنفَكِّينَ (v.1)** — ⚠️ **from *fakka*, TO UNTIE** — ⭐ **the same root as *fakku raqabah* (Balad 90:13, freeing a slave).** **Two bondages, one verb.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **يُقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **NOT "they pray" but "they MAKE IT STAND."** ⭐ **And *q-w-m* appears three times in three verses (*qayyimah*, *yuqīmū*, *al-qayyimah*).**
4. ⭐⭐ **حُنَفَاءَ (v.5)** — ⚠️ **a DIPTOTE**: fatḥah, **no tanwīn**. ⭐ **And it's **Ibrāhīm's title**.**
5. ⭐⭐ **الْبَيِّنَةُ (v.1 vs v.4)** — ⚠️ **v.1: they were stuck UNTIL it came. v.4: they SPLIT only AFTER it came.** ⭐ **Read them together or you miss the irony.**
6. ⭐ **رَّضِيَ عَنْ (v.8)** — ⚠️ **takes *ʿan*, not *bi-*.** **Learn the preposition with the verb.**
7. ⭐ **يُقِيمُوا / يُؤْتُوا (v.5)** — ⚠️ **subjunctive after the *lām* of purpose: they DROP their final nūn.**
8. ⭐ **خَيْرُ / شَرُّ (v.6–7)** — ⚠️ **IRREGULAR** comparatives/superlatives. **No *afʿal* form.**
9. ⭐ **الْبَرِيَّةِ (v.6–7)** — ⚠️ **means ALL CREATION**, not just people. ⭐ **Root of *al-Bāriʾ*, a Divine Name.**
10. ⭐ **عَدْنٍ (v.8)** — ⚠️ **not a place-name: it means PERMANENT RESIDENCE.** ⭐ **Same root as *maʿdin*, A MINE.**
