# Surah At-Taghābun (64) — Never-Forget Vocabulary Guide

**Level:** intermediate, explained super simple · **Order:** mushaf (v.1–18) · **Complete coverage — every important word**

**Layers per word:** Root feeling · Here it means · Word magic (balāgha, simplified) · Grammar bite · Why this exact word · 🧠 Never forget

---

## ⭐ The One Thing That Makes This Surah Special

**Its name is a MARKETPLACE word — and it turns the Day of Judgment into the day the BOOKS are balanced.**

⭐ ***Taghābun*** comes from ***ghabn*** — **being CHEATED in a trade, selling too cheap or buying too dear.** ⭐ **Form VI (*tafāʿul*) = MUTUAL: the Day of MUTUAL loss and gain** — ⭐ **when it becomes clear who made the good trade and who got swindled.**

⭐⭐⭐ **And the vocabulary of COMMERCE runs through the whole surah:**
- ⭐ **v.9** — *yawm at-**taghābun*** — the Day of mutual LOSS and GAIN
- ⭐ **v.9** — *al-**fawz** al-ʿaẓīm* — the great WIN
- ⭐ **v.15** — *amwālukum wa awlādukum **fitnah*** — your wealth and children are a TEST (gold in the fire — checking purity)
- ⭐ **v.16** — *wa **anfiqū** khayran* — SPEND, it's better for you
- ⭐ **v.17** — *in tuqriḍu'llāha **qarḍan** ḥasanan **yuḍāʿifhu*** — LEND to Allah, He'll MULTIPLY it

⭐⭐ **And the Prophet ﷺ used this root: *"There are two blessings in which many people are CHEATED (maghbūn): health and free time"*** (Bukhārī). ⭐ **The Day of *taghābun* reveals what you did with them.**

⭐⭐ **It closes the trio with Al-Jumuʿah and Al-Munāfiqūn:** ⭐ **Jumuʿah opened *yusabbiḥu… al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*; Taghābun opens *yusabbiḥu* and CLOSES *al-ʿAzīzu'l-Ḥakīm*.** ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:8 named Him *ʿālim al-ghayb wa'sh-shahādah*; Taghābun's LAST verse names Him the same.**

🧠 **The hook:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TAGHĀBUN* ← *GHABN* = being CHEATED in a trade → the Day the books are balanced: who made the good deal?** ⭐ **"Two blessings many are CHEATED in: HEALTH and FREE TIME" (Bukhārī).** ⭐ **Wealth & children = a TEST; spending = a LOAN to Allah, MULTIPLIED.**

## The Big Picture

1. **Glorified, Owner, Praised, Powerful** (v.1) — everything glorifies Him.
2. **Created you — believer and disbeliever** (v.2–4) — formed you beautifully; knows what you hide and reveal.
3. **The earlier nations** (v.5–6) — "Shall mere humans guide us?" — Allah was free of need.
4. **"They claim no resurrection"** (v.7–8) — Yes, by my Lord! — so believe in the Light.
5. **The Day of Mutual Loss and Gain** (v.9–10) — the gathering; the great win; the Fire.
6. **Every calamity is by His permission** (v.11–13) — He guides the believer's heart; obey; rely on Him.
7. **Your spouses and children** (v.14–15) — some are enemies to you; pardon and forgive; wealth and children are a test.
8. **Fear Him as much as you can** (v.16–18) — spend; whoever is saved from his own greed succeeds; lend to Allah a good loan.

## §1 — Glorified, Owner, Praised, Powerful (v.1)

**1. يُسَبِّحُ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ — *yusabbiḥu li'llāhi mā fi's-samāwāti wa mā fi'l-arḍ*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *yusabbiḥu* — ⭐⭐ **GLORIFIES** (*sabaḥa* = to swim far; ⭐ ***tasbīḥ***; ⭐ ***masbaḥ* = SWIMMING POOL**; ⭐ ***misbaḥah* = PRAYER BEADS**). *mā* — ⭐ **WHATEVER** (living and non-living).
- **Here it means:** "**Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth glorifies Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **IDENTICAL OPENING to AL-JUMUʿAH 62:1** — ⭐ ***yusabbiḥu li'llāhi mā fi's-samāwāti wa mā fi'l-arḍ*** — ⭐ **PRESENT tense — glorification happening NOW, continuously.** ⭐⭐ **The *Musabbiḥāt* (glorifying surahs):** ⭐ ***SABBAḤA*** (past) — **Ḥadīd 57, Ḥashr 59, Ṣaff 61** ⭐ ***YUSABBIḤU*** (present) — **Jumuʿah 62, Taghābun 64** ⭐ ***SABBIḤ*** (command) — **Aʿlā 87:1** ⭐ ***SUBḤĀNA*** (timeless) — **Isrāʾ 17:1**. ⭐⭐⭐ **Jumuʿah and Taghābun — the two *yusabbiḥu* surahs — BRACKET Munāfiqūn.** ⭐ **The surah of HYPOCRISY sits between two surahs of TRUE glorification** — ⭐ **as if the whole universe's honest praise surrounds the hypocrites' false testimony.**
  ⭐⭐ ***MĀ* = WHATEVER** — ⭐ **stones, stars, trees** — ⭐ *wa in min shayʾin illā yusabbiḥu bi-ḥamdih* (17:44).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yusabbiḥu*** = Form II **present** (continuous). ⭐ ***mā*** — relative for all things.
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **the same opening as Jumuʿah — framing Munāfiqūn between two glorifications.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= JUMUʿAH 62:1 word-for-word — the two *YUSABBIḤU* surahs BRACKET Munāfiqūn: hypocrisy surrounded by the universe's honest praise.** ⭐⭐ **Tasbīḥ in every tense: *sabbaḥa* (57, 59, 61), *yusabbiḥu* (62, 64), *sabbiḥ* (87:1), *subḥāna* (17:1).** ⭐ ***Mā* = WHATEVER (17:44).**

**2. لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ ۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ — *lahu'l-mulku wa lahu'l-ḥamd · wa huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr*** · v.1
- **Root feelings:** *mulk* — ⭐ **DOMINION / sovereignty** (⭐ **Mulk 67:1** — *bi-yadihi'l-mulk*!; ⭐ ***mamlakah* = KINGDOM**). *ḥamd* — ⭐⭐ the "**PRAISE born of LOVE and GRATITUDE**" family — ***ḥamd* = PRAISE** (⭐ ***al-ḥamdu li'llāh***!; ⭐ ***Muḥammad*** = "the much-PRAISED"!; ⭐ ***Aḥmad*** = "the MOST praiseworthy"!; ⭐ ***Maḥmūd*** = "praised"!; ⭐ ***ḥamīd* = PRAISEWORTHY** — v.6!; ⭐ ***al-maqām al-maḥmūd*** — the Praised Station!). *qadīr* — ⭐ **ALL-POWERFUL** (*qadr*).
- **Here it means:** "**To Him belongs all dominion and to Him belongs all praise, and He has power over all things.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE ATTRIBUTES — and the FRONTING makes each EXCLUSIVE.** ⭐ ***LAHU'l-mulku*** — "**to HIM [alone] belongs DOMINION**" ⭐ ***wa LAHU'l-ḥamdu*** — "**and to HIM [alone] belongs PRAISE**" ⭐ ***wa huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin QADĪR*** — "**and HE has POWER over all.**" ⭐⭐⭐ ***Lahu* FRONTED twice = restriction: ALL dominion and ALL praise are HIS.** ⭐ **Other "kings" own a little, temporarily; other "praised" people are praised for gifts HE gave.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY *MULK* + *ḤAMD* together?** ⭐ Classical: **many who hold POWER are NOT PRAISED (tyrants), and many who are PRAISED have no POWER (the weak righteous).** ⭐ **Allah has BOTH — absolute power AND absolute worthiness of praise.** ⭐ **Power without tyranny; praise without weakness.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤAMD* — the root of MUḤAMMAD, AḤMAD, MAḤMŪD** — ⭐ ***Muḥammad* = "the one praised AGAIN AND AGAIN"** (Form II passive — intensive!). ⭐ ***Al-ḥamdu li'llāh*** — **you say it after every sneeze, every meal, every prayer.** ⭐ **And *al-maqām al-maḥmūd* (17:79) — the PRAISED STATION promised to the Prophet ﷺ.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Lahu'l-MULK* — MULK 67:1** — *tabāraka'lladhī bi-yadihi'l-**mulk*** — ⭐ **the same word.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***lahu*** fronted ×2 = **restriction**. ⭐ ***al-mulku*** / ***al-ḥamdu*** — **definite = ALL of it**.
- **Why this exact word:** *mulk + ḥamd* — ⭐ **power and praiseworthiness united, as no human ruler unites them.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***LAHU'L-MULKU WA LAHU'L-ḤAMDU* — *lahu* FRONTED ×2 = ALL dominion and ALL praise are HIS ALONE.** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY together? Tyrants have POWER without PRAISE; the weak righteous have PRAISE without POWER — He has BOTH.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ḤAMD* → *MUḤAMMAD* (praised again and again), *AḤMAD*, *MAḤMŪD*, *al-maqām al-maḥmūd* (17:79).** ⭐ **Mulk 67:1.**

## §2 — Created You — Believer and Disbeliever (v.2–4)

**3. هُوَ الَّذِي خَلَقَكُمْ فَمِنكُمْ كَافِرٌ وَمِنكُم مُّؤْمِنٌ — *huwa'lladhī khalaqakum fa-minkum kāfirun wa minkum muʾmin*** · v.2
- **Root feelings:** *khalaqakum* — ⭐ **CREATED you**. *kāfir* — ⭐⭐ the "**COVERING (a seed in soil)**" family — *kafara* = to cover; ⭐ ***kāfir* = a DISBELIEVER** (one who covers the truth) — ⭐ and classically ***kāfir* = a FARMER** (who covers seeds with soil! — ⭐ *ka-mathali ghaythin aʿjaba'l-**kuffāra** nabātuh*, 57:20 — "like rain whose plants please the FARMERS"!); ⭐ ***kaffārah* = EXPIATION** (covering a sin!); ⭐ ***kufr* = INGRATITUDE / disbelief**. *muʾmin* — ⭐ **a BELIEVER** (*īmān* ← *amn*, safety; ⭐ ***al-Muʾmin*** — a Name of Allah: the Giver of Safety!).
- **Here it means:** "**It is He who created you — then among you is a disbeliever and among you is a believer.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ONE CREATOR — TWO OUTCOMES.** ⭐ ***Khalaqakum*** — **He created ALL of you, the same way** — ⭐ ***FA-minkum kāfirun wa minkum muʾmin*** — **"THEN (*fa*) — among you a disbeliever, among you a believer."** ⭐⭐ **Same origin, same faculties (hearing, sight, hearts — Mulk 67:23) — and people CHOOSE different paths.** ⭐ **Insān 76:3** — *innā hadaynāhu's-sabīla **immā shākiran wa immā kafūrā*** — ⭐ **"We showed him the way — whether GRATEFUL or UNGRATEFUL."** ⭐ **Balad 90:10** — *wa hadaynāhu'n-najdayn* — ⭐ **"We showed him the TWO paths."** ⭐⭐⭐ **And this sets up *TAGHĀBUN*: two kinds of "traders" — one will win, one will lose.**
  ⭐⭐ ***KĀFIR* first, then *MUʾMIN*** — ⭐ classical: **because disbelievers were the majority, or because the surah's warning addresses them first.** ⭐⭐ ***Kāfir* = a FARMER** (57:20) — ⭐ **who COVERS seeds with soil** — ⭐ **the disbeliever covers the seed of truth (*fiṭrah*) planted in him.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Al-MUʾMIN*** — ⭐ **a Name of Allah (59:23) — the Giver of SAFETY** — ⭐ **the believer is "one who has found safety."**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa*** = "**then / and so**" (sequence + result). ⭐ ***minkum*** fronted ×2 (*ʿaṭf* — parallel).
- **Why this exact word:** **the split** — ⭐ **one creation, two paths — the setup for the Day of mutual loss and gain.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ONE CREATOR → TWO OUTCOMES: *fa-minkum KĀFIRUN wa minkum MUʾMIN*.** ⭐ **Same origin, same faculties — different CHOICES.** ⭐⭐ **Insān 76:3 (*shākiran / kafūrā*), Balad 90:10 (*an-najdayn*).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***KĀFIR* = also a FARMER who COVERS seeds (57:20) — covering the seed of truth.** ⭐ ***Al-Muʾmin* = a Name of Allah (Giver of Safety).** ⭐⭐ **Sets up *taghābun*: two traders.**

**4. وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ — *wa'llāhu bimā taʿmalūna baṣīr*** · v.2
- **Root feeling:** *baṣīr* — ⭐⭐ the "**CLEAR SIGHT / insight**" family — ***baṣīr* = ALL-SEEING** (⭐ ***al-Baṣīr*** — a Name of Allah); ⭐ ***baṣar* = SIGHT**; ⭐ ***baṣīrah* = INSIGHT** (⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:14** — *bali'l-insānu ʿalā nafsihi **baṣīrah***!); ⭐ ***tabṣirah* = a clear lesson**.
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah sees what you do.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***Baṣīr* — ALL-SEEING** — ⭐ **after "some disbelieve, some believe" — He SEES which you chose and what you do with it.** ⭐ **Not just knows — SEES, like an eyewitness.** ⭐ **And Mulk 67:19** — *innahu bi-kulli shayʾin **baṣīr*** (He SEES everything — even the birds' wings). ⭐ ⭐ ***Bimā taʿmalūna*** **FRONTED before *baṣīr*** — ⭐ **emphasis on YOUR DEEDS** (not just "He sees").
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bimā taʿmalūna*** fronted = emphasis. ⭐ ***baṣīrun*** = *faʿīl* intensive.
- **Why this exact word:** *baṣīr* — ⭐ **eyewitness knowledge of each person's chosen path.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***BAṢĪR* = ALL-SEEING — an EYEWITNESS to which path you chose.** ⭐ **Mulk 67:19 (sees the birds); Qiyāmah 75:14 *baṣīrah* (you're a witness against yourself).** ⭐ ***Bimā taʿmalūna* fronted — YOUR DEEDS in focus.**

**5. خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ بِالْحَقِّ — *khalaqa's-samāwāti wa'l-arḍa bi'l-ḥaqq*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** *ḥaqq* — ⭐⭐ the "**TRUTH / what is DUE / purpose**" family — ***ḥaqq* = TRUTH, a RIGHT, PURPOSE** (⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:1** — *al-**Ḥāqqah***!; ⭐ **69:51** — *ḥaqq al-yaqīn*; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:24** — *ḥaqqun maʿlūm*; ⭐ ***ḥuqūq* = RIGHTS**).
- **Here it means:** "**He created the heavens and the earth in truth [with purpose]…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BI'L-ḤAQQ* — "with TRUTH / with PURPOSE"** — ⭐ **not as a game, not randomly, not in vain** (⭐ *mā khalaqnā's-samāʾa wa'l-arḍa wa mā baynahumā bāṭilā* — 38:27 — "**We did not create them in VAIN**"). ⭐ **Since creation has PURPOSE, there must be a RECKONING** — ⭐ **a "Day of balancing the books" (*taghābun*, v.9).** ⭐⭐ ***Ḥaqq* — the AL-ḤĀQQAH root** — ⭐ **the TRUE Day (69:1)** — ⭐ **creation made with *ḥaqq* leads to the Day that is *ḥaqq*.** ⭐ **And *ḥaqq* = a RIGHT that's DUE** — ⭐ **creation has a debt; it will be settled.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bi'l-ḥaqqi*** = "**with / for** truth" (*bāʾ al-mulābasah* — accompanied by purpose).
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥaqq* — ⭐ **purpose in creation implies a reckoning at the end.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BI'L-ḤAQQ* = with TRUTH / PURPOSE — not in vain (38:27).** ⭐ **Purpose → RECKONING → the Day of *taghābun*.** ⭐⭐ ***Ḥaqq* — AL-ḤĀQQAH (69:1): creation made with *ḥaqq* leads to the Day that is *ḥaqq*.** ⭐ ***Ḥuqūq* = RIGHTS — a debt to be settled.**

**6. وَصَوَّرَكُمْ فَأَحْسَنَ صُوَرَكُمْ — *wa ṣawwarakum fa-aḥsana ṣuwarakum*** · v.3
- **Root feelings:** *ṣawwara* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**FORM / SHAPE / IMAGE**" family — *ṣawwara* (Form II) = **to FORM, SHAPE, FASHION, DEPICT**; ⭐ ***ṣūrah* (pl. *ṣuwar*) = a FORM, shape — and a PICTURE / PHOTO** (⭐ everyday: ***ṣūrah* = a PHOTO**!); ⭐ ***taṣwīr* = PHOTOGRAPHY / filming / depiction** (⭐ *taṣwīr bi'l-ashiʿʿah* = **X-RAY imaging** — clinical!; ⭐ *at-taṣwīr bi'r-raʾīn al-maghnāṭīsī* = **MRI**!); ⭐ ***muṣawwir* = a PHOTOGRAPHER** — and ⭐⭐ ***AL-MUṢAWWIR*** = **a Name of Allah — the FASHIONER** (59:24)!; ⭐ ***taṣawwur* = a CONCEPT / imagination** (an image in the mind!). *aḥsana* — ⭐ **made BEAUTIFUL / excellent** (*ḥusn*; ⭐ **Mulk 67:2** — *aḥsanu ʿamalā*; ⭐ **Tīn 95:4** — *fī aḥsani taqwīm*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and He formed you and made your forms beautiful…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE DIVINE PHOTOGRAPHER — and a THREE-SURAH thread.** ⭐ ***Ṣawwarakum fa-aḥsana ṣuwarakum*** — ⭐ **"He FASHIONED you and made your FORMS BEAUTIFUL."** ⭐⭐⭐ **INFIṬĀR 82:8** — *fī **ayyi ṣūratin** mā shāʾa rakkabak* — ⭐ **"in whatever FORM He willed, He assembled you."** ⭐ **TĪN 95:4** — *laqad khalaqna'l-insāna **fī aḥsani taqwīm*** — ⭐ **"We created man in the BEST STATURE."** ⭐ **TAGHĀBUN 64:3** — *wa ṣawwarakum **fa-aḥsana ṣuwarakum*** — ⭐ **"and made your FORMS BEAUTIFUL."** ⭐⭐ **Three surahs on the beauty of the human form — Infiṭār (shaped as He willed), Tīn (best stature), Taghābun (beautiful forms).** ⭐ **And Tīn's warning: *thumma radadnāhu asfala sāfilīn* — the best form can sink lowest, if the soul fails.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṢŪRAH* = a PHOTO; *TAṢWĪR* = PHOTOGRAPHY — and *AL-MUṢAWWIR* = the FASHIONER (a Name of Allah)!** ⭐ **Clinical: *taṣwīr bi'l-ashiʿʿah* = X-RAY imaging; *taṣwīr ar-raʾīn al-maghnāṭīsī* = MRI** — ⭐ **every scan you read images what *al-Muṣawwir* formed.** ⭐ ***Taṣawwur* = a CONCEPT** — **an image in the mind.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Aḥsana* — the *ḥusn* root** — ⭐ **Mulk 67:2** — *ayyukum **aḥsanu** ʿamalā* — ⭐ **He made your FORM *aḥsan*; now make your DEEDS *aḥsan*.** ⭐ **A beautiful form is a gift; a beautiful deed is the test.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ṣawwarakum*** = **Form II** (intensive — fashioned carefully). ⭐ ***fa-aḥsana*** = "**and [so] made excellent**." ⭐ ***ṣuwarakum*** — broken plural (cognate with *ṣawwara*). ⚠️ ⭐ **Recitation variant: *ṣiwarakum*.**
- **Why this exact word:** *ṣawwara* — ⭐ **the root of form and image — the Fashioner's craftsmanship, and the Name *al-Muṣawwir*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṢAWWARAKUM FA-AḤSANA ṢUWARAKUM* = He FASHIONED you and made your FORMS BEAUTIFUL.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE SURAHS: Infiṭār 82:8 (*fī ayyi ṣūratin*), Tīn 95:4 (*aḥsani taqwīm*), Taghābun 64:3 (*fa-aḥsana ṣuwarakum*).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṢŪRAH* = PHOTO; *TAṢWĪR* = PHOTOGRAPHY — X-RAY (*bi'l-ashiʿʿah*), MRI (*ar-raʾīn al-maghnāṭīsī*); *AL-MUṢAWWIR* = the FASHIONER (59:24).** ⭐⭐ **Mulk 67:2: He made your FORM *aḥsan* — make your DEEDS *aḥsan*.** ⭐ ***Taṣawwur* = CONCEPT.**

**7. وَإِلَيْهِ الْمَصِيرُ — *wa ilayhi'l-maṣīr*** · v.3
- **Root feeling:** *maṣīr* — ⭐⭐ the "**BECOMING / end-point**" family — *ṣāra* = **to BECOME**; ⭐ ***maṣīr* = DESTINATION / fate** (⭐ ***taqrīr al-maṣīr* = SELF-DETERMINATION**!; ⭐ **Mulk 67:6** / **Taḥrīm 66:9** — *biʾsa'l-maṣīr*!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and to Him is the final return.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***Wa ILAYHI'l-maṣīr*** — ⭐ **"to HIM [alone] is the DESTINATION"** — ⭐ ***ilayhi* FRONTED = restriction** — ⭐ **no other destination exists.** ⭐ ***Maṣīr* ← *ṣāra* (to BECOME)** — ⭐ **you're not just travelling TO Him; you're BECOMING what you'll be when you arrive.** ⭐⭐ **Created (v.2) → formed beautifully (v.3a) → returning to Him (v.3b)** — ⭐ **the whole life-arc in one verse.** ⭐ **And v.10** — *wa biʾsa'l-maṣīr* — ⭐ **for some, the destination is wretched.** ⭐ **Same word: "to Him is the destination" (all) / "wretched is the destination" (some).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***ilayhi*** fronted = restriction.
- **Why this exact word:** *maṣīr* — ⭐ **the destination as a becoming — closing the creation verse.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***WA ILAYHI'L-MAṢĪR* = to HIM [alone] is the DESTINATION (*ilayhi* fronted).** ⭐ ***Maṣīr* ← *ṣāra* = to BECOME — you're becoming what you'll be.** ⭐⭐ **v.3 (to Him — all) / v.10 *biʾsa'l-maṣīr* (wretched — some).** ⭐ **Created → formed → returning: a life in one verse.** ⭐ ***Taqrīr al-maṣīr* = SELF-DETERMINATION.**

**8. يَعْلَمُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَيَعْلَمُ مَا تُسِرُّونَ وَمَا تُعْلِنُونَ — *yaʿlamu mā fi's-samāwāti wa'l-arḍi wa yaʿlamu mā tusirrūna wa mā tuʿlinūn*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *tusirrūna* — ⭐ **you CONCEAL / keep SECRET** (*sirr*; ⭐ ***sirrī* = CONFIDENTIAL**; ⭐ **Ṭāriq 86:9** *as-sarāʾir*). *tuʿlinūn* — ⭐ **you DECLARE / make PUBLIC** (*iʿlān* = an ANNOUNCEMENT / ADVERTISEMENT!).
- **Here it means:** "**He knows what is in the heavens and the earth, and He knows what you conceal and what you declare…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ZOOM-IN: from the cosmos to your secrets.** ⭐ ***Yaʿlamu mā fi's-samāwāti wa'l-arḍ*** — ⭐ **He knows the WHOLE UNIVERSE** — ⭐ ***wa yaʿlamu mā TUSIRRŪNA wa mā TUʿLINŪN*** — ⭐ **AND what YOU hide and show.** ⭐ ***Yaʿlamu* REPEATED** — ⭐ **as if: "don't think the universe is too big for Him to notice YOU."**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *SIRR / ʿALAN* PAIR — across FOUR surahs:** ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:9** — *innī **aʿlantu** lahum wa **asrartu** lahum isrārā* — ⭐ **Nūḥ preached PUBLICLY and PRIVATELY.** ⭐ **Mulk 67:13** — *wa **asirrū** qawlakum awi'**jharū** bih* — ⭐ **SECRET or LOUD — He knows.** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:3** — *wa idh **asarra'n-nabiyyu** ilā baʿḍi azwājihi ḥadīthā* — ⭐ **a SECRET confided — Allah revealed it.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:4** — ***mā tusirrūna wa mā tuʿlinūn*** — ⭐ **what you HIDE and DECLARE.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And MUNĀFIQŪN (just before!) — the hypocrites' whole life was a gap between *sirr* and *ʿalan*** — ⭐ **Taghābun answers: He knows both.**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***iʿlān* = an ADVERTISEMENT**; ⭐ ***sirrī* = CONFIDENTIAL.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yaʿlamu*** ×2 — **repetition for emphasis**. ⭐ ***tusirrūna / tuʿlinūna*** — **Form IV** pair (*ṭibāq*).
- **Why this exact word:** *sirr / ʿalan* — ⭐ **the hypocrite's gap, closed by His knowledge — right after Munāfiqūn.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ZOOM-IN: the whole UNIVERSE → what YOU HIDE (*tusirrūn*) and DECLARE (*tuʿlinūn*).** ⭐ ***Yaʿlamu* ×2 — the universe isn't too big for Him to notice YOU.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SIRR / ʿALAN* ×4 surahs: Nūḥ 71:9 (preached both), Mulk 67:13 (secret or loud), Taḥrīm 66:3 (a secret revealed), Taghābun 64:4 (He knows both).** ⭐⭐ **Right after MUNĀFIQŪN — the hypocrite's gap, closed.** ⭐ ***Iʿlān* = ADVERTISEMENT; *sirrī* = CONFIDENTIAL.**

**9. وَاللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ بِذَاتِ الصُّدُورِ — *wa'llāhu ʿalīmun bi-dhāti'ṣ-ṣudūr*** · v.4
- **Root feelings:** *dhāt* — ⭐ **the POSSESSOR / CONTENT of** (fem. of *dhū*; ⭐ ***dhāt* = SELF / ESSENCE** — *bi'dh-dhāt* = **specifically / in person**!; ⭐ ***dhātī* = PERSONAL / self-**: *sīrah dhātiyyah* = **CV / autobiography**!). *ṣudūr* — ⭐ **CHESTS** (plural of *ṣadr*; ⭐ ***ṣadr* = CHEST / front / beginning** — ⭐ *ṣadr al-majlis* = the **head of the gathering**; ⭐ **Sharḥ 94:1** — *a-lam nashraḥ laka **ṣadrak***!).
- **Here it means:** "**…and Allah knows what is within the chests.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **DEEPER THAN SECRETS — the CONTENTS of the CHEST.** ⭐ **v.4a: what you SAY secretly or openly.** ⭐ **v.4b: *DHĀT AṢ-ṢUDŪR* — what's IN the chests — thoughts, intentions, feelings you never even spoke.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Three layers: what you DECLARE → what you WHISPER → what you never voice at all. He knows all three.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***DHĀT AṢ-ṢUDŪR* — across surahs:** ⭐ **Mulk 67:13** — *innahu ʿalīmun **bi-dhāti'ṣ-ṣudūr*** — ⭐ **identical phrase.** ⭐ **ʿĀdiyāt 100:10** — *wa ḥuṣṣila mā fi'**ṣ-ṣudūr*** — ⭐ **the chests THRESHED open on the Day.** ⭐ **Nās 114:5** — *yuwaswisu fī **ṣudūri**'n-nās* — ⭐ **the whisperer whispers INTO chests.** ⭐ **Sharḥ 94:1** — *a-lam nashraḥ laka **ṣadrak*** — ⭐ **the EXPANDED chest.** ⭐⭐ **The chest: where Shayṭān whispers (114), what will be threshed (100), what He expands (94), what He fully knows (67, 64).**
  ⭐ Hooks: ⭐ ***sīrah dhātiyyah* = a CV / autobiography**; ⭐ ***bi'dh-dhāt* = SPECIFICALLY.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***dhāti'ṣ-ṣudūri*** — ***dhāt*** = feminine of *dhū* ("**the [thoughts] possessing / residing in** the chests").
- **Why this exact word:** *dhāt aṣ-ṣudūr* — ⭐ **knowledge reaching below speech, to unvoiced intent.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE LAYERS: what you DECLARE → WHISPER → never VOICE (*dhāt aṣ-ṣudūr*) — He knows all three.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***Ṣadr* thread: Shayṭān WHISPERS into it (Nās 114:5), THRESHED on the Day (ʿĀdiyāt 100:10), EXPANDED (Sharḥ 94:1), FULLY KNOWN (Mulk 67:13 — identical; Taghābun 64:4).** ⭐ ***Sīrah dhātiyyah* = CV; *ṣadr al-majlis* = head of the gathering.**

## §3 — The Earlier Nations (v.5–6)

**10. أَلَمْ يَأْتِكُمْ نَبَأُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِن قَبْلُ — *a-lam yaʾtikum nabaʾu'lladhīna kafarū min qabl*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *nabaʾ* — ⭐⭐⭐ **momentous NEWS** (⭐ **SŪRAT AN-NABAʾ 78:2** — *ʿani'n-nabaʾi'l-ʿaẓīm*!; ⭐ ***nabiyy* = PROPHET**; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:3** *nabbaʾat… nabbaʾaniya*; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:8** *fa-yunabbiʾukum*; ⭐ ***tanabbuʾāt jawwiyyah* = WEATHER FORECAST**). *min qablu* — ⭐ **BEFORE** (built on ḍammah).
- **Here it means:** "**Has there not come to you the news of those who disbelieved before?**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***A-LAM YAʾTIKUM NABAʾU…*** — ⭐ **"Hasn't the NEWS reached you?"** — ⭐ **the history of ʿĀd, Thamūd, Pharaoh, Nūḥ's people** — ⭐ **you've studied them all (Ḥāqqah 69, Fajr 89, Nūḥ 71, Shams 91, Burūj 85).** ⭐ ***Nabaʾ* = MOMENTOUS news** — ⭐ **not gossip (*khabar*) — news that should CHANGE you.** ⭐⭐ ***A-lam*** — **the "haven't you…?" formula** — ⭐ **Fajr 89:6** — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka bi-ʿĀd* ⭐ **Fīl 105:1** — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka bi-aṣḥābi'l-fīl* ⭐ **Mulk 67:8** — *a-lam yaʾtikum nadhīr* (Hell's keepers' question!). ⭐⭐⭐ **Mulk 67:8: "didn't a WARNER come to you?" (asked in Hell — too late). Taghābun 64:5: "didn't the NEWS reach you?" (asked NOW — still time).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***a-lam*** + jussive (*yaʾti* — *yāʾ* dropped). ⭐ ***min qablu*** — built on ḍammah.
- **Why this exact word:** *nabaʾ* — ⭐ **history delivered as momentous, life-changing news.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***A-LAM YAʾTIKUM NABAʾU…* = "Hasn't the NEWS reached you?" — ʿĀd, Thamūd, Pharaoh, Nūḥ's people (all studied!).** ⭐⭐ ***Nabaʾ* = MOMENTOUS news (Nabaʾ 78:2) — not gossip.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Mulk 67:8: "didn't a warner come?" (asked in HELL) vs Taghābun 64:5: "didn't the news reach you?" (asked NOW).** ⭐ ***A-lam* formula: Fajr 89:6, Fīl 105:1.**

**11. فَذَاقُوا وَبَالَ أَمْرِهِمْ وَلَهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ — *fa-dhāqū wabāla amrihim wa lahum ʿadhābun alīm*** · v.5
- **Root feelings:** *dhāqū* — ⭐ **they TASTED** (*dhawq*; ⭐ ***dhawq* = good TASTE**). *wabāl* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**HEAVY DOWNPOUR → heavy CONSEQUENCE**" family — ***wabl* = a heavy, drenching RAIN**; ⭐ ***wabīl* = HEAVY, crushing** (⭐ **Muzzammil 73:16** — *akhdhan **wabīlā***!); ⭐ ***wabāl* = the evil CONSEQUENCE**. *amr* — ⭐ **AFFAIR / matter**. *alīm* — ⭐ **PAINFUL** (*alam* = pain).
- **Here it means:** "**…they tasted the bad consequence of their affair, and they will have a painful punishment.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD with ṬALĀQ 65:9!** ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:9** — *fa-**dhāqat wabāla amrihā*** — ⭐ **the rebellious TOWN tasted the consequence of ITS affair.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:5** — *fa-**dhāqū wabāla amrihim*** — ⭐ **the earlier PEOPLES tasted the consequence of THEIR affair.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Same phrase, three surahs apart** — ⭐ **and both followed by a FURTHER punishment** (Ṭalāq 65:10 — *aʿadda'llāhu lahum ʿadhāban shadīdā*; Taghābun 64:5 — *wa lahum ʿadhābun alīm*). ⭐⭐ **TWO stages: the worldly taste (*dhāqū*) → the Hereafter's full meal (*ʿadhābun alīm*).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***WABĀL* — the DOWNPOUR** — ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:16** — Pharaoh seized ***akhdhan wabīlā*** (a seizure heavy as a DOWNPOUR). ⭐ **Their choices "rained back" on them.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa*** = consequence. ⭐ ***wa lahum*** = "**and [moreover] for them**" — the second stage.
- **Why this exact word:** *wabāl* — ⭐ **the downpour of consequences, echoing Ṭalāq exactly.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA-DHĀQŪ WABĀLA AMRIHIM* = WORD-FOR-WORD ṬALĀQ 65:9 (*fa-dhāqat wabāla amrihā*).** ⭐⭐ **TWO STAGES: worldly TASTE → Hereafter's PAINFUL punishment (both surahs).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WABĀL* ← *wabl* = DOWNPOUR — Muzzammil 73:16 *akhdhan wabīlā*.** ⭐ ***Dhawq* = good TASTE.**

**12. ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُ كَانَت تَّأْتِيهِمْ رُسُلُهُم بِالْبَيِّنَاتِ — *dhālika bi-annahu kānat taʾtīhim rusuluhum bi'l-bayyināt*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *rusul* — ⭐ **MESSENGERS** (plural of *rasūl*). *bayyināt* — ⭐⭐ the "**CLEARNESS / separating**" family — ***bayyinah* = a CLEAR PROOF** (⭐ **SŪRAT AL-BAYYINAH 98**!; ⭐ ***bayān* = a STATEMENT / clarity**; ⭐ ***bayn* = BETWEEN** — clarity separates!; ⭐ ***al-bayyinah ʿala'l-muddaʿī*** — "the BURDEN OF PROOF is on the claimant" — legal maxim!).
- **Here it means:** "**That is because their messengers used to come to them with clear proofs…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY were they punished? Not ignorance — they HAD clear proofs.** ⭐ ***Kānat taʾtīhim*** — ⭐ **"USED TO come to them" — past continuous: AGAIN AND AGAIN** — ⭐ **messenger after messenger, proof after proof.** ⭐⭐ ***Bi'l-BAYYINĀT*** — ⭐ **SŪRAT AL-BAYYINAH 98:1** — *ḥattā taʾtiyahumu'l-**bayyinah*** — ⭐ **"until the CLEAR PROOF comes to them."** ⭐ **Bayyinah: the proof came. Taghābun: they had proofs repeatedly — and refused.** ⭐ **Mulk 67:9** — *qālū balā qad jāʾanā nadhīr* — ⭐ **in Hell they ADMIT the warner came.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Al-bayyinah ʿala'l-muddaʿī*** — ⭐ **"PROOF is on the claimant"** — ⭐ **the messengers MET their burden of proof; the deniers rejected it anyway.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bi-annahu*** = "**because**" (*-hu* = *ḍamīr ash-shaʾn*, "the matter is that…"). ⭐ ***kānat taʾtīhim*** = past continuous (habitual).
- **Why this exact word:** *bayyināt* — ⭐ **clear proofs, repeatedly — removing every excuse.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY punished? Not ignorance — CLEAR PROOFS came AGAIN AND AGAIN (*kānat taʾtīhim*).** ⭐⭐ ***BAYYINĀT* — SŪRAT AL-BAYYINAH 98:1; Mulk 67:9 — they ADMIT it in Hell.** ⭐ ***Al-bayyinah ʿala'l-muddaʿī* = BURDEN OF PROOF on the claimant.** ⭐ ***Bayn* = BETWEEN — clarity SEPARATES.**

**13. فَقَالُوا أَبَشَرٌ يَهْدُونَنَا — *fa-qālū a-basharun yahdūnanā*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *bashar* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**SKIN / outer surface → HUMAN BEING**" family — ***basharah* = the outer SKIN** (⭐ clinical: ***basharah* = EPIDERMIS / skin surface**!); ⭐ ***bashar* = HUMANS** (the "skin-beings" — visible, unhaired skin!); ⭐⭐ ***bushrā / bishārah* = GOOD NEWS** (it shows on the SKIN of the face — glowing!); ⭐ ***mubāshir* = DIRECT** (skin-to-skin! — ⭐ *baththun mubāshir* = **LIVE BROADCAST**!); ⭐ ***tabshīr* = EVANGELISM / missionary work** (spreading "good news"); ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:25** — *in hādhā illā **qawlu'l-bashar***!; ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:29** — *lawwāḥatun li'l-**bashar*** (scorching the SKIN!). *yahdūnanā* — ⭐ **GUIDE us** (*hudā*).
- **Here it means:** "**…but they said: 'Shall mere humans guide us?'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE OLDEST OBJECTION: "a HUMAN as a messenger?!"** ⭐ ***A-BASHARUN yahdūnanā?*** — ⭐ **"Will mere HUMANS guide US?"** — ⭐ **they wanted an ANGEL, or a king, or no one** — ⭐ **every nation said it** (⭐ Nūḥ's people — *mā narāka illā basharan mithlanā*, 11:27; Quraysh — *mā li-hādha'r-rasūli yaʾkulu'ṭ-ṭaʿāma wa yamshī fi'l-aswāq*, 25:7 — "why does this messenger EAT food and WALK in markets?"). ⭐⭐ **The irony: a human messenger is a MERCY — someone who can be FOLLOWED, imitated, understood.** ⭐ **An angel couldn't show you how to pray, fast, raise children.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***BASHAR* — from *BASHARAH* (SKIN)** — ⭐ **humans are named for their visible SKIN** (unlike furred animals) — ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:29** — Saqar is ***lawwāḥatun li'l-BASHAR*** — ⭐ **"scorching the SKIN."** ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:25** — al-Walīd: *in hādhā illā **qawlu'l-bashar*** — ⭐ **"this is just the word of a HUMAN!"** ⭐⭐⭐ **Muddaththir: "just a human's WORD" (rejecting the message). Taghābun: "mere HUMANS guiding us?" (rejecting the messengers).** ⭐ **The same contempt for the human.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***BUSHRĀ* = GOOD NEWS — same root!** ⭐ **Classical: good news shows on the SKIN of the face — it glows** (⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:38–39** — *wujūhun yawmaʾidhin musfirah · ḍāḥikatun **mustabshirah*** — faces "rejoicing at GOOD NEWS"!). ⭐ **The human messengers brought *bushrā*; the deniers saw only *bashar*.**
  ⭐ Clinical hook: ⭐ ***basharah* = EPIDERMIS**; ⭐ ***mubāshir* = DIRECT (*baththun mubāshir* = LIVE BROADCAST).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***a-basharun*** — question-hamzah + **indefinite** (*bashar* = "**a mere human**" — contempt). ⚠️⭐ ***yahdūnanā*** — **PLURAL verb** with singular-form *bashar* (because *bashar* is a collective — "humans").
- **Why this exact word:** *bashar* — ⭐ **the skin-word for humanity, chosen by those who couldn't see past the surface.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***A-BASHARUN YAHDŪNANĀ?* = "Mere HUMANS guiding us?!" — the oldest objection (11:27, 25:7).** ⭐ **A human messenger = a MERCY you can FOLLOW.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BASHAR* ← *BASHARAH* (SKIN — EPIDERMIS): Muddaththir 74:25 (*qawl al-bashar*), 74:29 (scorching the SKIN).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BUSHRĀ* = GOOD NEWS — it GLOWS on the skin (ʿAbasa 80:39 *mustabshirah*).** ⭐ **They brought *bushrā*; deniers saw only *bashar*.** ⭐ ***Baththun mubāshir* = LIVE BROADCAST.** ⚠️ **Plural verb for collective *bashar*.**

**14. فَكَفَرُوا وَتَوَلَّوا ۚ وَّاسْتَغْنَى اللَّهُ ۚ وَاللَّهُ غَنِيٌّ حَمِيدٌ — *fa-kafarū wa tawallaw wa'staghna'llāh · wa'llāhu ghaniyyun ḥamīd*** · v.6
- **Root feelings:** *tawallaw* — ⭐ **TURNED AWAY** (*walla*; ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:1** *ʿabasa wa tawallā*; ⭐ **Layl 92:16**; ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:17**). *istaghnā* — ⭐⭐⭐ **Form X of *ghaniya*** = **to be FREE OF NEED, self-sufficient** (⭐ **YOUR LONGEST *GHINĀ* THREAD!** — ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:5** — *ammā mani'**staghnā***; ⭐ **Layl 92:8** — *wa'**staghnā***; ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:7** — *an raʾāhu'**staghnā***!). *ghaniyy* — ⭐ **RICH / free of all need** (⭐ ***al-Ghaniyy*** — a Name of Allah!). *ḥamīd* — ⭐ **PRAISEWORTHY** (⭐ v.1 *lahu'l-ḥamd*!).
- **Here it means:** "**So they disbelieved and turned away — and Allah dispensed [with them]. And Allah is Free of need, Praiseworthy.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE GREATEST REVERSAL OF THE *ISTAGHNĀ* THREAD — in your entire study.** ⭐ **For FOUR surahs, *istaghnā* has been the SIN of man:** ⭐ **ʿAlaq 96:6–7** — *inna'l-insāna la-yaṭghā · an raʾāhu'**staghnā*** — ⭐ **man transgresses when he SEES HIMSELF self-sufficient.** ⭐ **ʿAbasa 80:5** — *ammā mani'**staghnā*** — ⭐ **the rich chief who thought he needed nothing.** ⭐ **Layl 92:8** — *wa ammā man bakhila wa'**staghnā*** — ⭐ **the stingy one who thought he needed nothing.** ⭐⭐⭐ **NOW — TAGHĀBUN 64:6 — *wa'STAGHNA'LLĀH* — ALLAH is the one who is TRULY free of need.** ⭐ **Man's *istighnāʾ* is a DELUSION; Allah's is REALITY.** ⭐⭐ **They turned away thinking THEY didn't need Him — and HE turned out not to need THEM.** ⭐ **Their rejection cost Him nothing; it cost THEM everything.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHANIYYUN ḤAMĪD* — FREE OF NEED + PRAISEWORTHY** — ⭐ classical: **many rich people are NOT praiseworthy (stingy, arrogant); Allah is rich AND praiseworthy — He GIVES.** ⭐ **v.1** — *lahu'l-mulku wa lahu'l-**ḥamd*** — ⭐ **the *ḥamd* returns.** ⭐⭐ **And the *ghinā* thread's other half:** ⭐ ***mā aghnā*** — **Masad 111:2, Ḥāqqah 69:28, Layl 92:11, Taḥrīm 66:10** — wealth (and even prophets) DIDN'T AVAIL. ⭐ **Ikhlāṣ 112:2** — *Allāhu'ṣ-ṢAMAD* — the One everyone needs, who needs no one.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***wa'staghna'llāhu*** — **Form X past** — ⭐ classically: "**Allah showed Himself free of need [of their faith]**." ⭐ ***ghaniyyun ḥamīdun*** — two *faʿīl* attributes.
- **Why this exact word:** *istaghnā* — ⭐ **the human sin-word, finally given its true subject.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA'STAGHNA'LLĀH* — THE GREAT REVERSAL: for 4 surahs *istaghnā* was MAN's delusion (ʿAlaq 96:7, ʿAbasa 80:5, Layl 92:8) — now ALLAH is the TRULY self-sufficient one.** ⭐ **They thought they didn't need Him; He didn't need THEM.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***GHANIYYUN ḤAMĪD* = RICH + PRAISEWORTHY — rich people are often not praiseworthy; He is both (He GIVES).** ⭐⭐ ***Ghinā* thread: *istaghnā* (delusion) / *mā aghnā* (didn't avail — 111:2, 69:28, 66:10) / *aṣ-Ṣamad* (112:2).** ⭐ **v.1 *ḥamd* returns.**

## §4 — "They Claim No Resurrection" (v.7–8)

**15. زَعَمَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا أَن لَّن يُبْعَثُوا — *zaʿama'lladhīna kafarū an lan yubʿathū*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *zaʿama* — ⭐⭐⭐ **CLAIMED (falsely)** (⭐ ***al-mazʿūm* = "the SO-CALLED"**; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:6** *zaʿamtum*!; ⭐ **Qalam 68:40** *zaʿīm*!). *lan yubʿathū* — ⭐ **will NEVER be RESURRECTED** (*baʿth*; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:2** *baʿatha*!).
- **Here it means:** "**Those who disbelieve claim that they will never be resurrected.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ZAʿAMA* — the "CLAIM with doubt" verb — completing the trio.** ⭐ **A saying: "*zaʿamū* is the riding-camel of lies."** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *ZAʿAMA* THREAD across THREE consecutive surahs:** ⭐ **Qalam 68:40** — *salhum ayyuhum bi-dhālika **zaʿīm*** — ⭐ **who will GUARANTEE their claim to Paradise?** ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:6** — *in **zaʿamtum** annakum awliyāʾu li'llāh* — ⭐ **you CLAIM to be Allah's exclusive friends.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:7** — ***zaʿama**'lladhīna kafarū an lan yubʿathū* — ⭐ **they CLAIM no resurrection.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Three false claims: guaranteed Paradise, exclusive favour, no Hereafter.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Lan yubʿathū*** — ⭐ **"NEVER be raised"** — ⭐ ***lan* = emphatic** — ⭐ **they're SURE.** ⭐ **The *baʿth* root — Jumuʿah 62:2** — *baʿatha fi'l-ummiyyīna rasūlā* (He SENT a messenger) — ⭐ **He sends messengers (*baʿatha*) and will raise the dead (*baʿth*) — the same verb; they deny both.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***an lan yubʿathū*** — ***an*** = lightened ***anna*** (*an al-mukhaffafah*) + *lan* + subjunctive (passive).
- **Why this exact word:** *zaʿama* — ⭐ **the doubt-laden claim, completing a thread of three unfounded certainties.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ZAʿAMA* thread — THREE false claims: guaranteed PARADISE (Qalam 68:40), exclusive FAVOUR (Jumuʿah 62:6), NO RESURRECTION (Taghābun 64:7).** ⭐ **"*Zaʿamū* = the riding-camel of lies."** ⭐⭐ ***BAʿTH*: He SENDS messengers (62:2) and RAISES the dead (64:7) — same verb; they deny both.** ⚠️ ***An* = lightened *anna*.**

**16. قُلْ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّي لَتُبْعَثُنَّ ثُمَّ لَتُنَبَّؤُنَّ بِمَا عَمِلْتُمْ — *qul balā wa rabbī la-tubʿathunna thumma la-tunabbaʾunna bimā ʿamiltum*** · v.7
- **Root feelings:** *balā* — ⭐⭐⭐ **"YES, INDEED!"** — ⚠️ **the YES that CONTRADICTS a negative** (⭐ **Mulk 67:9** — *qālū **balā** qad jāʾanā nadhīr*!; ⭐ *a-lastu bi-rabbikum? qālū **balā*** — 7:172!). *wa rabbī* — ⭐ **"BY MY LORD!"** (an OATH). *la-tubʿathunna* — ⭐ **you WILL SURELY be raised** (*lām* + heavy *nūn*). *la-tunabbaʾunna* — ⭐ **you WILL SURELY be informed** (*nabaʾ*).
- **Here it means:** "**Say: 'Yes indeed — by my Lord — you will surely be resurrected, then you will surely be informed of what you did.'**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST EMPHATIC ANSWER POSSIBLE — FIVE layers of certainty:** ⭐ **(1) *BALĀ* — "YES!" (contradicting their "never")** ⭐ **(2) *WA RABBĪ* — an OATH "by my Lord"** ⭐ **(3) *LA-* — the *lām* of oath** ⭐ **(4) *-UNNA* — the HEAVY *nūn*** ⭐ **(5) repeated: *la-tubʿathunna… la-tunabbaʾunna*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **They said "*lan*" (NEVER) once; Allah answers with FIVE emphases.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***QUL BALĀ WA RABBĪ* — one of THREE places the Prophet ﷺ is commanded to SWEAR by his Lord about the Hereafter:** ⭐ **Yūnus 10:53** — *qul **ī wa rabbī** innahu la-ḥaqq* — ⭐ **"Say: YES, by my Lord, it is TRUE."** ⭐ **Sabaʾ 34:3** — *qul **balā wa rabbī** la-taʾtiyannakum* — ⭐ **"Say: YES, by my Lord, it WILL come to you."** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:7** — *qul **balā wa rabbī** la-tubʿathunna* — ⭐ **"Say: YES, by my Lord, you WILL be raised."** ⭐⭐ **The three Qurʾānic oaths of the Prophet ﷺ — all about the Hereafter.** (classically noted by Ibn Kathīr)
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***BALĀ* — the YES to a NEGATIVE** — ⭐ **7:172: *a-lastu bi-rabbikum? — BALĀ!*** (souls before creation) ⭐ **Mulk 67:9: *a-lam yaʾtikum nadhīr? — BALĀ!*** (the damned in Hell) ⭐ **Taghābun 64:7: "you'll NEVER be raised"? — *BALĀ!*** ⭐⭐ **Three *balā*s: the covenant, the confession, the correction.**
  ⭐⭐ ***LA-TUNABBAʾUNNA bimā ʿamiltum*** — ⭐ **"you'll be INFORMED of what you did"** — ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:8** — *fa-**yunabbiʾukum** bimā kuntum taʿmalūn* — ⭐ **identical idea.** ⭐ **The *nabaʾ* (news) of your own life.**
  ⭐ ⭐ **Compare Takāthur 102:6–7** — *la-tarawunna'l-jaḥīm · thumma la-tarawunnahā ʿayna'l-yaqīn* — ⭐ **double *la-…-unna*.** ⭐ **Taghābun: double *la-…-unna* too.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⚠️⭐⭐⭐ ***balā*** = YES to a negative. ⭐⭐ ***wa rabbī*** = *wāw al-qasam* (oath). ⭐⭐ ***la-tubʿathunna / la-tunabbaʾunna*** = **oath-answers with HEAVY *nūn*** (passive — *tubʿath-ūna* → *tubʿathunna*, the plural *nūn* dropped before the emphatic *nūn*).
- **Why this exact word:** **five emphases** — ⭐ **meeting their single "never" with overwhelming certainty.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **FIVE EMPHASES: *BALĀ* + *WA RABBĪ* (oath) + *LA-* + HEAVY *-UNNA* + repeated — vs their single *LAN*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Prophet's ﷺ 3 commanded OATHS on the Hereafter: Yūnus 10:53 (*ī wa rabbī*), Sabaʾ 34:3, Taghābun 64:7 (*balā wa rabbī*).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***BALĀ* ×3: the COVENANT (7:172), the CONFESSION (Mulk 67:9), the CORRECTION (64:7).** ⭐⭐ ***La-tunabbaʾunna* = Jumuʿah 62:8 *yunabbiʾukum*.** ⭐ **Takāthur 102:6–7: double *la-…-unna*.**

**17. وَذَٰلِكَ عَلَى اللَّهِ يَسِيرٌ — *wa dhālika ʿala'llāhi yasīr*** · v.7
- **Root feeling:** *yasīr* — ⭐⭐⭐ **EASY** (⭐ **the *YUSR* THREAD** — **Sharḥ 94:5–6, Aʿlā 87:8, Layl 92:7, Inshiqāq 84:8, Ṭalāq 65:4, 7**!; ⭐ ***yasīr* = EASY / slight**; ⭐ ***taysīr* = FACILITATION**).
- **Here it means:** "**And that is easy for Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿALA'LLĀHI YASĪR* — "EASY for Allah"** — ⭐ **the deniers think resurrection is IMPOSSIBLE (decayed bones! scattered dust!)** — ⭐ **for Allah, it's EASY.** ⭐ ***ʿAla'llāhi* FRONTED** — ⭐ **"for ALLAH [specifically] it's easy"** — **hard for you to imagine, easy for Him to do.** ⭐⭐ ***Wa huwa'lladhī yabdaʾu'l-khalqa thumma yuʿīduhu wa huwa ahwanu ʿalayh*** (30:27) — ⭐ **"He originates creation, then repeats it — and that is EASIER for Him."** ⭐ **The second time is easier than the first** (by human logic). ⭐⭐⭐ **The *yusr* thread: EASE for the believer (Sharḥ, Aʿlā, Layl, Ṭalāq) — and here, EASE for Allah to raise the dead.** ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:3–4** — *a-yaḥsabu'l-insānu allan najmaʿa ʿiẓāmah · balā qādirīna ʿalā an nusawwiya banānah* — ⭐ ***BALĀ* again — and even the FINGERTIPS restored.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿala'llāhi*** fronted = restriction/emphasis. ⭐ ***yasīrun*** = *faʿīl*.
- **Why this exact word:** *yasīr* — ⭐ **the "impossible" called "easy" — from the root of ease you've traced through the juzʾ.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿALA'LLĀHI YASĪR* = EASY for ALLAH — "impossible" to them, easy for Him.** ⭐⭐ **30:27: repeating creation is EASIER than originating it.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUSR* thread: ease for believers (94:5, 87:8, 92:7, 65:4, 65:7) — and ease for Allah to raise the dead (64:7).** ⭐ **Qiyāmah 75:4: *BALĀ* — even the FINGERTIPS.**

**18. فَآمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ وَالنُّورِ الَّذِي أَنزَلْنَا — *fa-āminū bi'llāhi wa rasūlihi wa'n-nūri'lladhī anzalnā*** · v.8
- **Root feelings:** *āminū* — ⭐ **BELIEVE** (*īmān* ← *amn*, safety). *an-nūr* — ⭐⭐⭐ **the LIGHT** (⭐ here = **the QURʾĀN**!; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:11** — *mina'ẓ-ẓulumāti ila'n-**nūr***; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — ***nūruhum** yasʿā*; ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:16** — *al-qamara fīhinna **nūrā***). *anzalnā* — ⭐ **We SENT DOWN** (Form IV — all at once; ⭐ **Qadr 97:1** *anzalnāhu*!).
- **Here it means:** "**So believe in Allah and His Messenger and the Light which We have sent down.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE QURʾĀN CALLED "THE LIGHT" (*AN-NŪR*).** ⭐ **Three objects of belief: ALLAH, His MESSENGER, and the LIGHT (the Qurʾān).** ⭐⭐ **Why "light"?** ⭐ **Because it does what light does: shows the road, reveals obstacles, lets you SEE where you're going** — ⭐ **in a surah about a "Day of Loss and Gain," the light shows you which trade to make.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *NŪR* THREAD — now complete across three consecutive surahs:** ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:11** — *li-yukhrija… mina'ẓ-ẓulumāti **ila'n-nūr*** — ⭐ **the messenger brings you OUT of darkness INTO the light.** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — ***nūruhum** yasʿā bayna aydīhim* — ⭐ **the believers CARRY their light on the Day.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:8** — *wa'**n-nūri'lladhī anzalnā*** — ⭐ **the light itself = the QURʾĀN, sent down.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The SOURCE (Taghābun — the Qurʾān), the JOURNEY (Ṭalāq — into the light), the DESTINATION (Taḥrīm — carrying it on the Day).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Anzalnā* — Form IV (sent down)** — ⭐ **Qadr 97:1** — *innā **anzalnāhu** fī laylati'l-qadr*.
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Fa-āminū*** — ⭐ **"SO believe"** — ⭐ **because the resurrection is certain (v.7) — SO believe now.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa*** = consequence ("**so**"). ⭐ ***wa'n-nūri*** — joined (genitive) to *Allāh* and *rasūlihi*. ⭐ ***anzalnā*** — shift to "**We**" (majestic plural — *iltifāt*).
- **Why this exact word:** *an-nūr* — ⭐ **the Qurʾān as the light that shows the winning trade.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA'N-NŪRI'LLADHĪ ANZALNĀ* = the QURʾĀN called "THE LIGHT."** ⭐ **Light shows the road — which TRADE to make.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***NŪR* across 3 surahs: the SOURCE (Taghābun 64:8 — the Qurʾān), the JOURNEY (Ṭalāq 65:11 — into the light), the DESTINATION (Taḥrīm 66:8 — carrying it on the Day).** ⭐⭐ ***Anzalnā* — Qadr 97:1.** ⭐ ***Fa-āminū* = SO believe (because v.7 is certain).**

**19. وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ خَبِيرٌ — *wa'llāhu bimā taʿmalūna khabīr*** · v.8
- **Root feeling:** *khabīr* — ⭐⭐ **ALL-AWARE (inside-knowledge)** (⭐ ***khibrah* = EXPERTISE**; ⭐ ***mukhtabar* = LABORATORY**; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:11** — *wa'llāhu **khabīrun** bimā taʿmalūn*!).
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah is fully aware of what you do.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SAME CLOSING as MUNĀFIQŪN 63:11** — *wa'llāhu khabīrun bimā taʿmalūn* — ⭐ **the previous surah's LAST words, echoed in this surah's 8th verse** (with the order flipped: *bimā taʿmalūna khabīr*). ⭐⭐ **And v.2** — *wa'llāhu bimā taʿmalūna **baṣīr*** (SEEING) ⭐ **→ v.8** — *wa'llāhu bimā taʿmalūna **khabīr*** (KNOWING the inside). ⭐⭐ **First He SEES your deeds (v.2); then He knows their INSIDE — your intentions (v.8).** ⭐ **Outside and inside, both covered.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Khabīr* thread:** ʿĀdiyāt 100:11, Mulk 67:14, Taḥrīm 66:3, Munāfiqūn 63:11, Taghābun 64:8.
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bimā taʿmalūna*** fronted = emphasis on your deeds.
- **Why this exact word:** *khabīr* — ⭐ **inside-knowledge — linking back to Munāfiqūn's final word.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **= MUNĀFIQŪN 63:11's last words — echoed (order flipped).** ⭐⭐ **v.2 *BAṢĪR* (SEES your deeds — outside) → v.8 *KHABĪR* (KNOWS their inside — intentions).** ⭐ ***Khabīr* ×5 surahs: 100:11, 67:14, 66:3, 63:11, 64:8.** ⭐ ***Mukhtabar* = LABORATORY.**

## §5 — The Day of Mutual Loss and Gain (v.9–10)

**20. يَوْمَ يَجْمَعُكُمْ لِيَوْمِ الْجَمْعِ — *yawma yajmaʿukum li-yawmi'l-jamʿ*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *yajmaʿukum* — ⭐⭐⭐ **He will GATHER you** (*jamaʿa*; ⭐ ***al-JUMUʿAH*** — **Sūrah 62!**; ⭐ ***jāmiʿ* = MOSQUE**; ⭐ ***jāmiʿah* = UNIVERSITY**; ⭐ ***jamāʿah* = CONGREGATION**; ⭐ ***mujtamaʿ* = SOCIETY**; ⭐ ***jamʿ* = PLURAL / ADDITION**). *yawm al-jamʿ* — ⭐⭐ **the DAY OF GATHERING** (a name for Judgment Day).
- **Here it means:** "**The Day He gathers you for the Day of Gathering…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TRIO'S BIGGEST BRIDGE — from FRIDAY to JUDGMENT DAY.** ⭐ **JUMUʿAH 62:9** — *idhā nūdiya li'ṣ-ṣalāti min **yawmi'l-JUMUʿAH*** — ⭐ **the weekly Day of GATHERING (for prayer).** ⭐ **TAGHĀBUN 64:9** — *yawma yajmaʿukum li-**yawmi'l-JAMʿ*** — ⭐ **the final Day of GATHERING (for judgment).** ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME ROOT — every Friday's *jumuʿah* is a rehearsal for *yawm al-jamʿ*.** ⭐ **And the ḥadīth: "*The Hour will come on a FRIDAY*" (Muslim).** ⭐⭐ **Recite Jumuʿah (62) in the 1st rakʿah, Munāfiqūn (63) in the 2nd** — ⭐ **and Taghābun (64) completes the thought: the GATHERING you rehearse weekly is coming.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAJMAʿUKUM… AL-JAMʿ* — a cognate doubling** — ⭐ **"He GATHERS you for the Day of GATHERING"** — ⭐ **the word repeated = the TOTALITY of it: every human, from Ādam to the last, in ONE place.** ⭐ **Compare Mursalāt 77:38** — *hādhā yawmu'l-faṣli **jamaʿnākum** wa'l-awwalīn* — ⭐ **"We've GATHERED you and the earlier ones."**
  ⭐⭐ **The *jamaʿa* thread — GATHERING for good or greed:** ⭐ **Humazah 104:2** — *jamaʿa mālan* (gathered MONEY) ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:18** — *jamaʿa fa-awʿā* (gathered and HOARDED) ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:9** — *yawm al-jumuʿah* (gathered for PRAYER) ⭐ **Taghābun 64:9** — *yawm al-jamʿ* (GATHERED for JUDGMENT). ⭐ **What you gathered (money vs prayer) decides how you'll stand on the day you're gathered.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yawma*** = adverb of time (linked to *la-tunabbaʾunna*, v.7, or "remember the day"). ⭐ ***li-yawmi'l-jamʿi*** — the ***li-*** = "**for**" (purpose / appointed time).
- **Why this exact word:** *jamʿ* — ⭐ **linking the weekly gathering of Al-Jumuʿah to the final gathering.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **JUMUʿAH 62:9 *YAWM AL-JUMUʿAH* (weekly GATHERING for prayer) → TAGHĀBUN 64:9 *YAWM AL-JAMʿ* (final GATHERING for judgment).** ⭐ **Every Friday = a rehearsal; the Hour comes on a Friday (Muslim).** ⭐⭐ ***Yajmaʿukum… al-jamʿ* = doubled: EVERYONE, Ādam to the last.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Gather MONEY (104:2, 70:18) or gather for PRAYER (62:9) → decides how you stand when GATHERED (64:9).** ⭐ **Mursalāt 77:38 *jamaʿnākum*.**

**21. ذَٰلِكَ يَوْمُ التَّغَابُنِ — *dhālika yawmu't-taghābun*** · v.9
- **Root feeling:** *taghābun* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**CHEATING in a TRADE / being duped / overlooking**" family — *ghabana* = **to CHEAT someone in a sale** (sell too dear / buy too cheap — so the other party LOSES); ⭐ ***ghabn* = being CHEATED, a bad deal, a LOSS in trade** (⭐ fiqh: ***khiyār al-ghabn*** = the **right to cancel a sale** if you were grossly overcharged!); ⭐ ***maghbūn* = CHEATED, DUPED** (⭐ ḥadīth: *niʿmatāni **maghbūnun** fīhimā kathīrun mina'n-nās: aṣ-ṣiḥḥatu wa'l-farāgh* — "**two blessings many people are CHEATED in: HEALTH and FREE TIME**" — Bukhārī!); ⭐ ***ghabiyy* = DIM-WITTED / foolish** (a different but related root *gh-b-y*: one who "misses" things!); ⭐ ***taghābun* (Form VI) = MUTUAL cheating / mutual LOSS and GAIN** — each party revealed as winner or loser; ⭐ ***ghabana* also = to OVERLOOK, to forget** (⭐ *ghabana'sh-shayʾ* = he overlooked it).
- **Here it means:** "**…that is the Day of Mutual Loss and Gain.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SURAH'S NAME — the MARKETPLACE of the Hereafter.** ⭐ ***Ghabn* = being SWINDLED in a trade** — ⭐ **you paid gold for brass.** ⭐ ***TAGHĀBUN* (Form VI — MUTUAL)** — ⭐ **the Day when ALL the trades are audited:** ⭐ **who traded their dunya for the Hereafter (WINNERS)** ⭐ **and who traded the Hereafter for dunya (SWINDLED — by Shayṭān, by their own desires).** ⭐⭐⭐ Classical meanings (all true): ⭐ **(1) the people of Paradise "GAIN" over the people of Hell — they inherit the places in Paradise the disbelievers WOULD have had** (ḥadīth — Bukhārī: every person has a place in both; the believers take the disbelievers' seats in Paradise — ⭐ *ulāʾika humu'l-wārithūn*, 23:10!); ⭐ **(2) everyone will feel "CHEATED" that Day — the disbeliever for disbelieving, the believer for not doing MORE good** (Ibn ʿAbbās and others); ⭐ **(3) the oppressed take good deeds from their oppressors** (the "bankrupt" — *al-muflis* — ḥadīth, Muslim!). ⭐⭐ **No one leaves that marketplace feeling they traded perfectly.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ḤADĪTH — *MAGHBŪN* — HEALTH and FREE TIME:** ⭐ ***niʿmatāni maghbūnun fīhimā kathīrun mina'n-nās: aṣ-ṣiḥḥah wa'l-farāgh*** — ⭐ **"two blessings in which many people are CHEATED: HEALTH and FREE TIME" (Bukhārī).** ⭐ **Same root — you "sell" your health and time too cheaply, for nothing.** ⭐ **Every hour of health spent on nothing is a *ghabn*.** ⭐⭐ **Health is the asset people only value after it's gone.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE COMMERCE THREAD — the Hereafter as a MARKET in your study:** ⭐ **ʿAṣr 103:2** — *la-fī **khusr*** (in LOSS — trade term). ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:9** — *humu'l-**khāsirūn*** (the LOSERS). ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:9** — *dharu'l-**bayʿ*** (leave the SALE) — ⭐ and 9:111: *Allah PURCHASED from the believers…* ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:8** — *ḥāsabnāhā **ḥisāban** shadīdā* (a severe AUDIT). ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20 / Taghābun 64:17** — ***qarḍ*** (a LOAN to Allah). ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:38** — ***rahīnah*** (COLLATERAL). ⭐⭐⭐ **Taghābun 64:9 — the AUDIT DAY of the whole market: *taghābun*.**
  ⭐ Fiqh hook: ⭐ ***khiyār al-ghabn* = the right to RESCIND a sale for gross overcharging.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***at-taghābuni*** = **Form VI verbal noun** (*tafāʿul* — **MUTUAL action**). ⭐ ***dhālika*** = "**THAT**" (far demonstrative — awe).
- **Why this exact word:** *taghābun* — ⭐ **the Hereafter named with a market term: the day every trade is shown as a win or a swindle.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TAGHĀBUN* ← *GHABN* = being CHEATED in a TRADE — Form VI = MUTUAL loss and gain — the AUDIT DAY of every trade.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Classical: (1) believers INHERIT the disbelievers' seats in Paradise (Bukhārī; 23:10); (2) EVERYONE feels cheated — even believers wish they'd done more; (3) the oppressed take the oppressor's deeds (*al-muflis*, Muslim).** ⭐⭐⭐ **"Two blessings many are CHEATED (*maghbūn*) in: HEALTH and FREE TIME" (Bukhārī).** ⭐⭐⭐ **COMMERCE thread: *khusr* (103:2), *khāsirūn* (63:9), *bayʿ* (62:9; 9:111), *ḥisāb* (65:8), *qarḍ* (73:20, 64:17), *rahīnah* (74:38) → *TAGHĀBUN* (64:9).** ⭐ ***Khiyār al-ghabn* = right to rescind (fiqh).**

**22. وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ وَيَعْمَلْ صَالِحًا يُكَفِّرْ عَنْهُ سَيِّئَاتِهِ — *wa man yuʾmin bi'llāhi wa yaʿmal ṣāliḥan yukaffir ʿanhu sayyiʾātih*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *yuʾmin* — ⭐ **BELIEVES**. *yaʿmal ṣāliḥan* — ⭐ **does RIGHTEOUS deeds** (*ṣalaḥa* = to be sound, fit; ⭐ ***iṣlāḥ* = REPAIR / reform**). *yukaffir* — ⭐⭐ **COVERS / EXPIATES** (*kaffara*; ⭐ ***kaffārah* = EXPIATION**; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:5** — *yukaffir ʿanhu sayyiʾātih*!; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — *yukaffira ʿankum sayyiʾātikum*!). *sayyiʾāt* — ⭐ **BAD DEEDS** (*sūʾ*).
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever believes in Allah and does righteousness — He will remove from him his misdeeds…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE WINNING TRADE — and the *yukaffir* THREAD completes across THREE consecutive surahs.** ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:5** — *wa man yattaqi'llāha **yukaffir ʿanhu sayyiʾātih*** — ⭐ **for TAQWĀ.** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:8** — *ʿasā rabbukum an **yukaffira ʿankum sayyiʾātikum*** — ⭐ **for TAWBAH.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:9** — *wa man yuʾmin bi'llāhi wa yaʿmal ṣāliḥan **yukaffir ʿanhu sayyiʾātih*** — ⭐ **for FAITH + GOOD DEEDS.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Three surahs, three routes — TAQWĀ, TAWBAH, ĪMĀN + ʿAMAL — the same result: sins COVERED.** ⭐ **Every door to forgiveness is open.**
  ⭐⭐ **On the Day of *taghābun*, the believer's DEBTS (*sayyiʾāt*) are WIPED — ⭐ that's the "gain."** ⭐ **And the disbeliever's — v.10 — stay on the books.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Yukaffir* — the *k-f-r* root** — ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:27 *kaffār* (covering truth) vs here, Allah COVERING sins** — ⭐ **v.2's *kāfir* (one who covers the seed of truth) vs v.9's *yukaffir* (Allah covers the believer's faults).** ⭐ **Same root in one surah: the disbeliever covers truth; Allah covers the believer's sins.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** + jussives (*yuʾmin, yaʿmal*) + jussive answers (*yukaffir, yudkhilhu*). ⭐ ***ṣāliḥan*** = object ("**a righteous [deed]**").
- **Why this exact word:** *yukaffir* — ⭐ **the third route to the same mercy, completing a thread across three surahs.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUKAFFIR ʿANHU SAYYIʾĀTIH* ×3 surahs: TAQWĀ (Ṭalāq 65:5), TAWBAH (Taḥrīm 66:8), ĪMĀN + ʿAMAL (Taghābun 64:9) — three routes, one result.** ⭐⭐ **On *taghābun* Day — the believer's DEBTS wiped = the GAIN.** ⭐⭐ ***K-f-r* in ONE surah: *kāfir* covers TRUTH (v.2) / Allah COVERS sins (v.9).** ⭐ ***Iṣlāḥ* = REPAIR.**

**23. وَيُدْخِلْهُ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا — *wa yudkhilhu jannātin tajrī min taḥtiha'l-anhāru khālidīna fīhā abadā*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *yudkhilhu* — ⭐ **ADMIT him** (Form IV of *dakhala*). *jannāt* — ⭐ **GARDENS** (*janna* = to cover). *tajrī* — ⭐ **FLOW** (*jarā*; ⭐ ***al-jāriyah* = the SHIP** — **Ḥāqqah 69:11**; ⭐ ***ṣadaqah jāriyah***; ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:12** — *ʿaynun jāriyah*). *anhār* — ⭐ **RIVERS** (*nahr*). *khālidīn* — ⭐ **ABIDING** (*khuld*; ⭐ **Humazah 104:3** — *akhladah*). *abadā* — ⭐ **FOREVER**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and admit him into gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein forever.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ **THE REWARD of the winning trade.** ⭐ ***Khālidīna fīhā ABADĀ*** — ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:11, Bayyinah 98:8** — ⭐ **identical phrase.** ⭐⭐ **Note the shift: *yudkhilHU* (singular — "him") → *khālidīNA* (plural — "they")** — ⭐ classically: **each individual enters; together they abide** — ⭐ **personal entry, shared eternity** (⭐ like Ṭalāq 65:11: *khālidīna… lahu rizqā* — the reverse!). ⭐⭐ ***Tajrī* — FLOWING** — ⭐ ***ṣadaqah JĀRIYAH*** — ⭐ **the "flowing charity" you set up in dunya flows as rivers in Paradise.** ⭐ **Humazah 104:3** — the hoarder **THOUGHT** his wealth made him ***akhlad*** (eternal); ⭐ **the believer IS *khālid*.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***yudkhilhu*** = jussive (joined to *yukaffir*). ⭐ ***khālidīna*** = *ḥāl* (plural — referring to *man* by meaning). ⭐ ***abadan*** = adverb.
- **Why this exact word:** *khālidīn abadā* — ⭐ **the eternal return on the winning trade.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ **REWARD of the winning trade: gardens, flowing rivers, *KHĀLIDĪNA FĪHĀ ABADĀ* (= Ṭalāq 65:11, Bayyinah 98:8).** ⭐⭐ ***YudkhilHU* (singular — each ENTERS) → *khālidīNA* (plural — together ABIDE).** ⭐⭐ ***Tajrī* ← *ṢADAQAH JĀRIYAH* — flowing charity → flowing rivers.** ⭐ **Humazah 104:3: the hoarder THOUGHT he'd be eternal.**

**24. ذَٰلِكَ الْفَوْزُ الْعَظِيمُ — *dhālika'l-fawzu'l-ʿaẓīm*** · v.9
- **Root feelings:** *fawz* — ⭐⭐ the "**WINNING / SUCCESS / escape to safety**" family — *fāza* = to WIN, to succeed, to escape danger; ⭐ ***fawz* = a WIN / VICTORY** (⭐ everyday: ***fawz* = a WIN** in sports — *fāza'l-fārīq* = **the team WON**!); ⭐ ***fāʾiz* = a WINNER**; ⭐ ***mafāzah* = a DESERT** (⭐ classically: "a place of WINNING" — named optimistically, since crossing it alive is a win!; ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:31** — *inna li'l-muttaqīna **mafāzā***!). *ʿaẓīm* — ⭐ **TREMENDOUS**.
- **Here it means:** "**That is the tremendous success.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-FAWZ AL-ʿAẒĪM* — THE GREAT WIN — the opposite of *taghābun*'s loss.** ⭐ **In the Day of mutual loss and gain — THIS is the WINNING side.** ⭐ ***Fawz* = a WIN** (everyday sports Arabic!). ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAFĀZAH* = a DESERT** — ⭐ **named "the place of WINNING" because crossing it alive was a victory** — ⭐ **NABAʾ 78:31** — *inna li'l-muttaqīna **mafāzā*** — ⭐ **"for the God-conscious is a place of WINNING."** ⭐ **Nabaʾ: the place of the win; Taghābun: the great win itself.**
  ⭐⭐ **BURŪJ 85:11** — *dhālika'l-**fawzu'l-kabīr*** — ⭐ **"that is the GREAT win"** (for the martyrs of the trench). ⭐ **Burūj: *al-fawz al-KABĪR*; Taghābun: *al-fawz al-ʿAẒĪM*.** ⭐ **Two adjectives, one victory.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Dhālika*** — **"THAT"** — ⭐ **the far demonstrative — the win is so great it's pointed at from afar.** ⭐ **v.9a: *dhālika yawmu't-taghābun* (THAT is the Day of loss/gain) → v.9b: *dhālika'l-fawzu'l-ʿaẓīm* (THAT is the great WIN).** ⭐ **Two *dhālika*s in one verse — the Day, and its best outcome.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***dhālika*** + ***al-fawzu'l-ʿaẓīmu*** (definite + adjective = THE great win — restriction).
- **Why this exact word:** *fawz* — ⭐ **a winner's word in a surah named for losing trades.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-FAWZ AL-ʿAẒĪM* = THE GREAT WIN — the winning side of *taghābun*.** ⭐ ***Fawz* = a WIN (sports!); *fāʾiz* = WINNER.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MAFĀZAH* = a DESERT ("place of winning" — crossing it alive) — Nabaʾ 78:31 *mafāzā*.** ⭐⭐ **Burūj 85:11 *al-fawz al-KABĪR* / Taghābun 64:9 *al-fawz al-ʿAẒĪM*.** ⭐ **Two *dhālika*s: the DAY (v.9a), the WIN (v.9b).**

**25. وَالَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا وَكَذَّبُوا بِآيَاتِنَا أُولَٰئِكَ أَصْحَابُ النَّارِ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا ۖ وَبِئْسَ الْمَصِيرُ — *wa'lladhīna kafarū wa kadhdhabū bi-āyātinā ulāʾika aṣḥābu'n-nāri khālidīna fīhā wa biʾsa'l-maṣīr*** · v.10
- **Root feelings:** *kadhdhabū* — ⭐ **DENIED** (Form II — called it lies). *aṣḥāb* — ⭐ **COMPANIONS / people of** (*ṣāḥib*). *biʾsa* — ⭐ **how WRETCHED**. *maṣīr* — ⭐ **DESTINATION** (v.3!).
- **Here it means:** "**But those who disbelieved and denied Our signs — those are the companions of the Fire, abiding therein; and wretched is the destination.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE LOSING SIDE — the perfect MIRROR of v.9.** ⭐ **v.9: believe + do good → gardens → *khālidīna fīhā ABADĀ* → *al-fawz al-ʿaẓīm*.** ⭐ **v.10: disbelieve + deny → the Fire → *khālidīna fīhā* → *biʾsa'l-maṣīr*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **NOTE: v.9 says *khālidīna fīhā ABADĀ* (forever — with *abadā*); v.10 says *khālidīna fīhā* (without *abadā*).** ⭐ Classical reflection: **the Qurʾān's emphasis of eternity is stronger for MERCY** — ⭐ ***sabaqat raḥmatī ghaḍabī*** — "**My mercy outstrips My wrath**" (Bukhārī) — ⭐ **(though eternity in the Fire for disbelief is affirmed elsewhere, e.g. 4:169, 33:65).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***BIʾSA'L-MAṢĪR* — and v.3's *wa ilayhi'l-MAṢĪR*:** ⭐ **v.3: "to HIM is the DESTINATION" (for ALL).** ⭐ **v.10: "WRETCHED is the DESTINATION" (for the deniers).** ⭐ **Everyone returns to Him; not everyone arrives well.** ⭐⭐ ***Biʾsa'l-maṣīr* — Mulk 67:6, Taḥrīm 66:9** — ⭐ **identical phrase, three surahs.**
  ⭐ ⭐ ***Kafarū WA KADHDHABŪ*** — ⭐ **two sins: inward disbelief + outward denial** — ⭐ **vs v.9's two virtues: inward faith + outward good deeds.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ulāʾika*** + ***aṣḥābu'n-nāri*** = definite = restriction ("THEY are THE companions"). ⭐ ***khālidīna*** = *ḥāl*.
- **Why this exact word:** **the mirrored structure** — ⭐ **two sides of the ledger, laid out in parallel.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **MIRROR: v.9 BELIEVE + GOOD → gardens → *khālidīna fīhā ABADĀ* → GREAT WIN. v.10 DISBELIEVE + DENY → Fire → *khālidīna fīhā* → WRETCHED destination.** ⭐⭐ ***Abadā* in v.9 but not v.10 — "My mercy outstrips My wrath" (Bukhārī).** ⭐⭐⭐ **v.3 *wa ilayhi'l-MAṢĪR* (ALL return) / v.10 *biʾsa'l-MAṢĪR* (not all arrive well).** ⭐ ***Biʾsa'l-maṣīr* ×3: Mulk 67:6, Taḥrīm 66:9, Taghābun 64:10.**

## §6 — Every Calamity Is by His Permission (v.11–13)

**26. مَا أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ — *mā aṣāba min muṣībatin illā bi-idhni'llāh*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *aṣāba* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**HITTING THE TARGET**" family — *ṣāba* = **to HIT the mark** (an arrow, rain); ⭐ ***aṣāba* = to STRIKE / befall / hit** (⭐ ***iṣābah* = an INJURY** (clinical!) / **a GOAL** in football!; ⭐ *iṣābah* = **an infection case** — *ʿadad al-iṣābāt* = **number of CASES** in an epidemic!); ⭐⭐ ***muṣībah* = a CALAMITY** (what "hits" you!); ⭐⭐ ***ṣawāb* = CORRECTNESS / the RIGHT answer** (hitting the target of truth! — ⭐ *Allāhu aʿlam bi'ṣ-ṣawāb* = "Allah knows best what's CORRECT"!); ⭐ ***muṣīb* = CORRECT / right on target**; ⭐ ***ṣayyib* = RAIN that hits** (⭐ *allāhumma ṣayyiban nāfiʿā* — the rain duʿāʾ!). *idhn* — ⭐ **PERMISSION** (⭐ ***adhān***; ⭐ ***udhun* = EAR**; ⭐ **Qadr 97:4** — *bi-**idhni** rabbihim*!).
- **Here it means:** "**No calamity strikes except by the permission of Allah.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST COMFORTING VERSE IN THE SURAH — and the root is ARCHERY.** ⭐ ***Aṣāba… MUṢĪBAH*** — ⭐ **a calamity is something that "HITS the target"** — ⭐ **like an arrow finding its mark.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And the arrow doesn't fly at random — *ILLĀ BI-IDHNI'LLĀH* — only by His PERMISSION.** ⭐ **Every illness, loss, accident — aimed, permitted, MEANT.** ⭐ **Not chaos — decree.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***MUṢĪBAH* (CALAMITY) and *ṢAWĀB* (CORRECTNESS) — ONE ROOT!** ⭐ **Both = "HITTING the target."** ⭐ ***Allāhu aʿlam bi'ṣ-ṣawāb*** — "**Allah knows best what's RIGHT**" — ⭐ **the calamity that hits you is also, in His knowledge, *ṣawāb* — on target, correct.** ⭐⭐ ***Iṣābah* = an INJURY / a CASE (epidemiology) / a GOAL (football)** — ⭐ **one word for whatever "hits."**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **BAQARAH 2:155–156** — *wa basshiri'ṣ-ṣābirīn · alladhīna idhā **aṣābathum muṣībatun** qālū innā li'llāhi wa innā ilayhi rājiʿūn* — ⭐ **"give good news to the patient — who, when a CALAMITY STRIKES them, say: we belong to Allah and to Him we return."** ⭐ **The same pair (*aṣāba + muṣībah*).** ⭐ **Taghābun tells you WHY you can say it: it came by His permission.**
  ⭐ Clinical hook: ⭐ ***iṣābah* = an INJURY / an infection CASE**; ⭐ ***ʿadad al-iṣābāt* = number of CASES.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***mā… illā*** = restriction ("**nothing… except**"). ⭐⭐ ***min muṣībatin*** — ***min*** + indefinite = "**ANY calamity at all**" (total). ⭐ ***aṣāba*** — the object (*you*) is DELETED — ⭐ **so it covers EVERYONE and EVERYTHING.**
- **Why this exact word:** *muṣībah* — ⭐ **calamity as an arrow hitting its target — aimed, permitted, and (from the same root) correct.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MĀ AṢĀBA MIN MUṢĪBATIN ILLĀ BI-IDHNI'LLĀH* = NO calamity hits except by His PERMISSION.** ⭐ **Not chaos — DECREE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AṢĀBA* = an ARROW HITTING its target → *MUṢĪBAH* (calamity) & *ṢAWĀB* (CORRECTNESS) — ONE ROOT: *Allāhu aʿlam bi'ṣ-ṣawāb*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Baqarah 2:156: *idhā aṣābathum muṣībah — innā li'llāhi wa innā ilayhi rājiʿūn*.** ⭐⭐ ***IṢĀBAH* = INJURY / infection CASE / football GOAL.** ⭐ ***Ṣayyiban nāfiʿā* — the rain duʿāʾ.** ⭐ ***Min* = ANY at all.**

**27. وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ يَهْدِ قَلْبَهُ — *wa man yuʾmin bi'llāhi yahdi qalbah*** · v.11
- **Root feelings:** *yahdi* — ⭐ **GUIDES** (⚠️ jussive — the final *yāʾ* dropped: *yahdī* → *yahdi*). *qalb* — ⭐⭐ **HEART** (⭐ from ***qalaba* = to TURN OVER** — the organ that TURNS!; ⭐ ***yā muqalliba'l-qulūb***; ⭐ ***inqilāb* = a COUP**; ⭐ ***qalb* = also the CORE / centre** — *qalb al-madīnah* = **downtown**!).
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever believes in Allah — He will guide his heart.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BELIEVER'S RESPONSE TO CALAMITY: a GUIDED HEART.** ⭐ **When the arrow hits (v.11a), the believer doesn't fall apart** — ⭐ ***yahdi QALBAH*** — ⭐ **Allah GUIDES his HEART** — ⭐ **to calm, to acceptance, to *innā li'llāh*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **ʿAlqamah (a great Tābiʿī) explained this verse: "*It is the man struck by a calamity who KNOWS it is from Allah — so he accepts it and is content.*"** (Ibn Kathīr, al-Bukhārī cites it) ⭐ **The guidance isn't that the calamity disappears — it's that the HEART finds its way through it.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***QALB* ← *QALABA* (to TURN)** — ⭐ **a calamity tries to OVERTURN the heart (*qalb* → *inqilāb* — a COUP!)** — ⭐ **faith keeps it steady** — ⭐ ***yā muqalliba'l-qulūb, thabbit qalbī ʿalā dīnik*** — "**O Turner of hearts, keep my heart FIRM on Your religion.**" ⭐⭐ **And the contrast across this trio:** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:3** — *fa-**ṭubiʿa ʿalā qulūbihim*** — ⭐ **the hypocrites' hearts SEALED.** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:4** — *ṣaghat **qulūbukumā*** — ⭐ **hearts merely LEANING.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:11** — *yahdi **qalbah*** — ⭐ **the believer's heart GUIDED.** ⭐⭐⭐ **SEALED (63) → LEANING (66) → GUIDED (64): three states of the heart.**
  ⭐ Hook: ⭐ ***qalb al-madīnah* = DOWNTOWN**; ⭐ ***inqilāb* = a COUP.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***man*** + jussive (*yuʾmin*) + jussive answer ⚠️ ***yahdi*** (**final *yāʾ* dropped**). ⭐ ***qalbahu*** = object (singular — ⭐ **the individual heart**). ⚠️ ⭐ **Recitation variant: *yahdaʾ qalbuhu*** ("his heart becomes CALM") — ⭐ **both meanings true!**
- **Why this exact word:** *qalb* — ⭐ **the turning organ, steadied by faith at the moment calamity tries to overturn it.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YAHDI QALBAH* = He GUIDES his HEART — through calamity, not around it.** ⭐⭐⭐ **ʿAlqamah: "the man struck by calamity who KNOWS it's from Allah — accepts it, is content."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QALB* ← *QALABA* (TURN) — *inqilāb* = a COUP; "*yā muqalliba'l-qulūb*."** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE HEARTS: SEALED (Munāfiqūn 63:3) → LEANING (Taḥrīm 66:4) → GUIDED (Taghābun 64:11).** ⚠️ ***Yahdi* — jussive, yāʾ dropped; variant *yahdaʾ qalbuhu* (his heart becomes CALM).** ⭐ ***Qalb al-madīnah* = DOWNTOWN.**

**28. وَاللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ — *wa'llāhu bi-kulli shayʾin ʿalīm*** · v.11
- **Root feeling:** *ʿalīm* — ⭐ **ALL-KNOWING**.
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah knows all things.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐ ***Bi-kulli shayʾin ʿALĪM*** — ⭐ **He knows EVERY calamity's purpose, every heart's state** — ⭐ **the calamity isn't random; the knowledge behind it is complete.** ⭐ **And v.18** — *ʿālimu'l-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādah* — ⭐ **the surah ends on His knowledge.** ⭐ ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:12** — *wa anna'llāha qad aḥāṭa bi-kulli shayʾin ʿilmā* — ⭐ **His knowledge SURROUNDS everything (like an ocean — *muḥīṭ*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***bi-kulli shayʾin*** fronted = emphasis on "**EVERYTHING**."
- **Why this exact word:** ⭐ **complete knowledge behind every permitted calamity.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐ ***Bi-kulli shayʾin ʿALĪM* — He knows EVERY calamity's purpose and every heart's state.** ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:12 (*aḥāṭa… ʿilmā* — knowledge like an OCEAN).**

**29. وَأَطِيعُوا اللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُوا الرَّسُولَ ۚ فَإِن تَوَلَّيْتُمْ فَإِنَّمَا عَلَىٰ رَسُولِنَا الْبَلَاغُ الْمُبِينُ — *wa aṭīʿu'llāha wa aṭīʿu'r-rasūl · fa-in tawallaytum fa-innamā ʿalā rasūlina'l-balāghu'l-mubīn*** · v.12
- **Root feelings:** *aṭīʿū* — ⭐ **OBEY** (*ṭāʿah*; ⭐ ***istiṭāʿah* = ABILITY** — v.16!; ⭐ **Nūḥ 71:3** *wa aṭīʿūn*). *tawallaytum* — ⭐ **you TURN AWAY** (⭐ v.6 *tawallaw*!). *balāgh* — ⭐⭐ the "**REACHING / conveying**" family — *balagha* = to reach; ⭐ ***balāgh* = CONVEYING the message** (⭐ **Jinn 72:23** — *illā **balāghan** mina'llāh*!); ⭐ ***balāghah* = ELOQUENCE / rhetoric** (the "word magic" layer of every guide!); ⭐ ***bāligh* = an ADULT**; ⭐ ***mablagh* = a SUM**; ⭐ ***iblāgh* = a REPORT / notification** (⭐ *iblāgh* = **reporting a crime / incident**!). *mubīn* — ⭐ **CLEAR**.
- **Here it means:** "**And obey Allah and obey the Messenger; but if you turn away — then upon Our Messenger is only clear conveyance.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AṬĪʿŪ* REPEATED — "obey ALLAH and OBEY the Messenger"** — ⭐ **the verb repeated for BOTH** — ⭐ classically: **obedience to the Messenger ﷺ is an INDEPENDENT obligation** (his Sunnah is followed in its own right, not only where it matches the Qurʾān's text). ⭐⭐ ***Fa-in TAWALLAYTUM*** — ⭐ **"if you TURN AWAY"** — ⭐ **v.6: the earlier nations *tawallaw* (turned away) — here YOU are warned against the same.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***INNAMĀ ʿALĀ RASŪLINA'L-BALĀGHU'L-MUBĪN* — "his ONLY duty is CLEAR CONVEYING"** — ⭐ **he can't FORCE your heart — only DELIVER the message clearly.** ⭐ **JINN 72:23** — *illā **balāghan** mina'llāhi wa risālātih* — ⭐ **"[I own nothing] except CONVEYING from Allah."** ⭐ **Ghāshiyah 88:21–22** — *innamā anta mudhakkir · lasta ʿalayhim bi-muṣayṭir* — ⭐ **"you're only a REMINDER — not a CONTROLLER over them."** ⭐⭐ **Responsibility for the RESPONSE lies with YOU.** ⭐ **The messenger delivers; the listener chooses.**
  ⭐⭐ ***BALĀGH* → *BALĀGHAH* (ELOQUENCE)** — ⭐ **the "Word magic" layer of every guide you've read — named for "reaching" the listener.** ⭐ ***Iblāgh* = a REPORT (to police / authorities).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***aṭīʿū*** **repeated** (for Allah and for the Messenger — independent obligations). ⭐ ***innamā*** = restriction. ⭐ ***al-balāghu'l-mubīnu*** — definite + adjective.
- **Why this exact word:** *balāgh* — ⭐ **the messenger's limited, complete task: to make it reach you.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AṬĪʿŪ* REPEATED — obey ALLAH and OBEY the MESSENGER — independent obligations (the Sunnah in its own right).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INNAMĀ ʿALĀ RASŪLINA'L-BALĀGHU'L-MUBĪN* = his ONLY duty: CLEAR CONVEYING — Jinn 72:23 (*balāgh*), Ghāshiyah 88:21–22 (not a controller).** ⭐ **Messenger DELIVERS; listener CHOOSES.** ⭐⭐ ***BALĀGH* → *BALĀGHAH* (ELOQUENCE — the "Word magic" layer!); *iblāgh* = a REPORT.** ⭐ ***Tawallaw* (v.6) → *tawallaytum* (v.12).**

**30. اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۚ وَعَلَى اللَّهِ فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ الْمُؤْمِنُونَ — *Allāhu lā ilāha illā huw · wa ʿala'llāhi fal-yatawakkali'l-muʾminūn*** · v.13
- **Root feelings:** *lā ilāha illā huwa* — ⭐⭐⭐ **"there is NO GOD but HIM"** (the *tawḥīd* formula). *yatawakkal* — ⭐⭐ the "**ENTRUSTING / relying**" family — ***tawakkul* = RELIANCE / trust in Allah**; ⭐ ***wakīl* = an AGENT / trustee / attorney** (⭐ ***wakīl* = a LAWYER / an AGENT** — *wakālah* = an **AGENCY**!; ⭐ ***tawkīl* = POWER OF ATTORNEY**); ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:9** — *fa'ttakhidhhu **wakīlā***; ⭐ **Mulk 67:29** — *wa ʿalayhi **tawakkalnā***; ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:3** — *wa man **yatawakkal** ʿala'llāhi fa-huwa ḥasbuh*!.
- **Here it means:** "**Allah — there is no god but Him. And upon Allah let the believers rely.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TAWḤĪD → TAWAKKUL: the logic in two sentences.** ⭐ ***Allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa*** — ⭐ **"Allah — there is NO god but HIM"** — ⭐ **no other power, no other source.** ⭐ ***FA*-l-yatawakkal** — ⭐ **"SO let the believers RELY on Him"** — ⭐ **if there's only ONE God, there's only ONE to rely on.** ⭐⭐ **After "every calamity is by His permission" (v.11) — the natural response: RELY on the One who permits.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***WA ʿALA'LLĀHI fal-yatawakkal* — *ʿala'llāhi* FRONTED = "upon ALLAH [and no one else]"** — ⭐ **MULK 67:29** — *āmannā bihi **wa ʿalayhi tawakkalnā*** — ⭐ **the same fronting — upon HIM ALONE.** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE *TAWAKKUL* THREAD — now FOUR surahs:** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:9** — *fa'ttakhidhhu **wakīlā*** (TAKE Him as trustee — COMMAND to the Prophet ﷺ). ⭐ **Mulk 67:29** — *wa ʿalayhi **tawakkalnā*** (upon Him we RELY — DECLARATION). ⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:3** — *wa man **yatawakkal** ʿala'llāhi fa-huwa ḥasbuh* (whoever relies — He's ENOUGH — RESULT). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:13** — *wa ʿala'llāhi **fal-yatawakkali'l-muʾminūn*** (let ALL believers rely — COMMAND to the ummah). ⭐⭐ **Command to the Prophet → declaration → result → command to everyone.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Wakīl* = a LAWYER / AGENT; *tawkīl* = POWER OF ATTORNEY** — ⭐ **tawakkul = giving Allah "power of attorney" over your affairs** — ⭐ **you do your part; He handles the outcome.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***Allāhu*** = subject; ***lā ilāha illā huwa*** = predicate. ⭐⭐ ***ʿala'llāhi*** fronted = restriction. ⭐ ***fal-yatawakkal*** = ***lām al-amr*** + jussive ("**let them rely**") — ⚠️ kasrah on *-kali* for two sukūns.
- **Why this exact word:** *tawakkul* — ⭐ **the logical consequence of tawḥīd, after a verse about permitted calamity.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **TAWḤĪD → TAWAKKUL: ONE God → rely on ONE.** ⭐ **After "every calamity by His permission" (v.11) — rely on the One who permits.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TAWAKKUL* thread: COMMAND to the Prophet (Muzzammil 73:9) → DECLARATION (Mulk 67:29) → RESULT (Ṭalāq 65:3) → COMMAND to all (Taghābun 64:13).** ⭐⭐ ***ʿAla'llāhi* FRONTED = upon ALLAH alone (like Mulk 67:29).** ⭐⭐ ***WAKĪL* = LAWYER / AGENT; *tawkīl* = POWER OF ATTORNEY — give Him power of attorney.** ⚠️ ***Fal-yatawakkal* = *lām al-amr*.**

## §7 — Your Spouses and Children (v.14–15)

*⭐ Background (Tirmidhī, from Ibn ʿAbbās): some men in Makkah accepted Islam and wanted to emigrate to the Prophet ﷺ in Madīnah — but their wives and children held them back. When they finally came, they found others had gone ahead and learned the religion, and they wanted to punish their families. This verse was revealed — warning them AND telling them to forgive.*

**31. يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِنَّ مِنْ أَزْوَاجِكُمْ وَأَوْلَادِكُمْ عَدُوًّا لَّكُمْ — *yā ayyuha'lladhīna āmanū inna min azwājikum wa awlādikum ʿaduwwan lakum*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *azwāj* — ⭐ **SPOUSES** (*zawj* = one of a PAIR; ⭐ **Nabaʾ 78:8** — *wa khalaqnākum azwājā*; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:1, 5** — *azwājik*!). *awlād* — ⭐ **CHILDREN** (*walad*). *ʿaduww* — ⭐⭐⭐ **an ENEMY** (*ʿadā* = to run against; ⭐ ***ʿadwā* = CONTAGION** — clinical!; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4** — *humu'l-**ʿaduww***!).
- **Here it means:** "**O you who believe, among your spouses and your children are enemies to you…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST SURPRISING WARNING IN THE TRIO — the ENEMY inside your own HOME.** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4** — *humu'l-ʿaduwwu fa'ḥdharhum* — ⭐ **the hypocrites (outside your home, in the community) = THE enemy.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:14** — *inna min azwājikum wa awlādikum ʿaduwwan lakum fa'ḥdharūhum* — ⭐ **SOME of your own spouses and children = an enemy to you.** ⭐⭐⭐ **SAME TWO WORDS (*ʿaduww* + *iḥdhar*) — two consecutive surahs: the enemy in the COMMUNITY (63), the enemy in the HOME (64).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **BUT CAREFULLY: *MIN* azwājikum — "AMONG / SOME of"** — ⭐ **NOT "your spouses and children ARE enemies"** — ⭐ **SOME of them, SOMETIMES — when they pull you AWAY from Allah** (holding you back from emigration, from prayer, from charity, from what's right). ⭐ Classical: **"enemy" here = in EFFECT, not in intention — they may love you dearly, yet act as an enemy to your Hereafter.** ⭐ **The love that holds you back from Allah is functioning as enmity.**
  ⭐⭐ ***ʿAduwwan* — SINGULAR for a group** (⭐ like Munāfiqūn 63:4's *al-ʿaduww*) — ⭐ **any of them, in that role.** ⭐ ***ʿAdwā* = CONTAGION** — ⭐ **a family's habits spread.**
  ⭐⭐ **And Munāfiqūn 63:9** — *lā tulhikum amwālukum **wa lā awlādukum** ʿan dhikri'llāh* — ⭐ **children as DISTRACTION (63:9) → as possible ENEMY (64:14) → as TEST (64:15).** ⭐ **A three-step teaching about the dearest people.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***min*** = **partitive** ("**SOME of**") — crucial! ⭐ ***ʿaduwwan*** = noun of *inna* (fronted *min azwājikum* = predicate) — ⭐ **singular form, plural sense.**
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿaduww* + *min* — ⭐ **a sharp warning, carefully limited to "some" — love that blocks the path.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **ENEMY in the COMMUNITY (Munāfiqūn 63:4 — *humu'l-ʿaduww fa'ḥdharhum*) → ENEMY in the HOME (Taghābun 64:14 — *ʿaduwwan lakum fa'ḥdharūhum*).** ⭐ **Same two words.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***MIN* = SOME of — not ALL — and "enemy" in EFFECT (pulling you from Allah), not intention.** ⭐ **Background: families held men back from emigrating (Tirmidhī).** ⭐⭐⭐ **CHILDREN: DISTRACTION (63:9) → possible ENEMY (64:14) → TEST (64:15).** ⭐ ***ʿAdwā* = CONTAGION.**

**32. فَاحْذَرُوهُمْ — *fa'ḥdharūhum*** · v.14
- **Root feeling:** *iḥdharū* — ⭐⭐ **BEWARE / be cautious** (*ḥadhar*; ⭐ ***ḥadhārī!* = "CAREFUL!"**; ⭐ ***taḥdhīr* = a WARNING**; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4** — *fa'**ḥdharhum***!).
- **Here it means:** "**…so beware of them.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA'ḤDHARŪHUM* — "BEWARE of them" — not "fight" them, not "leave" them.** ⭐ ***Ḥadhar* = CAUTION** — ⭐ **be ALERT to when their pull goes against Allah** — ⭐ **don't obey them in what displeases Him.** ⭐ ***Lā ṭāʿata li-makhlūqin fī maʿṣiyati'l-khāliq*** — "**no obedience to a creature in disobedience to the Creator**" (Aḥmad). ⭐⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4** — *fa'ḥdharhum* (beware the HYPOCRITES) → **Taghābun 64:14** — *fa'ḥdharūhum* (beware within the FAMILY). ⭐ **Same verb, two circles.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***fa'ḥdharū*** = imperative plural + *-hum*. ⭐ ***fa*** = consequence.
- **Why this exact word:** *ḥadhar* — ⭐ **vigilance, not hostility — the right response to love that may misdirect.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA'ḤDHARŪHUM* = BEWARE — be ALERT, not hostile.** ⭐ **"No obedience to a creature in disobedience to the Creator" (Aḥmad).** ⭐⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:4 (beware HYPOCRITES) → Taghābun 64:14 (beware within FAMILY).** ⭐ ***Ḥadhārī!* = CAREFUL!**

**33. وَإِن تَعْفُوا وَتَصْفَحُوا وَتَغْفِرُوا فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ — *wa in taʿfū wa taṣfaḥū wa taghfirū fa-inna'llāha ghafūrun raḥīm*** · v.14
- **Root feelings:** *taʿfū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**ERASING TRACES / effacing**" family — *ʿafā* = **for the wind to ERASE footprints / for a trace to vanish**; ⭐ ***ʿafw* = PARDON** (erasing the offence completely, leaving no trace!); ⭐ ***ʿāfiyah* = WELL-BEING / health** (⭐ *Allāhumma innī asʾaluka'l-ʿāfiyah*!; ⭐ ***muʿāfā* = EXEMPT / healthy**); ⭐ ***al-ʿAfuww*** = a Name of Allah (⭐ *Allāhumma innaka **ʿafuwwun** tuḥibbu'l-ʿafwa fa'ʿfu ʿannī* — the Laylat al-Qadr duʿāʾ!); ⭐ ***ʿafwan* = "you're WELCOME / EXCUSE me / SORRY"** (everyday!); ⭐ ***ʿafw* = also SURPLUS** (⭐ *yasʾalūnaka mādhā yunfiqūn qul al-ʿafw*, 2:219 — "spend the SURPLUS"!). *taṣfaḥū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**TURNING THE PAGE / the broad side**" family — ***ṣafḥ* = the SIDE / broad surface** (of a face, a sword, a page); ⭐ ***ṣafaḥa* = to TURN ASIDE, overlook** (turn the side of your face away from the offence!); ⭐⭐ ***ṣafḥah* = a PAGE** (⭐ everyday: *ṣafḥah* = a **PAGE** — of a book, a **WEB PAGE**!); ⭐ ***muṣāfaḥah* = a HANDSHAKE** (palm against palm — broad sides!); ⭐ ***ṣafīḥah* = a METAL SHEET / plate**. *taghfirū* — ⭐⭐ **COVER / forgive** (⭐ ***mighfar* = a HELMET** — what COVERS!; ⭐ ***al-Ghafūr***).
- **Here it means:** "**But if you pardon, overlook, and forgive — then indeed Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TURN — from "BEWARE" to "FORGIVE" — and THREE LEVELS of forgiveness, each deeper:** ⭐ **(1) *TAʿFŪ* — PARDON = ERASE the offence** — ⭐ **like the wind wiping footprints from sand: no trace left, no punishment.** ⭐ **(2) *TAṢFAḤŪ* — OVERLOOK = TURN THE PAGE** — ⭐ ***ṣafḥah* = a PAGE** — ⭐ **don't even rebuke or mention it; move on to a fresh page.** ⭐ **(3) *TAGHFIRŪ* — FORGIVE = COVER it** — ⭐ ***mighfar* = a HELMET** — ⭐ **cover it from others' eyes, protect them from its shame.** ⭐⭐⭐ **ERASE it, TURN THE PAGE, COVER it — three escalating levels of generosity.** ⭐ **And the reward: *fa-inna'llāha GHAFŪRUN RAḤĪM*** — ⭐ **forgive them, and Allah (who is Forgiving) forgives you** — ⭐ **measure for measure** (⭐ *wa'l-yaʿfū wa'l-yaṣfaḥū a-lā tuḥibbūna an yaghfira'llāhu lakum* — 24:22 — "let them PARDON and OVERLOOK — don't you love that Allah should FORGIVE you?" — revealed about Abū Bakr forgiving a relative who slandered ʿĀʾishah!).
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿAFW* — the LAYLAT AL-QADR DUʿĀʾ** — ⭐ ***Allāhumma innaka ʿAFUWWUN tuḥibbu'l-ʿAFWA fa'ʿFU ʿannī*** — ⭐ **"O Allah, You are the PARDONER, You love PARDON — so PARDON me"** — ⭐ **taught by the Prophet ﷺ to ʿĀʾishah for the Night of Decree (Tirmidhī)** — ⭐ **ask Allah to ERASE your sins as you ERASE your family's faults.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿĀfiyah* = WELL-BEING** — ⭐ **same root: health = the "erasing" of illness.** ⭐ ***ʿAfwan* = "you're WELCOME / SORRY."**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṢAFḤ* → *ṢAFḤAH* (a PAGE) → *MUṢĀFAḤAH* (a HANDSHAKE)** — ⭐ **turn the page on the offence — and shake hands.** ⭐ **The ḥadīth: "*No two Muslims meet and shake hands (yataṣāfaḥān) except that they are forgiven before they part*" (Abū Dāwūd, Tirmidhī).** ⭐ **The handshake IS the *ṣafḥ*.** ⭐ ***Ṣafḥah* = a WEB PAGE!**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **And the *ṢAFḤ JAMĪL* — the *jamīl* triad:** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:10** — *hajran jamīlā* (BEAUTIFUL leaving) ⭐ **Maʿārij 70:5** — *ṣabran jamīlā* (BEAUTIFUL patience) ⭐ **Ḥijr 15:85** — ***fa'ṣfaḥi'ṣ-ṣafḥa'l-jamīl*** (BEAUTIFUL **OVERLOOKING**) — ⭐ **the *ṣafḥ* of this verse!**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***in*** + three jussives (*taʿfū, taṣfaḥū, taghfirū* — *nūn* dropped). ⭐ ***fa-inna*** = answer (nominal, with *fa*) — ⭐ **the answer is a REASON ("because Allah is Forgiving") implying "He'll forgive you too."**
- **Why this exact word:** **three verbs** — ⭐ **erase, turn the page, cover — a ladder of forgiveness for the people closest to you.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE LEVELS: *TAʿFŪ* (ERASE — wind wiping footprints), *TAṢFAḤŪ* (TURN THE PAGE — *ṣafḥah*), *TAGHFIRŪ* (COVER — *mighfar*, a helmet).** ⭐⭐⭐ **24:22: "PARDON and OVERLOOK — don't you love that Allah FORGIVE you?" (Abū Bakr).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿAFW* — the LAYLAT AL-QADR duʿāʾ: *Allāhumma innaka ʿafuwwun tuḥibbu'l-ʿafwa fa'ʿfu ʿannī*.** ⭐⭐ ***ʿĀfiyah* = WELL-BEING; *ʿafwan* = "you're WELCOME."** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ṢAFḤ* → *ṢAFḤAH* (PAGE / WEB PAGE) → *MUṢĀFAḤAH* (HANDSHAKE — "forgiven before they part").** ⭐⭐ ***Jamīl* triad: *hajr* (73:10), *ṣabr* (70:5), *ṢAFḤ* (15:85).** ⭐ **BEWARE (v.14a) → FORGIVE (v.14b).**

**34. إِنَّمَا أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَأَوْلَادُكُمْ فِتْنَةٌ — *innamā amwālukum wa awlādukum fitnah*** · v.15
- **Root feelings:** *amwāl* — ⭐ **WEALTH**. *awlād* — ⭐ **CHILDREN**. *fitnah* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**SMELTING GOLD in FIRE**" family — ***fatana* = to put GOLD into FIRE to separate it from impurities / to TEST its purity**; ⭐ ***fitnah* = a TRIAL / TEST / temptation — and civil STRIFE** (⭐ ***fitnah* = TURMOIL / sedition** — modern!); ⭐ ***fātin* = CHARMING / captivating**; ⭐ ***maftūn* = INFATUATED / afflicted** (⭐ **Qalam 68:6** — *bi-ayyikumu'l-**maftūn***!); ⭐ **Burūj 85:10** — *inna'lladhīna **fatanū** al-muʾminīn* (persecuted — with FIRE — the trench!); ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:31** (the 19 = a *fitnah*); ⭐ **Jinn 72:17** — *li-naftinahum fīh*.
- **Here it means:** "**Your wealth and your children are only a test…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FITNAH* = GOLD IN THE FURNACE.** ⭐ **The original image: a goldsmith puts gold into FIRE — the impurities burn off, the TRUE gold is revealed.** ⭐ **Your wealth and children are the FIRE — they REVEAL what your faith is really made of.** ⭐⭐ **Will you hoard (Humazah 104), be distracted (Munāfiqūn 63:9), be held back (Taghābun 64:14)? — or will they purify you through giving, patience, and raising them well?** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INNAMĀ* — "ONLY"** — ⭐ **wealth and children are NOTHING BUT a test** — ⭐ **not permanent possessions, not your identity — TESTS.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE WEALTH & CHILDREN ARC — across the trio:** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:9** — *lā **tulhikum** amwālukum wa lā awlādukum* — ⭐ **DON'T let them DISTRACT you.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:14** — *inna min azwājikum wa awlādikum **ʿaduwwan*** — ⭐ **SOME may act as ENEMIES.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:15** — *innamā amwālukum wa awlādukum **fitnah*** — ⭐ **ALL are a TEST.** ⭐⭐ **DISTRACTION → ENEMY → TEST: the honest truth about the two things people love most.** ⭐ **And Nūḥ 71:12** — *yumdidkum bi-**amwālin wa banīn*** — ⭐ **they're also a BLESSING (promised for istighfār).** ⭐ **Blessing and test — both true.**
  ⭐⭐ ***Fitnah* thread:** ⭐ **Burūj 85:10** — *fatanū* (persecuted with FIRE — literal!). ⭐ **Qalam 68:6** — *al-maftūn* (the one who FAILED the test). ⭐ **Jinn 72:17** — *li-naftinahum* (tested by abundance). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:15** — wealth and children = *fitnah*. ⭐ ***Fitnah* = TURMOIL (modern); *fātin* = CHARMING.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***innamā*** = restriction ("**ONLY / nothing but**"). ⭐ ***fitnatun*** = singular predicate for two subjects (wealth + children = **one test**).
- **Why this exact word:** *fitnah* — ⭐ **the furnace image: dear things as the fire that reveals your metal.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FITNAH* = GOLD IN THE FURNACE — the fire REVEALS what you're made of.** ⭐ **Wealth & children = the FIRE.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***INNAMĀ* = ONLY a test — not your identity.** ⭐⭐⭐ **WEALTH & CHILDREN ARC: DISTRACTION (63:9) → some an ENEMY (64:14) → all a TEST (64:15) — and a BLESSING (Nūḥ 71:12).** ⭐⭐ ***Fitnah*: Burūj 85:10 (FIRE — literal), Qalam 68:6 (*maftūn* — failed), Jinn 72:17.** ⭐ ***Fitnah* = TURMOIL; *fātin* = CHARMING.**

**35. وَاللَّهُ عِندَهُ أَجْرٌ عَظِيمٌ — *wa'llāhu ʿindahu ajrun ʿaẓīm*** · v.15
- **Root feelings:** *ʿindahu* — ⭐ **WITH HIM** (⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:11** — *ʿindaka baytan*; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:11** — *mā ʿinda'llāh*!). *ajr* — ⭐ **a REWARD / wage** (⭐ ***ujrah* = a FEE**; ⭐ ***ījār* = RENT**). *ʿaẓīm* — ⭐ **TREMENDOUS**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and Allah has with Him a tremendous reward.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE COMPARISON: what you HAVE (wealth, children — a test, temporary) vs what's WITH HIM (a tremendous reward — permanent).** ⭐ ***ʿINDAHU*** — ⭐ **"WITH HIM"** — ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:11** — *mā **ʿinda'llāhi** khayrun mina'l-lahwi wa mina't-tijārah* — ⭐ **"what's WITH Allah is better than amusement and trade."** ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:11** — Āsiyah: *ibni lī **ʿindaka** baytan* — ⭐ **"build me a house WITH You."** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE surahs: what's WITH Allah — better than trade (62), a house worth leaving a palace for (66), a reward greater than wealth and children (64).**
  ⭐⭐ ***AJR* — a WAGE** — ⭐ **pass the test (*fitnah*), collect the wage (*ajr*)** — ⭐ **the commerce language again: a test is a job; the reward is the pay.** ⭐ ***Ajrun ʿAẒĪM*** — ⭐ **Qalam 68:3** — *ajran ghayra mamnūn* (the Prophet's ﷺ endless wage); ⭐ **here: TREMENDOUS.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***ʿindahu*** fronted = emphasis on "**WITH HIM**." ⭐ ***ajrun ʿaẓīmun*** = indefinite + adjective (vast, unspecified).
- **Why this exact word:** *ʿindahu* — ⭐ **what is with Him, weighed against what is with you.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **What you HAVE (wealth, children — a TEST) vs what's WITH HIM (*ʿINDAHU* — a TREMENDOUS reward).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿINDA'LLĀH* ×3: better than TRADE (Jumuʿah 62:11), a HOUSE worth leaving a palace (Taḥrīm 66:11), a REWARD greater than wealth & children (Taghābun 64:15).** ⭐⭐ **Pass the TEST (*fitnah*) → collect the WAGE (*ajr*).** ⭐ ***Ujrah* = FEE; *ījār* = RENT.**

## §8 — Fear Him as Much as You Can (v.16–18)

**36. فَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ — *fa'ttaqu'llāha ma'staṭaʿtum*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *ittaqū* — ⭐⭐⭐ **SHIELD yourselves / be mindful** (*taqwā* ← ***wiqāyah* = PROTECTION**; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:6** — *qū anfusakum*!; ⭐ ***al-wiqāyah khayrun mina'l-ʿilāj*** = PREVENTION is better than CURE!). *istaṭaʿtum* — ⭐⭐⭐ **you are ABLE** (Form X of *ṭāʿa*; ⭐ ***istiṭāʿah* = ABILITY / CAPACITY**; ⭐ ***mustaṭāʿ* = POSSIBLE**; ⭐ **Qalam 68:42** — *fa-lā **yastaṭīʿūn*** (on the Day, they'll be UNABLE to prostrate)!; ⭐ ***man istaṭāʿa ilayhi sabīlā*** — Hajj "for whoever is ABLE" — 3:97!).
- **Here it means:** "**So fear Allah as much as you are able…**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MERCIFUL MEASURE — *MA'STAṬAʿTUM* — "as much as you're ABLE."** ⭐ **Not perfection — your best effort within your capacity.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Compare ĀL ʿIMRĀN 3:102** — *ittaqu'llāha **ḥaqqa tuqātih*** — ⭐ **"fear Allah as He DESERVES to be feared."** ⭐ Classical: **when 3:102 was revealed, the Companions found it heavy — who can fear Him as He truly DESERVES? — so this verse (64:16) came: "as much as you're ABLE."** ⭐ **(Many scholars see it as explaining 3:102 rather than cancelling it: "as He deserves" = "to the full extent of YOUR ability.")** ⭐⭐ **Ṭalāq 65:7** — *lā yukallifu'llāhu nafsan illā mā ātāhā* — ⭐ **"no burden beyond what He gave."** ⭐ **Baqarah 2:286** — *illā wusʿahā* — **"beyond its capacity."** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:16** — ***ma'staṭaʿtum*** — **"as you're ABLE."** ⭐⭐⭐ **Three formulations of divine fairness — capacity, gift, ability.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ISTAṬAʿTUM* — and QALAM 68:42–43:** ⭐ **on the Day, they're called to prostrate *fa-LĀ YASTAṬĪʿŪN* — "and they will NOT BE ABLE"** — ⭐ ***wa qad kānū yudʿawna ila's-sujūdi wa hum sālimūn*** — **"they were called when they were ABLE."** ⭐ **Taghābun: use your ABILITY now — Qalam: the ability will be taken.** ⭐ ***Istiṭāʿah* — the Hajj condition (3:97).**
  ⭐⭐ ***Ittaqū* — the *taqwā* thread:** Taḥrīm 66:6 (*qū* — shield your FAMILY), Ṭalāq 65 (×5), Muzzammil 73:17, Muddaththir 74:56 — ⭐ **and here: as much as you CAN.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***mā*** = ***maṣdariyyah ẓarfiyyah*** — "**for as long as / to the extent that**" you are able. ⭐ ***fa*** = consequence (because wealth and children are a test — SO be mindful).
- **Why this exact word:** *istiṭāʿah* — ⭐ **ability as the measure — mercy built into the command.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***FA'TTAQU'LLĀHA MA'STAṬAʿTUM* = fear Allah AS MUCH AS YOU'RE ABLE — not perfection, your BEST.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Āl ʿImrān 3:102 *ḥaqqa tuqātih* (as He DESERVES) — found heavy — explained by 64:16 (as you're ABLE).** ⭐⭐⭐ **DIVINE FAIRNESS ×3: *wusʿahā* (2:286 — CAPACITY), *mā ātāhā* (Ṭalāq 65:7 — what He GAVE), *ma'staṭaʿtum* (64:16 — ABILITY).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Qalam 68:42: on the Day, *LĀ YASTAṬĪʿŪN* — use your ability NOW.** ⭐ ***Istiṭāʿah* — Hajj (3:97).** ⭐ ***Al-wiqāyah khayrun mina'l-ʿilāj*.**

**37. وَاسْمَعُوا وَأَطِيعُوا وَأَنفِقُوا خَيْرًا لِّأَنفُسِكُمْ — *wa'smaʿū wa aṭīʿū wa anfiqū khayran li-anfusikum*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *isma ʿū* — ⭐ **LISTEN** (*samʿ*; ⭐ ***samāʿah* = a STETHOSCOPE / earphone** — clinical!; ⭐ ***sumʿah* = REPUTATION**). *aṭīʿū* — ⭐ **OBEY** (*ṭāʿah* — same root as *istaṭaʿtum*!). *anfiqū* — ⭐⭐⭐ **SPEND** (*infāq* — ⭐ **the *munāfiq* root!**; ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:10** — *wa anfiqū*!). *khayran* — ⭐ **[it is] BETTER**. *anfusikum* — ⭐ **YOURSELVES**.
- **Here it means:** "**…and listen, and obey, and spend — it is better for yourselves.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THREE ACTIONS: LISTEN → OBEY → SPEND.** ⭐ ***Isma ʿū*** — **receive the message** ⭐ ***aṭīʿū*** — **act on it** ⭐ ***anfiqū*** — **give from what you have.** ⭐⭐ **The opposite of the hypocrites:** ⭐ **they LISTENED but didn't understand (*lā yafqahūn* — 63:3)** ⭐ **they claimed obedience but blocked others (*ṣaddū* — 63:2)** ⭐ **they said "DON'T SPEND" (*lā tunfiqū* — 63:7).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Taghābun reverses all three.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AṬĪʿŪ* (OBEY) and *ISTAṬAʿTUM* (you're ABLE) — the SAME ROOT (*ṭ-w-ʿ*), in the SAME verse!** ⭐ ***Ṭāʿah* = OBEDIENCE; *istiṭāʿah* = ABILITY** — ⭐ **you OBEY with the ABILITY you've been given.** ⭐ **The Arabic itself ties duty to capacity.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ANFIQŪ KHAYRAN LI-ANFUSIKUM* — "SPEND — it's BETTER for YOURSELVES"** — ⭐ **the twist: spending isn't LOSS — it's for YOU.** ⭐ **What you give is what you keep** (⭐ the ḥadīth: ʿĀʾishah gave away a slaughtered sheep except one shoulder; the Prophet ﷺ said "*ALL of it remains except its shoulder*" — Tirmidhī). ⭐ ***Anfiqū* … *anfusikum*** — ⭐ **a near-pun: SPEND (*anfiqū*) for your SOULS (*anfus*).** ⭐⭐ **The *infāq* thread: Ṭalāq 65:6–7 (spend on her), Munāfiqūn 63:7 (hypocrites: DON'T spend), 63:10 (SPEND before death), Taghābun 64:16 (SPEND — for yourselves).**
  ⭐ Clinical hook: ⭐ ***samāʿah* = a STETHOSCOPE** (from "listen"!).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ three imperatives. ⭐⭐ ***khayran*** — classically **accusative of an implied verb** ("[and do] what's BETTER") or ***khabar* of an implied *yakun*** ("[it will be] better").
- **Why this exact word:** *anfiqū khayran li-anfusikum* — ⭐ **spending reframed as self-benefit — the cure for the hypocrite's "don't spend."**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **LISTEN → OBEY → SPEND — reversing the hypocrites: didn't UNDERSTAND (63:3), BLOCKED (63:2), "DON'T SPEND" (63:7).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***AṬĪʿŪ* (OBEY) & *ISTAṬAʿTUM* (ABLE) — SAME ROOT, SAME VERSE: obey with the ability given.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ANFIQŪ KHAYRAN LI-ANFUSIKUM* = SPEND — it's for YOURSELVES ("all of it remains except its shoulder" — Tirmidhī).** ⭐ ***Anfiqū* / *anfusikum* — near-pun.** ⭐⭐ ***Infāq* thread: 65:6, 63:7, 63:10, 64:16.** ⭐ ***Samāʿah* = STETHOSCOPE.**

**38. وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهِ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ — *wa man yūqa shuḥḥa nafsihi fa-ulāʾika humu'l-mufliḥūn*** · v.16
- **Root feelings:** *yūqa* — ⭐⭐⭐ **is PROTECTED / shielded** (**passive** of *waqā* — ⭐ **the *TAQWĀ* root!**; ⭐ **Taḥrīm 66:6** — *qū anfusakum*!). *shuḥḥ* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**GREEDY AVARICE / tight-fistedness**" family — ***shuḥḥ* = intense, grasping GREED** (⭐ worse than *bukhl* — stinginess WITH greed: you won't give AND you want what others have!); ⭐ ***shaḥīḥ* = AVARICIOUS / greedy**; ⭐ ***shuḥḥ* = SCARCITY / shortage** (⭐ modern: ***shuḥḥ* = SHORTAGE** — *shuḥḥ al-miyāh* = **WATER SHORTAGE**!; ⭐ ***shaḥīḥ* = SCARCE / rare**!). *mufliḥūn* — ⭐⭐⭐ **the SUCCESSFUL** (*falāḥ* ← the FARMER splitting the soil; ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:10** — *laʿallakum **tufliḥūn***!; ⭐ ***ḥayya ʿala'l-falāḥ***!).
- **Here it means:** "**And whoever is protected from the greed of his own soul — those are the successful ones.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE MOST QUOTED LINE OF THE SURAH — and the deepest diagnosis.** ⭐ ***SHUḤḤA NAFSIH*** — ⭐ **"the GREED of his OWN soul"** — ⭐ **the enemy isn't outside — it's the grasping, hoarding, clutching instinct INSIDE you.** ⭐ **(⭐ Maʿārij 70:19–21 — *halūʿ… manūʿā* — man created anxious and WITHHOLDING!)** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YŪQA* — PASSIVE: "is PROTECTED from it"** — ⭐ **you can't free yourself alone; you need Allah to SHIELD you** — ⭐ **the *taqwā* root (*waqā*) in the passive** — ⭐ **v.16a: *ittaqū* (YOU shield yourselves) → v.16b: *yūqa* (you ARE shielded — by Him).** ⭐ **Your effort + His protection.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **WORD-FOR-WORD in AL-ḤASHR 59:9** — *wa man **yūqa shuḥḥa nafsihi fa-ulāʾika humu'l-mufliḥūn*** — ⭐ **describing the ANṢĀR who welcomed the Muhājirūn and "preferred others over themselves even though they were in need."** ⭐ **The Anṣār — the model of being FREED from *shuḥḥ*.** ⭐⭐ **And recall MUNĀFIQŪN 63:7 — the hypocrites (also Madīnans!) said "don't spend on those with the Messenger" — ⭐ the OPPOSITE of the Anṣār.** ⭐ **Same city: the Anṣār (freed from *shuḥḥ*) vs the hypocrites (enslaved by it).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHUḤḤ* = SHORTAGE (modern)** — ⭐ ***shuḥḥ al-miyāh* = WATER SHORTAGE** — ⭐ **the greedy soul lives in a permanent sense of SCARCITY** — ⭐ **always feeling there isn't enough.** ⭐ **The ḥadīth: "*Beware of SHUḤḤ — it destroyed those before you: it drove them to shed blood and violate what was sacred*" (Muslim).**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***HUMU'L-MUFLIḤŪN* — the SUCCESSFUL** — ⭐ **Jumuʿah 62:10** — *laʿallakum **tufliḥūn*** — ⭐ **remember Allah in the market → success.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:16** — freed from greed → ***al-mufliḥūn***. ⭐ **Shams 91:9** — *qad **aflaḥa** man zakkāhā* — ⭐ **SUCCESS = purifying the soul — and freedom from *shuḥḥ* IS that purification.** ⭐ ***Falāḥ* ← *fallāḥ* (FARMER) — harvest after hard ploughing.** ⭐ **And Munāfiqūn 63:9 — *humu'l-KHĀSIRŪN* (the LOSERS) → Taghābun 64:16 — *humu'l-MUFLIḤŪN* (the WINNERS).** ⭐⭐⭐ **The trio's two verdicts, same structure (*ulāʾika humu'l-…*).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐⭐ ***yūqa*** = **passive jussive** of *waqā* (*yūqā* → *yūqa*, final alif shortened). ⭐ ***shuḥḥa*** = 2nd object. ⭐⭐ ***humu'l-mufliḥūna*** — separating pronoun + definite = **"THEY are THE successful"** (restriction).
- **Why this exact word:** *shuḥḥ* — ⭐ **the inner greed that the whole trio has circled — named, and its cure given.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHUḤḤA NAFSIH* = the GREED of your OWN soul — the enemy INSIDE (Maʿārij 70:19–21).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YŪQA* = PASSIVE — PROTECTED (by Him) — the *taqwā* root: v.16a YOU shield (*ittaqū*) → v.16b you ARE shielded (*yūqa*).** ⭐⭐⭐ **= AL-ḤASHR 59:9 word-for-word — the ANṢĀR, who preferred others in their need — vs the HYPOCRITES of the same city ("don't spend," 63:7).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHUḤḤ* = SHORTAGE (*shuḥḥ al-miyāh* = WATER SHORTAGE) — the greedy soul lives in permanent SCARCITY.** ⭐ **"Beware of *shuḥḥ* — it destroyed those before you" (Muslim).** ⭐⭐⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:9 *humu'l-KHĀSIRŪN* (LOSERS) → Taghābun 64:16 *humu'l-MUFLIḤŪN* (WINNERS).** ⭐⭐ ***Falāḥ*: Jumuʿah 62:10, Shams 91:9.**

**39. إِن تُقْرِضُوا اللَّهَ قَرْضًا حَسَنًا يُضَاعِفْهُ لَكُمْ وَيَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ — *in tuqriḍu'llāha qarḍan ḥasanan yuḍāʿifhu lakum wa yaghfir lakum*** · v.17
- **Root feelings:** *tuqriḍū* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**CUTTING OFF a portion → LENDING**" family — *qaraḍa* = **to CUT, to snip off**; ⭐ ***qarḍ* = a LOAN** (a portion "cut off" from your wealth and given!); ⭐⭐ ***miqrāḍ* = SCISSORS** (the "cutter"!); ⭐ ***qarḍ ḥasan* = an interest-free LOAN** (⭐ a modern Islamic finance term!); ⭐ ***iqtirāḍ* = BORROWING**; ⭐ ***inqarāḍa* = to become EXTINCT** (⭐ modern: *inqirāḍ* = **EXTINCTION** — "cut off" completely!); ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** — *wa aqriḍu'llāha **qarḍan ḥasanā***!. *ḥasan* — ⭐ **GOOD / beautiful** (*ḥusn*). *yuḍāʿif* — ⭐⭐ the "**DOUBLING / weakness**" family — *ḍaʿufa* = to be WEAK; ⭐ ***ḍiʿf* = DOUBLE / multiple** (⭐ ***aḍʿāf* = MANY TIMES**); ⭐ ***ḍuʿf* = WEAKNESS**; ⭐⭐ ***ḍāʿafa* (Form III) = to DOUBLE / MULTIPLY**; ⭐ ***muḍāʿafāt* = COMPLICATIONS** (⭐ clinical!: *muḍāʿafāt* = **complications** of a disease — things that "multiply"!).
- **Here it means:** "**If you lend Allah a goodly loan, He will multiply it for you and forgive you.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BOLDEST METAPHOR IN THE QURʾĀN — LENDING TO ALLAH.** ⭐ **Allah owns everything (v.1 — *lahu'l-mulk*), the treasuries are His (Munāfiqūn 63:7 — *khazāʾin*) — and He says: "LEND to Me."** ⭐⭐ **Why "lend"? Because a loan is RETURNED** — ⭐ **your charity isn't lost; it's a LOAN you'll be repaid — MULTIPLIED.** ⭐ **A loan to the richest, most reliable Borrower there is.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QARḌ* ← *QARAḌA* (to CUT)** — ⭐ **you "CUT OFF" a portion of your wealth and hand it over** — ⭐ ***MIQRĀḌ* = SCISSORS!** ⭐ **Every act of charity is a snip of the scissors — and the snipped piece grows.** ⭐ ***Qarḍ ḥasan* = an INTEREST-FREE loan** — **the Islamic finance term comes straight from this verse!** ⭐ ***Inqirāḍ* = EXTINCTION.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ **THE BANKING SET — complete across your study:** ⭐ **Muzzammil 73:20** — *wa aqriḍu'llāha **qarḍan ḥasanā*** (a LOAN to Allah — the first). ⭐ **Ḥāqqah 69:24** — *bimā **aslaftum*** (what you PAID IN ADVANCE). ⭐ **Muddaththir 74:38** — ***rahīnah*** (held as COLLATERAL). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:9** — ***taghābun*** (the AUDIT of mutual loss/gain). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:17** — ***qarḍan ḥasanan yuḍāʿifhu*** (a LOAN, MULTIPLIED). ⭐ **And *dayn* (debt) — *yawm ad-dīn*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Loan, advance payment, collateral, audit, multiplication, settlement — the Hereafter is the great bank.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUḌĀʿIFHU* — MULTIPLY** — ⭐ ***ḍiʿf* = DOUBLE** — ⭐ **2:261: like a seed that grows SEVEN ears, each with a HUNDRED grains — 700×, and Allah multiplies more for whom He wills.** ⭐⭐ **And the root's other face: *ḍuʿf* = WEAKNESS** — ⭐ **your small, WEAK gift is DOUBLED by the Strong.** ⭐ Clinical hook: ⭐ ***muḍāʿafāt* = COMPLICATIONS** (what "multiplies" from a disease).
  ⭐⭐ ***Wa yaghfir lakum*** — ⭐ **not just multiplied — FORGIVEN too.** ⭐ **Charity = a loan repaid many times + a sin-eraser** (⭐ *aṣ-ṣadaqatu tuṭfiʾu'l-khaṭīʾah* — "charity extinguishes sin like water extinguishes fire" — Tirmidhī).
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***in*** + jussive (*tuqriḍū*) + jussive answers (*yuḍāʿifhu, yaghfir*). ⭐ ***qarḍan ḥasanan*** = cognate accusative + adjective. ⭐ ***yuḍāʿif*** = **Form III** (multiply). ⚠️ ⭐ **Variant: *yuḍaʿʿifhu*** (Form II — also "multiply").
- **Why this exact word:** *qarḍ* — ⭐ **charity as a loan to the One who owns everything — guaranteed, multiplied, and forgiving.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***TUQRIḌU'LLĀHA QARḌAN ḤASANĀ* = LEND to ALLAH — the Owner of everything says "lend to Me" — a loan is RETURNED, MULTIPLIED.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***QARḌ* ← *QARAḌA* (CUT) — *MIQRĀḌ* = SCISSORS: each charity is a snip that grows.** ⭐ ***Qarḍ ḥasan* = INTEREST-FREE loan (Islamic finance!); *inqirāḍ* = EXTINCTION.** ⭐⭐⭐ **BANKING SET: *qarḍ* (73:20 & 64:17), *aslaftum* (69:24), *rahīnah* (74:38), *taghābun* (64:9), *yuḍāʿif* (64:17), *dayn* (*yawm ad-dīn*).** ⭐⭐⭐ ***YUḌĀʿIFHU* = MULTIPLY (2:261 — 700×); *ḍuʿf* = WEAKNESS — your weak gift DOUBLED; *muḍāʿafāt* = COMPLICATIONS (clinical).** ⭐ **"Charity extinguishes sin as water extinguishes fire" (Tirmidhī).**

**40. وَاللَّهُ شَكُورٌ حَلِيمٌ — *wa'llāhu shakūrun ḥalīm*** · v.17
- **Root feelings:** *shakūr* — ⭐⭐⭐ the "**GRATITUDE / appreciation that GROWS**" family — *shakara* = **to thank, to appreciate**; ⭐ ***shukr* = GRATITUDE** (⭐ ***shukran* = THANK YOU**!); ⭐ ***shākir* = grateful** (⭐ **Insān 76:3** — *immā **shākiran** wa immā kafūrā*!); ⭐ ***shakūr* = intensely, abundantly GRATEFUL** (*faʿūl* intensive); ⭐ ***ash-Shakūr*** — **a Name of Allah: the APPRECIATIVE, who rewards small deeds abundantly**; ⭐ classically, ***dābbah shakūr* = an animal that FATTENS on very little fodder** (gives a big return on a small input!). *ḥalīm* — ⭐⭐ the "**FORBEARANCE / mature calm**" family — *ḥaluma* = to be forbearing, gentle, not hasty to punish; ⭐ ***ḥilm* = FORBEARANCE, mature patience**; ⭐ ***al-Ḥalīm*** — a Name of Allah; ⭐ ***ḥulm* = a DREAM** (⭐ same letters *ḥ-l-m*!; ⭐ *aḥlām* = **DREAMS**); ⭐ ***iḥtilām* = PUBERTY / reaching maturity** (⭐ fiqh: ***bulūgh al-ḥulum*** = **reaching PUBERTY** — the age of religious responsibility!; ⭐ clinical!).
- **Here it means:** "**And Allah is Most Appreciative, Most Forbearing.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ASH-SHAKŪR* — ALLAH IS GRATEFUL?!** ⭐ **We thank HIM — but He calls Himself *SHAKŪR* — the APPRECIATIVE, the One who THANKS** — ⭐ **meaning: He appreciates your smallest deed and rewards it out of all proportion.** ⭐⭐ **Classical image: *dābbah shakūr* — an animal that grows FAT on a tiny bit of fodder** — ⭐ **Allah turns a tiny deed into a huge reward.** ⭐⭐⭐ **And it explains v.17a: you lend a little (*qarḍ*) → He MULTIPLIES it (*yuḍāʿif*) → BECAUSE He is *SHAKŪR*.** ⭐ **The Borrower who appreciates the loan more than the lender.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHAKŪR* — the INSĀN thread** — ⭐ **Insān 76:3** — *innā hadaynāhu's-sabīla immā **shākiran** wa immā **kafūrā*** — ⭐ **man: GRATEFUL or UNGRATEFUL.** ⭐ **Taghābun 64:2** — *fa-minkum **kāfirun** wa minkum muʾmin* (disbeliever / believer). ⭐ **Taghābun 64:17** — *wa'llāhu **SHAKŪR*** — ⭐ **ALLAH is the ultimately GRATEFUL one — He "thanks" the grateful by multiplying their reward.** ⭐ ***Shukran* = THANK YOU — you say it daily.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***AL-ḤALĪM* — FORBEARING** — ⭐ **He doesn't rush to punish** — ⭐ **why pair it with *shakūr*?** ⭐ Classical: **He's *shakūr* toward your good deeds (multiplying them) and *ḥalīm* toward your bad deeds (not punishing hastily, giving time to repent).** ⭐ **Generous with your good; patient with your bad.** ⭐⭐ ***Ḥulm* = a DREAM; *iḥtilām* / *bulūgh al-ḥulum* = PUBERTY** — ⭐ **same letters: *ḥilm* = MATURE calm — the maturity you're supposed to reach at *bulūgh*.**
  ⭐ Clinical hook: ⭐ ***iḥtilām* = PUBERTY / nocturnal emission (the fiqh marker of maturity).**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ ***shakūrun ḥalīmun*** — two intensive attributes (*faʿūl*, *faʿīl*).
- **Why this exact word:** *shakūr* — ⭐ **the explanation of the multiplied loan: the Borrower is grateful.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ ***ASH-SHAKŪR* = ALLAH is the APPRECIATIVE one — He "THANKS" your smallest deed by MULTIPLYING it.** ⭐⭐ ***Dābbah shakūr* = an animal that FATTENS on a little fodder — tiny deed → huge reward.** ⭐⭐⭐ **WHY the loan multiplies (v.17a): because He is *SHAKŪR*.** ⭐⭐ **Insān 76:3 — man *shākir* or *kafūr*; Allah is *SHAKŪR*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***SHAKŪR* (generous with your GOOD) + *ḤALĪM* (patient with your BAD).** ⭐⭐ ***Ḥilm* (forbearance) / *ḥulm* (DREAM) / *iḥtilām* (PUBERTY — *bulūgh al-ḥulum*).** ⭐ ***Shukran* = THANK YOU.**

**41. عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ — *ʿālimu'l-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādati'l-ʿAzīzu'l-Ḥakīm*** · v.18
- **Root feelings:** *ʿālim* — ⭐ **KNOWER**. *ghayb* — ⭐ **the UNSEEN** (⭐ **Jinn 72:26**; ⭐ ***ghaybūbah* = a COMA**). *shahādah* — ⭐ **the WITNESSED / visible** (⭐ ***shahādah* = TESTIMONY / CERTIFICATE**). *al-ʿAzīz* — ⭐ **the ALMIGHTY / precious**. *al-Ḥakīm* — ⭐ **the ALL-WISE** (⭐ ← *ḥakamah*, the BRIDLE).
- **Here it means:** "**Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, the Almighty, the Wise.**"
- **Word magic:** ⭐⭐⭐ **THE TRIO'S GRAND CLOSING — two bookends at once.** ⭐ **BOOKEND 1 — JUMUʿAH 62:8** — *thumma turaddūna ilā **ʿālimi'l-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādah*** — ⭐ **Taghābun 64:18** — ***ʿālimu'l-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādah*** — ⭐ **the same Name, three surahs apart.** ⭐ **BOOKEND 2 — JUMUʿAH 62:1, 3** — *al-Maliki'l-Quddūsi'l-**ʿAzīzi'l-Ḥakīm*** / *wa huwa'l-**ʿAzīzu'l-Ḥakīm*** — ⭐ **Taghābun 64:18** — ***al-ʿAzīzu'l-Ḥakīm*** — ⭐⭐⭐ **The three Friday-themed surahs (62, 63, 64) OPEN with *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* (62:1) and CLOSE with *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* (64:18).** ⭐ **Inside that frame: the Prophet's ﷺ mission, the Friday prayer, the hypocrites exposed, the Day of mutual loss and gain.**
  ⭐⭐⭐ ***ʿĀLIM AL-GHAYB WA'SH-SHAHĀDAH* — the PERFECT closing for a surah about AUDITING trades** — ⭐ **He knows the UNSEEN (*ghayb* — your intentions, the hypocrite's hidden exit, your secret greed — *shuḥḥ*) AND the WITNESSED (*shahādah* — your visible spending, your public prayers).** ⭐ **The Auditor sees the hidden ledger AND the public one.** ⭐⭐ ***Shahādah* = also TESTIMONY** — ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:1** — the hypocrites' false ***nashhadu*** — ⭐ **He knows the *shahādah* (what's witnessed/said) and the *ghayb* (what's hidden behind it).**
  ⭐⭐ ***AL-ʿAZĪZ* (ALMIGHTY) + *AL-ḤAKĪM* (WISE)** — ⭐ **power to settle every account + wisdom to settle it justly.** ⭐ ***ʿAzīz* also = DEAR / precious (*ʿazīzī* = "my dear")** — ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:8** — *wa li'llāhi'l-**ʿizzah*** — ⭐ **all honour is His.**
- **Grammar bite:** ⭐ **four attributes in a row**, all nominative — ⭐ **predicates of an implied *huwa* ("[He is] the Knower…")**. ⭐ ***al-ghaybi wa'sh-shahādati*** = **merism** (unseen + seen = EVERYTHING).
- **Why this exact word:** **the closing Names** — ⭐ **bookending the whole trio and naming the perfect Auditor for the Day of *taghābun*.**
- 🧠 **Never forget:** ⭐⭐⭐ **DOUBLE BOOKEND: *ʿĀLIM AL-GHAYB WA'SH-SHAHĀDAH* (= Jumuʿah 62:8) + *AL-ʿAZĪZ AL-ḤAKĪM* (= Jumuʿah 62:1, 3).** ⭐ **The trio (62–63–64) OPENS and CLOSES on *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The perfect AUDITOR: knows the HIDDEN ledger (intentions, *shuḥḥ*, the hypocrite's exit) AND the PUBLIC one (spending, prayers).** ⭐⭐ ***Shahādah* — the hypocrites' false *nashhadu* (63:1).** ⭐⭐ **ALMIGHTY (power to settle) + WISE (wisdom to settle justly).** ⭐ **Munāfiqūn 63:8 *ʿizzah*.**

## ⚠️ Never-Forget Traps

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **إِنَّ مِنْ أَزْوَاجِكُمْ (v.14)** — ⚠️ ***min* = SOME of** — ⭐ **not "your spouses ARE enemies"**; enemy in EFFECT, not intention.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **وَاسْتَغْنَى اللَّهُ (v.6)** — ⚠️ **the human sin-word, here describing ALLAH** — ⭐ **His true self-sufficiency, not arrogance.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **بَلَىٰ (v.7)** — ⚠️ **YES to a NEGATIVE** ("you'll never be raised?" — "YES you will!").
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **يُوقَ (v.16)** — ⚠️ **PASSIVE jussive of *waqā*** — "is PROTECTED" (by Allah).
5. ⭐⭐⭐ **مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ (v.16)** — ⚠️ ***mā* = "as much as / to the extent"** (*maṣdariyyah ẓarfiyyah*), not "what."
6. ⭐⭐ **يَهْدِ قَلْبَهُ (v.11)** — ⚠️ **jussive, *yāʾ* dropped** — ⭐ **variant *yahdaʾ qalbuhu*.**
7. ⭐⭐ **أَبَشَرٌ يَهْدُونَنَا (v.6)** — ⚠️ **PLURAL verb for collective *bashar*.**
8. ⭐⭐ **شَكُورٌ (v.17)** — ⚠️ **Allah as APPRECIATIVE** — rewarding small deeds abundantly, not "grateful" in need.
9. ⭐⭐ **لَتُبْعَثُنَّ · لَتُنَبَّؤُنَّ (v.7)** — ⚠️ **passive + heavy *nūn*** (plural *nūn* dropped).
10. ⭐⭐ **أَن لَّن يُبْعَثُوا (v.7)** — ⚠️ ***an* = lightened *anna*.**
11. ⭐⭐ **يُدْخِلْهُ / خَالِدِينَ (v.9)** — ⚠️ **singular then plural** — each enters, together they abide.
12. ⭐⭐ **خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا (v.9) vs خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا (v.10)** — ⚠️ ***abadā* only with mercy here** (eternity of the Fire is affirmed elsewhere).
13. ⭐⭐ **فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ (v.13)** — ⚠️ ***lām al-amr*** + kasrah for two sukūns.
14. ⭐⭐ **وَأَطِيعُوا ... مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ (v.12, 16)** — ⚠️ **same root *ṭ-w-ʿ*** — obedience and ability.
15. ⭐⭐ **يُضَاعِفْهُ (v.17)** — ⚠️ **variant *yuḍaʿʿifhu*** (Form II).
16. ⭐ **صُوَرَكُمْ (v.3)** — ⚠️ **variant *ṣiwarakum*.**
17. ⭐ **خَيْرًا لِّأَنفُسِكُمْ (v.16)** — ⚠️ ***khayran* — accusative of an implied verb** ("[do] what's better").
18. ⭐ **فِتْنَةٌ (v.15)** — ⚠️ **ONE test for TWO things** (singular predicate).

## 🌙 Al-Jumuʿah · Al-Munāfiqūn · At-Taghābun Together (62 · 63 · 64)

⭐⭐⭐ **The Prophet ﷺ recited Al-Jumuʿah and Al-Munāfiqūn in the Friday prayer (Muslim). At-Taghābun completes the thought.**

| | **Al-Jumuʿah (62)** | **Al-Munāfiqūn (63)** | **At-Taghābun (64)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opens** | *yusabbiḥu… al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* | *idhā jāʾaka'l-munāfiqūn* | *yusabbiḥu… lahu'l-mulk wa lahu'l-ḥamd* |
| **Gathering** | *yawm al-jumuʿah* — the weekly GATHERING | *ḥattā yanfaḍḍū* — the plot to SCATTER | *yawm al-jamʿ* — the final GATHERING |
| **Trade** | *dharu'l-bayʿ* — LEAVE the sale | *humu'l-khāsirūn* — the LOSERS | *yawm at-taghābun* — the AUDIT of every trade |
| **Distraction** | *lahwā* — a caravan's drums | *lā tulhikum amwālukum wa lā awlādukum* | *amwālukum wa awlādukum fitnah* |
| **Spending** | *khayru'r-rāziqīn* | *lā tunfiqū* (them) / *wa anfiqū* (Him) | *wa anfiqū khayran li-anfusikum* · *qarḍan ḥasanā* |
| **The heart** | — | *ṭubiʿa ʿalā qulūbihim* (SEALED) | *yahdi qalbah* (GUIDED) |
| **Enemy** | — | *humu'l-ʿaduww fa'ḥdharhum* (in the community) | *ʿaduwwan lakum fa'ḥdharūhum* (in the home) |
| **The verdict** | *laʿallakum tufliḥūn* | *humu'l-khāsirūn* | *humu'l-mufliḥūn* |
| **Closes** | *wa'llāhu khayru'r-rāziqīn* | *wa'llāhu khabīrun bimā taʿmalūn* | *ʿālimu'l-ghayb wa'sh-shahādah, al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* |

⭐⭐⭐ **Al-Jumuʿah gathers you every Friday and tells you to leave your trade for an hour. Al-Munāfiqūn shows you the people who sat in that gathering with a secret exit — and refused to spend. At-Taghābun tells you the real trade: everything you hold is a test, every coin you give is a loan to Allah, and one Day every account will be audited.** ⭐ ***Al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* frames all three — first and last.**
